## Mandatory literature-audit packet

`notes/literature-audit-conventions.md` in full.

### Search questions

1. Is the 182-vertex cubic bipartite coset graph for
   `PGL(2,13)` with vertex stabilizers `S_4` and `D_24` already named,
   classified, or tabulated?
2. Is the bidirectional three-neighbor binary transform between these two
   homogeneous spaces already recorded, either as a coset-incidence identity,
   a Hecke/Radon transform, or a design/frame relation?

   the modular representation or graph-code literature?
4. Is the `[182,37,28]_2` code, its 78-word minimum shell, or the resulting
   reconstruction of the two 91-by-78 frames already known?
