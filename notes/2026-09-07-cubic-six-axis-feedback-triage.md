## What adds something

The focused project of determining the complex stabilization level along
the six-axis/A5 pencil is useful and more concrete than another universal
obstruction programme. It is not an explicit deliverable of C914's
known-locus comparison, C921's residual Voisin question, or C1116--C1120.
C907 covers broader higher-stabilization mechanisms. The suggested pencil
project would give those mechanisms a specific family on which to succeed
or fail.

Start with ell_C(X_b), keeping arithmetic rationality questions separate.
The feedback takes non-isotriviality and universal CH0-triviality of every
smooth member as inputs. Historical C910/C914 records describe those
claims and their source checks, but they are not re-proved by the current
two stabilization manuscripts or by C1116. A pencil project should first
identify the exact current model, smooth locus, and theorem establishing
that premise, rather than silently treating an older epilogue snapshot as
the current pair.

## A bounded first target

Look for a two-variable rationalization over the pencil's parameter field
C(B), potentially by finding a quartic del Pezzo fibration satisfying the
surface theorem over its relevant generic field. A birational map and its
inverse over C(B) spread out after deleting finitely many parameter values.
Together with the universal one-stabilization lower bound, this would give
exact level two on a nonempty open of the pencil. The exceptional smooth
fibres would then be a separate finite problem.

If no uniform construction is available, look first for individual
level-two members. C914/C923 identify the two Fermat parameters as one
exceptional moduli point; they are a natural test case, **not** an already
proved level-two point. Our two displayed TZ cubics have not been identified
with members of this pencil. Such an identification or a new rationalization
must be proved, not inferred from shared universal CH0-triviality.

Keep three questions distinct: the level of individual complex fibres,
the level of the geometric generic fibre, and rationality over C(B).
A parameter-field construction is a sufficient route to an open-family
result; failure to find one does not establish irrationality of the fibres.

Nor does stable irrationality of a very general cubic in the entire moduli
space imply the existence of a stably irrational member in this special
pencil. The pencil could lie entirely in the exceptional locus of that
theorem. Producing variation within a universally CH0-trivial family is an
additional research result, not a consequence of the ambient moduli result.

The useful discipline for any varying refinement is to require two things
before building a new formal framework: demonstrate variation on this
pencil, and prove compatibility with the relevant birational operations.
Marked integral/Hodge data, cycle descent and monodromy are candidates,
not established obstructions. The theta-lattice calculation is a warning
about losing integral information, not a ready-made bridge to stabilization.
