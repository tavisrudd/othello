## Summary, contribution, and significance

The central claim is irrationality of the first stabilization of every smooth complex cubic threefold. The mechanism selects whole quantum primary factors whose canonical rank-two modification has separated residue exponents. Faithful blowup transport and vanishing for all surface centers turn the selection into a fourfold birational invariant. A small quantum calculation and flatness in mixed bulk directions compute the stabilized endpoint.

The assembled paper adds two substantial results. First, combining the resonant rank-two residue invariant with odd dimension on even-rank-three factors gives the complete seventeen-family Picard-rank-one Fano classification after one stabilization. Second, a Hodge-fixed parameter base with the full cohomology fiber retained gives rational third-Hodge-structure conservation for the nine detected families. Very-general cancellation and arithmetic geometric finiteness are then applications of separately cited theorems.

These results would be significant for a specialist algebraic-geometry journal. The uniform cubic assertion is stronger in its quantifiers than a very-general stable-irrationality result; the numerical classification and Hodge conservation have distinct mathematical content. The manuscript appropriately credits the existing quantum/Hodge-atom framework and the known small cubic calculation. I have not performed an independent novelty or forward-citation survey, and this report does not certify priority.

## Correctness and dependency assessment

I found no fatal mathematical defect in the assembled argument. The following are the points on which that assessment rests, and the boundaries of the assessment.

1. **Canonical lattice and persistence.** The rank-two pairing argument really uses the nondegenerate pairing on an entire primary connection factor, rather than assuming a Jordan line inherits a nondegenerate pairing. The elementary modification retains the first-order lower-left coefficient, which is essential for the residue. In the double-primary persistence proof, cyclicity is established over the formal local ring before nilpotence is claimed; the commuting centered base pole is then a scalar multiple of the centered Euler operator. Thus the proof does not assume persistence in order to prove it. In rank three, the displayed trace equations follow from the cyclic commutant and Cayley–Hamilton; their zero initial solution forces nilpotence without asserting a higher-rank logarithmic modification. This is a valid separation of two different arguments.

2. **Blowup transport.** Iritani's Theorem 5.18 actually supplies an isomorphism over the specified completed polynomial-in-z module, commutation with the quantum connection, and an invertible joint parameter Jacobian. Section 5.8.2 actually introduces independent external center coordinates and writes the shifted coordinates in (5.47). These are stronger inputs than an abstract meromorphic differential-module equivalence. They support the manuscript's insistence on regularity of both comparison maps and preservation of the canonical modified lattice, including resonant factors. The faithful-center argument addresses a real potential failure: raw Novikov pushforward can identify distinct numerical classes. Its target-only divisor variables and finite exponential-character argument avoid that failure. The polynomial-in-nondivisor-coordinates conclusion depends on the reduced graded source, so the restrictions printed in the lemma are load-bearing, not cosmetic. I found no illicit translation of an unrestricted formal power-series ring in the proof as written.

3. **Low-dimensional vanishing.** The nef-canonical degree argument selects the whole even primary factor, whose rank is at least three. Ruled surfaces are treated through a full even potential on the product with a curve and point-center surface factorization. This avoids assuming a big tensor-product formula at mixed bulk. For the rank-three odd-dimension invariant, the exceptional case b2=1 is correctly disposed of by the square-zero class of a holomorphic one-form and Poincaré duality. The center vanishings do not circularly assume fourfold invariance.

4. **The cubic and specialized product.** The universal rank-two calculation retains the coefficient contributed by the first off-diagonal gauge; the reported cubic residue has eigenvalues -1/6 and -5/6 and discriminant 4/9. At the small product point, the independent fiber Novikov parameter separates opposite shifts. Flatness and cyclic persistence, not an unproved big product formula, then continue the two factors in the mixed even directions. The revised nine-family domain of `lem:special-projective-line` suffices for every main-theorem use. The subsequent all-threefold criterion and general bundle/additive appendices retain their separate Iritani–Koto dependency. The source does not silently use that general theorem to establish the cubic or Hodge theorem.

5. **All seventeen families and the external quantum input.** The nine displayed matrices are cyclic; the rank-two residue branch and the rank-three odd-dimension branch are logically separate. The eight other families enter through classical every-smooth-member rationality, not deformation invariance of rationality. The special genus-three and special Gushel–Mukai bridges describe actual smooth deformations of presentations, providing the required scope extension for deformation-invariant GW data. In particular, the numerical argument does not claim Hodge structures remain constant in such a deformation.

   The source-to-matrix bridge is not merely a match of characteristic polynomials. Böhning–Graf von Bothmer–Su'a Section 10 explicitly identifies the full index-one and index-two matrices in associated reconstruction data, based on CCGK periods, and attributes earlier computations to Przyjalkowski. Its preceding Section 9 explains the reconstruction method: constrain the quantum-product matrix by degree/classical data and pairing symmetry, and determine coefficients from the scalar differential equation of the period, using the small J-function input. Przyjalkowski's Theorem 6.1.1 explicitly prints the genus-six and genus-eight matrices, with scalar shifts 6 and 4; adding those shifts gives the manuscript's displayed diagonal entries. Thus I found an external mathematical source for actual counting matrices. The first-eight-period comparison in the artifact is an independent consistency/normalization check; it is not, by itself, a proof that arbitrary matrices are GW operators. I did not independently reconstruct all nine matrices from correlators or replay the associated programs.

6. **Hodge conservation.** The fixed locus is a parameter restriction, not a restriction of the fiber to invariants. The common rational reductive quotient is necessary for comparing representation classes across a weak factorization. Iritani's Lemma 7 and Proposition 8 support respectively quantum-product equivariance and equivariance of the comparison and coordinate maps; Lemma 10 supplies fixed initial shifts. Algebraic divisor coordinates survive on this base, so the faithful-coordinate proof still applies. The odd part of an even-rank-one Frobenius factor is zero: supercommutativity kills the only possible product and nondegeneracy then kills the odd space. Representation multiplicities are constant for the continued equivariant projector. Cancellation in the free representation group and descent through the determinant polynomial on rational equivariant Hom are valid. Using equal weight three recovers the Hodge bidegrees from the circle weights. No integral lattice or polarization has been recovered or covertly assumed.

7. **Torelli and arithmetic domains.** Voisin explicitly formulates generic Torelli for rational primitive Hodge structures with the first hypersurface very general and the second arbitrary smooth of the same degree. Her Theorem 0.2 excludes neither (d,n)=(3,4) nor (4,4), and Remarks 0.1 and 0.3 justify the unpolarized/rational formulation. Thus the manuscript's cubic/quartic statement uses the cited theorem at its stated strength. Orr's corrected Theorem 5.1 explicitly permits finitely generated characteristic-zero fields, a target defined over a finite extension, and an isogeny over the algebraic closure of bounded degree. The proof only counts geometric kernels, then principal polarizations modulo automorphisms, so it does not accidentally assert bounded fields of definition for the isogeny or finiteness of twists. The final finite-kernel and cubic Torelli argument is sound with the separately cited arithmetic intermediate-Jacobian and polarization-finiteness inputs.

The additive appendices use the blowup relation and projective-bundle formula consistently. The resulting map is an additive group invariant, not a multiplicative motivic measure or a stable-birational invariant. The fivefold-center limitation is explicitly demonstrated. I did not see an appendix assertion feeding back into a main theorem while concealing its stronger external dependency.

## Exposition, major comments, and minor comments

There are **no outstanding major correctness objections from this read**. The principal editorial issues are the balance between the now-expanded scope and the cubic-specific title, and making the exact imported quantum-data assertion easy to locate. Neither requires a new proof architecture.

The organization is substantially better for the assembled scope: three early theorems identify the different outputs; Parts I and II prevent the numerical result from appearing conditional on Hodge descent; the optional general operations are genuinely optional. The proof of rational descent is particularly clear. The full fixed-base comparison repeats some technical reasoning from the numerical comparison, but the repetition is justified by a change of domain at precisely the point a referee should inspect.

Minor suggestions:

- Cite the reconstruction explanation in Böhning–Graf von Bothmer–Su'a Section 9 alongside Section 10 and the data, and say explicitly that the finite period agreement checks normalization rather than establishing the GW identification. The existing last sentences of the data appendix already point in the right direction.
- The specialized product lemma's nine families have been listed in the introduction, but the symbol F is fully tabulated only in the following section. An explicit backward reference to the introduction's list would remove a small first-pass navigation cost; its hypotheses already suffice for the cubic calculation.
- The title is defensible because the cubic is the model and headline, but it understates the complete Fano classification and Hodge-conservation theorem. This is a positioning choice, not a correctness issue.
- The final rank-three obstruction example is mathematically useful, but an adjacent reader may benefit from a one-sentence reminder that rank-three cyclic persistence was proved without constructing this higher-rank modified lattice.

The originally read abstract omitted the rank-three odd-dimension branch while advertising all seventeen families. The amendment I reread explicitly restores that branch; I do not leave the original wording as an outstanding objection.

## Nonblind before/after preference

**Specialist preference: the assembled manuscript, with high confidence about organization and moderate-to-high confidence about the newly added mathematics.** The old 20-page paper is a coherent, narrower numerical cubic/index-two paper. It makes the general projective-bundle theorem part of the main route, and places the general additive theory beside the main obstruction. The assembled 31-page paper does more mathematics: all seventeen families, a rank-three numerical branch, nine-family rational Hodge conservation, and separate Torelli/arithmetic applications. The eleven-page increase cannot fairly be scored as editing bloat or as a same-content readability experiment.

For an expert interested only in the cubic obstruction, the shorter PDF remains quicker to finish. Nevertheless, the assembled main route is better isolated: the cubic can be read through its proof without taking on the Hodge theorem or general-bundle appendix. The first-page comparison also favors the assembled hierarchy: the classification is visible immediately instead of emerging only as a later specialization. I would retain the larger manuscript for its present intended contribution. This preference does not certify every final page break; only the first pages were visually compared here.

## Editorial recommendation and confidence

**Recommendation: accept after minor revision, subject to the journal's ordinary independent verification of the imported GW/comparison results and the submitted computational supplement.** I would not request a redesign or a retreat from the stated every-member numerical or Hodge scopes on the evidence examined.
