## (a) Are the repairs correct and complete?

1. **OK — round-one item 1, the deformation family, is fully repaired.** The replacement is
   correct in every step I checked.
   - \(k=\lfloor a/2\rfloor\ge1\) and \(a-k=\lceil a/2\rceil\ge1\) for \(a\ge2\), so both
     \(\OO(k)\) and \(\OO(a-k)\) have at least two sections; a general \(s_1\) has \(k\) distinct
     zeros and a general \(s_2\) avoids them, so a general pair has no common zero. Correct.
   - \((s_1,s_2)\) nowhere vanishing gives \(\OO\hookrightarrow\OO(k)\oplus\OO(a-k)\) with
     locally free quotient of rank one and degree \(a\), hence \(\OO(a)\). Correct.
   - \(\PP(\OO(k)\oplus\OO(a-k))\cong\PP(\OO\oplus\OO(a-2k))=F_{a-2k}\) by twisting with
     \(\OO(-k)\), and \(a-2k=a\bmod2\). Correct.
   - The transport computation is correct and now correctly justified: \(\psi(f)=f\) because the
     ruling is global over the base; \(\psi(s)=\alpha s_0+\beta f\), pairing with \(f\) gives
     \(\alpha=1\), and \(s_0^2=-(a-2k)\) with \(\psi(s)^2=-a\) gives \(\beta=-k\). Hence
     \(\psi(mf+ns)=(m-nk)f+ns_0\), \(\psi(s+kf)=s_0\). Uniqueness needs only the intersection
     form, which is what the new text uses — the round-one over-claim about the Chern number is
     gone.
   - The semipositivity clause is right: with \(\dim_\R M=4\) the McDuff–Salamon condition
     "no \(A\) with \(\omega(A)>0\) and \(3-n\le c_1(A)<0\)" reads \(1\le c_1(A)<0\), which is
     vacuous, so every symplectic four-manifold is semipositive. And a smooth projective family
     with a relatively ample class does carry a smooth family of Kähler forms.
   - Parallel transport is unambiguous because \(\A^1\) is simply connected.
   Two things are used silently and should each get a clause (MINOR, listed in (b) below):
   that the family is the pullback of the universal extension along \(t\mapsto t\varepsilon\),
   and that scaling an extension class by \(t\in\C^\times\) gives an isomorphic middle term, so
   that the displayed claim holds for *every* \(t\ne0\) rather than just \(t=1\). Neither
   affects correctness: connectedness of \(\A^1\) plus the single fibre at \(t=1\) already
   delivers the conclusion.

3. **OK — round-one item 3, the intrinsic vanishing of the minimal model, is restored and its
   inputs check out.** The identity specialization really is strictly Novikov-admissible: with
   \(A=\Lambda_T\) the completed numerical Novikov ring and \(v=H\cdot(-)\) for an ample \(H\),
   \(A\) is a complete separated valued \(\C\)-domain, \(\operatorname{gr}A=\C[\mathrm{Eff}(T)]\)
   is the monoid algebra of a submonoid of a lattice and hence a domain, every monomial has
   nonzero image, and \(\ell=H\cdot(-)\) is positive and proper on the effective monoid. It is
   also graded-monomial, since \(\operatorname{gr}(\mathrm{id}(Q^d))=Q^d\) is a basis monomial of
   \(\C[\mathrm{Eff}(T)]\). Both assertions are stated without proof; one clause naming \(H\)
   and the monoid algebra would discharge them, and the same fact is already used silently in
   the unchanged `prop:direct-specialized-lowdim`(iii).
   The case split is exhaustive: a minimal smooth projective surface has nef canonical class, or
   is \(\PP^2\), or is geometrically ruled over a curve — which is the standard classification
   and is exactly what the new text states; geometrically ruled splits into \(g(C)\ge1\)
   (case (iii), with 5.7R) and \(C=\PP^1\) (the Hirzebruch surfaces, new proposition). The
   point and positive-genus-curve cases are covered by the unchanged first paragraph of the
   proof, so "those are zero" for the point terms of the blowup formula is now supported.

8. **MINOR — `lem:hirzebruch-euler-spectrum`, Truncation, first sentence.** "A three-point
   invariant of \(F_a\) with two divisor insertions vanishes unless
   \(1\le c_1(T)\cdot\beta\le2\)" is stated without the qualifier \(\beta\ne0\); read literally
   it discards the classical products, which the presentation does use (for \(a\) odd the
   classical terms \(-\mathrm{pt}\) and \(+\mathrm{pt}\) in \(S^{\flat2}+S^\flat F\) cancel, so

   on the fundamental-class axiom, which is worth naming: for \(a=2k\) there are infinitely many
   effective \(\beta=ns\) with \(c_1\cdot\beta=0\), and they are excluded either by that axiom
   or, redundantly, by \(\psi(\beta)\) being non-effective.

11. **MINOR — `lem:center-specialization-nondegenerate`, \(a=1\) branch, a vestigial reason.**

## Summary

The three repairs that carried proof obligations — the single one-parameter deformation family,
the truncation of the \(F_a\) quantum product, and the restored intrinsic vanishing of the
minimal model — are correct and complete, and the hypothesis-scope claim
"5.7T only for surface centres that are neither minimal nor geometrically ruled" is exactly
what the proofs support. The one repair that is not complete is the terminology one: two
sentences still call the Hirzebruch family "minimal", and the second of them
(`rem:tagging-scope`, last sentence) is a false statement about \(F_1\). Everything else is a
clause-length clarification. No item found in this round blocks the result.
