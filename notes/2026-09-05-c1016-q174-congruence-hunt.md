# C1016 — the congruence hunt on the q174 deviation, and what the plateau near 3,500 actually is

**Lane**: `complete-ports` · **Task**: C1016 · 2026-09-05.

The predecessor (`2026-09-05-c1016-full-q174-correlation-and-the-order-six-pair.md`)
extended phase two to the full `q174` correlation, found that the order-six
character pair had held between 99.2% and 99.8% of the deviation, and left a new
plateau near a score of 3,500 — a hundred times the floor of 32 that the two
invariants it had recorded permit. Its open question was whether some congruence
beyond those two explains the gap. This is that hunt.

The answer is no, and it is a decisive no rather than a search that came up
empty. But the hunt found something else on the way: the exact `q29` shell pins
**fifteen** aggregates of the `q174` deviation, not the one the predecessor
recorded, so the deviation has free rank 73 rather than 87. That is a real
structural correction to the invariant set, and it changes what the search's
moves can do without changing the floor.

## What a congruence would have to be, and why the question is decidable

A congruence on the deviation is exactly the statement that every achievable
deviation lies in one coset of a proper finite-index subgroup of the integer
vectors. Take the differences of achievable deviations, write `L` for the
subgroup they generate inside the invariant lattice `H` below, and a congruence
modulo `m` would force every difference into `m H`. So the whole class of
congruences is decided by one quotient group, `H / L`: it has an element of order
`p` exactly when the differences fail to span `H / p H`, which is a rank over the
field of `p` elements and is exact.

The differences are generated by single moves. A phase-two transfer moves one
unit between two classes of one column, every state of a shell's fibre is
reachable from every other by such transfers, and the deviation change of one
transfer is small — no coordinate exceeds six — so the transfer deltas are
exactly the right generators, and no sampling or search enters the decision.

## The invariant set, corrected

Write `y` for a block's signed `q174` values, `y_x = 2 c_x - 3` on counts in
`0..=3`, so every value is odd; write `u_x = c_x - 2` in `-2..=1`, so `y = 1 + 2u`;
write `A(g)` for the combined correlation over the four blocks and
`D(g) = A(g) - target(g)` for its deviation.

**Every deviation is a multiple of four** (banked). For a nonzero shift each of
the `4 * 174` products expands as `1 + 2(u_x + u_{x+g}) + 4 u_x u_{x+g}`, so
`A(g) = 696 + 4 sum_b U_b + 4 sum_b sum_x u_x u_{x+g}`, and both targets are
multiples of four.

**The energy deviation is a multiple of eight** (new). At the zero shift the
products are squares and `y^2` is `1` or `9`, so `A(0) = 696 + 8 N` where `N`
counts the cells of absolute value three, and the target is `2080 = 696 + 8 * 173`.

**The self-inverse-shift deviation is a multiple of eight** (new). At shift 87
each unordered pair `{x, x+87}` is counted twice, so `A(87) = 2 P` over `4 * 87`
positions; expanding `P = 348 + 2 sum_b U_b + 4 W` and using
`sum_b U_b = -348 +- 1` — the block sums are even and square-sum to four on a
shell, so exactly one block has sum `+-2` and the other three have sum zero —
gives `D(87) = -684 +- 4 + 8 W`.

**The shell pins fifteen aggregates of the deviation, not one** (new, and the
substantive correction). The `q29` correlation is the `q174` correlation
aggregated over the order-six shift subgroup generated by 29,

```text
A29(v) = sum_{g in Z/174, g = v mod 29} A174(g),
```

and the targets aggregate the same way: `2080 + 5 * (-12) = 2020` at the zero
residue and `6 * (-12) = -72` at each other. A phase-two transfer fixes every
column total and therefore the whole `q29` view, so on an exact shell every one
of these aggregates is exactly zero. The deviation is symmetric, so `v` and `-v`
give the same relation and there are fifteen distinct ones. Their total is the
mean-zero invariant the predecessor recorded, so fourteen are new.

Each relation is confined to one `q29` residue: the residue-zero group is the
four canonical classes `{0, 29, 58, 87}` weighted `1, 2, 2, 1`, and each of the
other fourteen groups is six canonical classes weighted two. So the invariant
lattice is

```text
H = { D in Z^88 : 4 | D(g) for all g,  8 | D(0),
                  sum_{g = v mod 29} D(g) = 0 for every v },
```

free of rank 73 rather than 87. The multiple-of-eight invariant at shift 87
follows from the energy one and the residue-zero relation, so it is a consequence
rather than an independent assumption.

All 24 banked phase-two states — the twelve registered-objective ones and the
twelve `q174` ones — satisfy every invariant exactly, with every one of the
fifteen aggregates equal to zero and the largest aggregate over the whole corpus
equal to zero.

## The verdict

Twenty-four uniform lifts, two above each of the twelve banked shells, give
63,984 transfer deltas whose largest lattice coordinate is six. Their rank over
the field of `p` elements is **73 of 73 at every one of the 78,498 primes below
one million**, with no prime falling short.

So the quotient `H / L` has no element of order `p` for any prime under a
million: **no congruence constrains the `q174` deviation at any modulus whose
prime factors are below one million**. The exact searched domain is those 78,498
primes; the stop condition is the bound, not an exhausted search. A congruence
whose modulus is a product of primes above a million is not excluded by this
computation, and nothing else here suggests one.

## The floor is still 32, and the new relations reshape the cheap moves

The objective is the squared deviation summed over the canonical shift classes,
every deviation is a multiple of four, so the score is a multiple of sixteen. Its
arithmetic floor over `H` is 32, unchanged by the fourteen new relations: put
`+4` and `-4` on two canonical classes lying in the *same* `q29` residue group
and every relation still cancels. Exactly 422 invariant vectors reach it — 30 in
each of the fourteen nonzero groups and 2 in the residue-zero group, whose two
self-inverse classes cannot carry an odd multiple of four. The next objective up,
48, is empty: three classes at `+-4` cannot cancel inside any one group.

What the relations do change is the shape of a cheap move. Deviation can be
traded only *within* a `q29` residue, never across one. That is a fact about the
landscape the search moves in, and it is new.

## The same condition in four letters

The substitution that produced the multiple-of-four invariant does more if it is
carried all the way through. Put `u_x = c_x - 2`, so `y = 1 + 2u` and every cell
holds one of the four letters `-2, -1, 0, 1`. The constant and linear parts of
the correlation then cancel against the target exactly, leaving

```text
D(g) = 4 (B(g) - target(g)),      B(g) = sum_b sum_x u_x u_{x+g},
```

with `target(g) = 171 - (sum_b S_b) / 2` at every nonzero shift and 523 more
than that at the zero shift, where `S_b` are the block sums of `y`. So the whole
phase-two condition is that **four sequences over the four-letter alphabet
`-2..=1` on `Z/174` have a constant aggregate autocorrelation off the zero
shift**, the two constants differing by 523 — equivalently that their combined
Fourier spectrum is flat at 523 off the trivial character. On a shell whose block
sums are `(2, 0, 0, 0)` the constants are 170 and 693.

This is an exact restatement, gated on sampled states, not a new condition. It is
worth recording because it removes the odd values, the target constants 2080 and
`-12`, and the factor of four from the statement, and puts the object in the same
form — flat spectrum at 523 — that the rest of the ladder and the plain-523 route
already speak.

## What the plateau is

With the congruence door closed, the remaining explanation is the fibre's own
richness, and it is measurable. Above one exact `q29` shell the neutral fibre
holds `2^1718.8` to `2^1726.6` states — each column independently distributes its
raised carrier slots among eighteen. Over 512 uniform lifts per shell the
deviation's per-coordinate spread has median `sigma` about 17.9 and largest about
20.1, so the 73 free equations each landing exactly on zero costs `2^399.5` to
`2^401.1`, leaving

**`2^1318` to `2^1327` expected exact `q174` solutions per shell fibre**, or one
solution in about `2^400` states.

That is the same shape of answer the plain-523 spin shard gave: one solution in
about `2^381` states there, and the same conclusion, that the object is
solution-rich and the search is the bottleneck.

The plateau's own depth is then the useful number. Fitting the objective of a
uniform lift to a gamma law by its first two moments — its mean runs 456,000 to
473,000 across the shells — places the plateau at 3,680 in the `2^-119` to
`2^-142` tail of that
distribution, so about `2^1580` to `2^1607` states sit at or below it. The
descent an exact hit needs is about 400 bits; the search has taken roughly 130 of
them and stalled, with `2^1322` solutions still below it. **The plateau is a
search depth, not an arithmetic wall.**

## Two things the residue-group split shows

Splitting each banked state's residual across the fifteen `q29` residue groups is
free once the groups are named, and it says two things.

The twelve registered-objective states put 400 of their 2,224,816 total residual
— 0.018% — in the residue-zero group, and up to 12.3% in single other groups.
That is exactly right and is a check rather than a discovery: the registered
conditions pin the correlation at the shifts that are multiples of 29, which are
precisely the residue-zero group, and leave the other fourteen unscored.

The twelve `q174` states spread 4.4% to 9.1% across the groups against an even
6.7%, with the residue-zero group at 5.0%. So at the new plateau the residual is
close to evenly distributed over the fifteen groups — the finer-grained version
of the predecessor's finding that it is spread across the sign, order-three and
order-six blocks rather than concentrated. No group is a lever.

## Provenance

The invariants, their derivations, the rank computation and the floor
enumeration are exact. The expected-solution count and the plateau's tail depth
are a heuristic model — a Gaussian density per equation and a two-moment gamma
fit — of the same kind used on the plain-523 shard, and they carry no negative
authority. The congruence verdict is exact over the primes it names and says
nothing about moduli built only from larger primes.

## Mystery ledger

- **The plateau near 3,500 is not arithmetic, and that is now settled.** No
  congruence exists below a million, the floor stays 32, and the fibre holds
  about `2^1322` exact solutions. The predecessor's open question is closed:
  the remaining explanation is search depth, and the plateau sits about 130 bits
  into a 400-bit descent. Settled by this pass.
- **What stalls the descent is unexplained.** The full-neighbourhood tabu step
  is already exact over every single transfer, and the plateau is insensitive to
  a hundredfold budget range, to staged starts and to every tabu policy tried.
  With the landscape now known to factor into fifteen residue groups that no
  single transfer can trade across, the first thing to try is a move that does:
  a paired transfer in two columns of the same residue group, or a search scoped
  to one group at a time. Neither is built.
- **The residual is evenly spread over the fifteen groups, and it is not obvious
  why.** 4.4% to 9.1% against an even 6.7%, with no group standing out. Whether
  that is the search finding the easiest available balance or a symmetry among
  the groups is unexamined, and it is the same open question the predecessor
  raised one level up about the three character blocks.
- **The `q174` fibre and the plain-523 spin shard have nearly the same solution
  density.** One in `2^400` here against one in `2^381` there, from two entirely
  different constructions of the same order-2092 object. Whether that is a
  coincidence of parameter counting or says the two routes are the same search
  problem in different coordinates is unexamined, and it would be cheap to check
  by counting each route's states and equations against the other.
- **A `q174` hit is still not a carrier solution.** Unchanged from the
  predecessor: `Z/522 -> Z/174` has fibre three, so even an exact hit leaves one
  factor of three to the carrier-522 replay, and nothing scores that yet.
