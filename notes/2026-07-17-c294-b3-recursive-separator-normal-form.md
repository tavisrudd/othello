## Normal-form relation

For a completed high-cycle residual `X` in the unchanged absolute traversal, enumerate every
genuine two-port separator side with at least eight core vertices and at most 16 expanded vertices.
For each occurrence `P`, compute its complete ordered two-port transition interface and serialize
`X-P` with an oriented two-vertex gadget carrying that interface. Minimize the serialization over
the two port orientations.

Two absolute residual classes are related when one of their complete context/interface
serializations is equal. Equality of such serializations gives a port- and interface-preserving
context isomorphism. The two-port replacement theorem replaces the pieces, and graph-isomorphism
invariance then proves equal Grundy values. Taking the transitive closure is therefore sound; the
least serialization in a closure component is a decomposition-independent normal form.

The implementation interns every single-piece serialization, so nested or overlapping separator
choices do not depend on which decomposition was chosen first. It additionally constructs a
canonical recursive chain of compatible parallel pieces: at each step it adds the candidate whose
complete oriented-context key is least and interns every prefix of length at least two. No choice
uses a previously observed transition class or quotient-cache hit. The fixed-prefix certificate
contains 345,086 representations and 31,426 repeated cross-absolute representation events; their
union closure removes 946 classes and reports zero value conflicts.

## Trusted boundary and scope

The primary trusted boundary is the predecessor graph generator; exact mex/xor and component
recursion; B1 attachment labels; two-core and separator extraction; the complete two-port
transition DAG; individualization/refinement canonicalization; equality of complete vector keys;
and the union/find closure. The independent replay shares the emitted graph and mathematical
interface definition but not the primary context canonicalizer or piece-isomorphism search.

This certificate covers only the fixed q=5 type-0 prefix, pieces with 8--16 expanded vertices, and
the canonical parallel prefixes actually selected there. It does not prove that these generators
are a complete contextual-equivalence basis, produce a live online quotient, classify the
116-vertex follower, or establish a q-uniform finite interface. The three new transition mergers
are too few to warrant another large value run. The next bounded step should either produce a
substantially larger generator closure, or compile this offline partition into a two-phase live
dictionary and demonstrate an actual traversal reduction at the same 100,000-state cap.
