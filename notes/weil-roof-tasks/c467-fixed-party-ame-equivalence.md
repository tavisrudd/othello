**Context:** bounded successor to C456. The states are LC/LU-equivalent after party permutation,
but the identity-labeled projective/monomial map fails and all indexed degree-six marginal moments
are blind.

## Task

First decide fixed-party local-Clifford equivalence by exact transition forcing on the two frozen
stabilizer Lagrangians. In parallel, compute the complete indexed bidegree `(4,4)` contraction
invariants (total degree eight) of the two equal-phase states, quotienting only by identities proved
inside the checker. If LC is negative and degree eight remains blind, state the exact residual LU

automorphism with party permutation `(2 4 3 5)`.

Do not launch an unconstrained continuous-unitary search or claim LU=LC without a proved rigidity
step.

## Acceptance

Deliver exact state/stabilizer conventions, a complete fixed-party LC map or obstruction

stated reason none is available.
