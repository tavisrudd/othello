# C457 / T10 — quaternion-order reduction

**Dependencies:** M2/C458 passed; C444 must close first. This is the structural upgrade, not a
restatement of M4's fibre test.

## Inputs

- C442 and C458 two-frame/golden-sheet bundles
- completed C444 bundle
- C382 icosian negative, for its exact category boundary
- C440 frozen conventions

## Task

Certify in the frozen coordinates that reduction of the icosian and binary-octahedral maximal
orders at the two primes above q yields the two sheet embeddings. State the integral orders,
localizations, prime ideals, residue maps, projective/spin representations, and comparison maps.

Stop on a residue-characteristic denominator, order mismatch, or need to change conventions.
Acceptance requires exact order arithmetic and embedding equality, not merely matching orbit sizes.
