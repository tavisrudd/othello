**Context:** C406 proves a three-dimensional outer-odd cubic space; this card asks whether its
structure has a Klein/Adler explanation.

- C406 matching-module primary and replay bundles

## Task

Decompose the H3 quotient module `W` in characteristic zero and 11; compute the relationship, if
any, between C406's relative-cubic space and the five-dimensional component's Adler cubic; explain
by character computation why the relative-invariant dimension is three in both B3 and H3.
