# C907 — the neutral-slice Gamma kernel and the remaining common-master gate

Date: 2026-08-14

**Status:** theorem-grade local analytic reduction, followed by an exact
application boundary.  The only nonpolynomial direction left by the C907
peak sieve is Calabi--Yau after restriction to its primitive one-parameter
subgroup.  For a genuine common Mellin--Barnes kernel, Aleshkin--Liu's
Gamma-central-charge wall theorem therefore identifies the complete neutral
residue with the grade-restriction/Fourier--Mukai correction.  Its restriction
to the common open is the identity, so its target has rank zero.  This proves
the desired rank-zero-target statement for every **common-kernel** peak.

It does not yet prove the stabilization theorem.  The two incident receivers
of a general AKMW peak have not been shown to be asymptotic expansions of one
common equivariant Fourier integral.  Gu--Yu--Yu formulate precisely the
needed Fourier-cone statement for an arbitrary intermediate GIT quotient as
Conjecture 1.11 and prove it only for highest/lowest quotients.  The remaining
global gate is consequently a common-master realization theorem, not a new
one-variable Stokes calculation.

## 1. The parameterized Calabi--Yau contour lemma

Let `A` be a finite-dimensional local complex Artin algebra.  Let

\[
 h_1,\ldots,h_N\in\mathbf Z,\qquad \sum_i h_i=0,                 \tag{1}
\]

and consider an Aleshkin--Liu wall hemisphere integral, with its usual
Gamma product, after restriction to the one-parameter subgroup generated by
`h`.  Allow the integrand to be multiplied by

\[
 P(\sigma,z)\in A[\sigma,z,z^{-1}]                               \tag{2}
\]

and allow every Gamma argument to have an `A`-nilpotent shift.  On a
grade-restricted strip, the two chamber expansions of this integral are
analytic continuations of one another.  Moreover, their complete contour
residue is the grade-restriction/Fourier--Mukai correction.  In particular,
on the numerical K-group modulo objects supported on the unstable locus,
the transition is the identity.

### Proof

Aleshkin--Liu Theorem 5.21 proves the assertion for the unmodified
hemisphere integrand.  In their one-wall proof, the Calabi--Yau input is the
identity

\[
 \sum_i h_i=0,                                                    \tag{3}
\]

which gives the balanced decay needed to move the contour and group all
essential residues.  The proof is unchanged when the transverse equivariant
parameters are held fixed.

Multiplication by `sigma^a` is differentiation `a` times in the Fourier
parameter.  Analytic continuation, the convergent complete residue sum, and
differentiation commute; the exponential estimates in Aleshkin--Liu's proof
absorb the resulting polynomial factor.  This proves the statement for a
scalar polynomial amplitude and hence, entrywise, for (2).  An `A`-nilpotent
Chern-root shift gives a terminating Taylor expansion of each Gamma factor.
The chamber identity is analytic in the equivariant shifts, so its finitely
many parameter derivatives give those Taylor coefficients; logarithmic
growth from Gamma derivatives is again dominated by the same exponential
estimate.  Finally `A` has finite complex dimension, and all contour
estimates are componentwise; there is no parameter-convergence issue.

The grade-restriction identification is K-theoretic.  The two window objects
restrict to the same object on the common stable open, so the cone of their
Fourier--Mukai comparison is unstable-supported.  Ordinary rank factors
through restriction to that open.  Therefore every complete residue block
has zero rank after subtracting the common-open identity.  This last
conclusion is independent of individual residue coordinates and is exactly
the blockwise granularity forced by the `dP7` regression.  \(\square\)

The same argument applies coefficientwise to a complete formal ring: reduce
modulo an ample-energy/parameter filtration, apply the lemma over its Artin
quotient, and pass to the inverse limit.  This does **not** evaluate a formal
Novikov variable at a nonzero number.

## 2. Why the neutral direction is automatically Calabi--Yau

At a two-wall peak, let `D_i` be the integral weights of a rank-two Fourier
presentation and let `delta` generate the primitive direction left after
fixing the positive `c_1` degree.  Put

\[
 h_i=\langle D_i,\delta\rangle .                                 \tag{4}
\]

The anticanonical character is `sum_i D_i`.  Hence

\[
 \sum_i h_i
 =\left\langle\sum_iD_i,\delta\right\rangle
 =c_1(Y)\cdot\delta=0.                                           \tag{5}
\]

Thus every affine tower `beta_0+n delta` which defeats the positive-carrier
polynomiality theorem is balanced in exactly the sense of (1).  Transverse
positive-degree variables can be retained formally: for a fixed coefficient
the master-space equivariant input is polynomial in the Fourier parameter,
and Chern roots are nilpotent.  The lemma therefore controls arbitrary
polynomial carrier insertions **provided both receiver expansions come from
the same kernel**.

This explains the first exact toric suspect.  Its charge

\[
 (3,1,1,-1,-1,-1,-2)
\]

sums to zero.  Its nonzero `_3F_2` Barnes coefficients are compatible with
the lemma: individual analytic branches are nonzero, while the complete
window residue still has rank-zero output.  Scalar continuation and output
rank are different shadows.

## 3. Common-kernel peak theorem

Let `Y` be a smooth projective chamber incident to two unit discrepant walls.
Assume:

1. the two fixed-sector receiver gauges are the two iterated asymptotic
   expansions of one rank-two equivariant Gamma/Fourier kernel;
2. after fixing the anticanonical degree, the kernel amplitude is polynomial
   in the neutral Fourier parameter over every Artin truncation;
3. the categorical grade-restriction transform of the neutral slice is the
   identity on the common stable open.

Then the transition `T_Y` between the receivers fixes the Gamma rank row:

\[
 r_YT_Y=r_Y.                                                      \tag{6}
\]

Indeed, strictly `c_1`-positive directions are coefficientwise finite and
ordinary crepant extremal directions are already covered by the flop
theorem.  Every remaining unbounded coefficient lies on `delta` in (5).
Apply the parameterized contour lemma to each fixed positive-degree and
Artin coefficient.  The complete neutral correction is supported on the
unstable locus, so the off-boundary point/rank row annihilates it.  Passing
to the inverse limit gives (6).  Consequently the primitive-sixth rank
Boolean agrees in the two receivers.

Together with the already proved one-wall, ordinary-flop, AKMW unit-wall,
and endpoint-product theorems, this common-kernel theorem would close the
`X x P^2` telescope.

## 4. Exact reason this is not yet the global theorem

Gu--Yu--Yu define the continuous fixed-component transform by the
stationary-phase expansion of

\[
 \int e^{\lambda\log S_F/z}\,G_F\,f|_F\,d\lambda,                 \tag{7}
\]

where `G_F` is the product of Gamma factors of the equivariant normal roots.
They construct the corresponding quantum-D-module map for every fixed
component.  For the discrete transform to a smooth GIT quotient, however,
their Conjecture 1.11 says that the transform is defined on the whole
equivariant Givental cone and lands on the quotient cone.  Their Theorem
1.12/4.15 proves this only for highest/lowest quotients.  The simple-wall
decomposition works because its three-component master puts the two chambers
in precisely that position.

A global birational cobordism has many chambers.  Treating two incident
wall receivers as expansions of one global master kernel requires the same
cone-preservation statement for an intermediate quotient.  It is not a
formal consequence of the two separate three-component masters.  Without
it, the carrier part of `f` in (7) is only known through two sectorial
realizations, and an exponentially small ambient shear can be hidden in
their transition.  The incomplete-Gamma countermodel shows that balanced
formal weights, pairing, integrality, and a nilpotent projective tag do not
remove that possibility.

Aleshkin--Fang--Wang do not fill this seam.  Their Theorems 4.5 and 5.5 give
the Gamma/SYZ-cycle identification for one fixed toric Fano quotient.  They
do not compare two secondary-fan chambers.  Conversely, Aleshkin--Liu compare
chambers in the Calabi--Yau case, but do not construct the missing global
intermediate-quotient Fourier transform for a non-toric carrier.  The two
papers supply the two local halves of the argument, not the common master.

Iritani's global formulation confirms that this is the right seam rather
than an artifact of the simple-wall proof.  In *Fourier analysis of
equivariant quantum cohomology*, Conjecture 39 proposes one equivariant QDM
over the global secondary-fan moduli, and Conjecture 43 says that its
discrete Fourier transform lands on the Givental cone of an arbitrary smooth
quotient.  Remark 42 separates the two cases exactly as C907 does:
nonzero-degree wall charts join inside the graded formal space, whereas a
degree-zero/crepant wall requires analytification along the linking torus
curve.  The parameterized contour lemma supplies that analytification on a
neutral Gamma kernel; what remains is the point-row/packet fragment of the
reduction conjecture at the intermediate quotients.

Thus the exact remaining theorem is:

> **Common-master rank-row theorem.**  For the smooth projective toroidal
> cobordism used by C907, the point row and the total primitive-sixth ambient
> packet at every intermediate chamber are conservative images of one
> equivariant Fourier solution object; on every neutral one-parameter slice,
> its analytic transform is the grade-restricted Gamma kernel of Section 1.

This is strictly more concrete than a general Gamma/Orlov conjecture, but it
is still a new theorem.

## 5. Stronger elimination for the fixed-curve suspect

There is one unconditional strengthening of the low-moment sieve.  Let `Y`
be a fivefold, `beta` satisfy `c_1(Y).beta=0`, and suppose every stable map of
class `beta` is supported on a fixed closed subset `B` of dimension `b`.
Then

\[
 \operatorname{vdim}_\mathbf C\overline M_{0,k}(Y,\beta)=2+k,    \tag{8}
\]

whereas the image of the total evaluation map has dimension at most `kb`.
Therefore

\[
 (\operatorname{ev}_1,\ldots,\operatorname{ev}_k)_*
 [\overline M_{0,k}(Y,\beta)]^{\rm vir}=0
 \quad\text{if }2+k>kb.                                         \tag{9}
\]

For `b=1`, inequality (9) holds for **every** `k`.  Hence the neutral
`_3F_2` tower supported on its fixed nodal curve has no nonequivariant
primary multi-leg correspondence at all, not merely vanishing one- and
two-leg moments.  Any nonzero hypergeometric calibration is descendant or
equivariant and must disappear from the primary small connection unless it
is joined to an off-boundary carrier before taking the nonequivariant
pushforward.

For `b=2`, the one-leg state vanishes and the two-leg state is the first
possible primary correspondence.  For `b=3`, a one-leg fundamental state can
already survive.  This gives the exact support-dimension ladder for future
candidate enumeration.

## 6. EJ / TT / AA

- **EJ:** the final nonpolynomial direction is not merely `c_1`-neutral; it
  is literally a Calabi--Yau GLSM after restriction to its primitive
  one-parameter subgroup.  The local residue/window theorem is therefore
  already available.
- **TT:** do not apply the local contour theorem to two unrelated sectorial
  amplitudes.  The missing hypothesis is one common equivariant Fourier
  object, and Gu--Yu--Yu mark that hypothesis as conjectural for intermediate
  quotients.
- **AA:** prove only the common-master **point row plus primitive-sixth
  packet** statement, not full cone preservation.  Alternatively, construct
  a genuine smooth-projective peak whose two receivers violate the common
  neutral Gamma kernel; that would be the first geometric counterexample to
  the C907 telescope.

## 7. Mystery ledger

### Settled in this pass

- **Why the surviving unbounded direction looks Calabi--Yau.**  This is no
  analogy: equation (5) is the exact restriction of the anticanonical
  character.
- **Whether nonzero Barnes coefficients refute rank-zero output.**  They do
  not.  Aleshkin--Liu continuation marks the complete residue by the window
  transform; the first `_3F_2` model is the explicit regression.
- **Whether the fixed-curve suspect can hide a higher primary moment.**  It
  cannot: (9) kills every number of primary legs.

### Still open

- **Common master.**  No theorem identifies the two incident sectorial
  amplitudes as expansions of one equivariant Fourier object.  Exact evidence
  gap: Gu--Yu--Yu Conjecture 1.11 / Iritani Conjecture 43 at an intermediate
  quotient.  Owning successor: the common-master rank-row theorem.
- **Smallest sufficient fragment.**  It is not known whether the adjoint
  transform of the common-open point row together with the formal
  primitive-sixth packet can be proved without full cone preservation.
  This is the highest-EV next proof attempt.
- **Geometric falsifier.**  The incomplete-Gamma model is not a
  smooth-projective peak.  No geometric unit-circuit peak is known whose
  complete neutral residue has nonzero common-open rank.  A direct
  `(2,3; curve)` or `(2,4; point)` computation remains the bounded
  falsification route.
