# C907 isolated-stacky weighted-hypersurface rank theorem

**Lane:** `clebsch`

**Status:** theorem-grade for the stated hypersurface class.

## Theorem

Let

\[
 X_d\subset\mathbf P(w_0,\ldots,w_4)
\]

be a quasismooth, well-formed Fano hypersurface whose coarse threefold is
smooth.  Assume:

1. the weights are pairwise coprime (equivalently, the stacky locus of the
   ambient weighted projective space consists only of coordinate points); and
2. `H=O_X(1)` is the primitive Picard generator.

Then, after the usual index-one reconstruction normalization, the full
small-even QDM is the reduced rank-four hypergeometric module

\[
 \theta^4-C s
 \prod_{a\in A}(\theta+a),\qquad s=q/z^r,
 \tag{1}
\]

where `r=sum_iw_i-d>0`, `C=d^d/prod_iw_i^(w_i)`, the multiset `A` is invariant under
`a mapsto1-a`, and `|A|=4-r`.  Consequently its framed primitive-sixth
multiplicity satisfies

\[
 \nu_6(X)\le2.
\]

## Proof

At an ambient stacky coordinate point of weight `w_i`, every nonidentity
stabilizer element acts nontrivially on every other coordinate: pairwise
coprimality makes all four tangent characters units modulo `w_i`.  If `X`
passed through that point, quasismoothness would let one equation remove one
character, leaving a smooth three-dimensional cover on which no nonidentity
element is a pseudoreflection.  Chevalley--Shephard--Todd would then make the
coarse quotient singular.  Thus coarse smoothness forces `X` to avoid every
stacky point.  Its defining equation therefore has a nonzero pure monomial
`x_i^(d/w_i)` for every `w_i>1`, so `w_i` divides `d`.

The untwisted weighted hypersurface period is

\[
 \Phi(s)=\sum_{n\ge0}
 \frac{(dn)!}{\prod_i(w_i n)!}s^n.
\]

For an integer `N`, write
`F_N={1/N,...,(N-1)/N}`.  Since each `w_i` divides `d`, the fractional
denominator set `F_(w_i)` is contained in `F_d`; pairwise coprimality makes
these nonzero sets disjoint.  Cancelling them in the coefficient recurrence
leaves

\[
 A=F_d\setminus\bigsqcup_iF_{w_i},
 \qquad |A|=(d-1)-\sum_i(w_i-1)=4-r.
\]

The five denominator `(n+1)` factors and the one numerator `(n+1)` factor
leave exactly `(n+1)^4`.  Hence the recurrence is that of (1).  Each `F_N`
is invariant under `a mapsto1-a`, so the same holds for `A`.

Because `X` avoids the stacky locus, its stack has trivial inertia and `H` is
an ordinary line bundle.  Let `W=P(w_0,...,w_4)` be the smooth weighted
projective stack and let `pi:W->W_coarse`.  Pairwise coprimality and `w_i|d`
give `lcm(w_i)|d`; hence `O_W(d)=pi^*M`, where `M=O_(W_coarse)(d)` is a
globally generated positive Cartier multiple of `O_(W_coarse)(lcm(w_i))`.
It is convex on genus-zero rational trees, so its pullback is convex by
Tseng's Example 5.2.2.  The defining section is regular by quasismoothness.
CCIT's toric-stack mirror theorem, followed by
Tseng's Theorem 5.1.6, Proposition 5.2.3, and Corollaries 5.2.4 and 5.2.6,
therefore identifies the hypergeometric modification above with the part of
the ordinary `J_X` generated by ambient classes: the required inequality is
`c1(O_W(d))<=c1(TW)`, namely `d<=sum_iw_i`, which follows from the strict
Fano inequality `d<sum_iw_i`.  Explicitly, Corollary 5.2.6 gives `J=I/F(t)` after the mirror map
`t mapsto tau(t)`; this is the reconstruction normalization used in (1).

That part is the whole small-even theory.  Indeed Fano vanishing, the assumed
`Pic(X)=Z H`, and Poincare duality give

\[
 H^{even}(X)=\langle1,H,H^2,H^3\rangle,
\]

of rank four.  The cohomology-valued `I`-function and its first three cyclic
derivatives have those four distinct classical leading terms, so they identify
`j^*H^*(W)` with all of `H^even(X)` and the scalar operator (1) with the full
small-even QDM.  In index one, the
mirror correction is the standard scalar string gauge (equivalently, the
scalar shift in quantum multiplication); it changes only the irregular
exponential and not the framed formal residues.  The rank-four support bound
therefore gives `nu_6<=2`.

## Exact boundary

This does not cover a weighted projective space with a positive-dimensional
stacky stratum, a hypersurface meeting a stacky stratum by a special smooth
quotient mechanism, a non-primitive `O_X(1)`, a weighted complete
intersection, or any raw stacky `I`-series whose order exceeds four.  In
particular it does not turn the singular `(3,6)` false positive into a
counterexample.
