## Delivered scope

The default /evolve snapshot is an actual 96-job, 25×25 capacity comparison.
32 CPU-only, 24 GPU-only and 40 CPU/GPU-flexible jobs form a modeled simultaneous
inference resource-allocation workload; this is not a production trace or a
precedence schedule. Target 80; transfer capacity fixed at 96. Run comparison
starts fresh browser/WASM execution. The old 24/72-job parameter modes remain.

The shared canonical WASM ranked evolution driver now proposes structural edits
in a bounded resource-cover grammar: resource terms, integer resource charges,
job partitions and saturation caps. A proposed group's every job option must
pay at least one declared resource charge. Each resource supplies at most
floor(capacity/charge) such charges. A count cap follows from group size; the
job partition lets us sum the group upper bounds. Shared resources between
groups can only weaken the upper bound. These checked, model-bound quantified
inequalities are not unrestricted theorem invention or newly invented proof rules.

The independent controller checker recomputes option obligations and partition
coverage from immutable source; proposer scores and baseline answers have no
admission authority. Invalid covers include concrete escaping job/option loads.
Checked rules need not add coverage or dominate their predecessor pointwise.
A structural predecessor is an adjacent prior-round ranked candidate, not a
claim that every inferred lineage edge is uniquely determined by WASM output.

Orange markers expose the formula, source scope, proof argument, groups,
predecessor, excluded example, receipt hash and local Recheck reduction action.
Selection persists during progress, and markers have larger touch targets.
The full proposal history includes rejected and checked-but-unhelpful rules.

## Concurrency and explicit remaining gap

A separate Worker owns Evolve proposal generation, scoring and its checks.
The solver Worker continuously processes capacity queries. The controller
independently checks each proposal and atomically promotes exclusions as ready;
in-flight queries are reserved and cannot also count as eliminated. No generation
barrier pauses solve dispatch. Quantized browser timestamps retain actual equal
times with ordered event sequences; rates for zero elapsed intervals are absent,
not infinite or fabricated. Solve-completion and discovery-completion times are
separate.
