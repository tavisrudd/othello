# C661 — Uniform factorization upgrade for Paper II

**Lane**: `clebsch`

**Status**: queued after C577; higher-tier Paper II upgrade; must not hold the
current release.

## Objective

Test whether the conic matching quotient admits a theorem materially stronger
than the three exceptional examples.  The task succeeds only through one of
the following routes, or a result of demonstrably equivalent mathematical
strength:

1. **Uniform recovery/Gorenstein theorem.**  Give natural intrinsic hypotheses
   on a balanced conic matching quotient under which quadratic products recover
   the unordered sheets, the first odd covariant is cubic, and the homogenized
   configuration is self-associated arithmetically Gorenstein.  The theorem
   must cover at least one family or genuinely new instance beyond the current
   \(B_3,H_3\) configurations; merely repackaging their certified ranks as
   hypotheses does not pass.
2. **Conceptual rank theorem.**  Replace the load-bearing \(A_3,B_3\) row
   reductions by representation-theoretic, invariant-theoretic, or geometric
   nonvanishing arguments that explain the \(3,6,10\) pattern at the same
   conceptual level as the \(H_3\) proof.
3. **Equivalent-strength upgrade.**  Prove a canonical obstruction,
   classification, or consequential application that expands Paper II's scope
   as much as either route above.  Another finite census, a new coordinate
   witness, or editorial reframing alone does not qualify.

## Bounded attack

Run one bounded pass on each route before choosing:

- extract the exact hypotheses used by the radical--Hadamard, Schur-power,
  Gale-duality, and cubic-orientation arguments;
- test whether matching orbits from a natural infinite or parametrized class
  satisfy those hypotheses for structural reasons;
- decompose the \(A_3,B_3\) quotient modules and seek a switch-generated or
  stabilizer-fixed nonzero vector proving full span without row reduction;
- inventory equivalent-strength consequences already one conceptual lemma away,
  while excluding unfinished Paper III claims.

Stop a route when its proposed theorem merely assumes the desired ranks,
requires a configuration-by-configuration enumeration, or has no new instance.
If all routes fail these kill tests, record the obstruction precisely and close
C661 rather than opening an unbounded search.

## Acceptance and evidence

A positive result requires:

- a precise theorem and complete proof at its natural characteristic scope;
- an explicit statement of which finite computations, if any, remain
  load-bearing;
- a focused primary-literature and novelty audit before any priority claim;
- a Paper II disposition that preserves the current release unless the new
  theorem passes an independent cold review;
- for any paper-facing computation, the repository's reproducibility bundle,
  independent replay, and trust-map update.

The final report must compare the result against the current Paper II crown:
quadratic recovery, cubic orientation, and Gorenstein duality for the exceptional
configurations.  Run the required `ej`+`tt` closeout and mystery ledger before
reporting a substantial positive or negative result.

## Non-goals

- Do not import the unfinished Hitchin, golden-fibre, \(T_{11}\), or harmonic
  claims from Paper III.
- Do not reopen Paper II v1 for a speculative theorem.
- Do not count shorter computations, more examples, or stronger rhetoric as an
  equivalent-strength upgrade.
