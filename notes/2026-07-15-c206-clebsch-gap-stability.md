# C206 — conceptual Clebsch gap and stability
**Lane**: `clebsch-next`
**Date:** 2026-07-15
**Status:** QUEUED

## Objective

Explain the sharp non-Clebsch nearest-conic discrepancy bound `delta(A)>=12` without using the
fifteen-class census as the proof. Test whether chord multiplicities, Brianchon defect, character
data, or the orbitals of the six-arc action force the gap, and whether the same mechanism yields a
stability theorem for extension loci of small arcs.

## Gate

A successful result must provide a genuinely smaller invariant proof or a parameterized stability
statement. Merely recomputing the fifteen discrepancy values under a new name is not an upgrade.
Computation may scout candidate invariants and coherent configurations but remains verification.
