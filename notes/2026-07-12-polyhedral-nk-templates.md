# Polyhedral regular-template Node-Kayles nimbers (S₄, A₅)

**Date:** 2026-07-12. Completes the polyhedral rows of the conic-involution small-subgroup
catalogue ([Schreier graphs](2026-07-12-conic-involution-schreier-graphs.md)); feeds the
appendix of the dihedral submission
([draft](2026-07-12-dihedral-schreier-node-kayles-submission.md)). The dihedral §14 called
evaluating the polyhedral **regular** (free-orbit) templates the "unresolved problem" — these
are in fact finite computations, done here.

## The object

The free-orbit template of a group `G = ⟨T⟩` generated by a legal involution triple `T`, acting
regularly, is the Cayley graph `Cay(G, T)`: `|G|` vertices, one edge `{g, g·t}` per generator
(the regular action is fixed-point-free, so no saturation deletions). Its Node-Kayles Grundy
value is the free-orbit `t_K` entry in the orbit-template formula
`𝒢(R_T(Ω)) = ⊕ (m_K mod 2)·t_K`.

## Results (exact)

| G | class (pairwise product orders) | `Cay(G,T)` | Node-Kayles 𝒢 |
|---|---|---|---:|
| V₄ | `(2,2,2)` | K₄ = M₄ | **1** |
| S₄ | `(2,3,3)` | 24-vtx cubic | **0** |
| S₄ | `(2,3,4)` | 24-vtx cubic | **0** |
| S₄ | `(3,3,3)` | 24-vtx cubic | **0** |
| S₄ | `(3,4,4)` | 24-vtx cubic | **0** |
| A₅ | `(2,3,5)` | 60-vtx cubic, girth 4 | **1** |
| A₅ | `(2,5,5)` | 60-vtx cubic, girth 4 | **1** |
| A₅ | `(3,3,5)` | 60-vtx cubic, girth 6 | **0** |
| A₅ | `(3,5,5)` | 60-vtx cubic, girth 6 | **0** |
| A₅ | `(5,5,5)` | 60-vtx cubic, girth 9 | **0** |

(Dihedral rows are in the submission: `M_{4n}=1`, prism `C_{2n}□K₂=0`, ladder `L_k = k mod 2`.)

So **every S₄ free orbit is null** (`𝒢=0`), like even cycles and prisms. A₅ is class-dependent:
of the five generating-triple classes, the two with a **commuting generator pair** (a pairwise
product of order 2, i.e. a `V₄` inside A₅) are N (`𝒢=1`); the three with all-odd pairwise
products are P (`𝒢=0`). This "order-2 pair ⟺ N" pattern is an A₅ observation only — it does **not**
hold for S₄, whose `(2,3,3)`/`(2,3,4)` classes are also `𝒢=0`.

## Caveat

These are **free-orbit** `t_K` values for the catalogue. `𝒢(S₄ regular)=0` is a fact about a full
S₄ orbit; it does **not** touch the S₄→full-`PSL/PGL` **escape** residual (the generic size-4
child), which is the separate open odd-plane kernel. A₅ likewise arises only as a residual
free-orbit type, not the escape.

## Method / reproduction

`rust/scripts/nodekayles_cayley.rs`: builds `Cay(G,T)` per generating-triple class and computes the
exact Node-Kayles value by memoized Sprague-Grundy with **connected-component decomposition**
(`𝒢(disconnected) = ⊕ component 𝒢`) and **full-graph-automorphism canonical memoization** — the
memo key is the `min` over the graph's automorphism group of the component bitmask. The automorphism
group used is `left-mult(G) × color-perm`, where the color-perm factor is the setwise stabilizer of
`T` under `S_n`-conjugation (trivial when the three product orders are distinct; `S₃` for the
symmetric `(5,5,5)` triple, giving a 360-element group and a 6× dedup that made girth-9 tractable).

```
rustc -O -C target-cpu=native rust/scripts/nodekayles_cayley.rs -o /tmp/nkcay
/tmp/nkcay S4            # all 4 classes -> 0
/tmp/nkcay A5            # all 5 classes (girth-9 is slow; run per-class)
/tmp/nkcay A5 5,5,5      # single class by pairwise-order signature
```

Cross-validated: S₄ agrees across three independent solvers (Python mask-DP, Python
pynauty-canonical, Rust); the V₄ row agrees with the closed form `#K₄ mod 2` and `Cor 3.2`.
