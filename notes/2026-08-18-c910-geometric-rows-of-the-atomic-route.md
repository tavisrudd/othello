## What the manuscript needs, and what is now proved

### The sign between the residual endomorphism and Euler multiplication

The manuscript reads the residual endomorphism `kappa` of the `A`-model
connection in the loop coordinate as the negative of Euler multiplication, and
then uses that every spectral statement transfers.  The transfer is now exact
linear algebra over a commutative ring.  For every scalar and every exponent, the
generalized eigenspace of the negated endomorphism at the negated scalar is the
generalized eigenspace of the original one, so the two decompositions agree
summand by summand and filtration step by filtration step, which is the equality
of Jordan block sizes.  The eigenvalue set of the negated endomorphism is the
image of the original eigenvalue set under negation and has the same cardinality.
The submodule generated by the orbit of a vector is the same for both, so a
vector is cyclic for one exactly when it is cyclic for the other and the two
endomorphisms are regular together.  The discriminant is unchanged twice over: in
rank four, negating the roots of a split monic quartic leaves its discriminant
unchanged, and more generally the universal quartic discriminant is unchanged
when the coefficients of the linear and cubic terms are negated, since every one
of its monomials has even total degree in those two; in rank two, the residue
discriminant `(trace R) ^ 2 - 4 * det R` is unchanged because sign reversal
negates the trace and fixes the determinant.  The last statement matters for the
atomic invariant: the manuscript computes it from the modified residue derived
from `kappa`, while the ledger inputs speak of Euler multiplication, and the two
readings now provably agree.

### The Hodge-fixed base as a subalgebra

The manuscript's proof is that the fixed tangent subbundle is closed under the
multiplication, contains the unit, and is preserved by the Euler field.  In Lean,
the simultaneous fixed points of a family of algebra automorphisms of a
commutative algebra form a subalgebra; multiplication by a fixed element maps it
to itself and the restricted product is the product of the subalgebra, so an
eigenvector of the restricted Euler multiplication is an eigenvector of the
ambient one with the same eigenvalue.  The powers of a fixed element are fixed, so
the span of the first four powers of the hyperplane class lies in the fixed
subalgebra, and four linearly independent powers span a four-dimensional
subspace.  The truncated polynomial algebra of exponent four over a field
realizes that configuration — vanishing fourth power, dimension four — so the
dimension count is not vacuous.

### The parity ranks of the cubic zero packet

The even rank is two, from the block reduction of the small even connection, and
is a hypothesis here.  The odd rank is the third Betti number, and that number is
now derived rather than quoted.  In a commutative ring where the fourth power of
the hyperplane class vanishes, the adjunction factor `1 + 3 * P` is a unit with
the explicit inverse `1 - 3 * P + 9 * P ^ 2 - 27 * P ^ 3`, so the adjunction
equation has the unique solution `1 + 2 * P + 4 * P ^ 2 - 2 * P ^ 3`; the third
Chern class is therefore `-2 * P ^ 3` and the top Chern number is `-6`.  With the
Lefschetz Betti numbers in the other degrees and the alternating sum, the third
Betti number is ten.  That the whole degree-three cohomology sits in the zero
packet is the degree count: Euler multiplication sends a class of degree three in
Novikov degree `d` to degree `5 - 4 * d`, which is five for `d = 0`, one for
`d = 1`, and negative afterwards, and the cohomology vanishes in each of those.
The separation of branches is topological: a locally constant rank function is
constant on a connected component, so a branch of rank twelve lies in a component
containing neither rank-one branch.  Assembling these gives parity ranks two and
ten of total rank twelve.

## Scope

The geometry remains outside Lean.  There is no `F`-bundle, `F`-manifold,
spectral cover, Hodge atom, variety, cohomology, Chern class, or quantum product.
That the residual endomorphism is the negative of Euler multiplication, that the
Hodge-fixed locus is a smooth `F`-submanifold whose tangent space is the Novikov
extension of the rational classes of Hodge type `(i, i)`, that Lefschetz
identifies that space with the even cohomology of a cubic threefold, that the
reduced spectral cover is finite etale over the maximal-eigenvalue locus with
locally free generalized eigenbundles, and that a connected component of the
cover defines a Hodge atom are all assumptions, either as typed hypotheses or as
the reading that motivates the algebra.  The Chern computation is an identity in
a truncated ring, not a computation in a cohomology ring; the Betti bookkeeping
is integer arithmetic given the alternating sum; and the branch separation is a
statement about a locally constant function on a topological space.  The final
clause of the manuscript's proposition, that the rank-twelve component has degree
one over its image near the small hyperplane point, is not represented.

## Mystery ledger

- The sign-reversal dictionary needs no finiteness, no field, and no
  diagonalizability: it holds for an endomorphism of any module over a
  commutative ring, because the only mechanism is that the `k`-th power of a
  negated operator is the original one up to `(-1) ^ k`.  The manuscript states
  it for the maximal `A`-model `F`-bundle.  Settled by this pass.
- Sign reversal fixes the residue discriminant in rank two and the discriminant
  of the characteristic polynomial in rank four for the same reason in different
  clothing: the discriminant is even in the odd characteristic coefficients.  The
  manuscript treats the two invariances separately.  Settled by this pass.
- The total Chern class of the cubic threefold was over-determined in the
  manuscript's presentation: the value is not merely consistent with the
  adjunction equation but is its unique solution, because the adjunction factor
  is a unit in the truncated ring.  Settled by this pass.
- Open: the degree-one statement for the atomic component.  The manuscript
  concludes that the rank-twelve component has degree one over its image in a
  neighborhood of the small hyperplane point; only the exclusion of the two
  rank-one branches from that component is formalized.  Evidence gap: no finite
  etale cover in the package.  Owner: a successor under this row, gated on
  representing the spectral cover.
- Open: the even rank two of the zero packet enters the parity-rank statement as
  a hypothesis rather than from the block reduction module, because the two speak
  different languages — one about a rank of a generalized eigenspace of an
  abstract packet, the other about an explicit four-by-four matrix over a
  coefficient field.  Evidence gap: no bridge from the explicit small even Euler
  matrix to the abstract rank.  Owner: a successor joining
  `Quantum/CubicSmallEvenBlockReduction.lean` to the ledger's parity ranks; this
  is the cheapest remaining upgrade in the atomic route.
