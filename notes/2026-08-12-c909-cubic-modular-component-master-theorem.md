## The theorem

Let

```text
 U = P^1_t - {the four singular A5 cubic parameters}
```

be the signed smooth nonstandard `A_5` cubic line, and put `T=81t^2`.
After the harmless finite level refinement which labels the five selected
axes and their three-primary scalar graph, there is a morphism

```text
                    U --> H_5^et
```

to the marked finite-etale elliptic-Hecke stack with the following
properties.

1. Its underlying ppav is the relative intermediate Jacobian `J(X_t)`.
2. Its elliptic-power presentation is the actual relative norm-axis
   isogeny

   ```text
             f:E^5 --> J(X_t),             f^*Theta=6I_5-J_5.
   ```

3. At two, the marking cover of its actual maximal-isotropic kernel is the
   congruence sign cover

   ```text
                           r^2=T,
   ```

   and the signed parameter selects `r=9t` (the opposite convention selects
   `r=-9t`).  At three, the graph is the monodromy-selected scalar packet.
4. Every divided power of every divisor class on `J(X_t)` belongs to the
   ordinary integral divisor-product lattice:

   ```text
        PD<NS(J(X_t))>^k = P_J^k,          0<=k<=5.
   ```

5. In particular `Theta^4/4!` is algebraic.  Voisin's cubic criterion makes
   every `X_t` universally `CH_0`-trivial.
6. Independently, the all-smooth-cubic quantum theorem makes
   `X_t times P^1` irrational.

After forgetting the sign, the reduced cubic period curve is normalized by

```text
       X_0(3) - {T=0,infinity,-27,729/5},
```

and the marking cover above is its index-two congruence cover
`Gamma_ex=rho_2^{-1}(A_3)`.  Hence the family is a nonisotrivial modular
component of the marked separation locus

```text
 {X smooth cubic : J(X) has finite-etale elliptic-Hecke presentation}.
```

## Proof architecture

The proof has four engines, each with a separate mathematical job.

### A. Group norms construct the relative presentation

For each of the six `D_5` subgroups, the integral subgroup norm on the
relative intermediate Jacobian has connected elliptic image.  The normalizer
of a `D_5` is itself and the subgroup acts trivially on its fixed line, so
conjugation canonically transports the six images.  Roulleau's connected
elliptic fibration identifies the primitive Rosati quotient with this norm
axis.  Its fibre intersections give diagonal/off-diagonal Gram entries
`(5,-1)`, hence `6I-J`.  This constructs the actual finite flat kernel; it
does not infer it from rational isotypy or Smith data.

### B. Prym functoriality identifies the actual modular marking

Van Geemen--Yamauchi's degree-five quotient of the discriminant double-cover
diagram induces pullback from their explicit elliptic Prym to the cubic Prym.
Norm after pullback is `[5]`, so its kernel is 5-primary and its image is the
`C_5`-fixed axis.  The `D_5` character calculation identifies that image
with the norm axis.  More precisely, both the Prym pullback and primitive
axis inclusion have Rosati norm `[5]`; their quotient isogeny has degree one.
Thus the explicit Prym is the primitive norm axis.  Its two-division
discriminant is `T` up to square, proving that `r^2=T` marks the actual
two-primary graph kernel.

### C. Finite-etale spectra force integral saturation

At every bad prime the projective graph packet has separable residual
spectrum.  After finite unramified faithfully flat splitting, its orthogonal
idempotent blocks make the full Neron--Severi coefficient lattice generated
by rank-one square-zero classes.  If `Theta=sum D_i`, then

```text
              Theta^k/k! = sum_{|I|=k} product_{i in I} D_i,
```

and the same rank-one decomposition gives every divided power generated by
`NS`, with no division by two.  Faithful-flat quotient-lattice descent and
localization return the integral statement.  The exotic `F_4` block at two
and the scalar block at three meet these hypotheses.

### D. Two independent geometric detectors fire

For cubic threefolds, algebraicity of the minimal intermediate-Jacobian
class is exactly the input to Voisin's universal-`CH_0` criterion.  The
irrationality of `X times P^1` comes from the independent quantum atom
theorem for every smooth cubic.  The modular component tells us where the
cycle detector fires; it supplies no premise to the quantum theorem.

## What this compresses

The theorem replaces a fibrewise exceptional calculation by the following
inevitable chain:

```text
 subgroup norms
   -> primitive relative axes
   -> odd Prym pullback / actual modular graph
   -> finite-etale spectral splitting
   -> rank-one square-zero generation
   -> all divided powers
   -> cubic minimal class.
```

Only the Gram normalization `(5,-1)` and one orientation of the two-sheeted
marking are special calculations.  The rest is structural and works over a
connected base.  In particular, no lattice certificate, exhaustive graph
census, or choice of a fibrewise isogeny is load-bearing.

## Exact scope

The theorem concerns the marked elliptic-power presentation.  It does not
say that the total finite-etale Hecke locus is closed or finite type, nor
that the presentation is recoverable from the unmarked ppav.  It proves
cohomological divisor-product saturation, not a relative Chow divided-power
identity.  It neither produces a universal decomposition of the diagonal
nor proves irrationality after two stabilizations.

The equality with `X_0(3)` is a normalization statement for the reduced
period image, not a compactified equality of cubic and modular families.
The four removed cubic boundary values include two interior modular points.
The sign cover labels the exotic pair only after a one-fibre symplectic
orientation; reversing that orientation is the deck involution.

## Mystery ledger

* **Settled — actual versus auxiliary cover.**  The Prym pullback and
  primitive polarization comparison show that `r^2=T` is the marking cover
  of the actual intermediate-Jacobian kernel.
* **Settled — relative isogeny.**  Integral subgroup norms and Roulleau's
  connected quotient construct it over the family; rational isotypy is no
  longer asked to descend an integral map.
* **Settled — why divided powers lift.**  The finite-etale spectral packet
  produces rank-one square-zero generators after unramified splitting, so
  the divided-power formula is an exterior expansion, not a factorial
  miracle.
* **Open — common cycle/quantum object.**  The Eisenstein polynomial appears
  in both packets, but no common action or torsor is known.  This belongs to
  neither proof.
* **Open — horizontal Chow class.**  Relative Neron--Severi sections need not
  lift to rigidified kernel-linearized line bundles.  This is the C908
  boundary and is not claimed here.
* **Open — further cubic components.**  No second positive-dimensional
  family was found in the bounded source audit.  Fixed modular data meet the
  cubic period locus finitely unless they share a component, but classifying
  all shared components remains open.
