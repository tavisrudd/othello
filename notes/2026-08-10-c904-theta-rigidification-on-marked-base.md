## Executive verdict

The smooth-base Picard gate is positive.  No additional two-primary cover is
needed after the exotic marking already required by the cubic family.

Let $U$ be the smooth exotic-marked $A_5$ cubic base and
$\mathcal J\to U$ its principally polarized intermediate-Jacobian scheme.
The mod-$2$ elliptic monodromy on the unmarked modular curve is $S_3$.  The
exotic quadratic marking cuts it to its even subgroup $A_3\cong C_3$; the
elliptic twist marking acts trivially on $\mathcal J[2]$.  An exact integral
matrix calculation on the actual principal homology lattice gives

\[
        M^2+M+I=0\quad\text{on }\mathcal J[2].
\]

Thus $M-I$ is invertible and $\mathcal J[2]^{C_3}=0$.

For every horizontal integral Neron--Severi class, its symmetric
line-bundle representatives form an affine torsor under $\mathcal J[2]$.
This torsor is canonically the space of quadratic refinements of the
associated mod-$2$ alternating form.  Odd-order group cohomology gives
$H^1(C_3,\mathcal J[2])=0$, so the torsor has a fixed point; the absence of
fixed vectors makes that point unique.  The fifteen divisor generators in
the fibrewise saturation proof therefore rigidify to relative symmetric line
bundles on $U$ itself.  Their fixed integral fourfold-product relation gives

\[
 Z_{\min}\in \operatorname{CH}^4(\mathcal J)
 \quad\text{with}\quad
 [Z_{\min}|_{\mathcal J_u}]=\frac{\Theta_u^4}{4!}
\]

for every $u\in U$.

The local mod-$2$ theta residues vanish at all four boundary points.  The
quartic $X_0(6)$ cusp widths are $1,2,3,6$; the exotic sign cover ramifies
exactly at the odd-width cusps, so the marked widths are $2,2,6,6$.  Every
local parabolic is therefore trivial modulo two.  This closes the finite
theta-characteristic residue calculation, though extension of the Chow cycle
through a chosen semiabelian compactification remains separate.

This corrects the preceding provisional checkpoint, which only claimed the
primitive relative minimal cycle after a further finite etale two-primary
cover.  Its index-$16$ calculation remains correct but was overinterpreted.
The quotient is the **Rosati norm-square defect**: it measures which divisor
classes are generated by pullbacks $f^*\Theta$, not which divisor classes
admit relative line bundles.  The latter torsors split uniquely on the
exotic marked base.

The integral relative universal cubic correspondence is still open.  The
Fano incidence cycle gives $[2]$ and hence a rational identity cycle, while
Voisin's fixed-fibre rationally connected lifting step does not formally
relativize over the surface supporting $Z_{\min}$.  An odd generic-fibre
index would combine with $[2]$ by Bezout and close this final smooth-base
gate.

## 1. A uniform odd-monodromy theorem

The calculation is an instance of a general elementary principle.

> **Odd-monodromy theta-rigidification theorem.**  Let $A\to S$ be a
> principally polarized abelian scheme over a connected complex curve, with
> $2$ invertible, and let $D$ be a horizontal integral Neron--Severi class.
> Suppose the torsor of symmetric representatives of $D$ has monodromy
> through a finite group $G$ of odd order.  Then $D$ has a monodromy-invariant
> symmetric representative.  If $A[2]^G=0$, that representative is unique.

Indeed, two symmetric line bundles with the same Neron--Severi class differ
by a two-torsion line bundle.  Hence their representatives form an affine
$A^\vee[2]$-torsor.  Under the principal polarization this is an affine
$A[2]$-torsor, canonically realized as the quadratic refinements of the
alternating form attached to $D$.  Since multiplication by $|G|$ is the
identity on an $\mathbf F_2$-module, averaging kills positive group
cohomology and

\[
                   H^1(G,A[2])=0.
\]

The affine torsor has a fixed point.  Its fixed points, if nonempty, form a
torsor under $A[2]^G$, proving uniqueness in the fixed-point-free case.

This theorem is stronger than the principal-theta case needed here: it
applies to every horizontal Neron--Severi class, including indefinite ones.
For such a class $D$, the normalized symmetry of a representative $L$
defines a quadratic function on $A[2]$; tensoring by an element of
$A^\vee[2]$ adds the corresponding Weil character.  Consequently the entire
torsor and its Galois action are determined by $A[2]$ and the alternating
form of $D$, so the action factors through the finite mod-$2$ monodromy.

On the present smooth affine complex curve, the invariant relative-Picard
section is represented by an actual line bundle: the only remaining
relative-Picard obstruction lies in $\operatorname{Br}(U)$, which vanishes
for a smooth complex affine curve.  Equivalently, one may construct the
line bundle on the generic fibre and extend its Picard section across the
good-reduction curve.

## 3. What the index-$16$ quotient really is

Let $R=\operatorname{End}(\mathcal J_u)$ be the generic integral
endomorphism order and let $\dagger$ be Rosati involution.  The principal
polarization identifies

\[
             \operatorname{NS}(\mathcal J_u)\cong R^+
             :=\{s\in R:s^\dagger=s\}.
\]

Under this identification, $f^*\Theta$ corresponds to the Rosati norm
$q(f)=f^\dagger f$.  Thus the previously computed sublattice is

\[
        N_{\mathrm{vis}}=\langle q(f):f\in R\rangle
          \subseteq R^+.
\]

This admits a uniform explanation.  For $s\in R^+$,

\[
  q(s+1)-q(s)-q(1)=2s,
\]

while

\[
  q(x+y)-q(x)-q(y)=x^\dagger y+y^\dagger x.
\]

Therefore the norm-square quotient

\[
              Q(R)=R^+/\langle f^\dagger f:f\in R\rangle
\]

is always elementary two-primary, and a basis of $R$ together with pairwise
sums computes it exhaustively.  For the exotic cubic order the exact Smith
and module calculation gives

\[
             Q(R)\cong(\mathbf Z/2)^4\cong H_{6,2},
\]

the four-dimensional six-point heart with commutant $\mathbf F_4$.

This is a useful structural theorem, but it is not a Picard obstruction.
It says that pullbacks of the already chosen principal theta bundle do not
generate all integral symmetric classes.  The missing classes have their own
quadratic-refinement torsors, and the order-three monodromy splits those
torsors uniquely.

The corrected hierarchy is therefore

\[
\boxed{
\begin{array}{c}
\text{exotic gluing selects the }C_3\text{ marked base}\\
\Downarrow\\
H^1(C_3,\mathcal J[2])=0,
\quad \mathcal J[2]^{C_3}=0\\
\Downarrow\\
\text{every horizontal NS class has a unique symmetric lift}\\
\Downarrow\\
\Theta^4/4!\text{ is a primitive relative Chow class};\\[2mm]
Q(R)\cong H_{6,2}\text{ independently records the norm-square defect.}
\end{array}}
\]

## 4. Exact scope after the positive gate

The following statements are now proved on the smooth exotic marked base:

1. every integral horizontal Neron--Severi class used in the certificate has
   a unique relative symmetric representative;
2. the primitive minimal class $\Theta^4/4!$ is represented by one integral
   relative signed Chow one-cycle;
3. Fano incidence gives an integral relative cubic correspondence inducing
   $[2]$;
4. dividing the incidence correspondence by two gives a rational relative
   cubic correspondence inducing the identity.

Three boundaries remain:

- **Integral universal cubic cycle.**  Voisin's fixed-fibre proof lifts
  curves through rationally connected Abel--Jacobi fibres.  Relatively, the
  support is a surface and rational connectedness alone does not force index
  one over its function field.  The exact remaining invariant is the common
  index $n$ of those generic fibres.  Odd $n$ suffices by Bezout with $[2]$.
- **Exotic-deck descent.**  The primitive class is constructed on the
  exotic marked base.  Its norm descends with class $2c$; descent of a
  primitive Chow representative to the unmarked base is a separate
  $C_2$-equivariance question and is not claimed.
- **Cusps.**  The finite mod-$2$ theta residues are zero at all four cusps.
  Extension of the signed fourfold-intersection cycle across a chosen
  semiabelian compactification, and compatibility with admissible Prym and
  cubic Chow residues, remain open.

## 7. EJ + TT closeout

The earlier pass treated three occurrences of two-primary arithmetic as one
obstruction.  The correct compression separates them:

| object | invariant | status |
|---|---|---|
| principal gluing | exotic $\mathbf F_4$ heart | selects the necessary quadratic marking |
| Rosati norm squares | $Q(R)\cong(\mathbf Z/2)^4$ | exact limitation of theta pullbacks only |
| symmetric-line-bundle torsor | $H^1(C_3,J[2])$ | zero; unique splitting on the marked base |
| cubic Abel--Jacobi lift | generic-fibre index $n$ | open; odd $n$ closes the integral identity |

The most efficient next attack is consequently not another divisor census.
It is an odd-index theorem for the relative Abel--Jacobi fibration, or an
exact counterexample showing that its index is two.  Two-stage section
spaces, the degree-five/degree-six Hilbert models in Voisin's property $(*)$,
and the common $A_5$ line are the only currently visible mechanisms that
could force oddness.
