# C909 — all-polarization divided powers on finite-etale graph ppavs

Date: 2026-08-11  
Lane: clebsch  
Status: bounded TT/EJ consequence and positioning pass; no manuscript, PDF,
mirror, Lean, or commit edit

## Verdict

There is a useful strengthening of the finite-etale cofactor theorem, but its
novelty has to be stated at the right layer.  The meaningful assertion is not
merely that the classes
\[
\gamma_k(D):=D^k/k!
\]
are integral: divided powers of an integral polarization are standard
cohomological classes.  The stronger assertion is ordinary divisor-product
membership in every degree,
\[
\gamma_k(D)\in P_A^k:=\operatorname{im}
 \bigl(\operatorname{Sym}^k\operatorname{NS}(A)\to H^{2k}(A,\mathbf Z)\bigr),
\tag{C909.1}
\]
for every divisor class \(D\) on a marked finite-etale elliptic graph
quotient \(A\), and hence equality between the divided-power envelope of
\(\operatorname{NS}(A)\) and the ordinary product lattice in each degree.

Once the local graph lattice is shown to be generated by admissible rank-one
coefficient forms, (C909.1) is a short formal corollary.  The genuinely new
input remains the finite-etale splitting, the exact integral graph-lattice
calculation, and faithful-flat descent.  Thus this is a good structural
corollary and proof compressor, not an independent crown unless the
rank-one-generation lemma is proved in the full stated scope.

## The precise candidate theorem

Let \(E\) be non-CM and let \(A\) be a principally polarized quotient of an
elliptic power by an elementary, block-respecting graph whose local source
polarization has blocks \(p^aB\) and whose literal algebra
\((\mathbf Z/p^a)[T]\) is finite etale.  Require the same local statement at
each bad prime and the usual localization away from the isogeny degree.
Then the proposed all-degree conclusion is
\[
\operatorname{PD}\!\left\langle\operatorname{NS}(A)\right\rangle^k
   =P_A^k\qquad(0\le k\le g),
\tag{C909.2}
\]
where the left side is the degree-\(k\) part of the divided-power algebra
generated by divisor classes in integral cohomology.

In particular, every principal polarization \(\Theta'\) on this presented
quotient satisfies
\[
\frac{(\Theta')^k}{k!}\in P_A^k
\quad(0\le k\le g),
\tag{C909.3}
\]
not only the descended polarization used to define the graph.  If “all
polarizations” is intended to quantify over principal polarizations on the
bare ppav without a common elliptic-power presentation, (C909.3) is not
licensed by the current theorem.

The minimal-class statement is the single case \(k=g-1\).  The case \(k=g\)
would additionally put the degree-one top class
\(\Theta'^g/g!\) in the ordinary divisor-product lattice; this is a genuine
integral zero-cycle cohomology consequence, though not a Chow equality.

There are two readings of “all-polarization-divided-powers.”  For the fixed
descended \(\Theta\), the block-supported lattice already in the cofactor
proof is enough: after splitting, decompose each unimodular block \(B_\lambda\)
into rank-one forms and apply (C909.5).  That fixed-\(\Theta\), all-\(k\)
statement is essentially a formal corollary.  The stronger uniform reading,
quantifying over every \(D\in\operatorname{NS}(A)\) or every principal
\(\Theta'\), requires the full-lattice rank-one generation, including
off-block terms, and is the substantive upgrade recorded below.

## Why the all-degree step is formal after the right lattice lemma

After a finite unramified splitting extension, write the coefficient module as
an orthogonal sum of the finite-etale scalar blocks
\[
M=\bigperp_\lambda L_\lambda.
\]
The exact graph congruence gives the following rank-one generators for the
local divisor coefficient lattice (with the evident unit-block variants):

* \(p^a vv^t\) with \(v\in L_\lambda\);
* cross-block coefficients carrying the extra \(p^a\)-divisibility, which can
  be straightened as
  \[
  p^{2a}\bigl((u+v)(u+v)^t-uu^t-vv^t\bigr),
  \qquad u\in L_\lambda, v\in L_\mu, \lambda\ne\mu.
  \]

The first family is the block-supported lattice already used in the cofactor
proof.  The second family is needed only if one wants a statement for every
divisor class, rather than just the selected block-diagonal polarization.  In
either case each resulting coefficient form has rank one as a symmetric
matrix and therefore represents a decomposable cohomological two-form
\[
R=\Bigl(\sum_i a_i x_i\Bigr)\wedge
   \Bigl(\sum_j a_j y_j\Bigr),
\qquad R^2=0.
\tag{C909.4}
\]

Thus every local divisor class has a finite expression
\(D=\sum_i c_iR_i\) with \(R_i^2=0\).  The divided-power identity then gives
\[
\gamma_k(D)=
 \sum_{i_1<\cdots<i_k}c_{i_1}\cdots c_{i_k}
 R_{i_1}\cdots R_{i_k},
\tag{C909.5}
\]
which is an ordinary product of \(k\) integral divisor classes.  Products of
such divided powers give the inclusion of the entire left side of (C909.2)
in \(P_A^k\); the reverse inclusion is tautological.

Membership obtained after the unramified splitting descends because the
finite multilinear product map commutes with finite free base change and
faithful flatness detects zero in the quotient lattice.  At primes away from
the graph degree, the literal elliptic-power lattice supplies the same
rank-one decomposition.  This is the same denominator-free descent mechanism
as in the cofactor theorem.

## Does it compress the cofactor proof?

Yes, conditionally.  The current top-degree proof constructs the target block
of \(\operatorname{adj}(p^aB)\) using mixed-adjugate identities and then
descends it.  The rank-one formulation replaces this degree-specific argument
by one lemma:

> after etale splitting, the full integral graph divisor lattice is generated
> by square-zero rank-one coefficient forms.

The cofactor is then just \(\gamma_{g-1}(\Theta)\), and all other degrees are
obtained simultaneously from (C909.5).  The compression does **not** remove
the hard parts: finite etaleness over \(\mathbf Z/p^a\), the coefficient-form
adjoint convention, cross-block divisibility, and faithful-flat lattice
descent still have to be proved.  If only the already-proved block-supported
lattice (rather than the full rank-one generation lemma) is available, the
argument proves all divided powers of the selected block-diagonal polarization
but not automatically all divisor classes or all principal polarizations.

## Consequences beyond the minimal class

The safe cohomological consequences are:

1. every polarization divided power \(\Theta^k/k!\), including the top
   degree \(k=g\), is an ordinary integral divisor product;
2. the entire integral divided-power envelope generated by
   \(\operatorname{NS}(A)\) coincides with its ordinary product image in every
   degree;
3. factorial saturation defects disappear for this presented finite-etale
   graph locus in all degrees, not only in degree \(g-1\); and
4. the conclusion is stable under the finite-etale local products and the
   faithful-flat descent used in the graph theorem.

This does not assert that every integral Hodge class is a divisor product,
that every algebraic cycle is generated by divisors, or that the statement
holds in Chow modulo rational equivalence.  It also does not classify
non-etale carry defects.  The theorem is a statement about the
divisor-generated cohomological subring.

## Position and novelty boundary

The literal \(E^g\) case is elementary: symmetric integral coefficient forms
are generated by rank-one forms, which come from elliptic pullback divisors;
their squares vanish.  A claim for every principal polarization on literal
\(E^g\) should therefore be presented as a useful split lemma, not as new
divided-power theory.

For a finite-etale graph quotient, the nontrivial content is the integral local
graph lattice and its no-trace descent.  Existing divided-power/Fourier
results provide closure or algebraicity of divided powers, but do not replace
ordinary divisor products after isogeny gluing.  Accordingly:

* “all divided powers are integral/algebraic” is formal or known;
* “all divided powers lie in the ordinary divisor-product lattice” is a
  substantive finite-etale graph corollary; and
* “all polarizations on every ppav isogenous to \(E^g\) satisfy it” is a much
  broader, currently unsupported claim.

The cleanest publication position is to state (C909.2) as an all-degree
corollary of the finite-etale graph lattice theorem, with the \(k=g-1\)
minimal class and \(k=g\) point class highlighted as applications.  Do not
present it as a new general theorem about divided powers or as a Chow-ring
generation result.

## Open gate / hostile checks

Before promotion, verify explicitly that:

1. every off-block graph divisor coefficient has exactly the extra \(p^a\)
   divisibility used in the rank-one straightening;
2. unit blocks and mixed unit/etale blocks admit the same rank-one generators;
3. each rank-one coefficient indeed has square-zero two-form after passage to
   the graph quotient;
4. the product-image lattice commutes with the unramified base change used
   for etale splitting; and
5. “all principal polarizations” is being used only on the marked quotient for
   which the graph lattice theorem is available.

If any of (1)--(4) fails, retain the weaker theorem for the selected
polarization and the top cofactor; do not infer all-degree saturation from
block idempotents alone.

## EJ/TT closeout

**EJ:** the theorem has useful reach beyond the minimal class, especially the
degree-one top class and equality of the PD(NS) envelope with the ordinary
product image.  Its proof can compress the cofactor construction to one
rank-one lemma.

**TT:** the broad slogan “all polarization divided powers on finite-etale
graph ppavs” is safe only with marked elliptic-power, finite-etale,
cohomological, and ordinary-product qualifiers.  Without them it is either
formal/known or unsupported.

**Mystery ledger:** the remaining mathematical gate is not divided-power
formalism; it is full rank-one generation of the integral graph NS lattice,
including off-block carries and base-change descent.
