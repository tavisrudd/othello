## Verdict

**MINOR.**  The orbital relabeling and every displayed mod-two identity in the
assigned sections are correct.  The main defects are local auditability gaps:
the pair table is asserted before a derivation visible in this scope, the
source-to-manuscript orbital dictionary is omitted, and the proof of the
operator-field proposition establishes the field generated by (A_9) but
does not explicitly dispose of (A_1) and (A_3).  Direct enumeration shows
that the omitted conclusions are true, so I do not find a false statement or
a defect requiring a change of theorem.

## Strongest theorem in scope

The minimum-support hypergraph determines, from pair data alone, the full
six-relation elliptic association scheme on the 78 coordinates; it thereby
recovers the binary code as a 36-dimensional image, equips that code with an
intrinsic operator field \(\mathbb F_8\) (canonically up to Frobenius), and recovers the
coordinate automorphism group as \(\PGL(2,13)\).  Unary incidence data are
constant, so the reconstruction arity is exactly two.

## Explicit orbital-label dictionary

Write \(u\) for the Hollmann--Xiang cross ratio, understood as the unordered
pair \(\{u,u^{-1}\}\), and write
\[
 \lambda=f(u)=\frac14+\frac1{-2+u+u^{-1}}
\]
for their odd-characteristic modified cross-ratio label.  Identify their
conic coordinates by \((X,Y,Z)=(y,x,z)\).  The polar of the manuscript point
\(P=(x:y:z)\), in the source line-coordinate order \((z_\ell,x_\ell,y_\ell)\),
is
\[
 (z_\ell,x_\ell,y_\ell)=(2y,-z,-x).
\]
Consequently
\[
 \Delta_{\rm HX}(P^\perp)=\frac1{4\Delta(P)},\qquad
 2x_\ell y_m+2x_m y_\ell-z_\ell z_m=-2\beta(P,Q),
\]
and Theorem 5.3 of Hollmann--Xiang gives
\[
 \lambda=\widehat\rho_{\rm HX}(P^\perp,Q^\perp)
 =\frac1{16}\frac{\beta(P,Q)^2}{\Delta(P)\Delta(Q)}
 =9\rho(P,Q)\quad\text{in }\mathbb F_{13}.
\]
Thus the required six-row dictionary is

| Hollmann--Xiang orbital \(\{u,u^{-1}\}\) | source label \(\lambda=f(u)\) | Paper IV relation |
|---|---:|---:|
| \(\{12,12\}\) | 0 | \(A_0\) |
| \(\{4,10\}\) | 9 | \(A_1\) |
| \(\{3,9\}\) | 1 | \(A_3\) |
| \(\{5,8\}\) | 3 | \(A_9\) |
| \(\{2,7\}\) | 12 | \(A_{10}\) |
| \(\{6,11\}\) | 4 | \(A_{12}\) |

The source valency formula gives valency 7 for \(A_0\) (the self-inverse raw
label \(-1\)) and valency 14 for each of the other five relations, exactly as
in the 78-point internal-point model.  Here \(A_0\) is the non-diagonal
\(\rho=0\) relation, not the diagonal matrix; the latter is \(I\).

## Independent check of the scheme identities

I enumerated the 78 normalized projective points
\((1,a,b),(0,1,b),(0,0,1)\) for which
\(\Delta=y^2-xz\) is a nonsquare in \(\mathbb F_{13}\), formed every relation
directly from \(\rho=\beta^2/(\Delta_P\Delta_Q)\), and counted intermediate
points.  This checks the full ordered-pair domain, not sampled entries.  The
integer coefficients relevant to the displayed parity identities are:

- \(A_0^2\): diagonal coefficient 7; coefficients 1 on
  \(A_9,A_{10},A_{12}\); coefficients 0 on \(A_0,A_1,A_3\).
- Every coefficient of \(A_0A_r\), for \(r=9,10,12\), is even.
- In \(A_9^2\), only the \(A_{10}\)-coefficient is odd.
- In \(A_{10}^2\), only the \(A_{12}\)-coefficient is odd.
- In \(A_{12}^2\), only the \(A_9\)-coefficient is odd.

Hence all identities in equation (scheme) hold over \(\mathbb F_2\).  The
unreduced \(A_{10}^2\) coefficients on \(A_1\) and \(A_3\) are respectively
2 and 4, so the common-neighbor split used to undo the sole fusion is also
correct.  As further cross-checks, \(A_0(A_{10}+A_{12})=0\),
\(\operatorname{rank}A_9=\operatorname{rank}A_{10}
=\operatorname{rank}A_{12}=36\), and
\(\operatorname{rank}(A_9+I)=78\) over \(\mathbb F_2\).

## Causal proof spine

1. Pair concurrence values distinguish four scheme relations and fuse only
   \(A_1\) with \(A_3\).  The integer \(A_{10}^2\) common-neighbor count
   separates that pair, recovering the six colors.
2. The concurrence-eight neighborhoods are precisely polar incidence rows,
   so the point-pair data recovers the incidence matrix.  Parity of the pair
   table gives \(P=A_{10}+A_{12}\); the relation identity \(A_0P=0\), together
   with rank 36, identifies \(\operatorname{im}P\) with the code \(K\).
3. With \(B=A_9\), the parity identities put \(\operatorname{im}B\) in \(K\)
   and make \(B\) injective on \(K\).  Invertibility of \(B+I\) then removes
   the factor \(t+1\) from
   \(1+t+t^2+t^4=(t+1)(t^3+t^2+1)\), giving the irreducible cubic and the
   \(\mathbb F_8\)-structure.
4. Each minimum-orbit Gram matrix is a nonzero field scalar on \(K\), so each
   orbit spans \(K\).
5. A regular three-anchor orbit, the unique fourth anchor, and injective
   four-anchor signatures force every scheme automorphism into
   \(\PGL(2,13)\).  Spanning then identifies the hypergraph and code
   coordinate-permutation groups.

The anchor assertions also survive direct checking: the first triple has
signature \((10,3,9)\), there are exactly 2184 ordered triples of this type,
the \((3,1,9)\) fourth-anchor signature has one solution, and all 78
four-anchor signatures are distinct.

## Earliest unsupported implication

The earliest implication not independently justified by the assigned
manuscript text and source is the opening exact concurrence table
\[
 (c_0,c_1,c_3,c_9,c_{10},c_{12})=(8,6,6,12,7,9).
\]
Hollmann--Xiang determines the orbitals and their valencies, but says nothing
about Paper IV's minimum-support hypergraph.  In the assigned subsection the
table is stated without representatives, a counting argument, or a pointer to
the exact audit evidence.  Everything in the arity-two reconstruction is
conditional on this table.  The finite domain is conceptually complete (all
coordinate pairs), the six orbital classes provide the symmetry quotient,
and transitivity supplies local-to-global transport; the actual support
generation, rejection criterion, and deduplication behind the counts are not
specified here.

## Ranked findings

1. **Unexplained computation (effect: reconstruction premise).**  The exact
   pair table is load-bearing but locally unsupported.  Add either a short
   intrinsic count for one representative of each of the six relations or a
   precise pointer to an auditable computation stating the complete support
   domain and deduplication rule.
2. **Convention mismatch / missing bridge (effect: all scheme imports).**
   The manuscript calls the \(\rho\)-levels the elliptic scheme without giving
   the source relabeling.  The correct conversion is
   \(\widehat\rho_{\rm HX}=9\rho\), with the six-row dictionary above.  Stating
   it would make the appeal to elliptic-scheme intersection numbers
   independently checkable and would prevent an erroneous literal matching
   of labels.
3. **Proof gap (effect: operator-field proposition, readily repairable).**
   The proof shows that the subalgebra generated by \(B=A_9\) acts as
   \(\mathbb F_8\), but the proposition claims that the image of the entire
   binary Bose--Mesner algebra is \(\mathbb F_8\).  It does not state why
   \(A_1\) and \(A_3\) add no operators.  Direct calculation gives
   \(A_1|_K=A_3|_K=0\); this fact, with its supporting intersection identity,
   should be inserted.
4. **Unexplained computation (effect: spanning and rigidity certificates).**
   The four Gram equalities, the full-rank elimination for \(B+I\), and the
   anchor uniqueness/injectivity assertions are exact and all checked claims
   available in this scope are correct, but they are presented as direct
   calculations without the actual representatives or compact count data.
   A small table would expose the trust boundary without lengthening the
   conceptual proof.

No false statement, exceptional-case failure, matrix-orientation error, or
polarity error was found.  The use of ordered-pair orbitals is compatible with
the source because generous transitivity makes the elliptic scheme symmetric.

## Novelty relative to the packet

Relative to Hollmann--Xiang's elliptic association scheme, the genuinely new
content is that minimum-codeword pair concurrence intrinsically reconstructs
all six orbitals and then turns three of their binary adjacency operators into
the Frobenius orbit of scalars of a hidden \(\mathbb F_8\)-structure on the
code.
