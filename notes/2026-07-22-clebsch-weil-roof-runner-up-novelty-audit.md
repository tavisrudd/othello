## 1. C459 — decorated descent and intrinsic resolvent

**Claimability:** very high.  **Novelty risk:** medium.

Classical substrate:

- the two degree-three characters of `A5` have field `Q(sqrt5)`;
- `Aut(A5)=S5`;
- for the outer `C2` action, cocycles correspond to the ten odd involutions in `S5`, forming one
  `A5`-orbit with fixed group `S3`; and
- Hilbert 90 and nonabelian `H^1` are standard.

The abstract calculation “ten cocycles, one outer-twisted form, fixed group `S3`” is not a novelty
crown.  The object-level synthesis is much more specific:

- uniqueness of the `Q(sqrt5)`-split form of the decorated golden six-arc;
- an explicit rational `S3` form with descended anisotropic conic and matching;
- three concurrent rational secants through the conjugate point-pairs;
- intrinsic recovery of `Spec Q(sqrt5)` as the `S3` quotient; and
- localization of the lost datum as a rational sheet section.

No predecessor was located within the recorded coverage for this exact descent/resolvent package.
The main remaining risk is older work on rational icosahedral configurations using neither
“decorated six-arc” nor current finite-geometry terminology.

Safe wording:

> For the frozen decorated golden six-arc, the outer `A5` descent calculation has a unique
> `Q(sqrt5)`-split class.  We exhibit its rational `S3` form and show that the three concurrent
> conjugate-pair secants recover the golden quadratic algebra intrinsically as the `S3` quotient.
> Thus descent preserves the unordered two-sheet object while forbidding a rational choice of
> sheet.

Do not advertise the abstract `H^1(C2,A5)` calculation as new.  Retain the proof audit's warning
that the `D5` boundary must be stated in its exact pointwise-rational-action sense unless twisted
forms are separately treated.

## 2. C457 — maximal-order reductions into the frozen sheets

**Claimability:** high.  **Novelty risk:** medium.

The icosian ring, its maximality, its binary-icosahedral units, the binary-octahedral coefficient
field `Q(sqrt2)`, exceptional quaternionic unit groups, and reduction of an unramified maximal
order to a full residue matrix algebra are classical.  Recent work also treats orders generated by
`2I` and `2O`; none of this should be claimed as new.

The paper-specific bridge is exact rather than abstract:

- the two primes above 11 and the two primes above 7 give the already frozen H3/B3 embeddings;
- explicit comparison matrices identify equal residue subgroups, not merely abstract groups of
  the same type; and
- one `O_5`-valued H3 comparison formula and one silver B3 formula coordinate the conjugate residue
  splittings.

No predecessor was located within the recorded coverage for these exact residue-to-frozen-sheet
identifications.  Older congruence-quotient literature may contain the primes 7 and 11 without the
matching-sheet language and remains a coverage gap.

Safe wording:

> Using the classical icosian and binary-octahedral maximal orders, we identify their reductions at
> the two primes above 11 and 7 with the four frozen H3/B3 sheet embeddings.  The comparison maps
> are reductions of single Galois-covariant integral formulas, so the sheets arise as residue
> realizations of one arithmetic spin object rather than unrelated finite subgroups.

## 3. C467 — golden signed-Fourier duality

**Claimability:** high.  **Novelty risk:** medium.

Local Fourier transforms are Clifford operations, character orthogonality carries an equal-phase
code state to its dual-code support, and local-Clifford equivalence of stabilizer states is
standard.  The new-looking content is the pencil identity

```text
H_(1-t) diag(-1,-1,-1,-1,1,1) H_t^T
    = diag(0,2(t^2-t-1),0),
```

and its exact consequences:

- uniform fixed-party Fourier duality of the golden pair over every odd split realization;
- the q=11 map with no party permutation and global phase `+1`;
- the complete fixed-party quotient `{2,6}`, `{3,4,7,8}`, `{10}`;
- agreement with the complete degree-six labeled invariant partition; and
- universal inversion symmetry `t <-> 1/t`.

No predecessor was located within the recorded coverage for this golden-pencil identity or the
q=11 fixed-party quotient.  Literature on six-point MDS/AME stabilizer equivalence and finite-field
perfect tensors was only weakly indexed in the returned results.

Safe wording:

> The standard Fourier/dual-code correspondence becomes unusually rigid on this pencil: a
> polynomial identity identifies the signed dual of `C_t` with `C_(1-t)` on the golden locus.  It
> yields a fixed-party local-Clifford equivalence of the two golden AME states over every odd split
> realization and, together with parameter inversion, gives the complete three-class q=11
> quotient.

The crown is the polynomial family identity and classification it induces, not the general fact
that Fourier transforms exchange code states with dual-code states.

## 4. C445 — strongest narrative near-miss

**Claimability:** high for the exact synthesis.  **Novelty risk:** medium-high.

Edge and Dye already own much of the surrounding q=11 geometry: two eleven-element systems, `A5`
stabilizers, `PSL/PGL` splitting, and outer exchange.  Standard `PSL_2(11)` subgroup theory supplies
the index-11 `A5` actions and much of the expected intersection/generation behavior.

The defensible refinement is the full frozen arithmetic seam:

- the golden reductions are the two exact singleton matchings;
- their stabilizers meet in the frozen `A4` and generate the frozen `PSL_2(11)`;
- one rational rotation `Rz` transports the characteristic-zero conjugates;
- its spinor norm and reduced determinant are exactly 2; and
- the common triangle realizes the `S4/A4` hinge whose outer coset swaps the sheets.

No predecessor was located within the recorded coverage for the entire rational-transporter,
spinor, and hinge package in these coordinates.  Dye was not reread line-by-line specifically
against the final C445 theorem during this pass.

Safe wording:

> Refining the classical q=11 `PSL/PGL` orbit split, the two golden reductions are the two
> `PSL_2(11)` halves of one `PGL_2(11)` orbit in our frozen matching model.  Their `A5` stabilizers
> meet in `A4` and generate `PSL_2(11)`, while the rational spinor-norm-2 rotation `Rz` reduces to
> the outer element in the common `S4/A4` hinge that exchanges them.

Credit Edge and Dye immediately.  Do not imply novelty for the 22-object orbit, its `11+11` split,
or generic `A5` subgroup facts.

## 6. C468 — exact local realization of classical architecture

**Claimability:** high as computed local arithmetic.  **Novelty risk:** high for structural
interpretation.

Roulleau records that the Klein cubic intermediate Jacobian is isomorphic as an abelian variety to
`E_11^5`, where `E_11` has CM by `Q(sqrt(-11))`; recent work also cites Adler's corresponding
description.  The order-five coordinate symmetry is classical, and work on cubic threefolds with
an order-five automorphism gives a one-dimensional plus four-dimensional decomposition.  Thus the
appearance of `Q(sqrt(-11))`, five CM copies, the `1+4` character split, and a rational quadratic
plus cyclotomic octic carrier is structurally expected.

The exact local package remains valuable:

- Frobenius polynomials at 31, 41, and 61;
- the order-11 Delsarte/Gauss calculation producing the live fifth trace at 31;
- the explicit irreducible quadratic-plus-octic factorization;
- the local norm formula over `Q(zeta5)`;
- independence from the mod-40 fusion character; and
- the all-extension recurrence and extremal degree-10 tower.

Safe wording:

> The classical description `J(X) ~ E_11^5`, together with the order-five symmetry, predicts the
> CM and `C5`-isotypic architecture.  Our contribution here is an exact good-prime realization:
> explicit Frobenius polynomials at 31, 41, and 61, an order-11 Gauss-product derivation at 31, and
> a quadratic-plus-octic norm factorization that makes the classical CM-times-`C5` structure
> visible in these local zeta functions.  These local factors are independent of the mod-40 fusion
> character.

Do not claim discovery of the CM field, `E_11^5`, or the basic fivefold decomposition.  Adler's
original paper was not obtained, and neither the field of definition of the isogeny nor prior
explicit Euler-factor formulas were settled.  Until those gaps close, even the exact local norm
decomposition is a computed realization rather than a priority claim.

## Positioning verdict

The runner-up tier is not weak; it is differently claimable.  C459, C457, and C467 contain exact,
object-specific syntheses that survived the bounded search and can be stated without competing
with their classical substrates.  C445 should remain prominent because it is structurally central
to Paper 1, but its novelty language must emphasize the rational transporter and frozen hinge, not
the ambient orbit split.  C460 is a useful exact geometric construction with terminology risk.
C468 should be presented as a deep exact local realization of classical CM-times-`C5`
architecture, not as discovery of that architecture.
