## Scope

This task closes the formal boundary left by the algebraic Golden proof spine

## Formal objects and statements

RelativeConicArcs.GoldenCubicNodesBase defines the centered five-coordinate
lift, the Golden cubic and its explicit gradient.  The theorem
cubic_eq_conference_triangleCubic identifies that cubic with
ClebschGoldenConference.triangleCubic for the fixed conference matrix.
derivative_coordinatePolynomial_eval proves that the five displayed
quadrics are its formal coordinate derivatives.

RelativeConicArcs.GoldenCubicNodeElimination is generated by
lean/scripts/generate_golden_cubic_elimination.py.  It contains exact
ideal-membership identities for the affine chart and for the hyperplane at
infinity.  Every identity is reproved by Lean's linear_combination tactic;
neither the generator's rational row reduction nor an external
computer-algebra system belongs to the trusted proof.

RelativeConicArcs.GoldenCubicNodes uses those identities to classify the
gradient-zero cone over every characteristic-zero field as the union of the
six lines spanned by the centered vectors \(1-6e_i\).  Its nonzero form is the
projective singular-point classification.  A separate scalar-injectivity
theorem records that the six rational labels are distinct.

RelativeConicArcs.GoldenCubicNodeHessians proves that its explicit chart
matrix is the second coordinate derivative matrix.  Its determinants at the
six normalized nodes are \(6480\) at five nodes and \(1296/5\) at the sixth,
so all are nondegenerate over characteristic zero.

RelativeConicArcs.GoldenCubicFrameCarrier proves that the six centered
vectors form a projective frame, models the ten squarefree cubic monomials,
and identifies double vanishing at the standard frame with the kernel of the
five-by-ten incidence matrix.  An explicit right inverse based on
\((3I_5+3J_5)^{-1}\) proves that this kernel has dimension five.  It also
proves value-and-gradient vanishing at all six frame points and identical
vanishing on all fifteen frame edges.

RelativeConicArcs.GoldenMatchingCarrier proves directly that the five
noncrossing centered matching forms are linearly independent and vanish to
second order at all six centered nodes.  This supplies the matching-side
dimension and containment used in the paper's complete-linear-system
identification.

## Trust boundary

The formal proof uses symbolic normalization, linear_combination, ordinary
finite case splits, and rational matrix algebra.  It imports no Singular
output, generated list of solutions, native decision procedure, or
task-specific axiom.  The generated elimination source is reproducible with:

    python3 lean/scripts/generate_golden_cubic_elimination.py --check

Lean is the independent checker: changing any generated multiplier while
preserving the claimed conclusion makes the corresponding polynomial identity
fail to elaborate.

The formalization classifies geometric singular points after every
characteristic-zero scalar extension and proves their nondegenerate local
quadratic forms.  It does not construct a primary decomposition object in
Lean, compute a Milnor algebra, or formalize Dolgachev's degree-six
determinantal theorem.  Those are not needed for the six-point and
ordinary-double-point conclusions once the characteristic-zero
classification and Hessian theorems are in place.

## Closeout and mystery ledger

The `ej`+`tt` closeout exposed two task-owned issues, both settled before the
gate.  First, affine normalization cannot identify the fifth exceptional node
with its displayed centered vector without retaining the scalar `-1/5`; the
classifier now returns an explicit scalar and the cone theorem composes it
with the chart scale.  Second, exhaustive differentiation of 150 matching
cases obscured the reason for second-order vanishing and exceeded the default
heartbeat budget.  The final proof evaluates coordinate brackets, applies the
three-factor product rule, and checks only which two original brackets vanish.

No genuine mathematical mystery remains inside the declared formal boundary.
Primary decomposition, the Milnor algebra, Dolgachev's determinantal Milnor
total, Specht multiplicity, global saturation, Luna slices, and Golden
normalization remain explicitly outside this gate; C757 and the manuscript
trust ledger continue to own those evidence boundaries.  The discovery-track
review found no incidental result: both closeout findings were needed to pass
the C758 acceptance gate.
