## Why these are separate tasks

C408 leaves one exact cheap signal and one materially larger construction problem.  Its
weighted-adjoint difference is

```text
(Q-7)x(x-1)^3(x+1),
```

so the first nonzero depth moment is cubic.  Understanding why that happens is a
structural theorem problem.  Cancelling the entire defect while retaining a pointed
difference is instead a witness-and-replay problem.  Combining them would blur the stop
rules and encourage an unbounded census.

## C409 — cubic first-memory principle and trade filtration

### Question

Do C403's scalar line-depth moment identity, C406's signed symmetric-tensor moments,
and C408's depth-difference polynomial instantiate one convention-independent
incidence-trade filtration in which balanced data through order two force the first
possible realization-sensitive obstruction to occur in degree three?

### Gate

1. Normalize the three mechanisms explicitly.  Record their ground sets, weights,
   moment conventions, and what “balanced through degree two” means before comparing
   them.
2. Express the C408 switch as a signed incidence trade and prove the factor
   `x(x-1)^3(x+1)` directly from its local ledger.
3. Test one fixed elementary family of projective/design trades.  Continue only if a
   general lemma predicts cubic first survival; three parallel examples are not a
   theorem.
4. Audit primary literature on design trades, harmonic/design moments, matroid
   switching, and arrangement incidence switches before any novelty or priority
   wording.

### Stop rule and deliverable

Stop with an exact comparison table if the commonality depends on incompatible
normalizations, or if elementary balanced trades can first survive in another degree.
A positive deliverable must state hypotheses that recover all three mechanisms or
clearly isolate a narrower new theorem.  No broad census is authorized.

### Outcome

C409 proves that all three mechanisms are exact-strength-two signed-feature trades:
their symmetric-tensor moments vanish through degree two and first survive cubically.
Affine coordinate changes preserve the first surviving tensor, the scalar specialization
is the `(x-1)`-adic filtration, and Pasch trades with a common core prove sharpness.  The
normalization is classical/formal rather than a new flagship.  Its free binomial-moment
corollary identifies the complete scalar depth tower with the aggregated collinear-subset
spectrum.  See `notes/2026-07-20-c409-cubic-first-memory-principle.md`.

## C410 — same-scalar-tower pointed collision by balanced composition

### Question

Can two arrangement complements have the same original characteristic polynomial and
the same universal weighted-adjoint depth polynomial—hence the same scalar-extension
enumerator tower—while differing in a puncture deck, coordinate repair profile, or
syndrome-multiplicity profile?

### Gate

1. Start from the exact C408 local defect vector
   `(Q-7)(-x+2x^2-2x^4+x^5)`; do not restart with raw arrangement enumeration.
2. Work first in the bounded signed local-trade module.  Let `U` record the original
   characteristic and universal weighted-adjoint depth defects, and let `P` record the
   chosen pointed puncture, repair, or `mu` defect.  Seek

   ```text
   Delta in ker(U) but Delta notin ker(P),
   ```

   then impose geometric realizability, nonnegative multiplicities, and essentiality.
   These last constraints are not linear and must not be inferred from a formal kernel
   vector.
3. Split the composition attack into two exact cases.

   - **Separable/additive backgrounds:** if a local defect is `F(Q,x)` and the background
     depth enumerator is `B(Q,x)`, the global defect is `F B`.  Two opposite copies in
     backgrounds `B_1,B_2` cancel iff `F(B_1-B_2)=0`, hence iff `B_1=B_2` in the domain
     `Z[Q,x]`.  Search for pointed-inequivalent backgrounds with the same unpointed
     background polynomial; different constant depth shifts cannot cancel.
   - **Nonseparable backgrounds:** retain the explicit cross-incidence correction and
     require it to cancel coefficientwise.  A construction that merely assumes product
     factorization does not pass.

4. Use the C408 scalar ledger also as the ideal degree-two equal-power-sum pair

   ```text
   {2,2,5}  versus  {1,4,4}.
   ```

   Parametric degree-two Prouhet--Tarry--Escott solutions are arithmetic prior art, not a
   novelty target.  They may supply a bounded alternative family of local depth ledgers
   only after freezing the admissible depth window and incidence-realizability conditions;
   do not expand this into an unrestricted Diophantine or arrangement census.
5. Retain only candidates whose pointed puncture, repair, or `mu` profile differs.
6. Freeze the first structurally natural field/size bound before computation.  A
   positive witness requires a deterministic checker, an independent replay, JSON, and
   checksums under the repository evidence contract.

### Stop rule and dependencies

Stop at the frozen bound if defect cancellation also forces equality of every tested
pointed profile.  Do not expand into random or unbounded canonical-code search.  C410

The linear-kernel and separable-factorization reductions are attack tools, not existence
