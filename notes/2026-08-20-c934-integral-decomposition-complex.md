# C934: integral decomposition complex of the cubic-threefold theta divisor

**Date:** 2026-08-20

**Status:** structural derived theorem proved.  The resolution complex splits
off its two outer point summands over `Z`; its central perverse summand carries
the sole nonunit local intersection block, at the prime three.

## The theorem

Let

\[
 \sigma:M=\operatorname{Bl}_0\Theta\longrightarrow\Theta,
 \qquad \mathcal K=R\sigma_*\mathbf Z_M[4],
\]

and let `i:{0}->Theta`, `j:U->Theta` be the two strata.  There is an
integral decomposition

\[
 \mathcal K\simeq \mathbf Z_0[2]\oplus\mathcal P
                    \oplus\mathbf Z_0[-2],
\tag{1}
\]

where `P` is a perverse sheaf extending `Z_U[4]`.  Its central attachment is
the exact sequence

\[
 H^3(K,\mathbf Z)\longrightarrow
 A=\mathbf Z\xrightarrow{-3}B=\mathbf Z
 \longrightarrow H^4(K,\mathbf Z),
\tag{2}
\]

in which the first map is zero and the last sends `1` to the torsion class
`tau=p^*ell` of order three.

After extension of scalars to `Z[1/3]`,

\[
 \mathcal P\otimes^L\mathbf Z[1/3]
 \simeq IC_\Theta(\mathbf Z[1/3])\oplus\mathbf Z[1/3]_0,
\]

and hence

\[
 R\sigma_*\mathbf Z[1/3]_M[4]
 \simeq IC_\Theta(\mathbf Z[1/3])\oplus
 \mathbf Z[1/3]_0[2]\oplus\mathbf Z[1/3]_0
 \oplus\mathbf Z[1/3]_0[-2].
\tag{3}
\]

The same formula follows over any field of characteristic different from
three.  Over `Z`, the central point splitting in (3) does not exist.  Thus
three is the unique prime detected by the local intersection matrices as an
obstruction to Kraemer's rational point-summand formula.

## Proof

### 1. Stalk, costalk, and attachment

Proper base change and Thom excision give

\[
 i^*\mathcal K\simeq R\Gamma(X,\mathbf Z)[4],\qquad
 i^!\mathcal K\simeq R\Gamma(X,\mathbf Z)[2].
\]

The natural map `i^! K -> i^* K` is cup product by the Euler class
`-h` of the normal line bundle.  Its algebraic blocks are

\[
 H^0(X)\xrightarrow{-h}H^2(X),\quad
 H^2(X)\xrightarrow{-h}H^4(X),\quad
 H^4(X)\xrightarrow{-h}H^6(X),
\]

which, in the integral generators `1,h,ell,[pt]`, are respectively

\[
 [-1],\qquad[-3],\qquad[-1].
\tag{4}
\]

The odd block is zero and is paired with the free link groups described in
the first C934 report.

### 2. Splitting the outer blocks

Here is the integral splitting argument.  Adjunction identifies maps from and
to `Z_0[m]` with the corresponding Borel--Moore classes of the fibre.  The
composition pairing is the fibre intersection form.  For `m=2` its matrix is
the first unit block in (4), and for `m=-2` its matrix is the last unit block.
Choose the fundamental and point classes with the displayed signs.  The
resulting inclusion and projection compose to the identity, so the elementary
idempotent-splitting argument gives `Z_0[2]` and `Z_0[-2]` as actual integral
direct summands.  The two constructions occupy different cohomological
degrees and their cross-compositions vanish.  Removing them leaves an object
`P` with

\[
 j^*\mathcal P=\mathbf Z_U[4],
\]

whose stalk has cohomology only in degrees `-4,-1,0` and whose costalk has
cohomology only in degrees `0,1,4`.  These are exactly the support and
cosupport bounds for a perverse sheaf on the fourfold with its isolated
point stratum.  This proves (1).

### 3. The central zig-zag

For `P`, the degree-zero costalk and stalk are

\[
 A=H^2(X,\mathbf Z)=\mathbf Z h,qquad
 B=H^4(X,\mathbf Z)=\mathbf Z\ell.
\]

The map `A->B` is `h -> -h^2=-3ell`.  The cohomology sequence of the
disk bundle and its boundary is

\[
 H^3(X)\xrightarrow{\sim}H^3(K)\longrightarrow
 H^2(X)\xrightarrow{-h}H^4(X)\longrightarrow H^4(K).
\]

It follows that the incoming arrow in (2) is zero and that the outgoing
arrow identifies `B/3A` with the torsion subgroup generated by `tau`.  This
is precisely (2).

### 4. Inverting three and the obstruction over `Z`

Over `Z[1/3]`, the middle arrow of (2) is an isomorphism.  The same
inclusion--projection construction splits a central point perverse sheaf from
`P`.  The complement restricts to `Z[1/3]_U[4]` and has neither a point
subobject nor a point quotient, so it is the intermediate extension
`IC_Theta(Z[1/3])`.  This gives (3).  The argument survives over every field
of characteristic different from three.

Over `Z`, the middle attachment map has Smith factor `3`, not a unit.  A
central point summand would contribute an identity block `Z -> Z`.  Since
both the degree-zero costalk and stalk have rank one, that would force the
whole map to be a unit, a contradiction.  Hence no integral central point
summand exists.  Since the two outer blocks are units, this is the sole
nonunit local intersection block.

## Relative Lefschetz warning

The two outer point objects split as objects, but relative multiplication by
`h^2` between them is multiplication by three:

\[
 H^0(X,\mathbf Z)\xrightarrow{h^2}H^4(X,\mathbf Z),
 \qquad 1\longmapsto3\ell.
\]

Thus integral relative hard Lefschetz also fails exactly at three.  This is
the same coefficient defect as (2), seen across perverse degrees rather than
inside the central perverse sheaf.  It must not be described as a second,
independent torsion phenomenon.

## Ordinary versus intersection complex

Normality gives a natural map

\[
 \mathbf Z_\Theta[4]\longrightarrow IC_\Theta(\mathbf Z).
\]

Its cone is the point object `H^3(K,Z)_0[1]`.  Indeed, in the unshifted
Deligne model

\[
 IC_\Theta(\mathbf Z)[-4]
   =\tau_{\le3}Rj_*\mathbf Z_U,
\]

the constant stalk has only
the degree `-4` copy of `Z`, while the intersection complex also retains the
degree `-1` copy of `H^3(K)=Z^10`.  Consequently ordinary and intersection
cohomology can differ abstractly only in the degree-three/four connecting
segment.  C928's surjectivity onto `H^3(K)` makes that connecting morphism
zero, so

\[
 H^k(\Theta,\mathbf Z)\xrightarrow{\sim}IH^k(\Theta,\mathbf Z)
 \quad(k\ge4),
\tag{5}
\]

while degree three has the already proved rank-ten extension.

Equation (5) is an isomorphism statement about the groups.  It does not make
the resolution complex split: the central obstruction (2) records how these
groups attach to the singular point and to the resolution.

## Consequences already fixed

1. The rational three-skyscraper formula is the localization of (1)--(2) at
   three.
2. In characteristic three the Gysin sequence gives
   `dim H^3(K,F_3)=dim H^4(K,F_3)=11`.  The central attachment becomes

   \[
    \mathbf F_3^{11}\twoheadrightarrow\mathbf F_3
      \xrightarrow{0}\mathbf F_3
      \hookrightarrow\mathbf F_3^{11}.
   \]

   The extra degree-three class is the Tor/Bockstein companion of `tau`; it
   is why one must derive base change rather than merely reduce (2).  The
   zero middle form rules out a central point summand.  Since the restriction
   to `U` is the simple rank-one local system, the residual perverse sheaf is
   indecomposable and nonsemisimple.  Thus the rational decomposition has no
   characteristic-three specialization.
3. The local order-three class is killed globally by the point-class Fano
   lift `u_4` of the first C934 report; it therefore measures an index-three
   attachment, not torsion in the boundary image.
4. The paper's mod-two degree-three fibre product and the present mod-three
   perverse attachment are distinct parts of one integral resolution: the
   former is global Lefschetz placement, the latter local decomposition
   failure.

## Remaining global gate

The derived theorem no longer depends on an all-degree Betti calculation.
For the manuscript-strength global corollary, C934 still needs to record the
integral ranks and torsion status of `H^4(Theta)=IH^4`, `H^4(U)`, and their
duals, and to print the exact index-three maps.  The local class and its fate
are already settled.

## Priority boundary

This report uses the rational fourfold perverse decomposition framework of
de Cataldo--Migliorini and the rational cubic-theta specialization of
Kraemer.  Juteau--Mautner--Williamson, Proposition 3.2 and Corollary 3.5,
identify point-summand multiplicities and the costalk-to-stalk map with the
fibre intersection forms over a field; this exactly corroborates the
characteristic-three rank drop, but does not print this cubic-theta example
or an integral splitting.  MacPherson--Vilonen's zig-zag formalism is used as
language for (2), not as an unverified equivalence over `Z`.  No novelty claim
is licensed until the remaining C934 priority pass is complete.
