# C921 — the pencil's forced level structure, and its Eckardt locus

**Lane:** `cubic-threefolds` · **Date:** 2026-08-19 · **Task:** C921

## Results

Two independent questions about the nonstandard `A_5`-cubic pencil, settled
against the explicit rational model — the first by point counts over finite
fields, the second by elimination over `Q`.

1. **The two level-structure predictions of
   `2026-08-19-c921-integral-glued-model.md` are confirmed.** Hartlieb's elliptic
   factor has square discriminant and carries a rational three-isogeny, over 112
   smooth members across five finite fields, with no violation. This is an
   independent confirmation of the epilogue's principal gluing packet
   proposition, arrived at from the equations rather than from the lattice.
2. **The Eckardt locus of the pencil is not empty, and is now determined
   exactly, by elimination over `Q`.** The smooth members carrying an Eckardt
   point are exactly the conjugate pair `b = 3ω` and `b = 3ω̄`, the roots of
   `b^2 + 3b + 9`, each carrying thirty Eckardt points — the Fermat cubic
   threefold's count. These are the two members that also admit the permutation
   action of `A_5`.
3. **The pencil's singular locus is determined too**, as a by-product of the same
   elimination: exactly six members, `b = 0` (the Segre cubic), `b = -6`, the two
   roots of `b^2 - 3b - 9` in `Q(sqrt 5)`, and the two roots of `7b^2 + 3b + 9` in
   `Q(sqrt -3)`. The previous report could only bound this.

The second result is negative for the upgrade it was meant to support, and the
negative is the useful part. The qualifier "all but finitely many" in the
manuscript's separation theorem **cannot** be removed, because the exceptional set
genuinely contains the Fermat members, which are of separated-variable type.
What replaces the hoped-for removal is better than a bound: the exceptional set is
now named exactly. `thm:separation-family` can say "every point of its
coarse-moduli image except the Fermat point" in place of "all but finitely many".

## 1. Why the Eckardt locus was the target

The epilogue's two finiteness propositions reduce to one object.
`prop:A5-not-coprime` ("Finite Eckardt locus") proves that only finitely many
members carry an Eckardt point, by a soft argument: the locus is closed, the
projection is proper, the base is an irreducible curve, and one computed member
has none. `prop:A5-nonseparated` ("Finite separated-variable intersection") is a
corollary of it through `lem:eckardt-rank`, since a smooth cubic threefold of
separated-variable type carries an Eckardt point. And `prop:A5-nonseparated` is
what puts "all but finitely many" into `thm:separation-family`, the manuscript's
separation theorem.

So emptiness of the Eckardt locus on the smooth part of the pencil would have
removed the qualifier from a theorem statement. It is not empty.

## 2. What the sweep found

For every prime in `{11, 19, 29, 31, 41}` and every member of the pencil over
that field, the script tests each point of `P^4(F_q)` on the member for the
manuscript's own Eckardt criterion, rank of the Hessian at most two, applied as a
cascade of the hundred three-by-three minors.

    q = 19    Eckardt at b = 2, 14        thirty points each
    q = 31    Eckardt at b = 13, 15       thirty points each
    q = 11, 29, 41                        none

`2, 14` are the roots of `b^2 + 3b + 9` modulo 19 and `13, 15` are its roots
modulo 31; the polynomial is irreducible modulo 11, 29 and 41. Its roots are
`b = 3ω` for `ω` a primitive cube root of unity, so the Eckardt members are
`F_q`-rational exactly when `q ≡ 1 mod 3`, which is precisely the observed
pattern. Both facts are checked in the script rather than inferred.

**The Fermat control.** The same detector run on `x_1^3 + ... + x_5^3` finds
thirty Eckardt points when `q ≡ 1 mod 3` and ten when `q ≡ 2 mod 3` — the
Eckardt points of the Fermat cubic threefold are the thirty points with two
nonzero coordinates in ratio a cube root of `-1`, of which ten are rational when
the cube roots of unity are not. The two Eckardt members of the pencil track that
count exactly. Together with the manuscript's own statement that the pencil
contains the Fermat cubic threefold as one of the two members admitting the
permutation action of `A_5`, this identifies them.

**New concrete datum.** In the rational model the Fermat members sit at
`b = 3ω, 3ω̄`. The manuscript exhibits the Fermat member only in the monomial
model, where the cube roots of unity are in the matrix entries.

## 3. The elimination over `Q`

The sweep sees `F_q`-rational Eckardt points on `F_q`-rational members. To make
the statement geometric, the ideal generated by `F_b` together with the hundred
three-by-three minors of its Hessian, in `Q[y_1..y_5, b]`, is saturated by the
irrelevant ideal `<y_1..y_5>` — the origin satisfies every generator and has to
be removed, which is what makes the statement projective — and eliminated down
to `b`. The same is done for `<dF/dy_1, ..., dF/dy_5>`, whose elimination is the
pencil's discriminant. Both run in Singular over `Q`.

    singular locus   b (b + 6) (b^2 - 3b - 9) (7b^2 + 3b + 9)
    Eckardt locus    b (b^2 + 3b + 9)

each factor with multiplicity one. The two eliminations run in the chart `a = 1`,
so the remaining pencil point `(a:b) = (0:1)`, the cubic `T_1 = 0`, is settled
separately: its singular locus and its Eckardt scheme are both empty, so it is a
smooth member with no Eckardt point.

Reading these off:

- **The singular members are exactly six**: `b = 0`, which is the Segre cubic and
  has ten nodes; `b = -6`; the two roots of `b^2 - 3b - 9`, which lie in
  `Q(sqrt 5)`; and the two roots of `7b^2 + 3b + 9`, whose discriminant is
  `-243 = -3^5`, so they lie in `Q(sqrt -3)`.
- **The Eckardt locus is `b = 0` together with the roots of `b^2 + 3b + 9`.** But
  `b = 0` is the Segre cubic, which is singular, and `lem:eckardt-rank` — the
  equivalence between the rank condition and being an Eckardt point — is a
  statement about smooth cubic threefolds. So on the smooth locus of the pencil
  the Eckardt locus is exactly the two roots of `b^2 + 3b + 9`.

**Cross-checks.** The same eliminations over `F_32003` give the identical Eckardt
polynomial and a discriminant with the same factor shape; regenerate with
`--field 32003`. More usefully, the numpy sweep and the elimination are
independent computations that agree completely: the sweep script now gates on the
elimination's discriminant, and at all five primes the members it found singular
are exactly the roots of `b (b+6) (b^2-3b-9) (7b^2+3b+9)`. That reproduces the
observed six-versus-four pattern from the arithmetic of the factors — `sqrt 5`
lies in all five fields, while `sqrt -3` lies in `F_q` only for `q ≡ 1 mod 3` —
and it is the same mechanism that makes the Eckardt pair rational exactly when
`q ≡ 1 mod 3`, since `b^2 + 3b + 9` has discriminant `-27`.

**Identification with the Fermat members.** The Fermat cubic threefold is smooth
and carries Eckardt points, and by the manuscript's own Remark the pencil contains
it. So the Fermat member is one of the two roots of `b^2 + 3b + 9`, and its Galois
conjugate is the other. Both are therefore projectively equivalent to the Fermat
cubic over `C`, being Galois conjugates of a form defined over `Q`, so **the two
Eckardt members map to a single point of the coarse-moduli image `M_{H_1}`.**

## 4. What this means for the manuscript

The direction I had hoped for is closed. Neither `prop:A5-not-coprime` nor
`prop:A5-nonseparated` can be strengthened to "no member", and
`thm:separation-family` keeps its qualifier. That is not a defect in the
manuscript; the manuscript is right as written, and the reason is structural
rather than accidental — the Fermat member is a member of the pencil, it is of
separated-variable type as literally as possible, and it is exactly where the
Colliot-Thélène criterion does apply. The remark on the Fermat member already
says as much from the other side.

The sharpening that is available is exact, and no longer conditional on anything.

- `prop:A5-not-coprime` currently says only finitely many points of `M_{H_1}` are
  represented by smooth cubic threefolds carrying an Eckardt point. It can say:
  exactly two members of the pencil carry one, the conjugate pair `b = 3ω`, and
  they represent a single point of `M_{H_1}`, the Fermat point.
- `prop:A5-nonseparated` can say the same, with equality rather than containment.
  Separated-variable implies Eckardt by `lem:eckardt-rank`, so its locus is
  contained in the Eckardt locus; and the Fermat form is separated-variable as
  literally as a cubic can be, a sum of five cubics in one variable each. So the
  two loci coincide.
- `thm:separation-family` can replace "all but finitely many points of its
  coarse-moduli image" with "every point of its coarse-moduli image except the
  Fermat point".

That converts two soft finiteness statements into an exact description, and it
makes `cor:fermat-separation` and the general separation statement the two halves
of one dichotomy over the pencil rather than a corollary and a generic statement
with an unquantified exceptional set.

No manuscript edit is made here. This is a research record; adopting it is the
user's call.

## 5. The level-structure tests

The gluing packet proposition says the relative kernel `K_p` is a section of the
local system of `A_5`-stable maximal isotropic halves. As derived in
`2026-08-19-c921-integral-glued-model.md`, that forces the mod-two monodromy of
`E_b` into the order-three subgroup of `GL_2(F_2)`, equivalently square
discriminant, and forces a monodromy-invariant line in `E_b[3]`, equivalently a
rational three-isogeny.

**Recovering the elliptic factor from point counts.** `A_5` acts by coordinate
permutations, hence over the prime field, so the isotypic decomposition
`H^3(X_b) = W_5 ⊗ H^1(E_b)(-1)` is Galois-equivariant with `W_5` carrying the
trivial action. For a smooth member,

    #X_b(F_q) = q^3 + q^2 + q + 1 - 5 q a_b,      a_b = tr(Frob | H^1(E_b)).

That `5q` divides the deficit on every smooth member, over all five fields, is a
nontrivial confirmation of the decomposition itself, not just a way to read off
`a_b`. It held without exception, as did the Weil bound `|a_b| <= 2 sqrt(q)`.

**Test at two.** If Frobenius acted on `E_b[2]` with order two there would be
exactly one rational point of order two, forcing `#E_b(F_q) ≡ 2 mod 4`. No smooth
member over any of the five fields has that residue. The test is invariant under
quadratic twist, since `(q+1-a) + (q+1+a)` is divisible by four for odd `q`, so
the sign ambiguity in `a_b` does not affect it.

**Test at three.** Frobenius fixes a line in `E_b[3]` exactly when `x^2 - ax + q`
has a root modulo three, that is when `3` does not divide `a_b` for `q ≡ 1 mod 3`
and does divide it for `q ≡ 2 mod 3`. Every smooth member satisfies the relevant
condition. This is also twist-invariant.

The three-adic test is the sharper of the two. Over `F_41` all thirty-eight
smooth members have `3 | a_b`; for curves without a rational three-isogeny that
would be a one-in-three coincidence repeated thirty-eight times.

**The singular members.** The sweep records six singular members when
`q ≡ 1 mod 3` and four when `q ≡ 2 mod 3`, always including `b = 0`. Section 3
explains that exactly, and the sweep now gates on it.

## 6. What is and is not proved

The Eckardt and singular loci are determined by elimination over `Q`, so they are
geometric statements, not statements about `F_q`-rational points. What they rest
on is the correctness of a Gröbner basis computation in Singular. Three things
reduce that risk: the same eliminations over `F_32003` give the same Eckardt
polynomial and the same discriminant factor shape; the numpy sweep is an
independent implementation over five fields, sharing no code with the elimination,
and it agrees on both loci; and the Eckardt count of thirty at each Eckardt member
matches the Fermat control computed by the same detector.

The identification of the two Eckardt members with the Fermat members uses the
manuscript's own Remark that the pencil contains the Fermat cubic threefold. That
direction is not re-proved here.

The two level-structure predictions are confirmed rather than proved.
They are consequences of the gluing packet proposition, so confirmation is a
consistency check on that proposition; the value was in the possibility of a
disagreement, which would have been a defect in a manuscript proposition. There
was none.

## Mystery ledger

- **Why the Eckardt locus is exactly the Fermat pair and nothing else.** Settled
  by the elimination, and the multiplicities settle the residual worry: every
  factor of both eliminated ideals appears to multiplicity one, so the loci are
  reduced. What the computation does not explain is *why* the Eckardt condition,
  which is a hundred equations on a curve and could easily have been empty, cuts
  out exactly the locus of extra symmetry. The mechanism is presumably that the
  Fermat members are the ones with a larger automorphism group, and Eckardt
  points are where that shows up; nothing here proves that is the only source.
- **`7b^2 + 3b + 9` and `b^2 + 3b + 9`.** The singular locus and the Eckardt locus
  each contribute one conjugate pair over `Q(sqrt -3)`, through quadratics that
  agree in two of three coefficients. Both discriminants are `-3` times a square,
  which is what the sweep sees as the shared six-versus-four and
  Eckardt-or-not switch at `q mod 3`. Whether the near-agreement of the
  coefficients is more than that is unexplained, and nothing depends on it.
- **Why the level-structure predictions came out so cleanly.** Settled. The
  three-adic condition is forced by a monodromy-invariant line in `E_b[3]`, and
  the data shows it with no near misses. Nothing here is surprising once the
  gluing packet proposition is granted; the point of the test was that a
  disagreement would have been serious, and there was none.
- **The geometric singular locus of the pencil.** Settled, and it was open in the
  previous report: exactly six members, `b = 0`, `b = -6`, the `Q(sqrt 5)` pair
  and the `Q(sqrt -3)` pair, each to multiplicity one in the eliminated ideal.
  This is the input any future degeneration analysis of the Schottky pullback
  needs, and it is now available.
- **The upgrade this pass was aimed at is closed negative, for a structural
  reason.** The separation theorem's qualifier is necessary because the Fermat
  member is in the pencil and is of separated-variable type. No mystery remains
  about why the qualifier is there.
