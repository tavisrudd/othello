## Outcome

C682's twelve embedded operator lines canonically determine the remaining
fifteen lines on the Clebsch cubic.  The construction is intrinsic to the
operator/apolar output:

1. the twelve lines impose rank \(19\) on the \(20\)-dimensional space of
   cubic forms on \(\mathbf P(V_F)\), so they determine the Clebsch cubic
   equation up to scalar;
2. for \(i\ne j\), the intersecting lines \(E_i,E'_j\) span a plane whose
   cubic section is
   \[
   E_i+E'_j+L_{ij};
   \]
3. the residual line obtained from \((E_i,E'_j)\) agrees exactly with the
   residual line obtained from \((E_j,E'_i)\).

Thus the operator data give fifteen canonically labelled lines
\(L_{ij}=L_{ji}\).  Their complete incidence is
\[
\begin{aligned}
E_i\cdot E_j&=E'_i\cdot E'_j=0 &&(i\ne j),\\
E_i\cdot E'_j&=1 &&(i\ne j),\\
E_k\cdot L_{ij}&=E'_k\cdot L_{ij}=1
   &&\Longleftrightarrow k\in\{i,j\},\\
L_{ij}\cdot L_{k\ell}&=1
   &&\Longleftrightarrow \{i,j\}\cap\{k,\ell\}=\varnothing .
\end{aligned}
\]
Every line has ten neighbors; the graph has \(27\) vertices and \(135\)
edges.  Its \(45\) tritangent planes split into
\[
30\{E_i,E'_j,L_{ij}\}\quad(i\ne j)
\qquad\text{and}\qquad
15\{L_{ij},L_{k\ell},L_{mn}\},
\]
where the latter triples are the partitions of \(\{1,\ldots,6\}\) into
three pairs.

## Exact minuscule dictionary

Let \(A\) have basis \(a_+,a_-\) and let \(U\) have basis
\(u_1,\ldots,u_6\).  In Manivel's convention the binary model is
\[
J=(A\otimes U)\oplus\bigwedge\nolimits^4U.
\]
After dualizing \(U\) and using a volume form, this is the convention in
the task card,
\[
J=(A\otimes U^\vee)\oplus\bigwedge\nolimits^2U.
\]
The explicit weight-label isomorphism is
\[
E_i\longleftrightarrow a_+\otimes u_i^\vee,\qquad
E'_i\longleftrightarrow a_-\otimes u_i^\vee,\qquad
L_{ij}\longleftrightarrow u_i\wedge u_j.
\]
Under this dictionary, the two kinds of tritangent plane are exactly the
two kinds of monomial in the restricted Cartan cubic:
\[
\sum_{i<j}(x_i y_j-x_j y_i)\omega_{ij}
 \;+\;\operatorname{Pf}(\omega).
\]
The first summand supplies the thirty ordered cross-row tritangents and
the Pfaffian supplies the fifteen perfect-matching tritangents.  This
proves the full weight-configuration and cubic-support identification,
not merely the dimension equality \(12+15=27\).

There is one deliberate boundary for C697: incidence determines the
Cartan cubic's signed monomial support only after choices of weight-vector
scales.  C695 constructs the weight carrier and its tritangent support; it
does not yet identify a canonical signed Cartan tensor directly in the
operator lines.

## Row exchange and conjugations

The permutation
\[
E_i\leftrightarrow E'_i,\qquad L_{ij}\mapsto L_{ij}
\]
is exactly the Weyl reflection of the \(A_1\) factor in
\(A_1\times A_5\subset E_6\).  A representative exchanges
\(a_+\) and \(a_-\), up to the usual sign, and acts trivially on
\(\bigwedge^2U\).  This is an action on the marking/Picard configuration;
it need not be induced by a projective automorphism of the fixed Clebsch
surface.

It is not the outer automorphism of \(E_6\): the latter exchanges the
minuscule \(27\) with \(27^\vee\), whereas the row reflection lies in the
Weyl group and acts inside this restricted carrier.

It is also not Galois conjugation.  The exact
\(\mathbf Q(\zeta_5)\) formulas carry the intrinsic action
\[
q_\infty\mapsto q_\infty,\qquad q_b\mapsto q_{b^a}
\quad(a\in(\mathbf Z/5)^\times),
\]
and residual formation gives
\(L_{ij}\mapsto L_{\sigma(i)\sigma(j)}\).  Every Galois element preserves
each row separately.  Complex conjugation, which fixes
\(\mathbf Q(\sqrt5)\), and the nontrivial automorphism of
\(\mathbf Q(\sqrt5)/\mathbf Q\) therefore both differ from row exchange.
The exact tower
\[
\mathbf Q\subset\mathbf Q(\sqrt5)\subset\mathbf Q(\zeta_5)
\]
is the field-of-definition tower of the displayed marking, not a trace
field or monodromy invariant.
