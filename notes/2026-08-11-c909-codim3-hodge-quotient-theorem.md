# C909 — exact codimension-three Hodge/product quotient for a one-depth etale graph

Date: 2026-08-11

Status: theorem-grade local computation, conditional only on the explicitly
recorded six-slot symbolic-minor certificate cited in §4.  No manuscript,
PDF, mirror, Lean, or commit change.

## Theorem

Let `R` be a DVR with uniformizer `p`, put `lambda=p^a` with `a>0`, and
consider a one-depth, polarized finite-etale graph quotient isogenous to
`E^g`, where `E` is non-CM.  Suppose that after a finite unramified extension
`O/R` the slope is diagonal with residual roots

\[
 t_1,\ldots,t_g,\qquad \delta_{ij}=t_j-t_i\in O^\times\quad(i\ne j).
\tag{1}
\]

Let

\[
 I_A^3=\operatorname{Hdg}^{6}(A,R),\qquad
 P_A^3=\operatorname{im}\bigl(\operatorname{Sym}^3\operatorname{NS}(A)
 \longrightarrow H^6(A,R)\bigr).
\tag{2}
\]

Then the `p`-primary local quotient is

\[
 \boxed{\displaystyle
 I_A^3/P_A^3\simeq
 (R/(p^a))^{5\binom g5+3\binom g6}
 \ \oplus\
 (R/(p^{2a}))^{\binom g6}.}
\tag{3}
\]

Over the splitting ring the same display holds with `R` replaced by `O`.
The individual split-support summands are not canonical before descent, but
the invariant-factor multiset, and hence (3), is.  This statement includes
`p=2`.

The theorem concerns **ordinary divisor products inside the ambient integral
Hodge lattice**.  It is separate from, and compatible with, the C909 result
that the divided-power envelope of the NS lattice has no further
finite-etale defect.

## 1. Split graph lattice and multidegrees

Use the integral pullback basis

\[
 X_i=\lambda x_i,\qquad Y_i=y_i-t_i x_i,
\tag{4}
\]

and contractions

\[
 \Omega_{ii}=x_i\wedge y_i,\qquad
 \Omega_{ij}=x_i\wedge y_j+x_j\wedge y_i.
\tag{5}
\]

The primitive divisor lattice is

\[
 D_i=\lambda\Omega_{ii}=X_iY_i,qquad
 C_{ij}=\lambda^2\Omega_{ij}\quad(i<j),
\tag{6}
\]

with literal expansion

\[
 C_{ij}=\delta_{ij}X_iX_j+
 \lambda(X_iY_j+X_jY_i).
\tag{7}
\]

No division by two occurs in (6)--(7).  If the original unit form on root
lines has coefficients `b_i`, they only multiply displayed contractions by
units; the determinant assertions below are correspondingly multiplied by
units.  In particular, no dyadic square-root rescaling is being used.

The diagonal coefficient torus splits rational Hodge tensors in degree six
by the occurrence vector of the root slots.  The only types are

\[
 (2,2,2),\quad (2,2,1,1),\quad (2,1,1,1,1),\quad(1,1,1,1,1,1).
\tag{8}
\]

Both the Hodge lattice and the product lattice are the direct sums of their
intersections with these multidegrees.  Thus it is enough to compute one
fixed support of each type.

## 2. Types `(2,2,2)` and `(2,2,1,1)`

The type `(2,2,2)` is one-dimensional, generated by
`\Omega_{ii}\Omega_{jj}\Omega_{kk}`.  Its exact integral scale is
`\lambda^3`, and it is generated by `D_iD_jD_k`; it has no quotient.

For a support `(2,2,1,1)`, the invariant space is likewise
one-dimensional.  The contraction identity, with its exterior sign,

\[
 \Omega_{ij}\Omega_{ik}=\mathord\pm\Omega_{ii}\Omega_{jk},
\tag{9}
\]

reduces every matching to
`\Omega_{ii}\Omega_{jj}\Omega_{kl}`.  Its all-`X` coefficient is a unit
times `\lambda^{-4}`, so its exact integral scale is `\lambda^4`, generated
by `D_iD_jC_{kl}`.  This is an integral identity with coefficient `\pm1`,
including in characteristic two, and proves saturation of the first two
types.

## 3. Type `(2,1,1,1,1)`

Fix a five-set `S` and designate its doubled slot `i`.  Repeated-contraction
straightening (9) identifies its invariant lattice, compatibly with the
weighted graph lattice, with

\[
 D_i\cdot \bigl[\text{the four-slot matching lattice on }S\setminus\{i\}\bigr].
\tag{10}
\]

This is a primitive direct-summand identification: multiplication by
`X_iY_i` puts the result into the unique exterior monomial sector using both
letters at `i`, so it is injective and has a coordinate projection inverse.
Both the Hodge and product lattices acquire the same factor `\lambda`.
The exact four-slot calculation therefore gives one and only one

\[
 O/(\lambda)
\tag{11}
\]

per ordered choice of the doubled slot in a five-set.  There are
`5\binom g5` such multidegrees.  Notice that (10), not merely a rational
factorization, is what prevents a hidden index in this step.

## 4. Squarefree six-slot certificate

Fix six distinct slots.  The integral two-row matching module has rank five;
take the five noncrossing matching columns.  In the `X/Y` exterior basis
their weighted graph-product matrix has block form

\[
 \begin{bmatrix}
 F_0\\ \lambda F_1\\ \lambda^2F_2\\ \lambda^3F_3
 \end{bmatrix},
\tag{12}
\]

where `F_r` is the coefficient block with `2r` `Y` letters.  The symbolic
jet-minor certificate records the exact determinantal-divisor valuations
of (12), measured in powers of `\lambda`, as

\[
 \nu_1,\ldots,\nu_5=(0,1,2,3,5).
\tag{13}
\]

More precisely, it gives explicit witnessing minors at every rank whose
non-`\lambda` factor is a product of the `\delta_{ij}`; the complementary
lower bounds give (13).  These determinants are consequently units after
dividing by their displayed powers of `\lambda` for every tuple satisfying
(1).  Successive differences in (13) are

\[
 (0,1,1,1,2).
\tag{14}
\]

Hence the exact six-slot Hodge/product quotient is

\[
 (O/(\lambda))^3\oplus O/(\lambda^2).
\tag{15}
\]

This is a symbolic, arbitrary-distinct-root statement, not a root census.
For the certificate and its scope see
`notes/2026-08-11-c909-symbolic-jet-minor-audit.md`; that note also records
why the analogous eight-slot statement is not yet promoted.  No factorial,
trace, or sign division enters (12)--(15), so the proof is unchanged at two.

There are `\binom g6` squarefree supports.  Their different slot
multidegrees are direct, giving the last two terms of (3).

## 5. Descent

Finite unramified `O/R` is finite free and faithfully flat.  Intersection of
the rational `SL_2`-invariant space with the integral exterior lattice
commutes with this extension, and so does the image of ordinary products of
the NS lattice.  Therefore

\[
 (I_A^3/P_A^3)\otimes_RO
 = (I_A^3\otimes_RO)/(P_A^3\otimes_RO).
\tag{16}
\]

For a finite `R`-module, its elementary-divisor multiplicities are recovered
from the dimensions of `p^{r-1}Q/p^rQ` over the residue field.  Those
dimensions are preserved by finite unramified base change.  Applying this to
the split calculation proves (3), without trace averaging or a trace
denominator.

## 6. Count check

\[
 \begin{array}{c|c|c}
 \text{type}&\text{number of supports}&\text{defect per support}\\ \hline
 (2,2,2)&\binom g3&0\\
 (2,2,1,1)&\binom g4\binom42&0\\
 (2,1^4)&5\binom g5&O/(p^a)\\
 (1^6)&\binom g6&(O/(p^a))^3\oplus O/(p^{2a}).
 \end{array}
\tag{17}
\]

Summing the final two rows is exactly (3).

## Scope

This is the complete codimension-three result only for the equal-depth,
finite-etale graph presentation.  It does not assert an all-codimension
Dyck filtration, an unequal-depth theorem, a non-etale result, or any Chow
statement.
