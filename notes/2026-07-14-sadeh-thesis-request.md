# Sadeh thesis / Hirschfeld–Sadeh 1984 acquisition request

Drafted request for C131 (confirmatory, non-blocking) — obtaining A. R. Sadeh's D.Phil.
thesis and the companion Giessen paper, to check whether either tabulates per-6-arc-class
extension-point counts (the deep-hole locus size |U|) for arcs of PG(2,11).

## Targets

1. **A. R. Sadeh**, D.Phil. thesis, University of Sussex, ~1984, supervisor J. W. P.
   Hirschfeld — *Classification of k-arcs in PG(2,11) and cubic surfaces in PG(3,11)*.
2. **J. W. P. Hirschfeld and A. R. Sadeh**, "The projective plane over the field of eleven
   elements," *Mitteilungen aus dem Mathematischen Seminar Giessen* 164 (1984), 245–257.

## Draft email

To: JWPH@sussex.ac.uk
Subject: Query on extension-point counts in Sadeh's 1984 PG(2,11) arc classification

Dear Professor Hirschfeld,

I'm working on a short paper about a small MDS code arising from an A5-invariant
6-arc in PG(2,11) — the "Clebsch hexagon" — and its deep holes. Your 1984 paper with
A. R. Sadeh, "The projective plane over the field of eleven elements" (Giessen 164,
245–257), and Sadeh's D.Phil. thesis are the definitive classification of arcs in this
plane, and I'd like to check our computation against them rather than re-derive it
from scratch.

The question that matters most for us: for each class of (incomplete) 6-arc in
PG(2,11), do the thesis or the Giessen paper tabulate the number of extension points —
equivalently, the size of the locus of points off every secant of the arc? If such a
table exists, we could cite a known count for the deep-hole locus we compute, rather
than presenting it as new.

I couldn't locate either document via EThOS, Sussex Research Online, or ProQuest, so
I'm reaching out directly. A scan of the relevant pages would be very helpful;
failing that, I'm happy to pursue library inter-library loan and would welcome any
pointer to where a copy is held.

Thank you for your time, and apologies if this is outside your current interests.

Best regards,
Tavis Rudd

(Word count: ~180)
