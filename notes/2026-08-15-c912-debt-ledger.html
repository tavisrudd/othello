Stabilization Debt Ledger

Tracking one obligation across the attempts to prove that X × P¹ is irrational for every smooth cubic threefold. The obligation keeps changing location; this page records where it currently sits, what has been paid outright, and what has been refuted.

The mechanism

Balance

4supplied at the left end, two copies of the cubic's rank-two block
1needed for the contradiction — only a lower bound
3slack: eigenvalues that may be lost anywhere without harm

The count is carried by a rank-two Jordan block whose sheared residue has trace −1. With that trace fixed, the count is nonzero exactly when the residue's determinant is 5/36 — the exponents are then −1/6 and −5/6. So the property is a codimension-one coincidence, which is why constancy along a displacement is the whole question.

Where the debt cancels

  1. Any step whose accumulated displacement lies in degrees zero and two — the string and divisor equations are exact there. This is what makes the left end free.
  2. An adjacent blowup and blowdown of the same centre, where the composite parameter map is the identity and no two parameters are ever compared.
  3. Any valley whose Euler operator has only multiplicity-one eigenvalues: the count is zero at every parameter unconditionally, so both arrivals agree trivially.
  4. Every centre. A leak would have to land in one, and they cannot receive it.

What survives all four: valleys that simultaneously carry a nonzero count and receive a displacement with a nonzero part in degree four or above.

What would end it

In our favour. Proving the residual debt for one variety, using the rigidity theorem along a pencil whose matrices are polynomial per slot, needs only that the four eigenvalues not vanish together.

Against us. A surface, or P⁴ itself, acquiring the count at a reachable bulk parameter would show the invariant is not zero below dimension three at all parameters, and the approach would have to be abandoned rather than repaired. The experiment that would decide this needs a model whose quantum product converges where its eigenvalues collide; the naive expansion does not reach that far.
