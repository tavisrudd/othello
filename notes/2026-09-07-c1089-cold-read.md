# Cold read: `Marginal certification and stochastic conversion` (03-exact-rigidity-atlas.tex, lines 514-712)

**VERDICT: MAJOR — the mathematics of both new proofs is correct as written; I found no gap,
no quantifier slip, and no false step in Proposition~\ref{prop:marginal-certification} (i)-(v)
or in Corollary~\ref{cor:stochastic-conversion}. The MAJOR rating is entirely for two
literature sentences (items 1 and 2) that are false as literally written and attribute more to
their sources than those sources prove. Everything else is MINOR or NIT.**

Verified explicitly, all correct:

* `\Pi_j = q^{m-1}\rho^\psi_{A_j}\otimes I_{A_j^c}` has the identity on the complement written
  in both the statement (531) and the proof display (564-565); the normalization is right:
  `q^{m-1}\cdot q^{-(m+1)} = q^{-2} = |L(A_j)|^{-1}`, so `\Pi_j` really is the normalized group
  sum and hence the joint `+1` projector.
* `\Tr(\sigma P_i)=q^{m-1}\Tr((\rho^\psi_{C_i})^2)=1`: `\rho^\psi_{C_i}=q^{1-m}\Pi|_{C_i}`,
  `\Tr(\Pi|_{C_i})=q^{m-1}`, so `\Tr((\rho^\psi_{C_i})^2)=q^{1-m}` and the product is 1. The
  step `\Tr(\sigma P)=1, 0\le P\le I \Rightarrow P\sigma=\sigma` is valid.
* The `G\neq L` counterexample: `\tau=q^{-2m}\sum_{\ell\in G}\widetilde W_\ell` has trace 1,
  rank `q^{2m}/|G|>1`, and `\tau_{C_i}=q^{-2m}q^{m-1}\sum_{L(C_i)}=q^{-(m+1)}\sum_{L(C_i)}
  =\rho^\psi_{C_i}` since `G\cap L(C_i)=L(C_i)`. Exponent arithmetic checks.
* "spectral gap exactly one" is justified on both sides: integrality of the spectrum of a sum
  of commuting projectors gives gap `\ge 1`, and the character argument produces an eigenvector
  of eigenvalue exactly 1. The surjectivity of `v\mapsto[\,\cdot\,,v]|_L` onto `\hat L` is
  correct because `L` is maximal isotropic (`L^\perp=L`), so the image has order
  `q^{4m}/q^{2m}=q^{2m}=|\hat L|`.
* Second inequality of (iv): `\Tr(\sigma(I-\Pi_j))=\Tr((\rho^\psi_{A_j}-\sigma_{A_j})P_j)` and
  `\Tr(XP)\le\Tr X_+=\tfrac12\|X\|_1` for traceless Hermitian `X` and `0\le P\le I`. Correct,
  and `P_j=q^{m-1}\rho^\psi_{A_j}` is indeed a projector on `A_j`.
* (v): `\langle\psi|h_k|\psi\rangle=q^{-|S_k|}\Tr_{S_k}h_k=q^{-2m}\Tr(h_k\otimes I)`; mean of
  eigenvalues equal to the minimum forces a scalar. Correct.
* Corollary: invertibility of each `A_i` from `\mathrm{rank}(A_iMA_i^\dagger)\le\mathrm{rank}A_i`
  and full-rank one-party marginals of `\phi`; polar decomposition with the positive factor's
  log split into trace part plus traceless `H_i` (the scalar `c_i=e^{\Tr\log P_i/q}>0` is real);
  `f'(t)=2\langle H\rangle_{\psi_t}`, `f''(t)=4\mathrm{Var}_{\psi_t}(H)\ge0`;
  `f'(0)=f'(1)=0` from one-uniformity of `\psi` and of `(\bigotimes U_i^\dagger)\phi` plus
  tracelessness; monotone `f'` pinned at both endpoints gives `f'\equiv0`, hence
  `\mathrm{Var}_\psi(H)=0`, and since `\langle H\rangle_\psi=f'(0)/2=0` this gives
  `H|\psi\rangle=0` (not merely `H\psi\propto\psi`);
  `\langle H^2\rangle=\sum_i q^{-1}\Tr H_i^2 + \sum_{i\neq j}q^{-2}\Tr H_i\Tr H_j
  = q^{-1}\sum_i\Tr H_i^2`, so each `H_i=0`. The unit-norm condition on both sides makes
  `c/\prod_i c_i` a phase, so Theorem~\ref{thm:lu-lc-rigidity} applies with `\pi=\mathrm{id}`.
  All correct.

1. **MAJOR — lines 644-646, Jones-Linden comparison overstates the source.**
   The paragraph's subject is determination *among all density operators*. Jones-Linden prove
   that a generic pure state is determined by its `\sim n/2+1`-party reductions **among pure
   states** — a "uniquely determined among pure states" result, not "among all density
   operators". As written, "Generic pure states on `n` parties are determined by reductions to
   about `n/2+1` parties" sitting two sentences after "determine the state among all density
   operators" reads as attributing the stronger conclusion to them, and it is exactly the
   sentence a referee working in the unique-determination literature will attack.
   *Fix:* "Generic pure states on `n` parties are determined **among pure states** by their
   reductions to about `n/2+1` parties \cite{JonesLinden2005}; here the locality is the same,
   the determination is among all density operators, and the family is explicit."

2. **MAJOR — lines 701-703, the Kempf-Ness sentence is false as literally written.**
   "Equality of the SLOCC and LU classes holds for every state with maximally mixed one-party
   reductions" is not true: the SLOCC class of a critical state contains plenty of non-critical
   states (e.g. `g\cdot\psi/\|g\cdot\psi\|` for generic `g`) that are not LU equivalent to it.
   What Kempf-Ness gives is the restricted statement about *pairs of critical states*.
   *Fix:* "Two states whose one-party reductions are all maximally mixed are SLOCC equivalent
   if and only if they are LU equivalent \cite{KempfNess1979,GourWallach2011}, and this is
   recorded for AME states in \cite[Corollary~1]{BurchardtRaissi2020}."

3. **MINOR — lines 519-521 vs. 652-660, the corollary is not a strict strengthening of
   Theorem~\ref{thm:lu-lc-rigidity}.** The subsection intro says the second consequence
   "removes the unitarity hypothesis from Theorem~\ref{thm:lu-lc-rigidity}", but
   Theorem~\ref{thm:lu-lc-rigidity} carries a party permutation `\pi` and the corollary does
   not, so the corollary as stated does not contain the theorem.
   *Fix (cheap, no proof change):* state the corollary as
   `(A_1\otimes\cdots\otimes A_{2m})\pi\lvert\psi\rangle=c\lvert\phi\rangle`. The proof goes
   through verbatim with `\psi` replaced by the stabilizer AME state `\pi\lvert\psi\rangle`;
   only the last sentence needs `\pi` carried into the appeal to the theorem.

6. **MINOR — lines 664-666, "succeeds with probability one / probability zero" is loose about

   set of successful branches).
   *Fix:* "the optimal probability of exact single-copy conversion is one if the states are LC
   equivalent and zero otherwise".
