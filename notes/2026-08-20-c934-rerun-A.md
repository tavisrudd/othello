### 1. Stalk/costalk shifts and the three blocks: (19)--(20)

For `K=R sigma_* Z_M[4]`, proper base change gives
`i^*K=R Gamma(X,Z)[4]`. Extraordinary base change and Thom excision for the
complex codimension-one exceptional divisor give
`i^!K=R Gamma(X,Z)[2]`. The natural costalk-to-stalk map in degree `r` is

`H^(r+2)(X,Z) -> H^(r+4)(X,Z)`

and is cup product by the Euler class `-h` of `O_X(-1)`. In the generators
`1,h,ell,[pt]`, its algebraic blocks are exactly

| degree `r` | block | matrix |
|---:|---|---:|

| `0` | `H^2(X) -> H^4(X)` | `[-3]` |

because `h^2=3ell` and `h ell=[pt]`. Equations (19)--(20), including the

### 2. Integral outer splitting, freeness, and point exclusion

JMW Proposition 3.2 and Corollary 3.5 identify point-summand multiplicity and
the costalk-to-stalk map with the fibre intersection form over a field. For
the two outer integral rank-one forms, the same adjunction calculation gives
inclusion--projection composites `-1`. After rescaling, each is an identity;
their cross-compositions vanish by degree. Hence both point objects split
integrally and simultaneously.

After removing them, the residual `P` has stalk groups

`Z` in degree `-4`, `Z^10` in degree `-1`, and `Z` in degree `0`,

and costalk groups

`Z` in degree `0`, `Z^10` in degree `1`, and `Z` in degree `4`.

### 5. Canonical Loewy filtration and nonsplitting

Let `S={0}`, `delta=i_*F3`, and `L=F3_U[4]`. Put

`A=^pH^0(i^!P3)` and `B=^pH^0(i^*P3)`.

Equation (23) gives one-dimensional `A` and `B` and zero canonical map
`A -> B`. Cipriani's projection sequences specialize to

`0 -> delta_A -> P3 -> P_*P3 -> 0`

and

`0 -> P_!P3 -> P3 -> delta_B -> 0`,

where `delta_A` is the maximal point-supported subobject and `delta_B` the
maximal point-supported quotient. Since `A -> B` is zero,
`delta_A` lies in `Q=P_!P3`.

The quotient `Q/delta_A` embeds in `P_*P3`. It restricts to the simple local
system `L`. It has no point quotient because `Q` has none, and no point
subobject because `P_*P3` has none. It is therefore
`j_!*L=IC_Theta(F3)`. This proves the canonical filtration

`0 < delta_A < Q < P3`

with successive factors `delta`, `IC_Theta(F3)`, `delta`.

Both adjacent two-factor extensions are nonsplit:

- `0 -> delta -> Q -> IC_Theta(F3) -> 0` cannot split, because a splitting
  would give `Q=P_!P3` a point quotient;
- `0 -> IC_Theta(F3) -> P3/delta_A=P_*P3 -> delta -> 0` cannot split,
  because a splitting would give `P_*P3` a point subobject.

The first projection sequence makes `Q` the unique maximal subobject with
simple quotient `delta`; the lower nonsplit sequence makes `delta_A` the
unique maximal subobject of `Q`. Dually, `delta_A` is the unique simple
socle and the same chain is the socle filtration. Thus this is the canonical
radical/Loewy filtration, the composition series is unique, and `P3` is
uniserial of length three. The filtration is canonical as a chain of
subobjects. Identifying either one-dimensional point line with a based copy
of `delta` is, as usual, only determined up to a nonzero scalar; the theorem
does not claim a basis.
