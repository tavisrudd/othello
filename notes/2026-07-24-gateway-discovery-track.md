### 2026-07-24 — one-factorizations of complete graphs as the recurring "forgotten decoration"

**Provenance:** cold read of the `arcs` and `clebsch-rigidity` manuscripts while setting up the
lane.
**Was I looking for this?:** no — the read was to distill the field-dependence contrast across
avatars, not to compare proof gadgets.
**Observed / musing:** the same micro-object — a proper edge-colouring / one-factorization of a
complete graph `K_n` — carries load in both papers independently. In `arcs` the zero-defect equality
structure is a `MATCH(k,m,1)` matching design on the Kneser graph `KG(k,2)`; in `clebsch` the
six-arc line bound is proved by one-factorizing `K_6` (via an edge-colouring of `K_5`), and the
support bipartition is the synthematic total (five perfect matchings of the six coordinates). In
gateway language the "small forgotten decoration" that makes each transport invertible keeps turning
out to be a matching / one-factorization.
**Why it may matter / strongest question:** if the forgotten decoration is *always* a
matching-class object, that is a candidate uniform structure cutting across the sporadic/uniform
split — a decoration that transports even where the numerical rigidity does not. Worth testing
whether the modular-carrier decoration (the endotrivial Lagrangian's fusion line) and the quantum
decoration (the CSS chirality / support bipartition) are also matching-class, which would make "the
decoration is a one-factorization" a cross-avatar invariant rather than two coincidences.
**Structural mechanism:** proper 1-factorizations of `K_n` encoding the arc's chord/secant pairing.
**Predicted siblings:** modular carrier — predict the fusion-descent line is indexed by a matching
of the perfect-code support; quantum AME — predict the `10+10` LC/LU chirality torsor is the
matching complementary to the synthematic total (already true at `q=11` per `clebsch` support
bipartition; the forecast is that it persists as the transport decoration at other `q`).
**Discriminator / falsifier:** exhibit an avatar transport whose invertibility relies on a
decoration provably *not* reducible to a matching / one-factorization; or show the `clebsch`
synthematic total is coincidental to `q=11` and does not recur.
**Evidence:** REASONED (both proofs read; cross-avatar persistence not checked).
**Status:** open lead.
