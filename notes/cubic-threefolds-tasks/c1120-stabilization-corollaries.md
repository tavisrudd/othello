## Deliverables

1. Strengthen the positive-marker threefold criterion to irrationality after
   P1 using surface-center vanishing and the projective-bundle formula.
2. Prove exact level two for the two explicit cubics over every
   characteristic-zero field. Descend both maps and their inverse identities
   to a finitely generated subfield before embedding into C.
3. Prove exact level two of the associated generic quartic del Pezzo surfaces
   over F(t) using the actual function-field identification. Do not assume
   that the original full Galois type survives arbitrary field extension.
4. Transfer exact level two to genus-eight partners only where the required
   birational P1-bundle correspondence to these specific cubics exists.
   Do not strengthen the assertion to every genus-eight Fano.
5. For a geometrically integral fibration satisfying the surface theorem
   generically, derive `Y x A2 ~ B x A4` and the exact truncated-level identity,
   including infinity. Separate this general formula from the rational-base
   examples and keep its hypotheses explicit.
