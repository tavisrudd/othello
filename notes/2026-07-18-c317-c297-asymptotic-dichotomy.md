## Permitted terminal outcomes

- **Extended obstruction:** every seed-legal constant-p/linear-p specialization is
  collision-forcing above an explicit threshold.
- **Construction-facing survivor:** an exact positive-dimensional stratum survives legality and
  collision analysis, with relative coverage stated as the next theorem gate.
- **Mixed bounded result:** generic strata are obstructed and a finite list of invariant exceptional
  strata remains, each with an exact reason current methods stop.

None is a global nonexistence theorem for `C`-complete `O(sqrt(q))` arcs.

## Proof strategy

- Work component by component from C316's equations; never hide a divisor under “generic.”
- Prove irreducibility structurally and audit the actual constant field.
- Normalize singular fibers before Hasse--Weil, or use Aubry--Perret only with exact hypotheses.
- Apply C301's fixed-point-free Frobenius list only after component fields are known.
- Keep internal legality, seed legality, genuine collision, and relative coverage as separate gates.
