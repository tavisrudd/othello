# C904: mixed BdGF divided cubes still have even p15 action

Date: 2026-08-11

Status: theorem-grade conceptual obstruction; Paper V research only; no
manuscript or Lean change

## Verdict

Beckmann--de Gaay Fortman's intersection divided powers do algebraize the
mixed halves

\[
                 \frac{x^2p_T}{2},\qquad
                 \frac{p_Ty^2}{2}
\]

in ordinary Chow modulo torsion.  Swap descent is also available
integrally: choose one oriented lift on \(M^2\) and push it through
\(q:M^2\to\operatorname {Sym}^2M\); its ordered cohomology class is the
lift plus its transpose.  Neither Chow torsion nor quotient normalization
is the obstruction.

The route nevertheless fails.  **Every mixed divided-cube p51 action is
even.**  The action formula contains \(\Theta^2\), and

\[
                         \Theta^2=2\Theta^{[2]}
\]

in integral exterior cohomology.  This factor survives after replacing a
divisor square by its divided half.  Hence the full mixed divided-cube
lattice maps into

\[
                         2\operatorname {End}(J).
\]

The exact finite lattice calculation independently shows equality.  In
particular its normalized identity still has order two.  A class inducing
\(2I\) descends to an integral symmetric-square cycle of addition degree
ten, not five.  It cannot combine with the existing scalar-two cubic
correspondence to yield the identity.

## 1. What the BdGF theorem supplies

Let \((A,\Theta)/K\) be the generic marked intermediate Jacobian,
\(K=\mathbf C(B)\), and let the relative minimal cycle restrict to an
integral lift of \(\Theta^4/4!\).  Beckmann--de Gaay Fortman Theorem 3.8
then gives an intersection PD structure on

\[
                       CH^{>0}(A)/\mathrm {tors}.
\]

Apply the same theorem to \(A\times A\), with product polarization
\(x+y\).  Its minimal curve is algebraic because

\[
 \frac{(x+y)^9}{9!}
 =\frac{x^4}{4!}\frac{y^5}{5!}
  +\frac{x^5}{5!}\frac{y^4}{4!},                           \tag{1.1}
\]

The already obtained PD structure on \(A\) supplies integral
representatives modulo torsion of the degree-one zero-cycle factors
\(x^5/5!\) and \(y^5/5!\).  Together with the integral minimal curves this
gives an actual lift of (1.1) after rationalization.  Thus
\(CH^{>0}(A^2)/\mathrm {tors}\) has intersection divided powers.

For any divisor \(D\) on the first factor and any integral cross/Poincare
divisor \(p_T\), this supplies

\[
          D^{[2]}p_T\in CH^3(A^2)/\mathrm {tors},
          \qquad 2D^{[2]}p_T=D^2p_T.                       \tag{1.2}
\]

Since quotienting by torsion is surjective, choose an actual Chow-cycle
representative \(S\) of (1.2).  Integral Betti cohomology of an abelian
variety is torsion-free, so

\[
                         [S]=\frac{[D]^2[p_T]}2             \tag{1.3}
\]

exactly in integral cohomology.  Different representatives differ by Chow
torsion and have the same cohomological action and addition degree.

The PD envelope of divisor cubes has no further p51 generators.  Indeed a
degree-three monomial has bidegree \((5,1)\) only when it contains two
first-factor divisors and one cross divisor.  Polarization of divided powers
shows that these are generated by

\[
             D_iD_jp_T\quad(i<j),qquad D_i^{[2]}p_T.       \tag{1.4}
\]

Terms containing a second-factor divisor or two cross divisors lie in other
Kunneth channels.  Thus (1.4) is the complete mixed divided-cube p51
lattice, not a sample.

## 2. Conceptual parity theorem

Let \(M=\operatorname {Bl}_0\Theta\) and \(h=b^*\Theta\).  More generally,
let \(s\in H^4(A,\mathbf Z)\) be any integral surface class and let \(p_T\)
be an integral cross divisor.  Pull the p51 class \(s_1p_T\) to \(M^2\).
On the Lefschetz summand

\[
                         LH^1(A)\subset H^3(M),
\]

its coefficient action is characterized, for \(u,v\in H^1(A,\mathbf Z)\),
by the contraction

\[
                   \int_A\Theta^2\,s\,u\,T(v).             \tag{2.1}
\]

One factor \(\Theta\) in (2.1) comes from the Lefschetz input and one from
Gysin through the theta divisor.  In the integral exterior algebra of the
unimodular symplectic lattice,

\[
                         \Theta^2=2\Theta^{[2]}.             \tag{2.2}
\]

Therefore (2.1) is even for every integral \(s,u,v,T\).

> **Mixed divided-cube parity theorem.**  The p51 action of every class in
> the intersection-PD divisor algebra of \(A^2\) lies in
> \(2\operatorname {End}(A)\).  In particular passing from \(D^2p_T\) to
> \(D^{[2]}p_T\) cannot produce the primitive identity.

The point is that the divided power removes the factor two in \(D^2\), but
not the independent factor two in \(\Theta^2\) forced by theta restriction
and Gysin.  This proof applies to every generator in (1.4) and requires no
finite enumeration.

The exact full-lattice replay strengthens containment to equality:

\[
 \mathcal L_{51}^{\mathrm {PD-div}}=2\operatorname {End}(A),
 \qquad
 \operatorname {End}(A)/\mathcal L_{51}^{\mathrm {PD-div}}
       \cong(\mathbf Z/2)^{25}.                             \tag{2.3}
\]

It includes all 120 divided-symmetric surface directions and all 25
integral cross-endomorphism directions.  Its normalized identity has exact
order two.  The conceptual proof above is sufficient for the no-go; (2.3)
shows the wall is sharp.

## 3. Swap, quotient, and torsion

Let \(\Gamma=(b\times b)^*S\) be one oriented p51 lift and let
\(t\) exchange the factors of \(M^2\).  There is no need to choose the
noncanonical PD structure or \(S\) to be swap-equivariant.  Define

\[
                      z=q_*\Gamma
        \in CH^3(\operatorname {Sym}^2M).                  \tag{3.1}
\]

This is an integral Chow cycle.  In integral cohomology its ordered pullback
is

\[
                         q^*[z]=[\Gamma]+t^*[\Gamma].       \tag{3.2}
\]

Thus (3.1) is precisely the desired norm descent.  Starting instead with
\(\Gamma+t^*\Gamma\) and then pushing down would unnecessarily double the
class.

Chow torsion in the choice of \(S\) does not alter (3.2), its Hodge action,
or its addition degree.  It also induces no nonzero homomorphism of the
intermediate Jacobian: the group of homomorphisms of abelian varieties is
torsion-free.

For a self-adjoint coefficient map \(R\), the symmetric transfer
\([\Gamma]+t^*[\Gamma]\) has unordered addition degree

\[
                              d=\operatorname {tr}(R)       \tag{3.3}
\]

on the five-dimensional coefficient space, with the global sign depending
only on the Poincare convention.  Hence a primitive identity would give
\(|d|=5\), while the smallest identity in (2.3), \(R=2I\), gives

\[
                              |d|=10.                       \tag{3.4}
\]

There is no additional factor two in (3.4): the quotient pushforward in
(3.1) already implements the norm.  The failure occurs before descent, in
the even p51 action lattice.  Equivalently, the exact obstruction to
halving the \(2I\) norm is the nonzero identity class in
\(\operatorname {End}(A)/\mathcal L_{51}^{\mathrm {PD-div}}cong
(\mathbf Z/2)^{25}\); it is not a Chow-torsion or quotient-descent class.

## 4. Relative and cubic consequence

All input cycles in a finite expression (1.4) are defined over the generic
field \(K\).  Choose Chow representatives there and spread them after
shrinking the smooth marked base.  Proper pushforward through the relative
symmetric-square quotient then spreads (3.1).  This supplies a horizontal
integral multisection with even degree.  BdGF do not supply a canonical
integral relative PD structure on the full semistable compactification, so
extension over the cusps remains separate and irrelevant to the parity
failure.

The existing Fano incidence correspondence induces \([2]\) on the cubic
intermediate Jacobian.  An odd multisection degree would combine with it by
Bezout to induce the identity.  Degree ten has gcd two with the incidence
multiplier and yields no improvement.  Thus the mixed BdGF divided cubes do
not produce a relative identity cubic cycle.

## 5. Exact scope and evidence

This theorem kills:

1. every ordinary divisor cube on \(A^2\);
2. every BdGF intersection-divided cube of divisors, including all graph
   and exotic cross-endomorphism directions;
3. every swap norm and symmetric-square pushdown constructed from those
   classes.

It does not kill an algebraic p51 correspondence outside the
intersection-PD divisor subalgebra.  Such a class would be a genuinely new
integral inverse-Lefschetz cycle, the same surviving gate isolated by the
full Kunneth audit.

## Mystery ledger (EJ + TT closeout)

- **Settled:** BdGF really supplies the mixed Chow halves modulo torsion.
- **Settled:** norm descent is integral and loses no extra factor two when
  performed as \(q_*\Gamma\).
- **Settled negatively:** the divided-square half removes the wrong factor
  two; \(\Theta^2=2\Theta^{[2]}\) keeps every p51 action even.
- **Settled computationally:** the divided-cube image is exactly
  \(2\operatorname {End}(A)\), not a smaller even lattice.
- **Open:** an algebraic p51 class outside the divisor PD subalgebra.  This
  is precisely the genuine integral inverse-Lefschetz gate.
