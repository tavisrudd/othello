## Target

Prove and publish

`rho_C(5) = L_2(5) = 4`.

The lower bound is already an immediate specialization of the paper's corrected-capacity bound:
`L_2(5)=4`. For the upper bound, use the standard projective four-frame in `PG(2,5)`. Clebsch C187
checks that its ordinary-uncovered locus is exactly the six rational points of the nonsingular conic

`X^2 + Y^2 + Z^2 + XY + XZ + YZ = 0`.

The frame is disjoint from that conic because the displayed zero set is the ordinary-uncovered
locus. Every point outside the frame and conic is therefore secant-covered, so the frame is complete
outside that displayed conic. Projective equivalence transports the witness to the fixed nonsingular
conic used in the definition of `rho_C`; the existing Lean normalization theorem is
`NonsingularConic.rho_points_eq_rhoC`. Hence the frame gives `rho_C(5)<=4`, independently of the
chosen nonsingular conic model.
