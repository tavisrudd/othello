Each claim-map row is now pinned by digest to the manuscript statement it

## Method

For each row: read the manuscript statement with its annotations stripped, read
the elaborated signature of every terminal the row registers, and ask three
questions.  Does the recorded conclusion state what the terminals prove?  Do the
recorded cautions name what is missing, at the strength the theorem types
actually have?  Is the coverage label right — in particular, is a row called a
conditional deduction one whose Lean conclusion is the manuscript's own
conclusion with the imported inputs exposed as premises, rather than a statement
about surrogate objects?

## Tranche one: the eighteen conditional deductions

All eighteen are accurate as recorded, and each carries the manuscript's own
conclusion with its external inputs typed: `thm:every-cubic`,
`thm:every-cubic-conditional`, `thm:separation-family`, `cor:universal-ch0`,
`lem:disc`, `lem:spectrum-transfer`, `lem:orthogonal`, `lem:A0preserve`,

`thm:nu6-birational-invariance`, `cor:p3-nu6`, `prop:cubic-packet`,
`cor:cubic-product-nu`, and `cor:v14-one-step`.

Two rows were checked in more detail because their cautions make a specific
structural claim.  The divisor-tagging row says the vanishing result remains
conditional on two final polynomial equalities; the input structure it consumes
has exactly two such fields, the tagged-against-intrinsic and the
specialized-against-tagged characteristic polynomial equalities, so the claim is
exact.  The framed-operations row says the two operation formulas are proved
from typed comparison inputs together with two multiplicity lemmas about
polynomials, which is what its four terminals are.

One caution was sharpened.  The manuscript calls the value of the framed count
on the product of a cubic threefold with a projective line unconditional, meaning
that it does not assume Hypotheses 5.7R and 5.7T, while the row records the
imported product formula and the passage from residue exponents to framed
monodromy as typed premises.  Both are true and they are about different things,
but a reader meeting the row alone could read the word as a claim that Lean
assumes nothing, so the row now says which sense is meant.

## Tranche two: the single complete row

`lem:dvr-rank-one` is warranted at `complete`.  Its final terminal is exactly the
manuscript's equivalence — a symmetric matrix-of-ideals lattice over a discrete
valuation ring is generated by its rank-one forms if and only if twice each cross
depth is at least the sum of the two diagonal depths — quantified over an
arbitrary finite index type, with the depth data taken as input exactly as the
lemma takes it.  The recorded cautions correctly reduce to two points that cost
nothing: the linear order on the index type is finite-sum bookkeeping, and the
construction of the depths from the geometric graph-coefficient lemma is audited
under its own row.
