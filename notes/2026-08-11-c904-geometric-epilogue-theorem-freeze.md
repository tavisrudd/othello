# C904 geometric epilogue: initial theorem freeze

**Lane:** `clebsch`

**Status:** manuscript work started at
`papers/cubic-stabilization-epilogue/`.  This freeze distinguishes the proved
baseline from optional period upgrades.

## Baseline main theorem

For the smooth nonstandard (A_5)-cubic pencil,

\[
X_b\text{ is universally }CH_0\text{-trivial},\qquad
X_b\times\mathbf P^1\text{ is universally }CH_0\text{-trivial and irrational}.
\]

Independently, (X\times\mathbf P^1) is irrational for every smooth complex
cubic threefold (X).  No full stable-irrationality claim is made.

## Proved structural inputs

1. The six elliptic axes have Gram matrix (6I-J) and five-axis Smith type
   ((1,6,6,6,6)).
2. Paper V's maximal golden normalization identifies its orientation torsor
   with the primitive pair ({\omega,\omega^2}\subset\mathbf F_4).
3. The cubic uses this exotic two-primary pair; its three-primary gluing is
   the unique monodromy-scalar line.
4. Squarefree graph slopes have primitive integral divisor-product minimal
   class, including at two.  Scalar/Jordan-scalar blocks satisfy the same
   conclusion at three.
5. Voisin's fixed-fibre Corollary 4.4 converts algebraicity of
   (\Theta^4/4!\) into universal (CH_0)-triviality.
6. The additive primitive-sixth-root multiplicity (\nu_6), together with
   the projective-bundle and weak-factorization formulas, proves
   (X\times\mathbf P^1) irrational.  The formal-isomonodromy lemma and all
   point/curve/surface exclusions are printed in the draft.

## Optional upgrades, not baseline premises

- Identify the normalization and generic degree of the cubic period image,
  rather than using only exotic landing and nonconstancy.
- Add the full quartic/Petersen/Prym shadow-sister geometry.
- Extend the irrationality theorem beyond one stabilization.
- Construct a horizontal minimal cycle or relative decomposition of the
  diagonal.

The first may strengthen venue positioning.  The other three are separate
papers or research programmes and must not delay the baseline epilogue.
