## Structural proof

Let \(H\triangleleft G\) have index two and let
\(\Omega=\Omega_+\sqcup\Omega_-\). Write \(E\) for the point-vector module,
\(\epsilon:E\to k\) for its affine augmentation, and \(F=\ker\epsilon\).
The quadratic moment map is
\[
 \mu:k[\Omega]\longrightarrow\operatorname{Sym}^2E,
 \qquad [\omega]\longmapsto v_\omega^2.
\]
Together with the linear moment map \(a([\omega])=v_\omega\), it satisfies
\(\partial\mu=2a\).

Suppose its kernel is the sheet-sign line and that a nontrivial simple
\(H\)-submodule \(S\subseteq k[\Omega_+]\) is outer-invariant and extends to
\(G\). The \(G\)-module generated by \(S\) is
\[
 \operatorname{Ind}_H^G S
 \simeq S^+\otimes k[G/H]
 \simeq S^+\oplus(S^+\otimes\chi).
\]
It meets the kernel of \(\mu\) trivially because the kernel is
\(H\)-trivial. Hence both outer parities embed in
\(\operatorname{Sym}^2E\).

There is a canonical exact sequence
\[
 0\longrightarrow\operatorname{Sym}^2F
 \longrightarrow\operatorname{Sym}^2E
 \mathrel{\mathop{\longrightarrow}^{\partial}}E
 \longrightarrow0,
 \qquad
 \partial(xy)=\epsilon(x)y+\epsilon(y)x.
\]
For either outer parity \(T\), let \(j=\mu|_T\) be the embedding forced by
the sheet-sign kernel and put \(i=\partial j=2a|_T\). Either
\(i=0\), so \(T\subseteq\operatorname{Sym}^2F\), or
\(i\) is injective and \(j\) itself splits the pullback of this
sequence along \(i:T\hookrightarrow E\).

Equivalently, applying \(\operatorname{Hom}_G(T,-)\) gives a connecting
map
\[
 \delta_T:\operatorname{Hom}_G(T,E)\longrightarrow
 \operatorname{Ext}^1_G(T,\operatorname{Sym}^2F),
\]
and the second alternative says \(\delta_T(i)=0\).

There is also a coordinate-free description of this connecting class. If
\(\xi\in\operatorname{Ext}^1_G(k,F)\) is the original affine-extension
class and
\(\pi:F\otimes F\twoheadrightarrow\operatorname{Sym}^2F\) is the canonical
quotient \(\pi(f\otimes f')=ff'\), then
\[
 \delta_T(i)=i^*\pi_*(F\otimes\xi).
\]
Indeed, over \(F\subset E\), the quadratic extension is the pushout of
\[
 0\to F\otimes F\to F\otimes E\to F\to0
\]
along \(\pi\). Thus C747 may attack a symmetrized Yoneda product rather than
the quadratic module in coordinates.

Thus a sheet-sign kernel is impossible as soon as one parity \(S^\delta\)
satisfies
\[
 \operatorname{Hom}_G(S^\delta,\operatorname{Sym}^2F)=0
\]
and, when \(i_\delta=2a|_{S^\delta}\ne0\),
\(\delta_{S^\delta}(i_\delta)\ne0\). This is the exact C747 target.

## What C747 must prove

For every matching stabilizer with \(\lambda>1\), C747 must establish all
parts of one statement:

1. \(k[G^+/K]\) contains a nontrivial simple submodule \(S\);
2. \(S\) is outer-invariant and has the two stated \(G\)-extensions;
3. for one parity \(S^\delta\),
   \(\operatorname{Hom}_G(S^\delta,\operatorname{Sym}^2F)=0\);
4. if \(i_\delta=2a|_{S^\delta}\ne0\), its connecting class
   \(\delta_{S^\delta}(i_\delta)\) is nonzero.

This is stronger and more precise than the manuscript's former
\(\operatorname{Hom}_{G^+}(S,F)\) composition-factor shorthand. In
particular, the principal-projective possibility belongs to item 1 and may
not be dismissed by the dimension \(q\lambda\).
