# C907 conic primitive sheaf kills the node socle

**Lane:** `clebsch`

**Status:** theorem-grade Betti/MHM singular-point excision for conic
bundles, plus an exact conditional bridge to the enriched carrier theorem.
The primitive cohomological sheaf has zero stalk at a rank-one double-line
fibre.  Hence the nonzero nodal Clifford product `ij` has zero image in every
realization which factors through the primitive direct-image sheaf.  What
remains is the strict Stokes/Gamma comparison asserting that factorization.

## Primitive direct-image sheaf

Let `A=Z[1/6]`, and let `pi:X->S` be a proper flat complex conic bundle with
`X` smooth and `S` a smooth surface.  For the global classification statement
below assume its singular fibres have the standard local forms: smooth
discriminant corank one, rank-one double-line nodes, or rank-two crosses.  The
relative hyperplane class gives a morphism

\[
 A_S(-1)\longrightarrow R^2\pi_*A_X. \tag{1}
\]

Define the primitive constructible sheaf

\[
 P_\pi=\operatorname{coker}(A_S(-1)\to R^2\pi_*A_X). \tag{2}
\]

Its stalks follow from proper base change and the topology of the reduced
fibres.

1. A smooth plane conic is `P^1`; its `H^2` has rank one and the ambient
   hyperplane class has degree two.  Since `2` is a unit in `A`, it spans `H^2`, so
   `(P_pi)_s=0`.
2. A rank-two fibre is two projective lines meeting at one point.  Its `H^2`
   is `A(-1)^2`; the hyperplane class is the diagonal `(1,1)`.  Hence
   `(P_pi)_s=A(-1)`, generated by the difference of the two component classes.
3. A rank-one double line has the Betti space of its reduced line `P^1`;
   nilpotents and multiplicity are invisible to singular cohomology.  The
   ambient hyperplane class has degree one there, so `(P_pi)_s=0`.

Thus, with `A` coefficients, `P_pi` is the rank-one component-difference local system on the
rank-two discriminant locus, extended with **zero stalk** across every
rank-one point.  Inverting `2` matters: integrally the degree-two map on a
smooth conic leaves a `Z/2` cokernel.  The chosen `Z[1/6]` is exactly the
coefficient localization already relevant to the primitive-sixth packet.

## Nodal consequence

For the local model

\[
 q=u^2+xv^2+yw^2, \tag{3}
\]

the two smooth branches of `xy=0` carry the two component-difference germs.
At their intersection the fibre is the double line `u^2=0`, so

\[
 (P_\pi)_0=0. \tag{4}
\]

Zero stalk alone does not kill an arbitrary derived point-supported class;
one also needs the costalk.  Here the component-difference local system on
either punctured branch has monodromy `-1` around the node, because the two
components are exchanged when the transverse square root changes sign.  Over
`A`, `T-1=-2` is invertible, so invariants and coinvariants both vanish.  If
`j` is the rank-two discriminant locus and `i` the node, the extension is
clean:

\[
 Rj_*\mathcal L=j_!\mathcal L,qquad i^*Rj_*\mathcal L=0,qquad
 i^!j_!\mathcal L=0. \tag{5}
\]

Therefore every support-local composite at the node vanishes after mapping
to this clean primitive complex.  In particular, although the even Clifford fibre is

\[
 E=\Lambda(i,j),\qquad (i,j)^2=kij\ne0, \tag{6}
\]

the class `ij` has zero image in every support-local realization factoring
through this clean primitive complex.  This is not an appeal to dimension or
duality: it is the exact stalk-and-costalk calculation (5).

The statement is stable under arbitrary base change for which proper base
change applies.  It also explains the biprojective calibration
`V={Xu^2+Yv^2+Zw^2=0}`: its node radical is visible in the Clifford order but
not in the primitive cohomology of the smooth total space.

## Conditional square-zero carrier theorem

Let `R_(1/6)(X)` be the strict value-localized Stokes/Gamma Rees packet.  For
a conic Mori fibre space assume:

1. its positive discriminant arrow factors functorially through the
   primitive sheaf `P_pi` (or its Hodge/D-module enhancement);
2. multiplication is support-local and maps a branch composite to the clean
   primitive complex at its support intersection; and
3. this comparison respects the Rees arrow and Gamma lattice.

At a smooth discriminant point assume the same-branch product agrees with the
square-zero reduced Clifford radical.  At a rank-one crossing, (5) kills the
support-local branch composite.  At a cross fibre the local form may be
written `q=ab` with a radical generator `c`.  In its even Clifford algebra the
nilpotent ideal is generated by `ac,bc`; using
`c^2=0` and `ab+ba=1` gives

\[
 (ac)^2=(bc)^2=(ac)(bc)=(bc)(ac)=0. \tag{7}
\]

Thus the cross primitive ideal is square-zero despite its nonzero
component-difference stalk.
Consequently the realized positive ideal `I_(1/6)` satisfies

\[
 I_{1/6}^2=0,\qquad \ell_{1/6}(X)\le1. \tag{8}
\]

This supplies the **singular-point excision target** of the earlier
coniveau-square-zero reduction in Betti and mixed-Hodge-module realizations
with `Z[1/6]` coefficients.
The conic branch still needs the strict, support-local identification of the
quantum Stokes/Gamma Rees packet with this clean primitive complex and the two
local multiplication compatibilities above.  Standardization is neither
needed nor available for double-line nodes.

## Limits

Equation (4) concerns the Betti/MHM geometric direct image of the conic
bundle.  A raw
module over the even Clifford order need not factor through it; the regular
module `E` itself retains `ij`.  Therefore the theorem does not permit one to
read Rees length from the order before imposing the geometric realization.
The statement is not extended to raw coherent or algebraic-de-Rham
realizations of the nonreduced fibre without a separate nilpotent-invariance
comparison.  Nor does it address del Pezzo fibrations, whose singular fibres can carry
higher primitive cohomology.

## EJ/TT and mystery ledger

- **EJ:** use the primitive fibre cohomology, not the radical of its algebraic
  presentation.  A double line has no component-difference class.
- **TT:** the node composite dies because its target stalk is literally zero;
  this is the missing singular-support statement in its smallest form.
- **Settled:** `Z[1/6]` zero stalk and zero costalk at the double-line node;
  conditional square-zero reduction for support-local primitive realizations.
- **Open:** strict Stokes/Gamma factorization through `P_pi`; the del Pezzo
  fibration branch; the remaining terminal/Fano comparison.
