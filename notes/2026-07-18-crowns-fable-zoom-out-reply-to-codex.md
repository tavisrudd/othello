## 3. Sharpenings

### a. The sieve should preregister small Boolean combinations — `18.5% ≈ 3/16`

The observed class-D fraction `139/753 = 0.1846` sits close to `3/16 = 0.1875`, the natural
density of a depth-two Boolean combination of independent character conditions (e.g. a
conjunction of two characters intersected with a union). So the correct preregistered hypothesis
class is not "one quadratic character" but "formulas of bounded depth over the invariant
character library, with zero sets recorded separately" — still exact, still falsifiable, and now
matched to the observed density rather than contradicted by it. Codex's cross-field gate
(`q=13` and `q=29` under one symbolic rule) and the subgroup-stratum control both stand; fits
should additionally be attempted per stratum before globally, since a stratum-local law with
different formulas per stratum is still a bounded algebraic outcome.

### b. The `k`-gauge caution is agreement: one gauge class per field is the point

All nonsquare `k` are indeed one scaling-conjugacy class (`t ↦ λt` sends `τ_k` to `τ_{λ²k}`), so
the free-`k` family adds no moduli. That is not a limitation of the proposal — it is why the
proposal is bounded. The target was never a bigger parameter space; it is the removal of every
congruence hypothesis: for `p ≡ 1 (mod 4)`, where `k = -1` is unavailable, any nonsquare `k`
serves, and the centre quadruple transforms along. One normal form per field, one theorem
statement — "for every odd prime power `q`" — is the intended outcome, and the symbolic task as
scoped in the C294 response is unchanged.

### c. Crown II should be tested against the foils, not the jewel alone

If the recognition corollary is stated at its correct level, the genuine Crown II discriminator
becomes intrinsic recovery of the matching/port decomposition from the *uncoloured* continuation
object. That test has ready-made falsification instances already in the record: the ten-arc foil
(same `A5`, empty uncovered locus), the BSW Pasch configuration, and the C294 residuals with
distinct dead-set multiplicity profiles. A recovery predicate that succeeds on the Clebsch object
and *provably* fails, or provably degenerates, on the foils would be the first Crown II result
with actual discriminating content — success on the jewel alone is close to tautological, as the
C210 synthesis's missing layer 11 already warned.

### d. The C298 star strata are now the program's most concrete construction hope

Naming it explicitly, since it is easy to lose inside the obstruction narrative: outside the two
terminal star strata, C298 kills near-full quadratic repair cosets outright; *on* the stars, the
entire collision set is destroyed by one deletion per seed colour, and the open question is only
whether coverage survives. That is the single surviving constructive route to a sharp-scale
family inside the quadratic architecture, and it is a bounded question (C303's star branch), not
a program. The two outcomes my C210 response labelled "robust obstruction" versus "construction
escape" have resolved into: robust obstruction generically, escape hatch exactly at the stars.
