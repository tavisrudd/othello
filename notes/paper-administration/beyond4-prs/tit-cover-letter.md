# Cover-letter source

Dear Editor-in-Chief:

Please consider the manuscript “Deep Holes of Projective Reed--Solomon Codes
Beyond Redundancy Four: Recursive Carriers and Exact Classifications Through
Redundancy Ten” for publication as a regular paper in the *IEEE Transactions on
Information Theory*.

The paper classifies projective Reed--Solomon split-free syndrome directions,
in the stated field ranges, from the first previously open case at redundancy
five through redundancy ten. Covering-radius inputs are kept separate, so each
split-free classification is promoted to a deep-hole classification only where
the required radius theorem applies.

At redundancy five, the paper gives an exact characteristic-free count of the
split members in a pencil of binary cubics and closes the resulting seven-field
finite bridge. At arbitrary redundancy it identifies the reduced recursively
contained locus as the union of the persistent catalecticant scheme and one
maximal adjacent-zero Lucas carrier. Coherent polar contraction then gives a
conditional uniform escape theorem, while explicit lower packages yield exact
fixed-level classifications through redundancy ten. A separate all-field
argument proves the first fresh binary Lucas carrier shallow.

The manuscript is accompanied by deterministic certificates, independent
replays where available, a declaration-level map of the conditional Lean
formalization, and Projective Reed--Solomon Toolkit. The toolkit computes exact
canonical forms and nearest-error data beyond the theorem's fixed-level range,
but its positive deep-hole classifications remain gated by a hash-pinned
theorem registry and replayable certificates.

The paper states the boundary between printed proof, cited input, trusted
search execution, certificate replay, and kernel-checked implication. It also
records the withdrawal of an earlier one-column MDS-extension consequence;
none of the present results depends on that withdrawn claim.

The manuscript contains an acknowledgment describing the use of OpenAI Codex
for proof exploration, computation, code generation, and drafting assistance.
The author checked the resulting arguments and computations and assumes
responsibility for every claim.

Before journal submission, the author will confirm originality and concurrent-
submission disclosures, related versions, conflicts, and private contact data
through the journal system. Those account-level fields are intentionally not
stored in the public research artifact.

Thank you for your consideration.
