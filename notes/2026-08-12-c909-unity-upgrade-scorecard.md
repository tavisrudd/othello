# C909 — unity upgrade scorecard for the cubic epilogue

Date: 2026-08-12

Status: interim editorial mathematics assessment; no manuscript edit

## Accepted structural gains

1. **Finite-etale saturation in every degree.**  The epilogue's local
   cofactor construction is the top-degree instance of a full saturated
   Lefschetz-algebra theorem on marked elliptic-power presentations.
2. **A natural moduli domain.**  Fixed integral graph data define finite-level
   modular presentation stacks.  Pullback along the cubic period map defines
   a non-tautological separation locus; the unrestricted domain is a
   countable union of fixed-data constructible images.
3. **A modular resolvent mechanism.**  The five two-primary kernels of the
   six-axis source are the smallest Borel/nonsplit-Cartan packet.  Over
   `X_0(3)`, the rational triple is the `X_0(6)` root cover and the exotic
   pair is the sign cover `r^2=T`; their fibre product is the full mod-two
   splitting curve.
4. **Exact cubic marking.**  The signed cubic coordinate satisfies
   `T=81t^2`, so `r=9t`: the outer/sign marking of the cubic pencil is the
   pullback of the exotic modular cover, not merely an analogous double
   cover.
5. **A rigidity dichotomy.**  For each fixed modular graph curve, its cubic
   period intersection is finite unless the whole curve is a shared modular
   cubic component.  In the shared case Torelli determines the cubic
   normalization from the graph curve.
6. **A sharp internal contrast.**  On one-depth rank-five components, full
   divided-power saturation coexists with
   `Hdg^4/P^2=Hdg^6/P^3=(Z/p^a)^5` and zero codimension-four defect.  The
   theorem is about the entire Lefschetz algebra, not a hidden claim of full
   integral Hodge generation.
7. **Orbit-axis family bridge (conditional on one normalization lemma).**
   Integral subgroup norms construct the relative elliptic image schemes and
   canonical transports among the six axes.  Five primitive inclusions then
   give the relative isogeny.  In the cubic normalization the raw `D_5` norm
   is twice the primitive Rosati endomorphism; proving that its image inclusion
   and adjoint agree with Roulleau's relative elliptic fibration fixes the
   `(5,-1)` Gram and closes the actual-kernel lift.

The upgrade is substantial because it changes the special calculation into
a general theorem and reveals the `A_5` pencil as a modular Cartan-level
realization.  It does not yet supply a second cubic family or a common
cycle--quantum invariant.  Accordingly it raises the epilogue's coherence
and referee confidence more than its headline theorem reach.

## Venue effect

The original epilogue already has top-tier-shaped statements.  These results
make a top-tier submission more defensible by removing the appearance of an
isolated lattice trick and replacing it with a reusable saturation theorem
and exact modular mechanism.  They do not by themselves eliminate the main
venue risks: dependence on recent quantum comparison results, novelty/source
scrutiny, and the lack of a second geometric component.

Provisional combined ceiling after full proof and independent audit:

```text
 Annals/JAMS/Inventiones first shot: defensible, roughly 15--25% acceptance
 JEMS/Duke/Compositio:              strong, roughly 40--60%
 IMRN/Algebraic Geometry/Adv. Math: high-confidence fallback
```

These are editorial odds, not correctness probabilities.  The Cartan packet
should appear as mechanism, not as an inflated independent headline.

## Best paper architecture

The clean order is:

1. state the two cubic headline theorems unchanged;
2. state the saturated Lefschetz theorem for fixed marked finite-etale
   presentations;
3. derive the marked cubic separation-locus corollary;
4. specialize the general packet theorem to the Borel/Cartan `3+2` diagram
   over `X_0(3)` and identify `r=9t`;
5. apply Voisin to the `A_5` family;
6. give the independent universal quantum argument;
7. end with the exact finite-or-shared-component dichotomy and boundaries.

The full lower-degree Hodge-defect formulas, indecomposable towers, and
Dyck-height conjecture belong in a successor or a short outlook paragraph.
Putting them all in the epilogue would dilute the cubic theorem.

## Remaining acceptance gates

1. hostile audit of the general Borel/Cartan packet theorem, especially
   projective monodromy and stack inertia at odd primes;
2. print the relative six-axis isogeny/graph lift before calling the `A_5`
   curve a component of a fixed-data stack;
   the orbit-axis theorem reduces this to the primitive Roulleau
   quotient/norm-image comparison and the Tate/Prym two-torsion comparison;
3. either prove the relative line-bundle descent lemma or retain pointwise
   saturation only;
4. bounded priority audit for the combined Cartan-level integral saturation
   statement;
5. no claim of a common Eisenstein cycle--quantum action.
