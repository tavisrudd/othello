## Result

The two assigned one-twist families have one common projective model.  If \(s=n-k\) is the
redundancy, their projective parity-check columns, after harmless nonzero column scaling, are
\[
 h_\theta(t)=
 [1:t:\cdots:t^{s-2}:t^{s-1}-\theta t^s]\in {\rm PG}(s-1,q).
\]
Fang--Xu--Zhu take all \(t\in\mathbb F_q\).  Gu--Wang--Zhang, in their classified
\(A=\mathbb F_q^\ast,l=k-1\) family, take the same columns with \(t=0\) deleted.

This is both a rank-one Hankel deformation
\[
 h_\theta(t)=\nu_{s-1}(t)-\theta t^s e_{s-1}
\]
and the linear projection of the degree-\(s\) normal rational curve
\[
 \nu_s(t)=[1:t:\cdots:t^s]
\]
from
\[
 z_\theta=[0:\cdots:0:\theta:1],
\]
the non-curve point on the tangent at \(\nu_s(\infty)\).  Thus the twist does retain a usable NRC
kernel; it is a tangent projection, not an unrelated non-GRS arc.

The full-length curve also retains an exact one-dimensional affine stabilizer.  For
\(t\mapsto at+b\), preservation of \(z_\theta\) is equivalent to
\[
 a+s b\theta=1.
\]
If \(p\nmid s\), this is the multiplicative group of homotheties about
\((s\theta)^{-1}\).  If \(p\mid s\), it degenerates to the full translation group
\(t\mapsto t+b\).  Since \(s=q-k\), the modular condition is equivalently \(p\mid k\).
Frobenius also survives after normalizing \(\theta=1\).

## Derivation

For the Fang--Xu--Zhu full-length code, their parity check is exactly the matrix with columns
\(h_\theta(t)\).  For Gu--Wang--Zhang, put \(s=q-1-k\) in their notation
\(r=q-k-2=s-1\); after removing their Lagrange multiplier \(u_t\), the last row is
\(t^{s-1}(1-\eta t)\), giving \(h_\eta(t)\) on \(\mathbb F_q^\ast\).

Let
\[
 \pi_\theta(x_0,\ldots,x_s)
 =(x_0,\ldots,x_{s-2},x_{s-1}-\theta x_s).
\]
Its kernel is the line generated by \((0,\ldots,0,\theta,1)\), and
\(\pi_\theta(\nu_s(t))=h_\theta(t)\).  Relative to the usual affine NRC parity check,
only the last row changes, by the outer product
\(-\theta e_{s-1}(t^s)_{t\in A}\); this is literal matrix rank one.

For the stabilizer, write the homogeneous NRC as
\([X^s:X^{s-1}Y:\cdots:Y^s]\).  The affine map \(t\mapsto at+b\) is
\([X:Y]\mapsto[X:bX+aY]\).  On the tangent basis at infinity it sends
\[
 e_s\mapsto a^s e_s,\qquad
 e_{s-1}\mapsto a^{s-1}e_{s-1}+sba^{s-1}e_s.
\]
It fixes the projective centre \(\theta e_{s-1}+e_s\) exactly when
\(a+sb\theta=1\).  This proves the stated multiplicative/additive dichotomy.

At the polynomial-code level the same boundary is visible directly.  Translation sends the top
coefficients by
\[
 (a_k,a_{k-1})\mapsto(a_k,a_{k-1}+kb\,a_k).
\]
If \(p\nmid k\), \(b=-(k\theta)^{-1}\) takes
\(a_k=\theta a_{k-1}\) to \(a'_{k-1}=0\).  If \(p\mid k\), every translation preserves the
twisted relation instead.  Nonzero scaling of \(t\) normalizes any \(\theta\neq0\) to \(1\).

The punctured 2025 family fails the same gate: an affine stabilizer of a fixed
\(h_\eta\)-curve must also preserve the missing point \(0\), forcing \(b=0\), and then
\(a=1\).  This explains why its exact syndrome criterion transfers but the useful
one-dimensional orbit reduction does not.

## Extra-juice closeout and mystery ledger

The closeout exposed the main extra value: loss of \(PGL_2\) is not loss of all symmetry.  The
stabilizer changes type exactly at the same modular boundary that prevents translation to the
2026 zero-coefficient code.  This makes the residual literature gap and the best geometric
attack coincide.

A requested second-order pass adds a canonical flag.  When \(p\mid s\),
\[
 (t+b)^{s-1}-\theta(t+b)^s
   =t^{s-1}-\theta t^s+\text{terms of degree at most }s-2,
\]
because the would-be \(t^{s-1}\) contribution from \((t+b)^s\) is \(sb=0\).
In the ascending monomial basis every translation matrix is therefore lower unipotent and fixes
the terminal syndrome line \(\langle e_{s-1}\rangle\), exactly the standard deep-hole direction.
At \(q=9,k=3\) the certificate proves that this is the entire common fixed space.  C514 can now
begin from the quotient by this canonical fixed line, not merely from a raw group action.

A requested third-order pass identifies the higher modular correction before it can become a
false uniqueness conjecture.  Put \(i\preceq_p j\) when every base-\(p\) digit of \(i\) is at most
the corresponding digit of \(j\).  In the full degree-\(s\) NRC module, translation sends
\[
 e_j\longmapsto\sum_{i=j}^s {i\choose j}b^{i-j}e_i.
\]
Because \(s<q\), the functions \(1,b,\ldots,b^s\) are linearly independent on \(\mathbb F_q\);
Lucas's theorem therefore shows that the common fixed space is spanned exactly by those \(e_j\)
whose \(j\) is maximal in \(\{0,\ldots,s\}\) under \(\preceq_p\).  For \(p\mid s\), both
\(s-1\) and \(s\) are maximal and their span contains the projection centre
\(\theta e_{s-1}+e_s\), leaving the standard fixed line in the projected module.  But there can
be more visible fixed classes: for example \(s=12=(110)_3\) also has the maximal exponent
\(8=(022)_3\), whereas \(s=6=(20)_3\) has only \(5=(12)_3\) and \(6=(20)_3\).

Thus the correct C514 object is a **Lucas-maximal fixed flag**, not an assumed unique line.
The `q=9,k=3` uniqueness is a clean base case; higher redundancies may carry modular-nucleus
directions already predicted by the digit poset.  The quotient could in principle gain further
fixed classes because invariants are not exact in characteristic \(p\); computing that connecting
class is now part of the theorem gate.
