## Reusable proof mechanism: normalizer-fusion product reduction

The fusion lemma supports a short general workflow.

Let `Γ◁G` have index two, let `H≤Γ`, and put `N=N_G(H)`. Suppose

```text
N∩Γ=H,       N meets G∖Γ.
```

Then:

1. **Split the abstract carrier.** Prove once that
   `G/H≅G/N×G/Γ`. This replaces a `2|G:N|`-point coset calculation by a base action and one sign
   bit.
2. **Localize the sign geometrically.** For a base object with stabilizer `N`, find an intrinsic
   local set on which `N` acts with a two-valued orientation character whose kernel is `H`. Its
   two orientations realize the fibre `N/H`. At q=5 the local set is the four complementary
   triangle pairs.
3. **Reduce strata to base ranks.** Since every pair stabilizer `K≤Γ` fixes the sign coordinate,
   for any based pair

   ```text
   #K\(G/H) = 2 · #K\(G/N).
   ```

   Same-base and transverse pairs can therefore be compared entirely in the smaller action
   `G/N`.
4. **Separate existence from uniformity.** A local orientation proves that a decorated avatar
   exists. Uniform middle-stratum count is the independent condition that the relevant
   pair-stabilizer ranks on `G/N` agree. Failure of rank equality is an immediate theorem
   falsifier; no decorated census is needed.
5. **Factor reconstruction profiles.** Any complete profile may be sought as
   `(external sign, base profile)`. A purportedly entangled invariant must prove that it contains
   information beyond those two factors.

For q=5, `G/N` is the five-point action. The same-base stabilizer `A4` has rank 2 and the
transverse stabilizer `C3` has rank 3, so the formula gives `4` and `6` immediately.

This mechanism is portable but sharply scoped. If `N∩Γ` strictly contains `H`, if `N` misses the
outer coset, or if no intrinsic local carrier realizes the quotient character `N/H`, the product
argument does not supply the desired geometric avatar. It also proves no game value or
reconstruction theorem by itself.

Potential consumption is cheap: C494's generic finite-action layer could formalize the product
bijection and doubled-orbit formula alongside its Mackey–swap lemmas. That is a hand-back, not a
new C493 successor or authorization to edit the clebsch lane.

## Third-order extra juice: the characteristic-two outer-gluing extension

The product cover has a modular shadow that is invisible in the set-level lattice. Let
`p:X=G/H→Y=G/N` be the oriented two-cover and work over a field `k`. On permutation modules define

```text
p*: k[Y] → k[X],        e_y ↦ Σ_{x∈p⁻¹(y)} e_x,
p_*: k[X] → k[Y],       e_x ↦ e_{p(x)}.
```

Their composite is `p_*p*=2·id`.

If `char(k)≠2`, the cover splits into fibre-even and fibre-odd sectors:

```text
k[X] ≅ k[Y] ⊕ (k[Y]⊗sgn).
```

If `char(k)=2`, then `im(p*)⊂ker(p_*)`; both spaces have dimension five, so there is an exact
sequence

```text
0 → k[Y] --p*→ k[X] --p_*→ k[Y] → 0.
```

It is nonsplit as a `kG`-module. A splitting would send the basis vector at the base matching to
an `N`-fixed vector in `k[X]`. But `N` is transitive on the two oriented lifts above that
matching, so their coefficients must agree and their fibre sum is zero in characteristic two;
points over other matchings cannot contribute to the base coefficient. This contradicts being a
right inverse to `p_*`. The certificate and independent replay additionally verify the two
`N`-orbits on `X`, of sizes `2` and `8`.

Restriction to `Γ=PSL_2(5)` splits the carrier into its two five-point sheets in every
characteristic. Thus characteristic two packages the exact phenomenon:

```text
split over Γ, nonsplit over G, glued by the outer element.
```

This is a reusable extension mechanism for any normalizer-fused two-cover, not a claim about the
dimension of an ambient `Ext¹` group. It may serve as a cheap modular falsifier or model when a
downstream carrier claims to encode an outer bit. No current task consumes the q=5 module, so the
observation is closed here rather than promoted.
