## Applied architecture

1. The abstract now defines arc and secant operationally, states the universal
   prescribed-hole theorem before the conic application, and describes the
   matching conclusion without undefined geometric shorthand.
2. The principal defect theorem is self-contained: it states the arbitrary
   projective plane, hole-set disjointness, secant index, extremal matching
   size, number of secants, covered required locus, and hole incidence before
   displaying the defect.
3. The analytic `q+1`-hole theorem and exact finite values are separate theorem
   scales. Their ordinary-proof, kernel-certificate, trusted-classifier, and
   checked-witness roles are stated once after the pair.
4. A causal proof map explains four moves: classical moments, pointwise
   factorization, matching rigidity/stability, and conic equality. A separate
   reading map identifies the complete main route, safe stopping point, exact
   finite continuation, and independently skippable appendices.
5. The early MDS/deep-hole aside moved behind the headline results and is
   labeled an optional dictionary rather than a proof input.
6. The matching/Kneser interface is operational at first use: secants are
   indexed by `E(K_k)`, disjoint endpoint pairs define Kneser adjacency,
   matching blocks are concurrent chord families, and dual star and matching
   lines are described before the coined realization terms are reused.
7. Repeated contribution claims in the literature discussion were reduced to
   exact source boundaries for classical moments, KNS localization, and
   abstract matching designs.
8. The conic section, characteristic-two sign proof, ten-point realization
   proof, finite examples, and verification appendix each state what changes
   language, what a first-pass reader should retain, and where computation or
   external classification begins.
9. The verification appendix remains in the paper. Both readers judged it
   skippable yet adequate after its opening was recast around evidence roles
   before repository and certificate metadata; moving it to a supplement is
   therefore not required by this gate.

No new conceptual diagram, model-case table, or results-and-mechanisms table
was added. The proof spine is linear enough that the readers found prose maps
clearer and less repetitive than another visual device.
