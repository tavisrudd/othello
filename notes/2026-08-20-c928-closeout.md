## Delivered theorem

For `M=Bl_0 Theta`, the paper proves:

1. `H^3(M,Z)` is free of rank 130 and lies in an abstractly split exact
   sequence with `wedge^3 Lambda` and `H^3(X,Z)`.
2. The simultaneous map `(b_*,e_X^*)` has the canonical mod-two fibre-product
   glue, and
   `b_*H^3(M)=L_3 wedge^3 Lambda + Theta^[2] wedge Lambda = Sat`.
3. The nonzero Smith factors of `L_3` are `1^110,2^10`, proved by complete-
   graph incidence rather than a finite certificate.
4. The dual escape group is `Z^10`; exceptional Gysin classes give exactly
   its doubled sublattice.
5. `IH^3(Theta,Z)=H^3(M,Z)`, with quotient `H^3(X,Z)` over ordinary
   `H^3(Theta,Z)`.
