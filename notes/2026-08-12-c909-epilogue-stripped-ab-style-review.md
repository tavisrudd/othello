## Recommendation

Adopt the stripped separation paper as the main submission.  Preserve the
omitted mathematics as a coherent abelian-variety successor rather than an
appendix: the universal envelope and golden marking belong with the integral
packet theory, while the ambient Hodge equality and sharp neighboring defect
belong with the finite-etale graph-quotient classification.  The main paper
should cite that successor only if and when it exists; it does not need it.

This is an architectural recommendation, not an applied edit.  The user must
approve the split before the authoritative manuscript is changed.

## Style-guide review

The PDF has no float, orphan, or typesetting problem.  The remaining issue is
hierarchy rather than diction; no meaningful chatbot-word cluster was found.

### Applies to either architecture

1. State the two main theorems before the character calculation defining the
   nonstandard pencil.  The current result first appears on PDF page two.
2. Shorten the abstract to the separation theorem, the all-cubic theorem, and
   the two governing mechanisms.  The genus-eight application belongs in the
   introduction, not the abstract.
3. Begin the quantum section with a three-sentence roadmap: define `nu_6`,
   prove operation formulas despite Novikov specialization, then kill all
   weak-factorization centers of dimension at most two.
4. Split the framed-operation proof into named paragraphs for blowups,
   projective bundles, and parity/frame preservation.
5. Keep the detector-independence table and the `m >= 2` boundary in the
   conclusion; delete one of the two surrounding summaries.
6. Compress “How the two proofs meet” to one cycle paragraph, one quantum
   paragraph, and their common consequence.  It currently pre-proves much of
   the paper.

### If the full architecture is retained

1. Reverse Section 2's rhetorical order: lead with the Fano-surface axes,
   `6I-J`, and the exotic packet; place the envelope/conference construction
   afterward as a marking refinement.
2. Put the primitive-minimal-class corollary immediately after the six-axis
   divided-power theorem.  Label the ambient Hodge equality and neighboring
   defect as an optional refinement.
3. Consolidate the post-graph-theorem scope and literature paragraphs into one
   scope remark.

### If the stripped architecture is adopted

The largest full-version hierarchy problems disappear automatically.  Apply
only the common six steps above, then perform a theorem-statement/section-opening
read and a fresh PDF-first cold read.  Do not import the omitted theorem's
novelty claims, notation, or proof obligations into the stripped introduction.
