## Verdict

The two C894 headlines survive unchanged.  The arithmetic extras need a
firmer demotion than the architecture notes presently give them.

1. **The exact local-Paley collision theorem remains useful and
   graph-specific, but it should not be advertised as a new general theorem
   about fields generated by Jacobi sums.**  Koblitz already proves a
   Stickelberger-based distinct-conjugates statement modulo Frobenius for a
   prime-conductor Jacobi family.  Aoki develops the Galois stabilizer of a
   single Jacobi sum and proves that a non-degenerate one generates the
   decomposition field.  Our eigenvalue
   \(\beta=-(X+Y)/2\) is not literally either predecessor's displayed Jacobi
   sum, so neither source supplies the local-Paley theorem or its exact
   mixed-sum collision classification.  They do occupy the broad arithmetic
   crown and must be cited as method and framing predecessors.

2. **The decomposition-field/CM statement is a proved corollary, not an
   independent novelty claim.**  Aoki's Theorem 6.1 gives the decomposition
   field for a non-degenerate single Jacobi sum, while Hoshi determines the
   field generated by all two-variable Jacobi sums: it is the decomposition
   field when that field is not totally real, and \(\mathbb Q\) otherwise.
   What remains specific here is that one local-Paley eigenvalue has exactly
   stabilizer \(\langle p\rangle\), hence is a generator.  State this as the
   conceptual interpretation of the collision theorem and cite Aoki and
   Hoshi; do not attach a separate novelty sentence.

3. **The primitive characteristic factor and CM form are Galois bookkeeping
   corollaries.**  Keep them if they compress the proof or exposition, but
   claim nothing independently for
   \(P_\beta(X)^n\), \(P_\beta(X)=P_{\beta^2}(X^2)\), or total negativity.

4. **The \(q=11\) integral golden operator is classical cyclotomic \(A_4\)
   structure in local-Paley coordinates.**  Let \(R\) be the cyclic shift in
   the directed-pentagon ordering.  Then
   \[
   B=R+R^2-R^3-R^4.
   \]
   On the augmentation lattice
   \(L=A_4=\{x\in\mathbb Z^5:\sum x_i=0\}\), the relation
   \(1+R+R^2+R^3+R^4=0\) gives
   \[
   \frac{B^2+5I}{2}
      =-R+R^2+R^3-R^4 \quad\text{on }L.                 \tag{1}
   \]
   Thus integrality is not only a parity accident: under \(R\mapsto\zeta_5\),
   (1) is multiplication by
   \(\zeta_5^2+\zeta_5^3-\zeta_5-\zeta_5^4\), whose square is \(5\).
   The order-five action already makes the rank-four lattice a
   \(\mathbb Z[\zeta_5]\)-module; Martinet explicitly treats the dimension-four
   order-five lattice, including \(A_4\), in those terms.  Retain (1) only as
   a compact explanatory endpoint or series bridge.  Do not call the
   construction novel, and do not promote it to a theorem headline.

5. **The 2025/2026 Javier--Llano--Zuazua paper creates no additional threat.**
   Its full text has no Jacobi, cyclotomic, eigenvalue, spectrum, or golden
   treatment.  Its relevant contribution remains exactly Proposition 4.4:
   the prime local out-neighbourhood is a multiplicative circulant.  It does
   not reach the collision theorem, exact automorphism group, unique
   extension, or the golden calculation.
