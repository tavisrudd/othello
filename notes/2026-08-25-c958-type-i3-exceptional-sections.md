# C958 type-I3 exceptional sections

**Lane:** `cubic-threefolds`

## Result

The generic cubic-surface fibre in Tschinkel--Zhang Proposition 5.2 now has
all sixteen exceptional sections explicitly over its degree-24 splitting
field.  In the coordinates used there,

```text
Y3*(a*Y1^2+2*a*Y1*Y2+(a^3+beta)*Y3^2)
+Y4*(Y1^2+Y1*Y2+Y2^2-a^2*Y3^2+Y4^2)=0.
```

The retained splitting algebra is generated by `r,d,g,delta` with

```text
r^3-a^2*r+a^3+beta = 0,
d^2+3*r^2-4*a^2 = 0,
g^2 = 3,
delta^2 = (-32*g-52)*a^4-(24*g+36)*a*beta.
```

It has the expected degree `3*2*2*2=24`.  The other radical is recovered
inside it from

```text
delta2 = 4*a*d1*d2*d3/delta,
```

and its square agrees exactly with the second formula in (5.4)--(5.5).

For each of the 32 choices of components in the five singular conic fibres,
the generator solves for a graph

```text
Y1=A*Y3+B*Y4,  Y2=C*Y3+D*Y4.
```

Exactly the sixteen odd choices survive.  They are labelled
`E1,...,E5,L12,...,L45,Q`; every displayed graph substitutes identically
into the cubic equation.

## Galois check

The four explicit automorphisms are the cubic 3-cycle, the cubic
transposition, the involution interchanging the fourth and fifth fibres, and
the simultaneous sign change of their components.  The checker proves that
they preserve the splitting relations and that their action on every one of
the 64 graph coefficients agrees with

```text
(1 2 3),  c1*c2*c3*c5*(2 3),  (4 5),  c4*c5
```

in the odd-subset model.  Thus this is the actual type-`I3` marking required
by the generic C956 Cox formulas, not merely a list of lines over an
unspecified closure.

The certificate contains 16 sections and four complete action tables.  Its
longest individual coefficient string is 540 characters; these are suitable
for a machine-readable ancillary artifact, not inline manuscript display.
