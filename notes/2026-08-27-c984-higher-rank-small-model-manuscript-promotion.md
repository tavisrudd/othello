## Integrated result

The manuscript now contains the admitted scalar core as one compact theorem
package:

1. **Pointed column-type normal form.**  An outer functional dual is presented
   by `L`-linear coordinate covectors; compatibility with the target block is
   exactly spanning by the external covectors, and every pointed spanning list
   is realizable up to basis change.
2. **Rank- and radius-bounded outer tests.**  Exact rank-`t` cost reduces to
   functional-dual subspaces of dimension at most `t`.  After truncation at
   helper radius `r`, exact response is the minimum over dual shortenings to
   the target plus at most `r` external blocks.
3. **Small separating contexts and witness cover.**  Every unequal bounded
   response is separated by a context of length at most `max(2,r+1)` and
   functional-dual dimension at most `min(t,r)`.  Every bounded coefficient
   witness restricts to and zero-extends from the same bounds; active blocks
   are defined from coefficient maps, so zero-label inner-dual lifts survive.
4. **Finite coarsest bounded quotient.**  The finite response vector over
   these contexts is exactly observational equivalence, hence the coarsest
   numerical quotient sufficient through radius `r`, and is a congruence for
   the paper's typed compatible concatenations.
5. **Exact recoverable-target test.**  The two-block full outer code exposes
   `z=rho_T(I)+d(I^perp)`.  Once two recoverable targets have the same `z`,
   truncation at `z` is exact, giving length at most `max(2,z)` and
   functional-dual dimension at most `min(t,z-1)`.
