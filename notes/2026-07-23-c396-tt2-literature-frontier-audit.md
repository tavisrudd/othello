## Verdict

The characteristic-31 `A5` six-arc, its ten Brianchon points, and its five associated triangles
are classical Clebsch-hexagon geometry. They are decisively pre-empted and must not carry novelty
wording. Classical work also owns the ambient bracket/Gale invariant theory of six points in
`P^2`, complete abstract LC invariant sets, and nonbinary graph-state LC algorithms.

No searched source contains C396's exact map

```text
t -> y=(t-1)^2/t -> w=-(y-y^-1)/4 -> z=w^2,
```

the proof that `z` exactly classifies projective/monomial/LC equivalence on this pencil, recovery
of `z` from the 450-entry holonomy signature, or the two identities that locate the
characteristic-17 and characteristic-31 specializations on one cover. The defensible claim is
therefore a new-looking **restricted synthesis**, not new ambient six-point geometry and not an
unrestricted priority claim.

## Deep overlap with PRS, Clebsch Paper 1, and the crowns

### PRS reconstruction is the ambient version of the same sheet problem

C481--C485 reconstruct a labelled six-point parent from coherent projection data. Four pure views
leave a separable quadratic ambiguity; C483 identifies its deck swap as Gale association and its
reduced branch divisor as the conic locus. C396 restricts to one explicit non-GRS pencil and then
quotients by party permutations:

```text
ambient six-point moduli       PRS: four-view quadratic Gale cover
          |                    branch = conic/self-associated locus
          |
          +-- C396 pencil -->  w=B/A  -->  z=w^2
                               degree 4     degree 8 from t
```

The exact next theorem is to write the pencil in the C481/HMSV coordinates and compute Gale's
action on `t,y,w,z`. Until that calculation is done, `w` is only a candidate pullback of the PRS
Gale sheet class. Proving equality would identify C396 as a one-dimensional slice of the PRS
reconstruction cover. Proving inequality would exhibit two distinct, interacting double covers.

The arithmetic method also matches PRS. C396's exceptional primes arise from exact resultants of
branch/spectral values. C519--C530 repeatedly isolate modular PRS carriers through discriminants,
Kummer/Artin--Schreier covers, and constant-field cycles. The reusable move is therefore:
normalize the cover integrally, compute the collision divisor over `Z`, and only then interpret
its characteristic factors geometrically.

### Clebsch Paper 1 supplies the classical endpoint and the forgetting/recovery template

The `F_31` tetrahedral member is the unique classical `A5` six-arc and hence the Clebsch hexagon by
Storme--Van Maldeghem. This gives a precise bridge to Paper 1, but it removes novelty from the
configuration itself.

The conceptual bridge is stronger than the shared endpoint. Paper 1's replacement spine repeatedly
has the form

```text
forget pairing/orientation -> retain a quotient -> recover sheets from higher data.
```

C396 has exactly such a quotient: `z=w^2` forgets the sign character of the four explicit
`y`-symmetries, and holonomy recovers the quotient coordinate `z`. The open comparison is whether
the `w`-sheet is any of Paper 1's already certified determinant-sign, matching-orientation, or
Gale/Clebsch torsors. It cannot be asserted from the shared two-sheet shape.

C402 gives the arbitrary-LU-readable statistic `60+b(A,A*)`. On the `F_31` Clebsch class it sees
the classical arc/Gale concurrence geometry and separates `H3` from every GRS class. At `q=13`,
however, both C396 classes have `b=6`, while `z` separates their LC classes. Thus the combined
picture is:

```text
b(A,A*)  = coarse arbitrary-LU incidence information,
z         = complete LC information on the pencil,
w         = latent signed refinement whose operational meaning is open.
```

This pinpoints the real quantum frontier: decide whether the q=13 pair is LU-equivalent, and more
generally whether minimal-support rigidity forces LU=LC on the admitted pencil.

### Shared crowns architecture

Across Clebsch reconstruction, PRS multi-view recovery, and C396 holonomy, the same architecture
now recurs:

1. a classical object is observed through a lossy functor;
2. the generic fibre is a small finite cover or torsor;
3. a discriminant identifies the branch/degeneracy locus;
4. coherent or higher-order data either selects the sheet or proves that it is intrinsically
   forgotten; and
5. modular primes are factors of the integral collision divisor, not unexplained census entries.

C396 is the cleanest one-dimensional model because all five steps are explicit rational
functions. That makes it a useful test bed for the harder four-dimensional PRS/six-point and
Clebsch torsor comparisons.

## Enumerated push frontier

1. **Compute the C481/HMSV embedding of the pencil.** Give the five classical coordinates as
   rational functions of `t`, compute the pullback of the Igusa quartic, and identify Gale on
   `t,y,w,z`.
2. **Identify or separate the sheet classes.** Compare the Čech/Kummer class of `w^2=z` with the
   PRS Gale class and Paper 1 determinant-sign torsor on overlaps. An equality or a proved mismatch
   is equally informative.
3. **Solve the q=13 LU pair.** Use exact one-site unitary equations after minimal-support
   normalization. This decides whether `b=6` is merely a weak invariant collision or a true
   non-Clifford LU identification.
4. **Prove odd-prime LU rigidity on the pencil.** Show overlapping weight-four stabilizer frames
   force every local unitary into the local Clifford normalizer. This would upgrade `z` from LC to
   arbitrary-LU classification.
5. **Compute the full holonomy collision divisor.** Factor all resultants among the five derived
   ratio values over `Z[z]`; match components with GRS degeneration, automorphism enhancement, or
   harmless spectral fusion.
6. **Intersect the pencil with automorphism strata.** Use the six-point/cubic-surface moduli
   embedding to prove scheme-theoretically that the good-characteristic enhancement primes are
   exactly `17,31` and recover the groups from the stratum, not from permutation enumeration.
7. **Lift holonomy to the four-dimensional six-arc quotient.** Express its trace ring in the HMSV
   coordinates and test generic birationality. This is the natural general completeness theorem.
8. **Compress the operational witness.** Find a minimal party-symmetric subset or polynomial of
   holonomies that recovers `z`, with a lower bound proving minimality.
9. **Transfer the integral-collision method to PRS carriers.** Use C396 as the toy model for
   predicting modular carrier primes from integral branch/resultant geometry before finite-field
   enumeration.
10. **Keep the classical endpoint attribution clean.** Import the q=31 Clebsch identification and
    10+5 incidence as prior art; reserve novelty language for the quantum recovery and common
    17/31 arithmetic mechanism.

Highest expected value is 1+2 first: the calculation is bounded and decides whether three major
programmes share one actual torsor or only a visual analogy. Quantum significance then makes 3+4
the next pair.
