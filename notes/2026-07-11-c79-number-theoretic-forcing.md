# C79 — number-theoretic forcing architecture for odd-q ProjectiveCap

## Goal and standard of success

The scope is the full odd-plane proof, not only C77.  The stable reduction asks for an escape from
every legal S3 state, preferably an on-conic P-valued S4 child.  Geometry has already supplied
strong normal forms, maximum pencils, balanced-center existence in the generic d4/d5 branches,
and exact descriptions of the characteristic-5/7 exceptions.  What is missing is game-semantic:
why one member of a geometrically defined packet is P, and how to reply recursively.

The number-theoretic lane must therefore produce a **forcing theorem**, not another P/N signature.
A successful invariant must do at least one of the following:

1. make a reply packet nonempty by an exact count, divisibility theorem, or character-sum bound;
2. quotient the residual game into arithmetic component types while preserving P/N;
3. give a two-ply descent in a well-founded arithmetic complexity;
4. descend the characteristic-5/7 prime-subfield exceptions to a smaller-field game.

Static character/order classifiers are already closed-negative.  Characters may certify abundance
or exclude bad fibers; they must not be asked to predict P directly.

## Likely final proof shape

The most plausible uniform proof is a generic arithmetic strategy plus finite exceptional bases.

1. **Stable reduction.** Normalize a legal S3 state by a projective frame and choose a conic through
   it. Reduce the initial theorem to a P-valued on-conic S4 extension.
2. **Arithmetic chooser.** On a maximum one-intruder pencil choose the `Low4` packet or the sharper
   balanced packet. The d4/d5 geometry already proves generic balanced-center existence.
3. **Two-ply reply/descent lemma.** For a balanced S4 root `R` and every opponent move `x`, construct
   a nonempty algebraic packet `Y(R,x)` such that some `y in Y` either lands in a proved base class
   or strictly decreases a lexicographic residual complexity
   `(conic defect type, off-conic zone complexity)`.
4. **Arithmetic existence.** Express membership in `Y` by bounded-degree rational equations and
   nonvanishing constraints. Prove `Y` nonempty for all sufficiently large q using an exact fiber
   identity, Chevalley–Warning/Ax-style divisibility, or a character-sum estimate. The point is to
   force a repair move, not to label its follower from a static signature.
5. **Bulk cancellation.** Quotient any structured conic residual into repeated arithmetic
   components. Identical Grundy components cancel in pairs, leaving bounded defect representatives
   plus the controlled off-conic zone.
6. **Exceptions.** Handle the explicit characteristic-5 `x=±2` and characteristic-7
   `x in {±2,±3}` subfield configurations by Frobenius/subfield descent or small reusable
   certificates. Handle the finite q below the character-sum threshold by checked certificates.
7. **Lean assembly.** Formalize the stable reduction, algebraic packet existence, descent, and
   finite certificate checker as separate layers. Do not import a numeric estimate as a game lemma.

This shape generalizes because every q-dependent step is either a finite-field identity, a uniform
count with a stated threshold, an induction on a decreasing game measure, or an explicit finite
exception list.

## Candidate forcing structures

### 1. Torus/dihedral structure behind conic involutions

An off-conic point induces an involution of the conic parameter line.  If the involutions occurring
in a repair family are simultaneously reflections in one split or nonsplit torus, choose a torus
coordinate in which

```text
r_a(u) = a/u,                 r_a r_b(u) = (a/b)u.
```

Then the even products lie in the cyclic group `F_q^*` (split case) or the norm-one subgroup of
`F_{q^2}^*` (nonsplit case).  For intruder parameters `a_i`, the conic graph components are governed
by the subgroup generated by `a_i/a_1`; their counts and lengths become gcd/order calculations.
Repeated clean components can cancel in nim-xor, leaving only boundary defects created by selected
conic points.

This is the strongest possible compression, but it has a hard gate: arbitrary conic involutions
need not share a torus.  For a family `m_i` to be reflections in one generalized dihedral group,
the rotations `m_0 m_i` must commute.  Test this first on maximum-pencil and exact repair families.
If commutators are generically nontrivial, retain the cyclic-torus argument only for the subfamilies
where it is proved and move the general descent to trace/Fricke or packet counting.

### 2. Character sums as existence engines

After normalization, legality and collision types are rational nonvanishing or square-class
conditions in one or two parameters.  For a fixed bounded list of square-class conditions
`chi(f_i(y))=epsilon_i`, the standard expansion of their indicator reduces the packet size to
character sums of products of the `f_i`.  Weil bounds then give

```text
#Y = q / 2^k + O_D(sqrt(q)),
```

unless a product is a square polynomial, which is an explicit degeneracy to classify.  This can
prove a repair packet nonempty for all q above a concrete threshold; small q and degenerate fibers
remain finite cases.  The previous q11/q17 character-signature flip is irrelevant to this use:
the characters certify that a strategy packet exists, not that a point with a given character is P.

The preferred target has one parameter and bounded total degree.  A multivariable packet with many
independent character conditions will give an unusably large threshold.

### 3. Polynomial-count divisibility

If a reply packet can be expressed as the zero set of low-total-degree equations in more variables
than the total degree, Chevalley–Warning or an Ax–Katz refinement can force its cardinality to be
divisible by the characteristic.  With one known or forbidden solution separated, divisibility can
force a second genuine reply.  This is attractive for proving `degree >= 2` or a nontrivial repair
fiber without locating the reply.

The gate is strict: legal-move inequalities must be encoded without raising degree to q-1 in a way
that destroys the hypothesis.  The exact q11/q17 leaves show there is no universal divisibility law
for the set of P replies itself.  Apply divisibility only to an algebraically defined candidate
packet whose game effect is proved separately.

### 4. Rédei polynomial and energy identities

For a selected parameter set `U` on the conic, package secant directions/products in a Rédei-type
polynomial rather than listing field values.  Coefficients, discriminants, resultants, and
multiplicative energies count repeated directions and forbidden product fibers.  This is a natural
source for:

- exact packet-size identities;
- low-degree exceptional factors;
- additive/multiplicative energy bounds;
- the transition term `A_y`, since `binom(n_l,2)` is a collision energy on the reply pencil.

The q17 value-opaque classifier supports this direction.  Reply-pencil load residues and labelled
old-secant incidence miss one P/N twin.  The twin is separated not by a field label but by a triple
collision in the multiplicative quotient multiset of the five reply directions: some quotient
`d_i/d_j` occurs three times on the P packet and at most twice on the N packet.  Equivalently, three
ordered ray pairs satisfy the same cross-product equation.  This is proof-shaped bounded-degree
arithmetic, although the full contextual classifier still has 182 types and is not itself a
uniform strategy.

### 5. Frobenius and subfield descent

The generic d4 selector fails exactly on configurations defined over the prime subfields F5 and F7,
and those failures persist in extension fields.  Treat this as descent data, not as unrelated small
characteristics.  A state defined over `F_{q0}` is fixed by Frobenius; moves outside the subfield
come in Galois orbits.  Possible mechanisms are:

- pair or cancel even Frobenius orbits when an involutory field automorphism exists;
- induct through the fixed field of that involution;
- prove that an outside-subfield reply enters the generic packet;
- reuse a finite prime-subfield reply certificate for fixed moves and handle nonfixed orbits
  uniformly.

Odd-degree extensions do not supply a Frobenius involution, so orbit pairing alone cannot be the
whole proof.  The success criterion is a descent lemma valid for every extension degree, with even
degree pairing only as one branch.

## Game-semantic arithmetic already proved or observed

For a P-valued S4 root `R`, the winning-reply graph `W_R` has minimum degree at least one: every S5
child is N and therefore has a P child.  Forced obligations are exactly leaves.  The exact corpus is:

```text
q=11: leaves exist (24 directed obligations)
q=13: all 12 balanced root orbits have minimum degree exactly 2
q=17: leaves exist (192 directed obligations)
q=19: all 85 balanced root orbits have minimum degree 43..55
```

All minimum-degree q11/q13/q17 S5 states have trivial grid stabilizer, and q13 winning degrees can
be odd.  Thus neither automorphism pairing nor parity explains the nondepleted lower bound.  The
leaf phenomenon tracks the depleted q11/q17 orders in the available corpus, so any arithmetic
theorem should be conditional on the depletion/packet structure rather than a residue class of q.

## Immediate probes and gates

1. **Common-torus gate.** On maximum-pencil and exact forced/repair rows, compute whether the
   rotations `m_0 m_i` commute. Report the fraction and exact exceptional families. A generic fail
   kills the global torus quotient but may leave a structured repair subfamily.
2. **Triple-quotient identity.** Write the q17 `multiplicity >= 3` condition as explicit determinant
   cross-products in the five labelled S5 points and candidate y; factor it on the exceptional row.
3. **Packet-count target.** From the existing d4/d5 rational normal forms, choose one bounded-degree
   reply/repair packet whose nonemptiness would advance the game descent. Derive its exact bad-factor
   list before invoking a character bound.
4. **Subfield gate.** For the characteristic-5/7 configurations, classify fixed and nonfixed legal
   moves under Frobenius and test whether every nonfixed move has a reply leaving the prime-subfield
   obstruction class.
5. **Outcome-compatible quotient gate.** Any proposed arithmetic state compression must be checked
   for P/N collisions on q13/q17/q19 before theorem work. A collision closes the quotient but may
   still leave its fibers usable as reply packets.

## Current proof odds

The geometry-only route has reached a selector wall because static local types can agree across P/N.
The arithmetic route improves the odds only if it is used for **existence plus descent**.  The most
credible architecture is:

```text
balanced/max-pencil geometry
    -> bounded algebraic repair packet
    -> character/divisibility existence
    -> two-ply defect/zone descent
    -> finite subfield and small-q bases.
```

The common-torus quotient would be a major simplification if its commutativity gate passes on the
repair family.  If it fails generically, the same final architecture remains viable with Rédei
energy and character-count packets replacing global cyclic decomposition.

## First exact probe results

### Common-torus gate: closed for the hard repair layer

For three conic involutions `m_c,m_x,m_y` to be reflections in one torus normalizer, the rotations
`m_c m_x` and `m_c m_y` must commute.  This necessary condition fails on every applicable forced
reply: q11 has 22 noncommuting and 2 boundary rows; q17 has 164 noncommuting and 28 boundary rows.
There are zero commuting triples in the full candidate controls as well.  Thus the global
cyclic/dihedral quotient is unavailable after arbitrary opponent moves.  The C74 one-intruder
pencil itself still has the proved common-torus form `tau_a(t)=a/t`; only the attempted recursive
closure is dead.

### Arithmetic ray core: exact separation, no extremal selector

For candidate reply `y`, let `v_i` be the five ray vectors from `y` to S5 and let `s_j` be the ten
old S5 secant vectors.  Define

```text
Z(y) = # {(i,j): det(v_i,s_j)=0},
S(y) = sum_(i,j) chi(det(v_i,s_j)),
V(y) = chi(product_(i<j) det(v_i,v_j)),
Q3(y) = [some nonzero direction quotient occurs at least three times].
```

`V` is a projective five-ray Vandermonde character.  `|S|` is coordinate-invariant; alternatively
multiplying `S` by the character of a fixed S5 triangle area gives an invariant signed version.
Together with the seven-valued reply-pencil load-residue pattern, the tuple

```text
(load residues, Z, floor(|S|/2), Q3, V)
```

is locally exact on all 192 q17 forced obligations with 102 forced types.  The division by two is
not lossy: `|S|` has the parity of `50-Z`.  This is substantially smaller and more arithmetic than
the exact field-valued Rédei packet.  However it is still only separation.  The best extremal linear
rule in `|S|` and `Z`, even after fixing the load pattern, selects 112/192.  Oriented sums do not
improve that bound.  Character correlation therefore supplies proof-friendly coordinates, not a
reply strategy.

The former quotient-set-size exception also compresses: the P packet's maximum quotient
multiplicity is three while the N twin's is two.  Equivalently, three ordered ray pairs obey one
cross-product identity.  Full quotient cardinality is unnecessary.

### Full cyclic-action repair packet: a structural but stratified hit

The q17 score-9 guards have opponent/reply involution-product order 16 or 18, exactly `q-1` or
`q+1`.  Testing every legal intruder candidate gives a stronger statement: each of the 28 worst
transitions has four primitive split/nonsplit candidates and exactly one is structurally clean
(empty conic, defect xor zero, exact zone Grundy zero), hence P.  At score 8 only 6/66 transitions
have a primitive clean candidate and at score 7 only 14/59 do.

Adding the full unipotent class gives orders `q-1,q,q+1` for prime q.  This expands score-8 coverage
but remains nonuniform.  At q13, whenever such a full-cyclic candidate exists, every one is clean
and P; it covers 1,333 of 2,353 intruder transitions.  Thus maximal cyclic action is a real
structural base certificate and a plausible character/primitive-element counting target, but not
the all-q descent law.

The score-9 packet now has a complete two-stage form.  All four primitive candidates are internal
and all four kill the last live conic parameter, so tangency and conic killing do not choose the
guard.  Their nine-vertex zone edge counts are `0,1,13,23` on 24 rows and `0,9,15,23` on four rows.
The unique maximum-edge candidate is the unique clean/P reply in all 28 transitions.  Its zone is
always one exact graph isomorphism type: 9 vertices, 23 edges, degree sequence
`(3,4,4,4,5,5,7,7,7)`, 21 triangles, canonical adjacency code `64399145184`.  The graph has only 20
reachable Node-Kayles live sets.  `ProjectiveCap.PrimitiveZoneBase` contains a reflected Grundy book
and proves its Grundy value is zero with kernel `decide`; the Lean target builds with only the
standard `propext`, `Classical.choice`, and `Quot.sound` axioms.

This rule does not extend by simple fallback.  Maximum zone edges over all intruder candidates is P
on only 18/59 score-7 and 32/68 score-8 transitions; full-order/edge and numeric-order/edge
lexicographic rules are no better.  The primitive maximum-edge theorem is therefore a finished
finite q17 base lemma, not the generic descent.

### Maximum-pencil arithmetic: classifier route closed

On each C74 pencil, tested normalized ratios `b/a`, Rédei-polynomial coefficient characters,
weighted Jacobi moments

```text
M_j(a) = sum_(b in B) chi(1-b/a) (b/a)^j,
```

cross/reciprocal character energies, and cyclotomic orders of `1-b/a`.  At q17 even the exact
unmarked ratio set is globally P-pure on only 6/21 maximum pencils; every compressed moment battery
does no better.  Restoring all proof-context marks—which endpoint is the frame point, selected versus
burned points in U, and the exact center parameter—makes the orbit signature exact, but produces
252 types for 252 rows.  The spectrum between those endpoints contains no clean quotient: pencil
arithmetic without game descent is either false or a fingerprint.

### Consequence for the final proof

The evidence now sharply favors:

```text
bounded algebraic packet
  + structural clean/base certificate
  + character or primitive-element existence count
  + recursive descent for the uncovered strata.
```

It disfavors all variants of “compute an arithmetic signature of a center and declare it P.”  The
next number-theoretic work should derive a packet equation for a structurally P follower, or prove a
counting/descent lemma for the balanced state family.  The score-9 four-candidate primitive packet
is the first exact model of the desired theorem shape.

### Orbital-intersection packet: positive counting gate, not yet a game lemma

There is a more precise number-theoretic home for the repair packet. Polarity identifies an
off-conic intruder with a non-tangent line of the conic. Hollmann--Xiang's coherent configuration
for the conic-stabilizer action has relations described by cross-ratio; on either the secant or
exterior type its intersection numbers count points in two prescribed orbital relations. Thus, for
two current intruders `u,v`, a reply packet

```text
Y_{I,J}(u,v) = { y legal : rel(u,y) in I and rel(v,y) in J }
```

has an exact association-scheme count before the bounded legal-point exclusions. For three or more
current intruders the analogous multiple intersection is not determined by pairwise labels alone,
but remains a bounded system of cross-ratio equations or character conditions. This is exactly the
place for a character-sum or primitive-element sieve: count the response fiber first, then prove its
structural game effect separately.

The primary-source scope matters: the coherent configuration and cross-ratio description apply in
odd order, but Hollmann--Xiang derive their displayed intersection-parameter formulas only after
specializing the rest of the calculation to even characteristic. We therefore get the correct
orbital algebra, not an off-the-shelf odd-q numerical formula. Fine single relations have small
two-relation intersections; the abundant packet should use a fusion of many relations, such as
prescribed secant/exterior type or quadratic character of the product-involution discriminant.

The q17 score-9 base supports this packet rather than a generic order classifier. Record the product
orders from a candidate reply to all three prior intruders. On 24/28 transitions the clean repair is
the unique candidate with vector `(18,18,18)`, i.e. nonsplit primitive order `q+1` in all three
relations. The other four transitions are one explicit exceptional fiber: the clean vector is
`(9,9,16)`, while decoys include `(9,9,18)`. Hence full orbital data isolates a generic primitive
triple-intersection packet plus a small exceptional relation stratum, but pairwise orders are still
not a universal P classifier. The maximum-zone-edge rule remains the exact finite-base selector.

A concrete large-q gate is now: choose a union of orbital classes with a positive main-term
intersection count at the two-intruder layer; subtract the bounded old-secant and played-point
exclusions; prove that every survivor preserves a strategy class or decreases a defect; and treat
vanishing/small intersection numbers as explicit cross-ratio exceptional fibers. The arithmetic counts a
**relational fiber of replies**. It never declares an individual orbital class P.

For a bounded active set of intruders, this existence step has the expected asymptotic shape. Each
requested secant/exterior or split/nonsplit relation is a quadratic-character condition on a
quadratic discriminant in the two coordinates of `y`. Expanding `r` such indicators gives a main
term of order `q^2/2^r`, with lower-order character sums unless a product becomes a square; legality
removes only the union of the boundedly many old secant lines. The square-product degeneracies are
an explicit factor audit, not mysterious game exceptions.

This exposes the real structural bottleneck more cleanly: `r` must stay bounded. The number of
selected intruders grows during play, so a uniform proof needs an **active-frontier lemma** saying
that only boundedly many old intruder matchings remain relevant to the reply/descent invariant; the
rest must be retired, canceled, or absorbed into a certified bulk state. Without that lemma the
character packet acquires linearly many simultaneous conditions and its density disappears. Thus
the next game-semantic target is bounded active memory, after which odd-q packet nonemptiness is a
standard finite-field count.

#### Exact odd-q orbital discriminant

The fused relation has a direct grid formula. Parametrize the conic `XY=Z^2` by
`P(t)=(t^2:1:t)`, and write an affine intruder as `x=(r,c,1)`. The chord through `P(t),P(s)` has
equation

```text
X + ts Y - (t+s) Z = 0.
```

Putting `x` on the chord and solving for `s` gives the projection involution

```text
sigma_x(t) = (t-r)/(ct-1),       M_x = [[1,-r],[c,-1]].
```

For `y=(u,v,1)`, therefore,

```text
tr(M_x M_y)  = 2-rv-cu,
det(M_x M_y) = (rc-1)(uv-1),
D_x(y)       = (2-rv-cu)^2 - 4(rc-1)(uv-1).                 (3)
```

The same polynomial is the discriminant obtained by intersecting the line `xy` with the conic.
Consequently `D=0`, `chi(D)=+1`, and `chi(D)=-1` mean respectively tangent/unipotent,
secant/split, and exterior/nonsplit. The product order is respectively `p` (for prime q), a divisor
of `q-1`, or a divisor of `q+1`. This is an exact value-opaque relation coordinate over every odd
field; no chosen conic labels remain in its square class.

`scripts/c79_orbital_packet.py` exhaustively compares (3) with both geometric line intersection and
the permutation-product order on every intruder pair at q=11,13,17,19: 114,132 pairs, zero
mismatches. The algebra above is the proof; the census guards the coordinate conventions.

### Terminal-parity shortcut: closed even for the finite base

The canonical nine-vertex primitive zone has six maximal independent sets, of sizes `2,2,2,3,3,4`.
Thus its Grundy-zero value is not explained by purity or common terminal parity. An all-q proof still
needs a reply strategy/descent certificate.

## Strategy-matching viewpoint and adjacent literature

A root-relative second-player strategy can be viewed as a rule matching every opponent-reachable
odd face of the residual cap complex to a legal even coface.  This is the right object for arithmetic
compression: a polynomial or character argument need only prove that the prescribed coface packet
is nonempty and remains inside the strategy class.  It need not evaluate arbitrary faces.

Adjacent work supports the language but does not transfer a theorem:

- Faridi--Huntemann--Nowakowski show that the legal simplicial complex of a strong placement game
  determines its game tree and value: <https://www.combinatorics.org/ojs/index.php/eljc/article/view/v26i3p34>.
- Di Benedetto--Solymosi--White use the Rédei polynomial and Szőnyi extension to force directions
  in affine Cartesian products: <https://arxiv.org/abs/2001.06994>.  The reusable mechanism is
  polynomial control of direction fibers, not their particular bound.
- Balandraud's polynomial-method addition theorems illustrate a useful reverse-Nullstellensatz
  pattern: choose a Cartesian product on which all coefficient-formula terms vanish except one,
  forcing a solution: <https://arxiv.org/abs/1702.06419>.
- Hollmann--Xiang describe the conic-stabilizer coherent configuration and its cross-ratio
  relations/intersection numbers: <https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0503573>. This is the natural counting
  algebra for intruder-reply fibers, but its displayed parameter calculation specializes to even
  characteristic and it supplies no game-value theorem by itself.

The final proof should therefore present an arithmetic **strategy matching** or descent kernel, not
an arithmetic outcome formula for the entire cap complex.
