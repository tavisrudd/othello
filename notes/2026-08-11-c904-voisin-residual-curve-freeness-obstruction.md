# C904: Voisin residual curves and the exact freeness obstruction

Date: 2026-08-11

Status: theorem-grade bounded negative; Paper V research only; no manuscript or
Lean change

## Verdict

Voisin's construction gives a rational curve

\[
 R_C\subset M_{6,1}
\]

in the elliptic-sextic Hilbert space.  It does **not** give, in the cited
proof, a complete nonconstant rational curve on the charge-three space
\(M_9\), much less a line on a named proper Abel--Jacobi fourfold.  On the
open set where the Serre map is defined there is only a rational projection

\[
 g_C:\mathbf P^1\dashrightarrow M_9.
\]

There is an exact trichotomy.

1. If \(g_C\) is constant, the construction supplies no curve on the
   charge-three Abel fibre.
2. If \(g_C\) extends to a nonconstant map \(\mathbf P^1\to V^\circ\), where
   \(V^\circ\) is the smooth locally free locus of an Abel--Jacobi fibre, then
   \(g_C\) is **not free**.  Indeed
   \(\deg g_C^*T_{V^\circ}=0\), while its differential is a nonzero map
   \(\mathcal O_{\mathbf P^1}(2)\to g_C^*T_{V^\circ}\).  Hence the latter
   bundle has a negative summand.
3. If the projection acquires missing points and only its closure exists on
   a proper model, all possible positive degree comes from elementary
   modifications at the boundary.  Voisin's paper supplies neither that
   proper model nor those boundary modifications, so its tangent splitting
   is not determined by the residual-scroll construction.

Thus the printed residual curves cannot presently supply free two-point
chains or a very twisting surface.  Any revival must be a boundary theorem,
not merely a calculation on the smooth instanton locus.

## 1. What Voisin actually constructs

Fix a general smooth rational sextic \(C\subset Y\subset\mathbf P^4\).  A
general hyperplane

\[
 H\supset W\subset H^0(\mathbf P^1,\mathcal O_{\mathbf P^1}(6))
\]

determines a two-dimensional base-point-free subspace
\(W'\subset H^0(\mathbf P^1,\mathcal O(4))\), hence a morphism

\[
 \varphi_H:\mathbf P^1\times\mathbf P^1\longrightarrow\mathbf P^4
\]

defined by a hyperplane of \(|2h_1+h_2|\).  Its image \(\Sigma_H\) has
degree four and contains \(C\) as a curve of class \(4h_1+h_2\).  On the
source scroll,

\[
 [Y\cap\Sigma_H]=6h_1+3h_2,
 \qquad
 [E_H]=2h_1+2h_2.
\]

Thus the residual curve \(E_H\) has degree six and genus one.  Hyperplanes
containing \(W\) form \(\mathbf P^1\), producing Voisin's curve \(R_C\).
This is Lemma 2.5 and the paragraph immediately following it in the primary
source.

For a smooth nondegenerate \(E_H\), Serre's construction gives

\[
 0\longrightarrow\mathcal O_Y
 \xrightarrow{s_H} A_H
 \longrightarrow I_{E_H}(2)\longrightarrow0,              \tag{1.1}
\]

with \(h^0(Y,A_H)=4\).  Voisin defines the rational map

\[
 \phi:M_{6,1}\dashrightarrow M_9,
 \qquad E_H\longmapsto[A_H],                               \tag{1.2}
\]

whose general fibre is \(\mathbf P(H^0(A_H))\simeq\mathbf P^3\).

The crucial source boundary is literal: Voisin calls \(R_C\) a curve in
\(M_{6,1}\), uses it in Principle 2.7 to make a divisor dominate the MRC
base, and never proves that \(\phi|_{R_C}\) is nonconstant, everywhere
defined, or a line for a polarization on a compactification of \(M_9\).
The degree-four surface \(\Sigma_H\subset\mathbf P^4\) is a liaison scroll;
it is not a twisting surface in the charge-three moduli fibre.

The Abel--Jacobi value is constant along \(R_C\): it is an algebraic family
over a rational base, equivalently the residual relation keeps
\([C]+[E_H]\) in the connected family of cubic sections of the scrolls.
Consequently, wherever (1.2) is defined, its differential lands in the
relative tangent of \(M_9\to J(Y)\).

## 2. Exact deformation sequences on the defined open

Let \(U\subset\mathbf P^1\) be the open on which \(E_H\) is a smooth
nondegenerate sextic, \(A_H\) is stable and unobstructed, and (1.2) is
defined.  Write

\[
 f:U\to M_{6,1},\qquad g=\phi\circ f:U\to M_9.
\]

Let \(\mathscr A\) be the resulting Serre family and let
\(\mathscr L\subset g^*\mathscr W\),
\(\mathscr W=p_*\mathscr A\), be the line generated by the section in
(1.1).  Since \(R^ip_*\mathscr A=0\) for \(i>0\) and \(\mathscr W\) has
rank four, the relative Euler sequence for the \(\mathbf P^3\)-fibre and
the differential of the Serre map give

\[
 0\longrightarrow
 \mathscr L^\vee\otimes(g^*\mathscr W/\mathscr L)
 \longrightarrow f^*T_{M_{6,1}}
 \longrightarrow g^*T_{M_9}
 \longrightarrow0.                                        \tag{2.1}
\]

At a point \(H\), this is the standard exact tangent sequence

\[
 0\longrightarrow
 \operatorname {Hom}
   (\langle s_H\rangle,H^0(A_H)/\langle s_H\rangle)
 \longrightarrow H^0(E_H,N_{E_H/Y})
 \longrightarrow \operatorname {Ext}^1_Y(A_H,A_H)
 \longrightarrow0,                                        \tag{2.2}
\]

of dimensions \(3,12,9\).  Equations (2.1)--(2.2) are valid on the generic
smooth locus; they do not assert an extension at the missing parameters of
\(R_C\).

On the smooth locus of a general Abel fibre \(V^\circ\), dominance of the
Abel--Jacobi map gives

\[
 0\longrightarrow g^*T_{V^\circ}
 \longrightarrow g^*T_{M_9}
 \longrightarrow T_{J(Y)}\otimes\mathcal O_U
 \longrightarrow0.                                        \tag{2.3}
\]

The derivative \(dg:T_U\to g^*T_{V^\circ}\) is the image under (2.1) of
the Hilbert-scheme Kodaira--Spencer class of the residual family.  This is
the exact deformation sequence available from the construction.  Computing
its splitting on \(\mathbf P^1\) requires extending every term through the
missing parameters, which is precisely the unprovided boundary datum.

## 3. Relative canonical degree zero

There is nevertheless a decisive calculation if \(g\) extends inside the
smooth locally free locus.  Let \(B\) be a complete curve in a smooth
Abel--Jacobi fibre and let \(\mathscr A\) be the corresponding rank-two
family on \(Y\times B\).  The calculation is insensitive to twisting
\(\mathscr A\) from \(B\), because it uses
\(R\mathcal Hom(\mathscr A,\mathscr A)\).

Put

\[
 \Delta=c_1(\mathscr A)^2-4c_2(\mathscr A).
\]

After the Abel--Jacobi mixed component is removed, the terms relevant to
degree on \(B\) have the form

\[
 \Delta=-4H^2+aH[B].                                      \tag{3.1}
\]

For a cubic threefold, \(\operatorname {td}_2(Y)=\frac23H^2\), while

\[
 \operatorname {ch}R\mathcal Hom(\mathscr A,\mathscr A)
 =4+\Delta+\frac1{12}\Delta^2+\cdots .                    \tag{3.2}
\]

The two degree-one terms in GRR cancel exactly:

\[
 \left[\frac1{12}\Delta^2\right]_{B}
   =-\frac{2a}{3}H^3[B],
 \qquad
 [\Delta\operatorname {td}_2(Y)]_{B}
   = \frac{2a}{3}H^3[B].                                  \tag{3.3}
\]

Therefore

\[
 \deg K_{M_9}|_B=0,
 \qquad
 \deg T_{V^\circ}|_B=0.                                  \tag{3.4}
\]

For \(B=\mathbf P^1\), the same conclusion needs no Abel--Jacobi
hypothesis for the mixed term, since \(H^1(\mathbf P^1)=0\).

## 4. Exact nonfreeness theorem

Assume now that \(g:\mathbf P^1\to V^\circ\) is nonconstant and everywhere
defined.  In characteristic zero its differential is generically nonzero:

\[
 \mathcal O_{\mathbf P^1}(2)=T_{\mathbf P^1}
 \longrightarrow g^*T_{V^\circ}.                          \tag{4.1}
\]

Write

\[
 g^*T_{V^\circ}\simeq\bigoplus_{i=1}^4\mathcal O(a_i).
\]

By (3.4), \(\sum_i a_i=0\).  The nonzero map (4.1) forces
\(a_i\ge2\) for some \(i\), hence \(a_j<0\) for some \(j\).  Thus
\(g^*T_{V^\circ}\) is not globally generated and \(g\) is not free.

Equivalently, the normal sequence is

\[
 0\longrightarrow\mathcal O(2)
 \xrightarrow{dg}g^*T_{V^\circ}
 \longrightarrow N_g\longrightarrow0,                    \tag{4.2}
\]

where the coherent normal sheaf has degree \(-2\); after removing the
ramification torsion, its vector-bundle quotient has degree at most \(-2\).
This is already enough to rule out the free-line condition of de
Jong--He--Starr.

## 5. Why a proper model does not repair this for free

Suppose only that \(g:U\to V^\circ\) extends to
\(\bar g:\mathbf P^1\to\bar V\) on a proper model.  At every point of
\(\mathbf P^1\setminus U\), the extension of the open deformation bundle is
changed by an elementary modification determined by the boundary stratum
and the limiting sheaf.  Its degree, and even the rank-one quotients that
receive those corrections, depend on:

1. the chosen compactification of the charge-three component;
2. whether the limiting Serre objects remain locally free and stable;
3. the boundary normal and discrepancy; and
4. the contact orders of \(\bar g\) with each boundary component.

None of these data is present in Voisin's residual-curve argument.  A blowup
of a boundary centre leaves \(V^\circ\) unchanged but changes the ordinary
tangent splitting of the strict transform.  Hence there is no
compactification-independent splitting to infer from Lemma 2.5.

The consequence for the proposed RSC route is exact:

- complete projected residual curves contained in the open fibre are
  nonfree;
- curves contracted by \(\phi\) give nothing on the fibre;
- a potentially free closure must acquire all of its positivity from a
  specified boundary calculation.

Moreover, a very twisting surface is a morphism into the moduli of
one-pointed **free lines**, with positivity for both the evaluation and
forgetful tangent bundles.  The scroll \(\Sigma_H\) used to construct
\(E_H\) is not such an object, and (4.1)--(4.2) rule it out whenever the
projected curve stays in the open fibre.

## 6. Minimal remaining gate

Before any normal splitting computation is meaningful, one must prove all
of the following on one named proper model \(\bar V\):

1. \(g_C\) is nonconstant;
2. \(g_C\) extends across the special scroll parameters;
3. the limiting sheaves and boundary strata are identified; and
4. the elementary modifications of (2.1)--(2.3) at those parameters are
   computed.

Only then can boundary contributions overcome the degree-zero obstruction.
This is the first exact obstruction, so the bounded attack stops here.
