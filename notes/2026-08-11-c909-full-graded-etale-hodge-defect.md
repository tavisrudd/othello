# C909 — full integral Hodge/product defect for one-depth etale graph packets

Date: 2026-08-11

Status: candidate all-degree formula with exact low-degree checks and a major
open integral-saturation lemma.  The codimension-two specialization is
proved; the general formula is not yet a theorem.  No manuscript, PDF,
mirror, Lean, or commit change.

## Verdict

The finite-etale graph theorem gives full divided-power saturation of the
Neron--Severi lattice, while exact calculations show a nonzero ambient
integral-Hodge defect.  The formula below is the strongly supported
all-degree candidate; its integral filtered-matching lemma remains open.

Let `R` be a DVR with uniformizer `p`, let the graph have depth `a>0`, and
suppose that, after finite unramified extension, its self-adjoint slope is

\[
 T=\operatorname {diag}(t_1,\ldots,t_g),\qquad
 t_i-t_j\in R^\times \quad(i\ne j).
\tag{1}
\]

Let `E` be non-CM, let `A` be the resulting principally polarized quotient,
and put

\[
 I_A^k=\operatorname {Hdg}^{2k}(A,R),\qquad
 P_A^k=\operatorname {im}(\operatorname {Sym}^k\operatorname {NS}(A)
               \longrightarrow I_A^k).
\tag{2}
\]

For `1<=h<=ell`, let `H(ell,h)` be the number of Dyck paths of semilength
`ell` and *exact* height `h`; thus

\[
 (H(1,*),H(2,*),H(3,*),H(4,*),H(5,*))
 =(1);(1,1);(1,3,1);(1,7,5,1);(1,15,18,7,1).
\tag{3}
\]

The candidate full local quotient is

\[
 \boxed{\displaystyle
 I_A^k/P_A^k\simeq
 \bigoplus_{\ell=2}^{\min(k,g-k)}\ \bigoplus_{h=1}^{\ell-1}
 \left(R/p^{a(\ell-h)}R\right)^{
 N(g,k,\ell)H(\ell,h)},
 \qquad
 N(g,k,\ell)=
 \binom g{k+\ell}\binom{k+\ell}{k-\ell}.
 }
\tag{4}
\]

Conditional on the saturated filtered-matching lemma in Section 3, the
direct sum is an isomorphism of finite `R`-modules; its individual summands
are not canonically labelled before splitting.  Formula (4) would then
descend unchanged through a finite unramified splitting extension.  Away
from the graph prime the quotient is zero.

In particular, the squarefree `2k`-slot component is

\[
 \bigoplus_{h=1}^{k-1}
 \left(R/p^{a(k-h)}R\right)^{H(k,h)}.
\tag{5}
\]

This refutes the tempting but false statement that every `2k`-slot component
is merely `(R/p^a)^{C_k-1}`.  Already at `k=3` it is

\[
 (R/p^a)^3\oplus R/p^{2a},
\tag{6}
\]

not four exponent-one copies.  The largest elementary-divisor exponent on a
squarefree `2k`-support is exactly `p^{a(k-1)}`.

## 1. Split graph normalization

Write `x_i,y_i` for source covectors and use the actual graph convention

\[
 X_i=p^a x_i,\qquad Y_i=y_i-t_i x_i.
\tag{7}
\]

For the rational contraction invariants

\[
 \Omega_{ii}=x_i\wedge y_i,\qquad
 \Omega_{ij}=x_i\wedge y_j+x_j\wedge y_i
\tag{8}
\]

we have

\[
 \Omega_{ii}=p^{-a}X_iY_i,qquad
 \Omega_{ij}=p^{-a}(X_iY_j+X_jY_i)
   +p^{-2a}\delta_{ij}X_iX_j,quad
 \delta_{ij}=t_j-t_i\in R^\times.
\tag{9}
\]

Therefore the complete split Neron--Severi lattice has primitive slots

\[
 D_i=p^a\Omega_{ii},\qquad C_{ij}=p^{2a}\Omega_{ij}.
\tag{10}
\]

This is exactly the one-depth finite-etale matrix-of-ideals calculation.  It
uses neither a trace pairing nor a factor `1/2`; hence it applies at the
dyadic prime as well.  If the source unit form is `B` rather than the identity,
the etale root summands are `B`-orthogonal and their one-dimensional
restrictions are units.  Rescaling them reduces to (7)--(10), so no diagonal
integral normal form for `B` is being assumed.

## 2. Multidegree reduction

The rational `SL_2` invariant space in degree `2k` is multigraded by the
number of appearances of each split slot.  A nonzero component has type

\[
 (2^{k-\ell},1^{2\ell})
 \quad(0\leq\ell\leq\min(k,g-k)).
\tag{11}
\]

Indeed, a slot can occur at most twice; a twice-occurring slot contributes
the invariant volume `\Omega_{ii}`, leaving `2ell` singly occurring slots.
Thus the rational component for a fixed type (11) is canonically the product
of its `k-ell` volume forms with the squarefree matching module on its `2ell`
single slots.  The number of components of this type is

\[
 N(g,k,\ell)=\binom g{k+\ell}\binom{k+\ell}{k-\ell},
\tag{12}
\]

since one selects the `k+ell` used slots and then selects the `k-ell` doubled
ones.

On such a component the volume forms contribute the common factor
`p^{a(k-ell)}` to both the integral Hodge lattice and the product lattice.
The latter has, on the squarefree residual matching module, the scale
`p^{2aell}` by (10).  It remains only to calculate that squarefree module.

## 3. The filtered matching lemma

Fix `2ell` slots.  Let `M_ell` be the free rank-`C_ell` standard matching
module: it is generated by products of the `\Omega_{ij}` over perfect
matchings, modulo the coefficient-one Pluecker skein relations.  Its usual
noncrossing matching basis identifies it with the Temperley--Lieb/Web module,
and has rank the Catalan number `C_ell`.

Define `F^rM_ell` to be the submodule whose expansion in (9) has no monomial
with fewer than `r` letters among the `Y_i`.  The following is the integral
standard-monomial fact used here.

> **Filtered matching conjecture / proof gate.**  If all `delta_ij` are units, there is an
> `R`-basis `e_gamma` of `M_ell`, indexed by the Dyck paths `gamma` of
> semilength `ell`, for which
> 
> \[
>  e_\gamma\in F^{\ell-\operatorname {ht}(\gamma)}M_\ell
>  \setminus F^{\ell-\operatorname {ht}(\gamma)+1}M_\ell.
> \tag{13}
> \]
> 
> Equivalently,
> \[
> \operatorname {rank}_R(F^rM_\ell/F^{r+1}M_\ell)
>       =H(\ell,\ell-r)\quad(0\leq r<\ell).
> \tag{14}
> \]

Here is the proposed structural route.  It does not yet prove saturation or
unit pivots.  Formally factor `p^{-2a}X_1\cdots X_{2ell}` from a matching product
in (9), and set formally `z_i=Y_i/X_i`.  Each edge becomes

\[
       \delta_{ij}+p^a(z_j-z_i).
\tag{15}
\]

The relation between the three matchings of four labels is the ordinary
three-term Pluecker relation for the differences
`u_j-u_i`, with `u_i=t_i-p^az_i`.  Straighten crossings by this relation,
and eliminate coefficients in increasing `z`-degree.  Reading the
noncrossing web from left to right, the open-arc count is the height of its
Dyck path.  The elimination that kills all terms of degree below `r` retains
exactly the webs whose path never rises above `ell-r`.  At every elimination
step the pivot is a product of differences `delta_ij`; it is a unit by (1).
To conclude, one must exhibit an integral transition matrix whose relevant
minors are monomials in the unit differences `delta_ij`.  The formal symbols
`z_i=Y_i/X_i` are only mnemonic because exterior variables are not
invertible.  Moreover, a nonzero associated-graded vector need not be
primitive over a DVR.  Saturated graded quotients with unit pivots—including
after reduction at `p=2`—are the exact missing lemma.

For a numerical description that does not choose webs, let `B(ell,h)` be the
number of Dyck paths of semilength `ell` of height at most `h`.  Then

\[
 H(\ell,h)=B(\ell,h)-B(\ell,h-1),
\tag{16}
\]

and `B(ell,h)` is obtained by the elementary bounded-walk recurrence on the
states `0,1,...,h`.  This is also the recurrence used by the preceding
open-arc elimination.

The expansion (9) now shows that `p^{a(2ell-r)}F^rM_ell` is integral, and
that a vector not in `F^{r+1}` requires exactly this scale.  Applying the
basis in (13) gives the exact diagonal form

\[
 \begin{aligned}
 I_{\mathrm{sq},\ell}
   &=\bigoplus_{\gamma}
       p^{a(\ell+\operatorname {ht}(\gamma))}R e_\gamma,\\
 P_{\mathrm{sq},\ell}
   &=p^{2a\ell}M_\ell,\\
 I_{\mathrm{sq},\ell}/P_{\mathrm{sq},\ell}
   &=\bigoplus_{h=1}^{\ell-1}
       \left(R/p^{a(\ell-h)}R\right)^{H(\ell,h)}.
 \end{aligned}
\tag{17}
\]

The `h=ell` paths produce unit elementary divisors and hence no quotient.
For `ell=2`, (17) is exactly the four-slot Pluecker class of the earlier
codimension-two theorem.

## 4. Conditional deduction of the full formula and descent

Assuming the saturated filtered-matching lemma, multiplying (17) by the
`k-ell` volume factors from (11) changes both scales
by the same `p^{a(k-ell)}`.  Therefore it leaves the quotient (17) unchanged.
There are exactly (12) independent multidegree components of that type.
Summing over `ell` would prove (4) after splitting.  A complete proof must
also show integrally that multiplication by the doubled-slot volume factors
identifies a primitive direct summand; rational multidegree factorization
alone is insufficient.

For an unsplit finite-etale slope, take a finite unramified faithfully flat
splitting extension `O/R`.  Both `I_A^k` and `P_A^k` commute with this finite
free base change.  The elementary-divisor multiplicities in (4) can be read
from the ranks of

\[
 p^{r-1}(I_A^k/P_A^k)/p^r(I_A^k/P_A^k),
\tag{18}
\]

which are unchanged by faithful flat extension.  Hence the split Smith form
descends to the identical Smith form over `R`.  Root permutations remove the
labels on the multidegree summands but do not alter their multiplicities.

At primes away from the graph degree the isogeny is an integral-local
isomorphism with the literal elliptic power.  Its integral invariant tensor
lattice is generated by contraction products, so no quotient remains there.

## 5. Checks and consequences

* `k=2` has only `ell=2,h=1`, giving
  \((R/p^a)^{\binom g4}\), the codimension-two theorem.
* `g=5,k=3` has only `(ell,h)=(2,1)` and `N(5,3,2)=5`, hence
  \(I_A^3/P_A^3\simeq(R/p^a)^5\).  These are the five
  `(2,1,1,1,1)` multidegrees.
* `g=6,k=3` gives
  \[
  (R/p^a)^{33}\oplus R/p^{2a}:
  \]
  thirty exponent-one classes from `(2,1^4)`, and on the squarefree six-slot
  component three exponent-one plus one exponent-two class.
* There is no defect in degrees `k=0,1,g-1,g`.  The first defect is precisely
  the four-slot codimension-two cancellation.  Formula (4) also exhibits the
  maximal possible exponent `p^{a(k-1)}` in a degree-`k` squarefree component.

## Boundaries

This theorem concerns the ambient **integral Hodge** lattice for a non-CM
elliptic-power quotient.  It does not assert a Chow realization, replace
`PDDef^*=0` by an ambient-Hodge statement, treat colliding/non-etale slopes,
or classify unequal-depth graphs.  The finite-etale hypothesis is used
exactly to make every `delta_ij` a unit in the web straightening.

## Mystery ledger

* **Strongly supported, not settled:** the whole one-depth finite-etale
  Hodge/product defect appears to be a Dyck-height Smith filtration.  Exact
  checks through squarefree semilength six support (4), but the integral
  unit-pivot lemma remains open.
* **Settled at `ell=2`; predicted generally:** the individual four-slot
  quotient is the first layer.  Higher exact computations exhibit elementary
  divisors through `p^{a(k-1)}`.
* **Settled:** finite-etale `PDDef^*=0` and the nonzero ambient Hodge defect
  coexist without tension, because their ambient lattices are different.
* **Boundary:** arbitrary-depth filtered graph data requires a weighted web
  straightening theorem; it is not claimed here.
