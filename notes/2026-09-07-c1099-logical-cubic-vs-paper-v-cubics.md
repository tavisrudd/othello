# The C1099 logical cubic inside Paper V's invariant pencil

Date: 2026-09-07. Lane: `clebsch`. Working directory:
`notes/2026-09-07-c1099-cubic-phase-strengthening/paperv/`.

Question (Sol, 2026-09-07): after restricting the p = 11 logical cubic `F_11` to the
five-dimensional `A_5`-summand of the logical space, which member of the invariant cubic
pencil of *Chordal and Conference Cubics* (Paper V) is it, and how do the sheet exchange
and Paper V's outer involution `q` act on it?

**Answer.** It is a chordal cubic — one of the two, never the conference cubic. The sheet
exchange acts on the shadow as `-1`, fixing the chordal line and inverting the gate. Paper
V's `q` exchanges the two chordal members by an explicit orthogonal involution of the
five-dimensional space, so the residual `C_2`-torsor is a Clifford frame change on the
shadow, not an invisible one and not a change of gate class.

## 1. Setup and conventions

All arithmetic is exact over `F_11` (`F_7` in section 8). The configuration is rebuilt from
the memo recipe rather than transcribed, so the E11 matrix and the cubic `B2` of
`notes/2026-09-06-clebsch-quantum-replay/common.py` serve as independent checks.

Conic `Q = XZ - Y^2`, conic points `a -> (1:a:a^2)` and `inf -> (0:0:1)`, normalized secant
factors `L_ab = abX - (a+b)Y + Z` and `L_a,inf = aX - Y`, base matching
`M_11 = {01, 25, 37, 49, 68, 10-inf}`, `P_M = prod L_ab`, and `x_M = (P_M - P_{M_11})/Q`, a
quartic form read off at the ten monomials of `EXP11`. The logical cubic is
`F_11(u) = sum_i eps_i (x_i . u)^3`, the signed third moment `mu_3`; `eps` is `+1` on the
`PSL_2(11)`-orbit of `M_11` and `-1` on the other. No rescaling of `P_M` is needed: the
memo's literal products already satisfy `P_M = P_{M_11} mod Q` for all 22 matchings.

The 22 reconstructed vectors reproduce the E11 row set exactly, with the same split into
sheets, and `sum_i eps_i (x_i . u)^3` equals `B2` on the nose, not merely up to a scalar.

## 2. Validating the group action

`PGL_2(11)` permutes the 22 matchings transitively. On the ten-dimensional span the induced
map is affine: for every one of the 1320 elements there is a unique `(A_g, b_g)` with
`x_{gM} = A_g x_M + b_g` for all 22 points, verified pointwise. Exactly 60 elements have
`b_g = 0`, and they are the stabilizer of `M_11`. Affineness rather than linearity is
forced by `x_{M_11} = 0`; the translations drop out of `F_11` because the signed moments
`mu_0, mu_1, mu_2` all vanish.

On the logical (`u`-) space the action is the contragredient `rho(g) = (A_g^{-1})^T`. It is
a homomorphism (checked on 3600 sampled pairs) and
`F_11(rho(g) u) = chi(g) F_11(u)` with `chi(g) = +1` exactly when `det g` is a square holds
for all 660 elements of `PSL_2(11)` and all 660 of the outer coset, with no failures — the
p = 11 analogue of the p = 7 check in
`notes/2026-09-07-c1090-resource-classification/check3.py`. The stabilizer is `A_5`:
1 + 15 + 20 + 24 elements of orders 1, 2, 3, 5, generated by `a = [[0,1],[2,3]]` and
`b = [[0,1],[7,8]]` with `a^3 = b^5 = (ab)^2 = 1`.

## 3. The `A_5`-decomposition of the logical space

Traces of `rho` restricted to the stabilizer are `10, 2, 1, 0` on elements of order
`1, 2, 3, 5`, so the character is `chi_W = (10, 2, 1, 0, 0)` and `W|_{A_5} = 1 + V_4 + V_5`.
Character idempotents over `F_11` (60 is invertible) have ranks `1, 4, 5` for the trivial,
four- and five-dimensional constituents, and rank `0` for `V_3 + V_3'`, matching the
character table displayed in Paper V section 2. The five-dimensional summand `W` is unique
and `A_5`-stable.

In the memo's invariant coordinates the same statement reads more sharply. Setting
`u = (z_0, z_1, 7z_2, 6z_3, 3s+6z_4, s+3z_4, 6z_5, 7z_6, z_7, z_8)`, the gate identity
`F_11 = 4 s I_2(z) + 2 I_3(z)` of memo section 4.2 is confirmed at 200 random points, and
both the `V_4` and the `V_5` summands lie in the hyperplane `s = 0`, i.e. inside the
nine-dimensional space of binary octavics `Sym^8`, while the `A_5`-trivial summand has
`s != 0`. Hence

```
F_11 restricted to W  =  2 I_3 restricted to W,
```

the classical cubic invariant `I_3 = ((f,f)_4, f)_8` of the binary octavic, restricted to
the five-dimensional icosahedral constituent of `Sym^8`.

## 4. The equivariant identification with Paper V's augmentation module

The six-set is the set of pairs of `M_11`, in the order
`{0,1}, {10,inf}, {2,5}, {3,7}, {4,9}, {6,8}`; the stabilizer acts on it transitively with
`D_10` point stabilizers, giving the image `A_5 < S_6` of order 60. `A_M` is the
augmentation module in the basis `f_a = e_a - e_5`.

`dim Hom_{A_5}(A_M, W) = 1`, so the bridge `T` is unique up to a scalar; the chosen `T` is
verified equivariant on all 60 group elements and is invertible. Write `psi = W . T` for
the resulting map `A_M -> u`-space and `c(y) = F_11(psi y)` for the restricted cubic. `c`
is `A_5`-invariant, and `dim Sym^3(A_M^*)^{A_5} = 2`, matching Paper V's `dim Pi = 2`.

Independent confirmation against Paper V's own certificate: 60 relabellings of the six axes
carry `c` onto the vector `projected_sheet_cubic_raw` of
`papers/chordal-conference-reconstruction/verification/evidence/paper_ii_chordal_axis.json`,
and 120 relabellings carry our conference triangle cubic onto their
`conference_triangle_cubic`. The two computations start from different bases — that one
from Paper II's frozen reduced-pivot basis and intertwiner, this one rebuilt from the
matchings — and land on the same cubic.

The logical shadow is member 9. Its singular locus over `F_11` is 12 points, every
five-subset of which is a basis — the signature of a rational normal quartic, and the
`|P^1(F_11)|` count Paper V's special-orbit proposition predicts. Each singular point has a
`C_5` stabilizer in `A_5`, so the singular scheme is the split degree-twelve `A_5/C_5`
scheme `Z_5`. Matching those twelve points with `P^1(F_11)` equivariantly and building the
resulting projectivity `L` gives

```
c(L z) = 8 * det [[z0,z1,z2],[z1,z2,z3],[z2,z3,z4]],
L = [[1,2,1,0,0],[0,0,1,9,1],[3,7,7,6,4],[4,5,7,4,3],[5,4,4,7,5]].
```

The equivariant matching works only after twisting by the outer automorphism of `A_5`; the
direct matching fails. That is exactly Paper V's remark that "the direct comparison fails
this test and the outer-twisted comparison passes it", reproduced here from the other end.
The scalar `8` agrees numerically with Paper V's `U(h_M) = 8H`, though the two `8`s come
from different normalizations and the agreement is not forced.

The conference cubic is member 10, built here from scratch: among all `2^15` Seidel signings
on the six pairs, 64 are conference matrices (`S^2 = 5I`) whose triple-sign function is
`A_5`-invariant; they form two switching classes `[B]` and `[-B]`, giving the opposite
triangle cubics `+-c_B`, hence one projective conference line. Its singular locus is the six
nodes and its census over `F_11^5` is `(1, 0, 0, 300, 22260, 138490)`, exactly the C1099
report's six-variable conference census `(11, 3300, 244860, 1523390)` divided by 11.

**Clifford-invariant separation.** The chordal census `(1,0,0,120,27720,133210)` and the
conference census `(1,0,0,300,22260,138490)` differ. Since the Hessian-rank census is a
Clifford invariant of a cubic-phase state, this upgrades Paper V's "not projectively
isomorphic over `\bar F_11`" to "not Clifford-equivalent as five-qudit resources", on the
same five-dimensional space rather than through the non-concise six-variable form used in
section 6 item 7 of the C1099 report. As magic resources the two are close but unequal:
`M_2 = 10.6517` nats for the chordal shadow against `10.7070` for the conference cubic
(five qudits at `p = 11`; the five-cubic-phase-qudit product bound is `8.7565`, the
pure-state maximum `11.2963`). Restricted to the augmentation the conference cubic is
marginally the more magic, reversing the six-variable comparison in which it was not even
concise.

## 6. The two torsors

**Sheet exchange (global negation).** Reversing the sheets replaces `eps` by `-eps` and
hence `F_11` by `-F_11`, i.e. `U_F` by `U_F^{-1}`. On the logical space this is realized by
`-I`, which is `A_5`-equivariant, preserves `W`, and sends `c` to `-c`; the projective
member is unchanged. It is not induced by any element of `PGL_2(11)`: `-I` is not in
`rho(PGL_2(11))`, and the 22-point configuration is not symmetric under `x -> -x`. Nor can
an outer-coset element do the job while preserving `W`, because
`N_{PGL_2(11)}(A_5) = A_5` — 60 elements, none outside `PSL_2(11)` — so exactly the 60
`A_5`-elements preserve `W`, and all of them fix `F_11`. The negation is an extra symmetry
of the cubic, not a geometric one, and it acts trivially on the pencil.

**Paper V's residual torsor `q`.** Take the outer permutation `tau = [1,0,4,5,2,3]` of the
six pairs (odd, normalizing the `A_5`-image; there are 60 such, 10 of them involutions) and
let `q` be its augmentation matrix. Then `q` is an involution preserving the standard
quadratic form `Q_0` with Gram matrix `I + J`, and is *not* `A_5`-equivariant — it twists
the action by the outer automorphism. On the pencil its orbits are
`0<->11, 1 fixed, 2<->6, 3<->4, 5<->9, 7<->8, 10 fixed`: it fixes the conference member
(the `-1` eigenline) and member 1 (the `+1` eigenline), and exchanges the two chordal
members. In the basis `(c_B, h)`,

```
q(c_B) = -c_B,      q(h) = alpha c_B + h,     alpha != 0,
```

so `[q_Pi] = [[-1, alpha], [0, 1]]`, Paper V's Proposition "Normalized outer action". In
this note's raw normalization `alpha = 6`; after relabelling the axes to Paper V's and
using Paper V's own outer permutation `(3 4 5 6)` — whose augmentation matrix is checked
identical to the `Q` displayed in the paper — the pivot-normalized value is exactly the
paper's: `[x_0^2 x_1] q(h) = 9` and `(q - 1)h = 8 c_B`.

## 7. Verdict on Sol's conjecture

1. **The global-negation torsor is the gate inversion.** Confirmed. Sheet reversal negates
   `mu_3`, hence `F_11` and hence the shadow cubic; on the ten-qudit gate this is
   `U_F -> U_F^{-1}`, and on the shadow it is multiplication by `-1`, which fixes the
   chordal line in `P(Pi)`. The chordal-versus-conference distinction is untouched by it.

2. **The residual chordal-line torsor is a Clifford frame change, not invisible.**
   Confirmed in the second alternative. `q` moves the shadow from one chordal member to the
   other, so it is visible: the two are distinct actual cubics on the same space. But the
   single explicit `Q_0`-isometry `q` carries one to the other, and a linear change of
   logical coordinates is a logical Clifford, so the two chordal gates are
   Clifford-equivalent — same Hessian census, same magic, same singular geometry.
   Sharpening: `q` is a symmetry of the *shadow*, not of the gate. Extending it by the
   identity on `1 + V_4` does not preserve `F_11` up to any scalar, and no element of
   `PGL_2(11)` induces `q` on `W`, so the torsor's Clifford realization acts on the five
   logical directions carrying the shadow, not on the full ten-qudit register.

## 8. The p = 7 analogue

At p = 7 there is no `A_5`; the symmetry is `S_4` inside `PGL_2(7)` and no projection is
needed. In the memo's coordinates `u = (a, b, s+c, 3s+c, d, e)` the identity
`F_7 = 4 s I + 3 J` with `I = ae - 4bd + 3c^2` and `J = det[[a,b,c],[b,c,d],[c,d,e]]` is
confirmed at 200 random points. Restricting to `s = 0` — the analogue of projecting out the
trivial summand — gives `3J`, three times the Hankel determinant of the binary quartic.

Over `F_7` the singular locus of `J` is 8 projective points, `|P^1(F_7)| = 8`, in general
position: the rational normal quartic. So the p = 7 logical cubic is chordal in exactly the
same sense as the p = 11 shadow, without any restriction step. Its Hessian-rank census over
`F_7^5` is `(1, 0, 0, 48, 4368, 12390)`, against `(1, 2796, 14010)` for ranks `(0,4,5)` of
the p = 7 conference triangle cubic on the augmentation (the C1099 six-variable census
`(7, 19572, 98070)` divided by 7). Again chordal and conference are not Clifford-equivalent.

The rank-3 count `48` reappears from the six-variable p = 7 Clebsch census
`(1, 48, 2940, 26502, 88158)` of C1090: the minimum-rank locus lies entirely in `s = 0`.

## 10. Not established

Singular loci are counted over `F_11` and `F_7` only; that the singular scheme is reduced
with no embedded or non-rational component is not proved here (Paper V's Hankel
singular-locus lemma and its Hilbert-numerator checker cover it, and the explicit
projectivity `L` onto the standard Hankel model makes the shadow's identification
unconditional). That `q` does not lift to a symmetry of the full ten-qudit cubic is shown
only for the identity-on-`(1 + V_4)` extension and for every element of `PGL_2(11)`; ruling
out all lifts needs the linear automorphism group of `F_11` up to scalars, which would
follow from a uniqueness theorem for the signed 22-point Waring decomposition of `mu_3`
that is not available. "Clifford frame change" uses the standard identification of
`GL_k(F_p)` with the linear part of the logical Clifford group modulo Paulis and phases; it
is used, not re-derived. The magic figures in section 5 describe the five-qudit
cubic-phase state of each shadow cubic, not a transversal gate on a five-qudit code — the
shadow is a projection of the logical space, not a subcode.

## 11. Mystery ledger

1. **Member 1 of the pencil.** The `+1` eigenline of `q_Pi` has 10 singular `F_11`-points
   spanning the space but not in general position, and is the only member with rank-two
   Hessian points (`N_2 = 150`). Neither Paper V nor C1099 names it. Its geometry is
   unidentified; the natural guess is a member with a singular locus of dimension zero but
   degree ten, or a nonreduced structure. Open, cheap to settle with a Hilbert-series
   computation.
2. **Why the conference cubic is the more magic shadow.** On the augmentation the
   conference cubic beats the chordal one, `10.7070` against `10.6517` nats, while on the
   six-variable ambient space the conference cubic is not even concise. The driver is the
   rank-four stratum, not the minimum-rank locus: the chordal shadow has the *smaller*
   minimum-rank locus (12 projective points against 30) but the larger rank-four count
   (27720 against 22260), and at `alpha = 2` the rank-four term carries the sum. Why the
   larger symmetry group buys a smaller minimum-rank locus and yet a fatter rank-four
   stratum is not explained.
3. **The scalar 8.** `c(L z) = 8 H` in this note's frame and `U(h_M) = 8H` in Paper V's are
   the same number reached through different normalizations. Whether that is forced or
   coincidental is not settled here.
4. Settled by this pass, previously open: the exact identification of the C1099 logical
   cubic with Paper V's chordal generator, flagged as "immediate" and deferred in section 6
   item 7 of the C1099 report. It is now computed, in both directions, and cross-checked
   against Paper V's own certificate vector.
