# C904: the universal cubic projector retains the gamma factor two

Date: 2026-08-11

Status: theorem-grade no-go for the non-divisor universal-family/CK route;
Paper V research only; no manuscript or Lean change

## Verdict

The integral middle Chow--Kunneth projector of the cubic threefold does not
remove the two-local obstruction on
\(M=\operatorname {Bl}_0\Theta\).

Let \(\mathcal E\) be the universal sheaf on \(M\times X\).  The projector
\(\pi_3^X\) is algebraic over \(\mathbf Z_{(2)}\): all its non-diagonal
terms have only denominator three.  The class \(c_2(\mathcal E)\) supplies
the primitive Abel--Jacobi block

\[
                         H^3(X)\longrightarrow H^1(M).
\]

To reverse the other leg one needs the
\(H^5(M)\otimes H^3(X)\) component of a codimension-four universal Chern
expression.  That component has an unavoidable even primitive coefficient.
Writing

\[
 f_i=[\operatorname {ch}_i(\mathcal E)]_
          {H^{2i-3}(M)\otimes H^3(X)},
\]

the exact gamma-filtration relation is

\[
 [c_4(\mathcal E)]_{5,3}
       =-6f_4+2D f_3+S f_2,                                \tag{0.1}
\]

for integral divisor/surface expressions \(D,S\) (their precise values
depend on the normalization of the universal sheaf).  The first two terms
are visibly even.  The last term is decomposable, and its induced p51
action is even because the theta-restriction/Gysin contraction contains
\(\Theta^2=2\Theta^{[2]}\).

Consequently every p51 correspondence obtained by composing the universal
Chern classes through \(\pi_3^X\) acts through

\[
                         2\operatorname {End}(H^1(M)).
\]

The integral diagonal formula and the fine universal family therefore do
not produce the missing odd inverse-Lefschetz correspondence.  The even
Chow--Kunneth pieces of \(X\) factor through algebraic Tate classes and
return to the already closed index-tautological p24 route.  A successful
class must be non-tautological even with respect to the universal family
and the cubic CK decomposition.

## 1. The cubic projector is integral at two

Choose a point and the hyperplane class \(H\) on the smooth cubic
threefold \(X\).  Its standard Chow--Kunneth projectors are

\[
\begin{aligned}
 \pi_0&=[x\times X],&
 \pi_2&=\frac13[H^2\times H],\\
 \pi_4&=\frac13[H\times H^2],&
 \pi_6&=[X\times x],\\
 \pi_3&=\Delta_X-\pi_0-\pi_2-\pi_4-\pi_6.
\end{aligned}                                               \tag{1.1}
\]

Thus \(\pi_3\) is an algebraic idempotent with
\(\mathbf Z_{(2)}\)-coefficients.  It acts as the identity on
\(H^3(X,\mathbf Z_{(2)})\) and kills every algebraic even cohomology group.
There is no hidden dyadic denominator in the source projector.

Bayer--Beentjes--Feyzbakhsh--Hein--Martinelli--Rezaee--Schmidt identify
\(M\) with a fine moduli space and the determinant-weight calculation gives
a universal sheaf \(\mathcal E\).  Hence all Chern classes used below are
ordinary integral Chow classes.  The question is the integral coefficient
with which they contain the odd Kunneth components.

## 2. Exact odd Chern-component calculation

Only the \(H^3(X)\)-part matters.  Since \(c_1(\mathcal E)\) has no odd
cohomology on the cubic factor, the usual Newton identities give

\[
 [c_2]_{1,3}=-f_2,                                         \tag{2.1}
\]

and

\[
 [c_3]_{3,3}=2f_3+D_1f_2                                  \tag{2.2}
\]

for an integral divisor expression \(D_1\) on \(M\).  In codimension four,

\[
 c_4=-6\operatorname {ch}_4
       +\frac{c_1^4}{4}-c_1^2c_2+rac{c_2^2}{2}+c_1c_3.   \tag{2.3}
\]

Project (2.3) to \(H^5(M)\otimes H^3(X)\).  The \(c_1^4\) term has no odd
component.  Using (2.1)--(2.2), every remaining lower term is either twice
a divisor times \(f_3\), or a surface times \(f_2\).  The apparent half in
\(c_2^2/2\) disappears on the odd cross term: the two orders of the cross
term cancel the denominator.  This proves (0.1) integrally at two.

The same conclusion holds for every integral codimension-four Chern
polynomial in \(\mathcal E\).  Such a polynomial is generated by

\[
 c_4,quad c_1c_3,quad c_2^2,quad c_1^2c_2,quad c_1^4.
\]

Its primitive \(f_4\) term can only come from \(c_4\), with coefficient a
multiple of six.  The other terms have the two forms already identified in
(0.1).

This is exactly the codimension-four gamma-filtration factorial
\((4-1)!=6\), now localized sharply: the factor three is harmless over
\(\mathbf Z_{(2)}\), while the factor two survives.

## 3. Composition through \(H^3(X)\)

Let \(A\in CH^2(X\times M)_{\mathbf Z_{(2)}}\) be the transpose of the
\(c_2\) block and let
\(B\in CH^4(M\times X)_{\mathbf Z_{(2)}}\) be any universal
codimension-four Chern expression.  Form

\[
          \Gamma=A\circ\pi_3^X\circ B
             \in CH^3(M\times M)_{\mathbf Z_{(2)}}.         \tag{3.1}
\]

The codimension is

\[
                       4+3+2-3-3=3.
\]

Its p51 action is the contraction of (2.1) with (0.1).  The
\(-6f_4\) and \(2Df_3\) contributions are even.  The remaining
\(Sf_2\) contribution is a Poincare/Abel--Jacobi cross class coupled to an
integral surface on \(M\).  On the Lefschetz copy
\(hH^1(M)\subset H^3(M)\), its matrix entries have the form

\[
                       \int_M h^2Suv,
\]

or equivalently the ambient contraction with \(\Theta^2\).  Since
\(\Theta^2=2\Theta^{[2]}\) integrally, this term is also even.  Therefore

\[
                 \Gamma_*|_{hH^1(M)}\in
                       2\operatorname {End}(H^1(M)).        \tag{3.2}
\]

This conclusion is invariant under twisting the universal sheaf: twisting
only changes \(D\) and \(S\), while the three-term shape (0.1) and the
parity proof remain unchanged.

The most direct proposed class

\[
 c_2(\mathcal E)^t\circ\pi_3^X\circ c_4(\mathcal E)
\]

is a special case of (3.1) and hence cannot induce the identity.

## 4. Why the diagonal does not bypass the calculation

The fine moduli space has the integral Porteous identity

\[
 [\Delta_M]=c_4\!left(-R\pi_{13*}
 R\mathcal Hom(\mathcal E_1,\mathcal E_2)\right).           \tag{4.1}
\]

Equation (4.1) is an undecomposed identity correspondence.  To obtain the
desired degree-lowering codimension-three cycle one must factor its odd
part through \(\pi_3^X\) and select the \((5,3)\) universal component.
Sections 2--3 show that every integral Chern-class selection of that
component carries the factor two.  Using Chern characters divides by six;
over \(\mathbf Z_{(2)}\) this is exactly the forbidden division by two.

Thus the integral diagonal proves no more than the diagonal itself.  A
rational decomposition follows by GRR, as in Li--Lin--Pertusi--Zhao, but an
integral-at-two decomposition would be precisely the missing new theorem,
not a consequence of (4.1).

## 5. The p24 side

The projector \(\pi_3^X\) pairs odd cohomology on the two universal legs,
so its Kunneth outputs on \(M^2\) have odd--odd bidegrees.  It can address
p51/p33, not p42.  The even projectors in (1.1) factor through the Tate
classes \(1,H,H^2,H^3\).  Composing the universal family through them gives
the algebraic index complexes

\[
          R\pi_{M*}(\mathcal E\otimes u),
          \qquad u\in K_{\mathrm {alg}}(X).
\]

Their integral twist-invariant codimension-three expressions are exactly
the formal universal-sheaf tautological ring audited previously.  Modulo
two that ring is generated by divisor--surface products and
\(\operatorname {Sq}^2\) of surface classes, both of even theta degree.
Hence the standard universal-family/CK construction supplies no p24 escape
either.

This does not classify arbitrary non-tautological p24 correspondences.

## 6. Exact surviving boundary

The following routes are now closed at two:

1. divisor cubes and their BdGF divided halves;
2. decomposable Poincare--surface p51 classes;
3. universal Chern classes composed through the integral-at-two cubic
   projector \(\pi_3^X\);
4. the undecomposed universal Ext diagonal followed by its standard
   Chern-character/GRR factorization;
5. p24 classes factoring through the even algebraic CK pieces of \(X\).

What remains is sharply defined: an algebraic codimension-three class on
\(M^2\) outside the one-cubic-factor universal Chern/CK constructions and
the divisor/lambda subrings audited above.  Equivalently, one needs a
genuinely non-split integral inverse-Lefschetz correspondence or a
non-tautological p24 class.  More elaborate iterated motivic constructions
are not classified by this calculation.

There is no theorem forcing factor two for **all** algebraic
correspondences.  Such a claim would exceed the calculation above; the
residual invariant cohomology contains odd classes, and fixed-fibre
geometric constructions show that non-tautological carriers are not
formally impossible.

## Mystery ledger (EJ + TT closeout)

- **Settled:** the cubic middle projector has no dyadic denominator.
- **Settled negatively:** selecting the universal \((5,3)\) component has
  the gamma-filtration coefficient six, and its lower terms are already in
  even decomposable channels.
- **Settled negatively:** the integral Ext diagonal does not cancel this
  coefficient; its decomposition is the step that needs division by two.
- **Open:** a correspondence outside the audited one-step
  universal-family/CK and divisor constructions.  This is now the only
  identified p51/p24 algebraicity escape.
- **Open:** whether a geometric fixed-fibre odd carrier can be made
  horizontal; the present theorem is algebraic-tautological, not a global
  impossibility result.
