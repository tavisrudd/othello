# C909 — fixed-dimension finite-etale Hecke towers with full PD saturation

Date: 2026-08-11

Status: candidate theorem with a complete local/global construction; awaiting
independent hostile and priority audits; no manuscript, PDF, mirror, Lean, or
certificate edit

## Theorem

Fix an integer `g>=2`, an odd prime `p`, and a non-CM complex elliptic curve
`E`.  There is a compatible sequence of principally polarized abelian
varieties
`(A_a,Theta_a)` and a polarized isogeny

\[
             f_a:(E^g,p^a\Theta_0)\longrightarrow(A_a,\Theta_a)
\tag{1}
\]

with the following properties.

1. The kernel is a maximal-isotropic graph whose slope algebra is the
   unramified degree-`g` algebra over `Z/p^a`.
2. `(A_a,Theta_a)` is polarized-indecomposable.
3. The full cohomological divided-power envelope of `NS(A_a)` equals its
   ordinary divisor-product image in every degree:

   \[
    \operatorname{PD}\langle\operatorname{NS}(A_a)\rangle^k
      =\operatorname{im}\bigl(
       \operatorname{Sym}^k\operatorname{NS}(A_a)
       \longrightarrow H^{2k}(A_a,\mathbf Z)\bigr)
       \qquad(0\le k\le g).
   \tag{2}
   \]

4. The varieties `A_a` are pairwise nonisomorphic, already as unpolarized
   abelian varieties.

The kernels satisfy `K_a subset K_(a+1)`, so the sequence carries natural
isogenies `A_a -> A_(a+1)`.  After adding full `p^a`-level structure to `E`,
each level spreads over a finite cover of the modular curve.  Hence every
`a` gives a non-isotrivial one-parameter family with the same properties on
its non-CM fibres.

The theorem is wholly within the finite-etale branch.  It asserts neither a
Chow identity nor an integral-Hodge theorem and uses no nilpotent or
`p`-typical classification.

## 1. The unramified self-adjoint slope

Let `O/Z_p` be the unramified extension of degree `g`.  The norm on units is
surjective, and so is the induced map on unit squareclasses.  Choose one
`c in O^times` so that
the determinant squareclass of

\[
                       h_c(x,y)=cxy
\tag{3}
\]

has trace transfer isometric to the standard unimodular form `I_g` over
`Z_p`.  Odd-primary unimodular symmetric forms are classified by rank and
determinant squareclass, so there is one isometry

\[
       (\mathbf Z_p^g,I_g)\simeq
       (O,\operatorname{Tr}_{O/\mathbf Z_p}(cxy)).
\tag{4}
\]

Choose one monogenic unramified generator `theta` of `O/Z_p`.  In the
`Z_p`-basis supplied by (4), multiplication by `theta` is represented by a
symmetric matrix `T in M_g(Z_p)`.  Put `R_a=Z/p^a`, `O_a=O/p^aO`, and let
`T_a` be the reduction of `T`.  Then

\[
                          R_a[T_a]=O_a.
\tag{5}
\]

The graph

\[
 K_a=\{(x,T_ax):x\in R_a^g\}
       \subset E[p^a]^g
\tag{6}
\]

is maximal isotropic for the Weil pairing of the product polarization
`p^a Theta_0`: self-adjointness gives isotropy, and its order is `p^(ag)`,
the square root of the kernel order.  The quotient by `K_a` therefore carries
the principal polarization `Theta_a` descending from `p^aTheta_0`.  This
proves (1).

The reductions are compatible.  In Tate coordinates the natural inclusion
`E[p^a] subset E[p^(a+1)]` sends `(x,y)` to `(px,py)`.  If `y=T_ax`, then

\[
                  pT_ax\equiv T_{a+1}(px)\pmod {p^{a+1}},
\]

so `K_a subset K_(a+1)`.  Quotienting by the inclusion produces an isogeny
`q_a:A_a -> A_(a+1)` of degree `p^g`, and comparison of the descended
polarizations gives `q_a^*Theta_(a+1)=p Theta_a`.  Thus “tower” means an
actual compatible sequence, not merely one separately chosen graph at every
level.

## 2. Full cohomological PD saturation

The source has one positive primary depth `a`, and its graph slope algebra
is literally finite etale by (5).  The C909 arbitrary-depth finite-etale
graph theorem applies.  After a finite unramified splitting extension, the
complete graph Neron--Severi coefficient lattice is generated by rank-one
forms.  Each rank-one divisor pulls back to a decomposable alternating
two-form on `E^g`, hence squares to zero.  Writing any divisor as a sum of
these square-zero classes gives

\[
                  D^{[k]}=\frac{D^k}{k!}
                  =\sum_{|I|=k}\prod_{i\in I}D_i.
\tag{7}
\]

Product-image formation commutes with the finite free unramified coefficient
extension, so faithful flatness descends (7) without a trace factor.  At all
primes other than `p`, the isogeny is integral-locally invertible.  Localizing
at every prime proves (2) over `Z`.

## 3. The exact local endomorphism order

Because `End(E)=Z`, every rational endomorphism of a variety isogenous to
`E^g` is a coefficient matrix in `M_g(Q)`.  Work at `p`.  The quotient
homology lattice is

\[
 \Lambda_a=\mathbf Z_p^{2g}
       +p^{-a}\{(x,T_ax):x\in\mathbf Z_p^g\}
       \subset\mathbf Q_p^{2g}.
\tag{8}
\]

Let `U in M_g(Q_p)` act diagonally on the two elliptic homology factors.
Preservation of the vertical integral generators `(0,y)` forces
`U in M_g(Z_p)`.  Preservation of the fractional graph generators is then
equivalent to

\[
                         [U,T_a]\equiv0\pmod{p^a}.
\tag{9}
\]

Let `O/Z_p` be the unramified degree-`g` extension lifting the compatible
system `O_a`, and choose `T in O` lifting `T_a`.  Since `T mod p` is regular
semisimple irreducible, its centralizer over `R_a` is exactly `O_a`.  Hence

\[
 \operatorname{End}(A_a)\otimes\mathbf Z_p
   =\{U\in M_g(\mathbf Z_p):[U,T]\equiv0\pmod{p^a}\}
   =O+p^aM_g(\mathbf Z_p).
\tag{10}
\]

The last equality follows by reducing `U` modulo `p^a`: condition (9) says
its reduction lies in the centralizer `O_a`; subtract an `O`-lift and divide
the remainder by `p^a`.

This lattice stabilizer is the completed global endomorphism order.  Indeed,

\[
 \operatorname{End}(A_a)
 =M_g(\mathbf Q)\cap\operatorname{End}_{\mathbf Z}(\Lambda_a).
\tag{10a}
\]

Localization of this intersection is exact because both sides are full
lattices in `M_g(Q)`.  At `p` it is the stabilizer just computed; at every
`q!=p` the quotient lattice equals the source lattice and the completion is
`M_g(Z_q)`.  This proves (10) for the completed global order and shows that
all local discriminant contributions away from `p` are constant.

The index in the maximal order is

\[
 [M_g(\mathbf Z_p):O+p^aM_g(\mathbf Z_p)]
       =p^{a(g^2-g)}.
\tag{11}
\]

Indeed, modulo `p^a` the full matrix algebra has `R_a`-rank `g^2`, while
`O_a` has rank `g`.  Under the reduced-trace pairing, the discriminant of an
order scales by the square of its index.  Therefore

\[
 v_p\bigl(\operatorname{disc}\operatorname{End}(A_a)\bigr)
      =2a(g^2-g)+C,
\tag{12}
\]

where `C` is independent of `a` (and is zero for the standard maximal order
normalization).  An isomorphism of abelian varieties identifies their
endomorphism rings and reduced-trace discriminants.  Formula (12) therefore
proves that `A_a` and `A_b` are nonisomorphic for `a!=b`.

## 4. Polarized indecomposability

A polarized product decomposition supplies a nontrivial Rosati-self-adjoint
idempotent in `End(A_a)`.  Its image under (10), reduced modulo `p^a`, is an
idempotent in the local ring `O_a`; it is therefore zero or one.  If an
integral idempotent `e` is congruent to zero modulo `p`, write `e=pX` in the
maximal order `M_g(Z_p)`.  The identity `e=e^2` gives `X=pX^2`, hence `X`
is again divisible by `p`; iteration and `p`-adic separatedness give `e=0`.
Applying this to `1-e` treats the other residue.  Thus no nontrivial
self-adjoint idempotent exists, and `(A_a,Theta_a)` is polarized-
indecomposable.  In fact this argument rules out every nontrivial integral
idempotent in the local endomorphism order.

## 5. Spreading over a modular curve

On the fine modular curve carrying full `p^a`-level structure, the constant
matrix `T_a` defines the finite flat graph subgroup (6) in the universal
elliptic curve's `p^a`-torsion.  The quotient abelian scheme and descended
principal polarization therefore spread over that curve.  The local
coefficient identities proving (2) are identities of integral cohomology
local systems, and the endomorphism-order argument is fibrewise for every
non-CM fibre.  The family is non-isotrivial because its rational weight-one
variation satisfies

\[
                         R^1(A_a)_\mathbf Q\simeq
                         R^1(E)_\mathbf Q^{\oplus g}.
\]

The elliptic variation over the modular curve is nonconstant, so the left
side cannot be a constant polarized rational variation.

CM fibres may have additional endomorphisms and additional Neron--Severi
classes.  They are excluded from the full-NS saturation, exact endomorphism-
order, indecomposability, and pairwise-distinct assertions.  The marked
coefficient sublattice retains its divided-power identities there, but no
claim is made for divided powers of the enlarged CM Neron--Severi lattice.

## 6. Scope and priority boundary

The construction of principal graph quotients and the local unramified order
`O+p^aM_g` are classical local-isogeny ingredients.  The candidate new
package is their conjunction with full ordinary integral divisor-product
saturation in all codimensions, uniformly in fixed dimension and unbounded
level.  A priority audit must check Hecke towers with prescribed endomorphism
orders before any novelty sentence is promoted.

No claim is made that the underlying locus is a new Shimura subvariety, that
the local unramified action globalizes to a number field action, or that all
integral Hodge classes are divisor products.  The result is about the marked
Hecke presentation and its underlying ppav fibres.

  prime, including even degree and the case `p|g`.
* **Settled:** the local endomorphism order is
  `O+p^aM_g(Z_p)`, so reduced-trace discriminants distinguish all levels.
* **Settled:** the tower remains in C909's finite-etale branch and gives full

* **Settled:** localization of the global stabilizer order and invariance of
