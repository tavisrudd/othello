# C909 — Abstract G-atoms, projective stabilization, and the P2 ceiling

**Date:** 2026-08-11  
**Lane:** `clebsch`  
**Status:** bounded mathematical extraction; no manuscript, PDF, mirror, or Lean change.

## Verdict

KKPYY give an exact abstract-atom criterion, and the projective-bundle
corollary used by the epilogue is correct after one missing hypothesis is
restored: the **total dimension** of the projective bundle must be at least
two.  The criterion is a statement about an abstract \(G\)-atom (a class
after quotienting by disjoint-union, blowup, and projective-bundle
equivalences), not merely about a connected spectral-cover component or a
geometric atomic \(F\)-bundle.

Let

\[
 h_G(\alpha):=\min\{\dim W:\;W/K\text{ smooth projective and }
       \alpha\in\operatorname{CF}_G(W)\}
\]

be the abstract carrier height.  If \(Z\) has dimension \(n\), \(V\) has
rank \(R\ge2\), \(s=R-1\), and \(D=n+s\ge2\), then

\[
 \alpha\in\operatorname{CF}_G(Z),\qquad h_G(\alpha)>D-2
 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad
 \mathbf P_Z(V)\text{ is not }K\text{-rational}.
 \tag{C909.1}
\]

Equivalently, replace the height inequality by

\[
 \alpha\notin \operatorname{Atoms}^{K}_{G,\,\dim\le D-2}.
 \tag{C909.2}
\]

For a carrier \(Z\) of minimal height \(h_G(\alpha)=n\), this abstract
criterion reaches fiber dimension \(s=1\) and stops exactly at \(s=2\):
for \(s\ge2\), the same atom is already represented by \(Z\), a \(D-2\)-fold
or lower.  Thus the KKPYY atom filtration cannot prove the \(\mathbf P^2\)
case for the cubic atom; it is not a source-level gap in the one-step proof.

The next obstruction for \(X\times\mathbf P^2\) is therefore an enriched
cubic-isotypic carrier bound, not a larger abstract carrier height.  In the
notation already owned by C907, the required carrier statement is the
threefold bound

\[
 \ell_{1/6}(Z)\le1
 \qquad\text{for every smooth projective threefold }Z,
 \tag{C909.3}
\]

together with strict cubic-isotypic Stokes/Rees blowup additivity in
codimension two.  This is C907's analytic/integral gate and is not duplicated
here.  The KKPYY abstract \(G\)-atom formula alone has no information with
which to prove (C909.3).

## Exact KKPYY setup and formulas

The following hypotheses are part of the criterion and should travel with
any citation of it.  KKPYY §5.2 works with \(K\subset\mathbf C\), a
characteristic-zero coefficient field \(k\), a proreductive group \(G/k\), a
central element \(\epsilon_G\) of order two, and a
\((G,\epsilon_G)\)-symmetric Weil cohomology theory

\[
 H^\bullet:\mathcal C_K\longrightarrow \operatorname{Rep}_k(G)
\]

on smooth projective \(K\)-varieties (the paper takes theories factoring
through André motives).  The construction uses the maximal non-archimedean
A-model \(F\)-bundle and its Euler operator.  The non-archimedean field is
enlarged to an algebraically closed Puiseux-type field when the spectral cover
is formed; this is part of the construction, not an extra geometric
hypothesis on \(X\).

For a non-empty smooth projective \(Y/K\), let \(U_Y\subset B_Y^G\) be the
locus where the reduced Euler spectral cover is unramified and write

\[
 \widetilde U_Y\longrightarrow U_Y
\]

for that cover.  KKPYY Definition 5.10 calls the finite set

\[
 \pi_0(\widetilde U_Y)
\]

the local \(G\)-atoms of \(Y\), with multiplicity equal to the degree of the
corresponding connected component over \(U_Y\).  After quotienting each local
set by \(\operatorname{Aut}(Y)\) and taking the disjoint union over isomorphism
classes of \(Y\), KKPYY quotient by the equivalence generated by:

1. disjoint union;
2. blowup \(\operatorname{Bl}_Z Y\), where \(Z\subset Y\) is smooth of
   codimension \(c\ge2\), identified with \(Y\sqcup(c-1)Z\);
3. a projective bundle \(\mathbf P_Y(E)\), where \(E\) has rank \(R\ge2\),
   identified with the disjoint union of \(R\) copies of \(Y\).

The resulting quotient is the abstract set
\(\operatorname{Atoms}^K_G\) (Definition 5.16).  Its chemical formula is the
finite positive multiset

\[
 \operatorname{CF}_G(Y)=
 \sum_{\alpha\in\operatorname{Atoms}^K_G}
       m_\alpha(Y)\,\delta_\alpha
 \in \mathbf Z_{\ge0}^{(\operatorname{Atoms}^K_G)}.
\]

The formulas needed here are (KKPYY §5.2.6.2):

\[
\begin{aligned}
 \operatorname{CF}_G(Y\sqcup Y')
   &=\operatorname{CF}_G(Y)+\operatorname{CF}_G(Y'),\\
 \operatorname{CF}_G(\operatorname{Bl}_Z Y)
   &=\operatorname{CF}_G(Y)+(c-1)\operatorname{CF}_G(Z),\\
 \operatorname{CF}_G(\mathbf P_Y(E))
   &=R\operatorname{CF}_G(Y).
\end{aligned}
\tag{C909.4}
\]

The projective-bundle input is KKPYY Theorem 4.11 (the Leray--Hirsch
decomposition): for a smooth projective \(Y/K\) and a rank-\(R\) vector bundle
\(E\to Y\), the maximal \(F\)-bundle of \(\mathbf P_Y(E)\) is canonically
identified, over connected non-empty analytic domains, with the maximal
\(F\)-bundle of a disjoint union of \(R\) copies of \(Y\).  No triviality of
\(E\), positivity, Fano hypothesis, or choice of a product structure is
required.  The blowup input is Theorem 4.5, with smooth center of codimension
at least two.

Define the dimension filtration by

\[
 \operatorname{Atoms}^{K}_{G,\dim\le q}
 :=\{\alpha:\alpha\in\operatorname{CF}_G(W)
     \text{ for some smooth projective }W/K,
     \dim W\le q\}.
\tag{C909.5}
\]

This is a filtration of **abstract** classes.  It is not the dimension of the
spectral-cover component itself and not the dimension of a chosen geometric
representative after forgetting the equivalence relation.

## Exact criterion and proof

KKPYY Proposition 5.17 says:

> If \(Y/K\) is a smooth projective variety of dimension \(D\ge2\), and an
> abstract \(G\)-atom \(\alpha\) appears in \(\operatorname{CF}_G(Y)\) but
> \(\alpha\notin\operatorname{Atoms}^{K}_{G,\dim\le D-2}\), then \(Y\) is not
> birational to \(\mathbf P^D_K\).

The dimension condition \(D\ge2\) is explicit in the proposition.  It cannot
be dropped: for a one-dimensional total space there are no codimension-two
weak-factorization centers, while the proposed filtration wording would make
the low-dimensional set vacuous and could falsely suggest an obstruction to
\(\mathbf P^1\).

For (C909.1), use (C909.4) with \(D=n+R-1=n+s\):

\[
 \operatorname{CF}_G(\mathbf P_Z(V))=(s+1)\operatorname{CF}_G(Z).
\]

The coefficient of \(\alpha\) remains positive.  If the projective bundle were
\(K\)-rational, Proposition 5.17 would force every atom in its formula into
\(\operatorname{Atoms}^{K}_{G,\dim\le D-2}\), contradicting (C909.2).

The criterion is an abstract consequence of the weak factorization theorem:
centers in a factorization of a \(D\)-fold have dimension at most \(D-2\), and
the blowup formula only adds atoms already carried by those centers.  The
projective-space formula starts with \(D+1\) copies of the point atom.  No
monodromy, Hodge polynomial, or positivity argument is present in this step.

## Three atom notions that must not be conflated

There are three levels in KKPYY's construction.

1. **Local atom:** a component of the reduced unramified spectral cover
   \(\widetilde U_Y\to U_Y\) for one chosen \(Y\), before the cross-variety
   equivalence.
2. **Geometric \(G\)-atomic \(F\)-bundle:** the \(F\)-bundle germ attached to a
   local component, modulo isomorphism and moving the point inside one
   connected component.  This is Definition 5.21's set
   \(\operatorname{Atoms}^{K,F}_G\), with geometric chemical formula
   \(\operatorname{CF}^{F}_G(Y)\).
3. **Abstract \(G\)-atom:** the quotient class in
   \(\operatorname{Atoms}^{K}_G\) after adding disjoint-union, blowup, and
   projective-bundle elementary equivalences (Definition 5.16).

KKPYY Proposition 5.22 supplies a map

\[
 \operatorname{Atoms}^{K}_G\longrightarrow
 \operatorname{Atoms}^{K,F}_G,
\tag{C909.6}
\]

by taking a representative local component to its geometric atomic
\(F\)-bundle.  The proposition does not identify the two sets or assert that
the map is injective.  Therefore:

* Proposition 5.17 and the carrier filtration use abstract classes and
  \(\operatorname{CF}_G\), not just \(\operatorname{CF}^{F}_G\).
* A formal-monodromy statistic is naturally first a statistic on geometric
  \(F\)-bundle classes.  To use it in Proposition 5.17, one must show that it
  is constant under the elementary correspondences and hence pulls back along
  (C909.6), or otherwise prove directly that the relevant abstract class is
  absent from the low-dimensional filtration.
* Equality of two geometric \(F\)-bundle classes does not, from Proposition
  5.22 alone, identify the corresponding abstract classes.  A geometric
  detector can prove abstract non-occurrence only when its values separate
  the relevant images (as the primitive-sixth-root zero/nonzero argument is
  intended to do).

The epilogue's free group on “geometric atom classes” is therefore a useful
geometric intermediate ledger, but the exact KKPYY rationality criterion should
be stated with the abstract target.  If the primitive-sixth-root multiplicity
\(\nu_6\) is retained, the needed bridge is:

\[
 \nu_6^{\rm geom}:\operatorname{Atoms}^{K,F}_G\to\mathbf Z,
 \qquad
 \nu_6^{\rm abs}:=\nu_6^{\rm geom}\circ(C909.6).
\tag{C909.7}
\]

The first map is defined by the descended formal-monodromy operator on the
original \(u\)-disc.  The blowup/projective-bundle \(F\)-bundle isomorphisms
must be checked to preserve this \(u\)-disc (KKPYY Definition 3.1 makes the
disc coordinate part of an \(F\)-bundle).  KKPYY establish the \(F\)-bundle
correspondences, but do not state (C909.7) as a primitive-root theorem; the
monodromy invariance is an additional comparison argument.  Once it is
proved, 

\[
 \nu_6^{\rm geom}(\text{low-dimensional class})=0,
 \quad
 \nu_6^{\rm geom}(\text{cubic class})>0
\]

does imply that the associated abstract cubic atom is not in the low-dimensional
filtration.  This is the precise logical use of Cai's exponent computation.

## Cubic reach and the sharp \(\mathbf P^2\) boundary

For a smooth cubic threefold \(X\), the zero block supplies a geometric class
with formal residues \(-1/6\) and \(-5/6\), hence descended monodromy
eigenvalues \(e^{-\pi i/3}\) and \(e^{\pi i/3}\).  The surface exclusion gives
zero primitive-sixth-root multiplicity for every point, curve, and surface
class.  After the bridge (C909.7), the corresponding abstract atom
\(\alpha_X\) satisfies

\[
 h_G(\alpha_X)=3.
\]

For \(X\times\mathbf P^1\), take \(V=\mathcal O_X^{\oplus2}\): \(n=3\),
\(s=1\), \(D=4\), and \(D-2=2\).  Since \(h_G(\alpha_X)=3>2\),
(C909.1) proves irrationality.  This is exactly the KKPYY abstract
criterion, not a signed cancellation argument.

For \(X\times\mathbf P^2\), \(s=2\), \(D=5\), and the threshold is
\(D-2=3\).  But \(\alpha_X\in\operatorname{CF}_G(X)\) already gives

\[
 \alpha_X\in\operatorname{Atoms}^{K}_{G,\dim\le3}.
\]

Thus the abstract carrier-height criterion is maximally sharp at one
stabilization for this carrier.  It cannot be strengthened to \(\mathbf P^2\)
by proving a larger height for the same atom: its carrier \(X\) itself fixes
the height at three.  The next obstruction must remember an enriched
filtration or extension datum that is not part of the abstract atom quotient.

C907's exact target is the appropriate replacement: the endpoint has enriched
cubic-isotypic length \(3\), while a threefold center should contribute length
at most \(1\) after a codimension-two blowup.  The formal strict comparison of
the cubic Stokes/Rees piece and the arbitrary-threefold bound are separate
gates.  They are not consequences of (C909.4), because the KKPYY atom quotient
forgets the integer/Tate placement and only retains the abstract spectral
component class.

## Test beyond the cubic \(\mathbf P^1\) application

There is real general reach at the level of a reusable theorem:

* Every top-height atom \(h_G(\alpha)=n\) on a smooth \(n\)-fold gives a
  non-rationality result for every rank-two projective bundle over that
  carrier, whenever \(n+1\ge2\).  More generally, (C909.1) handles any
  projective fiber dimension \(s\) for which \(h_G(\alpha)>n+s-2\).
* KKPYY's own Hodge-atom application to a very general cubic fourfold is a
  second geometric family for the un-stabilized criterion: their introduction
  records an atom with Hodge-class dimension at most two and Hodge-polynomial
  coefficient \([t^2]=1\), which cannot arise from a variety of dimension at
  most two; this is their Theorem 6.8.  It is prior work, not a new C909
  family, and it does not establish irrationality after multiplying by
  \(\mathbf P^1\), since that would require exclusion of threefold carriers.
* Their motivated-atom example over a non-algebraically closed field (the
  \((1,1,1,1)\) hypersurface in \((\mathbf P^1)^4\)) likewise illustrates the
  field-sensitive abstract criterion, but supplies no new stabilization
  theorem here.

No second Fano or complete-intersection **stabilization** follows from the
KKPYY formulas alone.  The formulas provide additivity, not a classification
of the low-dimensional carrier filtration.  Any claimed new family must
verify an atom's absence from dimensions \(D-2\) and, for a one-step
stabilization of an \(n\)-fold, from dimension \(n-1\).  The cubic proof has
exactly that latter surface-carrier input; no analogous arbitrary
Fano/complete-intersection input is available in the cited formulas.

## `ej` + `tt` closeout / mystery ledger

The cheap upgrade from the pass is the carrier-height form (C909.1), which
makes the stopping point a one-line inequality rather than a cubic-specific
numerical observation.  The hostile check was to apply it to the edge cases
\(D=1\), \(s=2\), and a geometric-versus-abstract class collision:

* \(D=1\) is excluded explicitly; otherwise the statement would falsely
  suggest an obstruction on \(\mathbf P^1\).
* At \(s=2\), a height-three cubic atom is already in the allowed carrier
  filtration; no positivity or gcd refinement changes this.
* The geometric-to-abstract map is only a map, so a monodromy detector must
  be shown invariant and pulled back before it can witness abstract
  non-occurrence.

Settled: the exact KKPYY criterion, its total-dimension hypothesis, the
projective-bundle height inequality, and the abstract/geometric distinction.
Remaining mystery: the enriched threefold cubic-isotypic length and strict
Stokes/Rees comparison in (C909.3), owned by C907.  No additional C909
computation or geometric census is justified by this pass.
