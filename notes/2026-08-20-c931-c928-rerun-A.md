## 3. Major findings

None.

The load-bearing checks are as follows.

1. **Cylinder adjoint -- verified.** On printed p. 5, (11), the map
   `pi_* p^*:H^3(X,Z)->H^1(F,Z)` is the cohomological correspondence defined in
   Clemens--Griffiths, §2, especially (2.7)--(2.9), and its integrality and
   invertibility follow from their Theorem 11.19 on printed p. 334, which makes
   `Alb(F)->J(X)` an isomorphism, not merely an isogeny. The adjunction identity
   is
   `int_F (pi_*p^* alpha) wedge beta = int_X alpha wedge (p_*pi^* beta)`.
   The pairings on `H^3(X,Z)` and `H^1(F,Z) x (H^3(F,Z)/tors)` are perfect on
   free lattices, so the adjoint `p_*pi^*` is unimodular. The explicit `/tors`
   in (11) removes the only possible torsion ambiguity.

2. **Albanese and minimal class -- verified.** The paragraph after Lemma 4.2 on
   printed p. 6 is the adjoint of the integral isomorphism on `H^1`: Poincare
   duality and Gysin adjunction identify
   `beta |-> y(a_* beta):H^3(F,Z)/tors -> Lambda` with the dual of
   `a^*:Lambda->H^1(F,Z)`, hence it is integral and unimodular. Translation of
   the Albanese embedding acts trivially on cohomology. For the normalization,
   Clemens--Griffiths (0.9), printed p. 285, gives the Fano surface class
   `theta^3/3!`; Debarre, printed p. 1, explicitly calls
   `theta_d=theta^d/d!` the minimal class and records the cubic-threefold Fano
   surface as class `theta_3`. Thus the paper's `[F]=theta^[3]` has no missing
   factor of six.

3. **Pontryagin endpoint (13) -- verified.** On printed pp. 5--6, the left side
   has degree `9+6-10=5`, matching `theta^[2] wedge y(xi)`. In a symplectic
   basis, `theta^[3]` is the sum of products of three distinct symplectic-pair
   classes, each with coefficient one. For a degree-nine basis monomial, the
   addition pullback must put its unique omitted basis vector on the first
   factor and the four complementary vectors on the second. Each admissible
   term therefore occurs once; the result is precisely a degree-one wedge-dual
   times `theta^[2]`. The degree-five wedge pairing is unimodular, so no rational
   or factorial ambiguity remains. Equivariance shows the residual sign is one
   constant sign for the whole lattice, not a class-dependent sign.

4. **Factor order, inversion, and (14) -- verified.** For
   `psi(r,s)=a(s)-a(r)` and `xi=a_*beta`, printed p. 6 correctly has
   `psi_*(beta tensor 1)=(-1)_*xi star [F]`. Inversion acts by `-1` on
   `H^9(J)`. Interchanging the two Pontryagin factors adds no Koszul sign,
   because `9*6` is even. Consequently inversion contributes only the same
   global minus sign already allowed in (13); composing the resulting map with
   the inverse of (11) absorbs it once in the definition of
   `y:H^3(X,Z)->Lambda`. Lemma 4.1, (12), then gives
   `e^*u_gamma=gamma`, while the projection formula gives
   `b_*u_gamma=theta^[2] wedge y(gamma)`. Also
   `b_*b^*alpha=[Theta] wedge alpha=theta wedge alpha`, so changing a lift
   changes the endpoint only by `im L`, exactly as required for (3).

5. **Canonical mod-two coset -- verified.** Theorem 3.1, (8), canonically and
   symplectically identifies `Lambda/2Lambda` with
   `S/L(wedge^3 Lambda)` by `z |-> [theta^[2] wedge z]`. Reversing the global
   sign changes an endpoint by
   `2 theta^[2] wedge z = theta wedge (theta wedge z)`, which lies in `im L`.
   Thus the coset in (3) is independent of the global sign. Translation of the
   Albanese map is cohomologically invisible, and swapping the difference-map
   factors produces only that same sign. The word **canonical** is justified.

6. **Dual-sequence insertion -- verified.** Printed p. 7 now displays
   `0 -> ker b_* -> H^3(M,Z) -> S -> 0`. Theorem 3.1 makes `S` saturated in
   `wedge^5 Lambda`, and all three groups are free, so dualization is exact.
   Unimodularity of the ambient degree-five pairing makes
   `wedge^5 Lambda -> S^vee` surjective; under Poincare/Gysin adjunction its
   image inside `H^5(M,Z)/tors` is exactly `b^*H^5(J,Z)`. Hence (16),
   `E_M = Hom(ker b_*,Z)`, follows without an `Ext` term. In the fibre-product

   pairing of `X` to this doubled lattice and therefore have image exactly
   `2E_M`. The insertion closes the previously implicit bridge and is fully
   compatible with the surrounding proof.

## 5. Literature boundary

Clemens--Griffiths supplies the integral family-of-curves correspondence, the
isomorphism `Alb(F) ~= J(X)`, and the minimal Fano class. Debarre independently
confirms the divided-power normalization and places the Fano surface among
minimal cohomology classes. These sources do **not** prove the paper's
degree-five Pontryagin endpoint in the form (13), its canonical simultaneous
integral fibre product (3), or the dual escape-lattice statement. Those are
established by the paper's own exterior-algebra calculation, projection
formulas, and integral dualization.
