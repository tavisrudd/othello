# C912 — frame-transport lemma: the receiver cannot host the solution algebra as built

**Lane:** `clebsch` · **Task:** C912 (WP owed to quantum referee A) · **Date:** 2026-08-15

**Status:** blocked on one architecture decision; no manuscript edit made.

## What was asked

Quantum referee A left one open item (`notes/2026-08-14-c912-quantum-referee-a.md`,
severity-ranked defect 1): the frame-transport step inside `prop:framed-operations`
— that a turn-invariant gauge conjugates framed operators — is asserted in one
sentence at `sections/04-one-step.tex:201-203` and used at `:328-330`, `:568-575`,
and `:707-708`. The referee asked for a lemma stating and proving it, with the
solution algebra built over the receiver by adjoining `z^rho`, `log z`, and the
exponential symbols formally, and expected about a page.

## The intended proof, written out

Over a differential field the argument is short and correct, and it is worth
recording because it is what the manuscript should end up containing.

Let `H` be a differential field of characteristic zero containing the universal
exponential field `Omega_V`, with derivation `theta`, distinguished element `z`,
`theta z = z`, `theta|_{Omega_V} = 0`, and constants `C = H^{theta=0}`. Put
`H_e = H[w]/(w^e - z)` and `theta_w = e·theta`, so `theta_w w = w`. Define
`U_e(H)` as the `H_e`-algebra generated by symbols `w^rho` (`rho` in `Omega_V`),
`ell`, and `E(phi)` (`phi` in `w^{-1}Omega_V[w^{-1}]`), with the evident
relations and

    theta_w(w^rho) = rho·w^rho,  theta_w(ell) = 1,  theta_w(E(phi)) = theta_w(phi)·E(phi).

Choosing coset representatives of `Omega_V/Z` makes `U_e(H)` free over `H_e` with
basis `w^rho ell^m E(phi)`, hence nonzero, with `H` embedded. The framed turn is
the `H`-algebra automorphism

    sigma(w) = e^{2 pi i/e} w,      sigma(w^rho) = Exp_V(rho/e)·w^rho,
    sigma(ell) = ell + 2 pi i/e,    sigma(E(phi)) = E(sigma phi),

consistent on integral exponents because `Exp_V(n/e) = e^{2 pi i n/e}`, commuting
with `theta_w`, and fixing `H` pointwise. This is exactly the object the phrase
"in the universal formal solution algebra" at `sections/04-one-step.tex:58-59`
refers to but never constructs; `sigma^e` is the manuscript's `M_{RS,V}` on every
returned regular-singular block.

Three facts then give the lemma.

1. **Constants.** When `H` is a Hahn field `Omega_V((Gamma + Z e_z))` with
   `theta` the `e_z`-degree derivation, `U_e(H)^{theta_w=0} = H^{theta=0}`. The
   `E(phi)`-components are independent and `theta_w`-stable; for `phi` nonzero the
   lowest-value term of `theta_w(phi)·c` is strictly below that of
   `theta_w(c) + rho·c`, so that block vanishes; the `ell`-degree drops by the
   weight-zero part of the recursion; and `theta_w(c) + rho·c = 0` with `rho` not
   an integer forces `c = 0` coefficientwise.
2. **Framed operator as a turn matrix.** For `D` with connection matrix `Omega`
   and a fundamental solution `Y` in `GL_n(U_e(H))`, the horizontal module is
   `Y·C^n`, free of rank `n` and `sigma`-stable, and `M` defined by
   `sigma(Y) = Y M` lies in `GL_n(C)` and is determined up to `GL_n(C)`-conjugacy.
   For `D` in Levelt–Turrittin form this `M` is `M_{f,V}(D)`.
3. **Transport.** If `G` in `GL_n(H)` carries `D` to `D'`, that is
   `Omega' = G Omega G^{-1} - theta(G)G^{-1}`, then `sigma(G) = G` automatically,
   because `sigma` fixes `H` pointwise; `GY` solves `D'` and `sigma(GY) = (GY)M`.
   Hence `M_{f,V}(D')` and `M_{f,V}(D)` are conjugate over `C`, so they have equal
   characteristic polynomials and equal multiplicity at every root of unity. The
   hypothesis that bites is `G` in `GL_n(H)` rather than `GL_n(U_e(H))`: over the
   solution algebra every module is trivial, and `Y` itself is a gauge there.

The rank-one check referee A supplied is the sanity test of this hypothesis: a
pro-Laurent `g` with `theta(g)/g = delta` forces `delta` in `Z`, so such a gauge
shifts residues only by integers, which `Exp_V` kills.

## Why this cannot be applied as the manuscript now stands

The lemma needs one ring holding the comparison gauge and both sides' formal
solutions. The manuscript's receiver

    R_j = Omega_{V,j} tensor_{H_{0,j}} H_{tot,j},   H_{tot,j} = C((Gamma_j + Z e_z))

is not such a ring, and the failure is not bookkeeping.

- Its elements are **finite** tensors, so a series `sum_j P_j w^j` whose
  coefficients `P_j` span an infinite `H_{0,j}`-dimensional subspace of
  `Omega_{V,j}` does not lie in `R_j`. Levelt–Turrittin gauges are exactly such
  series.
- Replacing `R_j` by a single Hahn field does not repair this. Two orders are
  available and each kills one of the two objects. With the coefficient degree
  dominant, as the manuscript orders it, pro-Laurent elements are legitimate —
  their `z`-order runs to `-infinity` while the coefficient degree grows — but a
  formal solution with coefficient degrees running to `-infinity` at fixed
  `z`-power is not well ordered. With `e_z` dominant the two verdicts swap.
- The two-field identification the tensor product performs is essential and is
  lost in any naive Hahn enlargement: `H_{0,j}` sits inside `Omega_{V,j}` as
  coefficients and inside `H_{tot,j}` as monomials, and only the tensor over
  `H_{0,j}` identifies the two copies. A multiplication map
  `Omega_V tensor_{H_0} H_tot -> Omega_V((Gamma + Z e_z))` is therefore not even
  well defined.

## The quantitative crux, and the computation of the two rates

A mixed order does exist, and it converts the obstruction into one growth
comparison. Weight a monomial of coefficient degree `d` and `z`-power `k` by
`a·d + b·k` with `a, b > 0`.

- **Pro-Laurent gauges gain exactly one filtration unit per unit of `z`-pole.**
  The recursion at `sections/04-one-step.tex:290-308` gives the bulk-degree-`m`
  coefficient `z`-order at least `-m` and puts it in `F^m`. Their weights grow
  like `m(a - b)`, so they are well ordered exactly when `a > b`.
- **Formal-decomposition gauges lose `w(Delta lambda)` filtration units per unit
  of `z`-power.** The splitting recursion divides once per order by differences
  of eigenvalues of the leading matrix `c_1 star`; the regular-singular
  normalization inside a block divides only by residue differences offset by
  integers, which are nonzero constants and cost nothing. So a gauge whose `w^j`
  coefficient has weight at least `-C j`, with `C = w(Delta lambda)`, has weights
  growing like `j(b - aC)` and is well ordered exactly when `b > aC`.

Both conditions hold together iff `C < 1`, measured in units of the minimal
weight `epsilon` of a generator of `J_j`; that is, iff

    w(Delta lambda) < epsilon.

**This ratio is scale-invariant**, so it cannot be arranged by rescaling the
weight function `w`: rescaling moves the pro-Laurent gain and the splitting loss
by the same factor. The choice of `L` at `sections/04-one-step.tex:445-451` is
therefore not a free parameter for this purpose.

**Option 3 is dead.** The hoped-for `C <= 0` is false, and the reason is
structural rather than accidental. The divisions cost weight rather than gaining
it: `Delta lambda` is built from Novikov monomials, which are generators of
`J_j` and so carry strictly positive weight, and dividing by a positive-weight
element lowers weight. The `lambda_j` of negative weight that the manuscript
separates at `sections/04-one-step.tex:273-277` is the exceptional twist
parameter of the comparison, not an eigenvalue of the small connection being
transported; the two are different objects and the favourable sign does not
transfer.

**What the ratio actually depends on.** The eigenvalues of `c_1 star` are
homogeneous of quantum degree one, where `Q^d` has degree `c_1 · d`. Hence
`w(lambda) = w(Q^{d_0})/(c_1 · d_0)` for the classes controlling the eigenvalue,
and the criterion reads: the fractional index dividing the eigenvalue must beat
the weight of its underlying class measured in minimal generator units.

- **At the cubic endpoint the criterion holds.** There `lambda` is a square root
  of a Novikov generator, so `w(Delta lambda)` is half a generator unit and the
  mixed order exists.
- **Weak factorization does not supply it in general.** `prop:framed-operations`
  quantifies over arbitrary smooth centers, including non-Fano ones carrying
  effective classes with `c_1 · d <= 0`. For those no positive weight function
  can satisfy both the separation requirement, positivity on every generator of
  `J_j`, and `w(Delta lambda) < epsilon`.
- A center whose small connection is regular singular costs nothing at all,
  `C = 0`, since no splitting divisions occur.

So the obstruction is confined to centers whose small even connection has at
least two distinct exponential factors whose separating eigenvalue difference is
of low fractional index. That is a smaller set than "all centers", but it is not
empty, and the proposition as stated covers it.

This is also the subtlety the section's own opening paragraph names
(`sections/04-one-step.tex:6-8`): a formal bulk gauge is coefficientwise Laurent
in the loop coordinate but need not specialize.

## Where that leaves the repair

1. **Mixed-order receiver plus a criterion.** Add the mixed-order Hahn receiver
   and prove the frame-transport lemma there, with `w(Delta lambda) < epsilon`
   verified for the centers the theorem actually needs. Two to three pages, and
   it re-opens accepted WP3 text, so it needs a fresh receiver review as well as
   the repeat review the lemma already owes.
2. **Levelwise transport.** Do the transport over each `(B_j/F^N)((z))`, which is
   an honest Laurent ring hosting both a bounded-order gauge and a formal
   solution algebra, then pass to the limit of characteristic polynomials rather
   than of solution algebras. This route is blocked for the same reason in a
   different costume: the splitting requires `Delta lambda` to be a unit, and
   `Delta lambda` lies in `J_j`, hence is nilpotent at every finite level. It
   also reintroduces framed monodromy over nonfield quotients, which WP2
   removed.
3. **A different proof of `prop:framed-operations`.** If neither of the above is
   made to work at the stated generality, the proposition needs either a proof
   that avoids comparing formal solutions across the pro-Laurent world, or an
   explicit restriction on centers.

The two rates above are the reusable content: any repair must reconcile a gain of
one filtration unit per `z`-pole against a loss of `w(Delta lambda)` per
`z`-power.

## Not done

No manuscript edit, no build, no export. `prop:framed-operations` remains
unproved as written, exactly as referee A left it.
