# Clebsch paper: high-level generalist cold-read questions

**Lane:** `clebsch`

**Date:** 2026-07-21

**Status:** internal exposition and theorem-compression checklist.  This note arose from the
question “what would a reader with Terence Tao's breadth and taste ask?”  It is not a report of
Tao's views, does not attribute any statement to him, and contains no external review.

## Executive read

A high-level reader is unlikely to be persuaded by the number of exact connections alone.  The
first test is whether the Clebsch case exports a reusable principle after the exceptional names,
small primes, and finite tables are removed.  The paper's best answer is the combination of C430,
C406/C412, C411, and C379:

```text
one-dimensional quadratic blind direction
  -> intrinsic recovery of an unordered index-two orbit split
  -> first outer-odd tensor in degree three
  -> orientation of the split
  -> singleton decorated parent recovery.
```

The paper should make that compression visible before presenting its wider design, arithmetic,
theta, quantum, and Weil consequences.

## The first questions

### 1. What is the reusable theorem?

Strip away the Clebsch hexagon and ask for the abstract orbit-fission statement.  The recurring
data are:

- a group `G` with index-two subgroup `G+`;
- one `G`-orbit splitting into two `G+`-orbits;
- an equivariant feature map into a vector or tensor space;
- a degree-two trade kernel generated by the sheet-sign vector;
- a first nonzero outer-odd symmetric tensor in degree three.

The paper should state the general radical--Hadamard recovery result early and identify the
Clebsch/B3/H3 data as its exact geometric realization.  It must distinguish an abstract theorem
already proved by C430 from a broader orbit-fission theorem schema that remains to be formulated.

### 2. Why degree three?

The strongest answer combines three mechanisms rather than one computation:

1. the full quotient sheet difference has vanishing first and second signed moments;
2. after the six-profile compression, antipodality kills every even signed moment and the
   primitive `1:4:6` barycentre kills degree one;
3. representation theory locates the outer-odd relative-cubic space and C454 explains its modular
   split.

The manuscript should say which clause proves minimality in which category.  “Cubic is minimal”
means minimal among signed tensor power sums and their linear functionals.  It is not a theorem
about every nonlinear statistic.

### 3. What kind of map is each arrow in `22 -> 6 -> 2 -> 1`?

The arrows are not a chain of faithful linear quotients:

- `22 -> 6` is the `A4` orbit/double-coset profile map;
- the six profile values span a two-dimensional linear plane but remain six distinct set labels;
- the two antipodal triples are the two sheet labels;
- `2 -> 1` means either forgetting orientation or choosing one decorated singleton, depending on
  the direction in which the diagram is read.

Every printed arrow should name its type: orbit map, linear image, torsor quotient, forgetting
map, or choice of section.  The figure must not let the dimension-two profile plane be mistaken for
the two-element sheet set.

### 4. What does “one bit” mean?

The primary mathematical object should be a nontrivial `C2`-torsor with no canonical equivariant
section.  The other uses then become propositions:

- the quotient character `PGL_2/PSL_2` orients the torsor;
- the cubic is an anti-invariant detector of that character;
- one bit of advice chooses a section;
- an entropy statement is available only after imposing a distribution.

“Bit,” “sheet choice,” “orientation,” “chirality,” and “advice” should not be used as synonyms
before these relationships are stated.  Quantum LU equivalence erases the geometric torsor; it
does not contradict the advice theorem.

**Adopted in the prose draft:** a terminology paragraph now defines the `C2`-torsor, orientation,
and advice meanings before the mechanism uses “bit” substantively.

### 5. Why do conics and perfect matchings produce this quotient?

The switch identity

```text
L_ab L_cd - L_ac L_bd = [a,d][b,c] Q
```

looks like a Pluecker, bracket, or skein relation for points on `P^1`.  A high-level reader will
ask whether the conic-ideal quotient is naturally a representation of a larger matching or
Grassmannian algebra, and whether the construction exists for arbitrary `2n` points on a rational
normal curve.

Paper 1 need not solve this.  It should, however, distinguish:

- the arbitrary-size switch/divisibility formalism already proved;
- the `Fin 4` switch-connectivity base actually Lean-formalized;
- the special Coxeter matching orbits on which ranks `3,6,10` and unique balanced sheets are
  proved;
- the unproved Pluecker/Temperley--Lieb/rational-normal-curve generalization.

### 6. What survives outside `q=5,7,11`?

The manuscript should separate three levels of generality:

1. conic secant-product divisibility and switch relations;
2. the abstract degree-two trade/recovery mechanism;
3. the exceptional A3/B3/H3 configurations and their unique balanced halves.

The common Coxeter law is genuinely uniform in rank three.  The deepest parent reconstruction is
an H3/characteristic-eleven theorem.  Stating both levels explicitly prevents an exceptional
realization from being mistaken for either an isolated census or an all-field theorem.

### 7. Is there a stability or inverse theorem?

The current theorem is exact and finite.  A natural successor asks whether approximate equality
of first and second moments in a characteristic-zero or integral lift forces a subset to be close
to one of the two sheets.  This would connect the result to inverse problems, pseudorandomness,
tensor identifiability, and sum-of-squares reasoning.

This is not a Paper-1 gate.  If stated, it belongs among the final bounded open questions and must
specify the ambient norm, lifted configuration, and allowed subsets.

### 8. Which computations remain essential?

C411 replaces twenty-two profile evaluations by subgroup marks and six canonical representatives.
C412 explains the target rank drop through `P(1)`, and C430 replaces balanced-half enumeration by
the radical--Hadamard argument.  A high-level reader will inspect every remaining table for the
same compression.

The verification section should classify each finite leaf as one of:

- establishment of an exceptional instance after conceptual reduction;
- independent replay of a hand proof;
- exhaustive classification used only for a sharp numerical clause;
- external certificate boundary not imported into the structural proof.

## Connections a broad reader may see

### Low-degree indistinguishability

The two signed sheet measures agree against every degree-at-most-two tensor statistic and differ
in degree three.  The degree-two blind direction is unique.  This is an exact finite model of:

- low-degree polynomial indistinguishability;
- `k`-wise independence and orthogonal arrays;
- cubature and design trades;
- tensor-method identifiability;
- low-degree and sum-of-squares barriers.

The portable statement is not merely “second moments fail and third moments work,” but:

> There is exactly one quadratic blind direction; it is the geometric sheet sign, and its first
> signed polynomial witness has degree three.

Any connection to the named modern frameworks needs a bounded literature audit before priority or
comparison wording enters the manuscript.

### A data-processing diagram

The project contains natural passages with different information behavior:

```text
geometric parent
  -> bare conic child        erases pairing and orientation
  -> conic-ideal quotient    restores an unordered split
  -> signed cubic            orients the split
  -> theta or quantum LU     erases orientation again.
```

This is better presented as an exact survival/forgetting theorem than as a claim that all carriers
are canonically identical.  Recovery is possible because the quotient retains presentation data
that the bare child discards.

### Independent arithmetic characters

The sequel contains at least three arithmetically distinct signals:

- `(5/p)` controls existence of the two golden reductions;
- `(2/p)` controls their visibility or fusion in `PSL_2(p)`;
- the Klein-cubic zeta signal is controlled by the order of `p` modulo `11` and is independent of
  fusion.

Paper 2 should organize these as independent characters and comparison maps, not as a sequence of
exceptional primes.

## What is most likely to impress

1. The compression from twenty-two cases to six double cosets and one `1:4:6` relation.
2. C430's unique quadratic blind direction and intrinsic sheet recovery.
3. The `1|9|1` projective-cover explanation of the depth rank drop.
4. Exact negative theorems at the correct representation-theoretic boundary.
5. The fact that the singleton profile and matching reconstruct the original parent, rather than
   merely distinguishing two colors.
6. The exact separation of object, decoration, torsor, and section.
7. The cross-characteristic `S4/A4` gluing theorem.
8. Formal proof and independent replay used to support, rather than replace, the conceptual proof.

## What could obscure the result

- presenting many mathematical communities before stating one exported theorem;
- using bit/chirality/orientation/advice without a primary torsor definition;
- making the Weil evidence sound more unified than the proved comparison maps;
- giving a consequence the same narrative weight as a reconstruction arrow;
- burying C430 behind the exceptional H3 instance;
- allowing the verification volume to dominate the mathematical compression;
- presenting the failures as historical dead ends instead of local boundary theorems.
