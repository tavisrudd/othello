## Goal

Extract a paper-facing theorem from C354's exact obstruction: prove that raw-object bases of the
same `[q+1,3]` conic MDS code can have the same internal/external type and the same axial service
capacities but different exact service-rate regions, in an infinite odd-field family.

## Stage A: bounded symbolic gate

Start from C354's q=5 `EEE` separator. Express each target's secant matching as a projection
involution on the conic and the two-colour union through the cycle structure of the product of two
involutions. Close the primary and forward literature boundary for conic-stabilizer association
schemes/cross-ratios, products of involutions, coloured fractional matchings, parametric linear
programming, and generator-matrix SRRs.

Before any larger classification, produce both:

1. one symbolic rational allocation valid on a character-defined parameter family; and
2. one symbolic dual fractional cover giving a facet that separates that family from a same-type,
   same-axis comparison family.

Replay the formulas against C354's exact q=5,7,9,11 certificates. Stop if the proposed invariant
controls only the pair-only subregion while size-three recovery sets change the claimed full SRR,
or if the construction survives only in isolated fields.

## Exit gate

Exit successfully with at least one of:

- an infinite same-type, same-axis, different-full-SRR family over an explicit infinite class of odd
  prime powers, with exact separating demand/facet and a publication-ready novelty boundary; or
- a closed two-target SRR theorem in terms of projection-involution product data, together with an
  unbounded lower bound on the number of distinct regions as `q` grows.

A finite table, axes-only statement, relabelling of C354's certificates, pair-orbit classification
without exact SRR consequences, or uncontrolled enumeration of full frame orbits fails the gate.
