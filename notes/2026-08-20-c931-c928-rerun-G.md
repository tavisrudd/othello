2. **Multiplicity one and the lift `Y -> M` pass.** At Lemma 4.1, p. 5,
   `psi^{-1}(0)=Delta` follows from injectivity of `a`; hence the set-theoretic inverse
   image of `X` is the irreducible exceptional divisor `P`. Thus, as Cartier divisors,
   `q^*X=mP` for some positive integer `m`. On a fibre `C` of `P -> F`, the two line
   bundles have degrees `-1` and `-1`: the first from
   `N_{X/M}=O_X(-1)` on the corresponding line of `X`, the second from the normal bundle
   of a blow-up exceptional divisor. Therefore `m=1`. This is the needed local/Cartier
   argument, not merely a set-theoretic comparison. Beauville's Proposition 6,
   pp. 204--205, constructs the same blow-up lift and identifies its exceptional map with
   the universal-line projection. **Severity:** none; verified.

   **Severity:** none; verified.
