# C909 — equal-depth finite-etale full divided-power saturation

Date: 2026-08-11  
Status: human local proof; bounded TT/EJ upgrade; no manuscript, PDF, mirror,
Lean, or certificate change

## Verdict

The all-polarization theorem has a clean full-lattice strengthening on one
equal-depth primary graph block.  Let `R=Z/p^a`, let the source polarization
block be `p^aB`, and let the self-adjoint graph slope `T` have literal
finite-etale algebra `R[T]`.  Then, on the associated principally polarized
elliptic-power quotient `A`, every divided power of every integral divisor
class is an ordinary integral divisor product:

\[
 \frac{D^k}{k!}\in
 P_A^k:=\operatorname{im}\bigl(
   \operatorname{Sym}^k\operatorname{NS}(A)
   \longrightarrow H^{2k}(A,\mathbf Z)\bigr)
 \qquad(D\in\operatorname{NS}(A),\ 0\le k\le g).
 \tag{1}
\]

Equivalently, in integral cohomology the divided-power envelope generated by
`NS(A)` equals its ordinary product algebra.  This is stronger than the
previous theorem for the distinguished principal polarization.  It is not a
Chow statement and it is not asserted here for mixtures of primary blocks of
unequal depth.

## 1. Exact split graph divisor lattice

Pass to a finite unramified faithfully flat extension `R -> S` splitting the
finite-etale slope algebra.  The self-adjoint primitive idempotents give an
exact orthogonal decomposition

\[
 M_S=\mathop\perp_{\lambda}L_\lambda,
 \qquad B=\mathop\perp_\lambda B_\lambda,
\]

and `T` is scalar modulo `p^a` on each `L_lambda`.  Distinct scalar residues
differ by units.  In symmetric-bilinear-form coordinates, the graph
Neron--Severi congruence therefore says precisely:

* a diagonal block has coefficient in
  `p^a Sym(L_lambda^*)`;
* a cross block between distinct `lambda,mu` has coefficient in
  `p^(2a)(L_lambda^* tensor L_mu^*)`.

The first factor `p^a` is the source-polarization integrality.  The second on
a cross block comes from the commutator congruence: before multiplying by the
outer `p^a`, a cross coefficient must itself be divisible by `p^a` because
the two slope residues differ by a unit.  Thus the displayed valuations are
both necessary and sufficient.  This describes the complete split local
graph divisor lattice, not merely a sublattice used for the cofactor.

## 2. Integral rank-one generation

Every diagonal symmetric block is integrally generated by rank-one squares:

\[
 (e_i+e_j)^2-e_i^2-e_j^2=e_ie_j+e_je_i.
 \tag{2}
\]

There is no division by two, so (2) is valid dyadically.  It remains to
generate a cross term.  For covectors `u in L_lambda^*` and
`v in L_mu^*`, all three rank-one forms below obey the graph congruence, and

\[
 p^a(u+p^av)^2-p^au^2-p^{3a}v^2
   =p^{2a}(uv+vu).
 \tag{3}
\]

Indeed, the cross coefficient of `(u+p^a v)^2` before the outer graph factor
is divisible by `p^a`; its diagonal terms are unrestricted.  Formula (3)
therefore expresses every required cross generator as an integral signed sum
of actual rank-one graph divisor classes.  Combining (2)--(3), the complete
local graph Neron--Severi lattice over `S` is generated by rank-one coefficient
forms.

The statement is basis-free: a rank-one form means `ell tensor ell` for a
coefficient covector `ell`.  The formulas merely prove integral generation.

## 3. Square-zero classes and divided powers

Let `R_i` be a quotient divisor class corresponding to one of these rank-one
coefficient forms.  Its pullback to the elliptic power is a decomposable
alternating two-form

\[
       c\,(x^*\otimes\ell)\wedge(y^*\otimes\ell),
\]

so its square vanishes.  Pullback by an isogeny is injective on rational
cohomology, and integral cohomology of a complex abelian variety is
torsion-free; hence `R_i^2=0` integrally on the quotient.

For any `D in NS(A) tensor_R S`, rank-one generation writes
`D=sum_i n_i R_i`.  Absorb each coefficient into its class.  Since divisor
classes commute and every `R_i^2` vanishes,

\[
 \frac{D^k}{k!}
   =\sum_{i_1<\cdots<i_k}R_{i_1}\cdots R_{i_k},
 \tag{4}
\]

an ordinary integral product expression.  Repeated indices vanish and the
`k!` orderings of each distinct set cancel exactly.  No factorial, trace,
positivity, or effectiveness assertion enters.

The graph divisor lattice is the kernel of an integral congruence map, so its
formation commutes with the finite free unramified base change.  Consequently

\[
 P_{A,S}^k=P_{A,R}^k\otimes_R S.
\]

Faithful flatness reflects membership in the quotient
`H^{2k}/P_A^k`, proving (1) over `R`.  Away from the isogeny degree the
elliptic-power presentation is integrally invertible.  Local membership at
every prime gives the global integral statement.

## 4. Exact scope and comparison

The theorem applies to a quotient whose whole local coefficient module is one
equal-depth `p^aB` graph block, including any number of finite-etale
eigenblocks after splitting and including `p=2`.  A separate unit orthogonal
factor is harmless for the divided powers of the distinguished block-diagonal
polarization, but the present full-`NS` theorem does not assert rank-one
generation for mixed unit/primary divisor terms.

The proof does **not** yet treat mixed unit/primary terms or cross terms
between two nonunit primary blocks of unequal exponents.  Their graph lattice
has asymmetric valuation constraints before one passes to symmetric-form
coordinates; a single formula such as (3) must be replaced by a
valuation-compatible straightening theorem.  That is the exact next gate,
not divided-power formalism.

For the equal-depth finite-etale stratum, the earlier mixed-adjugate proof is
now unnecessary.  Its minimal-class case is simply (1) with `D=Theta` and
`k=g-1`; the top point class and all intermediate divided powers follow at no
extra cost.
