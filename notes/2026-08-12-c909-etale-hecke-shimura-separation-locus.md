# C909 — the finite-etale Hecke–Shimura separation locus

Date: 2026-08-12

Status: theorem architecture with a structural proof; no manuscript, PDF,
mirror, or Lean edit

## 1. The moduli object

Fix a rank-`g` coefficient lattice `M`, a rational positive form `B` on
`M_Q`, and a self-dual integral symplectic lattice

```text
Lambda subset H_1(E,Q) tensor M_Q
```

obtained from `H_1(E,Z) tensor M` by finite self-adjoint Lagrangian gluing.
After adjoining a full level structure that trivializes the finite kernel,
the same lattice datum can be used for every elliptic curve in a modular
curve `Y(Gamma)`.  Quotienting the universal `E^g` and descending the
polarization gives an algebraic morphism

```text
             s_D : Y(Gamma) --> A_g .
```

Here `D` denotes the finite integral gluing datum.  Changing the auxiliary
level only replaces the source by a finite modular cover; the image depends
only on the unmarked self-dual datum.  Its generic rational Hodge structure
is `H^1(E) tensor M_Q`, so the varying Mumford--Tate factor is the diagonal
`GL_2` (or `SL_2` after fixing the polarization).  It is enough below that
the marked source is a finite-level modular curve.  Calling its image a
closed Shimura curve would require an extension/closure statement and is not
used.

Call `D` **spectrally etale** when, at every prime dividing the gluing level,
its projective self-adjoint spectral packet is finite etale on every positive
Jordan-depth block.  This condition is independent of a transverse graph
chart: after choosing an infinity line the packet becomes `Spec R[T]`, while
a change of ruling sends

```text
       T |--> (c+dT)(a+bT)^(-1)
```

and preserves both the finite algebra and its primitive idempotents.  It is
also constant along `Y(Gamma)`, because it belongs to the fixed finite
integral gluing datum rather than to the varying elliptic curve.

For fixed data `D`, write `H_D^et` for this marked finite-type stack.  Define

```text
 H_g^et = union_D im(H_D^et --> A_g) subset A_g,
```

where `D` ranges over spectrally-etale self-dual data.  This is an intrinsic
existential locus on coarse ppavs, but its useful moduli presentation is the
disjoint union of the marked modular curves.  At fixed `D` its image is
constructible; the total locus is a countable union of such images, and at
every bounded gluing level only finitely many integral kernel types occur.
No claim that an image is closed, or that the union is finite type, is
intended.

## 2. Saturated Lefschetz theorem on each component

Let `(A,Theta)` be a geometric point of a marked component.  At a bad prime,
pass faithfully and unramifiedly to a ring which splits the finite-etale
packet.  The exact Neron--Severi coefficient lattice is a symmetric
matrix-of-ideals lattice.  If diagonal slots have depths `a_i` and a cross
slot has depth `e_ij`, the graph calculation gives

```text
 e_ij = max(a_i,a_j,a_i+a_j-v(t_j-t_i)),
```

with `v(0)=infinity`.  In particular

```text
             2 e_ij >= a_i+a_j.
```

The integral rank-one hull theorem says that this midpoint inequality is
equivalent to generation of the coefficient lattice by rank-one symmetric
forms.  The corresponding divisor classes on the elliptic power have
square zero.  If `D=sum D_i` is any integral divisor class written as an
integral signed sum of these classes, then in divided-power cohomology

```text
 D^[k] = D^k/k! = sum_{|I|=k} product_{i in I} D_i .
```

There is no factorial or off-diagonal division.  Membership in the product
lattice descends faithfully from the unramified splitting ring, and
localization at every prime gives the global integral statement.

Thus every component of `H_g^et` carries the following uniform theorem:

> **Saturated Lefschetz theorem.**  For every geometric point `A` and every
> `0<=k<=g`, the integral divided-power envelope generated by `NS(A)` equals
> the ordinary degree-`k` divisor-product image.  In particular every
> `D^k/k!`, and especially `Theta^k/k!`, is an integral algebraic class.

The conclusion is about the Lefschetz subring.  It does not say that all
integral Hodge classes are divisor products.  Indeed, on the one-depth
rank-five `p^a` components the exact complementary defect is

```text
 Hdg^4/P^2 = Hdg^6/P^3 = (Z/p^a)^5,
 Hdg^8/P^4 = 0.
```

This makes the mechanism sharper: finite etaleness saturates every divided
power forced by divisors while leaving a computable ambient Hodge defect.

## 3. Cubic separation locus

Let `j : M_cub --> A_5` be the intermediate-Jacobian period map for smooth
complex cubic threefolds and set

```text
             S_cub^et = j^(-1)(H_5^et).
```

Equivalently, `X` belongs to `S_cub^et` if its principally polarized
intermediate Jacobian admits at least one spectrally-etale marked
elliptic-power presentation.  This existential formulation is intrinsic to
the cubic even though the proof uses a marked lift.

For every `X` in this locus, the saturated Lefschetz theorem gives

```text
              Theta_X^4/4! in P^4(J(X)).
```

The right side consists of algebraic divisor products.  Voisin's cubic
criterion therefore gives universal `CH_0`-triviality of `X`.  Universal
`CH_0`-triviality is preserved by product with projective space.  Independently,
the framed-monodromy theorem for every smooth cubic gives irrationality of
`X x P^1`.  Hence:

> **Hecke–Shimura separation theorem.**  Every `X in S_cub^et` is universally
> `CH_0`-trivial, while `X x P^1` is universally `CH_0`-trivial and irrational.

The nonstandard `A_5` pencil supplies a non-isotrivial curve in this locus at
the level of geometric points.
Its six-axis presentation has the exotic finite-etale `F_4` packet at two
and a scalar block at three.  The marked quadratic cover chooses one of the
two exotic slopes; the conclusion descends to the unmarked pencil.

## 4. What this adds to the epilogue

This packaging strengthens the quantifier and compresses the cycle proof:

```text
 spectrally-etale integral Hecke datum
      => saturated Lefschetz algebra in all degrees
      => algebraic primitive minimal class in dimension five
      => Voisin universal CH_0-triviality.
```

The special six-axis calculation now verifies membership in a generally
defined moduli locus rather than carrying the whole theorem.  The quantum
argument remains independent and universal over the cubic moduli space.  The
mathematical unity is therefore an intersection theorem for two detectors,
not a claim that either detector causes the other.

This is stronger than the current epilogue's local cofactor lemma, but it is
not by itself a second geometric crown: only the `A_5` cubic component is
currently verified.  A second component, or a classification of the
intersection with the cubic period locus, would materially raise the result.

## 5. Proof and wording hazards

1. The unmarked locus must use an existential marked lift; a bare isogeny to
   `E^5` is insufficient.
2. “Shimura curve” applies to each fixed rational/integral datum after a
   level cover, not automatically to their unbounded union.
3. The equality is the divided-power envelope of the Neron--Severi lattice
   with the ordinary product image, not the full integral Hodge ring.
4. The separation conclusion uses Voisin only in dimension five and only on
   the cubic intermediate-Jacobian locus.
5. The all-cubic quantum theorem is restricted to the locus; it is not a
   consequence of the Hecke hypothesis.
