# C904 theta-resolution topology and integral-projector audit

Date: 2026-08-11

Status: frozen source audit; Paper V research only; no manuscript or Lean edits

Scope: the exact topology of
\(M=M_X(v)=\operatorname {Bl}_0\Theta\), universal-family cohomology
generation, and the proposed passage from an odd codimension-three cycle on
\(\operatorname {Sym}^2M\) to an odd curve supported on \(\Theta\)

## Verdict

The topology is favorable at two, but it does **not** supply the needed
algebraic projector.

The theta resolution has

\[
 (b_0,\ldots,b_8)=(1,10,46,120,247,120,46,10,1)
\]

and Hodge numbers

\[
\begin{array}{c|c}
k&(h^{p,k-p})_{p=0}^k\\ \hline
0&(1)\\
1&(5,5)\\
2&(10,26,10)\\
3&(10,50,50,10)\\
4&(5,60,117,60,5),
\end{array}
\]

with the remaining rows given by Poincare duality.  The torsion-free middle
intersection lattice is the odd unimodular lattice

\[
             H^4(M,\mathbf Z)/\mathrm {tors}\cong I_{122,125}.
\]

The local link of the contracted cubic has

\[
 H^4(L,\mathbf Z)=\mathbf Z^{10}\oplus\mathbf Z/3.
\]

Thus the only local integral gluing defect is three-primary.  After
localizing at two, the exceptional contributions are saturated.  This is a
useful negative result: the live dyadic obstruction does not come from the
ordinary triple point or its exceptional cubic.

Li--Lin--Pertusi--Zhao prove that the rational cohomology algebra of every
primitive cubic Kuznetsov moduli space is generated by Kunneth components of
the Chern classes of a universal family.  Their statement and proof are
over \(\mathbf Q\) and use Grothendieck--Riemann--Roch.  It is not an
integral Chow--Kunneth decomposition, does not algebraize arbitrary rational
Hodge tensors on \(M^2\), and does not isolate the \(H^1\otimes H^5\) or
\(H^2\otimes H^4\) channel at two.

Consequently an odd-degree codimension-three cycle on
\(\operatorname {Sym}^2M\) does **not** presently force an odd algebraic
curve on \(M\), hence on \(\Theta\).  The \(H^3\otimes H^3\) channel is
always even.  The live escape lattices are exactly the previously computed

\[
 (\mathbf Z/2)^{10}\quad(H^1\otimes H^5),\qquad
 (\mathbf Z/2)^{44}\quad(H^2\otimes H^4).
\]

Closing either escape requires a new integral-at-two algebraic projector,
not another Betti- or Hodge-number calculation.

## 1. Geometry and exact numerical topology

Let \(J=J(X)\), let \(h\) be the pullback of the principal polarization to
\(B=\operatorname {Bl}_0J\), and let \(E\) be the exceptional divisor.
Bayer et al. prove

\[
 M\sim h-3E,\qquad E|_M=X,\qquad
 N_{X/M}=\mathcal O_X(-H).
\]

Kramer's Proposition 5 records Fulton's blowup formula in exactly this
situation:

\[
                   c(T_B)=(1+E)(1-E)^5.
\]

Since mixed top intersections of \(h\) and \(E\) vanish,
\(\int_Bh^5=120\), and \(\int_BE^5=1\), adjunction gives

\[
\begin{aligned}
 \int_Mc_1^4&=117,&
 \int_Mc_1^2c_2&=114,&
 \int_Mc_2^2&=108,\\
 \int_Mc_1c_3&=138,&
 \int_Mc_4&=81.
\end{aligned}
\]

For auditability, the pure ambient part of \(c(T_M)\) is
\((1+h)^{-1}\), while its pure exceptional part is

\[
 \frac{(1+E)(1-E)^5}{1-3E}
       =1-E+2E^2+6E^3+13E^4+\cdots.
\]

Hirzebruch--Riemann--Roch then gives

\[
 \chi_y(M)=1-21y+37y^2-21y^3+y^4.                 \tag{1.1}
\]

Kramer independently computes \(e(M)=81\) and
\(\chi(IC_\Theta)=78\).  Di Gennaro--Franco's isolated-singularity form of
the decomposition theorem says over \(\mathbf Q\) that the resolution adds
one Tate class in each of degrees two, four, and six.  Intersection
cohomology weak Lefschetz gives the ambient cohomology of \(J\) below the
middle degree.  These facts yield the Betti numbers above.  Combining them
with (1.1) gives the displayed Hodge diamond.  In particular,

\[
\begin{aligned}
 H^2(M,\mathbf Q)&:\ (10,26,10),\\
 H^3(M,\mathbf Q)&:\ (10,50,50,10),\\
 H^4(M,\mathbf Q)&:\ (5,60,117,60,5).
\end{aligned}
\]

The ambient part of middle cohomology has Hodge numbers
\((5,50,100,50,5)\).  Hence the nonambient rank-37 part is
\((0,10,17,10,0)\); one of its \((2,2)\) classes is the exceptional Tate
summand.

The signature is \(\chi_y(M)|_{y=1}=-3\).  The middle form on the
torsion-free quotient is unimodular.  It is odd because

\[
             \int_M(E|_M)^4=-3.
\]

The classification of indefinite odd unimodular lattices therefore gives
\(I_{122,125}\).

## 2. The integral and two-local boundary

In degree two there is an integral splitting

\[
 H^2(M,\mathbf Z)_{\mathrm {free}}
   =f^*H^2(J,\mathbf Z)\oplus\mathbf Z[E|_M].       \tag{2.1}
\]

The exceptional class is primitive: on a line in the exceptional cubic it
has degree \(-1\), whereas every pullback from \(J\) has degree zero.

Degree four is subtler integrally.  Let \(L\) be the circle-bundle link of
the affine cone over \(X\).  Its Euler class is \(H\).  The integral Gysin
sequence, together with

\[
 H^2(X,\mathbf Z)=\mathbf ZH,qquad
 H^3(X,\mathbf Z)=\mathbf Z^{10},qquad
 H^4(X,\mathbf Z)=\mathbf Z\ell,qquad H^2=3\ell,
\]

gives

\[
 H^i(L,\mathbf Z)=
 \begin{cases}
 \mathbf Z,&i=0,7,\\
 \mathbf Z^{10},&i=3,\\
 \mathbf Z^{10}\oplus\mathbf Z/3,&i=4,\\
 0,&\text{otherwise}.
 \end{cases}                                      \tag{2.2}
\]

The same factor three is visible from restriction:

\[
             (E|_M)^2|_X=H^2=3\ell.
\]

Thus the rational exceptional degree-four generator need not split over
\(\mathbf Z\), but it does split over \(\mathbf Z_{(2)}\): multiplication
by three is a unit there.  The local surgery and exceptional divisor create
no two-primary index.  This does **not** produce an algebraic Kunneth
projector; it only removes a possible local topological explanation for its
failure.

The odd cohomology has no new rational summands.  Explicitly,

\[
\begin{array}{c|c|c}
k&b_k&\text{Hodge numbers}\\ \hline
1&10&(5,5)\\
3&120&(10,50,50,10)\\
5&120&(10,50,50,10)\text{ in bidegrees }(1,4),\ldots,(4,1)\\
7&10&(5,5)\text{ in bidegrees }(3,4),(4,3).
\end{array}
\]

In degrees one and three, pullback identifies the rational structures with
those inherited from \(J\); degrees five and seven are their Poincare
duals.  At two, (2.2) shows that the local modification introduces no
additional odd torsion or nonsaturation.

## 3. What the universal-family theorem actually gives

The Bayer class is primitive in the numerical Kuznetsov lattice.  Hence
Li--Lin--Pertusi--Zhao, Corollary 3.9, gives a fine moduli space and a
universal family.  Their Lemma 7.5 states:

> \(H^*(M,\mathbf Q)\) is generated by the Kunneth components over \(M\)
> of the Chern classes of the universal family.

The proof writes the cohomology class of the diagonal using the top Chern
class of the relative Ext complex and then applies
Grothendieck--Riemann--Roch.  Three limitations are load-bearing here.

1. The coefficient field in the statement is \(\mathbf Q\).
2. Cohomology generation is weaker than a Chow--Kunneth decomposition.
3. Kunneth components of an algebraic class need not themselves be
   algebraic cycles.  In particular, generation does not prove the Hodge
   conjecture for \(M^2\).

The integral top-Chern identity for the undecomposed diagonal discussed in
the earlier C904 audit does not repair this: the diagonal acts as the
identity, but selecting a prescribed degree-two/degree-four summand is the
missing projector.

No audited theorem provides any of the following for this fourfold:

- an integral or \(\mathbf Z_{(2)}\) Chow--Kunneth decomposition;
- integral generation of cohomology by universal Chern components;
- an integral or \(\mathbf Z_{(2)}\) codimension-three algebraic
  inverse-Lefschetz correspondence on \(M\times M\);
- a theorem that all rational Hodge tensors on \(M^2\) are algebraic.

Markman's integral-generator results are for moduli of sheaves on
symplectic/Poisson **surfaces**.  Bülles's motivic factorization is for K3
and abelian-surface moduli and uses rational Chow motives.  King--Walter's
module-moduli theorem requires a decomposable universal projective
resolution; importing its conclusion here would wrongly force the odd
cohomology of \(M\) to vanish.  None matches the cubic-threefold moduli
hypotheses.

## 4. Consequence for the symmetric-square gate

The full-Kunneth parity calculation remains the correct reduction.
For an odd-degree class in
\(CH^3(\operatorname {Sym}^2M)\):

- the \(H^0\otimes H^6\) channel is an actual odd curve on \(M\);
- the \(H^3\otimes H^3\) channel is always even because
  \(\Theta^2=2\Theta^{[2]}\);
- decomposable algebraic \(H^2\otimes H^4\) tensors again cut out actual
  curves;
- the unresolved channels are the ten dyadic
  \(H^1\otimes H^5\) directions and the forty-four dyadic nonsplit
  \(H^2\otimes H^4\) directions.

There is in fact a rational algebraic codimension-three lowering
correspondence on \(M^2\).  If \(b:M\to J\) is the resolution map and
\(\Lambda_J\) is Lieberman's algebraic rational inverse-Lefschetz operator,
then

\[
                 b^*\circ\Lambda_J^2\circ b_*
       \in CH^3(M\times M)_{\mathbf Q}.
\]

The graph Gysin shifts degree by two, the two lowering operators shift by
minus four, and pullback has degree zero, so the composite has the required
net degree minus two.  On the inherited odd cohomology it gives the desired
rational \((1,5)\) lowering component, up to the standard nonzero Lefschetz
scalars.  This corrects the earlier claim that even rational algebraicity on
\(M^2\) was unsupported.  What remains unsupported is precisely an integral
or \(\mathbf Z_{(2)}\) refinement; Lieberman's correspondence is rational
and does not control the Smith-factor twos.

Therefore the safe implication is

\[
 \text{odd symmetric-square cycle}
 \Longrightarrow
 \begin{cases}
 \text{odd theta-supported algebraic curve},\quad\text{or}\\
 \text{a genuinely nonsplit two-local algebraic correspondence}.
 \end{cases}
\]

There is no source-backed theorem eliminating the second alternative.

## 5. Priority and red-team ledger

### Source-backed

- \(M=\operatorname {Bl}_0\Theta\), exceptional divisor \(X\), and
  \(N_{X/M}=\mathcal O_X(-H)\): Bayer et al.
- \(e(M)=81\), \(\chi(IC_\Theta)=78\), and the needed blowup Chern formula:
  Kramer.
- Rational decomposition into intersection cohomology plus the three
  exceptional Tate summands: Di Gennaro--Franco/decomposition theorem.
- Fine moduli and rational universal-Chern cohomology generation:
  Li--Lin--Pertusi--Zhao.

### New calculations in this audit

- the five Chern numbers and \(\chi_y\)-polynomial (1.1);
- the full Hodge diamond, especially
  \(H^4=(5,60,117,60,5)\);
- the odd unimodular middle lattice \(I_{122,125}\);
- the link calculation (2.2) isolating the local defect at the prime three.

### Not established

- full integral torsion decomposition of every cohomology group of \(M\);
- an integral Chow--Kunneth decomposition of \(M\);
- integral-at-two algebraicity on \(M^2\) of the inverse-Lefschetz tensor;
- odd symmetric-square cycle \(\Rightarrow\) odd theta-supported curve.

The first missing item is not the current bottleneck: the local calculation
already shows that the geometric resolution contributes no dyadic defect.
The last three are the same algebraic two-local gate.
