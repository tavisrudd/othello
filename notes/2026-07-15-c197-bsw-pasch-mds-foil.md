# C197 — the BSW Pasch configuration as a non-MDS foil

**Lane**: `clebsch`

**Date**: 2026-07-15

**Status**: integrated as a bounded related-work clarification.

## Source fact

Blokhuis, Seress, and Wilbrink report on p. 146 that their exhaustive `q=11` complete-exterior-set
census has two configurations: one six-arc and one Pasch configuration. The six-arc is the
Clebsch/Edge configuration. A Pasch configuration has six points on four three-point lines, so it
is not an arc.

## Why it helps this paper

A complete exterior set relative to a conic requires all joins of its points to be passants. This
ensures only that the conic is contained in the uncovered locus. The q=11 Pasch example shows, in
the same field and at the same six-point cardinality, that this classical condition does not imply
the arc/MDS hypothesis.

The manuscript now uses the example as a one-paragraph foil:

```text
complete exteriority  +  arc/MDS  ->  Clebsch branch;
complete exteriority alone         ->  also the Pasch branch.
```

No coordinates, new classification, uncovered-set computation, or priority claim is attached to
the Pasch configuration. BSW owns the census and the example.
