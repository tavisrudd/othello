# C907 inertia--cyclotomic compression

**Lane:** `clebsch`

**Status:** theorem-grade, including the non-Cartier full-QDM identification
under the stated Fano/Picard hypotheses.

## The compression theorem

Let

\[
 X=X_{d_1,\ldots,d_c}\subset
 W=\mathbf P(w_0,\ldots,w_{c+3})
\]

be a quasismooth weighted complete-intersection threefold with smooth coarse
space.  Assume the ambient weighted projective space is well formed and
`codim_X(X intersect Sing(W_coarse))>=2`.  For every integer `m>1` put

\[
 r_m=\#\{i:m\mid w_i\},\qquad
 e_m=\#\{j:m\mid d_j\}.
\tag{1}
\]

Then

\[
 r_m\le e_m\qquad(m>1).
\tag{2}
\]

Equivalently, with `Q_N(t)=(t^N-1)/(t-1)`, one has the cyclotomic
divisibility

\[
 \prod_iQ_{w_i}(t)\ \mid\ \prod_jQ_{d_j}(t).
\tag{3}
\]

Consequently the factorial period

\[
 \Phi(q)=\sum_{n\ge0}
 \frac{\prod_j(d_jn)!}{\prod_i(w_in)!}q^n
\tag{4}
\]

reduces exactly to the order-four operator

\[
 \theta^4-Cq\prod_{a\in A}(\theta+a),\qquad
 C=\frac{\prod_jd_j^{d_j}}{\prod_iw_i^{w_i}},
\tag{5}
\]

where `A` is an effective multiset, is invariant under `a mapsto 1-a`, and

\[
 |A|=4-r,\qquad r=\sum_iw_i-\sum_jd_j.
\tag{6}
\]

No pairwise-coprime or Cartier-degree hypothesis is needed for (2)--(6).

## Proof

### Smooth coarse space removes target inertia

Suppose a point of `X` has nontrivial cyclic inertia.  Quasismoothness gives
a smooth three-dimensional local cover `U`.  If its coarse quotient is
smooth, Chevalley--Shephard--Todd says that the effective cyclic image is
generated by pseudoreflections.  A nontrivial pseudoreflection fixes a
divisor in `U`; in the ambient weighted projective stack this divisor lies
over a nontrivial fixed coordinate locus and therefore inside the singular
locus of the coarse ambient space.  This contradicts strong
well-formedness, which makes `X intersect Sing(W_coarse)` have codimension at
least two in `X`.  A trivial effective image would put all of `U` in that
locus and gives the same contradiction.  Thus `X` avoids every nontrivial
ambient inertia stratum.

For fixed `m>1`, let

\[
 P_m=\mathbf P(w_i:m\mid w_i).
\]

Only the `e_m` equations whose degrees are divisible by `m` can restrict
nontrivially to `P_m`; every other weighted monomial vanishes there.  If
`e_m<r_m`, these `e_m` positive homogeneous equations cannot have empty
projective zero locus in a weighted polynomial ring on `r_m` variables:
Krull height is at most `e_m<r_m`.  Hence `X` would meet `P_m`, contrary to
inertia avoidance.  This proves (2).

### One inequality family performs every factorial cancellation

The exponent of the cyclotomic polynomial `Phi_m(t)` in
`prod_i Q_{w_i}(t)` is `r_m`; its exponent in `prod_j Q_{d_j}(t)` is `e_m`.
Thus (2) is exactly (3).  Equivalently, if

\[
 F_N=\{1/N,\ldots,(N-1)/N\}
\]

is a multiset, then

\[
 A=\left(\bigsqcup_jF_{d_j}\right)
   \setminus\left(\bigsqcup_iF_{w_i}\right)
\tag{7}
\]

is an effective multiset.  Each `F_N` is invariant under `a mapsto 1-a`, so
`A` is self-dual.  Cancelling the factorial recurrence leaves four copies of
`n+1` in the denominator and precisely (5).  Finally,

\[
 |A|=\sum_j(d_j-1)-\sum_i(w_i-1)=4-r,
\]

which proves (6).

## Quantum and carrier consequences

If `X` is Fano and `Pic(X)=Z[O_X(1)]` primitively, Wang's all-complete-
intersection toric-stack mirror theorem identifies (5) with the full
ordinary small-even QDM.  Indeed, target inertia avoidance kills every term
of Wang's explicit big `I`-function outside the identity sector.  For the
standard `C^*` presentation of weighted projective space, `g_beta=1` iff
`beta(O_W(1)) in Z`; restriction to `H` and its primitivity identify this
with the primitive ordinary `H`-Novikov degree `n>=0`.  For `n>0`, Wang's
Corollary 3.3(1) has empty coordinate-divisor product and gives the
identity-sector class `1`; the `n=0` coefficient is `1` by normalization.
Thus the surviving `t=0` slice is precisely the cohomology-valued factorial
series (4).  His Theorem 1.1 supplies its `I=J(q,mu,z)` Birkhoff transform
without a Cartier or convexity hypothesis.

The degree-`n` coefficient of `I` begins in `z^(-rn)`, hence that of `zI`
begins in `z^(1-rn)`.  This gives the small mirror
normalization for `r>=2`; in index one the sole extra term is removed by the
string factor `exp(-Cq/z)`, which does not change formal residues.  Fano
vanishing, Poincare duality, and `Pic(X)=Z[O_X(1)]` give the full even basis
`1,H,H^2,H^3`.  At `q=0`, `z nabla_(q partial_q)` is cup product by `H`, so
the unit is cyclic.  The normalized scalar solution has the exact recurrence
(5), hence its cyclic relation is the full rank-four small-even QDM.  The
hypergeometric support theorem applies:

\[
 \nu_6(X)\le2.
\tag{8}
\]

This includes arbitrary positive non-Cartier defining degrees.  The direct
source is Wang's published nonconvex mirror theorem, not an unqualified
orbifold quantum-Lefschetz principle.

For C907's current enriched length-two carrier, two independent
primitive-sixth packets require `nu_6>=4`.  Thus (8) is a decisive exclusion
for the stated WCI class.  The converse is false:
`nu_6>=4` is only an admission test; it does not construct the required
Rees/Stokes placement, polarization, or Gamma/Orlov marking.

## Compression and implications

1. The hypersurface pairwise-weight lemma is the `c=1` shadow of (2).
2. All factorial cancellation scans for smooth strongly well-formed WCIs
   collapse to the single divisor test (3).
3. A reduced factorial weighted-CI operator of order greater than four, or with four
   primitive-sixth zero branches, cannot come from a smooth strongly
   well-formed coarse threefold with the stated factorial period.  Some
   hypothesis must fail: smoothness, strong well-formedness, inertia-free
   coarse geometry, or reduction to the ordinary sector.
4. Rank four and self-duality alone do not prove (8): they allow four
   primitive-sixth ranks.  The real structural input is the eligible-sector
   bound `|A|=4-r<=3`; self-duality then makes `nu_6` even, hence at most two.
5. The remaining carrier search is therefore pushed toward non-WCI Fano
   models, nonordinary sectors if the carrier definition permits them, and
   genuinely non-Fano or non-nef threefold centers.  Mori--Mukai finiteness
   does not classify the latter and does not turn every Picard-one Fano into
   a weighted CI.
6. Inside the classical Picard-one Fano list, the reduction is sharper.
   Indices four and three are `P^3` and the quadric.  At index two, degrees
   one through four are weighted/ordinary CIs or cyclic covers already
   covered here; the sole first non-CI test is `V_5`.  At index one, the WCI
   genera `2,3,4,5` are closed, leaving the Mukai/homogeneous-section genera
   `6,7,8,9,10,12`.  Thus the finite Fano reconnaissance order is
   `V_5` first, then the remaining prime families of those six genera.  This
   does not replace the
   universal non-Fano carrier theorem.

## Exact primitive-sixth test

The same quotient gives a complete support test without an HLT computation.
Set

\[
 R(t)=\frac{\prod_jQ_{d_j}(t)}{\prod_iQ_{w_i}(t)}.
\tag{9}
\]

Its roots are `exp(2 pi i a)` for `a in A`, and `deg R=4-r`.  The framed
residue of the corresponding zero branch is `r a-3/2`.  It is primitive
sixth precisely when

\[
 ra\equiv 1/3\text{ or }2/3\pmod{\mathbf Z}.
\tag{10}
\]

Since `R` is self-reciprocal and has degree at most three, this gives:

- `r=1`: `nu_6=2` exactly when `R=Phi_2 Phi_3`; otherwise it is zero;
- `r=2`: `nu_6=2` exactly when `R=Phi_3` or `R=Phi_6`; otherwise it is zero;
- `r=3`: `R=Phi_2` and `nu_6=0`;
- `r=4`: `R=1` and `nu_6=0`.

For `r=1`, odd degree and self-duality force the remaining root to be `-1`;
for `r=2`, the condition that squaring a root gives a primitive cube root
allows exactly orders three and six.  This recovers the known cubic,
`(2,3)`, and degree-one del Pezzo calibrations from a single polynomial
division and proves that no WCI scan can produce a four-packet.

## Exact boundary

This note identifies only the inertia-free identity-sector slice, not an
arbitrary unreduced stacky `I`-series.  It does not treat odd or big quantum
sectors and does not prove that a primitive-sixth packet lifts to the
operation-framed carrier.  It also does not weaken the two well-formedness
hypotheses: ambient well-formedness sends nontrivial inertia into the coarse
singular locus, and the codimension condition then turns the local
pseudoreflection alternative into the global contradiction.  Without the
ambient condition, `X_2 subset P(1,2,2,2,2)` can have smooth coarse space but
divisorial `mu_2` inertia.
