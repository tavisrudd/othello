The program should promote at most one new flagship mechanism into the present paper.  C376 makes

lemmas or sequels rather than extra vertices forced into one manuscript.

For fast progress, every speculative global construction has two passes.  Pass one is a disposable
bounded scout using fixed coordinates and existing exact infrastructure; it answers only the
promotion question.  Pass two creates a durable certificate, invariant-ring presentation, stack,
or general theory only after the scout exposes a productive theorem.  In particular no broad GIT,
stack, normalization, or cubic-surface formalization preceded the q=11 fusion/fibre gates.  Those
gates are now positive in corrected forms.  C379's still-smaller `11+11` matching-factorization
gate is also certified; its focused source audit and a productive framed moduli map must precede

## Uniform promotion gate

A proposed transported structure is headline-worthy only if every row passes.

| gate | required evidence | automatic failure |
|:---|:---|:---|
| canonicity | invariant under the correct projective, monomial, marked-surface, graph, scheme, or local equivalence | a coordinate choice or unrecorded marking selects the map |
| compatibility | explicit maps make a stated square or closed walk commute, including scalars and group actions | two endpoints merely have isomorphic abstract groups or equal counts |
| nontriviality | the bridge is stronger than unpacking definitions or a standard dictionary | deepest syndrome to arc extension is asserted without a further consequence |
| productivity | transport proves a classification, obstruction, reconstruction, exact phase, or operational formula unavailable at one endpoint alone | the only conclusion is that both constructions can be applied |
| literature | primary-source and forward-citation audit clears the exact edge or commuting diagram | absence is inferred from keyword screening or shallow metadata |

Verdicts are deliberately coarse.

- **Green:** all five gates pass.  The branch may compete to become the flagship upgrade.
- **Yellow:** an exact useful bridge passes canonicity and compatibility but is classical,
  tautological, or nonproductive.  Use it as exposition, a lemma, or a short corollary.
- **Red:** canonicity or compatibility fails, or the apparent match is only numerical.  Record the
  bounded negative and stop.

No average score can compensate for failed canonicity, compatibility, or productivity.

## Wave I — three independent falsifiers

### A. Clebsch cubic incidence and `E6` — green in C376

C376 has completed this gate in its strongest clean form.

1. The anticanonical system embeds the q=11 six-point blow-up as the Clebsch cubic with the exact
   `6+15+6` line decomposition and complete incidence data.
2. The distinguished `(E_i)` and `(Q_i)` rows form an `A5`-invariant double-six.
3. The natural quintic Cremona passage from the `(E)`-row blowdown to the `(Q)`-row blowdown induces
   exactly the 60 permutations in `N_S6(A5) - A5`.
4. Identifying the second blowdown with the golden-conjugate model induces exactly `A5`.
5. Hence blowdown exchange and code-chirality exchange are the same quotient character
   `S5 -> S5/A5 = C2`.
6. A separate implementation independently replays the exact finite certificate.

**Green verdict.**  The equality is proved at the level of exact permutation sets and the natural
Cremona passage, not inferred from two ten-element orbits.  It identifies the exchange character
without choosing a false canonical `+` sheet.

**Remaining gate.**  C376 read the directly load-bearing Prokhorov sections in full, but the exact
code/scheme compatibility claim still needs MathSciNet, zbMATH, Google Scholar, and forward-
citation closure before a priority sentence enters the manuscript.

### B. Deep holes as extension geometry

First isolate the exact but potentially classical lemma

```text
projective deepest syndromes
  = points outside the fifteen secants
  = points that extend the six-arc to a seven-arc.
```

Then demand new output.

1. Classify the twelve q=11 extension points and their stabilizers under `A5`.
2. For every point, test the additional no-six-on-a-conic conditions needed for a degree-two del
   Pezzo surface; a seven-arc alone does not certify an `E7` blow-up.
3. Construct the associated `[7,4,4]` kernel and `[7,3,5]` dual MDS code in fixed conventions.
4. State precisely whether this supplies an `AME(7,11)` presentation and whether any relationship
   to the six-party state is canonical.  Code extension is not automatically physical tensor
   extension.
5. Give a presentation-independent definition of one deep-hole-transform iteration and compute
   `D(D(C))`, or prove why no intrinsic iteration exists.

**Green gate.**  The conic parametrizes a canonical `E7`, MDS-extension, or iterative-transform
structure and yields a classification or rigidity theorem.

**Yellow off-ramp.**  The extension equivalence is exact but standard.  Use it to explain the
deep-hole conic with the honest sentence “the hardest errors are the directions in which the code
can grow.”

**Red off-ramp.**  Surface general position fails or `D(D(C))` is essentially presentation-
dependent.  Classify the finite obstruction only if it has a clean invariant statement.

**C379 disposition.**  Every deep-hole point gives a seven-arc and `[7,4,4]`/`[7,3,5]` MDS pair,
but every blow-up is a weak degree-two del Pezzo with one effective `E7` root and `A1` singularity.
The undecorated transform terminates because `D(Q)=empty`.  The proposed two-parent marked fibre
also fails: the child has 22 parents.  The obstruction supplies the positive theorem instead—six
five-parent conics give a perfect matching `M_X` of `Q`, and `(Q,M_X)` recovers `X` uniquely on the
complete locus.  C379's pre-freeze extension certifies a further `11+11`
one-factorization/biplane refinement; see
`notes/2026-07-19-c379-one-factorization-biplane-companion.md`.  The exact finite theorem is green,
while novelty remains ungraded pending its focused source audit.

### C. One common duality

Run cheap kill tests before constructing a grand duality.

1. Decide monomial isoduality of the q=11 `[6,3,4]` code and determine whether an isoduality, if it
   exists, is unique up to the intrinsic automorphism group.
2. Compute the Gale transform of the marked six-arc and decide whether it returns either golden
   fibre canonically.
3. Compare those maps with the invariant `H3` pairing that already gives C372's identification
   `V -> V*` and `P=Q`.
4. Only after those pass, test the relevant `E6` Picard or diagram involution.
5. Only after a classical square passes, ask whether the induced operation is a local symplectic
   symmetry of the AME presentation rather than an analogy with complementary `3|3` cuts.

**Green gate.**  One `A5`-equivariant map, unique up to the correct scalar or marking ambiguity,
makes at least two independently constructed routes commute and forces a new consequence.

**Yellow off-ramp.**  Only the representation-to-Fourier square survives.  Fold it into C372's
conceptual proof without claiming one duality in six languages.

**Red off-ramp.**  The code is not isodual, Gale duality chooses extra data, or the surface and AME
operations are unrelated.  A sharp non-isodual or non-Lagrangian result may instead constrain C374.

**C378 disposition.**  The exact surviving common map is the invariant quadratic pairing.  Adjoining
C377's `J` completes `A5` to `PO_3(11)~=PGL_2(11)` and gives exactly C372's rank-four orthogonal
fusion.  The conjugate rank-eight schemes have a Fourier-self-dual rank-16 scalar-`A4` common
refinement and a four-dimensional `J`-odd signed Fourier block.  This is a positive compatibility
theorem, but not a literal statement that rank four forgets precisely one chirality bit: the
`J`-fixed algebra has dimension 12.

## Wave III — quantum consequences

C374 and C375 already own the exact quantum-equivalence and circuit gates.  They should consume a
green classical result if available, but the classical program must not depend on them.

Two outcomes justify promotion:

- a presentation-independent scheme profile or finite invariant separates an LC class or sharply

## Lean foundation track

Lean should protect the logical seams of the gateway, not attempt to recreate the whole
computational and algebro-geometric stack at once.  Its first job is to make category mistakes,
hidden markings, and misuse of standard dictionaries impossible to state.

C380 has completed the first bounded formalization slice described below.

### F0. Freeze typed interfaces

Record separate types and equivalences for labelled projective configurations, arcs, parity-check
column systems, linear codes, syndrome points, and permutation torsors.  Do not identify projective
equivalence with monomial code equivalence or a labelled configuration with its unmarked quotient
by definitional convenience.

### F1. Formalize the generic bridge

Build on the existing `Arc.lean` and `CodingBridge.lean` results to prove one paper-facing theorem:
for a spanning triple-independent column system in projective dimension two, a nonzero projective
syndrome has minimum weight three exactly when its point is outside every secant, exactly when it
can be inserted while preserving the arc condition.

This formalizes the seam

```text
deep syndrome <-> uncovered projective point <-> one-point MDS extension
```

and prevents the later `E7` and AME discussions from silently switching between a parity-check
kernel and its dual generator code.

### F2. Certify the q=11 instance narrowly

Give the six q=11 columns and conic in fixed conventions.  Prove, through small reducible finite
evaluators or generated leaf certificates, that the six columns form an arc and that their
uncovered projective set is exactly the twelve conic points.  Shard finite certificates across
module boundaries if needed; do not place a large `decide` proof in a paper-facing module.

### F3. Formalize the quotient-character inference

Do not formalize the entire cubic-surface construction in the first slice.  Instead formalize the
abstract inference used by C376: if the natural blowdown transition induces precisely the outer
normalizer coset and the second-model identification induces precisely `A5`, then the two exchange
operations define the same quotient character `S5 -> C2`; no preferred element of the resulting
two-set follows.  The heavy quintic and 27-line enumeration remains an exact external certificate
behind an explicit trusted boundary.

### F4. Add arithmetic only after C377 stabilizes

Once C377 has a final symbolic `J(tau)` and cocycle statement, formalize its algebraic identities
and specialization lemmas.  Do not formalize a moving conjectural API or attempt a general theory
of cubic surfaces over the golden integer ring first.

### F5. Quantum bridges remain optional

Formalize MDS-to-AME or local-symplectic consequences only if C374 produces a compact exact theorem
and the necessary finite linear-algebra infrastructure is already available.  Lean is not a reason
to broaden a presentation-dependent quantum claim.

**Lean green gate.**  A lightweight import-only gateway module reaches every paper-facing formal
terminal, exact-target validation is trace-current, the prescribed axiom audit is clean, and no
generated finite leaf is trusted without its checker/schema boundary.

**Lean yellow off-ramp.**  The generic deep-syndrome/arc-extension theorem and abstract quotient-
character inference land, while q=11 enumeration remains externally certified.  This still
substantially derisks the manuscript's most error-prone changes of language.

**Lean red off-ramp.**  Cubic-surface infrastructure would require a new foundational library or
finite reflection creates an unbounded elaboration target.  Stop at the typed interfaces and
generic bridge; do not equate absence of formalization with a failed mathematical theorem.

## Literature and reproducibility gates

Run the expensive literature audit only after a candidate theorem is written exactly.  Cover the
specific edge or commuting diagram, not just its endpoints.  A promoted branch requires full-text
reading of the directly load-bearing sources, MathSciNet and zbMATH coverage, Google Scholar and
forward-citation closure, and the read-depth ledger required by the repository conventions.

Every computational task ends with one atomic evidence bundle:

1. exact theorem or bounded negative;
2. coordinate-free proof or an explicit statement of the trusted computational boundary;
3. deterministic script, compact canonical certificate, hashes, and `--check` replay;
4. an independent check where practical; and
5. a one-page green/yellow/red disposition.

No second-stage search or census is authorized by an aesthetically suggestive first output.

## Red-team findings applied to this plan

The first draft overestimated six risks.  The plan above incorporates the corrections.

### 1. Analogy masquerading as transport

Invariant forms, polarity, Gale duality, code duality, Fourier duality, `E6` duality, and AME
multiunitarity are different kinds of maps.  The common-duality task therefore begins with
isoduality and exact commutative squares; it does not begin by naming a shared principle.

### 2. Classical dictionary masquerading as a theorem

Deepest syndrome to admissible seventh arc point follows directly from the syndrome-span lemma.
The extension branch earns green status only through a further classification, iteration, surface
correspondence, or obstruction.

### 3. General-position and equivalence mismatches

Arc extension does not imply del Pezzo general position, and code extension does not imply a
canonical tensor extension.  Both are mandatory negative tests rather than presumed arrows.

### 4. Automatic orbit matches — cleared for Door I

Two transitive ten-element `A5`-sets could have matched automatically.  C376 avoids that false
positive: the quintic contraction and independent projective exhaustions give equality of the
actual inner and outer permutation sets.  What remains unproved is a preferred orientation; the
correct invariant is the quotient character, not a named sheet.

### 5. Information loss between moduli problems

Projective, monomial, graph, marked-surface, and LC quotients forget different data.  Every task
must name its markings and prove descent through the relevant quotient before using “intrinsic” or
“the same.”

C379 makes this concrete.  Forgetting `M_X` collapses 22 parent arcs to one conic.  Its certified
`PSL_2(11)` split shows that a binary orientation chooses one of two eleven-matching systems, while
choosing a parent requires one matching inside that system.  Neither operation is C378's rank-four
fusion, whose fixed algebra retains 12 of the rank-16 dimensions.

### 6. Scope and famous-object tourism

The gateway atlas is a reservoir of later questions, not a work queue.  C381 is the sole bounded
exceptional-lattice probe now allocated: it classifies the weak-degree-one effective-root data
forced by C379.  C382 is allocated but cannot start unless C381 produces a canonical marked `E8`
invariant.  Generic icosian/600-cell exposition, genus-two theta data, clusters, Hilbert modular
surfaces, type-II matrices, Mathieu groups, and holography remain unallocated.

### 7. Formalization theatre

Lean can prove only the stated model.  C380 therefore exposes every computational boundary, keeps
generated tables behind checked leaves, and formalizes only the stable dictionary,
quotient-character logic, matching sheets, and C378 finite interfaces.  It deliberately excludes
cubic-surface, general biplane, and `E8` foundations.  C381/C382 must first stabilize their
mathematical categories; no Lean expansion is implied by their allocation.

## Hard stop rules

- Never promote a match based only on `6`, `10`, `12`, `27`, `A5`, `E6`, or a familiar graph.
- Never write “the same” without an explicit natural map and its equivalence category.
- Never call a code-length increase a physical tensor extension without a state-level map.
- Never put a famous-object connection in the introduction before it produces a theorem.
- Never let a negative quantum gate delay the pure-mathematical manuscript.
- Never allocate a downstream destination solely because an upstream computation is pretty.
- Never treat the universal Picard `E8` of eight blow-ups as C381's transported invariant, or
  start C382 without a canonical effective-root datum and named `A5` comparison category.
- Promote at most one gateway mechanism into the current paper.
