## Objective

Compress the q11 fifteen-class census, numerical gap, low-degree rigidity, and
seven-arc exclusions into invariant summaries and small proof-carrying
certificates without claiming a nonexistent uniform classification theorem.

## Work

1. Recast the fifteen classes as projective-orbit records with canonical
   representative, stabilizer, orbit mass, triple-concurrence statistic, and a
   global mass/completeness identity.
2. Derive every uncovered-set size from the chord-defect formula and certified
   concurrence data rather than a fresh point scan.
3. For low-degree rigidity, seek one explicit nonsingular cubic-evaluation
   minor for each non-Clebsch orbit and prove the Clebsch kernels symbolically
   as generated by the conic equation.
4. For the q11/q13 seven-arc exclusions, quotient by projective orbits and
   package a rank witness per orbit plus an orbit-mass identity. Preserve the
   full normalized-domain replay as an independent audit.
5. Record the failed first-order rational LP and its exact bound so it cannot
   be mistaken for a structural exclusion later.

## Acceptance

Every classification remains complete, but the reader-facing certificate is
an invariant orbit ledger plus local determinant/rank witnesses. The verifier
checks those proof objects directly and also confirms agreement with the old
enumeration.

## Boundary and handoff

Do not force a conceptual classification of all fifteen classes. If orbitwise
minors or mass identities are larger than the current transparent replay,
retain the replay and document why compression loses clarity. Pass the frozen
finite-classification boundary to C725.
