## Disposition

The bare lattice gate is positive: the affine McKay Cartan lattice on the
nine \(2.A_5\) irreducibles, modulo its dimension-vector radical, is
explicitly identified with the Hamming Construction-A \(E_8\) by eight
displayed simple roots and their affine completion.

The simultaneous Clebsch marking is negative by three independent
obstructions.  No two-coordinate minor of \(R_{10}\) is \(H_8\); the
ten-node module has no equivariant rank-eight subspace or quotient under
\(S_6\), the conference \(S_5\), or golden \(A_5\); and \(Q_{10}\) contains
no unmarked \(E_8\) root subsystem.  The nearest positive lattice is the
canonical hyperbolic double
\[
 L_{R_{10}}\oplus L_{R_{10}}^*\cong II_{10,10},
\]
where the isoduality torsor becomes a literal exchange of two maximal
isotropic halves.  An exchange is involutory exactly for a self-adjoint
isoduality, recovering precisely the \(36\) \(W_{10}\) polarities; their
fixed and anti-fixed graph lattices have signature \((5,5)\).  Thus
\(Q_{10}\), not positive-definite \(E_8\), is the
nearer code-lattice parent of the \(W_{10}\) sister seam.
