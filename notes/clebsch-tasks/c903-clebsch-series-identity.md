# C903 — five-paper Clebsch series identity

**Lane:** `clebsch`

**Status:** active editorial task

## Objective

Land the author-selected series title *Clebsch: Rigidity from Sparse Shadows*,
make five numbered papers authoritative on current public and routing surfaces,
then settle and apply the five-clause epigraph, final series diagram, and short
series blurb. Preserve dated historical records until the separately requested
cleanup and archive pass.

## Acceptance gates

1. Papers I--IV carry the new series banner and identify the numbered series as
   five papers without counting either unnumbered companion.
2. Current summary, index, live handoffs, and unity plan agree on the title and
   count.
3. The epigraph gives Paper IV an accurate clause and reserves the closing
   clause for Paper V without asserting an unproved cubic bridge.
4. The final diagram distinguishes the exact marked Rosetta route through
   Papers I--III and V from Paper IV's parallel minimum-word reconstruction.
5. Paper PDFs build warning-free and receive a visual title-page check; no Lean
   work is run.

## Final design

- Series title: *Clebsch: Rigidity from Sparse Shadows*.
- Series blurb: “How much of a structured object can be reconstructed from its
  sparse shadows?” followed by the upper Papers I--III/V round trip and Paper
  IV's independent lower reconstruction branch.
- Epigraph: “From deep holes, the cubic takes shape, finds its bearings, / stands
  fixed while its shadows move; from minimum words, the plane returns, / and
  the scattered shadows gather home.”  Papers I--IV bold their own clause;
  Paper V reserves the final one.
- Series map: Papers I--III point by dashed, Paper-V-deferred comparisons to the
  marked cubic and golden operator; Paper V is joined by a two-way recognition
  and return arrow.  Paper IV is the independent lower branch from minimum-word
  pairs to the marked conic plane and polarity.
- The short perspective paragraph, map, and caption stay together in all four
  manuscripts.  The caption explicitly distinguishes the future dashed
  comparisons, Paper V's two-way arrow, and Paper IV's independent solid arrow.

## Verification

- Deterministic manuscript builds: Paper I 29 pages plus 13-page computational
  companion; Paper II 46 pages; Paper III 33 pages; Paper IV 15 pages.
- TeX warning scans: zero warnings in all four builds.
- Visual cold read: three sealed readers inspected only the rendered title and
  map pages.  After the pagination and caption refinements, all three returned
  `GO` and recommended retaining the diagram in all four papers.
- Lean was not run.
