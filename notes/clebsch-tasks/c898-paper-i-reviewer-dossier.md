## Acceptance gate

- The likely-reviewer ranking is tied to venue, subject overlap, citation
  neighborhood, and current research activity rather than fame alone.
- Every persona convention is traceable to a read source and is not invented
  from biography or reputation.
- The packet spans Paper I's finite-geometry, coding-theory, two-graph,
  representation-theory, and exposition interfaces without routing unrelated
  expert context into ordinary Paper I work.
- A review coordinator can launch several genuinely different cold reads from
  the dossier without loading the normal Paper I lane context.
- The first-pass reports are independent, identify the earliest unsupported
  implication, and distinguish proof, citation, normalization, computation,
  and exposition findings before synthesis.
- Every review round records one manuscript commit and PDF hash, freezes all
  reports before synthesis, and records which findings were adopted, rejected,
  or deferred with evidence.
- Every remediation round validates the changed scholarly surface, records the
  new freeze, and leaves C898 active for the next cold-read round until the user
  explicitly closes the programme.
