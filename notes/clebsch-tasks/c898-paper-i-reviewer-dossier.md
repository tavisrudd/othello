# C898 — Paper I reviewer and critic dossier

**Lane:** `clebsch`

**Status:** active; source-grounded dossier in progress

## Objective

Identify the likely reviewers and most relevant critics of Paper I,
*Reconstructing the Clebsch code and its golden orientation from its deep-hole
syndrome locus*, and build a review-sub-agent dossier sufficient for several
independent cold reads focused on the human proofs and the paper's exposition.

Use the Paper II and Paper III reviewer dossiers as procedural and formatting
precedents.  For each selected persona, extract the conventions, proof
expectations, terminology, and directly related papers that should shape a
faithful cold read.  Distinguish likely journal reviewers from useful critic
personas whose expertise exposes a specific risk surface.

After the dossier is frozen, run the selected persona reads independently,
freeze their reports before cross-comparison, and synthesize overlapping and
conflicting findings without routing the dossier into normal Paper I context.

## Deliverable

Primary dossier:
`notes/2026-08-09-clebsch-paper-i-reviewer-dossier.md`.

Cold-read reports and their synthesis will be added as dated `C898` notes once
the batch is launched.

The dossier must include:

1. a ranked reviewer/critic slate with selection evidence and confidence;
2. the Paper I claims or sections each person is positioned to scrutinize;
3. a bounded reading packet of their related papers, with stable locators;
4. extracted conventions and likely objections grounded in those papers;
5. persona instructions for independent human-proof and exposition cold reads;
6. conflict, availability, and uncertainty cautions; and
7. a source ledger separating full-text reading from metadata-only evidence.

## Acceptance gate

- The likely-reviewer ranking is tied to venue, subject overlap, citation
  neighborhood, and current research activity rather than fame alone.
- Every persona convention is traceable to a read source and is not invented
  from biography or reputation.
- The packet spans Paper I's finite-geometry, coding-theory, two-graph,
  representation-theory, and exposition interfaces without routing unrelated
  expert context into ordinary Paper I work.
- A review coordinator can launch several genuinely different cold reads from
  the dossier without loading the normal Paper I lane context.
- The first-pass reports are independent, identify the earliest unsupported
  implication, and distinguish proof, citation, normalization, computation,
  and exposition findings before synthesis.

## Boundaries

This task is literature and review preparation only.  It does not edit the
Paper I manuscript, Lean sources, trust/release surfaces, or standalone mirror;
it does not contact prospective reviewers; and it does not assert private
editorial knowledge.  The dossier and any persona packets remain
review-sub-agent material only.  Any later conventions-only manuscript context
requires a separately selected, specialized extraction.

Numerical review grades are reported in chat only.  Do not persist them in
handoffs, task cards, dossiers, cold-read reports, or review syntheses; on-disk
records keep findings and categorical verdicts only.
