## Acceptance gate

- Reviewer likelihood is tied to the venue routes, closest cited work,
  subject overlap, and visible current activity, with uncertainty stated.
- Every attributed convention or likely objection is traceable to a read
  source; biography and reputation do not stand in for evidence.
- The packets cover the conic-code convention, module descent, elliptic
  association scheme, tangent-product sign, finite certificates, and
  Sylow/involution recovery of the plane.
- Each cold reader receives only the frozen paper, its own packet, and the
  neutral question set.  It does not see another persona, earlier Paper IV
  reviews, the Clebsch handoff, or the formal-remediation tasks.
- Reports distinguish a false statement, human-proof gap, missing citation,
  unexplained computation, convention mismatch, and exposition friction.
- The dossier is never added to startup, named-expert, Lean, or ordinary
  Paper IV context.
