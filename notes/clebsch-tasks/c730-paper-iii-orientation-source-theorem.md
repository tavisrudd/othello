# C730 — Paper III arithmetic--harmonic orientation source theorem

**Lane:** `clebsch`

**Status:** queued; precedes C680 manuscript integration

## Objective

Strengthen Paper III around one canonical source theorem: on the Clebsch
chart, identify a chosen sheet of Hitchin's incidence cover with the golden
orientation datum, identify its oriented cubic line, and compare that line
canonically with the Petersen degree-six harmonic realization.  Match the
deck, Galois, conference-orientation, and cubic-sign involutions exactly.

The result should turn the paper's two appearances of the Clebsch cubic into
two realizations of one arithmetic orientation torsor.  It must stop at the
source class `([C], [Z_C])`; marked completion and every functorial shadow
belong to the Golden paper.

## Work package

1. Freeze the dimensional and marking interfaces among the Hitchin harmonic
   seven-space, the Clebsch chart, the golden six-axis carrier, its
   augmentation quotient, and the Petersen harmonic four-space.
2. Prove the strongest scheme-theoretic comparison available between the
   normalized incidence cover on the Clebsch locus and the golden
   orientation/conference cover.  If a global identification fails, state the
   maximal open locus and the sharp obstruction.
3. Track incidence deck exchange, golden Galois conjugation, `C ↦ -C`, cubic
   sign, switching, and projective scaling without suppressing a torsor.
4. Construct the canonical `A₅`-equivariant harmonic comparison, normalize it
   by the invariant quadratic forms, and derive the exact cubic scalar from a
   structural norm or one-vector calculation.
5. Determine the exact integral localization for the geometric comparison,
   or replace the current unspecified finite set by the sharpest proved
   obstruction and ownership statement.
6. Write and cold-read the human proof companion.  Extend Lean only through
   the finite source interface and exact harmonic normalization that genuinely
   correspond to paper statements; reuse C712 rather than duplicating it.
7. Run a proportionate novelty audit for the orientation-cover identification
   and canonical harmonic comparison before manuscript priority language.
8. Hand the frozen theorem and claim map to C680 for a short source-interface
   section, revised opening/conclusion, and full release replay.

## Acceptance

- One theorem explains the arithmetic and harmonic cubic as realizations of
  the same normalized orientation datum.
- Every sign, field, marking, and deck involution is identified explicitly.
- The exact open locus and integral/bad-prime boundary are proved or sharply
  delimited.
- The proof is conceptual; exact computation and Lean audit normalizations but
  do not replace the cover or comparison argument.
- Paper III contains no outer-six, commutator Pfaffian, cross-golden,
  Segre--Igusa, Cartan, Majorana, anomaly, exceptional-parent, or lattice
  theorem.
- The result can be read independently of Papers I and IV.
