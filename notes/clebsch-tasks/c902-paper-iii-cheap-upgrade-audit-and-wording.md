### Gate 3 — proof packets for new mathematics

For every candidate classified as new mathematics, prepare before any wording:

1. an exact theorem or proposition with fields, quantifiers, marking groupoid,
   equivalence relation, nonvanishing hypotheses, and integral boundary;
2. a complete paper-grade proof, not a computational observation or a citation to an
   internal task result;
3. an explicit dependency and trust ledger distinguishing human, cited, formal, and
   finite inputs;
4. falsifiers and boundary examples, including weighted or zero-shadow degenerations
   when relevant; and
5. an independent specialist red-team report that reconstructs the proof and attacks
   signs, constants, canonicity, generality, and predecessor overlap.

Failure at this gate rejects or narrows the candidate.  It does not license prose
that anticipates a future proof.

### Gate 4 — independent red-team synthesis

- Run independent algebraic-geometry/arithmetic, conference/principal-minor,
  invariant/operator, and cross-paper attribution reads on only the candidates in
  their competence.
- Require each reader to distinguish falsehood, proof gap, standard lemma, citation
  overreach, novelty pre-emption, marking regression, and mere exposition friction.
- Freeze first-pass reports before synthesis.  A candidate passes only if its proof,
  attribution, scope, and claimed significance all survive.
- Red-team the combined packet against the C897 hierarchy: no second overview figure,
  no restoration of a flat theorem list, no duplicate marking disclaimers, and no
  abstract overload.
