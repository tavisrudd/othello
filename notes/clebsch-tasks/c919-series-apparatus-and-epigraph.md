## Goal

Restore the five-clause series epigraph to every paper; move it and the
series-map figure out of the front matter of Papers I–IV; collect every
cross-paper sentence from the "Reconstruction perspective" interludes into that
same block; merge each interlude's local content into its introduction; and
tighten the epigraph. Paper V keeps its current arrangement and takes the
epigraph back on its title page.

## Plan

Full proposal: `../2026-08-18-c919-series-apparatus-and-epigraph-plan.md`. It
records the current placement in all five papers, the per-sentence
move/merge/cut decision for each interlude, three epigraph rewrites with
mix-and-match clause slots, and the execution gates.

Recommendation in brief: option (a), placing the block as the last item of the
main body — after the conclusion where one exists, before `\appendix` where
appendices follow. The argument is that sibling-paper citations outside the
interludes are almost nonexistent (Paper III none, Paper IV none, Paper II one in
an appendix, Paper I one inside its own introduction), so nothing in the bodies
needs the map early, and option (b) would relocate the interruption rather than
remove it.

## Author decisions this task is blocked on

1. Placement: option (a) as refined above, or option (b) after the introduction.
2. Which epigraph proposal, or which mix of clause slots.
3. Whether the closing clause keeps "the scattered shadows gather home", which
   overclaims a merging that Paper V explicitly rules out.
4. Whether C919 edits Paper III directly or hands that share to C816 on the
   deterministic Paper III route.
5. Whether Paper II gains a conclusion — it is the only paper of the five without
   one, and the placement rule therefore lands its series block against a
   technical section. Separate improvement, not bundled.
