# C897 — Paper III reviewer and critic dossier

**Lane:** `clebsch`

**Status:** active; reviewer/critic dossier built; sealed cold-read batch pending

## Objective

Identify the likely reviewers and most relevant critics of Paper III,
*Golden descent and operator realizations of the Clebsch cubic*, and build a
review-sub-agent dossier sufficient for several independent cold reads focused
on the human proofs and the paper's exposition.

Use the Paper II reviewer dossier as the procedural and formatting precedent.
For each selected persona, extract the conventions, proof expectations,
terminology, and directly related papers that should shape a faithful cold
read.  Distinguish likely journal reviewers from useful critic personas whose
expertise exposes a specific risk surface.

After the dossier is frozen, run the selected persona reads independently,
freeze their reports before cross-comparison, and synthesize overlapping and
conflicting findings without routing the dossier into normal Paper III
context.

## Deliverable

Primary dossier:
`notes/2026-08-09-clebsch-paper-iii-reviewer-dossier.md`.

Cold-read reports and their synthesis will be added as dated `C897` notes once
the batch is launched.

The dossier must include:

1. a ranked reviewer/critic slate with selection evidence and confidence;
2. the Paper III claims or sections each person is positioned to scrutinize;
3. a bounded reading packet of their related papers, with stable locators;
4. extracted conventions and likely objections grounded in those papers;
5. persona instructions for independent human-proof and exposition cold reads;
6. conflict, availability, and uncertainty cautions; and
7. a source ledger separating full-text reading from metadata-only evidence.

## Acceptance gate

- The likely-reviewer ranking is tied to venue, subject overlap, citation
  neighborhood, and current research activity rather than fame alone.
- Every persona convention is traceable to a read source and is not invented
  from biography or reputation.
- The packet spans the paper's algebraic-geometry, invariant-theory,
  two-graph/conference-matrix, harmonic, and exposition interfaces without
  routing unrelated expert context into ordinary Paper III work.
- A review coordinator can launch several genuinely different cold reads from
  the dossier without loading the normal Paper III lane context.
- The first-pass reports are independent, identify the earliest unsupported
  implication, and distinguish proof, citation, normalization, and exposition
  findings before synthesis.

## Boundaries

This task is literature and review preparation only.  It does not edit the
Paper III manuscript, Lean sources, trust/release surfaces, or standalone
mirror; it does not contact prospective reviewers; and it does not assert
private editorial knowledge.  The dossier and any persona packets remain
review-sub-agent material only.  Any later conventions-only manuscript context
requires a separately selected, specialized extraction.

Numerical review grades are reported in chat only.  Do not persist them in
handoffs, task cards, dossiers, cold-read reports, or review syntheses; on-disk
records keep findings and categorical verdicts only.
