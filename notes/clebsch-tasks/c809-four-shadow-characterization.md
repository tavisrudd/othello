# C809 — Four-shadow characterization of the golden operator

**Lane:** `clebsch`  
**Status:** queued; mathematics only; cheap falsifiers first; paper promotion excluded

## Objective

Determine whether the order-six golden symmetric conference operator is characterized, up to the expected signed-permutation, scalar, and marking dualities, by the coincidence of its four cubic shadows: the triangle cubic, the diagonal of the third compound, the Pfaffian cubic, and the cross-golden determinant cubic.

## Cheap gates first

1. Normalize all four cubics for a generic symmetric zero-diagonal $6\times6$ matrix and solve the coincidence constraints incrementally, rather than assuming the conference equations.
2. Enumerate the small exact signing/switching space at order six and test which proper subsets of the identities already force $C^2=5I$ and the golden splitting.
3. Compute the Jacobian rank and tangent space at the golden point to distinguish rigidity from a positive-dimensional accidental family.
4. Only if a non-tautological statement survives, make a bounded literature check under principal Pfaffians, compound matrices, determinantal representations, cubic covariants, and symmetric conference matrices.

## Mathematical continuation if the gate passes

Extract the weakest exact hypotheses that force the conference relation and golden orbit. Separate labelled, switching, and fully unmarked conclusions, and identify any exceptional or degenerate components scheme-theoretically.

## Stop conditions

Stop the positive characterization branch if the identities hold on a broad ambient family or admit a positive-dimensional non-Clebsch component. Record the exact counterfamily and salvage the strongest valid partial characterization.

## Acceptance

An exact characterization theorem or counterclassification, reproducible symbolic/finite certificates, and a human proof route with every equivalence and degeneracy stated.
