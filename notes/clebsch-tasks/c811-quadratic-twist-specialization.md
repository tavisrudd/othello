# C811 — Quadratic-twist specialization principle

**Lane:** `clebsch`  
**Status:** queued; mathematics only; cheap falsifiers first; paper promotion excluded

## Objective

Formulate the sharp general criterion for a quadratic cover or finite Stein normalization $z^2=cJ$ in which the branch divisor fixes $J$ up to scalar and one complete étale special fibre or residue algebra determines the square class of $c$.

## Cheap gates first

1. Write the function-field calculation at a rational unramified point explicitly and determine exactly what the fibre algebra recovers about $cJ(P)$.
2. Stress-test the statement against missing rational points, incomplete or nonreduced fibres, rescaling of $J$, nonnormal bases, nonconstant units, Picard $2$-torsion, and vertical bad primes.
3. Decide early whether the proposed result is only the elementary specialization of a Kummer torsor; if so, seek the precise geometric obstruction sequence or close it as standard.
4. Make a quick literature check before investing in a general proof: quadratic twists, double covers, specialization of $\mu_2$-torsors, discriminant covers, and Stein factorization.

## Mathematical continuation if the gate passes

Give function-field and integral versions with precise hypotheses, functoriality under base change, and sharp counterexamples when a hypothesis is removed.

## Stop conditions

If the strongest honest result is a direct Kummer calculation already standard in exactly this form, record that ceiling and do not manufacture novelty. Preserve any useful sharpness examples.

## Acceptance

A precise proposition, proof, failure-mode ledger, and a bounded precedence assessment distinguishing standard Kummer theory from any genuinely stronger geometric statement.

## Promotion boundary

Do not edit a paper source or public package. Freeze any positive result in the task report; manuscript integration, public positioning, and a new cold read require a separately allocated follow-up.
