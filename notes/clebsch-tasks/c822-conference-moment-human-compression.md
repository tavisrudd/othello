# C822 — Human compression of the order-26 conference separator

**Lane:** `clebsch`  
**Status:** queued; mathematics only; C812 exact values frozen; paper promotion excluded

## Objective

Compress C812's classification-backed exact census into the shortest honest
human structural proof: reduce the eight-coordinate triple-union profile to
the minimal local configuration counts forced by conference regularity, then
derive those counts directly from one representative of each of the four
order-26 Latin-square/Steiner construction classes.

## Frozen input

C812 proves that representatives \(1,2,4,8\) have four distinct centred
third moments. Their eight-coordinate profiles have empirical affine rank two,
with union sizes five and six as pivots. Treat the four exact profiles and
moments as falsifiers and targets, not as the desired proof.

## Cheap gates first

1. Prove or refute that the conference \(3\)-design equations and regular
   two-graph constraints force the observed two-dimensional profile plane.
   Do not infer this from the four data points.
2. Express the candidate pivot counts as named local configurations on five
   and six vertices, with an exact conversion to the third centred moment.
3. Use exactly four canonical representatives, one per classified switching
   class; do not retain the other eleven descendants in the proof.
4. Attempt construction-level counts for the two Latin-square and two Steiner
   classes. Prefer orbit-stabilizer, incidence, or character sums to induced
   subgraph enumeration.

## Alternative attacks

- If the rank-two plane is not forced, seek the smallest linear functional of
  local configuration counts that still gives the four C812 moments.
- If one uniform count is unavailable, give four short construction-specific
  lemmas with a common final conversion theorem.
- If a low-automorphism class resists hand counting, freeze the smallest
  transparent orbit ledger and state exactly why a fully human proof would be
  longer or less informative than the checker.

## Stop conditions

Stop if the apparent rank-two compression is accidental or if its proof
requires a case table comparable to the existing certificate. Retain the
strongest genuine compression: four representatives, the minimal local
counts, and a tiny checker. Do not relabel a finite orbit ledger as a
conceptual proof.

## Acceptance

Either:

1. a human theorem deriving the third moment from at most two local counts,
   direct counts for all four construction classes, and a proof that the four
   values separate the classified classes; or
2. a sharp obstruction identifying the irreducible finite step, together with
   a materially smaller four-representative certificate and replay.

The report must distinguish imported classification, human derivation,
finite orbit accounting, and machine verification.

## Promotion boundary

Do not edit a manuscript or public package. Any paper integration, novelty
positioning, or formalization requires a separately allocated successor.
