# C907 — marked punctual saturation is not a formal consequence

Date: 2026-08-13

Status: exact final singular-shadow no-go.  Double-boundary punctuality,
primitive-sixth formal monodromy, a flat pairing, and the projective-space
nilpotent tag do not make the surviving punctual marking unique.  The proposed
one-dimensional marked quotient is therefore a geometric theorem, not a
formal shadow.  Forcing it by quotienting by the kernel of the rank row is
tautologically equivalent to the rank-row theorem one is trying to prove.

## 1. A marked punctual countermodel

Let `D_tau` be the two-variable Weyl algebra and let

\[
 \delta_0=D_\tau/D_\tau(\tau_1,\tau_2).
\]

Choose a vector space `C` of dimension at least two and a nonzero covector
`r:C -> K`.  Put

\[
 \mathcal B=\delta_0\otimes C.                                \tag{1}
\]

Localization at either Fourier coordinate kills `B`, but inverse Fourier
transform gives the constant full-support module

\[
 \operatorname{FL}^{-1}(\mathcal B)
   =\mathcal O_{\mathbf A^2}\otimes C.                         \tag{2}
\]

Thus the punctual Fourier space modulo wall-supported punctual classes can
have arbitrary dimension.  Choosing one vector `p in C` as the common point
marking does not make it span `C`.

There is also no formal uniqueness of the marking.  Let `G in GL(C)` satisfy
`rG != r`.  Two incident receivers may use the same punctual object (1) and
differ only by the marking transition `G`.  All support and characteristic-
cycle shadows agree, while the point row changes.

## 2. Pairing, monodromy, and the nilpotent tag do not repair it

Replace `C` by the hyperbolic space

\[
 \widehat C=C\oplus C^\vee
\]

with its standard perfect pairing.  Extend `G` by

\[
 \widehat G=G\oplus G^{-\mathsf T}.                            \tag{3}
\]

Then `G_hat` preserves the pairing but still changes the chosen row on the
first summand.  Tensor that summand with a rank-one `z`-connection of formal
monodromy `zeta_6`, tensor the dual summand with its dual connection, and
finally tensor everything with

\[
 K[N]/(N^3).
\]

The transition (3) preserves the pairing, the primitive-sixth/dual formal
blocks, and the common nilpotent tag.  It remains invisible after either
Fourier localization, yet it changes the marked primitive-sixth point row.
Consequently none of those formal structures implies marked punctual
saturation.

## 3. Why the one-dimensional quotient is the theorem in disguise

Let `W` denote the punctual subspace known to come from wall-supported
classes.  The proposed shortcut was

\[
 \dim(C/W)=1
\]

with the quotient generated by the common point marking.  The countermodel
above shows that support theory supplies no such dimension bound.  Enlarging
`W` to `ker(r)` makes the quotient one-dimensional, but the assertion that
the analytic transition preserves `ker(r)` is exactly

\[
 rG=r                                                   \tag{4}
\]

up to a harmless nonzero scalar if only the Boolean is measured.  Equation
(4) is the desired rank-row statement, not a cheaper singular consequence.

In geometric language, numerical classes of rank zero need not be supported
on the unstable boundary.  Therefore quotienting only by boundary-supported
classes cannot be replaced silently by quotienting by the whole rank kernel.

## 4. Exact surviving formulation

The last singular reduction remains useful, but only as a change of language.
For the complete oriented two-wall correction `B_corner`, one must prove

\[
 \mu_{00}(B_{\rm corner})=0
\]

after applying the actual Gamma point row, or equivalently prove directly
that the incident transition preserves the rank kernel.  Punctuality,
self-duality, formal monodromy, and `N`-linearity do not imply this scalar
identity.

This closes the cheap-shadow search.  Any further proof must use geometric
input which remembers the augmentation row: an unlocalized Gamma/window
comparison, an oriented residue computation, or a common intrinsic
large-radius realization.  There is no remaining formal singular shortcut.

## EJ / TT / AA

- **EJ:** the marked punctual quotient can have arbitrary rank even when
  every one-wall localization sees zero.
- **TT:** declaring the quotient one-dimensional by modding out the rank
  kernel assumes the desired theorem.
- **AA:** retain the punctual formulation only as an exact scalar target;
  stop searching for a proof from unmarked support or formal Stokes data.
