## Main theorem in my own words

Fix the literal quadratic augmentation module of the six Sylow-5 subgroups of
\(A_5\). A normalized metric chordal cubic on that carrier intrinsically
recovers a split twelve-point orbit on its singular rational normal quartic,
and stabilizers quotient that orbit to the original six-set. The nontrivial
outer permutation of that six-set acts linearly on the invariant cubic pencil;
its difference from the identity carries either selected chordal line
isomorphically to the oriented conference line. Consequently normalized metric
chordal shadows, the expanded recovered carrier, and oriented conference
packages with a selected compatible chordal line form equivalent groupoids.
If the line is forgotten, one residual \(C_2\)-action remains, so the unselected
correspondence is a quotient rather than an equivalence.

The recovered six-set also controls two distinct integral lattices with a
common binary heart. More generally, for every normalized symmetric conference
matrix of order \(n\equiv2\pmod4\), the root-weight operator
\((I+B)/2\) minimally stabilizes \(D_n^\vee\), satisfies a quadratic relation,
and induces the stated split/inert mod-eight coefficient algebra. At \(n=6\)
the quotient is the unique nonsplit extension of a trivial \(\mathbf F_4\)-line
by the natural two-dimensional \(\mathbf F_4A_5\)-module. Reversing conference
orientation and applying the outer normalizer both act as Frobenius on its
endomorphism field. Paper IV's separate construction has the analogous
degree-three Frobenius-orbit commutant, without a claimed geometric map between
the two branches.
