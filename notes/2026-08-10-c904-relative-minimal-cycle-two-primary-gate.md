## Executive verdict

The fibrewise odd-minimal-class theorem globalizes on the present smooth
exotic marked base.  This strengthens the provisional conclusion originally
recorded in this report.

On the smooth marked $A_5$ base, the Fano incidence construction gives an
integral relative cubic correspondence inducing $[2]$ on the intermediate
Jacobian.  Dividing by two gives a relative rational correspondence inducing
the identity.  Gate V proves that the residual order-three monodromy uniquely
rigidifies the full Neron--Severi lattice, so the divisor-product certificate
already gives an integral relative one-cycle of class

\[
                 c=\frac{\Theta^4}{4!}.
\]

This still does not supply an integral identity correspondence on the present
marked base.  Voisin's fixed-fibre proof uses rationally connected lifting over
a curve; in a one-parameter family the support of a relative minimal curve is
a surface, and the corresponding generic fibre is over a transcendence-degree
two field.  Its index is the exact remaining obstruction.  An odd index would
combine with the incidence $[2]$ correspondence by Bezout and close the gate.

The exact calculation explains why every route using only pullbacks of the
chosen principal theta bundle stops at two.  Let
$N$ be the full integral divisor lattice of the exotic principal quotient and
let $N_{\mathrm{vis}}$ be the lattice generated by pullbacks of the principal
theta bundle through the full generic endomorphism order.  Then

\[
 N/N_{\mathrm{vis}}\cong(\mathbf Z/2)^4.
\]

As an $A_5$ module this quotient is exactly the four-dimensional six-point
heart whose commutant is $\mathbf F_4$.  It is the same modular object that
selects the exotic gluing.  It is not the Picard torsor.  Moreover the
fourfold products of
$N_{\mathrm{vis}}$ have saturation quotient

\[
 (\mathbf Z/2)^6\oplus(\mathbf Z/4)^5,
\]

and $c$ has exact order four modulo those products.  Thus the exotic gluing
and multiplier-four Hecke neighbor meet the same two-primary norm-square
arithmetic.  Relative Picard rigidification is a separate torsor and is
positive.

This is a theorem about the Rosati norm-square sublattice, not an obstruction
to arbitrary relative line bundles.  The Picard torsors do vanish by the
order-three argument in gate V.  The relative Abel--Jacobi lifting index is
the remaining smooth-base obstruction to an integral identity cycle.

## 1. The relative theorem that is already available

Let $U$ be the smooth part of Roulleau's $A_5$ cubic pencil and let
$\mathcal J\to U$ be its principally polarized intermediate-Jacobian scheme.
After the exotic-gluing and elliptic-twist markings, the integral local system
is a fixed principal quotient of the six-axis source.  Deligne's equivalence
between abelian schemes and polarized integral variations of Hodge structure
therefore makes every integral monodromy-equivariant coefficient operator a
relative endomorphism.

There are three different relative conclusions, and they must not be
conflated.

### 1.1 An integral relative correspondence inducing two

The pencil has Roulleau's common line, so the relative Albanese embedding
$a:\mathcal F\to\mathcal J$ of the Fano surfaces can be normalized over $U$.
Let $\mathcal C$ be the corresponding relative incidence divisor.  The
classical identities are

\[
 a^*\Theta\equiv_{\mathrm{num}}2\mathcal C,
 \qquad
 a_*[\mathcal F]=\frac{\Theta^3}{3!}.
\]

If $\mathcal P$ is the normalized Poincare bundle on
$\mathcal J\times_U\mathcal F$, then

\[
 c_1(\mathcal P)\cdot[\mathcal C]
       \in \operatorname{CH}^2(\mathcal J\times_U\mathcal F)
\]

induces $2\operatorname{id}_{\mathcal J}$.  Composing with the universal line
on $\mathcal F\times_U\mathcal X$ gives an integral relative codimension-two
cubic cycle inducing $[2]$.  Therefore one half of this cycle is a rational
relative universal cycle inducing the identity.

The same factor is visible directly on curve classes:

\[
 a_*[\mathcal C]
 =\frac12\Theta\cdot\frac{\Theta^3}{3!}
 =2\frac{\Theta^4}{4!}=2c.
\]

### 1.2 A primitive relative minimal cycle on the marked base

A horizontal symmetric homomorphism defines a section of the relative
Neron--Severi sheaf.  Its symmetric line-bundle representatives form an
affine $\mathcal J[2]$-torsor.  Gate V computes the actual residual mod-$2$
monodromy on the exotic marked base: it is an order-three group with no fixed
vector on $\mathcal J[2]$.  Odd-order cohomology vanishes, so every such
torsor has a unique invariant point.  The fifteen integral divisor classes
used in the fibrewise saturation proof therefore become relative line
bundles on $U$ itself.  The fixed integral fourfold-intersection identity
defines

\[
       Z_{\min}\in\operatorname{CH}^4(\mathcal J/U)
\]

with class $c$ on every fibre.  This is a relative primitive minimal
one-cycle on the present marked base.  It is signed and need not be
effective.

### 1.3 What does not follow

Voisin's theorem that a minimal one-cycle yields a universal codimension-two
cycle is a fixed-threefold theorem.  Its decisive step decomposes the minimal
cycle into curves and lifts each curve through a rationally connected
Abel--Jacobi fibration by Graber--Harris--Mazur--Starr.

For a family over $U$, a relative curve has a surface as total support.  The
pulled-back rationally connected fibration is over the function field of that
surface.  Rational connectedness does not force index one over a
transcendence-degree two field.  If the indices of the generic fibres have
common multiple $n$, the relative construction gives $[n]$, not the identity.

Consequently:

- a relative minimal one-cycle does not formally imply an integral relative
  universal cubic cycle on the same base;
- after a further generically finite base change, spreading the fixed-fibre
  construction does give such a cycle;
- on the original marked base an odd lifting index would combine with the
  already constructed $[2]$ cycle to give the identity integrally.

This corrects the earlier shorthand that called the two relative problems
equivalent.  They are equivalent fibre by fibre, not relatively without an
index argument.

## 2. The exact relative divisor defect

Write the generic non-CM Jacobian as the exotic principal quotient of the
six-axis source.  Its full generic endomorphism order is the lattice of
rational $5\times5$ coefficient matrices preserving the principal homology
lattice.  Every such endomorphism is horizontal on the marked base.

For an endomorphism $f$, the pullback $f^*\Theta$ is an honest relative line
bundle.  The theorem of the cube shows that the lattice generated by all such
pullbacks is already generated by the pullbacks for an integral basis of the
endomorphism order and their pairwise sums.  The exact computation is
therefore exhaustive, not a short-vector search.

> **Relative divisor-defect theorem.**  On the exotic $A_5$ principal
> quotient, the lattice $N_{\mathrm{vis}}$ generated by all $f^*\Theta$,
> with $f$ in the full generic endomorphism order, has index $16$ in the full
> integral Neron--Severi lattice $N$.  Its quotient has Smith type
> $(2,2,2,2)$.  As an $A_5$ module it is isomorphic to the simple
> four-dimensional six-point heart; it has no fixed vector and its commutant
> has dimension two over $\mathbf F_2$.

The smaller $\mathbf Z[A_5]$ endomorphism order reaches the same index-$16$
lattice.  Adding the divisors obtained from the six elliptic quotient maps and
their Poincare cross terms does not enlarge it.

> **Fourfold-product theorem.**  The lattice generated by fourfold products
> of $N_{\mathrm{vis}}$ has full rational Hodge rank fifteen.  Inside its
> saturation its Smith quotient is
> \[
>        (\mathbf Z/2)^6\oplus(\mathbf Z/4)^5,
> \]
> of order $2^{16}$.  The minimal class $c$ has exact order four modulo this
> product lattice.

Thus the subalgebra generated by endomorphism pullbacks of the chosen theta
bundle constructs $4c$ but not $c$ or $2c$.  Arbitrary horizontal divisor
classes are larger: their uniquely split symmetric representatives construct
$c$ itself.  The incidence geometry separately constructs $2c$.

The order-two deck representative

\[
 \nu:x\longmapsto 2/x,
 \qquad (0\ \infty)(1\ 2)(3\ 4),
\]

has determinant $-1$ on the five-axis lattice, preserves $6I-J$, normalizes
but does not belong to $A_5$, and conjugates $\omega$ to $\omega^2$ on the
four-dimensional heart.  It exchanges the two exotic principal lattices
integrally for every three-primary slope.  This identifies the index-$16$
relative divisor defect with the same heart on which the exotic deck acts by
the nontrivial $\mathbf F_4/\mathbf F_2$ automorphism.

## 3. The visible-carrier parity theorem

All classical geometric carriers can be placed on one line.  With
$c=\Theta^4/4!$:

\[
\begin{array}{c|c}
\text{carrier}&\text{class or transport multiplier}\\ \hline
\text{Fano incidence / Abel--Prym}&2c\\
\text{quartic--cubic Hecke pushforward}&4c\\
\text{six elliptic axes}&6c\\
\text{fifteen genus-two curves}&12c\\
\text{Hecke pullback}&4^4c=256c.
\end{array}
\]

The axis identity is the six-vector tight frame.  For the genus-two curves,
Roulleau gives $C_sD_g=2$ and the intersection table.  If
$D_{\mathrm{tot}}=\sum_gD_g$, then each $D_g$ meets $D_{\mathrm{tot}}$ in
$12$, while $6C_s$ has the same intersections.  On the generic rank-fifteen
Neron--Severi group this gives $D_{\mathrm{tot}}\equiv6C_s$, and the relation
extends throughout the smooth pencil.  Since $a_*C_s=2c$, one obtains
$a_*D_{\mathrm{tot}}=12c$.

The Hecke identities follow from

\[
 \Phi^*\Theta_X=4\Theta_Q,
 \qquad \deg\Phi=4^5:
 \qquad
 \Phi^*c_X=4^4c_Q,
 \quad \Phi_*c_Q=4c_X.
\]

Hence the subgroup of the invariant curve-class line generated by all
currently licensed canonical carriers is exactly $2\mathbf Zc$.  This is an
exact ceiling for these constructions, not a proof that some new geometric
carrier cannot be odd.

## 4. Deck descent

The two deck characters behave differently.

| deck | action | result for the relative cycle problem |
|---|---|---|
| elliptic twist | $-1$ on the elliptic multiplicity $H^1$ | trivial on divisor and even cohomology classes; symmetric theta pullbacks and the incidence $[2]$ cycle descend |
| exotic gluing | exchanges $\omega$ and $\omega^2$ through $\nu$ | fixes $c$ but acts nontrivially on the four norm-square quotient classes; norm of a primitive cycle has class $2c$ |

The fibrewise divisor certificate contains no equivariant lift through the
cycle-class presentation kernel.  If $P$ is a chosen presentation module and
$K$ its kernel, the obstruction to a deck-invariant lift of $c$ is the
connecting class

\[
                \partial(c)\in H^1(C_2,K).
\]

The present computation proves that the easy theta-generated submodule cannot
settle deck descent: there $c$ has order four.  Gate V settles Picard
rigidification on either exotic sheet but does not compute $\partial(c)$ in
the full Chow group for the involution exchanging the sheets.  The only
unconditional descent to the unmarked base is still the norm, with class
$2c$.

## 7. EJ + TT closeout

The later gate-V correction separates two different parity phenomena:

\[
\boxed{
\begin{array}{c}
\text{exotic gluing heart }H_2
\cong N/N_{\mathrm{vis}}\text{ as a Rosati norm-square defect};\\
\text{residual }C_3\text{ monodromy separately gives }
H^1(C_3,J[2])=0\text{ and unique Picard lifts}.
\end{array}}
\]

The high-EV next attack is not another divisor or orbit search.  The Picard
torsor is closed.  It is to compute the Abel--Jacobi generic-fibre index and
test whether the common line supplies a degree-one or at least odd-degree
zero-cycle.

Two cheap consequences are now settled:

1. the fifteen named genus-two curves cannot hide the odd class; their sum is
   $12c$;
2. the six elliptic quotient maps and all their Poincare cross terms do not
   enlarge the canonical relative divisor lattice beyond index $16$.

## 8. Mystery ledger

- **Why does the same four-dimensional heart recur?**  Settled
  computationally and representation-theoretically: it is both the exotic
  $\mathbf F_4$ gluing module and $N/N_{\mathrm{vis}}$.  A conceptual theorem
  deriving both from one Rosati-order construction is still open.  The
  Picard torsor itself is closed.
- **Does the primitive relative minimal cycle exist on the present marked
  base?**  Settled positively in gate V by unique order-three theta
  rigidification.  Descent from the exotic marked base to the unmarked base
  remains a separate $C_2$ Chow-equivariance question.
- **Does a relative minimal cycle imply the integral relative cubic identity
  cycle?**  Settled negatively as a formal implication.  The remaining exact
  invariant is the index of the rationally connected Abel--Jacobi generic
  fibre over the relative curve surface.  Odd index suffices by Bezout with
  the incidence $[2]$ cycle.
- **Can the Hecke neighbor transport the odd class?**  Settled negatively for
  the isogeny alone: pushforward multiplies by four and pullback by $256$.
- **Can the named Fano curves resolve parity?**  Settled negatively for the
  printed incidence, six-axis, and genus-two carriers: their generated class
  line is exactly $2\mathbf Zc$.
- **Does the cycle extend over all four cusps?**  Open and independent of the
  smooth-base parity theorem.  Gate V proves that all four local mod-$2$
  theta residues vanish.  The exact remaining gap is extension of the signed
  Chow cycle through a semiabelian model and Abel--Jacobi residue control at
  the four admissible degenerations.
- **Priority.**  Open at manuscript standard.  The claim-specific primary
  audit found no predecessor, but citation-graph closure is incomplete.
