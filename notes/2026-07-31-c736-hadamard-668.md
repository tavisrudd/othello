## New exclusion

Let `H` be either stable subgroup ID 9, generated by `{73,85}`, or ID 10,
generated by `{73,121}`, in the numbering of arXiv:2607.20765v1, Table A1.
Both groups have order 6, image of order 3 modulo 9, and image of order 6
modulo 37.

For an `H`-invariant sign sequence, write its 9-compression as

```text
(c0, x, y, c3, x, y, c6, x, y).
```

The three fixed columns `0,3,6` have multiplier-orbit sizes
`1,6,6,6,6,6,6`, hence

```text
c0,c3,c6 in {+-1,+-11,+-13,+-23,+-25,+-35,+-37}.
```

In particular, each is `+-1 (mod 12)`.  The two moving column-orbits have
stabilizer of order 2 and allow every odd value from `-37` through `37`, so
`x,y` are odd.  Independently negate each sequence to normalize its row sum to
1.  With `S=c0+c3+c6`,

```text
S + 3(x+y) = 1.
```

The sum of three residues in `{+-1}` modulo 12, together with `S = 1 (mod 6)`,
forces `S = 1 (mod 12)` and therefore `x+y = 0 (mod 4)`.  At shift 1 the
compressed periodic autocorrelation is

```text
R = S(x+y) + 3xy.
```

If `x+y=0 (mod 8)`, then `S=1 (mod 8)` and `xy=7 (mod 8)`; if
`x+y=4 (mod 8)`, then `S=5 (mod 8)` and `xy=3 (mod 8)`.  In either case
`R=5 (mod 8)`.  Both compressed sequences therefore contribute
`5+5=2 (mod 8)`, while the Legendre compression identity requires

```text
PAF_atilde(1) + PAF_btilde(1) = 37*(-2) = -74 = 6 (mod 8),
```

a contradiction.  This proof is exact and does not depend on a solver.

Consequently IDs 9 and 10 are impossible.  Combined with the baseline, every
fixed common-multiplier subgroup of order 6 is excluded except ID 7
`<73,112>`.

translation-twisted multipliers, other Goethals--Seidel/SDS constructions, or a
Hadamard matrix of order 668.  Seven fixed common-multiplier cases remain:
IDs `0,1,2,3,4,5,7`.  The highest-value next target is ID 7: it is the sole
surviving order-6 case, its 9-compression is genuinely feasible, and any further
