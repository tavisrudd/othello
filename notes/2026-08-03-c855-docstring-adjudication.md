## 2. Provenance stratification: what the "66 generated modules" actually covers

The generated boundary is not what the checklist's phrasing implies, and the difference
changes which declarations can be fixed through the template.

`scripts/generate-q11-a5-point-action.py` in the q11 package emits exactly two file families:

- `Q11A5PointOrbitsRowsG{group:02d}.lean` for **groups 5–59 only** (`for group in range(5, GROUP_COUNT)`), and
- `Q11A5PointOrbitsRows{block:02d}.lean` for **blocks 1–11 only** (`for block in range(1, GROUP_COUNT // BLOCK)`).

That is 55 + 11 = **66 emitted modules**, and exactly those 66 carry the provenance line
`Generated by lean/scripts/generate-q11-a5-point-action.py`. The remaining machine-shaped
modules are hand-committed:

- `Q11A5PointOrbitsRowsG00`–`G04` (five leaves) are *pre-landed*. The generator does not write
  them; `main()` only asserts that the recomputed action table agrees with the table parsed
  out of the landed file (`landed_table`, `assert tables[group] == old`). Their headers are a
  single sentence and carry **no generator attribution at all**, although their bodies are
  byte-shaped exactly like the emitted leaves.
- `Q11A5PointOrbitsRows00` (block aggregator 0) is likewise hand-committed and unattributed.
- `Q11A5PointOrbitsData.lean` is the generator's **input** (`DATA = RCA / "Q11A5PointOrbitsData.lean"`,
  parsed at line 20), not its output. It is the semantic data module.
- `Q11A5PointOrbitsFixed00`–`06`, `Support00`–`11`, `Matrix00`–`11`, `Representatives`,
  `Arithmetic`, and `FixedFast` are hand-committed, machine-shaped, and have **no generator**.

Consequences for section 4 and section 6:

1. The generator's `--check` staleness mode cannot detect drift in the six unattributed
   machine-shaped modules, so a docstring fix applied there is not template-enforced and can
   silently diverge from the emitted 66.
2. Any template change that adds docstrings must be paired with a decision about the five
   pre-landed group leaves: either bring groups 0–4 into the emitted range (preferred — it makes
   the leaf family uniform and closes the attribution gap), or give them their own attributed,
   independently checked provenance header.
3. The 470 candidates in the Fixed/Support/Matrix/Representatives/Arithmetic/Data family have no
   template to route through today. They are adjudicated below as *template-eligible*: the
   remedy is to bring them under a generator (or one row-family docstring each), not to hand-write
   470 docstrings.

## 4. Adjudication of the bulk strata

**(b) Generated leaf — fix through the template, not by hand.** All 275 candidates in the 55
attributed emitted leaves, plus the 10 in the five pre-landed leaves. The template must emit,
for each row `N`:

- `pointActionCode_N` — "the length-133 image table of the `N`-th normalized projective matrix
  acting on the standard representatives of `PG(2,11)`, in representative index order";
- `pointAction_eq_code_N` — "for every point index `p`, the semantic action of group element `N`
  agrees with the tabulated code";
- `orbitIndex_code_N` — "the tabulated image of `N` preserves the declared orbit index";
- `action_on_witness_row_N` and `orbitIndex_pointAction_row_N` — the same two statements
  restricted to the witness set.

Each emitted docstring must additionally name the semantic input (`Q11A5PointOrbitsData`), the
deterministic invocation, and the fact that the row is checked by `decide` over a finite
domain rather than proved symbolically. That is one template edit covering 285 candidates.

**(b-conditional) Template-eligible, currently ungenerated — 470 candidates in 35 modules.**
Recommended remedy in priority order: extend the generator to emit the `Fixed`, `Support`,
`Matrix`, and `Representatives` families from the same `Q11A5PointOrbitsData` input, which makes
their docstrings template-enforced and staleness-checked; failing that, one shared family
docstring per row family with a per-module header stating the row range covered.

- `Q11A5PointOrbitsArithmetic` — its 14 candidates are the `*Fast` definitions and the

  only thing the mathematics depends on, and that the fast form carries no trust obligation of

### `canonicalLabel` — canonicity audit (feeds the rename plan)

Definition, verbatim (lines 39–41):

```lean
/-- A canonical labelling of a six-element finset by `Fin 6`. -/
noncomputable def canonicalLabel (A : Finset (Point K)) (hcard : A.card = 6) : Fin 6 → Point K :=
  fun i => ((Finset.equivFinOfCardEq hcard).symm i : A)
```

Theorems that **would** justify the name, if any existed: none. The two properties actually
proved are

```lean
theorem canonicalLabel_injective (A : Finset (Point K)) (hcard : A.card = 6) :
    Function.Injective (canonicalLabel A hcard)

theorem mem_iff_exists_canonicalLabel (A : Finset (Point K)) (hcard : A.card = 6) (x : Point K) :
    x ∈ A ↔ ∃ i : Fin 6, canonicalLabel A hcard i = x
```

Together these say only that `canonicalLabel` is a bijection `Fin 6 → A` — the defining property
of *any* labelling of a six-element finset. Nothing pins down *which* bijection: no theorem
relates `canonicalLabel` to an order on `Point K`, to the underlying `Multiset` order of `A`, to
`Finset.orderIsoOfFin`, or to any equivariance/naturality property. `Finset.equivFinOfCardEq` is
noncomputable and its underlying `Finset.equivFin` is an arbitrary chosen bijection, so the map is
canonical in no mathematical sense; every downstream use (`labelledArcPair`, `chordColor`,
`incidencePrismWitness_of_five`) is invariant under relabelling precisely because no canonicity is
needed. Recommend the rename plan treat `canonicalLabel` → a neutral name such as `arcLabel` or
`chosenLabel`, with the docstring saying "an arbitrary but fixed bijective labelling" rather than
"canonical". The same objection applies to `canonicalLabelEmbedding` and to the parallel use of
`Finset.equivFinOfCardEq hfive` inside `chordColor` and `colorPoint`.
