## The proposal is not new as a spine; the rigidity import is new

The atom spine itself is on file as the 2026-08-10 enhanced-atom bridge blueprint, with
the same five steps: the cubic zero atom carries primitive-sixth monodromy; the
projective-bundle formula puts two copies of it in `X x P^1`; no atom represented in
dimension at most two carries such monodromy; the decoration is invariant under the atom
equivalences; the non-rationality criterion gives the contradiction in dimension four.

What the proposal adds is the mechanism for the fourth step, which the blueprint left as a
paper-local lemma with a five-line proof sketch. That sketch argues through the
regular-singular residue alone (`d_s R = [Gamma_s(0), R]`), and the frame-transport memo's
Section 7 shows that reading only the compressed leading data is insufficient: in the
manuscript's own cubic block, deleting the `z^2` coefficient's `-8/81` entry moves the
indicial roots from `-1/6, -5/6` to `+/-1/18`, which contribute nothing. So the blueprint's
step 4 has the same hole the memo located, and the proposal is a candidate repair for it.

## Verified source clauses

**Remark 3.14 exists and says what was claimed.** For a maximal F-bundle and a rigid even
point `b` of the base, there is a unique germ `W` at `b` of a purely even closed analytic
submanifold with `span_k{Eu_w^k}_{k>=0} = T_{W,w}` for all `w` in `W` near `b`; the number
of distinct eigenvalues of `kappa|_W` and the list of their multiplicities are constant near
`b`; and if the F-bundle is of exponential type at one point of `W` it is so at all points,
which the authors say implies that the isomorphism classes of the formal meromorphic
connections with regular singularities attached to the eigenvalues by
Hukuhara--Levelt--Turrittin are constant along `W`.

That last clause is the one that matters here, because it freezes the whole
regular-singular formal type rather than the compressed pair, and therefore retains the
higher-loop-order data that carries the primitive-sixth exponents.

**The Witt relation and the Euler-power germ are as described.** The Frobenius powers
`Eu^k` satisfy `[Eu^k, Eu^l] = (l-k) Eu^{k+l-1}` (Remark 2.4, restated in Section 3.4 for
maximal F-bundles).

**The non-rationality criterion is Proposition 5.30**, the Hodge-atom specialization of
Proposition 5.17: a smooth complex projective `d`-fold carrying a Hodge atom not in
`HAtoms_{dim<=d-2}` is not rational. The blueprint cites 5.17; the Hodge-atom form is the
right citation for a Hodge-atom spine.

**Cai's bulk-rigidity argument is real and proved.** In the quartic-threefold paper he
derives, from the Witt relations and linear independence of `1, E, E^2, E^3`, the system
`E^k(lambda_i) = lambda_i^k`, reads it as an ODE system with unique solutions and small
quantum eigenvalues as initial data, and concludes that the multiplicity-three eigenvalue
persists across the big quantum germ.

**The cubic cyclicity claim checks out against our own data.** The manuscript's small even
connection has leading operator with eigenvalues `6r, -6r, 0, 0` and a nonzero nilpotent
part on the zero block (`J_0 = [[0,2],[0,0]]`), so its minimal polynomial is
`lambda^2 (lambda^2 - 36 r^2)`, of degree four. For a multiplication operator on a unital
algebra the minimal polynomial of the operator equals that of the element, so the subalgebra
generated by the Euler element has dimension four, which is exactly
`dim H^even` of a cubic threefold. The Euler-power germ is therefore not a distinguished
leaf at the cubic small point: it is the whole even germ. The cyclicity is borderline and
holds only because the zero block is nonsemisimple -- the same nonsemisimplicity that made
the memo's coalesced case hard is what makes the germ maximal here.

## The one clause that is not proved in its source

KKPY state the eigenvalue-multiplicity constancy of Remark 3.14 flatly, but introduce the
Hukuhara--Levelt--Turrittin constancy with "more fine results can be proved, like, e.g.".
It is an unproved remark, not a theorem with a proof, and it is precisely the clause the
spine needs. Cai's argument proves the multiplicity clause and can be transplanted to the
cubic essentially verbatim -- with the cyclicity input cleaner here than in his quartic
case -- but it says nothing about the formal type beyond the eigenvalues.

So the state after this assessment is:

1. **Block splitting is closed**, or closeable by a short transplant of Cai's Section 5
   argument: the double zero eigenvalue cannot split under formal even bulk deformation in
   the cubic germ. This kills one of the two dangers the memo named.
2. **Formal-type constancy is open**, and is now the single load-bearing gap. It must be
   proved rather than cited, or sourced from a proved statement elsewhere.

## What proving the remaining clause looks like

The germ is the whole even germ at the cubic point, and along it the eigenvalues satisfy
`E^k(lambda_i) = lambda_i^k`. What is wanted is that the regular-singular factor attached to
the double zero eigenvalue has constant isomorphism class over that germ. Two routes:

- **Isomonodromy on the germ.** With the eigenvalue branch not splitting, the block's
  connection is a family of formal meromorphic connections over `W` with fixed exponential
  factor; constancy of the isomorphism class is an isomonodromy statement for the
  regular-singular part. The memo's Proposition on the isomonodromy criterion covers only
  the semisimple case and must be redone with the full Sylvester inverse, but along `W` the
  Witt structure supplies the extra rigidity the memo did not have.
- **Exponential type plus rigidity of the residue conjugacy class.** The relevant invariant
  is the conjugacy class of the residue, hence of `exp(2 pi i R)`; freezing it requires
  controlling the whole normal form, which is where the `z^2` entry lives. Any candidate
  proof must reproduce the cubic `2x2` regression test.

## Scope consequence, which is a decision and not a finding

The atom spine does not repair `prop:framed-operations`; it removes the need for it at the
cubic endpoint by never transporting a framed operator across the comparison isomorphisms
in the first place. The endpoint theorem -- `X x P^1` irrational for every smooth cubic
threefold -- would become unconditional on a proved rigidity clause, with no receiver, no
mixed Hahn field, and no hypothesis of the form the manuscript currently exposes.

It does not give the conditional all-projective-stabilizations theorem. That theorem rests
on the Gamma-rank and transport machinery, and the atom route replaces exactly the part of
it that generalizes. Restructuring around the atom spine is therefore a trade of the general
conditional theorem for an unconditional crown, and is an architecture decision for the
author.

Two further consequences worth recording. Multiplicity alone cannot work in dimension four,
which is why the decoration is needed at all: a K3 or abelian surface center has a
zero Euler eigenvalue of large multiplicity, so Cai's quartic method -- which excludes
multiplicity three for symplectically rational six-manifolds -- has no dimension-four
analogue that would subsume the cubic. And the cubic zero atom is known to resemble a
genus-five curve atom at the level of the undecorated Hodge polynomial, which is the
independent reason the primitive-sixth packet has to do the separating work.

## Open checks before anything is written into the manuscript

1. **Rigid point.** "Rigid" is used at KKPY lines for Lemma 3.10 and Remark 3.14 but its
   definition was not located in the cached extraction; the cubic small point must be shown
   to be a rigid even point of the maximal A-model F-bundle base.
2. **Exponential type at the cubic point**, in the sense of the reference KKPY cite for it.
   The manuscript's own Levelt--Turrittin data makes this very likely but it is unchecked.
3. **Where the displacement lives.** The memo's bulk parameter is a positive-filtration
   element over a Novikov-completed coefficient ring. KKPY's germ is non-archimedean
   analytic over a Novikov-type field, so the fit looks favourable, but that the displaced
   parameter is a point of `W` must be checked rather than assumed.
4. **Atom-level well-definedness.** Remark 3.14 moves the base point inside one germ; the
   decoration must also be invariant under the atom equivalence relation used in the blowup
   and projective-bundle formulas. The blueprint's paper-local formal-monodromy lemma is
   still owed, now with the germ statement doing its hardest step.
