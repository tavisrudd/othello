## 3. What this means for the construction route

The exclusion theorem is **not** available: the transfer does not miss any part
of the escape lattice, so no obstruction argument can be built from a dead image
here. The route is licensed. Two caveats worth carrying into any write-up:

1. Liveness needs `u` transverse to `Theta`. The two divisibility-killed
   subfamilies (`Theta^[3] ^ a` and `u = Theta`) are exactly the ones a naive
   argument would look at first, and both are dead — so an argument that only
   examined those would have wrongly concluded exclusion.
2. Liveness also needs more than one `z`: each single `z` reaches only a
   hyperplane.
