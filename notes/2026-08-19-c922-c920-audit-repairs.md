All seven findings of the C920 audit are repaired. The mathematics of C920 needed no

## Repairs

1. **The published prior art is cited.** `lem:hirzebruch-euler-spectrum` derives the small
   quantum cohomology of every Hirzebruch surface by transporting `F_0` and `F_1` along one
   deformation. That result, by that route, is Cotti, *Cyclic stratum of Frobenius
   manifolds, Borel--Laplace `(alpha,beta)`-multitransforms, and integral representations of
   solutions of quantum differential equations*, Mem. Eur. Math. Soc. 2, EMS Press, 2022,
   doi:10.4171/MEMS/2, Chapter 9, Section 9.3, Theorems 9.3.1 and 9.3.3. Added: the
   bibliography entry `Cotti`; a paragraph in the proof, after the truncation step, saying
   that the two transported presentations are the published ones and what the derivation
   adds beyond them; the gated import `Cotti:hirzebruch-quantum-presentations` on the
   lemma, with a registry entry recording the exact substitution in both parities and the
   two conventions; and the regenerated dependency-graph node and edge.

   The substitution, checked in both parities: with `q_1` the fibre monomial and
   `q_1^k q_2` the shifted-section monomial, so `u = q_1` and `w = q_1^k q_2`, the source's
   even relations under `T_1 - k T_2 = S-flat`, `T_2 = F` become `S-flat^2 - u` and
   `F^2 - w`; the odd relations under `T_1 - (k+1) T_2 = S-flat`, `T_2 = F` become
   `S-flat^2 + S-flat F - u` and `F^2 - w S-flat`. Both are the displayed presentations.

   What the source does not provide, and what the manuscript keeps: the family realizing
   the deformation, the truncation of the transported product to finitely many Novikov
   monomials, and the resulting presentation of the ring specialized along a strictly
   Novikov-admissible map, which is the form the Euler quartic consumes.

2. **The claim map's scope string is corrected.** The `prop:low-dimensional-vanishing`
   cautions read "only for nonminimal surface centers; minimal rational ruled targets are
   covered by the direct specialized argument". Both clauses were false after C920 — `F_1`
   is nonminimal and is covered directly, and the class covered directly is every
   Hirzebruch surface — and it was the last occurrence of the terminology C920 renamed away.
   Now: "only for surface centers that are neither minimal nor geometrically ruled;
   Hirzebruch surface targets are covered by the direct specialized argument". This was a
   round-three referee blocker reported as applied and never applied.

3. **The novelty ledger is brought current.** Its preamble and three rows still said the
   birational-invariance and `V_14` claims depend on Hypotheses 5.7R and 5.7T without
   qualification. All four now state the residual scope of 5.7T. A new row records the
   specialized primitive-sixth vanishing for rational geometrically ruled centers, with the
   priority posture split: none claimed for the two presentations, which are Cotti's;
   claimed for the truncation, the degeneracy dichotomy, and the valuation and symbol
   arithmetic. The posture on the Euler quartic itself is recorded as unsettled, since only
   Cotti's Chapter 9 was read and the Frobenius-manifold literature on the spectrum of
   Euler multiplication for these surfaces was not searched.

   The ledger is not read by `check_formal_artifact.py`, which is why the drift went
   uncaught. One occurrence of a word forbidden by the workspace style rules was removed
   from the `V_14` row while editing it.

4. **`lem:ruled-degeneracy-dichotomy` cites both lemmas its closing sentence needs.** It
   said "By Lemma `lem:center-specialization-nondegenerate`, case (b) does not arise for a
   center specialization when `a >= 1`", but that lemma requires `chi` graded-monomial when
   `a = 1`, which for center specializations holds by `lem:center-maps-monomial`. Both are
   now cited. Changing the statement invalidated its recorded statement digest; the row's
   cautions were re-examined and extended to say that the first of the three cited results
   is absent from Lean, so at index one the graded-monomiality is argued in the manuscript
   only, and then the one digest was refreshed with
   `lean/verification/refresh_claim_digests.py lem:ruled-degeneracy-dichotomy`.

5. **One Lean docstring keeps the manuscript's term.**
   `Quantum.oddCombination_ne_zero_of_monomialLeadingTerms` said "monomial specialization"
   and opened unconditionally; it now says graded-monomial, matching the structure, the two
   interface docstrings and `def:monomial-specialization`, and conditions on its premise as
   its sibling already did.

6. **Two prose leftovers in the deformation passage.** The sentence "One family is used
   rather than `k` successive ones, which keeps the transport explicit" argued against a
   superseded draft and is deleted. The nonsplitness sentence no longer runs two
   independent claims through "; and": it now closes on `epsilon != 0`, which the
   construction uses, and the projectivization starts its own sentence.

7. **The Lean README inventory covers the C920 modules.** Three bullets were added, for the
   discriminant separation of the rank-four Euler spectrum, the algebraic core of strict
   Novikov admissibility used to exclude the degeneracy loci, and the assembled specialized
   vanishing statements, each stating what is not constructed. The bullet on the second also
   records that `lem:center-maps-monomial` is absent from Lean because the package builds no
   associated graded ring, which closes a round-three request that had been left to the
   claims row alone. C910's comparable additions are still undescribed there; that is its
   lane's to close, not touched here.

## Re-review of this task's own edits

Read cold after the repairs, two of my own sentences needed correcting and were corrected
before commit.

- The crediting paragraph first said the source used "the same shift of the section class".
  That is too strong: the shift is by `k` in the even parity and by `k+1` in the odd one in
  Cotti's generators, while the manuscript uses `S + kF` in both, the difference being the
  normalization of the section generator. It now says a shift by a multiple of the fibre
  class, and the exact substitution in each parity is recorded in the import registry, which
  is where it belongs.
- The new README bullet called `lem:center-maps-monomial` "the one premise of
  `thm:nu6-birational-invariance` recorded as absent". Nine claims are absent and the
  theorem's premise set was not enumerated, so the uniqueness was unsupported. It now says
  only that the lemma is recorded as absent from Lean.

Nothing else in the diff changed a mathematical statement. The only statement-identity hash
moved is `lem:ruled-degeneracy-dichotomy`, for the added cross-reference in item 4.
