# C242 — rp-next session-ideas review and EV agenda

**Lane:** `rp-next`  
**Status:** COMPLETE. This report deduplicates the ideas raised in the 2026-07-17 session and the
three Fable direction documents added in commit `4e7babd1`, reconciles them with C226--C241, and
orders the surviving work by expected research value. It proves no new literature claim and does
not silently revive routes closed by C240.

## Decision rule

“EV” here is deliberately qualitative. An item rises when it combines:

1. a valuable theorem, paper, or deployed capability if successful;
2. a high probability of a decisive result, including a useful negative;
3. a cheap first gate using committed objects and certificates; and
4. strong dependence on results this lane uniquely owns.

It falls with literature risk, prototype burden, vague novelty, unavailable scaling, or a long
proof program before the first falsifier. The order is therefore not a prestige ranking. A cheap
paper-strengthening lemma can outrank a speculative A+ program.

## Corrections forced by C240--C241

Three boundaries are fixed before ranking anything.

- **Peeling completeness:** the general minor/excluded-minor program is closed. C240 gives
  deletion, duality, and 2-sum counterexamples at radii two and three. The direction document
  `2026-07-17-fable5-peeling-completeness-classification.md` correctly allowed a decisive negative;
  that tier has already landed. Only explicitly restricted symmetric-family classifications may
  return.
- **Repair-profile shape:** universal ULC is false by C240's seven-column binary counterexample.
  Ordinary LC remains a conjecture supported by 10,760 sampled pointed cases. No Newton-bound or
  “repair profiles are Lorentzian” claim survives without a new theorem.
- **Interface finiteness:** the branchwidth proposal is no longer speculative. C241 proves exact
  contextual sufficiency and finite composition for terminal response maps. Exact synchronous
  timing remains on C234's infinite carrier.

The q=9 harmonic gate also admits one cheap strengthening checked during this review: C236's five
curve-point seed is inert and spanning without the nucleus, while adjoining the nucleus makes all
five missing curve points enter in one parallel radius-four round. This is a q=9 theorem candidate,
not yet an infinite-family statement.

### 4. C246 — realizable response profiles and contextual minimality

### 5. C247 — tract/foundation home audit

**Why fifth:** high conceptual ceiling and a short initial reading bill, but substantial risk that

**Bounded task:** test the proposed dictionary between support/labels/valuations and matroids over

**Success:** a verified exact dictionary plus a repair-specific theorem target. **Kill:** if this

### 6. C248 — field-sensitive monotone-span-program scoping

**Why sixth:** highest speculative complexity-theory ceiling, but the current gadget argument
does not yet prove a size separation.

**Bounded task:** formalize the port-to-MSP dictionary; distinguish existence/representability over
a field from minimum MSP size over that field; and test whether the AG(2,3)/anharmonic gadgets can
feed a known lifting or lower-bound theorem.

**Success:** one explicit, correctly parameterized field-sensitive succinctness target with a
credible lower-bound route. **Kill:** a characteristic obstruction alone is not an MSP size
separation.

### 7. C249 — transversal-robust agentic plan portfolios

**Why seventh:** the cheapest agentic-planning experiment with a crisp falsifier and an exact
metric derived from blocker theory.

**Bounded task:** construct a small planning/remediation corpus where many syntactically or
embedding-diverse plans share a small common failure-domain transversal; implement the exact
transversal objective; and compare equal-cost portfolios against standard diversity baselines.

**Success:** a strict, reproducible robustness separation plus a generation objective. **Kill:**
if existing diverse-planning/interdiction baselines already optimize the same frontier, retain the
metric as an application and do not claim a new planning problem.

## Outcome-gated watchlist, in EV order

These ideas are documented but deliberately not allocated. Promotion requires the stated gate or
an explicit user choice.

1. **Proof-carrying agent remediation.** Strong product fit: an untrusted proposer emits an action
   plan and a small checker verifies prerequisites, effects, capacities, closure, and stopping-core
   impossibility claims. Gate: define one real workflow language and show the checker catches a
   failure missed by the host planner.
2. **Failure-aware multi-extraction.** Multi-root extraction under unavailable operators, causal
   materialization, shared capacity, and certificates. Gate: beat or separate from a fair global
   ILP/e-graph/query baseline on a small corpus; weighted deduction and hyperpaths already own the
   generic core.
3. **Port-capacity converse.** C240 killed competitiveness of the disjoint C216+C218 construction,
   not of the port itself. Gate: distinguish full inner-code restriction, exact-port realization,
   and port containment, then improve the trivial independent-rank-density bound.
4. **Dynamic terminal controls.** Maintain C241 closure/core data under helper changes, especially
   deletions, with cost parameterized by actual boundary-control changes. Gate: a nontrivial
   amortized bound or benchmark win over recomputation.
5. **Delsarte harmonic-arc route.** Use association-scheme LP machinery on the block-free-set
   enumerator. Gate: verify the relevant scheme and show its LP is sharper than the elementary
   design bound before allocating an asymptotic arc task.
6. **Ideal/Mengerian and hyperflow classification.** C235 proves a packing gap, not the global
   claim that the harmonic repair clutter is ideal but non-Mengerian for every right-hand side.
   Gate: verify idealness and the exact minor/classification hypotheses; otherwise use only the
   packing/covering vocabulary.
7. **Tropical twins/Krob boundary.** Useful candidate positioning for C232--C234. Gate: prove the
   repair transfer objects fall inside the cited rational-series model and that the proposed
   sequential/nonsequential correspondence is exact; do not import undecidability by analogy.
8. **Continuation resilience.** Rank states by the smallest disruption hitting every safe future
   continuation. Gate: a strict rolling-migration example not already captured by viability
   kernels, solution-graph connectivity, or robust control.
9. **Zero-forcing/power-domination dictionary.** Gate: on graphic matroids, determine whether
   radius-truncated circuit closure equals any established forcing variant. A mismatch changes the
   pitch from translation to a new rule in their taxonomy.
10. **Restricted code-equivalence export.** Holonomy and Schur fingerprints are real for structured
    codes, but accessible circuit data is an explicit assumption. Requires the separate canonical-
    form audit and user promotion recorded in the restricted companion.
11. **Restricted quantum/CSS peeling.** Compare fixed-generator peeling with generator-independent
    complete bounded-port closure at equal locality on non-topological product-code instances.
    Promote only for a material measured gap after the specialist audit.
12. **Scaled C216 hardness.** Requires growing inner gadgets, a uniform confinement theorem, and
    honest length/rate accounting before hardness can be claimed inside good code families.
13. **GLDPC/component EXIT.** Requires an equal-rate comparison against mature SPC/Hamming/AG
    baselines before any threshold-saturation work.

- knowledge compilation is the evaluation discipline for compiled ports;

  causal valuation;

  pattern once the precise rule system is matched;

- the Repair Port Capsule/storage simulator remains the first product because it has a trustworthy

## Closed routes

Do not reopen these without a new hypothesis that survives the named counterexamples:

- universal ULC of pointed repair profiles;
- minor-, dual-, or 2-sum-closed `P_r` and its excluded-minor linter;
- plain-LRC frontier claims for the disjoint C216+C218 construction;
- equality of the full harmonic cascade with affine closure;
- a finite radius/width-only exact timing alphabet;
- generic novelty for Horn closure, semiring evaluation, provenance, e-graphs, sheaf holonomy,
  weighted automata, safe reconfiguration, or ordinary plan diversity.

## Portfolio shape

The EV order intentionally alternates consolidation and exploration. C243--C245 can strengthen or
close mathematical claims quickly. C246--C248 test the three highest-ceiling theory exports only
after C241 supplies a stable theorem base. C249 is the first agentic prototype because it has a
cleaner metric and cheaper falsifier than a general remediation compiler.

No item below C249 should be allocated merely because a neighboring field supplies an attractive
name. The promotion discriminator remains: a theorem, strict operational separation, explanatory
compression, or decisive negative.
