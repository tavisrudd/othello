# C909 — Prym orbit-axis saturation theorem

Date: 2026-08-12

Status: general structural theorem extracted from the cubic modular presentation curve;
no manuscript, PDF, mirror, or Lean edit

## Setup

Let `S` be a connected characteristic-zero base and let

```text
                       C --> D
```

be a family of etale double covers of smooth proper curves.  Write
`P=Prym(C/D)` and suppose a finite group `G` acts on the cover.  Let
`H_1,...,H_n` be one conjugacy orbit of cyclic subgroups of the same odd
order `m`.  Assume:

1. the quotient double cover `C/H_i --> D/H_i` is smooth etale and its Prym
   `E_i` is elliptic;
2. `P^{H_i,0}` is elliptic and the `H_i`-fixed coefficient lines form a
   basis of `Lie(P)` after deleting their unique linear relation, if any;
3. the primitive inclusion `i_i:P^{H_i,0}-->P` has Rosati norm
   `i_i^dagger i_i=[m]`;
4. the Gram matrix `Q=(i_i^dagger i_j)` of a selected basis is integral and
   positive definite; and
5. at every prime dividing `det Q`, the projective spectral packet of the
   corresponding self-dual graph kernel is finite etale and block-respecting.

The assumptions are geometric.  They do not include a chosen isogeny from an
elliptic power or a chosen graph kernel.

## Theorem

Under these hypotheses:

1. pullback along `C-->C/H_i` identifies the quotient Prym `E_i` with the
   primitive fixed axis `P^{H_i,0}`;
2. the selected axes define a relative polarized isogeny

   ```text
                         product E_i --> P
   ```

   with pulled polarization `Q` and its actual finite flat self-dual kernel;
3. the prescribed graph Neron--Severi coefficient lattice of `P` is, after finite unramified
   faithfully flat splitting at each bad prime, generated by rank-one
   square-zero divisor classes; and therefore

   ```text
       PD<N_graph^1(P)>^k = im(Sym^k N_graph^1(P))  for every k;
   ```

4. in particular every divided power of the polarization belongs to the
   ordinary integral divisor-product image.

The conclusion is compatible with base change on `S`.  If the quotient
curves and the group action are defined before a level refinement, only the
labels of the axes and graph sheets require that refinement.

## Proof

Let `pi_i:C-->C/H_i`.  Since `pi_i` commutes with the double-cover
involution, pullback and norm restrict to Pryms.  The standard identity gives

```text
                    Nm_{pi_i} pi_i^*=[m].                  (1)
```

The image of `pi_i^*` is fixed by `H_i`; its source and the connected fixed
part are elliptic, so

```text
                         pi_i^*=i_i phi_i
```

for an elliptic isogeny `phi_i`.  On etale Pryms the ambient Jacobian theta
restricts to twice the principal Prym polarization.  Applying Rosati to (1)
therefore gives

```text
                 (pi_i^*)^dagger pi_i^*=[m].               (2)
```

On the other hand, assumption 3 gives `i_i^dagger i_i=[m]`.  Substitution in
(2) yields

```text
             [m]=phi_i^dagger [m] phi_i
                =[m deg(phi_i)].
```

Thus `deg(phi_i)=1`: quotient Prym and primitive fixed axis coincide.  This
is the key recognition step.  It upgrades rational fixed-space equality to
an integral family statement without choosing periods.

The basis hypothesis makes the sum of the primitive axes an isogeny, and
assumption 4 computes its pulled polarization as `Q`; its finite flat kernel
is now an output, not input.  Assumption 5 puts that actual kernel in the
finite-etale graph regime.  Orthogonal spectral idempotents split its local
coefficient lattice into rank-one blocks over a finite unramified extension.
Every symmetric matrix unit is an integral signed sum of `vv^t` terms, even
at two, and the corresponding divisor classes square to zero.  Hence a sum
`D_1+...+D_g` has divided powers given by its squarefree exterior expansion.
The same decomposition applies to every divisor in the prescribed graph
lattice.  For a non-CM coefficient elliptic curve this is the full
Neron--Severi lattice; at CM points no equality for the additional divisor
classes is claimed.  Faithful-flat descent of
the quotient lattice, followed by localization over `Z`, proves the integral
equality in every degree.

## Cubic corollary

For the nonstandard `A_5` cubic pencil:

* `G=A_5`, the six relevant subgroups are the `D_5` normalizers of the six
  `C_5` subgroups, and `m=5`;
* Van Geemen--Yamauchi provide the quotient double covers and elliptic Pryms;
* Roulleau's connected elliptic fibrations and intersection calculation give
  the primitive axes and Gram `6I-J`;
* the two-primary graph has the irreducible `F_4` packet and the
  three-primary graph is scalar.

Thus the cubic modular-component theorem is a direct corollary.  The
equation `r^2=T` is the discriminant cover of the actual two-primary graph,
not an additional hypothesis.

## Why this is the right abstraction

The theorem explains two features that otherwise look accidental.

First, the degree of the quotient curve map and the polarization exponent of
the primitive fixed axis must agree.  Their equality forces the quotient
Prym to land *primitively* rather than merely up to isogeny.  Second, once
that primitive presentation is known, finite-etale spectral separation turns
factorial divisibility into an exterior-algebra identity.  The two steps are
independent but composable:

```text
 odd quotient-Prym geometry  +  finite-etale graph arithmetic
         = primitive axes    +  integral divided powers.
```

This predicts further examples: search for group-equivariant etale double
covers whose odd-order quotient Pryms are elliptic, whose fixed-axis
polarization exponent equals the quotient degree, and whose resulting graph
packet is spectrally etale.  It is a concrete geometric test, not a search
over arbitrary ppavs.

## Boundaries

The theorem is about ordinary integral cohomological products of divisors.
It does not construct a horizontal Chow class, and it does not say every
algebraic Hodge class is divisor-generated.  It also does not classify
colliding/non-etale graph packets; that is the p-typical C908 boundary.
