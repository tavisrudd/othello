# C1118: Full-I3 rank-four Cox descent obstruction

**Lane:** `cubic-threefolds`

## Result

Astra's finite claims are reproduced. The full type-I3 character action has
exactly one invariant rational line, generated by the primitive character
m=(-1,-1,-3,-3,3). It defines the unique rank-four subtorus of the rank-five
projective Cox torus. All sixteen restricted weights are distinct, with
orbits E3,E4,L34,Q and its twelve-element complement. No five-weight sum
can descend. Exactly 1992 of the 4368 five-subsets are unimodular over the
splitting field; the obstruction is descent.

The manuscript proposition `prop:rank-four-descent` is added after the
existing geometric consequences. It is independent of the quantum lower
bound and applies only to this Cox embedding with full I3 Galois image.
There is no claim about nonlinear sections, other embeddings, smaller
Galois images, or new priority. The unavailable ChatGPT checker was not used.

## Mystery ledger — ej + tt

The main comparison is settled: the large coordinate orbit merges into four
weight spaces at rank three, but not at rank four. Unimodularity and descent
are independent requirements, as witnessed by the 1992 geometric choices
and zero descended choices. The bounded calculation leaves no unexplained
case in its stated domain. Nonlinear methods and smaller Galois images are
outside this task, rather than exceptions asserted to be impossible.

The post-gate closeout settles rank five as well. The full T0 weights are
distinct because their rank-four restrictions are, and the checker already
verifies the same full affine permutations. The criterion would require a
stable set of six weights, impossible for orbit sizes 4 and 12. This direct
consequence was added to the manuscript: rank three is maximal for the
criterion in this full-I3 Cox representation. No new numerical search or
claim about other methods is needed. This is a task-owned strengthening,
not an incidental discovery or a newly allocated task.
