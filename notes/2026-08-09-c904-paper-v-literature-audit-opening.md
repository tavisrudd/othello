## Opening summary

Seven primary sources with recorded **full-text** reads and four sources at
**partial** depth were screened for the proposed Paper-V round trip.  The
classical outer-`S_6`, six-point, matching, Joubert, Segre, conference, and
conic-hexagon infrastructure is heavily occupied.  The plausible new claim is
therefore not the existence of those models or an abstract dictionary among
them.  It is, if true, an exact marked compatibility theorem whose maps respect
the paper-specific involutions and whose forward and inverse composites are
proved at a declared retained-output level.

The audit also catches a load-bearing representation issue.  For the
degree-six action of `A_5`, the augmentation module is the irreducible
five-dimensional representation, but

\[
 \dim (\operatorname{Sym}^3 V^*)^{A_5}=2.
\]

This follows directly from the character `(5,1,-1,0,0)` and the
symmetric-cube character formula.  It is an audit calculation, not a claim
attributed to a source.  Paper V may not identify two cubics merely because
both are nonzero and `A_5`-invariant; it needs an exact selector and comparison.
