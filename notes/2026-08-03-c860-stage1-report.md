## Stage 1e — split `ProjectiveCap.Sym2ConicBridge` (added; not in the charter)

**Deviation, and why it was necessary.** With stages 1a–1d exactly as written,
`CapGame.BuildGame` leaves only 20 of the 51 exposed gates. It is retained by the other 31 — every
Clebsch, arcs, and alternate-orbit-repair gate — because `ProjectiveCap.Sym2ConicBridge` has a
game section of its own: `sym2Collineation_isP_transport`, `sym2Collineation_win_transport`,
`onconic_value_bridge`, and `onconic_win_bridge` state `FiniteBuildGame.IsP` and
`FiniteBuildGame.Win` transport along the symmetric-square collineation. The charter's section 1.1
inventory lists only the fourteen geometric `Sym2Bridge` declarations that `RelativeConicArcs` uses
and never records this fourth blocker; its section 2 names three.

The remedy is the same shape as the other three splits and stays inside the authorized envelope:
relocation only, cap-owned file, four theorems with no consumer anywhere in the repository. Without
it stage 1 delivers roughly a third of what the charter's section 3.5 promises. It was executed and
is recorded here as a deviation rather than silently folded into 1c or 1d.

**Files touched**

- new `lean/ProjectiveCap/Sym2ConicBridgeGame.lean`
- `lean/ProjectiveCap/Sym2ConicBridge.lean` — the four game theorems removed, the transitional
  `import ProjectiveCap.ProjectiveCapGame` from stage 1b dropped, module header rewritten

**Declarations moved**, namespace `ProjectiveCap.Sym2Bridge` unchanged:
`sym2Collineation_isP_transport`, `sym2Collineation_win_transport`, `onconic_value_bridge`,
`onconic_win_bridge`. `sym2Collineation_image_veronesePoint` is pure incidence geometry with no game
content and stays in `Sym2ConicBridge`, where the two orbit bridges import it.

**Prose repairs required by the review gate.** `lean/AGENTS.md` forbids task identifiers, internal
record references, and work-history prose anywhere in the referee-facing artifact, and applies that
standard to the whole of any module a change touches, without grandfathering. The old
`Sym2ConicBridge` header carried a task identifier in its title and twice more in section prose,
cited two internal dated notes as authority for its mathematics, and narrated a superseded earlier
restriction as a "sound-but-unnecessary downgrade". All of that is gone: the header now states the
objects, the three polynomial identities the module rests on, the characteristic-two caveat, and the
terminal geometric statement, and the moved docstrings state their content without the identifier or
the note's internal lemma numbering. No statement or proof changed.

cores 20-23, trace-only aggregate gate: `ProjectiveCap.Sym2ConicBridge` and
