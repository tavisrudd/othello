## Audit verdict

The old section established only a conditional statement: the secant-capacity argument gave
`q<=14`, after which an `A5` rationality filter was supposed to isolate the icosahedrally symmetric
cases. Its q=9 step asserted without proof that the relevant `A5 < PGL(2,9)` was unique up to
conjugacy, while the cited checker verified only one witness orbit. The abstract and introduction
also described the conditional conclusion more broadly than the proof warranted.

Repairing that subgroup-classification route would have produced a weaker theorem while leaving
the natural reader question—whether a nonsymmetric counterexample exists—open. C170 instead
exhausts every six-arc projective class in the elementary finite range and proves:

> If `U(A)=C(F_q)` for a nonsingular conic, then `q=11`; at `q=11`, rigidity identifies `A` with
> the Clebsch hexagon.

This needs no stabilizer hypothesis. The old Dickson-filter proof and q=9 conjugacy assertion were
removed from the manuscript. The q=19 icosahedral construction remains only as a concrete account
of how the symmetric recipe fails beyond the capacity bound; it is not evidence for the theorem.
