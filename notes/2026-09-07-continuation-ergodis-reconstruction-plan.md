## C1112 — ergodis: autonomous representation discovery and repair

After C1111; consume C1109's hand-written algorithm as a control when ready. Use an existing
proposer with a bounded language of neighbourhoods, intersections, partitions and coordinate
expressions. Propose representations with executable readouts; check independently and return
distinguishing counterexamples. Read relevant CEGIS/strong-preservation/library-learning
sources before claiming a new method. Candidate operations, supplied algebraic rules, and
reference-oracle access must be explicit; tests on held-out orders are not universal proofs.

Acceptance: an unattended campaign discovers a useful representation and repairs or rejects
an inadequate one, with exact agreement and measured benefit on held-out instances. Compare
direct graph operations and a hand-written control, accounting for compilation, repeated
queries, memory and verification. Report a bounded negative honestly if discovery or benefit
fails; do not substitute a scripted recognizer for autonomous discovery.
Report: `notes/2026-09-07-c1112-autonomous-representation-discovery.md`.
