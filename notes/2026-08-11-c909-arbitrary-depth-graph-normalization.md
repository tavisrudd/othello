# C909 — arbitrary-depth finite-etale graph normalization

Date: 2026-08-11  
Status: human local proof closing the block-respecting arbitrary-depth graph
case; no manuscript, PDF, mirror, Lean, or certificate change

## Verdict

The normalization gate closes. For a block-respecting finite-etale graph over
an orthogonal Jordan polarization with arbitrary primary depths, the complete
split local Neron--Severi lattice is a symmetric matrix-of-ideals lattice. Its
cross depths always dominate both adjacent diagonal depths, hence satisfy the
tropical rank-one criterion. Therefore the **full** marked graph
Neron--Severi divided-power envelope equals its ordinary divisor-product image
in every cohomological degree.

## 1. Exact graph-basis calculation

Let `O` be an unramified DVR, let

\[
       P=\operatorname{diag}(p^{a_1},\ldots,p^{a_g}),
\]

and use the graph basis

\[
 C_T=\begin{pmatrix}P^{-1}&P^{-1}T\\0&I\end{pmatrix}.
\]

For a symmetric bilinear coefficient `A`, write

\[
 \Omega_A=\begin{pmatrix}0&A\\-A&0\end{pmatrix}.
\]

Direct multiplication gives

\[
 C_T\Omega_A C_T^t=
 \begin{pmatrix}
 P^{-1}(AT^t-TA)P^{-1}&P^{-1}A\\
 -AP^{-1}&0
 \end{pmatrix}.
 \tag{1}
\]

Thus graph integrality is exactly

\[
 P^{-1}A,\quad AP^{-1},\quad
 P^{-1}(AT^t-TA)P^{-1}\in\operatorname{Mat}_g(O).
 \tag{2}
\]

For a nonorthonormal unimodular coefficient form `B`, take `A` to be the
symmetric bilinear coefficient after applying the unimodular Gram
identification. This changes no valuation and retains (1)--(2). It also makes
the self-adjoint convention explicit rather than confusing symmetric matrices
with raw coefficient endomorphisms.

## 2. Etale splitting and coefficient ideals

Assume the graph respects the orthogonal Jordan blocks and its literal slope
algebra at every truncated depth is finite etale. After one finite unramified
faithfully flat extension, primitive self-adjoint idempotents split each block
orthogonally. On a resulting scalar eigenblock of depth `a_i`, choose an
integral lift

\[
                       T_i=t_iI+p^{a_i}S_i.
 \tag{3}
\]

For a coefficient slot `A_ij`, put `e=v_p(A_ij)`. The two off-diagonal
conditions in (2) first give

\[
                       e\ge\max(a_i,a_j).
 \tag{4}
\]

The commutator is

\[
 A_{ij}T_j^t-T_iA_{ij}
 =(t_j-t_i)A_{ij}
 +p^{a_j}A_{ij}S_j^t-p^{a_i}S_iA_{ij}.
 \tag{5}
\]

After multiplication by `p^{-a_i}` on the left and `p^{-a_j}` on the right,
the two error terms are integral by (4): their valuations are respectively
`e-a_i` and `e-a_j`. Hence they impose no coupling. The scalar term imposes
only

\[
 e\ge a_i+a_j-v_p(t_j-t_i),
 \tag{6}
\]

with the right side interpreted as no extra condition if the scalar
difference vanishes. Consequently the complete allowed coefficient slot is
the principal ideal

\[
 I_{ij}=p^{e_{ij}}O,qquad
 e_{ij}=\max\bigl(a_i,a_j,
                  a_i+a_j-v_p(t_j-t_i)\bigr),
 \tag{7}
\]

again omitting the last entry when the difference is zero. Diagonal slots
have `e_ii=a_i`. Thus the complete graph Neron--Severi coefficient lattice is
a symmetric matrix-of-ideals lattice; equal eigenvalues cause no residual
normalization problem.

Unit blocks are simply `a_i=0`. Formula (7) covers unit/primary and
primary/primary cross terms uniformly. Nothing in (1)--(7) divides by two.

## 3. Tropical criterion and full divided powers

By (4),

\[
 2e_{ij}\ge a_i+a_j,
 \qquad\text{equivalently}\qquad
 I_{ij}^{,2}\subset I_{ii}I_{jj}.
 \tag{8}
\]

The tropical rank-one classification therefore applies: the complete split
graph Neron--Severi lattice is generated integrally by admissible rank-one
coefficient forms. Their divisor classes pull back to decomposable alternating
two-forms and hence square to zero integrally. For every divisor `D`,

\[
       \frac{D^k}{k!}
        =\sum_{r_1<\cdots<r_k}R_{r_1}\cdots R_{r_k}
\]

is an ordinary integral product expression.

The graph congruence kernel and the image of each finite multilinear product
map commute with the finite free unramified splitting extension. Faithful
flatness descends membership without a trace multiplier. Localizing at every
prime gives the global statement:

> **Arbitrary-depth finite-etale graph PD theorem.** For a marked
> block-respecting polarized elliptic-power graph whose literal primary slope
> algebras are finite etale, the divided-power envelope generated by the full
> Neron--Severi lattice equals its ordinary divisor-product image in every
> integral cohomological degree.

## 4. Scope

This theorem is presentation-intrinsic under changes of transverse elliptic
ruling, not intrinsic to a bare ppav under arbitrary symplectic charts. It is
cohomological, not Chow-theoretic. It neither says all Hodge classes are
divisor products nor treats non-etale/nilpotent carry. The finite-etale and
block-respecting hypotheses are load-bearing: without scalar idempotent
blocks, (5) need not decouple slotwise.
