# C907 — the point `(1,3)` standard wall has only an index-one or index-two obstruction

Date: 2026-08-13

Status: exact local-index reduction.  Every smooth projective standard
`(1,3)` flip over a point has one integer obstruction to the Gu--Yu--Yu
simple-VGIT criterion.  Its local Cartier restriction index is either one or
two.  Index one supplies the primitive `O(1)` wall polarization and the
Gu--Yu--Yu theorem.  Index two is genuinely nonprimitive: the canonical
bundle supplies only the second Veronese, and the expected master carries a
`mu_2` stabilizer.  Thus the uncovered point row is a precise orbifold/index-
two case, not an arbitrary global-gluing problem.

## 1. The restriction index

Let `X_- dashrightarrow X_+` be a smooth projective standard flip of type
`(1,3)` over a point.  Its two small contractions have exceptional loci

\[
 P_-=P^3,\quad N_{P_-/X_-}=O_{P^3}(-1)^{\oplus2},
 \qquad
 P_+=P^1,\quad N_{P_+/X_+}=O_{P^1}(-1)^{\oplus4}.                  \tag{1}
\]

Work on a sufficiently small Zariski neighbourhood of the exceptional
fibre.  Define the negative-side Cartier restriction index by

\[
 \operatorname{im}\bigl(\operatorname{Pic}(X_-)
       \longrightarrow\operatorname{Pic}(P^3)=\mathbf Z\bigr)
       =d\mathbf Z.                                                 \tag{2}
\]

Adjunction gives

\[
 K_{X_-}|_{P^3}=O(-4)\otimes\det(O(-1)^{\oplus2})^{-1}=O(-2),       \tag{3}
\]

so the image in (2) contains `2`.  It follows immediately that

\[
 \boxed{d\in\{1,2\}.}                                               \tag{4}
\]

The same index occurs on the positive side.  Let
`pi_-:tilde X->X_-` and `pi_+:tilde X->X_+` be the common blowup.  Its
exceptional divisor is

\[
 E=P^1\times P^3,\qquad O_E(E)=O(-1,-1).                            \tag{5}
\]

If a Cartier strict transform `D_-` has restriction `O_{P^3}(-a)`, its
corresponding divisor `D_+` satisfies

\[
 \pi_-^*D_-=\pi_+^*D_+ + aE.                                      \tag{6}
\]

Restricting (6) to `E` shows `D_+|_{P^1}=O(a)`.  Hence the positive and
negative restriction images have the same generator `d`.  In particular,
adjunction on the positive side gives the matching class

\[
 K_{X_+}|_{P^1}=O(-2)\otimes\det(O(-1)^{\oplus4})^{-1}=O(2).        \tag{7}
\]

## 2. Index one is simple VGIT

Assume `d=1`.  Choose a local Cartier divisor `D_-` restricting to `O(-1)`
on `P^3`; its strict transform `D_+` restricts to `O(1)` on `P^1` by (6).
Taking the closure of its Weil pushforward across the isolated wall point
gives the `D` used by the local section algebra.

On every affine wall chart, the negative and positive section algebras are
generated in degrees `-1` and `+1`.  Their exceptional fibres use the
normally generated rings of `O_{P^3}(1)` and `O_{P^1}(1)`; higher cohomology
of the positive powers vanishes, so formal functions, base change, and
Nakayama upgrade fibrewise generation to the local contraction algebra.

The ideal generated by all nonzero degrees is the ideal of the wall point.
The common blowup identity

\[
 \pi_-^*O(-D_- )\otimes\pi_+^*O(D_+ )\cong O_{\widetilde X}(-E)     \tag{8}
\]

identifies its products with the Rees ideal; the quotient is the residue
field of the point and is prime.  Thus all three hypotheses of Gu--Yu--Yu
Lemma 6.9 hold.  Their Corollary 6.12 supplies the full pairing-compatible
QDM decomposition.  The C907 simple-wall theorem then supplies the rank
identity in the wall's fixed-sector receiver.

This includes the split local model and the finite-disconnected six-point
regression already used in the Gold campaign.

## 3. Index two is the exact uncovered case

Assume `d=2`.  No algebraic Cartier class on the contraction neighbourhood
restricts to `O(1)`.  The canonical divisor is now the primitive available
class in the local Cartier lattice, but its fibre ring is the second
Veronese

\[
 \bigoplus_{m\ge0}H^0(P^3,O(2m))                                  \tag{9}
\]

on one side and the matching second Veronese of `P^1` on the other.  Giving
the canonical class grading degree one therefore produces weight two in the
primitive normal coordinate.  Taking a square root restores `O(1)` only on
a `mu_2` root stack.  This is exactly why Gu--Yu--Yu's smooth semi-free
criterion does not apply: their weight-`(+/-1)` master has been replaced by
an index-two/finite-stabilizer wall.

No contradiction to smoothness of `X_+` or `X_-` follows.  The obstruction
lives in algebraization of the primitive formal/local Picard class across the
singular wall point, not in the normal bundles (1), which are already
standard.

Consequently the remaining point-row source target is precise:

> prove the Gamma-rank comparison for the index-two root-stack wall, or prove
> that AKMW's pi-nonsingular elementary walls always have `d=1`.

The first route is an orbifold extension of Gu--Yu--Yu; the second is a
unit-circuit theorem in toroidal factorization.

## 4. Boundary

This note does not close adjacent-receiver coherence.  Even in index one,
the Gu--Yu--Yu/Shen--Shoemaker rank theorem is wall-local.  The index
dichotomy removes only the remaining **geometric coverage** ambiguity for
point `(1,3)` walls.

Nor does the formal existence of `O(1)` on the completion force `d=1`.
Formal Picard classes need not algebraize on a Zariski neighbourhood; the
failure is exactly what the index in (2) records.  Treating formal extension
as algebraic descent would erase the only genuine case.

- **TT:** `K|_{P^3}=O(-2)` proves only `d|2`; it does not manufacture an
