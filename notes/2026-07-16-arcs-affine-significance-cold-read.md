# Cold read: affine-demand revision

An independent context-free read of the rebuilt prescribed-conic manuscript
found that the new line-hole specialization genuinely answers the external-demand
objection without overstating the literature.  In particular:

- the complete-affine interpretation and its corrected defect inequality are
  mathematically accurate;
- the equality criterion follows from the general prescribed-hole identity and
  the fact that every secant meets the ideal line once;
- the hyperfocused-arc discussion is appropriately divided between the
  secret-sharing motivation and the existence of off-line-complete examples;
- the early MDS paragraph correctly speaks about projective syndrome directions
  whose cosets have distance three, rather than identifying all such directions
  with deep holes;
- the material remains suitable for a specialist finite-geometry/coding venue.

The read found one stale introduction cross-reference to the Clebsch witness.
The manuscript now uses the label `sec:clebsch-witness`, so the reference follows
the reordered section automatically.  Its remaining wording suggestions were
also adopted: call Corollary `cor:affine` an affine defect inequality with an
exact equality criterion, split the two historical citations by what each
supports, and echo the affine specialization in the conclusion.
