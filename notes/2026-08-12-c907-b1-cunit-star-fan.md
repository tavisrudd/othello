# C907 $B=1$, $C\notin\{0,1,\infty\}$ translated-star certificate

**Lane:** `clebsch`

**Status:** exact generic one-star calculation for arbitrary toric $y$
weights; no global fan claim.

## Saturated translated graph

Put $u=1-B$.  After a finite ramified base change clearing rational weights,
use the torus chart

\[
 y_i=\delta^{-p_i}x_i,\qquad
 u=\delta^\beta b,\qquad C=c,\qquad \beta>0,
\tag{1}
\]

where $x_i,b,c,1-c$ are units, and hence

\[
 B=1-\delta^\beta b
\]

is also a unit at the central divisor.  Write $p=\sum_i p_i$ and

\[
 m=\max\{p_1,p_2,p_3,-p,2-\beta\}.
\tag{2}
\]

All exponents in

\[
\begin{aligned}
G=Xc(1-\delta^\beta b)\bigg(&\delta^mL-
 \sum_i\delta^{m-p_i}x_i
 -\delta^{m+p}\frac{Q}{Xc(1-\delta^\beta b)}\\
 &-\delta^{m+\beta-2}b(1-c)\bigg)=0
\end{aligned}
\tag{3}
\]

are nonnegative.  On the dense original torus the chart map and inverse are

\[
(\delta,x,b,c,L)\longmapsto
(\delta,\delta^{-p_i}x_i,1-\delta^\beta b,c,L),
\qquad
(x_i,b,c)=(\delta^{p_i}y_i,\delta^{-\beta}(1-B),C),
\tag{4}
\]

and (3) is exactly $\delta^mXcB(L-F_\delta)=0$.  In the localized chart,
the pulled-back nonvanishing condition $\delta YBC\ne0$ is exactly
$\delta\ne0$, because $X,c,B$ are units.  Since some coefficient in (3) has
order zero by (2), $\delta\nmid G$, whence the exact graph saturation is

\[
 (G):\delta^\infty=(G),
\tag{5}
\]

This is the graph closure used below; no component from $B=0$ or a coordinate
face is retained in this translated torus chart.

## Positive-order central faces

Suppose $m>0$.  The reduced central graph loses $L$ and is

\[
 H=\sum_{p_i=m}x_i+
 \mathbf1_{-p=m}P+
 \mathbf1_{2-\beta=m}R=0,
 \qquad
 P=\frac Q{Xc},\quad R=b(1-c).
\tag{6}
\]

Every displayed term is a torus unit.  Thus a singleton support is empty. On
every other support, the tangent-logarithmic critical ideal of the reduced
graph is the unit ideal after localization: if $R$ occurs,
$D_bH=R$ is a unit; if $R$ does not occur but $P$ does,
$D_cH=-P$ is a unit; and if neither occurs, an occurring $x_i$ has
$D_{x_i}H=x_i$ a unit.  The central graph is therefore smooth, reduced, and
normal.  The total graph is smooth along it, $L$ is a free tangent coordinate,
and the relative tangent-Fitting ideal is `(1)`.

So every nonempty $m>0$ face is **free**.  This is the existing
five-support **simplex** configuration, rather than a new circuit: the
translated leading term $R=b(1-c)$ itself has a unit $b$ derivative.  It is
not the reciprocal--linear mixed case of the $B=\infty$ chart.

## The index-zero face, where $L$ survives

The inequality $\max\{p_i,-p\}\ge0$ is an equality only when
$p_1=p_2=p_3=0$.  Hence $m=0$ occurs exactly for

\[
 p_1=p_2=p_3=0,\qquad \beta\ge2.
\tag{7}
\]

It must not be folded into the $L$-free calculation.  On this reduced central
graph, put $P=Q/(Xc)$ and $R=b(1-c)$; its equation is

\[
 E_>:=L-x_1-x_2-x_3-P=0\quad(\beta>2),
\tag{8}
\]

or

\[
 E_=:=L-x_1-x_2-x_3-P-R=0\quad(\beta=2).
\tag{9}
\]

These are smooth expected-codimension hypersurfaces since $\partial_LE$ is a
unit.  Their exact relative tangent-Fitting ideals are generated by $E$ and
its logarithmic derivatives in $x_1,x_2,x_3,b,c$, then saturated by the chart
units.  For (8), $D_cE_>=P$ is a unit; for (9), $D_bE_=-R$ is a unit.
Consequently both index-zero faces are **free** even though $L$ is not a free
coordinate.  They have no residual support because $C=1$ is excluded.

## Finite replay

For $m>0$ there are five possible leading terms

\[
x_1,x_2,x_3,Q/(Xc),b(1-c).
\]

The Singular replay enumerates all 31 nonempty masks, saturates the central
graph by $Xbc(1-c)$ before forming its tangent ideal, and reports 5 empty,
26 free, and 0 hold masks.  It then tests the two $m=0$ Fitting ideals (8)
and (9) separately; both are free.


The direct derivative argument establishes smoothness/reduction before the
Fitting conclusion; the replay is a check of the same saturated graph data,
not an initial ideal of the open critical scheme.

## Symmetry and boundary

Interchanging $(B,b)$ and $(C,c)$ gives the $C=1$, $B$-generic star with the
same saturation, support theorem, and index-zero split.  This calculation
does not include $C=0$, $C=1$, or $C=\infty$; those are respectively the
translated $(1,0)$ seam, the residual/intersecting-one locus, and the
translated/infinity seam.  It does not prove a common fan or collar theorem.

## EJ/TT and mystery ledger

- **EJ:** arbitrary $y$ weights again reduce to five leading supports, and
  the translated generic-one star uses the known simplex lemma rather than a
  new discriminant circuit.
- **TT:** $m=0$ is a real exceptional face: $L$ survives and must be tested
  by its own Fitting ideal; it is nevertheless submersive by a unit tangent
  derivative.
- **Settled:** the generic $B=1$ star and its $B,C$ swap are empty/free.
- **Open:** the $(1,0)$ and $(0,1)$ seams with noncompact $y$, the
  $B=C=1$ intersection, infinity seams, common fan, and collars remain.
