# C895 Steinberg-source Hom calculation

**Date:** 2026-08-09  
**Task:** C895  
**Status:** positive R2 hard-gate calculation; specialist challenge pending

## Claim

Let `q=p^e` with `p` odd, let `k` be algebraically closed of characteristic
`p`, and let `\mathbf G=SL_2(k)`.  Put

\[
 \operatorname{St}=L(q-1),\qquad
 Y_1=L(1)\otimes L(1)^{(e)}.
\]

If `M` is a rational `\mathbf G`-module with no weight above `2q-6`, then

\[
 \operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbf G}(\operatorname{St}\otimes Y_1,M)=0.
\]

In Paper II, `M=Sym^2 F` and the top weight of `F` is `q-3`, so this applies.

## Source structure

Doty--Henke's bottom-alcove tensor rule gives

\[
 L(p-1)\otimes L(1)=T(p).
\]

Steinberg's tensor-product theorem and Doty--Henke's twisted tensor
description of indecomposable tilting modules then give

\[
 \operatorname{St}\otimes L(1)
 \simeq
 T(p)\otimes\bigotimes_{j=1}^{e-1}T(p-1)^{(j)}
 \simeq T(q).
\]

Tensoring by `L(1)^(e)=T(1)^(e)` appends the final canonical tilting digit:

\[
 \operatorname{St}\otimes L(1)\otimes L(1)^{(e)}
 \simeq T(2q).                                           \tag{S1}
\]

The canonical tilting digits of `2q` are

\[
 (p,p-1,\ldots,p-1,1),
\]

with the middle string occupying positions `1` through `e-1`.  This also
covers `e=1`, when the string is empty.

## Weyl filtration

The small tilting module `T(p)` has one Weyl section `Delta(p)` and one
Weyl section `Delta(p-2)`.  All other factors in (S1) are simple tilting
modules.  Equivalently, the formal character is

\[
 \operatorname{ch}T(2q)
 =\operatorname{ch}\Delta(2q)
  +\operatorname{ch}\Delta(2q-2).                       \tag{S2}
\]

Here is a direct character check.  Write `chi_n` for the Weyl character of
highest weight `n`.  Then

\[
 \operatorname{ch}T(p)=\chi_p+\chi_{p-2}
 =\chi_{p-1}\chi_1,
\]

the product of the Frobenius-twisted characters `chi_(p-1)` is
`chi_(q-1)`, and multiplication by the final
`chi_1(z^q)=z^q+z^{-q}` gives

\[
 (\chi_q+\chi_{q-2})(z^q+z^{-q})
 =\chi_{2q}+\chi_{2q-2}.
\]

Since `T(2q)` has a Weyl filtration and Weyl characters are triangularly
independent, (S2) says that its Weyl sections are exactly
`Delta(2q)` and `Delta(2q-2)`, each once.

## Hom vanishing

For `n>2q-6`, every map `Delta(n)->M` is zero: a Weyl module is generated by
its highest-weight vector, while `M` has no vector of weight `n`.  Hence

\[
 \operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbf G}(\Delta(2q),M)=0,
 \qquad
 \operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbf G}(\Delta(2q-2),M)=0.
\]

Apply `Hom_G(-,M)` to a two-section Weyl filtration of `T(2q)`.  The two
outer Hom spaces vanish, so

\[
 \operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbf G}(T(2q),M)=0.
\]

Together with (S1), this proves the claim.  This repairs the cold referee's
specific objection: the argument uses the complete source filtration, not
only its simple head `L(2q-2)`.
