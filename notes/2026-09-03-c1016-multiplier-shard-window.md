# C1016 — the multiplier-assumed program collapses to one order-14 subgroup

**Lane**: `complete-ports` · **Task**: C1016 · 2026-09-03.

Acting on move A of the Fable reduction review
(`2026-09-03-c1016-fable-reduction-review.md`): one uniform necessary test,
applied to every unit of `Z/522` at every level, replaces the four bespoke
multiplier-sector arguments and decides 148 of the 167 nontrivial units in
twelve seconds of one core.

## The test

The searched shape is the bordered supplementary difference set
`4-{522; 260, 261, 261, 261; 520}`, that is four subsets of `Z/522` with
`sum_i X_i X_i^{-1} = 523 + 520 T` in the integral group ring. Fix a level
`d | 522`, let `H` be the subgroup of order `f = 522/d`, and write
`B_{i,c} = |X_i ∩ (c + H)|`. Summing the group-ring identity over the cosets
gives the exact level identity

    sum_i sum_{c in Z/d} B_{i,c}^2 = 1043 + 520 (f - 1).

A shard assumes each `X_i` is invariant under the subgroup generated by a unit,
so each `X_i` is a union of orbits. An orbit maps onto an orbit of the same
unit acting on `Z/d` and meets every coset there equally often, contributing
`|O| / |P|` layers to each coset of its projected orbit `P`. A set placing
`L_P` layers over `P` therefore has size `sum_P |P| L_P` and level energy
`sum_P |P| L_P^2`. The energies achievable at a given size are an exact
subset-sum over projected orbits, and the shard is empty at that level unless
the level target is one achievable energy at size 260 plus three at size 261.

The condition is necessary and never sufficient: an exclusion is a theorem, a
survivor is only a search target.

## Result

Of the 167 nontrivial units, 148 are emptied at some level, with 744 recorded
exclusions. Every unit of order 3, 6, 12, 21, 28, 42 and 84 dies, including all
120 units of order 21, 28, 42 and 84 that the banked program had never
examined. The surviving cyclic subgroups are the order-14 subgroup generated by
53, its order-7 subgroup generated by 181, two order-4 subgroups, and three of
order 2.

The multiplier-assumed program is therefore reduced to the subgroup generated
by 53 together with its subgroups and groups of order at most four. The banked
`g = 53` work already exhausted invariance under the full order-14 group, so
what remains genuinely open in this program is invariance under its order-7
subgroup and under the small groups, where the assumption is weak and the
search space correspondingly large.

## Agreement with the banked sectors, and one new closure

- `g = 41` is emptied at level 174 with every four-block energy at least 2665
  against a target of 2083. This reproduces the banked exclusion.
- `g = 91` is emptied at level 18. The energy interval there contains the
  target, so the exclusion comes from the exact four-block subset-sum, not from
  the interval — the counting analogue of the banked impossible equation
  `13 n27 + 15 n29 = 34`.
- `g = 133` is emptied at level 174. This sector was still open and was being
  worked by exact interior joins over millions of roots; the subgroup generated
  by 133 is distinct from the one generated by 41, so this is a new closure by
  a counting argument that costs milliseconds.
- `g = 53` survives every level, consistent with that sector having needed the
  sparse-defect coordinate change and an exhaustive join.

An independent Python implementation, written before the Rust kernel and
sharing none of its code, agrees on all seven spot checks used to validate the
sweep, including the two banked exclusions and the new `g = 133` one.

Two defects were found and fixed during the build, both by invariants rather
than by inspection. The first energy table was sized for `f <= 29`, so every
level below 18 overflowed and reported a false exclusion; the level-one
identity test, where the single coset count is `k_i` itself and the target
271,963 must always be attained, now catches exactly that. The projection step
originally read the multiplier from a field set by a different method, which a
direct unit test exposed; the multiplier is now passed explicitly.
