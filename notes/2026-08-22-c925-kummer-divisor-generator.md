# C925 cubic Kummer divisor and calibration boundary

**Lane:** `cubic-threefolds`

## Result

The \(m=2\) correction problem is local and marker-specific.  At the generic
numerical-reduced even base, point, curve, and surface centers have no C924
factors with

\[
  \operatorname{rank}=2,\qquad N\ne0,\qquad
  \delta^\sharp=4/9.
\]

The only unresolved correction is the single outer factor belonging to a
connected codimension-two threefold center.  One must exclude an exact
three-cycle of factors with this exact marker.  Replacing \(4/9\) by the
qualitative condition \(\delta^\sharp\ne0\) is invalid: the strict upper
orientation has discriminant \(4\).

## Source normalization

Iritani's arXiv:2307.13555v3 uses normal weights \(-1\) of rank \(r\)
and \(+1\) of rank one at the fixed center.  Formula (4.9) gives
\(\lambda_j=e^{-2\pi i(j+r/2)/(r-1)}q^{1/(r-1)}\).  Setting \(r=2\)
in (5.19), Theorem 5.18(6), (5.40), and (5.43) gives

\[
  \lambda_0=q,\qquad
  \varsigma_0=-q+\pi i\rho_Z+O(q^{-1}),\qquad
  Q_Z^d\longmapsto Q^{\iota_*d}q^{-\rho_Z\cdot d}.
\]

For \(D_L=\xi Q\partial_Q+a q\partial_q\), the static center charge is
\(\beta=i^*\xi-a\rho_Z\), while the pulled-back Higgs operator is

\[
  C_{D_L}=(\beta+D_L\varsigma_0)\star.
\]

In a parameter-dependent marked frame \(G\), put
\(B_{D_L}=G^{-1}D_LG\).  The block equation is gauge-covariant:

\[
 D_LU'+[B_{D_L},U']
   =C'_{D_L}+[C'_{D_L},\mu'].
\]

Thus the Laurent comparison supplies a genuine pulled-back logarithmic QDM
direction, but not a native flat occurrence frame.  The full Euler field is
preserved; a selected product-loop direction need not be a pure center Euler
coordinate.

## Finite divisor bridge

Three algebraic implications survive this frame issue.

1. Let \(\sigma^3=1\) and let \(D\) commute with \(\sigma\).  If every
   vector in \(\ker D\) is \(\sigma\)-fixed, then
   \(\sigma(Dx)=Dx\) if and only if \(\sigma x=x\).  Applied to the Euler
   derivative on a Laurent or Puiseux field, differentiation preserves the
   exact cubic fixedness fingerprint.
2. If a primary projector \(P\) commutes with multiplication by \(C\), then
   the trace of \(P[C,\mu]P\) is zero.  Taking traces in the block flatness
   equation identifies the scalar divisor eigenvalue with the derivative of
   the Euler eigenvalue only after the moving-projector and scalar-block
   identifications have been supplied.
3. A moved point in an order-three orbit has three distinct iterates.  Once
   the divisor scalar is an element of one connected cubic conjugacy orbit,
   its three values therefore generate the split cubic algebra: the evaluation
   matrix on \(1,d,d^2\) is Vandermonde and invertible.

The Lean module
`Comparison.KummerDivisorGenerator` proves these implications without any
geometric occurrence or QDM assumption.

## Mandatory red tests

The first red test is geometric.  For a curve \(C_g\) of genus at least two,

\[
 QH^{\mathrm{ev}}(\mathbb P^2\times C_g)
   =K[x,e]/(x^3-q,e^2)
\]

has an exact cubic orbit of three rank-two Jordan blocks under the
\(\mathcal O(1)\) Novikov loop.  Their modified-residue discriminant is zero.
Hence no theorem may exclude rank-two cubic orbits without using the exact
marker.

The second red test shows that the divisor bridge is not the missing theorem.
In the basis \((1,e,x,xe,x^2,x^2e)\), let

\[
 R=I+E_{1,3}-E_{2,4}.
\]

This is a Poincare isometry and fixes \(1,e,x\).  Conjugating the dual-cubic
multiplication algebra by \(R\) preserves its unit, its two divisor vectors,
and its three rank-two local factors.  The normalized selected residue is

\[
  \begin{pmatrix}-1/6&2\\0&-5/6\end{pmatrix},
\]

whose discriminant is \(4/9\).  Thus a cubic quotient generated by the
actual divisor, together with the unit and the pairing, still permits the
forbidden marker at a generic fibre.  A flat-coordinate conformal check must
transport both the multiplication operators and their input indices; omitting
the latter gives a false obstruction.

## Exact remaining provider

For each actual codimension-two threefold-center occurrence, construct the
chosen loop on the exact-\(4/9\) marked factors and prove that none has period
three.  A sufficient, more structured statement is:

- the total marked carrier and its elementary modification descend to a
  saturated module over the center's native effective Novikov lattice;
- the descent is compatible with the standard cohomological grading,
  Poincare pairing, divisor equation, and cup-product limit at the large-
  radius point; and
- the transported loop and the Iritani occurrence map preserve this
  calibration.

After the moving-projector, scalar-block, and connected-conjugacy adapters are
supplied, the Kummer derivative and Vandermonde lemmas identify the cubic
divisor quotient.  The effective large-radius theorem must then exclude the
parabolic shear and any transverse bulk deformation with the same generic
data.  Iritani's Laurent comparison, KKPYY's generic atom splitting, and the
existing C924 projector do not supply this descent.

Even this formulation leaves a finite classification problem.  A connected
rank-six threefold has a two-dimensional divisor space, but its classical cup
product is governed by an arbitrary binary cubic intersection form.  The
special order \(K[x,e]/(x^3,e^2)\) corresponds to one such form; self-duality
and the grading alone do not select it.  A proof must either show that the
dangerous Kummer carrier has this special fibre or classify every graded
self-dual Frobenius order with the same generic cubic dual-number algebra and
check the modified residue in each case.

The first stratification is elementary.  Write

\[
 F(u,v)=a u^3+3b u^2v+3c uv^2+d v^3
\]

for the intersection cubic and take the chosen divisor to be \(u\).  With the
dual bases in degrees two and four, multiplication by \(u\) has the following
Jordan types.  The determinant in the first two rows is that of the matrix
\(\bigl(\begin{smallmatrix}a&b\\ b&c\end{smallmatrix}\bigr)\).

| coefficients | Jordan type |
| --- | --- |
| \(a\ne0,\ ac-b^2\ne0\) | \(J_4\oplus J_2\) |
| \(a\ne0,\ ac-b^2=0\) | \(J_4\oplus J_1\oplus J_1\) |
| \(a=0,\ b\ne0\) | \(J_3\oplus J_3\) |
| \(a=b=0,\ c\ne0\) | \(J_2\oplus J_2\oplus J_2\) |
| \(a=b=c=0\) | \(J_2\oplus J_2\oplus J_1\oplus J_1\) |

For example, in the \(J_2^{\oplus3}\) row the chains are represented by
\(1\mapsto u\), \(v\mapsto c\,v^*\), and
\(u^*\mapsto[\mathrm{pt}]\).  This table identifies a finite next target:
classify the effective self-dual orders in these binary-cubic strata and run
the residue recurrence on each.  It does not by itself show that the generic
marked Kummer carrier extends to any one row.

This boundary is narrower than a global packet or Stokes theorem.  It is also
stronger than generic Frobenius-algebra or divisor data, as the two red tests
show.
