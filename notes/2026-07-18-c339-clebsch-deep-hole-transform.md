## The theorem

Let `F=F_q` contain an element `tau` with `tau^2=tau+1`, and reduce the standard fifteen
projectivized `H3` mirrors

```text
(1,0,0), (0,1,0), (0,0,1),
cyclic permutations of (1, +/-tau, +/-(tau-1)).
```

Assume the reduction preserves the characteristic-zero singular-point and special-line incidence
ledger certified below.  Write `A_q` for the union of the fifteen mirror lines and
`B_q=PG(2,q)\A_q`.  Then

```text
|B_q| = (q-5)(q-9).
```

For a nonmirror line `L`, put

```text
delta(L) = sum_{P in L singular} (m(P)-1),
```

where `m(P)` is the number of mirrors through `P`.  Intersections of `L` with distinct mirrors
collide exactly at those singular points, hence

```text
|B_q cap L| = q+1-(15-delta(L)) = q-14+delta(L).
```

The complete line spectrum is:

| `delta` | `|B_q cap L|` | number of nonmirror lines |
|---:|---:|---:|
| 0 | `q-14` | `(q-11)(q-19)` |
| 1 | `q-13` | `15(q-11)` |
| 2 | `q-12` | `10(q-11)` |
| 3 | `q-11` | `40` |
| 4 | `q-10` | `6(q-9)` |
| 5 | `q-9` | `66` |

There are additionally fifteen mirror lines with intersection zero.  Thus the degree-one
evaluation code `E(B_q)` has exact parameters

```text
[(q-5)(q-9), 3, (q-6)(q-9)]_q.
```

Its complete weight enumerator is

```text
W(z) = 1 + 15(q-1) z^n
         + (q-1) sum_{delta=0}^5 N_delta z^(q^2-15q+59-delta),
n = (q-5)(q-9),
```

where the six `N_delta` are the line counts in the table.  This is an equality, not a lower bound.

## Deep-hole origin and inverse theorem

Take the six fivefold points as the parity-check columns of the parent `[6,3,4]_q` arc code.  Its
weight-at-most-two projective syndrome locus is exactly the union of their fifteen secants, which
is `A_q`.  Every direction of `B_q` therefore has spanning weight three and is a projective
deep-hole syndrome direction.  In this precise sense, `E(B_q)` is the code generated by the
projective deep-hole transform of the parent code.

For `q>14`, every nonmirror line has at least `q-14>0` points of `B_q`.  Consequently the lines
disjoint from the unmarked projective system `B_q` are exactly the fifteen mirrors.  Their
intersection multiplicities are `6_5,10_3,15_2`; selecting the six fivefold intersections recovers
the parent six-point projective system.  Hence a generator matrix of `E(B_q)`, modulo the usual
row operations, column scalings, and column permutation, recovers the parent code up to projective
equivalence.  No prescribed mirror marking or parent-column marking is needed.

This inverse statement is genuinely beyond C211: C211 identifies the arrangement and uses its
lattice for decoder strata, while C339 starts from the *complement code*, reconstructs the
arrangement as its disjoint-line locus, and then reconstructs the parent code.

## Exact incidence proof

Over `Q(tau)`, the checker constructs all 15 mirrors and all 31 singular points exactly.  It finds

```text
6 points of multiplicity 5,
10 points of multiplicity 3,
15 points of multiplicity 2.
```

There are 106 nonmirror lines through at least two singular points.  Exactly 40 have `delta=3` and
66 have `delta=5`.  The numbers of special nonmirror lines through a fixed singular point are:

| arrangement multiplicity | point count | special lines through each point |
|---:|---:|---:|
| 2 | 15 | 10 |
| 3 | 10 | 9 |
| 5 | 6 | 5 |

The remaining lines through one singular point are obtained from its `q+1`-line pencil after
removing its incident mirrors and special lines.  Subtracting all mirror, special, and
one-singularity lines from the `q^2+q+1` projective lines gives the `delta=0` count.  These three
steps yield the six displayed polynomials and also check that all line counts sum to
`q^2+q+1` after the fifteen mirrors are restored.
