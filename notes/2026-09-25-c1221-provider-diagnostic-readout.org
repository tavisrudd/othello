** TODO Fix the readout contract and failure lifetime

Use =EXECUTE= with a four-byte little-endian selector, zero handle, empty body, =ergodis-diagnostic.v1= output, and a 4096-byte maximum. Require the descriptor entry's name, format, effect, and bound to agree before the host uses it; older descriptors without the entry remain admissible. A failed operation records one current diagnostic; a successful ordinary operation clears it. Reading succeeds once and consumes it. A missing, already consumed, oversized, malformed, or failed readout becomes a typed host =diagnostic_unavailable= with original status and operation, never a guessed producer reason. Neither retrieval nor legacy refusal reads should replace the failure being retrieved. Keep conversion and JSON outside successful evaluator/checker loops.

Red-team gates: stale failure after success; another call between failure and readout; readout with insufficient capacity; forged descriptor ID/name or oversized envelope; provider failure or WASM trap during retrieval; wrong instance; non-ASCII Rel source span; verifier rejection versus record mismatch. A host helper must preserve the original status even when retrieval fails.

** TODO Implement Datalog and Rel provider adapters

Construct the diagnostic from the typed cold failure where possible, preserving exact budget/source/trust facts. A conversion that cannot fit or has no faithful mapping produces no diagnostic; the host reports unavailable. Retain old selectors and their output. Add tests for one-shot consumption, stale clearing, source/budget, and checker/record distinctions.

** TODO Implement native and Worker/WASM host retrieval

The native helper accepts an exclusive =Provider= borrow and retrieves before returning the failure. The Worker calls it before publishing a failed checkpoint or accepting another provider call. Structured transport carries the provider envelope or a typed unavailable/host fault, including original status and operation. Success returns ordinary output unchanged.
