Date: 2026-08-10

Literature depth: two primary sources were read at full text and four further

## Executive verdict

The first thing to prove is negative, and it changes the shape of the desired
Annals-level theorem:

> There cannot be a nonzero diagonal-`A5`-invariant algebraic correspondence
> from the Winger `V4` constituent to the cubic `W5` constituent.

Indeed the two rational irreducibles are nonisomorphic, so

```text
Hom_A5(V4,W5)=0.
```

This is a fundamental representation-theoretic impossibility, not a missing
geometric trick.  A single invariant cycle is the wrong target.

The obstruction has an exact optimal repair.  Over the common modular base,
the two multiplicity motives are the same rank-two elliptic motive after the
quadratic twist

```text
D(T)=(T+27)(T-729/5),          T=5w-27.
```

Hence the bridge is naturally **correspondence-valued**:

```text
W5 tensor V4^*  -->  Corr^2(C_w,X_T).
```

It has dimension twenty, and twenty is minimal for a full bridge.  It is
generated by one marked cycle and its `A5` orbit.  The orbit has thirty
members, naturally indexed by the pairs

```text
(D5 subgroup, A4 subgroup),
```

and the ten row/column relations leave exactly `30-(6+5-1)=20` independent
directions.  Equivalently,

```text
Q[A5/D5]=1+W5,       Q[A5/A4]=1+V4.
```

This gives the right conceptual replacement for a scalar correspondence:

> The cubic and Winger pencils are not joined by one invariant cycle.  Their
> shared elliptic motive produces a minimal twenty-dimensional
> `A5`-equivariant carrier of marked algebraic cycles.

At the abelian/motive level this yields, over the quadratic splitting cover,

```text
J(X_T)^4  ~  A_V(C_w)^5,
```

and over the unsplit base the Winger elliptic multiplicity factor must be
twisted by `D(T)`.  This is an algebraic isogeny statement, not merely an
isomorphism of complex local systems: both multiplicity motives are elliptic,
and the twist is an actual quadratic twist of elliptic curves.

The exact rational-Chow construction over the common complex function field is
now rigorous.  What remains before manuscript promotion is arithmetic descent
to `Q(T)`, the integral symplectic lattice and polarization kernel, and a
relative cycle written cleanly over the whole common smooth base.

## 1. The two cohomological types

Let `G=A5`.  Write

```text
V=V4       the four-dimensional reflection representation,
W=W5       the five-dimensional rational irreducible.
```

For a smooth Winger curve `C_w`, Looijenga--Zi prove

```text
H^1(C_w,Q)_V = V tensor M_W,
rank_Q(M_W)=2,
```

where `M_W=Hom_QG(V,H^1(C_w,Q))` is the weight-one Hodge structure of an
elliptic curve with a distinguished point of order three.  The complementary
constituent is the six-dimensional rational representation

```text
E6=3+3'.
```

For a smooth cubic in the nonstandard `A5` pencil, Hartlieb's character
calculation gives

```text
H^3(X_T,Q)(1) = W tensor M_X,
rank_Q(M_X)=2.
```

Van Geemen--Yamauchi's algebraic Prym/Fano construction shows that `M_X` is an
elliptic motive, not only a rank-two Hodge structure.  Their Section 1.5 states
the motive comparison

```text
h^3(X) = h^1(Alb(F(X))) tensor Lefschetz,
```

and their Sections 2--3 construct the elliptic factor by explicit quotient and
Prym correspondences.

The Fricke--Hecke dossier proves that, on the `A5` line,

```text
M_X = H^1(E_T^(D)),
D=(T+27)(T-729/5),
```

where

```text
E_T: y^2+(T+27)xy+(T+27)^2y=x^3
```

is the universal level-three Tate curve.  Looijenga--Zi identify the completed
Winger base with `X_1(3)` and their elliptic multiplicity factor with its
universal elliptic curve.  Matching the orbifold and the two labelled cusps
gives the already certified base change `T=5w-27`.  Consequently, over the
common complex function field,

```text
M_X = M_W tensor chi_D.
```

This equality is algebraic.  On the quadratic extension obtained by adjoining
`sqrt(D)`, the two elliptic curves are isomorphic; the deck involution changes
the isomorphism by `[-1]`.  Over the original field this is precisely the
quadratic-twist descent datum.

The common smooth base for an unqualified family statement should remove both
families' exceptional fibres:

```text
S = P^1_T minus {infinity,0,-27,729/5,-32}.
```

Here `T=-32` is Winger's Bring boundary.  On `S`, the cover
`S_D -> S`, `z^2=D(T)`, is etale.

## 2. Elliptic quotients on both sides

The multiplicity motives have concrete subgroup-quotient realizations.

### Winger side

Let `K` be any of the five `A4` subgroups of `A5`.  From the character table,

```text
dim V^K=1,        dim E6^K=0.
```

Therefore

```text
H^1(C_w/K,Q)=H^1(C_w,Q)^K=M_W.
```

In particular, `C_w/K` has genus one.  Its Jacobian is isogenous to the
Looijenga--Zi elliptic multiplicity factor.  This follows either immediately
from invariant differentials or from Carocca--Rodriguez's rational-idempotent
description of intermediate quotient Jacobians.

Thus the Winger elliptic shadow can be made geometric without choosing cycles
in a homology lattice:

```text
E_W,K = Jac(C_w/K).
```

There are five conjugate choices, indexed by `G/K`.

### Cubic side

Let `H` be any of the six dihedral normalizers `D5=N_G(C5)`.  Then

```text
dim W^H=1.
```

The rational group-algebra projector

```text
e_H=(1/10) sum_{h in H} h
```

therefore has elliptic image in the intermediate Jacobian/Albanese:

```text
E_X,H = Image(e_H : J(X_T) -> J(X_T)).
```

All six choices are isogenous and their translates span the full `W`
isotypic fivefold.  Roulleau's Proposition 29 gives a geometric version for
the type-II dihedral action, characterized there by involution trace one on
the five-dimensional one-form space; this is the trace of an involution on
`W`.  The resolved Fano-surface quotient by `D5` is ruled over a genus-one
curve.  The group-projector argument is the load-bearing statement here and
does not require the quotient surface to be smooth.

There are six conjugate choices, indexed by `G/H`.

## 3. The sharp obstruction theorem

Let `Z` be a codimension-two algebraic cycle on `C_w x X_T`.  It induces

```text
Z_*: H^1(C_w,Q) -> H^3(X_T,Q).
```

Suppose `Z` is invariant under the diagonal `G` action.  Then `Z_*` is
`G`-linear.  Its restriction to the Winger elliptic constituent lies in

```text
Hom_G(V tensor M_W, W tensor M_X)
 = Hom_G(V,W) tensor Hom(M_W,M_X).
```

The `A5` character table gives

```text
<chi_V,chi_W>=0,
```

so this space is zero.

### Obstruction theorem

Every diagonal-`A5`-invariant algebraic cycle acts trivially from the Winger
`V4` constituent to the cubic `W5` constituent.  In particular, no ordinary
invariant cycle can realize the modular bridge, even after base extension.

This conclusion is independent of the Hodge conjecture and independent of the
formula for `D`.  It applies to any cohomological correspondence.  It does not
say that every invariant cycle is rationally or homologically trivial; it says
that its load-bearing `V -> W` action is zero.

The theorem explains why searches for a single `A5`-invariant Fano/incidence
cycle are structurally doomed.

## 4. Optimal repair: a representation-valued algebraic correspondence

Over the quadratic splitting field, choose the elliptic isomorphism or isogeny

```text
phi: M_W -> M_X.
```

For every linear map `a:V->W`, the tensor product

```text
a tensor phi: V tensor M_W -> W tensor M_X
```

is algebraic.  This uses only:

1. rational group-algebra idempotents, which are sums of graphs of the finite
   group action;
2. the graph of the elliptic isogeny `phi`; and
3. the algebraic motive comparison between the cubic and its Fano/Prym
   intermediate Jacobian.

Consequently there is an equivariant injection

```text
W tensor V^* -> Corr^2(C_w,X_T)_Q
```

whose realization sends `a` to `a tensor phi`.  Here `G` acts on
correspondences by conjugating the actions on source and target.  The carrier
has dimension

```text
dim(W tensor V^*)=5*4=20.
```

It has no invariant vector, exactly as the obstruction theorem demands.

### Minimality

Any coefficient space that realizes every matrix direction from `V` to `W`
must surject onto `Hom(V,W)`, hence has dimension at least twenty.  The carrier
above meets the bound.  Thus it is the unique optimal shape up to replacing it
by another twenty-dimensional realization of the same `G` module.

One non-invariant marked cycle can of course realize one rank-one elliptic
bridge.  The twenty-dimensional lower bound concerns a full equivariant
carrier, not the existence of a single marking-dependent map.

### The motivic/power form

Let `A_V(C_w)` denote the four-dimensional Winger abelian factor cut out by
the central `V` idempotent.  Rationally,

```text
J(X_T)       ~ W tensor E_X,
A_V(C_w)     ~ V tensor E_W.
```

After adjoining `sqrt(D)`, tensor symmetry gives the marked isogeny

```text
J(X_T)^4 ~ A_V(C_w)^5.
```

Over the unsplit complex function field the exact statement is

```text
J(X_T)^4 ~ (A_V(C_w) twisted on its elliptic factor by D(T))^5.
```

At the Chow-motive level, with the usual Lefschetz twist,

```text
V tensor h^3(X_T)(1)
    = W tensor h^1(C_w)_V tensor chi_D.
```

This is the precise algebraic version of the earlier VHS prediction.  It is a
Morita-type bridge: the irreducible `A5` factors differ, while their
multiplicity motive is the same.

## 5. The thirty quotient correspondences

The abstract carrier has a remarkably concrete generator system.

Let

```text
H_1,...,H_6       be the six D5 normalizers,
K_1,...,K_5       be the five A4 point stabilizers.
```

For every pair `(H_i,K_j)`, use the quotient/projector maps

```text
C_w -> E_W,K_j,
E_W,K_j -> E_X,H_i,
E_X,H_i -> J(X_T),
```

and the cubic motive correspondence to obtain a codimension-two cycle

```text
Z_ij in CH^2(C_w x X_T)_Q.
```

The exact finite-group geometry is:

```text
number of pairs                         6*5=30,
the diagonal A5 action on pairs         transitive,
H_i intersection K_j                   C2 for every pair,
pair orbit                              A5/C2.
```

Thus one marked cycle and its conjugates produce all thirty.

Passing from the two permutation modules to their augmentation modules imposes
the six row-sum and five column-sum relations, with one dependency.  Hence

```text
30-(6+5-1)=20.
```

The double-augmented cycles span the full carrier `W tensor V^*`.  This is an
exact geometric reason for both numbers in the predicted power isogeny:
the six axes carry `1+W`, the five cubes carry `1+V`, and deleting the two
trivial directions leaves the `5 by 4` bridge.

### The edge-cycle surprise

Looijenga--Zi's edge vanishing cycles also form a transitive thirty-element
`A5` set with stabilizer `C2`.  Therefore, after one marked seed is chosen,
their edge-cycle orbit and the subgroup-pair orbit are the same `A5/C2` set.
Moreover `N_A5(C2)/C2` has order two, so this orbit has a unique nonidentity
equivariant involution.  Under a seed identification it is exactly the
edge-cycle sign exchange `delta -> -delta`; on subgroup pairs it exchanges the
two pairs with the same `C2` stabilizer.

This is more than numerology.  The Winger cyclic index-three defect is proved
by divisibility of a chain assembled from those edge cycles.  The exact orbit
that generates the cubic--Winger correspondence carrier is therefore the same
orbit on which the Winger `3`-isogeny defect lives.  A comparison of the two
integral incidence maps is now the highest-EV route to the missing integral
polarization theorem.

The present work proves the `A5`-set identification, not yet equality of the
two integral cycle maps.  That distinction must remain explicit.

## 6. Integral label-lattice audit

Looijenga--Zi use the integral models

```text
0 -> Z -> Z^5 -> V_0 -> 0,
0 -> Z -> Z^6 -> W_0 -> 0.
```

Their duals are the root lattices `A4` and `A5`.  On the polarization-compatible
double-augmentation label lattice, the primitive integral representative of a
pair `(i,j)` is

```text
(6e_i-1_6)(5e_j-1_5)^t.
```

The thirty representatives have rational rank twenty.  Relative to the full
integral lattice of `6 by 5` matrices with zero row and column sums, their
Smith data are

```text
1^4, 6^1, 30^15,
```

so their saturation index is

```text
6^16 * 5^15.
```

There is a short human derivation.  The six vectors `6e_i-1_6` span a
sublattice of `A5` of Smith type `(1,6,6,6,6)` and index `6^4`; the five
vectors `5e_j-1_5` span a sublattice of `A4` of type `(1,5,5,5)` and index
`5^3`.  Tensoring gives index

```text
(6^4)^4 (5^3)^5 = 6^16 5^15,
```

and reorganizing the elementary divisors gives the displayed Smith form.

This is **not** yet the degree of the desired abelian-variety isogeny.  It is
the saturation defect of the most symmetric double-augmentation label model.
Primitive idempotents, the actual cohomology lattices and their polarizations
can cancel or replace part of it.  Its valid conclusion is narrower:

> A rational group-projector construction does not automatically give the
> primitive integral correspondence.  The primes `2,3,5` are already forced
> before the two geometric lattices are compared.

That is exactly why an integral/polarization theorem remains a real gate.

## 7. A general theorem template

The mechanism is not special to `A5`.

Let a finite group `G` act on two smooth projective families and suppose the
relevant cohomologies have the form

```text
V tensor M,       W tensor N,
```

for absolutely irreducible rational `G` modules `V,W`.  Assume `M,N` are
rank-two motives realized by elliptic abelian subquotients and

```text
N = M tensor chi
```

for a quadratic character `chi` of the base.

Then:

1. a scalar diagonal-`G` correspondence exists on these constituents only in
   `Hom_G(V,W)`;
2. regardless of whether `V` and `W` are isomorphic, there is an algebraic
   coefficient-valued correspondence with carrier `W tensor V^*` after the
   quadratic base change;
3. it descends over the original base with coefficient character `chi`;
4. if `V` and `W` arise as augmentations of transitive `G` sets, subgroup
   quotient projectors give a finite orbit of explicit rank-one generators;
5. the integral theorem is controlled by the saturation of those projectors
   inside the polarized multiplicity lattices.

For the present pair,

```text
(G,V,W,chi)=(A5,V4,W5,chi_D).
```

The obstruction is nonvacuous because `V4` and `W5` are different.  The
repair is unusually concrete because both are augmentations of transitive
sets of sizes five and six.

## 8. Extra representation structure

The twenty-dimensional carrier is not irreducible.  Its exact decomposition is

```text
W5 tensor V4 = (3+3') + V4 + 2 W5
              = E6 + V4 + 2 W5.
```

The `E6=3+3'` summand is precisely the rational representation supporting the
complementary Hilbert-modular constituent of the Winger Jacobian.  This does
not yet construct a bridge to that constituent: the multiplicity fields are
different, and the elliptic `X_1(3)` argument does not apply to it.  It is,
however, a genuine unexplained degree of freedom in the carrier and a sharp
test for any proposed larger correspondence algebra.

An ambitious general theorem should explain why the elliptic bridge selects
the whole matrix carrier while only one rank-two multiplicity system is
identified, and whether the `E6` coefficient channel has a geometric meaning
or is merely representation-theoretic baggage.

## 10. Annals-level theorem target

The strongest plausible theorem is now sharply stated:

> **Twisted icosahedral Morita correspondence.**  Over the common smooth base,
> the Winger `V4` motive and the cubic `W5` motive have a common elliptic
> multiplicity motive with quadratic character `D`.  No scalar
> `A5`-invariant correspondence can join them.  Instead one marked
> `A5/C2` orbit of algebraic cycles generates the optimal carrier
> `W5 tensor V4^*`; after saturation it induces a polarized isogeny between
> `J(X)^4` and the fifth power of the twisted Winger factor, with explicitly
> determined kernel and boundary monodromy.

The rational-Chow and optimal-obstruction halves are now in hand.  The result
reaches an Annals-type conceptual level only if the integral half is equally
sharp: the kernel, polarization type, descent field and boundary extension must
be calculated, not hidden behind “up to isogeny.”

The highest-EV next steps are:

1. compute `Jac(C_w/A4)` explicitly and compare it over `Q(w)` with the Tate
   model, fixing any residual arithmetic twist;
2. identify the van Geemen--Yamauchi elliptic curve with the `D5` primitive
   idempotent factor on the exact integral homology lattice;
3. identify the marked `A5/C2` pair orbit with Looijenga--Zi's edge cycles and
   transport their divisibility-by-three proof to the correspondence lattice;
4. compute the Smith form of the actual symplectic map, hence the degree and
   polarization kernel of the power isogeny;
5. package the generic construction as a relative Chow correspondence over
   `S_D`, then analyze admissible limits at the four shared modular markers.

Steps 1--4, not more modular formulas, decide whether this becomes the intended
Annals-level crown.
