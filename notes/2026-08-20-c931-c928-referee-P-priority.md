# C931 / C928 referee P -- priority and decomposition boundary

## 1. Verdict

**B -- minor revision.** The integral fibre-product glue and its dual escape lattice survive the reviewed prior art, and the rational comparison with Krämer is correct. The required revision is a local but substantive priority correction: the Smith factors and the abstract saturation defect of the exterior-Lefschetz map are already an immediate specialization of Faulkner Valiente--Miller Eismeier, not merely a calculation situated in a nearby general framework. The manuscript should say this explicitly and reserve its headline for the geometric realization of that defect inside \((b_*,e^*)\), which is the part not recovered from either rational decomposition or the integral Lefschetz filtration.

Of the seven external works characterized below, one was read in full (Krämer) and six from available full text at the targeted sections stated below. The frozen eight-page manuscript was read in full. Every negative below means only **not found in the reviewed sources and bounded searches**, not global novelty.

## 2. Claim map

- **Theorem 1.1:** the abstract exact sequence and rank are compatible with Krämer's rational decomposition, while the mod-two simultaneous \((b_*,e^*)\)-placement is not contained in the reviewed prior sources.
- **Theorem 1.2:** the free rank-ten escape lattice with exceptional image \(2E_M\) is integrally meaningful, rationally invisible, and not found in the reviewed sources; it is best presented as the dual consequence of Theorem 1.1 rather than a separate priority crown.
- **Corollary 1.3:** the rational equality \(IH^3(\Theta,\mathbf Q)\simeq H^3(M,\mathbf Q)\) is prior in Krämer's Corollary 6, whereas the integral identification and exact sequence are not stated there and follow in the manuscript from the degree-three truncation and local topology.
- **Theorem 3.1:** its Smith factors \(1^{110},2^{10}\) and quotient \(\Lambda/2\Lambda\) are an immediate off-central specialization of the integral Lefschetz filtration of Faulkner Valiente--Miller Eismeier; the complete-graph proof and the particularly transparent representatives \(\theta^{[2]}\wedge z\) remain useful exposition, not an independent priority claim.
- **Equation (18):** the shifts, the three skyscraper summands, and the boundary at \(\mathbf Q\) are correct; the displayed splitting should be understood as a noncanonical rational decomposition-theorem splitting.

## 3. Major findings

1. **The FVME comparison presently understates direct dependence.**
   - **Location:** manuscript p. 3, final two paragraphs of the introduction; p. 4, paragraph after Theorem 3.1.
   - **Assertion:** FVME is described as the "nearest general algebraic framework," while the manuscript says its own specialization identifies the exact elementary divisors.
   - **Finding:** FVME's Theorem 2.9 and Corollary 2.10, together with the compatible integral splittings of Proposition 2.14, already give those elementary divisors. In degree \(3\to5\) for \(g=5\), the filtration has primitive layers \(P^3\) of rank \(\binom{10}{3}-\binom{10}{1}=110\) and \(P^1\) of rank 10; wedge by \(\theta\) acts with factors 1 and 2. Hence the Smith form \(1^{110},2^{10}\) and torsion layer \(P^1/2P^1=\Lambda/2\Lambda\) are immediate. The manuscript's unsigned-incidence calculation independently and elegantly exhibits the representative \(\theta^{[2]}\wedge z\), but does not establish priority for the abstract Smith data.
   - **Severity:** required priority repair, but local; it does not affect the headline geometric glue.
   - **Smallest adequate remedy:** replace "nearest general algebraic framework" by an explicit statement that the Smith form and abstract saturation quotient specialize from FVME Corollary 2.10/Proposition 2.14, then say exactly what the occupancy proof adds: a short direct block computation and the divided-power representatives used by the Fano endpoint.

2. **Krämer's rational boundary is correctly stated and does not pre-empt the mod-two theorem.**
   - **Location:** manuscript p. 7, equation (18), through p. 8 first paragraph.
   - **Assertion:** \(R\sigma_*\mathbf Q_M[4]\simeq IC_\Theta\oplus\mathbf Q_0[2]\oplus\mathbf Q_0\oplus\mathbf Q_0[-2]\), giving corrections only in degrees \(2,4,6\), and no information about (3).
   - **Verification:** Krämer Corollary 6 proves over \(\mathbf C\) that the point-supported complement is \(\delta_0[2]\oplus\delta_0\oplus\delta_0[-2]\). His stalk calculation uses \(H^{i+4}(X)\), relative hard Lefschetz, and the fact that the nonnegative stalk cohomology of \(IC_\Theta\) vanishes. All of these steps work over \(\mathbf Q\), so the manuscript's coefficient change is justified. Hypercohomological reindexing by \([4]\) puts the three one-dimensional corrections in ordinary degrees \(2,4,6\). The ten-dimensional \(H^3(X,\mathbf Q)\) contribution is absorbed in \(IC_\Theta\), not a fourth skyscraper summand.
   - **Severity:** verified; no mathematical repair.
   - **Smallest adequate remedy:** optionally add "noncanonically" before the rational direct-sum display and cite the rational decomposition theorem, since Krämer himself writes complex coefficients.

3. **The mod-two fibre product and doubled escape lattice are genuinely outside the rational decomposition.**
   - **Location:** manuscript pp. 1--2, (2)--(4) and Theorem 1.2; p. 6, Proposition 4.3 and the final paragraph; p. 7, (15)--(16).
   - **Assertion:** the arithmetic lies in simultaneous \((b_*,e^*)\)-glue, not in a nonsplit abelian-group extension.
   - **Verification:** tensoring (3) with \(\mathbf Q\) kills \(S/L\bigwedge^3\Lambda\simeq\Lambda/2\Lambda\); it therefore forgets both the parity coset \([\theta^{[2]}\wedge y(\gamma)]\) and the index-two kernel that dualizes to \(e_*H^3(X)=2E_M\). Krämer's noncanonical rational splitting carries neither map compatibility nor integral index. FVME knows the algebraic defect of \(L\) but not that the Fano correspondence realizes every quotient direction with the stated cylinder label. No reviewed source states formulas (3), (14), or the doubled escape lattice.
   - **Severity:** positive priority verification.
   - **Smallest adequate remedy:** make this division of labor the last sentence of the introduction's literature paragraph: FVME supplies the algebraic defect; Proposition 4.3 supplies its geometric realization; formula (3) is the surviving theorem.

4. **The classical inputs are accurately used, but the geometric novelty is an assembly, not wholly new Fano geometry.**
   - **Location:** manuscript pp. 5--6, opening of Section 4, Lemmas 4.1--4.2, Proposition 4.3.
   - **Verification:** Clemens--Griffiths already prove the integral Albanese/intermediate-Jacobian isomorphism, the minimal class \([F]=\theta^3/3!\), and the degree-six difference description; Beauville proves the unique ordinary triple point with tangent cone \(X\) and the relevant blow-up geometry. Artebani--Kloosterman--Pacini later restate the degree-six Fano model and identify Clemens' model essentially with the blow-up of \(\Theta\) at its triple point. The manuscript's formal Pontryagin endpoint is therefore close to an immediate calculation from classical data. What was not found is the clean integral exceptional-restriction formula followed through to the labelled ten endpoint lifts and the fibre-product lattice.
   - **Severity:** verified with a positioning clarification.
   - **Smallest adequate remedy:** describe Lemmas 4.1--4.2 as extracting an integral endpoint from classical correspondences, and identify Proposition 4.3 plus the final fibre-product argument as the new geometric step.

## 4. Minor findings

1. On p. 3 and in the references, standardize the prose spelling to "Krämer"; the citation label already uses the umlaut.
2. On p. 7, say explicitly that (18) is a noncanonical splitting in the rational derived category. This prevents readers from importing a canonical splitting into the integral discussion.
3. Cite the exact FVME locations (Theorem 2.9, Corollary 2.10, Proposition 2.14), not only the paper as a whole.
4. Two modern treatments of the same geometric neighborhood are obvious contextual omissions: Bayer--Beentjes--Feyzbakhsh--Hein--Martinelli--Rezaee--Schmidt identify \(M=\operatorname{Bl}_0\Theta\) as a moduli space, while Zhang analyzes \(Y=\operatorname{Bl}_\Delta(F\times F)\), the ordered skew-line space used in Section 4, and its Abel--Jacobi factorization. Neither treatment computes the integral lattice at issue here, so this is a context/citation issue, not detected pre-emption.
5. Artebani--Kloosterman--Pacini is also worth citing for an independent birational model and for its concise restatement of the degree-six Fano description; it does not contain the lattice result.

### What the reviewed sources establish

Krämer establishes the rational/complex decomposition boundary. FVME establishes the integral exterior-Lefschetz filtration strongly enough to force the Smith data and abstract mod-two defect. Clemens--Griffiths and Beauville establish the classical Fano, cylinder, minimal-class, singularity, and resolution inputs. Artebani--Kloosterman--Pacini and Bayer et al. give other models of the theta resolution, while Zhang analyzes the ordered Fano blow-up mapping to it. None of the reviewed sources was found to identify the simultaneous mod-two coset in \((b_*,e^*)\) or the doubled exceptional sublattice in the dual escape quotient.
