# C904: degree (15) in the (D_{3,3}) relay -- exact scope

Date: 2026-08-11
Status: quarantined red-team certificate; no manuscript or Lean promotion
Scope: the factorable-wedge packet, its type-((3,3)) interpretation, and
the relative Abel--Jacobi index implication

## Verdict

The degree-(15) computation is a strong and useful compression, but it is
not an odd point on the charge-three Abel--Jacobi fourfold.  Conditional on
identifying the raw rank-two wedge packet with the genuine generic
type-((3,3)) packet, it proves that three apparently different two-primary
indices are equal:

\[
 v_2\!\left(\operatorname{ind}V\right)
 =v_2\!\left(\operatorname{ind}D\right)
 =v_2\!\left(\operatorname{ind}Y\right).
\]

Here (V) is the charge-three (M_9\to J) fibre, (D) is the
type-((3,3)) relay, and (Y) is the unordered-theta fibre.  Since the
existing C904 constructions give index dividing two on the two ends, the
packet shows that (V) and (Y) are simultaneously of index one or index
two.  It transfers the gate to (Y); it does not decide it.

The exact scripts prove that one characteristic-zero *raw* rank-two packet is
reduced, prime over \(\mathbf Q\), and of degree (15).  Three bridges must
be distinguished before using it on the marked (A_5) family:

1. the constructed quotient bundle is a stable point of the required
   (M_9) component;
2. the finite transverse packet persists at the generic point of (M_9) on
   the marked (A_5) cubic family;
3. every raw rank-two packet point is in the type-((3,3)) stratum, rather
   than another nonregular or reducible degree-three stratum.

The first bridge is closed for the displayed model by the Pfaffian/linkage
normal form and null-correlation restriction, once that identification is
included in the certificate.  The third is closed there by an ordinary-
degeneracy argument and a good etale specialization at (p=5).  The sole
load-bearing transfer still missing is the second: neither openness in the
universal component nor a random cubic proves that the good open meets the
special marked (A_5) fibre.

## 1. The index diagram and all divisibility directions

Let (K) be the function field of one relative support component over which
the Abel--Jacobi lift is required, and let its generic point define
(a\in J(K)).  After taking proper smooth models, put

\[
 V=(M_9)_a,\qquad
 Y=(\operatorname{Sym}^2\Theta)_a,\qquad
 D=(D_{3,3})_a.
\]

The two maps used by Voisin give the diagram

\[
 \begin{array}{ccc}
 &D&\\[-2mm]
 {}^{f}\swarrow&&\searrow^{\chi}\\[-1mm]
 V&&Y\\
 &\searrow\quad\swarrow&\\[-2mm]
 &\operatorname{Spec}K.&
 \end{array}
\]

Over (k(V)), let (R) be the factorable-wedge packet.  The proposed line
description says that the normalization of the generic (f)-fibre is
birational to

\[
                    Z=\mathbf P({\cal U}|_R).
\]

If (R) has degree (15), then (Z), hence the generic (f)-fibre, has a
zero-cycle of degree (15).  Over (k(Y)), Voisin identifies the generic
(\chi)-fibre birationally with (\operatorname{Sym}^2(E_3)), where (E_3)
is a plane cubic.  A rational line cut gives an effective degree-three
zero-cycle (P_1+P_2+P_3) on (E_3); the three pair sums
(P_1+P_2,P_1+P_3,P_2+P_3) descend as a degree-three zero-cycle on
(\operatorname{Sym}^2(E_3)).  Thus this fibre has index dividing three.

The required general index lemma is direction-sensitive.  If
(h:W\dashrightarrow X) is dominant between proper smooth (K)-varieties
and its generic fibre has a zero-cycle of degree (n), then

\[
 \operatorname{ind}(X)\mid\operatorname{ind}(W)
 \quad\hbox{and}\quad
 \operatorname{ind}(W)\mid n\operatorname{ind}(X).
\]

The first divisibility is pushforward: degrees of zero-cycles do not change.
For the second, spread the generic-fibre cycle to a generically finite
multisection of degree (n), move zero-cycles on the smooth base into its
domain of definition, and pull them through that multisection.  Applying the
lemma twice gives

\[
 \operatorname{ind}(V)\mid\operatorname{ind}(D)
       \mid15\operatorname{ind}(V),
\]

\[
 \operatorname{ind}(Y)\mid\operatorname{ind}(D)
       \mid3\operatorname{ind}(Y).
\]

Both multipliers are odd, proving equality of the three two-adic valuations.
There is no direct map (V\dashrightarrow Y), and neither odd multiplier
produces a zero-cycle on (V) over (K).  A degree-(15) cycle over
(k(V)) is a multisection of (D\to V), not a multisection of
(V\to\operatorname{Spec}K).  This is the precise flaw in the proposed
positive exit.

The argument works after base change to each actual relative support surface;
working over the full generic point of (J) is not a substitute for that
restriction.

## 2. Factorable wedge quadrics versus type ((3,3))

Let (U=\langle s,t\rangle\subset H^0(X,E)) and suppose geometrically that

\[
                  s\wedge t=h_1h_2
\]

has rank exactly two.  Write (S_i=X\cap H_i) and
(\Gamma=S_1\cap S_2).  Assume (S_1,S_2) and (\Gamma) have the expected
ordinary intersections, the evaluation matrix has generic rank one on each
(S_i), and a general section in (U) is regular.

On (S_i), saturate the image of
(U\otimes\mathcal O_{S_i}\to E|_{S_i}) as

\[
                    L_i\otimes I_{Z_i}\subset E|_{S_i}.
\]

The rank-zero scheme of the same (2\times2) evaluation matrix is intrinsic.
At a rank-zero point the differential of its determinant vanishes, so this
scheme lies in the singular locus (\Gamma) of (H_1\cup H_2).  Restricting
the common image sheaf to (\Gamma) therefore identifies

\[
 L_1|_\Gamma(-Z)=L_2|_\Gamma(-Z),
\]

with the same rank-zero correction (Z).  Hence

\[
 H\,c_1(L_1)=\deg L_1|_\Gamma
             =\deg L_2|_\Gamma=H\,c_1(L_2).
\]

A regular section curve has total degree six, so its two divisorial parts on
(S_1,S_2) both have degree three.  If one general part were a plane cubic,
varying the hyperplane through its plane would give a positive-dimensional
family of rank-two quadrics through the same section.  Via

\[
 H^0(E)/\langle s\rangle\simeq H^0(I_{C_s}(2)),
\]

this would give a positive-dimensional raw packet, contrary to finiteness.
Thus, once generic integrality is checked, both degree-three parts span their
hyperplanes and are twisted cubics.  Since the total curve has arithmetic
genus one, the union formula then forces intersection length two.

This repairs the equality of the two component *degrees*.  The
scheme-theoretic equality of the two rank-zero corrections should be stated as
an ordinary-degeneracy hypothesis when this lemma is used abstractly.  For the
displayed model, the specialization argument in Section 4 separately excludes
the nonintegral degree-three alternative without assuming that local lemma at
all fifteen geometric points.

There is no extra factor two merely because a rank-two quadric is nonsplit
over a residue field.  The two hyperplanes may be conjugate, while the
two-plane (U) and its honest projective line \(\mathbf P(U)\) are already
defined over that residue field.  Type ((3,3)) is a geometric condition and
the unordered union descends.

## 3. Normalization and the finite base

When (R) is finite etale and all its points are good, the incidence
(\mathbf P({\cal U}|_R)) is a disjoint union of honest projective lines over
the residue fields of (R).  A general type-((3,3)) section recovers its two
spanning hyperplanes, their product, and the unique two-plane (U).  Thus the
incidence is generically one-to-one onto the (D_{3,3})-fibre; after
normalization it is the required smooth proper birational model.

Consequently

\[
 \operatorname{ind}\mathbf P({\cal U}|_R)
   =\gcd_{r\in R}[k(r):k(V)].
\]

An odd total length guarantees an odd residue degree even before a full
factorization of (R).  Reducedness is useful for the normalization statement
but is not needed for this parity observation.  Conversely, the index of the
unnormalized singular image need not equal the index of its normalization in
general: a rational singular point can have only quadratic preimages.  All
index claims here therefore use the smooth normalized incidence, not an
unsupported birational-invariance assertion for singular curves.

A prime degree-(15) finite scheme over (\mathbf Q) is integral over
(\mathbf Q), not geometrically integral.  In characteristic zero its
geometric fibre is a reduced set of fifteen points when it is etale.

## 4. What the exact packet scripts establish

For their deterministic characteristic-zero model, the Sage and independent
Macaulay2 calculations agree on the following raw rank condition:

- the residual cubic is smooth and the six wedge quadrics are basepoint-free;
- one residual section curve is a smooth integral sextic with Hilbert
  polynomial (6t);
- the Pluecker-plus-(3\times3)-minor packet is zero-dimensional,
  reduced, prime over (\mathbf Q), and has degree (15);
- the rank-one packet is empty.

The Macaulay2 replay checks the fixed symmetric matrix independently of the
Sage ideal operations.  These are exact claims about the displayed model.
They do not by themselves establish stability of its bundle, dominance over
the relevant (M_9), or persistence on the special marked (A_5) family.

The auxiliary finite-field audit examined all rational raw packet points in
the reductions at (p=5,7,11).  There was one point at (p=5), one at
(p=7), and two at (p=11).  The latter three rank-two quadrics were
nonsplit over the prime field and were split over the quadratic extension.
For every one of the four points, the regular sections tested had component
Hilbert polynomials

\[
                         3t+1,\qquad 3t+1.
\]

The (p=5) fibre is the decisive one: its full packet Hilbert polynomial is
still (15), and the rational point has projective Jacobian rank five, so it
is an etale point of a horizontal finite component.  Its rank-two quadric
splits and its line contains a section for which both degree-three component
ideals are prime and have exactly one independent linear equation; they are
therefore nondegenerate integral cubics in their hyperplanes, hence twisted
cubics.  Goodness is open in the universal section incidence.  The
characteristic-zero packet is prime of degree (15) over (\mathbf Q), so
the horizontal generic point is the sole degree-(15) field point and all
fifteen geometric points are its Galois conjugates.  This proves that the
displayed characteristic-zero raw packet is the good type-((3,3)) packet.
The (p=7) rational point has Jacobian rank four, and the (p=11) packet
acquires degree (27); those are bad reductions and are used only as
diagnostics.

This specialization proof still says nothing formal about the generic bundle
on the special marked (A_5) family.

## 5. The Pfaffian/linkage normal form

The proposed normal form is sound on its ordinary open, with one important
correction.  Lift the six Pluecker coordinates of the evaluation map to
quadrics (q_{ij}) on (\mathbf P^4).  Since a cubic has no quadratic
equations, the lifts are unique.  Their degree-four Pfaffian vanishes on
(X=(F=0)), hence

\[
                         \operatorname{Pf}(q)=F L
\]

for a linear form (L).  When (L\ne0) and the (q_{ij}|_H) have no common
zero on (H=(L=0)\simeq\mathbf P^3), they define a globally generated
rank-two bundle (B) on (H).

For a regular section, the three wedge quadrics are a complete intersection
of type ((2,2,2)), of degree eight and arithmetic genus five.  The sextic
elliptic section curve (C) has residual curve (T\subset H) of degree two.
The codimension-three linkage formula gives

\[
 p_a(C)-p_a(T)
 =\frac{(2+2+2)-5}{2}\bigl(\deg C-\deg T\bigr)=2,
\]

so (p_a(T)=-1).  A reduced locally Cohen--Macaulay degree-two curve with
this genus is geometrically a pair of skew lines; a conic or two intersecting
lines has genus zero.  Nonreduced double lines are a closed bad stratum that
must still be excluded in an abstract use of the normal form.  Serre's
construction then identifies (B) with a null-correlation bundle (N(1)).

The exact displayed model has

\[
                     P_T(t)=2t+2,
\]

and its sextic zero scheme on (X) is exactly the saturated linked residual,
not that residual plus hidden isolated points from (T\cap X).

This normal form also supplies a clean stability proof once the restriction
identification is written down.  Namely
(E|_{X\cap H}=N(1)|_{X\cap H}).  The null-correlation sequence and the Euler
sequence give

\[
 H^0\bigl(X\cap H,N(-m)|_{X\cap H}\bigr)=0\qquad(m\ge0).
\]

A section of (E(-1)) would restrict to zero and hence be divisible by (L);
iterating gives a section of arbitrarily negative twist, a contradiction.
Thus (H^0(E(-1))=0), which is the stability criterion for the normalized
rank-two bundle on the Picard-rank-one cubic.  This closes moduli placement
for the displayed model provided the quotient-bundle/restriction
identification is included in the certificate.

The important correction is that the packet line is not the full Abel linear
system on either cubic surface.  If (D) is a twisted-cubic class, then

\[
 D^2=1,\qquad H D=3,\qquad h^0(D)=3,
\]

so (|D|\simeq\mathbf P^2), as in the Harris--Roth--Starr description.  The
line (\mathbf P(U)) maps only to a pencil inside (|D|).  It is nonconstant:
if the two induced sections of (\mathcal O_S(D)) were proportional, a
nonzero member of (U) would vanish identically on (S=X\cap H_i), producing
a section of (E(-1)), which stability forbids.  Nonconstancy, not equality
with (|D|), is what the line-packet birationality needs.

Finally, a finite all-good fibre of the proper relative packet gives a finite
etale degree-(15) packet on an open of the universal (M_9) component.  It
does not formally show that this open meets the special marked (A_5) fibre.
That specialization remains the only family-specific bridge.

## 6. The (15)-edge (A_5) coincidence

The numerical coincidence has a canonical group-theoretic form.  The six
(D_5) normalizers in (A_5) contain five involutions each.  Every one of the
fifteen involutions lies in exactly two of them, giving an (A_5)-equivariant
bijection

\[
 \{\text{involutions}\}
 \simeq \binom{\{\text{six }D_5\text{ axes}\}}2.
\]

The stabilizer is (V_4), and the degree-(15) permutation representation is

\[
                 \operatorname{Ind}_{V_4}^{A_5}\mathbf1
                 =\mathbf1\oplus\mathbf4\oplus\mathbf5\oplus\mathbf5.
\]

This does not identify the generic (D_{3,3}) packet.  A generic bundle
(E\in M_9) is not (A_5)-fixed: (g) sends (R(E)) to (R(g^*E)), not to
itself.  Likewise a generic Abel--Jacobi value is not fixed.  A fibrewise edge
identification therefore requires an (A_5)-linearized charge-three bundle or
multisection.  On the fully axis-marked base it would split the packet into
fifteen rational projective-line components, far stronger than the prime
degree-(15) packet presently computed.

There is a sharper obstruction to the obvious fixed-bundle test.  For an
(A_5)-linearized bundle with (H^0(E)\simeq\mathbf4), the Pfaffian in the
normal form is invariant because (A_5) has no nontrivial determinant
character.  Since (F) is invariant, (\operatorname{Pf}(q)=FL) would force
(L) to be an invariant linear form on the irreducible ambient
(\mathbf5).  None exists.  Thus an (A_5)-fixed bundle cannot lie in the
ordinary (L\ne0) normal-form stratum; it would have
(\operatorname{Pf}(q)=0) identically and belong to a qualitatively different,
potentially excess stratum.  Symmetry alone therefore cannot identify the
generic packet with the edge set.  Even if some different marked construction
did split (D\to V) and provide a rational map (V\dashrightarrow Y), it
would still not give a zero-cycle on (V\to\operatorname{Spec}K).

## 7. Primary-source boundary

Claire Voisin, *Abel--Jacobi map, integral Hodge classes and decomposition of
the diagonal*, arXiv:1005.5621, Section 2, constructs (M_9), proves that
(D_{3,3}\dashrightarrow M_9) is dominant with generic fibre of dimension at
most one (Lemma 2.4), and describes
(D_{3,3}\to\operatorname{Sym}^2\Theta) with generic fibre birational to
(\operatorname{Sym}^2(E_3)).  She does not identify the first fibre with a
factorable-wedge line packet and does not compute its degree or index.

Michal Szurek and Jaroslaw A. Wisniewski, *Fano bundles over
(\mathbf P^3) and (Q_3)*, Pacific J. Math. **141** (1990), Section 2,
record the null-correlation construction and explicitly use the fact that a
twisted null-correlation bundle has a section vanishing on any prescribed two
disjoint lines.  This supports the Serre/null-correlation identification in
the linkage normal form; it does not discuss the cubic-threefold packet.

The bounded exact-title and invariant search located no primary source that
prints the degree-(15) packet or the index-transfer statement.  This is a
bounded search result, not a priority claim.

## 8. EJ / TT closeout

### Error-judo failures found

1. **Directionality trap:** an odd multisection of (D\to V) is not an odd
   point of (V).
2. **Raw/good-stratum trap:** rank at most two records factorability, not the
   type-((3,3)), regularity, or integrality conditions.
3. **Specialization trap:** one exact model does not automatically control the
   generic marked (A_5) family.
4. **Normalization trap:** singular curves and their normalizations can have
   different indices; use the smooth incidence model.
5. **Geometric-integrality trap:** prime degree fifteen over (\mathbf Q) is
   not geometrically integral.
6. **Ordering non-trap:** nonsplit hyperplane factors do not impose an extra
   quadratic cost on the unordered (D_{3,3}) line.

### Triangulation

- Voisin supplies only dominance, the fibre dimensions, and the
  (\operatorname{Sym}^2(E_3)) description.
- Sage and Macaulay2 independently certify degree (15) for the same raw
  characteristic-zero matrix.
- Three finite-field reductions test the omitted component Hilbert
  polynomials and find only (3+3).
- The abstract index lemma fixes both divisibility directions and shows that
  the correct output is parity equivalence, not a positive lift.

### Mystery ledger

- **Closed conditionally:** if the raw generic packet is the good
  type-((3,3)) packet, degree (15) makes the (V,D,Y) two-primary indices
  equal.
- **Closed for the displayed model:** its prime degree-(15) raw packet is
  type ((3,3)), by the good etale (p=5) specialization.
- **Closed for the displayed model, conditional only on recording the
  quotient restriction:** the null-correlation normal form proves stability
  and placement in (M_9).
- **Open and load-bearing:** finite-flat persistence, including exclusion of
  nonintegral degree-three pencils, on the generic marked (A_5) family.
- **Still the original gate:** index one versus two for the unordered-theta
  fibre over each relative support surface, equivalently for the required
  (M_9\to J) fibre.
