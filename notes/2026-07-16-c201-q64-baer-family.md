# C201 Gate 2 — the Frobenius-stable Baer-conic family at `q=64`

**Lane:** `relconic`

## Family and quotient

Work in `GF(64)` with binary modulus `x^6+x+1` and fixed subfield
`GF(8)`.  Start with the nine `GF(8)`-rational points of the standard conic
`XZ=Y^2`.  Extend them by two point-pairs conjugate under the relative
Frobenius `x -> x^8`.  This gives a natural Frobenius-stable family at the
corrected-bound candidate size thirteen.

Exactly 784 conjugate pairs extend the subfield conic to an eleven-arc.
Exactly 207,144 unordered pairs of those pairs give thirteen-arcs.  The
natural `PΓL(2,8)` action has 152 orbits, with orbit sizes

```text
378: 4, 756: 24, 1512: 124.
```

The orbit sizes sum to 207,144.  An orbit of size 1,512 also confirms that
the displayed translation, scaling, inversion, and subfield-Frobenius
generators realize the full order-1,512 action on this family.

## Result and obstruction

All 152 representatives have quadratic evaluation rank six.  Weighting by
orbit size, all 207,144 raw family members lie in the full-rank alternative.
They occupy 110 incidence-profile cells.  Their ordinary-uncovered sizes and
integral defects range over

```text
|U(A)| = 860..949,       6 Delta_U(A) = 5466..6000.
```

This is not the desired continuation of the low-defect `q=16` mechanism.
Every nonzero plane quadratic over `GF(64)` has at most `2q+1=129` rational
points, with equality only for two distinct rational lines.  Thus
`|U(A)|>=860` forces full rank for the elementary cardinality reason alone.
Moreover, a thirteen-arc complete outside any conic would have
`U(A)` contained in its 65 rational points.  No member of this Baer family is
even close to that necessary coverage condition.

The family therefore supplies a precise negative result: adjoining two
Frobenius pairs to a Baer-subplane conic is a tractable, symmetric source of
thirteen-arcs, but it cannot model the `q=16` rank obstruction or produce a
relative-conic candidate.  Future `q=64` families must first pass the cheap
filter `|U(A)|<=65`; quadratic rank should not be computed for families that
fail it.

## Independent checks

`probe_c201_q64_baer.py` performs the orbit reduction and records rank,
forced-hit status, uncovered size, full secant-index spectrum, scaled defect,
orbit size, and family stabilizer.  A fixed nontrivial projectivity and point
reversal preserve every recorded invariant on all 152 representatives.

`check_c201_q64_baer.cpp` independently enumerates all 207,144 raw family
members, without using the orbit quotient, and checks rank six directly from
their ordinarily uncovered loci.  The two implementations agree on the 784
legal conjugate pairs, 207,144 compatible pair-pairs, and universal full rank.
