# C855 section 3 — Paper I semantic rename plan

**Date:** 2026-08-03
**Lane:** `clebsch` (Paper I stream)
**Task:** C855 — Paper I Lean referee-artifact standards remediation
**Scope of this record:** checklist section 3 (replace manuscript-relative and false-strength API
names). **This is a plan only. Nothing was renamed, edited, built, generated, or regenerated.**

The audit behind this plan re-walked the Paper I gate closure from
`RelativeConicArcs.Gates.ClebschRigidityTrust` in the q11 certificate package and confirmed the
frozen figure of 198 project-owned modules independently.

## 1. Why every rename in this plan is one batched change

Three facts, established by the audit, force the batching:

1. **The renamed sources are in the base package, the gate is in the q11 package.** All eleven
   `PaperIOrientation*` modules live in `~/src/lean/finitegeom/RelativeConicArcs/`. The q11
   certificate package contains none of them; it reaches them through exactly one import edge,
   `import RelativeConicArcs.PaperIOrientationSpine` at line 10 of
   `RelativeConicArcs/Gates/ClebschRigidityTrust.lean`. So a base-side rename is simultaneously a
   q11-side edit, and the two repositories must move in the same window.
2. **The gate names individual declarations, not just modules.** The same gate file carries 23
   `#print axioms` lines naming orientation declarations. A declaration rename edits the gate even
   when no module is renamed.
3. **Three artifacts are build-produced or hash-bearing and cannot be hand-edited into
   consistency.** The recorded axiom audit
   `verification/clebsch_rigidity_trust/axiom-audit.txt` (23 lines) must be regenerated from a
   rebuilt gate; the base `TARGET_MANIFEST.json` and the q11 `MANIFEST.json` carry SHA-256 digests,
   module counts, root names, and a cross-repository dependency commit pin; and the manuscript-side
   `verification/trust_manifest.json` (47 orientation occurrences in one claim row) is rebuilt from
   both repositories after they are committed.

There is therefore no way to land renames incrementally without leaving the release gate red
between steps. The correct unit of work is a single owning build window that performs source
renames, gate rewiring, and manifest/audit regeneration, and validates once at the end.

## 2. Module renames — all eleven `PaperIOrientation*` modules

The family prefix `PaperI` encodes a manuscript, not an object. The mathematics is uniform: the
oriented `A₅` axis cover, the signed conference matrix it carries, the support two-graph and its
cubic, the cubic's nodes and stabilizer, and the commutant of the odd six-dimensional `A₅` module.
The proposed prefix `A5Orientation` is kept only where the object really is the orientation cover;
the downstream modules take names from what they actually compute.

| current module | proposed module | what the module actually contains |
|---|---|---|
| `PaperIOrientationCover` | `A5AxisCover` | the affine five-letter model of `A₅`, its `C₅` and `D₅` subgroups, the twelve oriented cosets `A₅/C₅` as a two-sheeted cover of the six axes `A₅/D₅`, and the two inverse-stable `C₅` double cosets |
| `PaperIOrientationPentagon` | `OrbitalConferenceMatrix` | one chosen lift above each axis, giving the symmetric signed `6×6` conference matrix; lift change is diagonal switching, orbital exchange is negation, the matrix squares to `5·I`, and the normalized pentagon sign pattern |
| `PaperIOrientationHolonomy` | `SupportTwoGraphHolonomy` | the triangle holonomy `supportSign`, its switching invariance and orbital-exchange oddness, the four-point two-graph identity, root-gauge reconstruction, vanishing of the degree-0/1/2 support moments, and translation invariance of the support cubic |
| `PaperIOrientationDeterminant` | `SupportCubicDeterminantPencil` | the principal-minor expansion of `det(M + diag x)`, the size-3/4/5/6 minor values, `det M = −125`, and the identification of the pencil's odd part with the support cubic |
| `PaperIOrientationTraceDual` | `GoldenTraceDual` | golden eigenspace bases and their orthogonality, the cross-golden compression block, `det(block) = −supportCubic`, the trace pairing and annihilator dimension count, and the cited Hassett–Tschinkel implication carried as a hypothesis |
| `PaperIOrientationNodes` | `SupportCubicNodes` | deleted `5×5` principal blocks, the exact singular locus of the support cubic, and ordinariness of the six frame nodes |
| `PaperIOrientationSymmetryCore` | `SupportCubicStabilizerCore` | the five distinguished perfect matchings, their normalizer in `S₆`, and the faithful conjugation action identified with `S₅` |
| `PaperIOrientationSymmetryGenerators` | `SupportCubicStabilizerGenerators` | three explicit frame permutations realizing a matching 3-cycle, 5-cycle, and an odd support-sign reverser |
| `PaperIOrientationSymmetry` | `SupportCubicStabilizer` | the terminal identifications: matching normalizer equals the projective stabilizer of the support-cubic line, `≃ S₅`, with the oriented stabilizer `≃ A₅` of index two |
| `PaperIOrientationCommutant` | `OddA5ModuleCommutant` | golden equivariance over the sixty coset matrices, the classical `3+3'` splitting interface, rational commutant `= ℚ[B]`, integral commutant `= ℤ[√5]` |
| `PaperIOrientationSpine` | `SupportCubicSpine` | declaration-free aggregator importing the other ten; the only module the gate imports |

The eleven modules carry 234 public declarations between them. Only seven of those declaration
names contain a manuscript reference themselves (§3); the other 227 acquire their
manuscript-relative fully-qualified names purely from the namespace, so renaming the namespaces
fixes them without touching a single declaration name.

**Namespace subtlety that must not be missed.** `SymmetryCore`, `SymmetryGenerators`, and
`Symmetry` are three files that all open the *same* namespace
`RelativeConicArcs.PaperIOrientationSymmetry`. Renaming the three files does not rename the
namespace, and renaming the namespace touches all three files. Both must be done, and the namespace
`RelativeConicArcs.PaperIOrientationSymmetry` becomes `RelativeConicArcs.SupportCubicStabilizer`.
Every other orientation module has a namespace equal to its own module name and renames one-to-one.

The remaining eight namespaces rename with their modules: `PaperIOrientationCover` →
`A5AxisCover`, `PaperIOrientationPentagon` → `OrbitalConferenceMatrix`,
`PaperIOrientationHolonomy` → `SupportTwoGraphHolonomy`, `PaperIOrientationDeterminant` →
`SupportCubicDeterminantPencil`, `PaperIOrientationTraceDual` → `GoldenTraceDual`,
`PaperIOrientationNodes` → `SupportCubicNodes`, `PaperIOrientationCommutant` →
`OddA5ModuleCommutant`. `PaperIOrientationSpine` declares nothing and has no namespace.

The import graph is a single linear spine — `Cover → Pentagon → Holonomy → Determinant →
TraceDual → Nodes → SymmetryCore → SymmetryGenerators → Symmetry → Commutant` — plus the
aggregator importing eight of them and the gate importing only the aggregator. That shape is what
makes a one-pass rename tractable: there are exactly ten intra-package import lines and one
cross-package import line to rewrite.

## 3. Manuscript-relative public declaration names

Scanning every public declaration in all 198 closure modules for `Paper`/`paper`, `Section`,
numbered `Lemma`/`Proposition`/`Theorem`/`Corollary`, `WP`, four-digit task numbers, `Appendix`,
`Figure`, and `Table` found **seven declaration-level hits, all in one module**
(`PaperIOrientationTraceDual`). There are **no** task-number, work-package, or
manuscript-section names anywhere in the closure's public surface. The eleven module names and
nine namespaces of §2 are the rest of the manuscript-relative surface.

| current declaration | proposed name | reason |
|---|---|---|
| `paperGoldenEigenspaceBasis` | `chosenGoldenEigenspaceBasis` | it is one explicit chosen integral basis of the golden eigenspace, not "the paper's" anything |
| `paperAxisOrder` | `chosenAxisOrder` | a chosen ordering of the six axes; the transport lemma is what relates it to the repository ordering |
| `paperAxisSign` | `chosenAxisSign` | a chosen sign gauge on the six axes |
| `paperConferenceMatrix` | `chosenGaugeConferenceMatrix` | the conference matrix in that chosen order-and-sign gauge |
| `paperConferenceMatrix_eq_transport` | `chosenGaugeConferenceMatrix_eq_transport` | follows its subject |
| `goldenEigenspaceBasis_eq_paper_transport` | `goldenEigenspaceBasis_eq_chosenGauge_transport` | follows its subject |
| `HassettTschinkelProposition10` | `HassettTschinkelSixNodesCriterion` | see below |

`HassettTschinkelProposition10` is a different case from the other six and deserves an explicit
decision rather than a mechanical rewrite. The number `10` is an external-literature coordinate
(Hassett–Tschinkel 2010, Section 3, Proposition 10), not a self-reference to this manuscript, so
the section 3 rule against manuscript-section prefixes does not strictly bite. The reason to
rename it anyway is that a proposition number in a public identifier goes stale if the cited
source is revised or republished, and the identifier is the one place a stale locator cannot be
corrected without a rename. The recommendation is to name it for its content and carry the exact
pinpoint in its docstring and the bibliography, where it can be corrected. **This is the one item
in the plan that is a judgement call rather than a defect repair; if the owner prefers to keep the
citation coordinate in the identifier, keep it and record the decision.**

## 4. False-strength names

Scanning the closure for public names containing `canonical`, `unique`, `exact`, `complete`,
`classification`, `reconstruct`, `optimal`, `maximal`, `sharp`, `universal`, or `normal` produced
82 declaration hits plus one namespace. Reading each statement, the large majority are *defined-term*
uses where the word names a predicate defined in the same development and the name is earned —
`CompleteOutside` (defined at `Arc.lean:136`), `SyndromeDistanceExactly` with its proved
`.unique`, `MaximalExtensionIn` as an explicit no-proper-superset filter, `existsUnique_line` as a
literal `∃!`, `catalog_complete` as a proved iff, and the `normalizer`/`normalizes` family which
matched only on the substring `normal`. Those need no change.

Six items need action.

### 4.1 `OddSixArcPrismExtraction.canonicalLabel` — the clear overclaim

```lean
/-- A canonical labelling of a six-element finset by `Fin 6`. -/
noncomputable def canonicalLabel (A : Finset (Point K)) (hcard : A.card = 6) : Fin 6 → Point K :=
  fun i => ((Finset.equivFinOfCardEq hcard).symm i : A)
```

`Finset.equivFinOfCardEq` is a `Classical.choice`-backed arbitrary bijection. There is no
canonicity, no naturality, and no invariance under the ambient projective symmetry. Everything
proved about it is choice-agnostic bookkeeping: `canonicalLabel_injective` gives injectivity from
`Equiv.symm.injective`, and `mem_iff_exists_canonicalLabel` gives surjectivity onto `A` — properties
every labelling has. A full-file scan for a choice-independence statement returns nothing.

The construction is sound: the surrounding argument only needs *some* bijection `Fin 6 ≃ A` to
import the `K₆` one-factorization combinatorics, and it is invariant under that choice. So the fix
is a rename plus a docstring, not a proof change.

| current | proposed |
|---|---|
| `canonicalLabel` | `chosenLabel` |
| `canonicalLabel_injective` | `chosenLabel_injective` |
| `mem_iff_exists_canonicalLabel` | `mem_iff_exists_chosenLabel` |
| `canonicalLabelEmbedding` | `chosenLabelEmbedding` |
| `canonicalLabel_mem_labelledArcPair` | `chosenLabel_mem_labelledArcPair` |

The new docstring must say: an arbitrary chosen bijection `Fin 6 ≃ A`; every downstream statement
is invariant under the choice, and no canonicity is claimed or proved. The same objection applies
to the bare `Finset.equivFinOfCardEq hfive` uses inside `chordColor` and `colorPoint` in the same
module — they are correct but should carry the same disclaimer in prose.

### 4.2 `canonical` qualifying a domain rather than an object

`Q11SemanticBase.canonicalSyndromeDistance` and the four `canonical_*` theorems in
`Q11SemanticSynthesis` use "canonical" to mean "on the 133 canonical projective representatives",
i.e. it qualifies the domain. That is defensible but reads to a cold referee as a claim that this
is *the* canonical notion of syndrome distance — which is exactly the misreading the rest of the
module works to prevent, since `canonicalSyndromeDistance` is a *declared* table whose agreement
with the true syndrome distance is a separate theorem.

| current | proposed |
|---|---|
| `Q11SemanticBase.canonicalSyndromeDistance` | `representativeSyndromeDistance` |
| `Q11SemanticSynthesis.canonical_syndromeDistance_exact` | `representative_syndromeDistance_exact` |
| the three other `Q11SemanticSynthesis.canonical_*` theorems | `representative_*`, following their subjects |

`Q11A5PointOrbitsData.canonicalIndex` and `Q11A5PointOrbitsArithmetic.canonicalIndexFast` are
**not** renamed: those are a genuine normal-form index of a homogeneous vector
(first-nonzero-coordinate normalization) and the word is earned.

### 4.3 The two Dye axioms read as theorems

`dye1991_brianchon_bound` and `dye1991_equality_classification` in
`RelativeConicArcs.Q11DyeAxioms` (namespace `RelativeConicArcs.ClebschDye`) are `axiom`
declarations, not theorems. The names are accurate about content; the risk is the *kind*. A
referee grepping the closure for `classification` finds an unproved axiom whose name gives no
signal that it is assumed rather than derived.

Proposal: move both under a namespace that states their status —
`RelativeConicArcs.ClebschDye.CitedAxiom.brianchon_bound` and
`...CitedAxiom.equality_classification` — rather than suffixing each name. A namespace carries the
disclosure to every use site and to the axiom audit, which is where it matters. Note this is a
naming repair only; checklist section 2 separately requires eliminating both axioms, and if that
succeeds the namespace disappears with them. Do the naming repair anyway: the elimination is a
larger piece of work and the artifact should not be misleading in the interim.

### 4.4 Stacked strength words

`Q11Coding.maximal_independent_extension_complete` stacks three strength words. Its statement is
`CompleteOutside (pointSet q11Witness ∪ S.map conicEmbedding) ∅` — ordinary projective
completeness with an empty hole set — and its docstring is careful about this. The name is
supported but unnecessarily loud. Proposed: `witness_extension_completeOutside_empty`, which says
exactly what the statement says.

`Q11A5PointOrbitsPartition.unique_six_orbit` and `unique_twelve_orbit` state uniqueness *within
the seven-orbit partition*, which the names do not convey. Proposed: `six_point_orbit_unique` and
`twelve_point_orbit_unique`.

### 4.5 A conditional terminal whose name hides its hypothesis

`PaperIOrientationTraceDual.hassettTschinkel_six_nodes_of_traceDual` is the terminal that consumes
the unproved `HassettTschinkelProposition10` hypothesis. It was not a strength-word hit, but it is
the same class of defect: the name does not show that the conclusion is conditional. Proposed:
`six_nodes_of_traceDual_of_hassettTschinkel`, following the Mathlib `_of_` convention so the
hypothesis is visible in the name. After the §3 rename of the hypothesis this becomes
`six_nodes_of_traceDual_of_hassettTschinkelSixNodesCriterion`, which is long; shortening the
hypothesis name is the better trade, and the plan should settle both names together.

### 4.6 No compatibility aliases

Checklist section 3 forbids compatibility aliases that keep misleading public names alive. None of
the renames above has a downstream consumer outside the two Lean packages and the manuscript
verification apparatus, all of which are edited in the same batch. **No `alias` declaration should
be introduced for any name in this plan.**

## 6. Dependency-ordered execution sequence for the one-window rename

Steps 1 to 6 are source edits with no build between them; the artifact is expected to be
inconsistent until step 7.

1. **Base package, source renames.** Rename the eleven files, their namespaces (remembering that
   three files share one namespace), the seven manuscript-relative declarations of §3, and the §4
   declarations in `OddSixArcPrismExtraction`, `Q11SemanticBase`, `Q11SemanticSynthesis`,
   `Q11DyeAxioms`, `Q11Coding`, and `Q11A5PointOrbitsPartition`. Rewrite the ten intra-spine
   imports and every use site of a renamed declaration. Introduce no aliases.
2. **Base package, build configuration.** Update the eleven `defaultTargets` strings in
   `lakefile.toml`.
3. **q11 package, gate rewiring.** Update the single `import` line and the 23 `#print axioms`
   declaration names in `Gates/ClebschRigidityTrust.lean`.
4. **Manuscript verification sources.** Update the hard-coded declaration list in
   `build_trust_manifest.py` and the `ODD_A5_COMMUTANT_TERMINALS` allowlist plus the
   `CLASSICAL_ODD_A5_SPLITTING` constant in `verify_trust_manifest.py`. These are the two
   hand-maintained lists; if they are skipped the release verifier fails rather than drifting
   silently, which is the desired failure mode but still a failure.
5. **Docstring repairs that are part of the same names.** Apply the `chosenLabel` disclaimer, the
   `CitedAxiom` status prose, and the conditional-terminal wording. Doing these later would change
   file bytes again and invalidate the hashes computed in step 8.
6. **Base-package elaboration through the guarded entry point.** First real build. Iterate on
   steps 1 to 5 until it is green. Nothing downstream is meaningful before this.
7. **q11 gate elaboration through the guarded entry point**, under the finite-certificate sharding
   rules. The generated q11 leaves are untouched by this plan, so no generator run is needed —
   but the generator's staleness check should still be run to prove that.
8. **Regenerate the base `TARGET_MANIFEST.json`** (sources, paths, byte counts, SHA-256 digests,
   the root name, module count) and commit the base package. The commit hash produced here is an
   input to the next step, so the order is forced.
9. **Regenerate the q11 `MANIFEST.json`** with the new base dependency commit and the new gate
   source hash, and commit the q11 package.
10. **Regenerate the axiom audit** `verification/clebsch_rigidity_trust/axiom-audit.txt` from the
    rebuilt gate and check it against the gate's terminal list one-to-one. This is the step that
    proves the declaration renames in step 3 were complete and consistent; a hand-edited audit
    would prove nothing.
11. **Rebuild `trust_manifest.json`** from both committed repositories, then run
    `verify_trust_manifest.py`.
12. **Run the full Paper I release verifier** with the explicit Lean root, then re-run the
    docstring/name/provenance scans of the companion adjudication record and require the renamed
    surface to be clean.
13. **Forward-synchronize** the manuscript-side changes to `~/src/math-papers/clebsch-rigidity` as
    an ordinary unsigned commit, and replay the standalone release gate.

Two ordering constraints are worth stating separately because they are the ones easiest to get
wrong: the axiom audit cannot be produced before the gate builds (step 10 after step 7), and the
q11 manifest cannot be produced before the base package is committed (step 9 after step 8).
