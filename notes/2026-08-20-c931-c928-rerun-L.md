   Corollary 2.10 (printed pp. 9--10), and Proposition 2.14 (printed p. 11).
   **Verification:** FVME Theorem 2.9 identifies each graded piece of the
   integral Lefschetz filtration with the relevant primitive module;
   Corollary 2.10 identifies contraction by one copy of the symplectic form on
   successive pieces with the scalar (r+1); Proposition 2.14 supplies
   integral splittings, compatible with contraction, through middle degree.

   \(\iota_\theta:\bigwedge^5\Lambda\to\bigwedge^3\Lambda\) is therefore the

   (P^1/2P^1\simeq\Lambda/2\Lambda). Thus the cited FVME results already

3. **The direct block proof is structurally complete.**
   **Locations:** PDF pp. 4--5, proof of Theorem 3.1 and (9)--(10).
   **Verification:** a degree-three monomial has either three singleton pairs
   and no full pair, or one singleton pair and one full pair. Multiplication
   by θ preserves the singleton label, so these basis subsets give disjoint
   integral blocks and exhaust the domain. The three-singleton blocks are
   primitive. For each of the (2g) choices of singleton basis vector, the
   one-singleton block is (U_n) with (n=g-1\ge3). Its displayed
   ((n-1)\)-minor is a unit; every (n\)-minor is even because the columns sum
   to zero modulo two; and the triangle rows together with
   \(\{1,k\}\), (4\le k\le n), give determinant ±2. For (n=3), the
   triangle itself is the determinant-±2 minor and the displayed unit
   (2\times2) minor still applies. Hence
   \(\operatorname{SNF}(U_n)=\operatorname{diag}(1^{n-1},2)\) in every
   claimed case. The all-edge vector has double equal to the sum of the
   vertex columns and cannot lie in the image (the triangle equations would
   force all vertex coefficients to be (1/2)); since the torsion quotient
   has order two, it generates the full defect. Finally,
   \(\theta^{[2]}\wedge e_k\) and
   \(\theta^{[2]}\wedge f_k\) are precisely these all-edge vectors, so the
   (2g) independent classes give the asserted saturation and natural map
   from Λ/2Λ.
   **Severity:** none. **Remedy:** none.

## 5. Literature boundary

FVME v1 establishes the integral Lefschetz filtration and its primitive
associated graded modules (Theorem 2.9), the exact binomial scalars for wedge
and contraction on those graded pieces (Corollary 2.10), and compatible
integral splittings through middle degree (Proposition 2.14). Their immediate
rank-ten specialization therefore establishes the manuscript's abstract
Smith multiplicities and abstract Λ/2Λ saturation defect.

The revised paper does not claim otherwise. What its Section 3 retains is the
direct basis-level realization by unsigned incidence matrices of
(K_{g-1}) and the concrete all-edge representatives
(\theta^{[2]}\wedge z). FVME does not establish the paper's Fano-labelled
geometric realization of those representatives, its fibre-product placement,
or its dual escape-lattice conclusion for the cubic-threefold theta divisor.
