** DONE Design the record and preserve the contract boundary
The shared [[file-rev:bacab8b:~/src/ergodis/crates/contract/src/rule_contract.rs][contract error]] serves grounded rules and Datalog. Keep the existing payload-free grounded =Source=, =Budget= and =Binding= variants for this bounded task. Add Datalog-specific typed variants that carry plain contract records, with no presentation text in the records. A budget record identifies bound, object, limit, observed or projected value, unit, optional round and optional knob. Source and binding records name the offending rule, relation or fact and a typed failed requirement. Give every refusal site a record; the private driver adds layer and source context without losing the core reason. The existing derivation and ranked =Rejection= payloads are already typed and should stay so.

The evaluator creates records only on refusal branches. Its row-capacity event is a per-relation, per-round attempted next row, distinct from zero configured rows, workspace address reservation and the round guard. A workspace reservation above 2^34 bytes is not a row-capacity instruction to raise =max_rows=. Public checker =direct_limit= is clamped to its own 2^26 direct-array ceiling independently of evaluator bounds. Route ceilings remain =REL0503= records with found, limit and source span; private record and checker disagreement errors carry typed attribution.

Review found that =Demand::workspace= also propagates a payload-free =Budget= if an individual =Pages::zeroed= reservation fails below the 2^34 aggregate policy. This is an external reservation failure with no knowable numeric system limit: the record needs a distinct bound and an optional limit, with the projected requested bytes and =max_rows= as the knob that can reduce the request. Do not synthesize a 2^34 breach. Conversely, the Datalog wire door calls =encode_source= only to serialize; the grounded path applies the 1 MiB =MAX_BYTES= check later. C1206 must not add a new wire-size admission policy while structuring refusals.

Red-team cases: zero =max_rows=, a workspace reservation that exceeds address policy before evaluation, capacity at exactly the next row, a round guard, independently excessive relations/rules/variables/domain/prepared bytes, malformed wire and prepared rule/atom/fact, core source identity mismatch, all three private 2^22 ceilings, a requested workspace too small, wrong record field, and the first differing tuple in a named relation. Refusal ordering and canonical prepared decoding must remain intact. A successful evaluator loop may not gain an allocation, branch, call or state field from the record.

For C1207 to revisit: the public contract temporarily keeps grounded payload-free variants beside Datalog-specific variants; the runtime =Reservation= limit is unknown (=None=) because the OS exposes no numeric ceiling; private =RouteBudget= carries both the existing =u32= =REL0503= fields and a =u64= projection; a product projected at =u64::MAX= means at least that many tuples. The checker =Rejection= enums remain distinct from source-admission refusals. This task does not choose a general rendering or versioned error schema.
