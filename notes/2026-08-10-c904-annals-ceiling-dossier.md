## Executive verdict

The broad rational story is not the crown.  Roulleau already owns the six
geometric ingredients separately--the $D_5$ elliptic fibrations--and already
states that the $A_5$ intermediate Jacobian is isogenous to $E^5$.
Popov--Zarhin and the group-algebra literature own the general rational
finite-group mechanism.

The new-looking exact core is instead integral, polarized, and
cross-geometric:

1. the six Roulleau axes have Rosati Gram matrix $6I-J$;
2. any five give polarization type $(1,6,6,6,6)$ and an isogeny of degree
   $6^4$ to the principally polarized intermediate Jacobian;
3. the $3$-primary coefficient discriminant is exactly the Winger $V_4$
   module, uniquely up to sign, while the $2$-primary discriminant is a
   different simple module with endomorphism field $\mathbf F_4$;
4. a scalar diagonal-$A_5$ cubic--Winger cycle is impossible, and the minimal
   repair is a twenty-dimensional representation-valued carrier generated by
   one thirty-element $A_5/C_2$ orbit; and
5. the quadratic splitting cover turns the shared multiplicity factor into
   the same elliptic motive, while its deck sign explains the exact
   $\Gamma_1(3)$ versus $\Gamma_0(3)$ distinction.

The integral red team finds a second, independent quadratic character at
two.  The twist cover has square class $D(T)$, while the sign of the full
mod-two monodromy has square class $T$.  Their composite is a genus-one base,
not the rational twist-splitting conic.  This is now the likely natural base
for a fully trivialized integral correspondence.  Arithmetically, that
genus-one curve has rank zero and only its two points at infinity over
$\mathbf Q$: no rational pencil member splits both obstructions at once.

This is already a strong high-end theorem package.  It becomes a credible
Annals theorem only after the marked cycles are constructed relatively, the
actual principal-polarization gluing kernel is determined, and descent,
boundary, and Hecke actions are proved geometrically.

## 1. The exact six-axis polarization theorem

Let $S$ be the Fano surface of a generic $A_5$ cubic threefold and
$(J,\Theta)=\operatorname{Alb}(S)$ its principally polarized intermediate
Jacobian.  For each of the six $D_5$ subgroups $H\subset A_5$, Roulleau's five
involution curves sum to a fibre

\[
F_H=\sum_{g\in H,\ o(g)=2}D_g
\]

of a map $S\to E_H$.  Write $q_H:J\to E_H$ for the induced Albanese quotient
and $i_H:E_H\to J$ for its Rosati dual.

Among the fifteen involutions, the $105$ unordered distinct pairs have product
orders $2,3,5$ in counts $15,30,60$.  Roulleau's intersection table therefore
gives

\[
D_{\rm tot}^2=15(-4)+2(30\cdot2+60\cdot1)=180.
\]

Every involution lies in two $D_5$ subgroups, so
$\sum_HF_H=2D_{\rm tot}$.  Two-transitivity on the six axes and $F_H^2=0$
then give

\[
30(F_HF_{H'})=(2D_{\rm tot})^2=720,
\qquad F_HF_{H'}=24\quad(H\ne H').
\]

At a generic non-CM fibre, coherently identify the six elliptic curves.  The
$D_5$-fixed line in $W_5$ is the trivial, not sign, character, so the orbit is
honest.  Put

\[
q_Hi_H=[d],\qquad q_Hi_{H'}=[m]\quad(H\ne H').
\]

The diagonal six-axis sum is the absent trivial $A_5$ constituent, hence
$d+5m=0$.  The minimal-class identity $[S]=\Theta^3/3!$ and the polarized
Riemann--Roch identity

\[
\frac{\alpha\beta\Theta^{g-2}}{(g-2)!}
=\operatorname{tr}(N_\alpha)\operatorname{tr}(N_\beta)
-\operatorname{tr}(N_\alpha N_\beta)
\]

give $24=d^2-m^2$.  Therefore

\[
d=5,\qquad m=-1.
\]

Thus the six-axis matrix and any five-axis minor are

\[
G_6=6I_6-J_6,
\qquad
G_5=6I_5-J_5.
\]

Consequently

\[
\operatorname{SNF}(G_5)=(1,6,6,6,6),
\qquad \det G_5=6^4.
\]

The induced five-axis map $E^5\to J$ has degree $6^4=1296$, and the pullback
of the principal polarization has type $(1,6,6,6,6)$.

This conclusion does **not** by itself determine the finite kernel group
scheme.  The kernel is a maximal isotropic half of the pulled-back
polarization kernel; Smith form and degree alone do not select that half.

## 2. The level-three bridge and the level-two obstruction

Let $L=\mathbf Z^6/\mathbf Z(1,\ldots,1)$ be the six-axis coefficient lattice
with simplex form.  Its coefficient discriminant has abstract Smith group

\[
L^*/L\cong(\mathbf Z/6)^4.
\]

For $p\mid6$, the relevant modular heart is

\[
H_{6,p}=\operatorname{Aug}(\mathbf F_p^6)/\langle(1,\ldots,1)\rangle.
\]

Exact intertwiner calculations for a generating pair of $A_5$ prove:

- at $p=3$, $H_{6,3}$ is isomorphic to the Winger deleted-permutation module
  $V_4\otimes\mathbf F_3$; the Hom space is one-dimensional, so the
  identification is unique up to sign;
- at $p=2$, there is no nonzero map from $H_{6,2}$ to the Winger module;
- both modules in each comparison are simple; and
- $\operatorname{End}_{A_5}(H_{6,2})\cong\mathbf F_4$, whereas the Winger
  module has endomorphism ring $\mathbf F_2$.

Dualizing gives the same conclusion for the coefficient discriminant.
Therefore the exact common integral shadow is $3$-primary, not a full
mod-$6$ identification.  This is a useful impossibility theorem: the
six-axis discriminant cannot simply be called the Winger lattice modulo six.

There is a sharper global no-go.  Since
$\operatorname{End}_{A_5}(H_{6,2})=\mathbf F_4$, the five simple
$A_5$-submodules of $H_{6,2}^{\oplus2}$ are parametrized by
$\mathbf P^1(\mathbf F_4)$.  Full mod-two elliptic monodromy is
$\operatorname{GL}_2(\mathbf F_2)\cong S_3$.  Its action on those five points
has orbits of sizes three and two and no fixed point.  Hence no
$A_5\times\operatorname{GL}_2(\mathbf F_2)$-stable half-dimensional simple
submodule exists.  A globally trivial product model for the two-primary
six-axis gluing is therefore impossible over the full $X_0(3)$ base.

The two-point orbit is $\mathbf P^1(\mathbf F_4)\setminus
\mathbf P^1(\mathbf F_2)$, and $S_3$ acts on it through its sign quotient.
Thus a quadratic cover kills precisely this obstruction.  Section 4
identifies that cover as $r^2=T$.

The positive $3$-part fits the geometry exceptionally tightly.  The full
$\Gamma_0(3)$ monodromy has a unique invariant line in elliptic $3$-torsion.
Since the Winger module is simple with scalar endomorphisms, an
$A_5$-stable maximal isotropic $3$-gluing, if it is the expected tensor
gluing, is forced to be

\[
(V_4\otimes\mathbf F_3)\otimes\ell,
\]

where $\ell\subset E[3]$ is that cyclic line.  A $\Gamma_1(3)$ marking chooses
a nonzero generator of $\ell$; the quadratic central twist forgets its sign
and leaves precisely the $\Gamma_0(3)$ line.  Turning this forced candidate
into the actual kernel is a remaining geometric/integral proof gate.

## 3. The obstruction and optimal correspondence carrier

The two carrier representations are inequivalent:

\[
\operatorname{Hom}_{A_5}(V_4,W_5)=0.
\]

Hence no nonzero scalar diagonal-$A_5$ correspondence can directly identify
the Winger and cubic constituents.  This is a fundamental mathematical
impossibility, not a missing trick.

The minimal repair is representation-valued:

\[
W_5\otimes V_4^\vee\longrightarrow
\operatorname{Corr}^2(C_w,X_T).
\]

Its dimension is twenty.  A concrete carrier is indexed by the thirty pairs

\[
(D_5\text{ subgroup},A_4\text{ subgroup}),
\]

which form one $A_5/C_2$ orbit.  The five row and six column augmentations
have one shared relation, leaving $30-(5+6-1)=20$ directions.  Exact character
calculation gives

\[
W_5\otimes V_4=E_6\oplus V_4\oplus2W_5.
\]

The orbit has a unique nontrivial equivariant involution because
$N_{A_5}(C_2)/C_2=C_2$.  Under the marked comparison with the thirty
Looijenga--Zi edge cycles, it is the sign operation $\delta\mapsto-\delta$.

Over the quadratic splitting field of

\[
D(T)=(T+27)(T-729/5),\qquad T=5w-27,
\]

the two extracted elliptic multiplicity motives agree, giving the rational
power isogeny

\[
J(X_T)^4\sim A_V(C_w)^5.
\]

The unsplit statement carries the character of $D(T)$.  The rational-Chow
carrier and power isogeny are developed in
`notes/2026-08-10-c904-cubic-winger-motivic-correspondence.md`; arithmetic
descent and an integral relative-cycle theorem remain open.

## 4. Exact modular and boundary structure

The twist-splitting conic is rational:

\[
T(z)=\frac{27(27z^2+5)}{5(z^2-1)},\qquad
\eta(z)=\frac{864z}{5(z^2-1)},\qquad \eta^2=D(T).
\]

Its deck map $z\mapsto-z$ is the twist cocycle $[-1]$.  Boundary preimages are

```text
T=-27 <- z=0,       T=729/5 <- z=infinity,
T=infinity <- z=+1,-1,       T=0 <- 27z^2+5=0.
```

After the twist is split, the pulled-back Tate surface has fibres
$2I_1+2I_3+IV$, root lattice $A_2^3$, and Mordell--Weil rank two; the
Oguiso--Shioda classification identifies the lattice as
$A_2^*\oplus\mathbf Z/3$.

The two-division cubic of the universal Tate model is

\[
4x^3+(T+27)^2x^2+2(T+27)^3x+(T+27)^4,
\]

with discriminant

\[
16T(T+27)^8.
\]

Hence the sign of the mod-two $S_3$ monodromy has square class $T$ and is
trivialized by the independent quadratic cover $r^2=T$.  It is not the twist
cover $\eta^2=D(T)$.  The composite cover has the genus-one model

\[
v^2=(u^2+27)(u^2-729/5),
\]

whose Jacobian has

\[
j=357911/2160.
\]

Taking either rational point at infinity as origin gives the birational
Weierstrass model

\[
Y^2=X\left(X^2+\frac{1188}{5}X+\frac{746496}{25}\right),
\]

with global minimal model

\[
y^2+xy=x^3-x^2+333x-7259.
\]

Exact two-descent gives conductor $450$, Mordell--Weil rank zero, and torsion
$\mathbf Z/2$.  The identity and the nonzero two-torsion point correspond to
the two points at infinity of the quartic.  Hence the composite cover has no
affine rational point.  Equivalently, there is no finite
$T\in\mathbf Q$ for which both $T$ and $D(T)$ are rational squares.

Thus the rational motive comparison lives over the $D$-cover, but a fully
trivialized integral six-axis gluing should be sought over this biquadratic,
genus-one cover.  Whether the geometry descends further through a nontrivial
two-adic cocycle is now a precise question.

For odd $N$, sign-twisted descent changes a marked generator into its
unoriented cyclic subgroup.  More exactly,

\[
\langle\Gamma_1(N),-I\rangle=\Gamma_0(N)
\]

if and only if every unit modulo $N$ is $\pm1$, equivalently
$N\in\{1,2,3,4,6\}$.  Thus $N=3$ is the unique nontrivial odd-prime level at
which orientation-forgetting is exactly the passage from $\Gamma_1$ to
$\Gamma_0$.

Fricke acts on $q=z^2$ by

\[
q\longmapsto\frac{-5q}{22q+5}.
\]

It does not lift to an automorphism of the splitting conic: the chordal branch
$T=729/5$ maps to the smooth value $T=5$.  A successful geometric Fricke
theorem must therefore be a correspondence between covers or models, not an
automorphism of this one cover.

## 5. General simplex-polarization theorem

The six-axis calculation is the first nontrivial instance of an elementary
general theorem.

Let a principally polarized abelian $(n-1)$-fold carry an honest
two-transitive orbit of $n$ elliptic quotient maps whose dual axes span the
augmentation representation and sum to zero.  Suppose, at a generic non-CM
point,

\[
q_i i_i=[d],\qquad q_i i_j=[m]\quad(i\ne j).
\]

Then $d+(n-1)m=0$, so for a positive integer $s$,

\[
d=(n-1)s,\qquad m=-s.
\]

The full Rosati matrix is therefore

\[
s(nI_n-J_n).
\]

If $c$ is the normalized mixed intersection supplied by the minimal-class
identity, then

\[
c=d^2-m^2=n(n-2)s^2.
\]

Any $(n-1)$-axis minor has

\[
\operatorname{SNF}=\bigl(s,\underbrace{sn,\ldots,sn}_{n-2}\bigr),
\qquad
\det=s^{n-1}n^{n-2}.
\]

The factor $n^{n-2}$ is Cayley's number of labeled spanning trees: the minor
is the reduced Laplacian of the complete graph $K_n$.  Thus the isogeny degree
is literally a spanning-tree determinant.

This gives three reusable consequences.

1. A proposed $n$-axis configuration is impossible unless
   $c/[n(n-2)]$ is an integer square.
2. Pair intersections alone recover the full polarization scale, type, and
   isogeny degree.
3. For every prime $p\mid n$, the $p$-torsion coefficient discriminant is
   controlled by the modular heart
   $\operatorname{Aug}(\mathbf F_p^n)/\langle\mathbf1\rangle$; its
   endomorphism field and the elliptic mod-$p$ monodromy decide whether a
   global maximal-isotropic gluing can exist without a cover.

This formal theorem is not claimed as literature-new.  Its value is that it
turns the six-axis geometry into a general local-to-global and impossibility
machine, and isolates exactly where group-specific modular representation
theory enters.

## 7. The credible Annals theorem

The target should be a single theorem, not a menu of formulas:

> Over the minimal cover of the common $X_0(3)$ base that trivializes both the
> quadratic twist character and the mod-two six-axis sign character, the marked
> $A_5$ cubic and Winger families admit a minimal $A_5/C_2$ orbit of relative
> algebraic correspondences.  These realize their common elliptic
> multiplicity motive, determine the principal-polarization gluing of the six
> $D_5$ elliptic quotients, and identify its forced level-three part with the
> Winger defect lattice.  The only descent obstruction is the explicit
> quadratic character $D$; Fricke and degree-three Hecke are induced by
> geometric correspondences with controlled limiting action at every
> boundary fibre.

The following are mandatory proof gates:

1. construct the six $q_H$ and their duals functorially over the smooth base;
2. prove the honest six-axis orientation and extend the polarization theorem
   through CM fibres;
3. determine the actual finite kernel group scheme, not merely its degree and
   ambient polarization discriminant, and decide whether the genus-one
   composite cover is minimal;
4. identify the $3$-primary gluing with the Looijenga--Zi edge lattice by an
   explicit integral incidence map;
5. construct the thirty marked cycles relatively and prove their saturation;
6. descend them arithmetically and exclude an extra constant character;
7. compare limiting mixed Hodge structures or Neron models at
   $I_1,II,I_0^*,I_3$; and
8. realize Fricke and Hecke as geometric correspondences and compute their
   action on the relative motive.

Without gates 3--8, the result is a strong specialist/high-end paper rather
than an Annals theorem.

## 8. Highest-EV proof attacks

1. **Prym-to-axis normalization.**  Use the degree-five quotient diagram of
   van Geemen--Yamauchi to compute the induced polarization of their explicit
   elliptic curve inside $J$.  Match it with the degree-five Roulleau axis,
   fixing the integral endpoint and every residual isogeny.
2. **Edge-incidence gluing.**  Write the $30\times(6,5)$ incidence maps for
   the $(D_5,A_4)$ orbit and compare their mod-three saturation directly with
   Looijenga--Zi's edge cycles.  Uniqueness of the $A_5$ intertwiner reduces
   the comparison to one nonzero geometric test.
3. **Kernel from monodromy.**  Classify $A_5\times\Gamma_0(3)$-stable maximal
   isotropics in the polarization discriminant.  The $3$-part should be
   forced; the $2$-part is the diagnostic for the correct integral model.
4. **Boundary first.**  Construct the correspondence over the Neron models
   at the four singular fibres.  If the limiting maps are primitive, relative
   algebraicity and saturation may follow with less ambiguity than a generic
   period comparison.
5. **Hecke from quotient composition.**  Compose the cyclic order-three
   quotient with its dual on the extracted elliptic factor, then lift through
   the thirty-cycle carrier.  This avoids seeking a nonexistent direct
   $A_5$ map $V_4\to W_5$.

## 9. Mystery ledger: explicit ej + tt closeout

- **Settled:** Why the two pencils see different congruence groups.  A central
  sign twist forgets the orientation of the order-three generator, and level
  three is the unique odd-prime case where this is exactly $\Gamma_0$.
- **Settled:** Why a direct invariant cubic--Winger cycle never appeared.
  The carrier Hom space is zero; twenty coefficient directions are minimal.
- **Settled:** Why six axes carry unexpectedly rigid numbers.  Roulleau's
  fibre intersections plus the minimal class force $d=5,m=-1$, hence
  $6I-J$ and degree $6^4$.
- **Settled and generalized:** An honest $n$-axis configuration always has
  complete-graph Laplacian polarization; its isogeny degree contains Cayley's
  tree number $n^{n-2}$, and its pair intersection must be
  $n(n-2)$ times a square.
- **Settled:** Whether the full discriminant is simply Winger mod six.  It is
  not: the match is exact at three and fails at two.
- **Settled in representation theory, open geometrically:** The mod-two
  $\mathbf F_4$ module forces a second sign cover $r^2=T$; together with the
  twist cover it gives a genus-one base.  What geometric datum on the six
  quotient curves realizes this cocycle remains open.
- **Settled arithmetically:** The composite genus-one base has conductor 450,
  rank zero, and torsion $\mathbf Z/2$; its rational points are only the two
  points at infinity.  Thus no rational smooth fibre splits both characters.
- **Open--central:** Prove that the unique mod-three intertwiner is the actual
  Looijenga--Zi edge-incidence map and selects the principal gluing kernel.
- **Open--boundary:** Explain geometrically why Fricke sends a branch point of
  the splitting cover to a smooth point; this may force a correspondence of
  compactifications rather than a map of covers.
- **Open--arithmetic:** Determine exact fields of definition and group schemes
  in characteristics two, three, and five.  No characteristic claim is made
  here.
- **Open--priority:** MathSciNet, Google Scholar, and zbMATH closure remains.
  Owner: Paper V claim--proof--novelty audit before manuscript promotion.
