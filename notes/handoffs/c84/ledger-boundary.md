# C84 facet: ledger applicability boundary

**Lane**: `cap`

## Domain distinction

| C63/C77 residual-grid ledger | C84 conic-only Schreier game |
|---|---|
| Retains the frame and burned row/column directions. | Retains off-conic centres and the induced live conic graph. |
| Defines a row/column reservoir floor and off-conic `zone_v`. | Has no canonical copy of that reservoir. |
| Conic xor is one component of a larger game state. | Conic xor is the whole P/N value, so using xor-zero is circular. |
| Ledger may support the C74/full-grid program. | Direct ledger transport is not a C84 strategy theorem. |

This is an applicability boundary, not a refutation of the ledger in its native domain.

## Exact C84 audit

A deliberately naive proxy counted all legal off-conic projective points, inserted that count into
the C63 floor formula, and omitted the circular xor term. Over the 470 forced class-D replies,
winning proxy descent was:

```text
q=13: 30/30
q=17: 57/58
q=19:  1/62
q=23:  0/125
q=29:  0/195
```

At q13 every losing alternative descended too. At q29 greedy maximum drain selected only 72/195
winning replies, and the full seven-feature proxy tuple collided with a losing reply in 96/195
obligations.

Therefore defect, drain, and the naive reservoir proxy are accounting data, not a field-stable
C84 response contract. Adaptive ledger mining is closed here.
