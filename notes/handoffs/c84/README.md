## Essential state

C84 asks whether P-valued conic-only residuals have q-uniform positive density when a fixed
rooted-S4 triple is extended by a legal fourth off-conic centre. The exact Schreier catalogue is a
boundary evaluator, and generic abundance remains plausible: the mirror-free q=29 class-D fibre
has exactly `139/753` P children.

C84 does not prove `(ON)` or `PG(2,q)=P`; its abundant children are mostly off-conic. Transfer to an
on-conic P child is a separate theorem.

Pairing, bounded one-reply cores, fixed involution words, rooted-S4 double-coset packets, and direct
ledger transport are closed as uniform C84 mechanisms. Adaptive finite-template and feature
mining is retired.

## Final decision

The three-candidate dossier found no schema that passes the deterministic, complexity, dimension,
and counting gates. An exact bounded check also found zero two-ply adaptive-pairing certificates
among all 753 q=29 class-D roots. C84 is therefore stopped and deprioritized; do not replace the
missing certificate with another census or classifier.

theorem, or a formally specified certificate class that evades the recorded obstruction.
