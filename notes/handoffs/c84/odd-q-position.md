# C84 facet: position in the odd-q conjecture

**Lane**: `cap`

## Logical chain

For odd q, the projective-plane goal reduces to the residual size-3 escape statement. The stronger
target `(ON)` asks for a P-valued fourth child on the canonical conic through the residual frame.

C84 studies a different but related population: off-conic fourth centres whose induced
**conic-only** residual graph is P. Its target is abundance in that population.

```text
C84 positive-density off-conic conic-only P children
        + separate exchange / small-bad-set theorem
        -> an on-conic P child for every size-3 state
        -> (ON)
        -> odd-q projective-plane P
```

The first arrow is open. C84 abundance alone does not establish `(ON)`.

## What C84 contributes

- Generic, mirror-free residuals remain P-rich through the q=29 class-D census.
- The P fraction does not show a corresponding collapse at the depleted on-conic orders q=11 and
  q=17, supporting the interpretation that depletion is a witness-location phenomenon rather than
  generic absence of P residuals.
- The Schreier description supplies a uniform graph family in which an abundance theorem can be
  stated precisely.

## What remains outside C84

- transfer from off-conic abundance to `(ON)`;
- extension from rooted class D to all residual triple types;
- characteristic/subfield exceptions; and
- the final game-semantic assembly proving `PG(2,q)=P`.

Do not claim progress on these from a C84 density result without the corresponding theorem.
