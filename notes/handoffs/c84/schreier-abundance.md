# C84 facet: Schreier family and abundance target

**Lane**: `cap`

## Exact family

Fix a rooted class-D triple `T=(x₁,x₂,x₃)` generating `H≅S4`. For each legal fourth off-conic
centre y, let `R_y` be the conic graph generated by the four projection involutions after deleting
their fixed and dead vertices. Conic-only play is Node--Kayles on `R_y`.

Let `Y_D(q)` be the legal fourth centres and

```text
P_D(q) = {y in Y_D(q) : 𝒢(R_y)=0}.
```

The class-D target is a q-uniform `c>0`, up to proved small exceptions, such that

```text
#P_D(q) >= c * #Y_D(q).
```

Only after class D is proved should the program extend to the other rooted S4 triple types.

## Established evidence

- The small-subgroup `V₄`, `D₈`, and `S₄` catalogue is exact but only evaluates the boundary; legal
  escape children enter generic PSL/PGL subgroups.
- The mirror-free q=29 class-D fibre has `139/753` P children.
- Exact class-D P fractions remain positive over the tracked q=13,17,19,23,29 corpus.

These are finite observations, not a density theorem.

## Model caution

The varying fourth involution is not an independent random matching. Its matching, fixed points,
dead-vertex deletion, and interaction with the rooted-S4 action are all correlated through y.
Erdős--Rényi or independent-matching heuristics are not the target distribution.

Spectral, orbital, character-sum, or equidistribution estimates become relevant only after a
deterministic P-certificate event is stated. See the
[certificate-density gate](certificate-density-gate.md).
