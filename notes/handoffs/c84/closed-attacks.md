# C84 facet: closed attacks and re-entry conditions

**Lane**: `cap`

These are mechanism negatives, not evidence against the odd-q outcome.

| Attack | Exact conclusion | Re-entry condition | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pairing/mirror | q=29 class D has no pairing-certified child, yet 139 children are P. | A genuinely different certificate, not a larger automorphism search. | [pairing obstruction](../../2026-07-17-c84-pairing-obstruction.md) |
| Immediate bounded core | Every q=29 P root has a worst first move whose winning replies leave a component of size at least 14. | A multi-step or non-component quotient with a theorem-level descent. | [adaptive core](../../2026-07-17-c84-adaptive-core.md) |
| Fixed coloured word | All 88 forced-reply word patterns also occur on losing replies. | Context must enter through a proved strategy rule, not another word table. | [word obstruction](../../2026-07-17-c84-forced-reply-algebra.md) |
| Rooted-S4 double-coset packet | No q=23 or q=29 P root is fully covered by pure packets; only one q=29 forced reply is a singleton packet. | A certificate not obtained by refining the same one-ply packet. | [double-coset obstruction](../../2026-07-17-c84-double-coset-packets.md) |
| Residual-grid ledger transport | The C63 reservoir is absent from the conic-only state; noncircular shared measures have no field-stable contract. | Work in the full grid domain, or state a new C84-native global theorem. | [ledger boundary](ledger-boundary.md) |

Also closed for C84: near-injective Fricke signatures, static feature refinement, greedy drain,
and enlarged finite selector libraries. A bounded raw-state automaton is **unsupported and
deprioritized, not proved impossible**: the measured quotient grows from q=11 to q=13. Re-entry
requires a theorem-level quotient and a reason its state count is q-uniform, not a larger census.

Do not reopen an attack by adding features, increasing a template bound, or running another q. A
re-entry proposal must state the new theorem shape and explain why the cited obstruction does not
apply.
