period-(n) orbit in the whole correction.  An outer orbit can cycle the
\(r-1\) correction copies.  The corrected nonsplit law factors the total
period as the outer-label period times the period of the actual return map on
one fibre.

and surface cases, so codimensions three through five contribute no marked

occurrence.  For composite packet lengths,
smaller return periods can survive after a nontrivial outer orbit; they remain
part of the all-stabilizations gate.
