`O(sqrt(q))` over an infinite odd tower of characteristic-two fields — is
closed: the surviving two-repair-coset mechanism is proved collision-forcing
for `q>=32768`, with the factorization and degenerate strata closed at
`q>=512`. Every task in that chain (C210, C297–C349) and the earlier
zero-defect/matching-design program that fed it (C554–C667) is complete.
**Do not reopen that construction question or recompute any of its closed
tasks; use them as citations, not open work.**

(`papers/arcs_complete_outside_conic/`), carries the completed exact

independent cold-read review (C900, below), not yet publicly released.

## Tool literature boundary

The geometric baseline is already in
[`arcs_complete_outside_conic.tex`](../../papers/arcs_complete_outside_conic/arcs_complete_outside_conic.tex):
the introduction defines `C`-completeness and distinguishes it from
saturating sets, almost-complete subsets of a conic, line-covering arcs,
hyperfocused arcs, and complete exterior sets, and records the Kim–Vu bound
`rho_C(q) <= sqrt(q)*(log q)^c` against the paper's matching `sqrt(q)` defect
lower scale.

For a fixed parameter specialization, prefer a normalization plus
Hasse–Weil with an explicit genus bound: [H. Stichtenoth, *Algebraic
Function Fields and Codes*, 2nd ed., GTM 254, Springer, 2009](https://link.springer.com/book/10.1007/978-3-540-76878-4),
DOI `10.1007/978-3-540-76878-4`, Proposition 3.7.8 (Artin–Schreier
ramification/genus) and Theorem 5.2.3 (Hasse–Weil). Use [Y. Aubry and M.
Perret, "A Weil theorem for singular curves," 1996](https://doi.org/10.1515/9783110811056.1),
DOI `10.1515/9783110811056.1`, only when point counts are taken directly on
a singular projective model rather than its normalization. Do not invoke
general Lang–Weil as a bare label without recording the constant field,
absolute irreducibility, genus/degree, removed points, and the resulting
extension threshold.
