## Goal and current frontier

Construct `C`-complete arcs of size `O(sqrt(q))` over an infinite odd tower of
characteristic-two fields, or prove that the surviving two-repair-coset
mechanism is collision-forcing. The only live frontier is the `a!=0` cover.
Do not reopen the closed single-quadratic graph census, fixed-coefficient scalar
extensions, `a=b=0`, or `a=0,b!=0` unless a new result directly contradicts a
committed certificate.

On the generic scope `a*delta*N*b*p != 0`, with

    theta = w^2+w+1,
    N = a^2+a+1,
    Q = u^2+u*delta+delta^2,
    G1 = u^2+u*p+p^2*theta,
    G2 = u^3+u^2*delta+u*p^2*theta+delta*p^2*theta+delta^2*p,
    G2a = G2+delta*a*G1,
    psi = tau^2+b*Q*tau,
    sigma = a*delta*N*G1*G2a,

the collision cover has the exact form

    F = psi^2 + sigma*psi + R1,       R1 = a^2*Q^2*B0.

The alternate quadratic slope is excluded on this scope, so this single
Artin--Schreier form owns the generic factorization locus. The residue system
has three `h1`-free branches:

1. `e=0`, `h0=0`;
2. `e=delta`,
   `h0=p^2*(w^2+w+1)+e^2+e*b+e*a*p`;
3. `delta=p`, `w in {0,1}`,
   `h0=e^2*a^2+e*a^2*p+e*a*p+e^2+e*b+e*p`.

These are certified branches of the polynomial residue system. Do not call
them original-cover components until the exact split packet below passes.
Branch 3 lies entirely on the merged-pole boundary `K1*K2=0`, where

    K1 = p^2*w^2+delta*p*w+p^2*w+delta^2+p^2,
    K2 = K1+delta*p.

Generic residue equivalence does not certify it.
