## Search discipline

Each round must produce at least one of:

- a general identity or theorem;
- a strict operational consequence or separating example;
- explanatory compression that replaces a census or ad hoc calculation;
- a decisive negative that kills a tempting route.

A name match, generic connection, unstructured q=9 table, or restatement of the complete Boolean
port does not pass. Every task has a bounded first probe and an explicit stop condition. The order
is C226 -> C227 -> C228 so that later questions consume the cleanest invariant exposed earlier.

### C227 — pointed Tutte or matroid-perspective identification

For a distinguished nonloop `x`, form the rank-jump subset polynomial using
`epsilon_x(A) = r(A union {x}) - r(A)`. Compare its exact subset expansion and
deletion--contraction law with the natural perspective between deletion and contraction, identify
the repair-reliability specialization, and test uniform, cubic q=9, and harmonic q=9 examples.

**Success gate:** a standard identification or genuinely compressed pointed refinement that imports
at least one useful consequence: duality, a sum/composition law, a bounded-branchwidth algorithm,
or a coefficient/enumerator identity. Stop if the object merely re-encodes the full Boolean truth
table or if a standard-polynomial name imports no theorem.

**Success gate:** a separating example with a general gauge-invariant explanation, or a structural
criterion showing exactly when multiplicativity can or cannot see holonomy. If the pair agrees and
the symbolic criterion exposes no holonomy dependence, record the negative boundary and stop; do
