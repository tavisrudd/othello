# C909 — six-axis saturation versus rank-five defect: priority and sharpness audit

Date: 2026-08-12

Status: bounded primary-source and local-lattice audit; no manuscript, PDF,
mirror, Lean, or certificate edit.

## Executive verdict

The proposed pair is mathematically sharp after one scope repair:

* For the **actual six-axis** presentation with polarization
  (6I_5-J_5), the marked graph calculation gives
  (operatorname{Hdg}^{2k}=P^k) in every degree at non-CM fibres.  At CM
  fibres the safe statement is equality for the prescribed graph NS lattice,
  not automatically for the enlarged full NS lattice.
* For a neighboring **one-depth rank-five** finite-etale graph tower with
  five distinct residual roots, the ambient quotient is exactly
  [
    operatorname{Hdg}^{4}/P^2cong
    operatorname{Hdg}^{6}/P^3cong(mathbf Z/p^a)^5,
    qquad Q^k=0 (k=0,1,4,5).
  ]
  The tower can be polarized-indecomposable and arbitrarily deep under the
  separately verified trace-form/graph hypotheses.

This is a sharp internal contrast, not a specialization of the actual
`6I-J` packet: the rank-five comparison has equal depth and five distinct
roots, whereas `6I-J` has a unit line plus a depth-one block whose roots are
repeated at the bad primes.

## 1. Classical predecessor and exact novelty boundary

J. S. Milne, *Lefschetz classes on abelian varieties*, Duke Math. J. 96
(1999), DOI `10.1215/S0012-7094-99-09620-5`, Theorem 3.2, proves that the
classes fixed by the Lefschetz group (S(A)) are exactly the classes in the
(mathbf Q)-algebra generated by divisor classes.  Milne's summary gives
the theorem explicitly at
`https://jmilne.org/math/articles/1999a.html`.

For a non-CM elliptic curve (E), the Hodge/Lefschetz group of (E^g) is
the diagonal rank-two symplectic group, so the rational Hodge algebra of a
power and its rational isogeny class is divisor-generated.  This is the
classical rational predecessor.  It says only

[
  operatorname{Hdg}^{2k}(A,mathbf Q)=P^k_Aotimesmathbf Q;

]

it does not compute the finite integral quotient

[
  Delta_A^k=operatorname{Hdg}^{2k}(A,mathbf Z)/P_A^k.
]

Moonen–Zarhin's Hodge-ring results and Moonen–Polishchuk's divided-power/
Fourier results are adjacent rational or Chow/Fourier inputs, not a theorem
for this weighted finite-etale graph lattice.  Beckmann–de Gaay Fortman's
integral Fourier results likewise concern integral Hodge questions for
one-cycles and do not compute the present ordinary divisor-product image.
No exact predecessor for the conjunction

```text
actual marked finite-etale graph kernel
 + exact weighted integral Hodge/product Smith calculation
 + all-degree six-axis specialization
```

was found in the focused corpus.  This is a bounded literature statement,
not a priority or firstness claim.

## 2. Local classification of the sharp pair

After an unramified split, write (a_i) for the graph depth and

[
 \Omega_{ij}=p^{-a_i-a_j}(t_j-t_i)X_iX_j
 +p^{-a_i}X_iY_j+p^{-a_j}X_jY_i.
 \tag{1}
]

The ordinary divisor generator on an off-diagonal block has scale
(p^{e_{ij}}), with

[
 e_{ij}=max{a_i,a_j,a_i+a_j-v(t_j-t_i)}.
 \tag{2}
]

For a four-slot invariant there are two matching products.  If every
relevant root difference is a unit, the only possible defect is the single
Plücker cancellation of their all-(X) terms:

* four depth-one slots with four distinct roots give Hodge scale (p^{3a})
  versus product scale (p^{4a}), hence one (R/p^a) quotient;
* if the four-slot support contains a repeated root, the same-root matching
  has zero all-(X) term and its Hodge scale already equals its product
  scale, so the quotient is zero;
* with one unit slot and three depth-one slots, the quotient is nonzero only
  when those three depth-one roots are pairwise distinct.  A repeated pair
  again supplies the product-scale matching.

This is the exact local classification needed in dimension five.  It is a
two-matching calculation, not an appeal to Poincare duality.

## 3. Actual (6I_5-J_5) packet

The integral decomposition at (p=2,3) is

[
  6I_5-J_5 = (\text{unit line})\perp(\text{depth-one rank-four block}),
 \tag{3}
]

up to unit rescaling.  At (p=2), the depth-one block has two repeated
residual roots, with multiplicities (2+2) after the unramified quadratic
split.  At (p=3), its residual slope is scalar, with multiplicity (4).
Thus every four-slot support is of one of the following forms:

```text
p=2:  (A,A,B,B), or (unit,A,A,B)/(unit,A,B,B);
p=3:  (A,A,A,A), or (unit,A,A,A).
```

Every support contains a repeated depth-one root.  By the classification in
§2, its Hodge and ordinary-product lattices coincide.  At primes away from
(2,3), the (6I-J) isogeny is integral-locally invertible, and the
contraction products on the elliptic power give the integral invariant
lattice.  Since (g=5), the only potentially nonsaturated squarefree
residual width is four slots, appearing only in codimensions (2) and (3);
codimensions (0,1,4,5) are already product-saturated.  Therefore

[
  operatorname{Hdg}^{2k}(J,mathbf Z)=P_J^k
  quad(0leq kleq5)
 \tag{4}
]

for non-CM fibres of the actual packet.  At CM fibres, (4) must be replaced
by the prescribed graph-lattice statement

[
  \operatorname{PD}\langle N^1_{\rm graph}(J)\rangle^k=P^k_{\rm graph};
 \tag{5}
]

extra CM divisor/Hodge classes are outside this calculation.

The repeated-root calculation is the substantive new specialization.  The
finite-group Borel/Cartan packet, the rational Hodge-generation theorem, and
Poincare duality do not imply (4) by themselves.

## 4. Rank-five one-depth sharpness tower

Let the split source have five depth-one slots, all with pairwise-distinct
residual roots, and let the graph depth be (p^a).  In codimension two,
each four-subset has the two-matching calculation of §2, giving one
(R/p^a) quotient. There are \(\binom54=5\) supports, hence

[
  \operatorname{Hdg}^{4}/P^2\cong(R/p^a)^5.
 \tag{6}
]

In codimension three, multiplication by the primitive volume class of the
unique complementary slot identifies each four-slot quotient with its
doubled-slot component.  This gives the canonical marked Lefschetz
comparison

[
  \operatorname{Hdg}^{6}/P^3\cong(R/p^a)^5.
 \tag{7}
]

There is no residual squarefree width (ge3) in the remaining degrees for
dimension five, so the quotient is zero in degrees (0,1,4,5).  The
finite-etale rank-one trace-form constructions and the centralizer/idempotent
argument give polarized indecomposability for the stated non-CM towers; their
arbitrary depth is a construction theorem under those explicit local trace
and self-adjoint-lift hypotheses, not a consequence of rational Hodge theory.

This pair is sharp in the strongest relevant sense: the same dimension and
finite-etale divided-power saturation mechanism can coexist either with no
ambient integral Hodge/product defect (actual (6I-J)) or with the exact
nonzero defect ((R/p^a)^5) (distinct-root one-depth tower).

## 5. Why the contrast is not Poincare duality

Poincare duality gives a perfect pairing on total integral cohomology, but it
does not identify the annihilator of (P^2) with (P^3).  The actual
six-axis equality uses the explicit multidegree decomposition and the
repeated-root two-matching calculation.  The rank-five tower uses the same
calculation plus a primitive volume-factor embedding.  The equality of the
two middle quotient groups is therefore a theorem about the marked graph
lattice, not a formal PD consequence.

Likewise, the statement is cohomological.  It is not an integral Hodge
conjecture assertion in Chow, does not produce effective cycles, and does
not claim that extra CM Hodge classes are divisor products.

## 6. Safe priority and paper wording

Use this hierarchy:

1. **Classical:** rational Hodge/Lefschetz generation by divisors for the
   non-CM elliptic-power isogeny class (Milne and related Hodge-group work).
2. **New internal theorem:** weighted integral Hodge/product Smith quotients
   for marked finite-etale graph lattices; the dimension-five actual-packet
   repeated-root specialization and the rank-five distinct-root sharpness
   theorem.
3. **Separate geometric input:** construction of the actual six-axis
   isogeny/kernel and its modular marking; this is not supplied by the Hodge
   literature.

The safe headline is:

> In dimension five, the actual six-axis graph packet is integrally
> Hodge/product saturated at non-CM fibres, while neighboring distinct-root
> one-depth indecomposable towers have the exact sharp defect
> ((\mathbf Z/p^a)^5) in codimensions two and three.

Do not call either result a known “integral Lefschetz theorem”; call it a
marked finite-etale weighted invariant-lattice theorem, with Milne cited only
for the rational predecessor.
