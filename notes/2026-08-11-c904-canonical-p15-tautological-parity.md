# C904: canonical decomposable p15 correspondences are even

Date: 2026-08-11

Status: exact no-go theorem and finite lattice certificate; Paper V research
only; no manuscript or Lean change

## Verdict

None of the canonical decomposable symmetric correspondences

\[
             P\,(S_1+S_2)
\]

can supply the odd \((1,5)\) half-anti-graph class.  This includes
\(S=c_2(T_M)\), \(h^2/2\), every exceptional surface class, all Chern
polynomials in these classes, and every A5-invariant product of actual
Neron--Severi divisors.

The clean reason is not special to the named candidates.  If an integral
A5-horizontal surface class has scalar coefficient \(a\) on the
five-dimensional coefficient lattice, then

\[
              5a=\int_Mh^2S=\int_J\Theta^3s,
              \qquad S=b^*s+S_{\rm exc}.
\]

The exceptional term has zero \(h^2\)-intersection.  Since
\(\Theta^3=3!\Theta^{[3]}\) integrally, \(6\mid5a\), hence \(6\mid a\).
In particular its coefficient trace is even.  Two-locally the same
argument only needs the factor two and remains valid for the integral
degree-four splitting of the theta resolution.

Thus an odd p15 class, if algebraic, must be genuinely nondecomposable.  It
cannot be built by coupling the Poincare divisor to a horizontal
tautological surface.  This sharpens the surviving gate to the integral
inverse-Lefschetz/non-split Kunneth correspondence.

## Half-anti-graph degree

Let \(q:M^2\to\operatorname {Sym}^2M\), let \(P\) be the restricted
Poincare class, and let \(j=(1,\iota)\) be the anti-graph.  For the descended
class \(Z_S\) with

\[
                 q^*Z_S=P(S_1+S_2),
\]

one has

\[
 j^*P=-2h,qquad j^*(S_1+S_2)=S+\iota^*S.
\]

Therefore its half anti-graph and addition degree are

\[
 \lambda(Z_S)=-h(S+\iota^*S),
 \qquad
 d(Z_S)=-2\int_Mh^2S.                         \tag{2.1}
\]

Even a hypothetical integral halving \(Z_S/2\) would have degree
\(-\int h^2S=-5a\), which is even by the divisibility theorem above.  Thus
the no-go survives the strongest possible factor-two descent improvement.

## Characteristic-number table

\[

 =5n^4-10n^3+10n^2-5n+1
\]

gives by Riemann--Roch

\[
 \int_Mh^4=120,
 \qquad
 \int_Mh^2(c_1^2+c_2)=240.

Independently, adjunction on \(B=\operatorname {Bl}_0J\), using
\(c(T_B)=(1+e)(1-e)^5\) and \([M]=h-3e\), gives

\[
 c_1(T_M)=-h-e,qquad
 c_2(T_M)=h^2-2he+2e^2.
\]

Since \(he=0\),

\[
                \int_Mh^2c_2(T_M)=120.
\]

With the coefficient normalization \(a(S)=\frac15\int h^2S\), the exact
values are

\[
\begin{array}{c|r|r|r}
S&\int h^2S&a(S)&d(P(S_1+S_2))\\ \hline

h^2/2&60&12&-120\\

The last two rows are rational characteristic diagnostics; they are not
being asserted as integral algebraic surface generators.  They are even

## Exact A5 divisor-product census

The script reconstructs the actual exotic principal lattice, its rank-15
NS lattice, and the two standard A5 generators.  The lattice generated by
the 120 pairwise NS products has rank 50 in \(\bigwedge^4\Lambda\).  Its
A5-fixed sublattice has rank three, and

\[
 \left\{\int_J\Theta^3S:S\text{ in the fixed product lattice}\right\}
       =60\mathbf Z.
\]

Equivalently, its scalar-coefficient ideal is \(12\mathbf Z\).  This is
twice as strong as the universal \(6\mathbf Z\) horizontal bound and rules
out every A5-orbit sum of divisor-product surfaces, not only the named
characteristic classes.

## Scope

This proves a no-go for decomposable p15 classes obtained from the Poincare
divisor and an integral horizontal or exceptional surface.  It does not
rule out a non-split algebraic Kunneth class whose degree-five factor is an
integral inverse-Lefschetz lift.  Such a class need not separate as
\(P(S_1+S_2)\), and that nonseparability is now essential rather than an
optional refinement.
