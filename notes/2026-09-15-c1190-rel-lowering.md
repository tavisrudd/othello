## Goal

An admitted Rel program (private `src/rel_frontend/admit.rs`: node pool, symbol pool with owners,
binders, references, applications, `REL04xx` outcomes) becomes a core rule program
(`ergodis_verify::rule_contract::Program`: `Relation`, `Fact`, `Rule` of `Atom`s over `Term::
Variable | Term::Constant(u32)`, Boolean or bounded-min-plus carrier) that the demand-driven
evaluator (core `crates/rules/src/demand.rs`, `Demand::new(Program)`) evaluates and whose
derivation certificate the core `verify` checker accepts. Source in, checked closure out, on
native and WASM through the one canonical engine. Ergodis retains lowering, rules, joins and
execution; no external evaluator or backend is adopted (user direction 2026-09-12 and
2026-09-14).

## Two decisions for Tavis before code

1. **Fragment staging.** The core rule contract is positive Datalog: Horn rules, positive body
   atoms, `u32` constants, no negation, no aggregation, no arithmetic. The proposal is three
   staged milestones, each a tracked coverage row, never a product target (architecture context,
   binding direction): (a) the positive fragment: `def` with conjunction `,`, disjunction `;`
   (one rule per disjunct after distribution), `exists`/anonymous binders (projection), constants,
   equality by repeated variables, module-owned definitions flattened to qualified relation names,
   free names as input relations; (b) stratified negation and `not` / `forall` via a stratification
   pass, which requires a core extension (a stratum order and a negated-atom kind in `Rule`, or a
   per-stratum sequence of `Program`s fed by the previous closure); (c) `reduce`/aggregation and
   arithmetic, which need a value carrier the core does not have. Recommendation: build (a) end to
   end first and reject (b)/(c) constructs with their own `REL05xx` diagnostic and span, so the
   rejection is a recorded coverage gap, not a silent approximation.
2. **Value encoding.** Rel constants are typed literals (integers, strings, entity references,
   dates); the core term is `u32`. Proposal: a per-program value dictionary (literal → dense id)
   emitted with the lowered program and carried into readout, with the Boolean carrier as the
   default and the min-plus carrier only when the source declares it. The alternative, widening
   `Term::Constant`, changes the core contract and every checker, and is not recommended now.

The choice in (1) is an architecture choice under the intent-based rules; the card records the
recommendation and waits.

## Contract (once the decisions are taken)

- **Semantics.** Figure 2–4 of Aref et al., arXiv:2504.10323, Addendum A (lit cache
  `arxiv:2504.10323`) for the logical core; the paper has no module construct, so module
  visibility, module parameters as binders and `M:x` member resolution remain the frontend's
  stated contract (`2026-09-14-c1170-module-scopes.md`, "Semantics adopted"). The lowering
  records which programs it admits and which fixed point it computes as its own contract, not as
  a paper claim. Least fixed point over the positive fragment; non-stratified programs are rejected
  in milestone (b), never approximated.
- **Range restriction** is the safety condition: every head variable and every variable in a
  negated or comparison position must be bound by a positive body atom; violations are rejected
  with a diagnostic naming the unbound variable and its span.
- **Zero-allocation stage** like admission: lowering runs over the admitted node pool into
  preallocated pools with capacity planned from the `Admission` counts; the `Program` value is
  materialized once at the boundary. Its cost is a separate measured stage with its own budget;
  parse and admission stages must not move (A/B against `ergodis-tools-e8b4c7c`, rustc 1.95.0,
  playbook protocol: `instructions,cycles,branches,branch-misses,page-faults,minor-faults`, cache
  events separately).
- **Parity.** The native/WASM parity corpus gains the lowered program's canonical hash per case;
  the corpus stays a finite parity gate, not a grammar or semantics oracle.
- **Differential oracle.** C1189's naive evaluator on shared inputs against the demand-driven
  closure, and the derivation certificate through the core checker. A lowering that changes the
  closure on any shared input is a defect in one of the three; the card for C1189 says which.
- **Diagnostics** follow the C1170 split: compact success, structured failure with primary and
  secondary spans, `REL05xx` identifiers for lowering rejections, and an error-only richer reparse
  that never runs a program.

## Not in scope

Negation, aggregation and arithmetic beyond their recorded rejection (milestones b/c are separate
allocations); tree-sitter; PLT Redex; any Rel conformance claim; micro-optimization of parse or
admission (priced candidates stay listed in the inline-reference and module-scope reports).
