# C84: rooted-S4 double-coset packets do not give a uniform reply strategy

**Date:** 2026-07-17  
**Lane:** `cap`  
**Status:** exact bounded negative for the first contextual response packet; C84 remains active.

## Result

Let `H ≅ S4` be the group generated by the rooted class-D triple and let `s` be the involution of
the escaping fourth centre.  For an opponent conic vertex `t`, partition possible replies by the
minimum number of uses of `s` in an alternating word

```text
H,  HsH,  HsHsH, ...
```

carrying `t` to the reply.  This is the coarsest response packet that retains the fourth-centre
context while quotienting all rooted-S4 word choices.  Call a nonempty layer packet *pure winning*
when every legal reply in it returns to a P-position.

The exact class-D census over `q = 13,17,19,23,29` gives:

| q | P roots | opponent moves | moves covered by a pure packet | fully covered roots | roots with no covered move |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 13 | 24 | 199 | 90 | 4 | 1 |
| 17 | 48 | 522 | 170 | 2 | 7 |
| 19 | 40 | 525 | 97 | 1 | 7 |
| 23 | 71 | 1,478 | 13 | 0 | 63 |
| 29 | 139 | 3,510 | 338 | 0 | 37 |

Therefore the packet is not a uniform second-player certificate.  It fails at the root level by
q=23: none of the 71 q=23 P roots, and none of the 139 q=29 P roots, has every opponent move
covered by a pure layer packet.

The obstruction is particularly transparent on the forced-reply corpus.  At q=23 the rooted S4
is transitive on the 24 conic parameters, so every one of the 125 unique winning replies lies in
layer zero; its legal layer packet has size 13--19 and contains losing replies.  At q=29, 140 of
195 forced replies lie in layer zero and 55 in layer one.  Only **one** of those 195 lies in a
singleton legal packet.  Across q=19 and q=23 there are no singleton forcing packets at all.

This closes the proposed rooted-S4/fourth-centre double-coset packet as the missing adaptive law.
It does not close contextual algebra generally: a finer invariant inside an H-layer could still
work.  But such a refinement is again response-specific unless it carries a multi-step descent
proof, so further one-ply signature refinement is not warranted by this result.
