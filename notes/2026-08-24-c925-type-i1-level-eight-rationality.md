# C925: the explicit type-I1 cubic is rational after eight stabilizations

**Lane:** cubic-threefolds · **Task:** C925 · **Date:** 2026-08-24

## Result

Let \(K\) be a field of characteristic zero and let \(S/K\) be a smooth
quartic del Pezzo surface with a \(K\)-point whose geometric Picard action is
of Tschinkel--Zhang type \(I_1\). Then

\[
S\times\mathbf A^8\quad\text{is }K\text{-rational}.
\]

Consequently the \(r=0\) smooth cubic threefold \(X/\mathbf Q\) in
Tschinkel--Zhang Proposition 5.1 satisfies

\[
\boxed{X\times\mathbf P^8\quad\text{is }\mathbf Q\text{-rational}.}
\]

This supersedes the level-ten result. Combined with C924, the certified range
for this explicit cubic is now

\[
2\le s(X)\le8.
\]

## Proof

Retain the notation of the level-ten argument. Let \(T\) be the rank-six
Néron--Severi torus of \(S\), let
\(\lambda:\mathbf G_m\to T\) be the primitive anticanonical scalar, and put
\(T_0=T/\lambda(\mathbf G_m)\). The projectivized universal torsor is
rational by Tschinkel--Zhang's proof of Theorem 3.4, and birationally

\[
\mathbf P(U)\sim_K S\times T_0. \tag{1}
\]

Their Lemma 4.2, restricted to the type-\(I_1\) Galois group, gives

\[
\operatorname{Pic}(\bar S)\oplus P\cong P',
\qquad \operatorname{rank}P=5,\quad \operatorname{rank}P'=11.
\]

The auxiliary permutation lattice is the sum of the displayed
\(w_0,w_1,w_2\) orbit and \(q_0,q_1\) orbit. Thus its quasi-trivial torus is

\[
Q=Q_3\times Q_2,
\qquad
Q_d=\operatorname{Res}_{E_d/K}\mathbf G_m
\]

for etale \(K\)-algebras of degrees \(3\) and \(2\). Let \(R\) be the
rank-eleven quasi-trivial torus attached to \(P'\). Then

\[
T\times Q_3\times Q_2\cong R. \tag{2}
\]

Let \(\Delta_d\cong\mathbf G_m\) be the diagonal split subtorus of \(Q_d\),
and define the split rank-three torus

\[
A=\lambda(\mathbf G_m)\times\Delta_3\times\Delta_2
\subset T\times Q_3\times Q_2.
\]

In the Lemma 4.2 permutation basis, the type-\(I_1\) action on \(P'\) has
orbits of sizes \(6,4,1\). Exact multiplication by the three cocharacter rows
gives the orbit-weight matrix

\[
W=
\begin{pmatrix}
2&-3&4\\
-2&3&-3\\
-3&4&-4
\end{pmatrix},
\qquad \det W=1. \tag{3}
\]

Therefore \(R/A\) is rational. Indeed, write the three Weil-restriction
blocks of \(R\) in \(K\)-linear coordinates
\[
(x_1,\ldots,x_6),\qquad(y_1,\ldots,y_4),\qquad z.
\]
The three selected coordinates \(x_1,y_1,z\) have weight matrix \(W\).
Because \(W^{-1}\) is integral, a unimodular Laurent-monomial change turns
them into three coordinates of standard \(A\)-weights. The invariant field is
then freely generated by

\[
x_i/x_1\ (2\le i\le6),
\qquad
y_j/y_1\ (2\le j\le4).
\]

It is purely transcendental of degree eight, proving
\[
R/A\sim_K\mathbf A^8. \tag{4}
\]

Quotienting (2) by \(A\) gives

\[
F:=
T_0\times(Q_3/\Delta_3)\times(Q_2/\Delta_2)
\cong R/A.
\]

Thus \(F\) is a rational rank-eight torus. Separately,
\(Q_d/\Delta_d\) is rational: it is a dense open subvariety of
\(\mathbf P^{d-1}\). Multiplying (1) by these two rational quotient tori gives

\[
\begin{aligned}
S\times F
&\sim_K
\mathbf P(U)\times(Q_3/\Delta_3)\times(Q_2/\Delta_2)\\
&\sim_K \mathbf A^{10}.
\end{aligned}
\]

Since \(F\sim_K\mathbf A^8\), this is precisely
\(S\times\mathbf A^8\) rational.

For the Tschinkel--Zhang cubic, the generic fibre of projection from their
displayed plane is birational to such an \(S\) over \(K=\mathbf Q(a)\).
Passing from the generic-fibre function field to the total function field
therefore proves \(X\times\mathbf P^8\) rational over \(\mathbf Q\).
