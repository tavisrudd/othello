# C201 Gate 2 — indexed split-`Z3` ternary relation

**Lane:** `relconic`

## Result

The mixed-orbit determinant condition from the split-`Z3` sizing gate has a
small exact incidence index.  For every compatible pair of legal length-three
orbits `(Oi, Oj)`, form the nine lines joining a point of `Oi` to a point of
`Oj`, and store the legal orbit labels meeting their union as a 1,302-bit
bitset `B_ij`.  Then

```text
F(Oi, Oj, Ok) = 0  iff  k is in B_ij.
```

Indeed, membership on one of the nine lines is exactly the vanishing of one
of the nine phase-reduced determinant factors.  The construction is
independent of the chosen representatives because changing a representative
permutes those lines.  Thus the four ternary tests for a pairwise-compatible
orbit quadruple are four bit lookups, with no determinant evaluation during
the search.

For the 730,380 compatible orbit pairs the index has

```text
bad ternary incidences = 127,477,770
bad third labels per pair = 171..179
mean bad third labels per pair = 174.536228
```

The script checks 20,000 seeded pair/third-label queries against all 27 direct
point determinants.  It also reconstructs the best sampled arc directly and
checks its uncovered count with the independent full profile routine.

## Bounded coverage probe

With seed 201, 199,459 attempts produced 100,000 accepted arc draws after all
six pair conditions and all four indexed ternary conditions.  Their ordinary
uncovered sizes ranged from 824 to 1,055.  The best label quadruple was

```text
(213, 486, 870, 1098),    |U(A)| = 824.
```

This misses the necessary `|U(A)| <= 65` prefilter by a factor greater than
twelve, so quadratic evaluation was correctly skipped.  This sample is
evidence about the family, not an exhaustive exclusion and not a uniform
sample of its arc classes.

## Consequence

The symbolic/indexing requirement from the sizing gate is discharged: the
degree-27-looking ternary condition is a sparse pair-indexed relation, with
only about 13.4% of third labels forbidden for a typical compatible pair.
That sparsity also explains why mixed collinearity alone cannot shrink the
43-billion pairwise-compatible lower bound into a bounded enumeration.

The next bounded step, if C201 continues, is coverage-directed optimization
using this index, with an independently checked witness as the early-stop
target.  Failure of a bounded optimizer is not a family theorem; an exhaustive
closure would require a certified coverage upper bound for all indexed arc
quadruples.
