## Recommendation

Integrate the repairs, selectively integrate validated extensions, and reserve
the moduli proposal for a separately gated follow-on. The full collection would
give the papers too many competing purposes. Organize each around one central
question and include extensions when they strengthen its answer.

| Destination | Material and purpose |
|---|---|
| First paper, main text | Intrinsic scalar proof outline; consolidated coefficient/lattice comparison; separate persistence and residue statements; universal rational Fano calculation; two clearly distinguished counts; index-two one-stabilization classification, conditional on its review gates. |
| Sharpness paper, main text | Intrinsic motivation for the selected torus; étale-algebra interpretation; explicit orbit normalization; separated geometric, tangent-open and arithmetic descent claims; residual action matrices with basis conventions. |
| Short corollaries or remarks | Cross-degree spectral separation; the ceiling on the additive method; exact trivial-stabilization linearization thresholds if the mathematical and attribution audit supports them. |
| Appendices or supporting notes | Alternative residue checks; arithmetic spectrum; detailed lattice certificates; executable parametrization inputs; finite-index/diagonal-torsion refinements. Each remains subject to its own audit. |
| Separate follow-on project | The audited cubic family and Cremona consequences, with intrinsic moduli/period geometry still open. The basic genus-two torsion construction is prior art; novelty of the cubic locus and intrinsic correspondence remain separate gates. |
| Research roadmap outside the manuscripts | Higher stabilization levels, positive characteristic, algorithmic benchmarks and commercial possibilities. |

## How to gain breadth without sacrificing accessibility

1. Replace the cubic-only radical calculation with the universal rational
   calculation, retain the cubic as the worked example, and give the other
   two families in a compact table. Move the independent radical check.
2. Use one eligible-block construction and shared proofs for the two counts.
   Make the distinction between exponent classes and the canonical lattice
   prominent. The stronger invariant introduces a real mathematical
   commitment and should not be hidden in an appendix footnote.
3. Give the Fano extension one payoff: the complete index-two classification
   after one stabilization. Avoid a sequence of separately developed examples.
4. Keep the constructive surface theorem at the center of the sharpness paper.
   Its torus interpretation should explain the existing construction, not
   become a second general treatise on algebraic tori.
5. Move or shorten duplicated formal apparatus before adding new material.
   Do not duplicate proofs, diagrams, certificate discussion or scope warnings.
6. Keep speculative directions and publication/commercial assessments out
   of the theorem narrative. A separate moduli paper must earn its own
   mathematical identity, preferably through intrinsic geometry.

The Fano extension could occupy only a few net pages if it replaces the
specialized calculation and shares the foundational argument. This is an
editorial estimate, not a page-budget guarantee. No comparable estimate is
appropriate for the separate moduli/period project.
