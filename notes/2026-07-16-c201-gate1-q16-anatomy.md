# C201 Gate 1 — invariant quadratic anatomy and the `q=64` sizing gate

**Lane:** `relconic`

## Structural form

For an arc `A`, let `U(A)` be its ordinary-uncovered locus and let

```text
K_A = { Q in Sym^2(V*) : Q(u)=0 for every u in U(A) }.
```

If `g` is a projectivity and `T_g(Q)=Q compose g^-1`, then secant transport
gives `U(gA)=gU(A)` and therefore

```text
K_(gA) = T_g(K_A).
```

After choosing vector representatives, evaluation functionals acquire only
nonzero square factors under rescaling.  Thus `dim K_A`, the projective
kernel, and whether `ev_a |_ K_A` vanishes are projective invariants.  The two
certificate alternatives have the following invariant content:

- `K_A=0` (six independent uncovered-point conditions); or
- the restrictions `{ev_a |_ K_A : a in A}` cover `K_A` by their kernels.

When `dim K_A=1`, the second condition is simply that the unique projective
quadratic in `K_A` contains at least one selected point.  This is the precise
geometric meaning of the three `forcedHit` leaves; it does not reprove the
general evaluation dichotomy.

## Independent frozen-list census

It reimplements `GF(16)`, secant indices, uncovered loci, quadratic row
reduction, conic type, projective stabilizers, and point orbits.  It does not
call or import the C++ generator.

For the prescribed hole set `H=U(A)`, all hole indices vanish.  With `k=8`
and `m=4`, the integral defect is

```text
4 Delta_H(A) = sum_(x notin A union U(A)) (r(x)-1)(4-r(x))
             = 2(n_2+n_3).
```

The 2,633 leaves occupy 62 full `(rank, |U|, index spectrum, defect)` cells.
There is no equality cell: the minimum scaled defect is 224.  The two leaves
in that minimum-positive cell are rank five.  The next cell, scaled defect

## `q=64` sizing

The corrected incidence bound first becomes admissible at `k=13`; its
integral slack is 696.  It fails at `k=12` by 2,526.  A full census is already
infeasible one level earlier.  For every `n`-arc, the union bound leaves at
least

```text
q^2+q+1 - n - choose(n,2)(q-1)
```

legal extensions.  At `q=64`, multiplying these bounds for `n=4,...,11`,
then dividing by the eight insertion orders, the order-24 frame stabilizer,
and the at most `choose(12,4)` normalized frames per projective class, gives
more than `10^18` projective twelve-arc classes.  This is a rigorous lower
bound, not a runtime extrapolation, and rejects the proposed full `q=64`
census before any generation.

The surviving Gate 2 route is therefore restricted to symbolically defined
or group-orbit candidate families.  Any such family must record the same
rank, forced-hit, defect, spectrum, and stabilizer fields as the `q=16`
analyzer.
