## Common quotient structure

The stable subgroups are

```text
ID 4: <121> = {1,121,322}
ID 5: <211> = {1,211,232}.
```

They are distinct diagonal subgroups modulo `333=9*37`, but their two
projections coincide:

```text

```

Consequently their separate 9- and 37-compression systems are identical.  The
full actions are not: each has 113 orbits (`1^3 3^110`) and 112 nonzero shift
orbits, with the mod-9 and mod-37 actions coupled in opposite CRT diagonals.

## Fixed-point refinement

Both order-3 actions have exactly the three singleton positions
`{0,111,222}`; every other position orbit has size 3.  After normalizing a row
sum to `+1`, its minus-set has size 166.  Its number of singleton minus signs
is therefore `166 mod 3 = 1`, and because that number lies between zero and
three it is **exactly one**.  Thus a pair has only two relative fixed-point
geometries up to common translation, decimation, and row exchange: the two
marked singleton positions are the same or they are different.

The committed generator also computes the compression action of the stabilizer
of each normalized geometry.  Starting from all 1,296 oriented positive-row
compression pairs gives exactly 324 representatives in the same-point case and
648 in the different-point case.  The nominal unit-action sizes alone would
under-count these representatives because the multiplier image `{1,4,7}` acts
trivially on the already invariant 9-compressions.  This refinement explains
the geometry of the hard lift core, but it is not an exclusion.

The 9-compression itself already recovers this geometry.  In each of the
residue fibres `r=0,3,6`, the row consists of the singleton at `37r` and twelve
orbits of size 3.  The twelve orbit signs have even sum, so their contribution
is divisible by 6.  Hence

```text
compressed_value[r] = singleton_sign[37r] (mod 6).
```

Exactly one of the three values is therefore `-1 mod 6`; it identifies the
unique marked singleton without an additional SAT split.  Applying this rule
to the global 108 representatives gives exactly **36 same-point** and **72
different-point** representatives.  This independently explains why earlier
four-way zero-bit experiments left exactly one compatible branch per global
representative; those experiments supplied no exclusion and are not evidence.

```text

b^ = (1,3,-1,1,-1,5,3,-5,3,-5,-5,5,-3).
```

witness, but the committed direct verifier does not trust the search process.

## Exact-lift boundary

The upstream proof-equivalent orbit encoding was reconstructed from source
commit `43ddd9107a61197f3b7f91d1233e6dde1097cfbd`.  For either ID its unaugmented
carry-save CNF has 226 primary orbit variables, 158,107 total variables, and
1,067,981 clauses before compression selectors.  Trial SAT, CP-SAT, SCIP, and
local-search runs did not decide the unrestricted lift.  Those solver statuses
and `/tmp` artifacts are deliberately not evidence and support no mathematical
claim.

Fixing individual feasible 9-compressions makes many branches easy to refute,
but several of the exact 108 symmetry representatives remain solver-hard.  C741
therefore stays open.  Completion requires a committed selector-aware exact
CNF (or an analytic mixed-character obstruction), checked UNSAT proofs for both
CRT diagonals, or a directly verified full Legendre-pair witness.  Separate
compression feasibility is not a lift.
