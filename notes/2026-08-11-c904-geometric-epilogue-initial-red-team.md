## Load-bearing repairs

1. **Two-primary packet.** The current proof compresses the identification of
   all five maximal isotropics and their `3+2` stabilizer split into one
   paragraph. The final paper must print the invariant alternating form, the
   `P^1(F_4)` parametrization, and the stabilizer calculation without invoking
   the discovery census.
2. **Three-primary packet.** “Unique monodromy-invariant line” must be derived
   from the actual `Gamma_0(3)` local system and then matched to the coefficient
   heart. Smith type alone is insufficient.
3. **Geometric local chart.** Lemma 3.2 now states the correct bridge from
   `6I-J` to the semisimple/scalar graph theorems, but its proof must display
   the orthogonal `1 perp p^4` chart and the actual kernel in that chart.
4. **Mixed adjugates.** The semisimple-slope theorem needs the polarized
   determinant and adjugate identities written as lemmas with coefficient
   `+1` or `-1`. Otherwise a referee cannot audit the absence of a factorial or
   off-diagonal factor two.
5. **Recent quantum sources.** The source clauses have been checked at theorem
   level, but the final paper needs an independent `F`-bundle specialist read
   of the scalar-extension identification and of the precise atom equivalence
   preserving the loop coordinate.

None of these repairs changes the theorem statement or requires a new
certificate. Items 1--4 are expansions of structural proofs already proved in
the C904 notes; item 5 is a publication safeguard.
