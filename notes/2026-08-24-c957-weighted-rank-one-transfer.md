## Result

Let \(\iota:L\xrightarrow{\sim}I\leq\mathbb F_q^E\) be the inner
encoder and

\[
 \Phi_I(y)(a)=\langle y,\iota(a)\rangle .
\]

For \(\beta\in L^*\), define the ordinary and target-constrained fiber
weights

\[
 \lambda_I(\beta)=\min_{\Phi_I(y)=\beta}\operatorname{wt}(y),
 \qquad
 \mu_{I,x}(\beta)=
 \min_{\substack{\Phi_I(y)=\beta\\y_x\ne0}}
 \operatorname{wt}(y).
\]

For an outer block \(j\), the exact minimum total weight of a concatenated
dual word through \((j,x)\) that is not an embedded inner-dual word is

\[
 Z_{j,x}(O,I)=
 \min\left\{
  \mu_{I,x}(0)+d(I^\perp),
  \min_{\substack{\beta=(\beta_h)\in\operatorname{FD}(O)\\\beta\ne0}}
  \left(\mu_{I,x}(\beta_j)+
        \sum_{h\ne j}\lambda_I(\beta_h)\right)
 \right\}.
\]

Consequently, restriction and zero-extension give inverse,
support-preserving bijections on all normalized radius-\(r\) equations and
their exact helper supports if and only if

\[
 Z_{j,x}(O,I)>r+1.
\]

Assume the outer projection onto block \(j\) is nonzero, hence surjective.
This theorem is finite and has no outer-distance hypothesis.  It complements,
rather than replaces, the arbitrary-rank theorem: the latter gives the exact
threshold \(M_t(D_P,K_P)+d(I^\perp)\) after
\(d(O^\perp)>r+1\) excludes every nonzero outer-functional tuple.  For one
target coordinate, the zero-functional branch is
\(z_x(I)=M_1(D_P,K_P)+1+d(I^\perp)\).

## Proof audit

Every concatenated dual word has independent inner blocks in fibers indexed
by one tuple \(\beta\in\operatorname{FD}(O)\).  For a fixed nonzero tuple,
independent minimization gives target-block cost
\(\mu_{I,x}(\beta_j)\) and other-block costs
\(\lambda_I(\beta_h)\).  In the zero tuple, every block lies in
\(I^\perp\); a nonconfined word must pay
\(\mu_{I,x}(0)\) in the target block and \(d(I^\perp)\) in another block,
and disjoint placement of minimizers attains the sum.  These two cases exhaust
all nonconfined words.  Normalizing an attaining word at \((j,x)\) proves the
converse transfer implication.

Surjectivity makes the concatenated dual words confined to block \(j\)
exactly the embedded words of \(I^\perp\).  Without it, a nonzero functional
supported only at a zero outer coordinate can give an extra but still
confined equation, so the hypothesis is load-bearing.

The exact equivalence is stated for equations and exact helper-support
families.  It is not stated as an equivalence for inclusion-minimal recovery
sets: an additional nonconfined support might contain an existing confined
support and hence fail to create a new minimal set.  Under the weighted gate,
minimal recovery sets do copy because all bounded equations are confined.

## Strictness family from a Singer cycle

Let \(I\) be the \([k+1,k,2]_q\) single-parity-check code, identify its
message space with \(L=\mathbb F_{q^k}\), and suppose

\[
 \frac{q^k-1}{q-1}>(k+1)^2.
\]

Its \(k+1\) coordinate functionals give \(k+1\) projective points \(S\) in
\(L^*\).  Multiplication maps modulo \(\mathbb F_q^*\) induce a Singer cycle
of order \((q^k-1)/(q-1)\).  At most \(|S|^2=(k+1)^2\) elements map some
point of \(S\) into \(S\), so a multiplication map has dual action \(a^*\)
with \(a^*(S)\cap S=\varnothing\).

For every \(k+1\leq n\leq2k+1\), the \(L\)-linear outer code

\[
 O=\{u\in L^n:u_0+a(u_1)+u_2+\cdots+u_{n-1}=0\}
\]

has functional-dual tuples \((f,f\circ a,f,\ldots,f)\), so
\(d(O^\perp)=n\).  A total-weight-\(n\) realization would use a weight-one
inner representative in every block, forcing both \([f]\) and
\([f\circ a]\) into \(S\), contrary to the choice of \(a\).  Thus every
nonzero tuple costs at least \(n+1\).  Since \(I^\perp\) is generated by
\((1,\ldots,1,-1)\), the zero-functional cost is \(2k+2>n\).  Therefore
\(Z_{j,x}>n\) at every block and inner coordinate: radius \(n-1\) transfers
exactly although the support-distance gate \(d(O^\perp)>n\) fails.  The
choice \((q,k,n)=(5,3,5)\) gives the original \([4,3,2]_5\) example.  The
range \(n\geq k+1\) makes this strictness nonvacuous because the inner
recovery equations have exactly \(k\) helpers.

An ephemeral exact \(\mathbb F_{125}\) enumeration found a Singer multiplier
with the required disjointness and minimum nonzero functional realization cost
exactly six.  The manuscript depends only on the human argument above; this
check is not paper evidence and no reproducibility claim is made for it.

## Mystery ledger

### Settled

- The apparent loss from the July manuscript was real: the ranked theorem did
  not retain the exact nonzero-functional sector before the outer-distance
  gate.  The restored formula closes that gap without weakening or duplicating
  the arbitrary-rank theorem.
- The strict family cannot be obtained from block-support distance alone; its
  improvement comes from incompatible weight-one inner realizations in two
  functional fibers.  The sufficient Singer inequality and the range
  \(k+1\leq n\leq2k+1\) expose exactly the requirements: a disjoint
  projective translate, nonvacuous helper radius, and zero-functional cost
  exceeding \(n\).
- No new name is needed for either fiber weight.  `Coset-leader weight`,
  `target-constrained fiber weight`, and `exact weighted finite-length
  confinement` describe the mathematics directly.
- The phrase `Singer-shifted` was removed from the manuscript.  It now uses
  the established term `Singer cycle` and describes the construction
  literally.
