# C383 — projective Reed--Solomon deep holes via marked extension geometry

**Lane:** `crowns`

**Date:** 2026-07-19

**Status:** active; highest-EV famous-problem transfer gate

## Question

Can C379/C381's presentation-independent obstruction decoration and marked-root extension
criterion become a coordinate-free invariant for genuine projective Reed--Solomon deep holes,
equivalently one-coordinate MDS extensions, beyond the already classified redundancy-three case?

The target is not another q=11 census.  It is a reusable theorem or a sharp categorical
obstruction explaining why the planar Clebsch mechanism cannot reach the projective
Reed--Solomon problem.

## Frozen input and boundary

- C379 recovers a rank-three non-GRS parent from a matching-decorated GRS deep-hole conic.
- C381 recovers that matching and decides two-point MDS status from the marked integral root datum
  `(R(S)<K^perp~E8,{alpha_u,alpha_v})`.
- Kaipa's deep-hole/MDS-extension equivalence is external prior art and must be cited, not reclaimed.
- Redundancy three is a control case because its projective geometry is planar and already
  classified.  The first potentially informative target is redundancy four, where the parity-check
  geometry is a normal rational curve in `PG(3,q)` and the plane-blow-up `E8` dictionary does not
  transfer automatically.

## Serial gate

1. Close the primary literature around projective Reed--Solomon deep holes through redundancy four
   and record the exact unresolved orbit/classification boundary.
2. Translate the known redundancy-three classification into the C379/C381 language as a control;
   require the marked invariant to recover something stricter than the ordinary MDS-extension
   predicate.
3. On the smallest odd-field redundancy-four models containing more than the standard extension
   orbit, test whether a coordinate-free incidence, singular-hypersurface, or root/intersection
   decoration separates the known deep-hole orbits.
4. Continue to a family theorem only if the same invariant is presentation-independent and
   survives at least two fields.  Otherwise close with the exact category mismatch or bounded
   negative.

## Success and stop

**Green:** a proved invariant or reconstruction theorem for a nontrivial projective
Reed--Solomon family that classifies or sharply separates deep-hole/MDS-extension orbits.

**Yellow:** an exact redundancy-three reinterpretation or a bounded redundancy-four separator with
no family transport; retain it only if it clarifies the known classification.

**Red:** the marked-root data are intrinsically planar, the target geometry has no natural retained
decoration, or the result reduces to the classical deep-hole/MDS-extension equivalence.  Stop
without a wider field census.

Any computational result must use an adjacent deterministic script/canonical certificate bundle,
exact replay command, hashes, and an independent incidence or direct-enumeration check.
