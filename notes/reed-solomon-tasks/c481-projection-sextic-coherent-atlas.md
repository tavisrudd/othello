# C481 — projection-sextic and coherent-atlas theorem

**Lane:** `reed-solomon`

## Entry gate

C475 proves the universal edge-torus quotient; C478 identifies coherence as the missing parent
datum on the four frozen controls.

## Exact domain

An arbitrary six-arc `A subset PG(2,F)` over a finite field, a deepest syndrome centre `u`, and
the quotient line `P(F^3/<u>)`.  Include characteristic two and semilinear covariance.  No field
census.

## Target

Prove that the thirty determinant ratios are exactly the projective moduli coordinates of the six
points obtained by projecting `A` from `u`.  Define, without quotient ambiguity, the pointwise,
diagonally coherent, Frobenius-colour-orbit, and Galois-equivariant atlas functors.

## Work package

1. Construct the induced alternating form on `F^3/<u>` and prove
   `det(u,h_i,h_j)=<bar h_i,bar h_j>_u` homogeneously.
2. Prove deepestness is equivalent to six distinct projected points and that the edge-torus
   quotient is the labelled `M_0,6`/binary-sextic moduli point.
3. Prove the exact diagonal-`S6` and semilinear transporter laws, including the correct Frobenius
   action on colours.
4. State the stabilizer criterion for pointwise and coherent parent recovery and recover every
   C478 quotient distinction as a corollary.

## Acceptance

One all-characteristic theorem with explicit maps and inverses, no hidden coordinate chart, and a
proof-level specialization matching the C478 certificate.  Report:
`notes/2026-07-22-c481-projection-sextic-coherent-atlas.md`.

## Boundaries

Do not attempt multi-centre reconstruction, exceptional-locus classification, a new finite-field
census, or modular-carrier claims.
