# C482 — three-centre gauge synchronization

**Lane:** `reed-solomon`

## Entry gate

C481 identifies every syndrome fibre with an abstract projected sextic and fixes the diagonal
support and Frobenius actions.

## Exact domain

Two or three distinct deepest centres of one six-arc, with one diagonal `S6` correspondence and an
independent `PGL_2` gauge on each quotient line.  Work over an arbitrary finite field and retain
characteristic-two formulas.

## Target

Derive the simultaneous compatibility equations and an explicit rational reconstruction map from
three coherently matched projected sextics to the parent six-arc on a stated nondegenerate open
locus.

## Work package

1. Choose gauge-free variables for the quotient-line `PGL_2` identifications.
2. Express the condition that corresponding projection rays meet in six common ambient points.
3. Eliminate the fibre gauges, produce a rational inverse, and isolate every denominator/minor
   used by the inverse.
4. Prove uniqueness modulo ambient projectivity and one diagonal `S6`; identify the exact
   two-centre residual freedom before imposing the third centre.

## Acceptance

A theorem and replayable symbolic identity package proving the three-centre inverse on an explicit
open locus, with all excluded factors recorded for C483.  Report:
`notes/2026-07-22-c482-three-centre-synchronization.md`.

## Boundaries

Do not classify the excluded divisor, claim an all-field global theorem, or import modular
language.
