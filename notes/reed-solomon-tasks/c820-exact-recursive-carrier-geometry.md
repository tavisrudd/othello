# C820 — exact recursive polar-carrier geometry

**Lane:** `reed-solomon` · **Status:** queued

## Objective

Close the three concrete geometric defects isolated by C656 and prove the
strongest honest arbitrary-level recursively contained-carrier theorem for
coherent polar flags.

## Gates and deliverables

1. Give the exact saturated primary-decomposition ledger for the bottom bad
   carrier, including multiplicities, exceptional fibres, and embedded-
   component exclusion, with independently checkable certificates where used.
2. Classify every modular nucleus relevant to one stage, prove the exact
   nesting or intersection law for their coherent lifts, and replace the
   invalid single-degree charge for their reduced union by the true pullback
   degree.
3. Prove recursive transport at generic points through noninjective boundaries,
   nilpotent structure, special fibres, and every coherent lift; a reduced
   geometric-point argument is insufficient.
4. Combine these results with C646's formal density/closure interfaces and
   state the resulting carrier theorem with an honest field threshold.
5. Run a theorem-level novelty audit, including Wang--Wu--Hu, and obtain an
   independent finite-geometer/coding-theorist proof review.

## Boundary

Do not infer split-freeness merely from membership in a modular contraction
kernel. Higher-Lucas incidence on individual carrier strata remains C620
unless it is forced as a lemma of the exact carrier theorem.
