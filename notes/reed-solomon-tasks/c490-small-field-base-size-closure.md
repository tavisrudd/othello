**Lane:** `reed-solomon`

## Entry gate

C485 proves base size zero for every six-arc complete child over `F_q` with `q>=16`, and reduces
the remaining `q<=13` problem to alternative fifteen-secant decompositions and a coherent
collision hypergraph.  At q=13 the common-secants of distinct equal-child parents have only four
possible skeletons, with child cardinality removing two of them in the dense-concurrency strata.

## Exact domain

diagonally coherent projected-sextic signature.  Empty or vacuous deep-centre domains must be
disposed of explicitly.  The frozen C478 fibres are regression controls, not the classification
domain.

`T subset U(A)` with `|T|<=3` makes `Sigma_T` injective, or classify every counterexample with its
true minimum base size.  Remove C485's remaining conditional small-field clause without weakening
the coherent-label or semilinear hypotheses.
