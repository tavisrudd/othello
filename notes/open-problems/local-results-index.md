# Local result index for open-problem matching

**Snapshot date:** 2026-08-30

This is a routing index, not a replacement for theorem reports. Its purpose is
to make later external-problem filtering possible without repeatedly searching
the repository. The 2026-07-26 portfolio snapshot supplies the first eleven
families; dated reports supply the subsequent Golden/operator layer.

## Finite geometry and classical coding

1. Clebsch-code reconstruction from deep-hole syndrome data at `q=11`, with
   rigidity, classification through eight points and decoding consequences.
2. Conic matching quotients: quadratic recovery, cubic orientation, arithmetic
   gluing and completeness of the two occurring rank-three configurations.
3. Complete arcs relative to a conic: prescribed-hole defect identity,
   zero-defect rigidity, stability and six exact values of the defect radius.
4. Projective Reed--Solomon deep holes: exact classifications at redundancies
   3, 5, 6 and 7, plus arbitrary-redundancy coherent polar containment.
5. Frobenius-equivariant extension and repair of eight-arcs, including the

6. Four-point-frame continuation graphs reconstruct their ambient plane
   semilinearly; exact low-cardinality obstructions are known.
7. Square-root complete arcs relative to a conic: positive-density legality,
   strong coverage obstructions and exceptional `q=64` three-layer examples;
   the infinite-family `O(sqrt(q))` problem remains open.

## Invariant-theoretic shadows of point configurations

24. Four-point Gram determinant on a Veronese module factors as discriminant
    times a residual invariant, \(G_{d,r}=\Delta\Phi_{d,r}\), the residual being
    the norm of the classical Wronskian covariant. Classical-derived; the
    quartic square-class case is pre-existing. Integrality with an exact content
    law, a modular nondegeneracy criterion \(d+1=ap^k\) with \(a<p\), and a
    parity ceiling forcing the shadow into even Veronese degree.
25. The four-point tower \(C_m:y^2=\Phi_{2m,4}\): proved Dickson closed form and
    order-three recurrence, product law over four generalized Fermat loci,
    genus \(2m-4\) dropping to \(2m-6\) exactly on \(m\equiv1\bmod3\), and
    proved anharmonic-\(S_3\) isotypic multiplicities
    \(a=b=\lfloor(m-2)/3\rfloor\). Modular at \(m\le7\) with conductors 14, 90,
    150, 637, 1584; generic from \(m=8\) on Sato–Tate evidence.
26. Finite-field census of the square-class shadow: periodicity in
    \(2m\bmod(p-1)\) from an identity over \(\mathbf Z\), a Jacobi-sum master
    formula, a fifteen-class classification of constant strata, and a matching
    trace-one-Gauss-sum classification on the non-split torus. The former
    sporadic \((47,30)\) is the last of exactly three members of a Fermat-cubic
    family. The census bias is always odd away from divisors of \(4^{m-1}-1\).
27. Reconstruction from the shadow coloring: constant colorings lose the marking
    entirely, every nonconstant one has automorphism group exactly
    \(P\Gamma L_2(q)\) and marking fibre exactly
    \(\operatorname{Gal}(\mathbf F_q/\mathbf F_p)\), with a Baer-subline/Miquelian
    stratum as the only refinement failure. No second quadratic-form invariant
    exists.

Primary baseline: `notes/2026-07-31-results-summary-snapshot.md`, section
"The Gram–discriminant shadow of four points and its Dickson tower."

## Matching cautions

- A solved low-order instance is not progress on an asymptotic existence
  conjecture unless the mechanism scales.
- A classical variety appearing in two subjects is not by itself a theorem
  transfer.
- Computational classifications should be matched only to externally posed
  finite cases with identical equivalence relations.
- Formal verification strengthens reliability, not novelty or external
  importance.
- A measured speedup answers no open problem. Match solver work only to
  externally posed instances, and only when the instance, the tool version and
  the timed region agree.
- A classical mechanism reused in a new setting is not a new mechanism. Match
  such work by the downstream consequence, not by the construction it reuses.
