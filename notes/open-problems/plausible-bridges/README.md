# Deep screen of the five plausible famous-problem bridges

**Assessment date:** 2026-07-31

These dossiers test the five comparisons left plausible by the first broad
crosswalk.  They distinguish three questions:

1. Is there an exact logical implication from a repository theorem to the
   famous problem?
2. Does the repository theorem improve a known case, bound or obstruction?
3. Is there a credible attack that does not depend on the repository theorem?

## Result

| Target | Exact overlap | Famous-problem progress now? | Best bounded project |
|---|---|---|---|
| MDS conjecture | PRS codes and rational-normal-curve geometry | **No.** The new content is deep-hole classification in the radius `r-1` regime, where the clean MDS-extension equivalence breaks. | Use the new strata to formulate and test a sharper PRS deep-hole conjecture, not the MDS conjecture. |
| Prime-power conjecture | arbitrary finite projective planes occur in the continuation reconstruction theorem | **No.** Reconstruction assumes a plane and supplies neither a construction nor an obstruction. | Proof-carrying elimination of restricted order-12 symmetry/oval cases. |
| Hadamard conjecture | the Golden sign factor gives a symmetric conference matrix of order 10 | **No.** This yields only the classical Hadamard order 20 and has no order-growing operation. | Finish structured Legendre-pair searches for order 668. |
| Maximum equiangular lines | the Golden factor gives an `ETF(5,10)` | **No.** This is the classical exact value `M(5)=10`. | Exact certified bounds for `M(18)`, `M(19)` or `M(20)`. |
| Transversal non-Clifford codes | exact transversal-group classification for stabilizer AME/MDS--CSS codes | **No.** The local class encodes one qudit and is not asymptotically good. | Fully good qLDPC codes with transversal non-Clifford gates, or finite-size exact gate classification. |

The first-pass label “direct partial bridge” for MDS was too strong and has
been corrected.  The old broad transversal-code target was also stale:
asymptotically good codes with transversal CCZ appeared in 2024 and
addressable qubit constructions in 2025.  The live asymptotic target adds
LDPC (bounded check weight) and linear distance.

## Reading order

- `mds.md`: exact point at which the PRS/MDS implication fails.
- `projective-planes.md`: what survives without coordinates and why it does
  not constrain possible orders.
- `hadamard.md`: the order-10 conference consequence and the order-668 route.
- `equiangular-lines.md`: the solved `ETF(5,10)` point versus open dimensions.
- `transversal-codes.md`: the 2024--2026 frontier shift and the local no-go.
- `independent-attack-portfolio.md`: ranked projects that do not require a
  connection to the repository, with first experiments and stop conditions.

## Decision rule

Do not name one of these famous problems in the paper as a consequence or
partial solution unless a later result crosses the dossier's explicit
promotion gate.  At present they belong, at most, in a restrained outlook
paragraph.  The independent projects are research leads, not claims that the
famous problems are easy.
