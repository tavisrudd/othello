## PHYS-2026-two-U1

Ben Gripaios and Khoi Le Nguyen Nguyen, *Anomaly cancellation for two `U(1)`
factors*, arXiv `2607.09879` (10 July 2026).

- Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.09879
- Kind: immediate source paper and frontier statement, not a general list.
- Scope: anomaly-free charges as rational linear spaces on cubic
  hypersurfaces; rank two; Segre-cubic six-fermion case.
- Read depth: `partial`, abstract, introduction, discussion-indexed passages
  and references inspected through arXiv HTML.

The paper frames systematic local anomaly cancellation for multiple abelian
factors as a formerly intractable arithmetic-geometric classification problem.
It gives rank-two examples and cites a forthcoming three-`U(1)` sequel.  Its
discussion, rather than a numbered list, is the authoritative place to extract
the next physics-facing questions.
