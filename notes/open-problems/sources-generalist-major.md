# Source catalogue: major generalist open-problem collections

**Collection date:** 2026-07-31

This is a discovery catalogue, not a claim that every old problem remains open.
It records the large, named collections that a broad comparison should not miss.
The `G-*` entries below are source sets; individual live problems are indexed in
`sources-generalist-named-problems.md`.

**Full-text count:** 0.  This pass used official or publisher landing pages,
searchable tables of contents and institutional descriptions.  Status-sensitive
work must promote the relevant source to `partial` or `full text` before use in a
paper.

## G-CLAY — Millennium Prize Problems

Clay Mathematics Institute, *The Millennium Prize Problems*.

- Source: https://www.claymath.org/millennium-problems/
- Kind: official prize list and current status page.
- Scope: seven problems selected in 2000; six remain in the official unsolved
  list and the Poincare conjecture is recorded under solved problems.
- Read depth: `partial`, official landing page and the seven problem/status
  links inspected through institutional web text; problem monographs not read.
- Authority: definitive for Clay prize status, not for all variants of each
  conjecture.

The six live prize headings are Birch--Swinnerton-Dyer, Hodge, Navier--Stokes
existence and smoothness, `P` versus `NP`, Riemann, and Yang--Mills existence and
mass gap.

## G-HILBERT — Hilbert's 23 problems, with a 2026 status review

David Hilbert, *Mathematical Problems*, English translation in Bulletin of the
AMS 8 (1902), 437--479; Evelyn Lamb, *Solved, Unsolved and Unsolvable: The
Status of Hilbert's 23 Problems in Mathematics* (Simons Foundation, 2026).

- Primary source: https://www.ams.org/journals/bull/1902-08-10/S0002-9904-1902-00923-3/
- Current review: https://www.simonsfoundation.org/2026/06/18/solved-unsolved-and-unsolvable-the-status-of-hilberts-23-problems-in-mathematics/
- Kind: canonical historical list plus a recent expert-facing status review.
- Read depth: `abstract/metadata only` for the AMS primary record; `partial` for
  the 2026 review, whose problem headings and status labels were inspected.
- Status warning: many entries are interpretation-dependent programmes, not
  clean yes/no questions.  The review explicitly treats several as
  "complicated" rather than open or solved.

The live generalist watch targets are chiefly Problem 8 (Riemann and related
prime questions), Problem 16 (real algebraic curves and limit cycles), and live
facets of Problems 1, 2, 6, 12, 20 and 22.  Do not describe that sentence as an
exhaustive modern status theorem.

## G-SMALE — Smale's 18 problems

Stephen Smale, *Mathematical Problems for the Next Century*, Mathematical
Intelligencer 20 (1998), no. 2, 7--15.

- Author/institution record: https://www.hkias.cityu.edu.hk/en/our-people/senior-fellows/professor-stephen-smale
- DOI: https://doi.org/10.1007/BF03025291
- Kind: famous mathematician's cross-field list for the twenty-first century.
- Scope: 18 problems spanning number theory, topology, dynamics, complexity,
  algorithms, economics and learning.
- Read depth: `abstract/metadata only`, author bibliography and bibliographic
  record; full article/status-by-problem not read in this pass.
- Status warning: the Poincare conjecture is solved and other entries have
  partial or claimed resolutions.  Treat every item as `refresh required`.

## G-LANDAU — Landau's four prime problems

Edmund Landau, *Geloste und ungeloste Probleme aus der Theorie der
Primzahlverteilung und der Riemannschen Zetafunktion*, address to the 1912
International Congress of Mathematicians.

- Primary source: https://www.mathunion.org/fileadmin/ICM/Proceedings/ICM1912.1/ICM1912.1.ocr.pdf
- Bibliographic record: https://eudml.org/doc/145337
- Kind: canonical short list in analytic number theory.
- Scope: Goldbach, twin primes, a prime between consecutive squares, and
  infinitely many primes of the form `n^2+1`.
- Read depth: `partial`, IMU-hosted OCR/search text around the problem address
  and EuDML metadata; complete German article not read.
- Status: all four are retained as live targets, but individual current-status
  sources are still required for manuscript use.

## G-ARNOLD — Arnold's Problems

V. I. Arnold, *Arnold's Problems*, English revised edition, Springer, 2004.

- Publisher record: https://link.springer.com/book/9783540207481
- Kind: major personal problem book, revised from the 2000 Russian edition.
- Scope: 861 problems across geometry, topology, singularity theory, dynamics,
  algebra, mechanics and mathematical physics.
- Read depth: `abstract/metadata only`, publisher/search metadata.
- Status warning: this is a historical corpus containing solved and open
  problems, not a maintained open-only list.  Use the edition's own status
  annotations and then perform a current update for any promoted item.

## G-THURSTON — Thurston's 24 questions

William P. Thurston, *Three Dimensional Manifolds, Kleinian Groups and
Hyperbolic Geometry*, Bulletin of the AMS 6 (1982), 357--381, DOI
`10.1090/S0273-0979-1982-15003-0`.

- Primary source: https://www.ams.org/bull/1982-06-03/S0273-0979-1982-15003-0/S0273-0979-1982-15003-0.pdf
- Kind: field-shaping list of 24 questions in 3-manifold and Kleinian-group
  geometry.
- Read depth: `partial`, AMS-hosted primary PDF through indexed text; opening
  and problem-list metadata inspected, not all 25 pages read.
- Status warning: this is mainly a historical success record, not a current
  open list.  Expert retrospective discussion reports Questions 1--18
  completely answered and 19--24 mostly open-ended, with a residual precise
  issue around Question 23.  Any exact modern remainder needs a fresh topology
  source; use `G-K3` for the current community list.

## G-NASH-RASSIAS — Open Problems in Mathematics

John Forbes Nash Jr. and Michael Th. Rassias, eds., *Open Problems in
Mathematics*, Springer, 2016, DOI `10.1007/978-3-319-32162-2`.

- Source: https://link.springer.com/book/10.1007/978-3-319-32162-2
- Kind: broad expert survey volume.
- Scope: 17 chapters including `P` versus `NP`, Riemann, Birch--Swinnerton-Dyer,
  Navier--Stokes, generalized Fermat equations, Plateau/unknotting questions,
  Novikov, Goldbach, Hadwiger, Hadwiger--Nelson, Erdos--Szekeres, unit distances
  and discrete logarithms.
- Read depth: `partial`, publisher overview and complete visible table of
  contents inspected; chapters not read.
- Status warning: 2016 snapshots require a 2026 refresh.

## G-ERDOS — Erdos Problems database

Thomas Bloom and collaborators, *Erdos Problems*.

- Lists: https://www.erdosproblems.com/lists
- FAQ: https://www.erdosproblems.com/faq
- Kind: maintained database keyed to Erdos's published problem lists.
- Scope: hundreds of numbered problems across number theory, combinatorics,
  graph theory, geometry and analysis; the list page currently indexes 147
  source lists and reports 65 as completely checked.
- Read depth: `partial`, list index and FAQ/status caveat inspected.
- Status warning: the maintainers explicitly say the site is not reliably up to
  date on every problem.  A database `open` label is a lead, not final proof of
  status.

## G-SIMON — Simon's mathematical-physics problems

- Author bibliography: https://www.math.caltech.edu/simon/biblio.html

## Deliberate exclusions from the authoritative spine

- Wikipedia and general web compilations may supply search terms but are not
  source authorities.
- Social-media lists, AI benchmark sets and olympiad problem archives are not
  research-open-problem catalogues.
- The Scottish Book and Dniester/Sverdlovsk notebooks are important historical
  corpora, but were not promoted in this first generalist pass because no
  current authoritative status edition was inspected.
- The Langlands programme is indispensable but is a programme, not a single
  canonical finite problem list.  Its precise conjectures enter through the
  named-problem index and specialist source files.
