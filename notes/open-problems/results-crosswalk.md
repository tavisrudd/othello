# Crosswalk: external problems versus repository results

**Baseline result inventory:** `notes/2026-07-31-results-summary-snapshot.md`,
supplemented by dated reports through 2026-07-31.  The baseline is known to be
stale and will be replaced by a refreshed result index before strong verdicts.

This file deliberately begins conservatively.  `candidate` means “worth a
bounded technical comparison,” not “likely solved.”

## Tiers

- **Famous/field-defining:** widely recognized beyond one paper or small
  specialty.
- **Recognized specialist:** explicitly posed in a current survey, maintained
  list or field-wide problem collection.
- **Paper-local/frontier:** posed by a research paper or workshop but not yet
  established as a community landmark.

- the winner of the cap/Nofil game on `PG(2,q)` for odd `q`;
- semilinear reconstruction from continuation graphs;
- the Gram--discriminant shadow of four points: its reconstruction dichotomy and
  marking fibres, and the finite-field strata of its square-class census
  (snapshot §19);
- Golden response/Pfaffian and two-`U(1)` synthesis;
- conference-cut local retractions and the `36 -> 6` marking quotient;
- exceptional `E_8` differential-operator algebra and spectrum.

These may be strong original programmes without being famous pre-existing
problems.  Their absence from current lists is evidence about positioning, not
about mathematical value.
