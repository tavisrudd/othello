# C909 — spectral packet versus PD-defect: converse audit

Date: 2026-08-12  
Status: structural theorem/converse audit; durable note only; no manuscript,
PDF, mirror, Lean, or certificate edit

## Executive classification

There are three statements that must not be conflated:

1. A transverse self-adjoint graph has a finite-etale slope algebra if and
   only if, after unramified faithfully flat splitting, its gluing kernel has
   a projective finite-etale spectral packet.
2. A block-respecting filtered finite-etale packet implies vanishing of the
   marked cohomological divided-power defect
   \(\operatorname{PDDef}^k=\operatorname{DP}^k/P^k\) in every degree.
3. Vanishing of \(\operatorname{PDDef}^*\) implies the existence of a
   finite-etale packet.

The first is a chart/packet equivalence. The second is the C909 saturation
theorem (non-CM full NS, or the conditional CM Hermitian extension in the
companion note). The third is **false**. PD-defect vanishing is a weaker
integral-product condition and cannot classify the spectral regime.

The strongest safe theorem is therefore the one-way implication

\[
 \text{projective finite-etale spectral packet}
 \Longrightarrow
 \operatorname{PDDef}^k=0\quad(0\le k\le g),                    \tag{1}
\]

with no converse. The packet is the maximal chart-free positive criterion on
the marked elliptic-power gluing groupoid, not a necessary invariant of the
abstract PD quotient.

## Why no PDDef converse can hold

Fix a prime \(p>g-1\). For every \(1\le k\le g\), \(k!\in\mathbf Z_p^\times\).
For any integral divisor lattice \(N\), independently of its graph slope,

\[
 D^{[k]}=D^k/k!=(k!)^{-1}D^k\in
 \operatorname{im}(\operatorname{Sym}^kN\to H^{2k}),       \tag{2}
\]

and products of such divided powers are ordinary products. Hence

\[
                         \operatorname{PDDef}^*\{p\}=0             \tag{3}
\]

for every graph, including non-etale and nilpotent graphs. The spectral packet
is a genuinely stronger condition at this prime, so (3) disproves an iff even
before any geometric realization issue.

There is also a local rank-one separation. Take a depth-one block over an odd
local prime with

\[
 B=\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\1&0\end{pmatrix},\qquad
 T=\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\0&0\end{pmatrix}.                         \tag{4}
\]

Then \(BT^t=TB\), but \(T\bmod p\) is a nonzero nilpotent Jordan block, so
the slope algebra is not finite etale. Writing a symmetric coefficient as
\(A=\left[\begin{smallmatrix}x&y\\y&z\end{smallmatrix}\right]\), the graph
integrality conditions in the standard depth-one basis give

\[
                         x,y\in pR,\qquad z\in p^2R.       \tag{5}
\]

The cross ideal has exponent \(1<\lceil(1+2)/2\rceil=2\), so the exact DVR
rank-one criterion fails. Nevertheless, if \(p>g-1=1\), (2) still forces
PD-defect vanishing. Thus even rank-one generation is stronger than PDDef
vanishing; it is a sufficient proof mechanism, not a converse.

The example is an algebraic self-adjoint graph test, not a claim that every
such local pair has already been globalized to a positive complex ppav. It is
enough to falsify the proposed formal iff. A global positive coefficient form
can have the displayed hyperbolic local form at suitable odd primes, but no
globalization is needed for the logical obstruction.

## What is genuinely equivalent

For a marked self-dual elliptic gluing presentation
\((V,\omega;M,G;K)\), retain the tensor ruling and the source depth
filtration. A projective packet after unramified base change is

\[
 M_{R'}=\perp_{s\in S}M_s,\qquad
 K_{R'}=\bigoplus_s\ell_s\otimes M_s,                       \tag{6}
\]

with each \(M_s\) nondegenerate and the projective lines \(\ell_s\) pairwise
distinct modulo \(p\). Choosing a transverse line gives affine slopes
\(\ell_s=\langle e+t_sf\rangle\), and the self-adjoint operator with roots
\(t_s\) has

\[
                         R'[T]\simeq\prod_sR'.             \tag{7}
\]

Conversely, splitting a finite-etale slope algebra gives (6) by its primitive
idempotents. This is the correct equivalence:

\[
 \text{finite-etale graph datum}
 \Longleftrightarrow
 \text{projective finite-etale spectral packet}.             \tag{8}
\]

It is invariant under transverse elliptic ruling changes by the projective
fractional-linear action. It is not invariant under arbitrary automorphisms
of the finite symplectic discriminant module, which need not lift to the
elliptic tensor subspace or preserve decomposable coefficient forms.

## Exact positive theorem and its scope

Let \(N_{G,K}\) be the integral divisor lattice in the marked gluing
presentation, \(P^k\) the ordinary product image, and \(DP^k\) the envelope
generated by integral divided powers. The split packet gives a
matrix-of-ideals lattice. In every cross slot its exponent satisfies

\[
 e_{ij}\ge \max(a_i,a_j)\ge
 \left\lceil\frac{a_i+a_j}{2}\right\rceil.                 \tag{9}
\]

The exact rank-one criterion and the signed three-term straightening identity
then give integral square-zero rank-one divisors. Expanding a divided power of
their sum proves (1), and finite free unramified descent returns to the base.
This argument is all-degree and denominator-free, including dyadic places.

For a CM source, (1) is currently conditional on the full NS lattice being
the free Rosati-Hermitian matrix-of-ideals lattice; see
2026-08-12-c909-cm-hermitian-pd-audit.md. The non-CM theorem uses the standard
\(\operatorname{End}^0(E)=\mathbf Q\) identification, so there the marked
divisor lattice is full NS.

## The ambient Hodge/product quotient is a different invariant

The positive theorem compares divided powers of NS with ordinary products. It
does not compare the full integral Hodge lattice with ordinary products. For
the one-depth non-CM finite-etale packet, the exact proven codimension-two
calculation is

\[
 \operatorname{Hdg}^{4}(A,\mathbf Z)/P_A^2
 \simeq (\mathbf Z/p^a)^{\binom g4}.                       \tag{10}
\]

After splitting, the degree-four invariant space decomposes by support
multidegree. The \((2,2)\) and \((2,1,1)\) components are product-saturated.
On each four-distinct-slot support, the two-dimensional matching module has
one Pluecker cancellation: the Hodge lattice starts at \(p^{3a}\), while the
product lattice starts at \(p^{4a}\). The quotient is one copy of
\(R/p^aR\), and the \(\binom g4\) supports descend by faithful flatness.

This defect coexists with (1): the extra Hodge classes are not divided powers
of divisors, so NS-PD saturation cannot remove them. At CM points the Hodge
group and NS lattice change; (10) is a non-CM \(\mathrm{SL}_2\)-invariant
statement and should not be extended to CM without a separate
unitary/Hermitian invariant calculation.

## Higher representation-theoretic filtration: exact boundary

The natural all-degree candidate decomposes the split invariant space by
multidegrees \((2^{k-\ell},1^{2\ell})\). The squarefree matching module of
semilength \(\ell\) has a Dyck-height filtration; the predicted elementary
divisors are

\[
 \bigoplus_{h=1}^{\ell-1}
 (R/p^{a(\ell-h)}R)^{H(\ell,h)},                  \tag{11}
\]

with the support multiplicity
\(\binom g{k+\ell}\binom{k+\ell}{k-\ell}\). Formula (11) is proved for
\(\ell=2\) and checked in low widths, but the required integral saturated
unit-pivot/filtered-web lemma is not yet proved. It is therefore a
**prediction**, not a theorem. In particular, no current claim should say
that finite-etale packets classify the entire ambient Hodge/product quotient;
they only supply the NS-PD vanishing and the clean unit-difference input to
the conjectural Dyck filtration.

## Final grade and next theorem

**GO:** packet/graph equivalence and finite-etale \(\Rightarrow\) marked
PDDef-vanishing are structurally sound under the stated graph/depth
hypotheses. **MAJOR overclaim:** replacing this implication by
PDDef-vanishing \(\Leftrightarrow\) finite-etale packet, or replacing NS-PD
by ambient Hodge/product equality. The factorial-unit argument (2) is the
earliest exact failure of the converse.

The strongest next theorem that is both intrinsic and honest is:

> On the marked self-dual elliptic gluing groupoid, the projective spectral
> packet is equivalent to finite-etale graph data and is a sufficient
> all-degree certificate for \(\operatorname{PDDef}^*=0\). The first ambient
> obstruction is the Pluecker quotient (10); higher ambient defects are
> governed by a Dyck-height filtration conditional on an integral saturated
> web lemma.

This theorem cleanly separates the cycle detector (ambient minimal/Hodge
class) from the atom detector (marked NS-PD saturation); neither detector
alone classifies the other.
