## Result

The first-normal-jet gate is generically positive.

Let \(D\cong\overline M_{0,6}\) be an \(A_1\) boundary divisor in Naruki's
\(W(E_6)\)-equivariant compactification of marked cubic-surface moduli.
Yoshida's ambient Coble embedding uses the unique ten-dimensional
\(W(E_6)\)-representation.  On the conic chart \(Q=0\), five selected
coordinates survive and five are divisible by \(Q\):
\[
 (y_{11},y_{12},y_{13},y_{15},y_{19}),\qquad
 (y_1,y_3,y_4,y_5,y_7)=Q(f_0,\ldots,f_4).
\]
After common projective rescaling, the surviving coordinates are
\[
 s=\bigl(
 -z_1(z_2-z_3),\ z_1-z_2,\ z_1-z_3,\
 z_3(z_1-1),\ -z_3(z_1-z_2)
 \bigr).
\]
They satisfy the Segre cubic equation
\[
\begin{aligned}
S={}&s_0s_1s_3+s_0s_2s_4-s_1s_2s_4+s_1s_3s_4\\
   &+s_2^2s_4-s_2s_3s_4.
\end{aligned}
\]
Exact evaluation on \(125\) deterministic chart points gives rank \(34\)
among the \(35\) cubic monomials, certifying that this is the unique cubic
relation in the marked presentation.

The first normal coefficients of the vanishing half are
\[
\begin{aligned}
f={}&\bigl(
-z_1(z_1-1)(z_2-z_3),\\
&z_2z_3(z_1-1)(z_2-z_3),\\
&-(z_1-z_2)z_3(z_3-1),\\
&-(z_1-z_3)z_2(z_2-1),\\
&(z_1-1)(z_1-z_3)z_2(z_3-1)
\bigr).
\end{aligned}
\]
With
\[
M=
\begin{pmatrix}
0&0&0&0&-2\\
0&2&2&0&0\\
2&0&0&0&0\\
2&0&0&2&-2\\
2&2&0&0&-2
\end{pmatrix},
\qquad \det M=32,
\]
the exact polynomial identity is
\[
 M\,\nabla S(s)=-2f.
\]
Thus the normal jet of the anti-invariant half of the ambient
ten-dimensional \(W(E_6)\) system is, after one constant target basis
change, precisely the Segre-to-Igusa polar map.

This supplies the sought operator-level common \(E_6\) parent on the dense
six-point locus.  The C705 matrix \(A\) is the differential of the same
Segre system in the centered six-point presentation; its left kernel
\(W\) is the polar vector recovered above.  Hence the rank-one adjugate
factorization is the boundary differential shadow of the ambient Coble
system, not merely an analogy between its two Mori contractions.

## Compactified lift and the \(B_3\) factor

Schock's restriction formulas, in the basis \((B_2,B_3)\), are
\[
 L_{\rm Seg}|_D=\frac{B_2+3B_3}{5},\qquad
 N_{D/Y}=\frac{-B_2-3B_3}{5},\qquad
 L_{\rm Igu}|_D=\frac{2B_2+B_3}{5}.
\]
Therefore the raw normal jet has class
\[
 L_{\rm Seg}|_D-N_{D/Y}
 =\frac{2B_2+6B_3}{5}
 =L_{\rm Igu}|_D+B_3.
\]
The missing fixed-factor statement is also exact.  At a generic point of
\(D_{123|456}\), write
\[
 z_1=a+tu,\qquad z_2=a+tv,\qquad z_3=a+tw.
\]
All five normal coefficients vanish to order exactly one in \(t\).
The ten \(B_3\) divisors form one \(S_6\)-orbit, so the whole \(B_3\) is a
common fixed divisor.  The divisor-class difference is exactly \(B_3\);
after division, the jet system has class \(L_{\rm Igu}\) and agrees with
the Igusa polar system on the dense open set.  Hence it agrees globally as
the morphism defined by that basepoint-free system.

Thus the compactified statement is:
\[
 \frac{\text{first normal jet of the anti-invariant Coble half}}
      {\text{canonical }B_3\text{ section}}
 =
 \text{Igusa polar system}.
\]
The boundary twist is the mechanism making the ambient and divisor-level
linear systems compatible, not an obstruction left unresolved.
