# C928: structural saturation of the integral symplectic Lefschetz map

**Date:** 2026-08-20

**Status:** structural proof complete; no finite certificate used

## Theorem

Let `Lambda` be a free unimodular symplectic lattice of rank `2g`, with
`g >= 4`, polarization

\[
\Theta=\sum_{i=1}^g e_i\wedge f_i
\]

in a symplectic basis, and divided square

\[
\Theta^{[2]}=\Theta^2/2.
\]

For

\[
L_3:\bigwedge^3\Lambda\longrightarrow\bigwedge^5\Lambda,
\qquad a\longmapsto\Theta\wedge a,
\]

let `Sat` be the saturation of its image. Then

\[
\operatorname{Sat}
=L_3\bigwedge^3\Lambda+\Theta^{[2]}\wedge\Lambda,
\]

and the coordinate-free map

\[
\tau:\Lambda/2\Lambda\longrightarrow
\operatorname{Sat}/L_3\bigwedge^3\Lambda,
\qquad
z\longmapsto[\Theta^{[2]}\wedge z]
\]

is an isomorphism.  Consequently the nonzero elementary divisors of `L_3`
are all one except for exactly `2g` copies of two.  At `g=5` they are

\[
1^{110},2^{10}.
\]

The construction is natural for every symplectic automorphism of `Lambda`.

## Pair-occupancy decomposition

Put `x_i=e_i wedge f_i`.  An exterior basis monomial has, at each symplectic
pair, one of four states: empty, the singleton `e_i`, the singleton `f_i`, or
the full pair `x_i`.

Fix a singleton set `S`, a choice of `e_i` or `f_i` for every `i in S`, and
write `u_(S,epsilon)` for their exterior product.  If `T` is disjoint from
`S`, write `x_T` for the product of the `x_i`, `i in T`.  The span

\[
B^r_{S,\epsilon}
=\langle u_{S,\epsilon}\wedge x_T:|T|=r\rangle
\]

is preserved by the occupancy label under multiplication by `Theta`, and
on it `L` is the unsigned up-incidence map

\[
U_{n,r}:\mathbf Z^{\binom nr}\longrightarrow
\mathbf Z^{\binom n{r+1}},
\qquad [T]\longmapsto\sum_{j\notin T}[T\cup\{j\}],
\]

where `n=g-|S|`.  Degree two causes no commutation sign.

For degree three there are only two cases:

1. `|S|=3`, `r=0`: the block is `U_(g-3,0)`, sending one to the all-ones
   vector.  Its image is primitive.
2. `|S|=1`, `r=1`: the block is `U_(g-1,1)`, the unsigned vertex-to-edge
   incidence map of the complete graph `K_(g-1)`.

This is an integral direct-sum decomposition of `L_3`; no rational Lefschetz
decomposition is being imported.

## Smith form of the complete-graph block

Let `n>=3`.  The matrix of `U_(n,1)` has one column for every vertex and one
row for every edge; the row `{i,j}` has ones in columns `i,j`.

The map is injective: equations `a_i+a_j=0` on every edge of a triangle
force all `a_i=0`.  An `(n-1)`-minor is one: use rows `{1,j}` for
`j=2,...,n` and columns `2,...,n`.  Thus the first `n-1` Smith factors are
one.

Every `n`-minor is even because modulo two the sum of all columns is zero.
There is an `n`-minor of absolute value two: take the three triangle rows
`{1,2},{1,3},{2,3}` and, for `k=4,...,n`, the row `{1,k}`.  Expanding the
last `n-3` columns reduces its determinant to that of the unsigned triangle
incidence matrix, which is `-2`.  Therefore

\[
\operatorname{SNF}(U_{n,1})=\operatorname{diag}(1^{n-1},2).
\]

Its saturated-image quotient is generated by the all-edge vector `w`.  It
has order two because the sum of all vertex columns is `2w`; it is not in the
image because the triangle equations `a_i+a_j=1` have no integral solution.

## Identification of the torsion generators

Since the `x_i` have even degree and square to zero,

\[
\Theta^{[2]}=\sum_{i<j}x_i\wedge x_j.
\]

For a singleton `z=e_k` or `f_k`, all terms containing `x_k` vanish, so

\[
\Theta^{[2]}\wedge z
=z\wedge\sum_{\substack{i<j\\i,j\ne k}}x_i\wedge x_j.
\]

This is precisely the all-edge vector in the singleton block indexed by
`z`.  The `2g` singleton blocks are distinct direct summands, so these
classes are independent and exhaust the saturation defect.  This proves the
theorem.

Although a symplectic basis was used to calculate the Smith form, `tau` is
defined solely by `Theta` and its integral divided square.  Hence for
`h in Sp(Lambda,Theta)`,

\[
\tau(hz)=\bigwedge^5h\,\tau(z),
\]

which proves full symplectic naturality, not merely the finite-generator
checks in the C908 certificate.

## Effect on the C928 dependency graph

This replaces C908 compression item K1 and the Smith-form portion of the
certificate by a coordinate-free theorem valid for all `g>=4`.

The remaining glue problem is now sharply separated.  Since `tau` is an
isomorphism, the onto half of the geometric glue `rho` will follow from the
single endpoint formula

\[
b_*q_*\mu^*(\beta\otimes1)
\equiv\Theta^{[2]}\wedge y(\beta)
\pmod{L_3\bigwedge^3\Lambda},
\]

together with the integral Clemens--Griffiths/cylinder isomorphism making
`y` unimodular.  Establishing this Pontryagin/divided-power identity from
the geometric correspondences, with its exact sign convention, is the next
structural proof.  Full integral surjectivity of `q_*mu^*` additionally asks
that its diagonal-zero part generate the kernel `b^* wedge^3 Lambda`; that is
a separate statement and is not smuggled into the saturation theorem.
