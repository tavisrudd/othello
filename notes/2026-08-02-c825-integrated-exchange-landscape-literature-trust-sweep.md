## Opening verdict

This audit individually characterizes **eleven sources**: **five were read at
`full text` depth**, three at `partial` depth, and three at
`abstract/metadata only` depth.  The five full-text reads include the four
closest ETF/principal-spectrum sources and the primary construction of the
order-six Hermitian conference family.  The inaccessible sources and bounded
search limits are explicit below.

The strongest defensible single-paper package is an **integrated theorem-first
exchange-landscape paper**, subject to the wording and access qualifications
in this report.  The sweep changes the trust assessment in two ways.

1. It confirms a substantial established boundary: complex ETF switching is
   encoded by triple products; ordinary three-subframe spectra depend on the
   real part of the triple product; ordinary complex three-uniformity selects
   the purely imaginary/skew class; and ordinary Hermitian principal-spectrum
   monomorphy is already classified.  The order-six Hermitian conference
   family and the existence of an order-six classification are also prior
   art.
2. Inside that boundary, the audited sources and bounded searches did not
   locate the paper's sign-blind squared/singular-spectrum rigidity, joint
   continuous-control Schur optimum, phase-uniform control bounds, exact
   exchange Pareto segment, holonomy-defect exchange identities, or
   defect-to-real-orbit stability theorem.  These remain qualified candidates,
   with the stability theorem the strongest single novelty claim.

The conceptual contrast that makes the package coherent is

\[
 \text{ordinary spectrum}\ \longleftrightarrow\ \operatorname{Re}z_T
 \ \longleftrightarrow\ \text{purely imaginary/skew class},
\]

versus

\[
 \text{squared exchange spectrum}\ \longleftrightarrow\
 (\operatorname{Re}z_T)^2
 \ \longleftrightarrow\ \text{real conference class}.
\]

This is a **qualified novelty clearance**, not a priority claim.  “We prove”
is supported; “first”, “new”, and an unqualified “to our knowledge” are not.

## What the closest prior work establishes

### Ordinary erasure uniformity is not squared-spectrum uniformity

The foundational real-erasure source defines uniformity through the norm of
erasure compressions, represents equiangular tight frames by signature/Seidel
matrices, proves that nontrivial real three-uniform ETFs do not occur, and
computes exact averages of three-erasure norms from switching-class counts.
The complex sequel shows that a three-by-three compression is controlled by
the real part of a dephased phase and classifies nontrivial complex
three-uniform ETFs by purely imaginary/skew Seidel data.  The recent
homogeneity paper records triple products as complete switching invariants and
places this three-uniform classification in its current symmetry context.

The Golden condition is different in two load-bearing respects.  It passes
from a principal compression to the complementary cross-block Gram operator,
and it squares the triangle contribution.  Consequently it depends on
\((\operatorname{Re}z_T)^2\), forgets the sign seen by ordinary spectra, and
selects the real rather than the purely imaginary endpoint.  This distinction
must appear in the theorem statement and related-work paragraph, not merely in
a proof footnote.

The general Hermitian spectral-monomorphy source is also close but distinct:
it classifies equality of the characteristic polynomials of the principal
blocks themselves.  It does not classify equality after squaring, passing to
complementary cross-block singular values, or applying Schur functors.

### The conference objects are prior; the exchange landscape is the candidate

The order-six one-parameter Hermitian family used to realize the Pareto edge
is explicitly constructed in prior work.  A later publisher abstract states
that all order-six Hermitian conference matrices are classified, but the
article body was not reached in this audit.  The associated seminar material
uses an equivalence relation with independent left and right diagonal unitary
factors, broader on its face than diagonal Hermitian conjugation.

The integrated paper therefore must cite the family as prior, use it only for
attainability, and keep the rigidity and stability proofs classification-free.
It must not infer a switching-orbit normal form from the inaccessible
classification theorem.

### Why the stability theorem carries the headline

The stability theorem promotes a qualitative rigidity statement into an
explicit metric result.  Its safe form is

\[
 \frac{10}{3}\delta(C)\le d_{\mathbb R}(C)^2,
\]

for every order-six Hermitian conference matrix, together with

\[
 d_{\mathbb R}(C)^2\le40\delta(C)
 \quad\text{when}\quad
 \delta(C)<\frac{6-\sqrt{35}}{20}.
\]

The audited literature establishes switching invariants and exact uniformity,
but no located result turns averaged squared triangle defect into distance to
the real conference orbit.  The proof's dephasing, sign rounding, conference
equation, and even-integral gap also make it structurally explainable.  State
the locality and non-sharp constants explicitly.

## Decisive package recommendation

Choose the **integrated headline**, not real-control-only strengthening and
not a sequel-only split.  The strongest single paper should have the following
spine:

1. the observable/Schur exchange dictionary;
2. the exact real Golden benchmark upgraded from Boolean controls to the full
   continuous cube;
3. the Hermitian order-six triangle-holonomy formulas and phase-uniform
   bounds;
4. the exact mixed-plane Pareto segment;
5. one-sector squared-spectrum rigidity and its averaged defect; and
6. the quantitative stability theorem, proved by the compressed
   parity/Pfaffian/rounding mechanism.

The prior literature supplies the contrast rather than displacing the package:
ordinary spectrum sees \(\operatorname{Re}z_T\), while exchange-square data sees
\((\operatorname{Re}z_T)^2\).  The stability theorem then turns that lost sign
into an exact real-orbit conclusion near zero defect.  That is a stronger and
more unified paper story than either the present operational manuscript or a
standalone complex-conference sequel.

### Blockers before manuscript mutation

1. The Et-Taoui--Makhlouf full text remains inaccessible.  Either obtain it
   before describing its classification beyond the publisher abstract, or
   omit all detail and keep the new proofs classification-free.
2. The Cheng--Lv--Sun full theorem body remains inaccessible.  Either obtain it
   before making a literature-wide squared-spectrum absence claim, or state
   only the direct definitional distinction and retain qualified wording.
3. Independently proof-check and reproduce every paper-facing theorem under
   the repository's reproducibility conventions.  This audit checks
   precedence and positioning, not mathematical correctness.
4. The stability theorem must remain accurately local on its reverse side;
   neither its constants nor its threshold are known sharp.

With those constraints, access gaps do **not** force a sequel.  They force
conservative attribution and the removal of priority language.  The
integrated mathematical package itself is the highest-EV paper.
