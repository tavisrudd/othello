# C909 — marked finite-etale Hecke locus: two independent certificates on cubic threefolds

Date: 2026-08-12

Status: theorem-grade synthesis of established C909 finite-etale saturation,
Voisin's cubic criterion, and the established one-step atom theorem.  The
moduli statement is deliberately **marked**.  No manuscript, PDF, mirror,
Lean, or commit change.

## The theorem

Work over `C`.  Define the marked finite-etale elliptic-Hecke stack
`H_5^et` as follows.  An object is

\[
 (E,(A,\Theta),f,\{\mathfrak c_p\}_{p\mid\deg f}),
 \qquad f:E^5\longrightarrow A,
\tag{1}
\]
\]

where `(A,Theta)` is principally polarized, `f` is a polarized elliptic-power
isogeny, and each `c_p` is a permitted transverse elliptic graph presentation
of the `p`-primary Lagrangian kernel.  Its source coefficient polarization is
an orthogonal sum of blocks `p^aB` with `B` unimodular, the graph is
block-respecting and self-adjoint in the declared coefficient convention, and
the corresponding literal slope algebra over `Z/p^a` is finite etale.  The
same condition is imposed at every bad prime.  This is exactly the marked
finite-etale regime of the C909 graph theorem.

For fixed elementary divisors, block ranks, and finite-etale slope algebras,
these data form an ordinary finite Hecke correspondence over the elliptic
power locus.  The notation `H_5^et` denotes their union; it is not asserted
to be one finite-type closed substack of `A_5`.  Let

\[
 \pi:\mathfrak H_5^{\rm et}\longrightarrow\mathcal A_5
\tag{2}
\]
\]

be the forgetful map and let

\[
 \mathcal C_3^{\rm et}:=
 \mathcal C_3^{\rm sm}\times_{\mathcal A_5}\mathfrak H_5^{\rm et},
\tag{3}
\]
\]

where the map from the smooth cubic-threefold moduli stack is the
intermediate-Jacobian period map.

> **Marked etale-Hecke two-certificate theorem.**  For every marked point
> `(X,h)` of `C_3^et`:
>
> 1. the integral divided-power envelope of `NS(J(X))` equals its ordinary
>    divisor-product image in cohomology in every degree;
> 2. the primitive minimal class
>    \(\Theta_{J(X)}^4/4!\) is an ordinary product combination of four
>    divisor classes, hence is algebraic;
> 3. `X` is universally `CH_0`-trivial; and
> 4. `X × P^1` is irrational.

Items 3 and 4 are certificates of opposite flavour: the first is supplied by
an integral cycle calculation on the intermediate Jacobian and the second by
the independent formal-monodromy/carrier exclusion.  Neither proof uses the
other certificate.

Because 3 and 4 are properties of `X`, not of the mark `h`, the same
conclusions hold for every smooth cubic whose intermediate Jacobian lies in
the set-theoretic image of (2).  This is the unmarked corollary; it does not
say that finite etaleness is an invariant of the bare ppav.

## Proof

The finite-etale graph theorem gives, at every bad prime,

\[
 \operatorname{PD}\langle\operatorname{NS}(A)\rangle^r
 =\operatorname{im}\bigl(
 \operatorname{Sym}^r\operatorname{NS}(A)\to H^{2r}(A,\mathbf Z)
 \bigr)
 \quad(0\le r\le5).
\tag{4}
\]
\]

Its proof is local on the marked graph presentation: unramified finite-etale
splitting produces orthogonal idempotent blocks, the coefficient lattice is
generated by square-zero rank-one divisor forms, and faithful-flat descent
returns ordinary-product membership without a trace denominator.  Applying
(4) to the descended principal polarization gives

\[
 \frac{\Theta_A^r}{r!}\in P_A^r
 \quad(0\le r\le5).
\tag{5}
\]

In particular `r=4` proves item 2 for `A=J(X)`.  A product of divisor
classes is algebraic, so Voisin's equivalence for a smooth cubic threefold
implies item 3:

\[
 \frac{\Theta_{J(X)}^4}{4!}\text{ algebraic}
 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 X\text{ universally }CH_0\text{-trivial}.
\tag{6}
\]

Item 4 is independent of (1)--(6).  The rank-two cubic formal block has
primitive sixth-root formal monodromy, no smooth projective carrier of
dimension at most two has that monodromy, and the projective-bundle and
weak-factorization formulas for abstract atoms preserve the resulting
nonzero additive detector.  The carrier criterion applied to the fourfold
`X×P^1` therefore proves irrationality for **every** smooth cubic `X`.
Restricting that theorem to the Hecke locus proves item 4.

This three-line factorization is the real proof compression:

\[
 \text{marked finite-etale graph}
 \xRightarrow{\rm PD/product}
 \Theta^{[4]}\text{ algebraic}
 \xRightarrow{\rm Voisin}
 \text{universal }CH_0,
 \qquad
 \text{cubic atom}\xRightarrow{\rm carrier}\ X\times\mathbf P^1
 \text{ irrational}.
\tag{7}
\]
\]

There is no asserted arrow between the two branches of (7).

## The explicit positive-dimensional component

The smooth one-parameter `A_5` cubic pencil supplies a positive-dimensional
subfamily of the image in (2).  Its six elliptic quotient axes give a
polarized isogeny of type

\[
 E^5\longrightarrow J(X),\qquad
 f^*\Theta\sim 6I_5-J_5.
\tag{8}
\]

At two, its exotic kernel is the graph of either primitive scalar in
`End_{A_5}(H)=F_4`; this is a finite-etale coefficient algebra.  At three,
the corresponding scalar graph is likewise finite etale.  The
block-respecting local finite-etale graph theorem therefore applies to the
marked six-axis presentation.  Choosing an exotic member is a quadratic
marking; after that finite etale marking cover the pencil lifts to
`H_5^et`.  Forgetting the marking shows that the original, non-isotrivial
smooth pencil lies in the image of (2).

The marking issue is real but harmless for the stated conclusion.  The
exotic pair may be exchanged by its quadratic deck involution, whereas both
ordinary product membership and universal `CH_0`-triviality live on the
target cubic and hence descend.  No global relative divisor cycle is claimed
on the unmarked pencil.

## Exact ambient integral-Hodge contrast in the one-depth sublocus

The preceding theorem says nothing about equality with the *ambient* integral
Hodge lattice.  In fact the sharp contrast is visible already in dimension
five.  Suppose in addition that the marked presentation has exactly one
nontrivial graph prime `p`, one equal-depth block `p^aB` of rank five, a
finite-etale slope algebra, and `E` is non-CM.  Then the direct four- and
six-slot calculations give

\[
 \begin{aligned}
 \operatorname{Hdg}^{4}(A,\mathbf Z)/P_A^2
     &\simeq(\mathbf Z/p^a)^{5},\\
 \operatorname{Hdg}^{6}(A,\mathbf Z)/P_A^3
     &\simeq(\mathbf Z/p^a)^{5},\\
 \operatorname{Hdg}^{8}(A,\mathbf Z)/P_A^4
     &=0.
 \end{aligned}
\tag{9}
\]

Indeed, in the low-squarefree-width theorem,

\[
 N_2=\binom54\binom{1}{k-2},\qquad N_3=0
 \quad(k=2,3),
\tag{10}
\]

so `N_2=5` in exactly those two degrees; for `k=4`,
`min(4,5-4)=1`, so no squarefree block can create an ambient defect.  Thus
the minimal class is an ordinary fourfold divisor product even though two
lower-degree integral Hodge/product quotients are nonzero.  This is a direct
multidegree calculation, not Poincare duality.

The `A_5` pencil in (8) is generally multi-prime and not being identified
with this one-depth sublocus.  Its cycle conclusion comes from the broader
marked finite-etale theorem; importing the numerical formula (9) for it
would be an unsupported conflation.

## Why this is substantive, and its exact boundary

The statement is more than the tautology "two conditions imply two
conclusions" for two reasons.

1. `H_5^et` is a checkable integral Hecke condition on a marked
   intermediate Jacobian, and the finite-etale lattice theorem converts it
   uniformly into Voisin's otherwise difficult minimal-class hypothesis.
2. The atom theorem is uniform over the whole smooth cubic moduli stack, so
   it supplies a genuinely independent irrationality certificate on the same
   non-isotrivial Hecke image.  The combination yields a moduli-theoretic
   locus of universally `CH_0`-trivial cubic threefolds with irrational
   one-step stabilizations.

The two inputs are nevertheless **orthogonal proof mechanisms**, not two
gradeds of one currently known category.  The cycle argument depends on
integral Neron--Severi lattices, rank-one coefficient forms, and finite-flat
descent.  The birational argument depends on formal connections, atom
chemical formulas, and center-dimension filtration.  The shared Eisenstein
label packet in the exotic `A_5` realization identifies a common `C_2`
orientation symmetry, but supplies neither a comparison functor nor an
implication between the two detectors.

## Hostile audit

* **Marked versus bare ppav.**  Finite etaleness belongs to the selected
  elliptic graph presentation and allowed transverse ruling changes.  It is
  not claimed to be intrinsic to every symplectic chart or to `(A,Theta)`
  without a mark.  Only the target-side conclusions descend through `pi`.
* **Image versus locus.**  The full union over isogeny degrees is a Hecke
  union, not asserted closed or finite type.  For fixed discrete data it is
  algebraic; all claims are pointwise on its image.
* **Voisin input.**  The bridge uses the cubic-specific equivalence (6), not
  a false general assertion that an algebraic ppav minimal class implies
  universal `CH_0` for an arbitrary variety.
* **No stable irrationality.**  The atom theorem stops at `P^1`; no claim is
  made for `X×P^m` with `m>=2`.
* **Ambient Hodge boundary.**  Equation (4) is divided-power versus ordinary
  divisor products.  It does not state `Hdg=P`.  Equation (9) demonstrates
  the distinction and needs the non-CM, one-depth hypotheses printed there.
* **Pencil input.**  Positive-dimensionality and the six-axis Hecke
  presentation are imported from the established `A_5`-pencil geometry.  No
  new period-map realization is proved in this note.

finite-etale integral saturation into a moduli condition that automatically
feeds Voisin, while the all-cubic atom theorem supplies the independent
one-step conclusion.  Formula (9) adds an unexpected internal contrast:
minimal-class saturation coexists with explicit lower-degree ambient Hodge
defects.

Hecke locus plus the torsor-level Eisenstein label comparison.
