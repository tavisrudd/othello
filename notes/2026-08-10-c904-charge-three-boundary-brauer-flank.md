# C904 charge-three boundary / Brauer flank

**Date:** 2026-08-10
**Scope:** quarantined Paper-V Annals research; no manuscript or Lean change

## Executive verdict

The charge-two ordering-cover obstruction does **not** pass through the
Faenzi charge-two-to-charge-three boundary as a nonzero Brauer class.  The
reason is formal but important: the Hecke parameter space is the
Severi--Brauer conic representing the charge-two class, and the function
field of a Severi--Brauer variety splits its own class.

More precisely, let \(M_2\) be the stable charge-two locus, let \(F=F(X)\),
and let

\[
 \alpha_2\in \operatorname{Br}(M_2)[2]
\]

be the moduli-gerbe class whose nonzero boundary residue is the cover ordering
the two generic Jordan--Hoelder factors.  Over the open part of
\(M_2\times F\) on which \(E|_L\simeq\mathcal O_L^2\), the quotient choices
form a Severi--Brauer conic

\[
 H=\mathbf P\!\operatorname{Hom}(E|_L,\mathcal O_L)
       \longrightarrow M_2\times F
\]

of class \(\alpha_2\).  The kernel construction

\[
 0\longrightarrow G\longrightarrow E\longrightarrow\mathcal O_L
   \longrightarrow0
\]

maps \(H\) birationally onto the generic Faenzi divisor \(B\) in the
charge-three component.  Consequently

\[
 k(B)=k(H),\qquad
 \alpha_2|_{k(B)}=0.
\]

The same remains true after passing to the generic fibre of the charge-three
Abel--Jacobi map: the boundary divisor dominates \(J(X)\), but the inherited
charge-two class is already split on its function field.

Thus the earlier shorthand “the charge-two Hecke boundary remains a nonsplit
conic” needs a precise base qualifier.  It is nonsplit **over
\(k(M_2\times F)\)**.  Its total space, hence the corresponding boundary
divisor in charge three, splits the class.  The boundary supplies neither a
nonzero unramified Brauer class on Voisin's generic fourfold nor an even-index
theorem for that fourfold.

This bounded pass used **zero full-paper reads and five claim-specific partial
primary-source reads**.  No source located here computes a global compactified
charge-three boundary, the Brauer group of Voisin's generic fourfold, its
index, rationality or stable rationality, rational simple connectedness, or
its top-intersection ring.

## 1. The generic Faenzi divisor

Faenzi Theorem 3.1, Steps 2--3, starts with a charge-\(k\) instanton \(E\), a
general nonjumping line \(L\), and a quotient \(E\twoheadrightarrow\mathcal
O_L\).  For \(k=2\), the resulting charge-three sheaf \(G\) is stable and
unobstructed.  The parameter count is

\[
 \dim M_2+\dim F+\dim\mathbf P\operatorname{Hom}
        (E|_L,\mathcal O_L)=5+2+1=8,
\]

whereas the smooth charge-three component has dimension nine.  Hence its
image is generically a divisor.

The following birational identification is an inference from the printed
theorems, not a statement quoted verbatim from Faenzi.  On the generic
non-locally-free sheaf \(G\), one intrinsically recovers

\[
 E=G^{\vee\vee},\qquad
 T=G^{\vee\vee}/G\simeq\mathcal O_L,
\]

and therefore the support line and quotient, up to the scalar already removed
by projectivization.  Comaschi--Jardim Lemma 18 and Theorem 24 prove the
double-dual and pure rank-zero-instanton assertions in this generality.
Thus the Hecke map is generically injective, so \(H\dashrightarrow B\) is
birational.

This is only a description of one generic divisor.  Neither Faenzi nor
Comaschi--Jardim proves that it is the complete boundary of the cubic
charge-three Gieseker component, or determines the other boundary strata,
normalization, divisor class, or intersection theory.

## 2. Why the charge-two class dies on the boundary

On the charge-two moduli gerbe, the rank-two vector space

\[
 \operatorname{Hom}(E|_L,\mathcal O_L)
\]

has scalar weight \(-1\).  Its projectivization descends to the coarse space
and is the Severi--Brauer conic of class \(-\alpha_2=\alpha_2\).  Set

\[
 K_2=k(M_2\times F).
\]

The generic conic \(H_{K_2}\) has a rational point over its own function
field, namely its generic point.  A Severi--Brauer conic with a rational point
is split, so

\[
 \alpha_2|_{K_2(H)}=0.
\]

Equivalently, this is the elementary direction of Amitsur's generic-splitting
theorem; the full theorem identifies the kernel
\(\operatorname{Br}(K_2)\to\operatorname{Br}(K_2(H))\) with the cyclic group
generated by \(\alpha_2\).

Since \(k(B)=K_2(H)\), the charge-two class is zero at the generic point of the
charge-three boundary.  Relative double dual gives the same geometric
explanation: on the charge-three fine locus, the universal \(G\) restricts to
the boundary, and \(G^{\vee\vee}\) supplies the charge-two family after the
quotient choice has neutralized its gerbe.

The nonzero ordering cover from the charge-two compactification is not lost as
geometry: it can reappear in the degeneration of a compactified conic over the
strictly semistable charge-two divisor.  It does not survive as a Brauer class
of the total function field.  Pulling the ramified quaternion class to its
Severi--Brauer conic makes the entire class, and therefore all of its pulled
residues, zero.

## 3. Restriction to Voisin's generic fourfold

For \([G]=[E]-[\mathcal O_L]\), additivity of \(c_2\) and Abel--Jacobi gives,
up to the harmless global sign/translation convention,

\[
 AJ_3(G)=AJ_2(E)+AJ(L).
\]

The quotient coordinate in the Hecke conic does not affect this value.
Because \(M_2\) is an open subset of a translate of \(J(X)\), the addition map

\[
 M_2\times F\longrightarrow J(X)
\]

is dominant; already a fixed general line gives a translation of an open
subset of \(J(X)\).  Therefore \(B\to J(X)\) is dominant.  Its intersection
with the generic fourfold fibre \(V/k(J(X))\) is generically a divisor of
dimension three.

But \(k(B_{\eta_J})=k(B)\) viewed as an extension of \(k(J)\), and the preceding
splitting says

\[
 \alpha_2|_{k(B_{\eta_J})}=0.
\]

Thus the Faenzi divisor cannot be used to specialize a nonzero inherited
charge-two class on \(V\).  This does **not** prove \(\operatorname{Br}(V)=0\):
an unrelated intrinsic Brauer class could exist.  It proves only that the
specific charge-two ordering-cover route carries no such class.

## 4. Purity and the degree mismatch

On a regular model \(V\), two-primary purity gives the residue gate

\[
 0\longrightarrow\operatorname{Br}(V)[2]
 \longrightarrow\operatorname{Br}(k(V))[2]
 \xrightarrow{\oplus\partial_Z}
 \bigoplus_{Z\in V^{(1)}}H^1(k(Z),\mathbf Z/2).
\]

Two cautions close the proposed extension argument.

1. An **unramified** class must have zero residue at the Faenzi divisor.  Using
   the charge-two ordering cover as a nonzero residue would instead construct
   a ramified class.
2. The ordering cover is originally an element of
   \(H^1(k(D_2),\mathbf Z/2)\), where \(D_2\) is the strictly semistable
   divisor of the charge-two compactification.  It is not itself a Brauer
   class on the charge-three boundary, and there is no canonical purity map
   carrying a codimension-one residue on \(M_2\) to an unramified
   codimension-zero class on \(V\).

Even after choosing compactifications and resolving rational maps, the only
natural Brauer class available before taking residues is \(\alpha_2\), and it
is killed by the Hecke conic.  Purity can test a proposed class; it does not
construct a replacement after that class has vanished.

## 5. What the compactification sources actually provide

- **Druel is charge two only.**  Lemma 4.3 and Theorem 4.6 give the smooth
  fivefold and the Luna slice at \(I_{\ell_1}\oplus I_{\ell_2}\); Theorem 4.8
  identifies its coarse compactification with a blow-up of \(J(X)\).  This is
  the source of the ordering-cover residue, not a charge-three boundary
  theorem.
- **Faenzi is local near one charge-three boundary family.**  He proves
  stability, unobstructedness, deformation to bundles, and the codimension-one
  parameter count.  He does not build or normalize the complete cubic
  charge-three compactification.
- **Comaschi--Jardim classify the defect, not this moduli space.**  Their
  Theorem 24 says every rank-two non-locally-free instanton has an instanton
  bundle as double dual and a rank-zero instanton quotient.  Their detailed
  compactified-moduli theorem concerns charge two on a quadric threefold, not
  charge three on a cubic.
- **Voisin's \(M_9\) is an open bundle moduli component.**  Theorem 2.1 uses a
  desingularization of the Hilbert closure of elliptic sextics and a dominant
  rational map to the nine-dimensional moduli of stable bundles.  It proves
  geometrically rationally connected general Abel--Jacobi fibre, but supplies
  no proper model of that fourfold and no sheaf-boundary decomposition.

The general Gieseker--Maruyama construction supplies a proper coarse moduli
space, but the sources checked do not identify the closure of Voisin's
component inside it or prove that the Faenzi divisor is its only divisorial
boundary.

## 8. EJ + TT closeout and mystery ledger

- **Settled:** the Hecke conic is nonsplit over the charge-two base but splits
  its own Brauer class on the charge-three boundary function field.
- **Settled:** the charge-two ordering-cover residue cannot be promoted by
  purity to a nonzero unramified class on the generic \(M_9\) fourfold.
- **Settled:** no choice of compactification changes this generic splitting;
  compactification only changes how the zero pullback is represented among
  boundary components.
- **Still open:** whether the generic fourfold has an unrelated intrinsic
  Brauer class.  Gate: compute \(\operatorname{Br}(V)\) on a regular proper
  model or find an explicit Azumaya/torsor construction not inherited from
  charge two.
- **Still open:** odd index of the generic fourfold.  Gate: an intrinsic odd
  carrier such as the \(D_{3,3}\) architecture, not the Faenzi divisor.
- **Still open:** global charge-three boundary and intersection theory.  Gate:
  identify the closure of Voisin's component and calculate its divisor/Chow
  data; no checked source provides this.

**Vibe:** useful negative correction.  The charge-two obstruction is strong on
its own base, but the very Hecke choice used to enter charge three is its
generic splitting variety.  The Brauer flank therefore closes cleanly and
pushes all remaining EV back to genuinely charge-three geometry.
