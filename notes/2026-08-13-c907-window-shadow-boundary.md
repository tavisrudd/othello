# C907 — the algebraic window shadow is rank-trivial

Date: 2026-08-13

Status: exact categorical shadow and exact limitation.  Along the
pi-nonsingular AKMW chain, every unit wall has an algebraic derived-category
comparison which is the identity on the common open set and whose
complementary terms are supported on the unstable/wall stratum.  Its action
therefore preserves ordinary rank, and its induced action modulo supported
numerical `K`-classes is the identity.  This does **not** identify the
adjacent sectorial Stokes transition with that algebraic window action.
Consequently any Gold counterexample must be a purely analytic Stokes anomaly
with trivial algebraic window shadow.

## 1. Source-level input

Ballard--Favero--Katzarkov compare derived categories across a VGIT wall by
semiorthogonal decompositions whose complementary components come from the
unstable fixed stratum.  Yeung develops the corresponding weight-truncation
construction specifically for wall crossings in birational cobordisms.  His
construction embeds the derived category of the semistable quotient as a
semiorthogonal summand of a weight-truncated category, requires no ambient
smoothness for that construction, and, under the stated regularity/local-
cohomology-duality hypotheses, yields the expected fully faithful functor for
a flip and equivalence for a flop.

For C907 no new general derived theorem is required.  After Morelli
pi-desingularization, every elementary wall is a smooth unit standard wall by
`2026-08-13-c907-akmw-pi-nonsingular-circuits-are-unit.md`.  The usual
standard-flip/blowup/flop semiorthogonal decomposition applies, and its
residual blocks are images of the smooth wall stratum.

## 2. Numerical shadow

Let `X_- dashrightarrow X_+` be one oriented unit discrepant wall with smooth
wall stratum `S`.  In the direction of the fully faithful ambient functor,

\[
 K_0^{\mathrm{num}}(X_-)
 \cong K_0^{\mathrm{num}}(X_+)
       \mathbin\oplus
       \bigoplus_j K_0^{\mathrm{num}}(S)_j.                       \tag{1}
\]

Every class in a residual summand is represented by a perfect object
supported on the exceptional locus, and hence has rank zero.  The ambient
functor restricts to the identity on the common dense open set, so it
preserves rank.  Therefore

\[
 \operatorname{rk}_{X_-}(F(E))=\operatorname{rk}_{X_+}(E),
 \qquad
 \operatorname{rk}_{X_-}(K_0(S)_j)=0.                             \tag{2}
\]

Equivalently, if `C_wall` is the subgroup generated by the unstable-stratum
blocks, the window action on

\[
 K_0^{\mathrm{num}}/C_{\mathrm{wall}}                            \tag{3}
\]

is the common-open identification and fixes the rank covector.  Changing the
grade-restriction window mutates only by unstable-stratum objects, so it has
the same property.

For a finite sequence of walls, the algebraic comparisons compose without a
parameter problem: every intermediate variety has its intrinsic numerical
`K`-group, and each functor agrees with the identity on the birational common
open.  Thus the algebraic rank row is coherent along the whole chain.

## 3. Why this is not Gold

The C907 Boolean is not ordinary rank on `K_0`; it is the restriction of the
Gamma rank covector to a sectorially embedded primitive-sixth formal packet.
The two incident walls at an intermediate variety provide two different
sectorial embeddings of that packet.  Neither the BFK window theorem nor
Yeung's weight truncation contains a Gamma lattice, a central connection
matrix, or a Stokes comparison identifying those embeddings.

Thus (1)--(3) do not prove that the analytic adjacent-receiver transition is
the categorical window transition.  A Stokes automorphism can be formally
invisible and still move the embedded primitive-sixth subspace through an
ambient rank-nonzero direction.  The categorical calculation says only that
such a move would have **no algebraic window shadow**.

This turns the remaining theorem into the following sharply weaker target.

> **Numerical window-shadow comparison.**  For two incident unit walls, the
> adjacent sectorial transition and the algebraic weight-truncation/window
> transition induce the same map on numerical `K`-theory modulo the two
> unstable-stratum subgroups—or, still more weakly, they induce the same rank
> covector on the primitive-sixth packet.

This is much less than full Gamma/Orlov compatibility.  It asks for one
numerical quotient and one covector, not an equivalence of lattices or a full
Stokes matrix.

## 4. Consequence for the dangerous object

Combine this shadow with
`2026-08-13-c907-remaining-mixed-stokes-shear-gate.md`.  A dangerous neutral
rubber tower must simultaneously:

1. survive the mixed-sign incidence and virtual-dimension/contact-budget
   tests;
2. produce a nonzero ambient low-moment coupling;
3. cross the relevant ambient anti-Stokes turning wall; and
4. act nontrivially on the sectorial primitive-sixth packet while acting
   trivially on the algebraic quotient (3).

The fourth condition is the strongest description presently available: the
object would be a **categorically phantom but analytically nontrivial**
ambient shear.  Pure boundary packets, mutations by wall objects, ordinary
flop packets, and the C908/Fano-surface packet cannot have this form.

## 5. Why toroidal localization does not automatically close it

The valence/contact-budget lemma forces a surviving tower into a connected
toroidal boundary neighborhood, so it is tempting to invoke toric mirror
symmetry there.  Iritani's global toric Landau--Ginzburg theorem does prove a
formal QDM decomposition for general discrepant toric wall crossings.  Its
analytic Gamma/Stokes identification with an Orlov `K`-decomposition is
proved, however, for weighted blowups of weak-Fano compact toric stacks along
toric centres; the general discrepant Riemann--Hilbert functoriality is stated
conjecturally.

A chamber supporting the present mixed-sign shadow has an effective ray on
which `c_1=-K` is negative.  It is therefore not weak Fano.  Moreover, the two
incident unit circuits need not combine into a single weighted blowup.  Thus
the toroidal normal model supplies the right finite Mellin--Barnes regression,
but the published toric analytic theorem does not already identify its two
window orders.

This is useful localization, not closure: any remaining anomaly is confined
to a non-weak-Fano two-circuit toric normal model over the common boundary
stratum.

## EJ / TT / AA

- **EJ:** the entire derived/window side already fixes rank; the only missing
  information is whether the analytic sectorial transition casts that same
  numerical shadow.
- **TT:** do not claim that weight truncation supplies Gamma/Stokes
  compatibility.  It supplies the algebraic comparison against which the
  missing analytic transition is to be tested.
