# C909 — complete low-squarefree-width integral Hodge/product theorem

Date: 2026-08-12

Status: theorem-grade in the equal-depth finite-etale graph regime, using the
proved four- and six-slot local calculations.  No manuscript, PDF, mirror,
Lean, or commit change.

## Theorem

Let `R` be a DVR with uniformizer `p`, let `lambda=p^a` with `a>0`, and let
`A` be a one-depth polarized finite-etale graph quotient isogenous to `E^g`,
where `E` is non-CM.  After a finite unramified coefficient extension
`O/R`, assume the self-adjoint slope splits as

\[
 T=\operatorname{diag}(t_1,\ldots,t_g),\qquad
 t_j-t_i\in O^\times\quad(i\ne j).
\tag{1}
\]

Put

\[
 I_A^k=\operatorname{Hdg}^{2k}(A,R),\qquad
 P_A^k=\operatorname{im}\bigl(\operatorname{Sym}^k\operatorname{NS}(A)
 \longrightarrow H^{2k}(A,R)\bigr).
\tag{2}
\]

If

\[
 m=\min(k,g-k)\leq3,
\tag{3}
\]

then

\[
 \boxed{\displaystyle
 I_A^k/P_A^k\simeq
 (R/(p^a))^{N_2+3N_3}\ \oplus\
 (R/(p^{2a}))^{N_3},}
\tag{4}
\]

where

\[
 N_2=\binom g4\binom{g-4}{k-2},\qquad
 N_3=\binom g6\binom{g-6}{k-3}.
\tag{5}
\]

As usual a binomial coefficient with an invalid lower index is zero.  Thus
the formula also covers `k=0,1` and the cases with no defect.  In particular,
for every `g<=7`, (4) is the complete ordinary divisor-product quotient in
**every** codimension.  It is not an invocation of Poincare duality: the
calculation in degree `k` is direct.

The formula is unchanged at `p=2`.  Before splitting, (4) remains valid over
`R`; only the labelled support summands used in the proof need not descend.

## 1. Split lattice and the primitive-volume lemma

Write

\[
 X_i=\lambda x_i,\qquad Y_i=y_i-t_i x_i,
\tag{6}
\]

and set

\[
 \Omega_{ii}=x_i\wedge y_i,\qquad
 \Omega_{ij}=x_i\wedge y_j+x_j\wedge y_i.
\tag{7}
\]

The exact finite-etale graph divisor lattice is generated by

\[
 D_i=\lambda\Omega_{ii}=X_iY_i,qquad
 C_{ij}=\lambda^2\Omega_{ij}\quad(i<j),
\tag{8}
\]

and

\[
 C_{ij}=(t_j-t_i)X_iX_j+
 \lambda(X_iY_j+X_jY_i).
\tag{9}
\]

Let `J` be a set of `d` indices and `S` a disjoint set.  Wedge multiplication
by

\[
 D_J:=\prod_{j\in J}D_j=\prod_{j\in J}X_jY_j
\tag{10}
\]

defines a primitive embedding of exterior lattices on the `S` coordinates:
its image is the direct coordinate summand in which every slot of `J` occurs
twice.  The coordinate projection that takes the coefficient of
`\prod_{j\in J}X_jY_j` is its integral left inverse.  Consequently, for
the multidegree `(2^d,1^{|S|})`,

\[
 I_{J,S}=D_J I^{\rm sq}_{S},
 \qquad
 P_{J,S}=D_J P^{\rm sq}_{S}.
\tag{11}
\]

The first equality follows directly from the integral exterior basis: a
rational tensor in that multidegree is integral precisely when, after
factoring `D_J`, its residual `S`-tensor is integral.  For the second,
ordinary products visibly contain the right side.  Conversely, contraction
straightening

\[
 \Omega_{ij}\Omega_{ik}=\mathord\pm\Omega_{ii}\Omega_{jk}
\tag{12}
\]

trades two cross terms meeting a doubled slot for the diagonal factor.  At
the weighted level it reads

\[
 C_{ij}C_{ik}=\mathord\pm\lambda D_iC_{jk};
\tag{13}
\]

so it introduces only an additional nonnegative factor of `lambda`, never a
denominator.  Repeating (13) moves every doubled slot to `D_J`; hence it
lies in the right side of (11).  This proves the product statement integrally
and rules out a hidden finite index.  It also proves (11) without Poincare
duality.

If the root lines have unimodular coefficients `b_i` rather than being
normalized to one, the same proof has harmless unit factors in (8)--(13).
It does not require dyadic square roots.

## 2. Direct multidegree decomposition

The exterior algebra decomposes by the number `0,1,2` of letters used from
each root slot.  Its diagonal `SL_2` invariant subspace, its integral
intersection, and the ordinary divisor-product image all respect this direct
decomposition.  A degree-`k` invariant multidegree has the unique form

\[
 (2^{k-ell},1^{2ell}),
 \qquad 0\leq ell\leq\min(k,g-k).
\tag{14}
\]

For fixed `ell`, the number of such blocks is

\[
 M_{g,k,ell}=\binom g{2ell}\binom{g-2ell}{k-ell}.
\tag{15}
\]

By (11), every block of type (14) is a primitive volume-factor copy of the
squarefree `2ell`-slot quotient.  This is the structural reason the local
defect is independent of `k-ell` doubled slots.

## 3. The only squarefree blocks needed when `m<=3`

* `ell=0`: the residual space is rank one and is generated by diagonal
  volume factors; it is product-saturated.

* `ell=1`: the residual two-slot invariant is rank one and generated by
  `C_{ij}`.  Its leading coefficient in (9) is a unit, so it too is
  product-saturated.

* `ell=2`: the exact four-slot theorem gives

  \[
  I^{\rm sq}_{S}/P^{\rm sq}_{S}\simeq R/(\lambda)
  \qquad(|S|=4).
  \tag{16}
  \]

* `ell=3`: the exact symbolic six-slot jet-minor computation has
  determinantal-divisor valuations

  \[
  (0,1,2,3,5),
  \tag{17}
  \]

  in the five-column noncrossing matching block.  Its Smith valuations are
  therefore `(0,1,1,1,2)`, giving

  \[
  I^{\rm sq}_{S}/P^{\rm sq}_{S}
  \simeq(R/(\lambda))^3\oplus R/(\lambda^2)
  \qquad(|S|=6).
  \tag{18}
  \]

The four-slot calculation is the theorem in
`notes/2026-08-11-c909-codim2-hodge-quotient-theorem.md`.  The six-slot
certificate is recorded in
`notes/2026-08-11-c909-symbolic-jet-minor-audit.md`: its relevant witnessing
minors are units times products of root differences, so (18) holds for
every distinct-root finite-etale tuple, not just a test tuple.

## 4. Count and conclusion

For `ell=2`, (15) is exactly

\[
 M_{g,k,2}=\binom g4\binom{g-4}{k-2}=N_2.
\tag{19}
\]

For `ell=3`, it is

\[
 M_{g,k,3}=\binom g6\binom{g-6}{k-3}=N_3.
\tag{20}
\]

All other blocks are saturated by §3, while (16) and (18) are copied
primitively by §1.  Directly summing (16) over (19) and (18) over (20)
proves (4).

For `k=3`, this specializes to

\[
 (R/(p^a))^{5\binom g5+3\binom g6}
 \oplus(R/(p^{2a}))^{\binom g6},
\tag{21}
\]

recovering the codimension-three theorem directly, rather than by duality.

## 5. Unramified descent

For a finite unramified splitting extension `O/R`, both `I_A^k` and `P_A^k`
commute with finite free base change.  Hence their quotient does too.  The
multiplicities of the factors `R/(p^e)` are recovered from the residue-field
dimensions of

\[
 p^{e-1}Q/p^eQ.
\tag{22}
\]

Those dimensions are unchanged by unramified scalar extension.  Thus the
split calculation descends to (4), with no averaging, trace map, or trace
denominator.

## Scope

The theorem is restricted to one equal-depth finite-etale graph block.  It
does not assert the unproved all-`ell` filtered-web formula, an unequal-depth
extension, a non-etale statement, or any Chow comparison.
