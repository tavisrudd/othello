# C909 — matrix-ideal divided-power saturation

Date: 2026-08-11  
Status: structural local theorem with an exact graph criterion; human proof;
no manuscript, PDF, mirror, Lean, or certificate change

## Theorem

Let `O` be an unramified DVR and let a divisor coefficient lattice have the
symmetric matrix-of-ideals form

\[
 \mathcal N=\{(a_{ij})=(a_{ji}):a_{ij}\in I_{ij}\},
 \qquad I_{ij}=I_{ji},
 \tag{1}
\]

in a fixed coefficient basis. Assume

\[
                         I_{ij}\subset I_{ii}\cap I_{jj}
                         \qquad(i\ne j).
 \tag{2}
\]

Assume also that, as for an elliptic-power coefficient realization, every
rank-one coefficient form in `mathcal N` represents a divisor whose pullback
is a decomposable alternating two-form.

> **Matrix-ideal saturation theorem.** The lattice `mathcal N` is generated
> integrally by rank-one square-zero divisor classes. Therefore, for every
> `D in mathcal N` and every `k`,
> 
> \[
>    \frac{D^k}{k!}\in
>    \operatorname{im}\bigl(
>       \operatorname{Sym}^k\mathcal N\longrightarrow H^{2k}
>    \bigr).
> \tag{3}
> \]
>
> Equivalently, the integral divided-power envelope generated by this divisor
> lattice equals its ordinary product image in every degree.

## Proof

Every ideal of `O` is principal. Diagonal symmetric forms are rank-one
generated without dividing by two:

\[
 (e_i+e_j)(e_i+e_j)^t-e_ie_i^t-e_je_j^t
   =e_ie_j^t+e_je_i^t.
 \tag{4}
\]

For a cross-slot generator `c(e_ie_j^t+e_je_i^t)` with `c in I_ij`, use

\[
 c(e_i+e_j)(e_i+e_j)^t-ce_ie_i^t-ce_je_j^t.
 \tag{5}
\]

The first rank-one form lies in `mathcal N` because its cross coefficient is
in `I_ij` and, by (2), its two diagonal coefficients lie in `I_ii` and
`I_jj`. The other two are diagonal rank-one elements. Thus (5) proves
rank-one generation, including at `p=2`.

Each rank-one class `R` has `R^2=0`: its pullback is decomposable; isogeny
pullback is rationally injective; and integral cohomology of a complex abelian
variety is torsion-free. Write `D=sum_r R_r`, absorbing integral coefficients.
Then

\[
 \frac{D^k}{k!}=\sum_{r_1<\cdots<r_k}R_{r_1}\cdots R_{r_k},
 \tag{6}
\]

because repeated indices vanish and the `k!` orderings cancel. This proves
(3). No positivity, effectiveness, factorial, trace, or off-diagonal factor
two is used.

## Graph criterion

For a marked diagonal graph presentation with denominator-depth matrix
`P=diag(p^{a_i})`, direct graph-basis multiplication gives

\[
 P^{-1}A\in\operatorname{Mat}(O),
 \qquad
 P^{-1}(AT^t-TA)P^{-1}\in\operatorname{Mat}(O).
 \tag{7}
\]

If, after finite unramified etale splitting, these congruences decouple
coefficient slot by coefficient slot, the allowed coefficients are ideals
`I_ij=p^{e_ij}O`. Symmetry plus the first condition in (7) forces

\[
                         e_{ij}\ge\max(a_i,a_j),
 \tag{8}
\]

while `I_ii=p^{a_i}O`. Hence (2) holds, and the matrix-ideal theorem applies.
The commutator condition can only increase a cross exponent; it cannot spoil
(8).

For one pure equal-depth finite-etale block, etale idempotents make the
decoupling automatic. Distinct eigenvalues give cross depth `p^{2a}` and
diagonal depth `p^a`, recovering the already proved full-NS theorem.

For arbitrary unequal truncated blocks, finite etaleness makes each separate
idempotent splitting exact but does not by itself prove that all splittings
admit one compatible block-scalar lift making (7) slotwise. Therefore the
arbitrary-depth graph corollary is conditional on this compatibility. The
matrix-ideal theorem itself is unconditional.

Formation of the prescribed graph divisor lattice and product image commutes
with a finite free unramified splitting extension when the slotwise
description holds. Faithful flatness then descends membership in
`H^{2k}/P^k` without trace averaging.

## Exact scope

This is integral cohomology, not Chow. It does not say all Hodge classes are
divisor products. It is intrinsic to the specified coefficient lattice but
not yet to a bare ppav. Nilpotent carry can couple matrix entries, so (1) can
fail; the theorem gives no converse in that regime.

## EJ/TT closeout and mystery ledger

* **Settled:** the true structural condition is the ideal containment (2),
  not equal depth or an adjugate identity.
* **Settled:** pure equal-depth finite-etale graphs satisfy it and hence have
  full Neron--Severi divided-power saturation.
* **TT compression:** one rank-one straightening identity replaces every
  degree-specific determinant calculation.
* **Open:** prove compatible slotwise splitting for all arbitrary-depth
  finite-etale graph packets, or give the first coupled-slot counterexample.
* **Open:** formulate (2) without a chosen coefficient basis and determine
  whether its failure measures an actual divided-power defect. Nilpotent
  elementary-divisor classification remains C908.
