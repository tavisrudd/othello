Rains recovered the three Pauli axes from a diagonal correlation tensor,
and Van den Nest, Dehaene, and De Moor used this mechanism in a
minimal-support LU-to-LC criterion for qubit stabilizer states
\cite[Theorem~13]{Rains1999}
\cite[Theorem~1 and Corollary~1]{VanDenNestDehaeneDeMoor2005}.
For \(q=2\), Proposition~\ref{prop:full-weyl-marginal} supplies all three
Pauli axes on each minimum support, so the AME profile satisfies their
criterion.  The LU-to-LC conclusion of
Theorem~\ref{thm:lu-lc-rigidity} is therefore already covered by their
result.  The new scope here is the uniform
prime-power theorem for arbitrary additive stabilizers, together with the
minimum-support atlas and its exact symmetry-group sequence.
