## The bounded theorem

Let `X` be C405's complete Cayley octad over `F_9`, let `N` be its three-dimensional net of
quadrics, and let `C` be the nonsingular Hermitian quartic cut out by the determinant of `N`. Let

their induced actions on `N` form the strict chain

```text

       7:3          <     PSL_2(7)      < PGU(3,3)=PSU(3,3),
       21           <       168         <       6048.
```

The indices are respectively `8` and `36`. The order-168 action on the eight octad points is
explicitly conjugate to the standard `PSL_2(7)` action on `P^1(F_7)`. Under that conjugacy the
order-21 group is exactly one point stabilizer, hence the nonabelian Frobenius group `7:3`.

The full unitary group acts faithfully and transitively on all 36 symmetric determinantal classes
of `C`. This is a rank-four permutation action with subdegrees

```text

```

In particular, all 36 classes have representatives over `F_9`; the numerical identity
`6048/168=36` is realized by an explicit coset action, not used as a stand-alone identification.

Two free corollaries sharpen the result. First, the eight conjugate C405 parent decorations are
canonically and equivariantly the eight octad points. Second, adjoining the `F_9/F_3` semilinear
symmetry doubles the ambient group and class stabilizer to `12096` and `336`; it exchanges the two
seven-point projective suborbits and fuses the subdegrees to `1,14,21`.

## Why the 36 cosets are the 36 determinantal classes

A symmetric representation `M=x_0Q_0+x_1Q_1+x_2Q_2` is considered up to `GL_4` congruence
`M -> U^T M U`. Its base locus is the Cayley octad. Therefore an automorphism of `C` fixes the
class of `M` exactly when its change of net coordinates is induced by a projectivity of the octad:

- a congruence fixing the class maps the three common quadrics' base locus to itself; and
- every projective octad stabilizer acts by congruence on the net and hence fixes the class.

The exhaustive order-168 octad group is thus the full class stabilizer inside the order-6048
unitary group. The checker partitions the unitary group into 36 explicit left cosets, constructs
the induced degree-36 permutations, verifies that their group still has order 6048, and directly
checks transitivity and subdegrees `1,7,7,21`.

characteristics number


classes/Cayley octads. Since `C` is smooth in characteristic three and its computed `F_9`-rational
orbit already has size 36, that orbit exhausts all classes over the algebraic closure. This also
proves that every class descends to `F_9`.
