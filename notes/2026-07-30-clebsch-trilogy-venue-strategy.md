# Clebsch trilogy venue strategy

**Lane:** `clebsch`

**Date checked:** 2026-07-30

## Recommendation

Do not require one journal to take the trilogy as a package.  Preserve the
shared title-page identity, but submit each logically independent paper where
its principal theorem has the strongest native audience.

The preferred route is:

1. **Paper III — Advances in Mathematics.**  After the bounded functorial
   operator-shadow import, the paper joins arithmetic geometry, invariant
   theory, harmonic analysis, integral representation theory, and affine
   \(E_8\).  The all-degree descent prevents the degree-ten Clebsch identity
   from looking accidental.  This is an ambitious but credible first
   submission if the promotion literature audit supports the originality
   claim.
2. **Paper II — Journal of Combinatorial Theory, Series A.**  Its general
   classification theorem, finite geometry, matching quotients, modular
   representation theory, and Gorenstein structure closely match JCTA's
   stated scope.
3. **Paper I — JCTA first, then a strong specialist finite-geometry or coding
   venue if necessary.**  The inverse reconstruction theorem and universal
   conic-filling bounds give it a credible JCTA case, although its exceptional
   \(q=11\) center and finite verification make it the most specialized
   paper.

Submit the two possible JCTA papers separately and make their distinct
theorem surfaces explicit in their cover letters.  Do not ask an editor to
accept the trilogy as one package unless invited.

## If one common home is preferred

Rank the common-home options:

1. **Forum of Mathematics, Sigma.**  Best intellectual umbrella.  Its
   editorial clusters span discrete mathematics, algebra, number theory,
   algebraic geometry, and computational mathematics, and it welcomes work
   crossing those categories.  It is fully open access, so institutional or
   other APC coverage must be checked.
2. **International Mathematics Research Notices.**  Strong broad alternative,
   especially for Papers II and III.  It publishes work of current interest
   across mathematics and normally considers papers no longer than 35 pages.
3. **Transactions of the American Mathematical Society.**  Broad and
   respectable common home, but less specifically aligned with the trilogy's
   cross-disciplinary identity than Forum Sigma.

## Stretch option

**Compositio Mathematica** is a stretch option for Paper III alone.  The
arithmetic-incidence theorem plus the functorial \(E_8\) descent may meet its
first-class-pure-mathematics standard, but only if the promotion audit
establishes a major advance relative to the closest Hitchin, Klein/McKay, and
invariant-theory literature.

JEMS is not the current recommendation.  It would require a broader
field-changing claim, most plausibly a canonical functor joining the three
torsor pictures, rather than the present collection of exact bridges.

## Submission ladder

- Send Paper III first to *Advances in Mathematics*.
- Send Paper II to JCTA.
- Send Paper I to JCTA on a separate timetable.
- If *Advances* declines Paper III for breadth or audience rather than
  correctness, next try *Forum of Mathematics, Sigma* or IMRN.
- Keep the shared Clebsch banner, but present every submission as a complete
  independent paper.

The functorial Paper III capstone is what makes the *Advances* first shot
credible.  The minimum import is the all-degree paired-tower descent, the
degree-ten recovery of the conference operator and cubic, and the exact
index-four integral comparison.  Do not import C682's transfer-phase,
all-weight corner, or preprojective-frontier machinery.
