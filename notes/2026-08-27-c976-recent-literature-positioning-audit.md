# C976 recent-literature positioning audit

Date: 2026-08-27

## Verdict

The bounded search found no source combining all three parts of the manuscript's
central result: a target-normalized minimum-support formula over the complete
outer functional dual, an exact coefficient/support correspondence, and a
closed min--sum state for repeated concatenation.  This supports the paper's
specific novelty framing, but not an absolute priority claim.  MathSciNet and
Google Scholar were not covered, Semantic Scholar was rate-limited, and no
complete forward-citation closure was available.

The audit did find four correctable positioning problems.

1. Kurz--Yaakobi concerns short PIR codes, not regenerating-code bandwidth; it
   was removed from that claim.
2. The service-rate definition needed the foundational Aktaş et al. paper and
   the Alfarano et al. polytope treatment.
3. The cooperative-locality inequality needed attribution to
   Abdel-Ghaffar--Weber at the theorem itself, and ordinary generalized weights
   needed Wei.
4. The computation section needed explicit comparison with CP-SAT, Graphillion,
   constraint-based code construction, and repair-DAG scheduling.  The safe
   claim is structure-aware algebraic compilation with exact witnesses, not
   priority for general constraint solving, decision diagrams, or scheduling.

The revised Introduction also now surfaces the closest mathematical neighbors:
fixed prescribed-coset support costs, generalized covering radii, exact-repair
bandwidth, service-rate polytopes, and matrix-product/concatenated LRC
constructions.  It states what each retains or optimizes and what additional
information the present theorem uses.

## Closest comparisons

- Zhang et al. minimize the union support of representatives of prescribed
  cosets; Elimelech--Firer--Schwartz maximize the analogous quantity over coset
  tuples.  The present state adds target normalization, outer-functional labels,
  and exact repeated composition.
- Abdel-Ghaffar--Weber derive the cooperative-locality generalized-weight
  inequality.  The present paper's added results are the best-target equality
  and the coefficient/support-preserving concatenation theorem.
- Aktaş et al. introduce fractional recovery-set allocation under server
  capacities, and Alfarano et al. develop its polytope.  The present corollary
  transfers the radius-bounded region generated by preserved supports.
- Guruswami--Wootters and Matthews et al. optimize base-field download for
  exact repair.  The present cost counts participating scalar helper
  coordinates and makes no bandwidth or subpacketization claim.
- Galindo et al. transport recovery structures in matrix-product LRCs, and
  Jin--Fu construct concatenated locality-two codes.  These are sufficient
  constructions and parameter results; the present theorem characterizes the
  exact obstruction for arbitrary represented inner codes and target splits.
- Aji--McEliece, Guruswami--Sudan, and Blomqvist et al. establish min--sum or
  symbol-labelled processing in adjacent decoding settings.  The new statement
  is closure of the target-normalized prescribed-coset state for exact first
  nonconfinement.
- CP-SAT, Graphillion, constraint-based code construction, and repair-DAG
  scheduling are established.  ergodis compiles the algebraic recovery fibres
  into a smaller exact state or residual solver while retaining witnesses.
