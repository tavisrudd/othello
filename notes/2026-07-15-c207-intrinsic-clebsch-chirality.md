**Lane**: `clebsch`
**Date:** 2026-07-15
**Status:** QUEUED

## Objective

Recover the unordered two-class chirality torsor from the abstract code or its coset-leader
incidence structure, without first choosing the displayed parity-check columns or identifying them
as a Clebsch hexagon. Candidate languages include a signed weight enumerator, an association
scheme, a canonical double cover, and the decoder-ambiguity reconstruction already proved in C185.

In the same intrinsic model, explain the outside coset of the exotic `S5` normalizer: it exchanges
the two support classes but cannot be corrected by coordinate scalings to preserve the code. The
deliverable is either a canonical obstruction class or a precise negative result showing which
extra structure is unavoidable.
