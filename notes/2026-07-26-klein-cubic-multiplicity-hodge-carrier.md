## Verdict

The proposed equivariant elliptic subvariety is the wrong carrier. The
surviving two-dimensional object is the multiplicity Hodge structure for
the five-dimensional icosahedral representation.

This audit corrects two points in that import: the standard cohomological
twist is \(H^1(J)\simeq H^3(X)(1)\), and Roulleau proves an unpolarized
isomorphism \(J(X)\cong E^5\), not merely an isogeny.

Let \(X\) be the Klein cubic threefold and \(J(X)\) its intermediate
Jacobian. With the standard cohomological Tate-twist convention,
\[
 H^1(J(X),\mathbf Q)\simeq H^3(X,\mathbf Q)(1),
\]
not \(H^3(X,\mathbf Q)(-1)\). The rational vector space has dimension ten.
Roulleau computes the period lattice and proves that, as an unpolarized
complex abelian variety,
\[
 J(X)\cong E^5,\qquad
 E=\mathbf C/\mathbf Z[\nu],\qquad
 \nu=\frac{-1+\sqrt{-11}}2.
\]
Thus \(\operatorname{End}^0_{\mathbf C}(E)=\mathbf Q(\sqrt{-11})\).
Writing \(\operatorname{End}_{\mathbf Q}(E)\) would be ambiguous here:
Roulleau's statement concerns the complex CM elliptic curve and does not
assert that every CM endomorphism is defined over a chosen
\(\mathbf Q\)-model. The displayed product is not an isomorphism of
principally polarized abelian varieties.

The complex \(PSL_2(11)\)-action on the holomorphic five-space is one of the
conjugate irreducible five-dimensional representations. Their sum is the
ten-dimensional rational representation. Its restriction to either
icosahedral subgroup \(A_5\) is two copies of the rational irreducible
five-space \(W_5\):
\[
 H^1(J(X),\mathbf Q)|_{A_5}\simeq W_5\oplus W_5
 \simeq W_5\otimes U,
\qquad
 U=\operatorname{Hom}_{A_5}
   (W_5,H^1(J(X),\mathbf Q)).
\]
The \(A_5\)-equivariant Hodge decomposition makes the two-dimensional
multiplicity space \(U\) a weight-one rational Hodge structure. It carries
the imaginary quadratic action inherited from
\(\mathbf Q(\sqrt{-11})\).

Consequently there is no two-dimensional rational \(A_5\)- or
\(PSL_2(11)\)-stable subrepresentation and hence no equivariant elliptic
subvariety. The two-dimensional carrier is transverse: it records the
multiplicity with which the icosahedral five-space occurs.

Hartlieb's Tables 1 and 3 give the two conjugate degree-five
\(PSL_2(11)\) characters and the rational \(A_5\) character
\((5,1,-1,0,0)\), so the restriction and multiplicity statements are
literature-backed. Schur's lemma then gives the rational commutant
\(M_2(\mathbf Q)\). This note does not yet contain the exact matrix
certificate or the relative-position computation; C654 owns those.

## Relative-position target

For the two icosahedral parents \(A_5^+\) and \(A_5^-\) inside
\(PSL_2(11)\), define
\[
 C_\pm=\operatorname{End}_{A_5^\pm}
   \bigl(H^1(J(X),\mathbf Q)\bigr).
\]
The expected exact identities are
\[
 C_\pm\simeq M_2(\mathbf Q),\qquad
 C_+\cap C_-=
 \operatorname{End}_{PSL_2(11)}
   \bigl(H^1(J(X),\mathbf Q)\bigr)
 \simeq\mathbf Q(\sqrt{-11}).
\]
The intersection equality follows formally once the two \(A_5\)'s generate
\(PSL_2(11)\); the isomorphism types still require an exact representation
calculation.

The relative-position proposal needs one repair. A matrix algebra
\(M_2(\mathbf Q)\) has no distinguished rank-one idempotents. Therefore
“cross-ratios of the rank-one idempotents” is not yet a canonical invariant.
C654 must first define extra intrinsic data selecting idempotents or replace
them by a conjugacy-invariant construction from the ordered pair of embedded
commutant algebras and the polarization. Candidate outputs include:

- characteristic polynomials of canonical compositions of trace-orthogonal
  projections onto \(C_+\) and \(C_-\);
- principal angles for the two commutant subspaces under the Rosati trace
  form;
- discriminants of the algebra generated by distinguished
  polarization-compatible involutions, if such involutions are intrinsic.

The decisive test is whether a canonical relative invariant has splitting
field or discriminant class \(\mathbf Q(\sqrt5)\). A positive result would
separate the roles of the two fields:
\(\mathbf Q(\sqrt{-11})\) controls the CM multiplicity dynamics, while
\(\mathbf Q(\sqrt5)\) controls the relative position of the two
icosahedral decompositions.

## Cycle-level route

Roulleau associates a smooth genus-two curve \(D_g\) on the Fano surface to
each of the 55 involutions \(g\in PSL_2(11)\), with
\[
D_gD_h=
\begin{cases}
-4,&g=h,\\
0,&o(gh)=2\text{ or }6,\\
2,&o(gh)=3,\\
1,&o(gh)=5.
\end{cases}
\]
These curves span a rank-25 index-two sublattice of the Néron--Severi
group. This gives a second exact representation of the finite group geometry
in characteristic zero. It does not yet produce the two 11-point sheets or
prove that the index-two saturation is their orientation bit. C654 may test
that possibility after the commutant calculation, but it is not part of the
current theorem.
