## Executive verdict

The mathematics of the prime-support/factorial-threshold theorem, the
composite-degree extension, and the explicit `p=3,g=4` counterexample is
sound.  The focused SymPy certificate has the correct polarization and
factorial normalization, uses the complete Neron--Severi congruence lattice,
and gives a genuine separating functional.  Combined with the prime-support
theorem it proves exact order three, not merely nonmembership.

The priority verdict is narrower than the current significance prose:

1. The bare dimension-three phenomenon

   ```text
   minimal class algebraic but not an ordinary integral divisor product
   ```

   is classical/immediate from the Jacobian case.  For a genus-three
   Jacobian with `NS=Z theta`, Poincare's formula gives
   `gamma=theta^2/2=[C]`, while the integral divisor-product subgroup is
   `Z theta^2=2 Z gamma`.  Thus the broad algebraicity-versus-product
   separation and exact defect two cannot be sold as new.

2. The refined result that is potentially new is the **explicit defect inside
   a non-CM elliptic-cube isogeny locus**, together with the complete
   prime-gluing lattice calculation and the odd-prime `p=3,g=4` example.  No
   exact predecessor for that refined result was located in this bounded
   audit.

3. The class called the internal divided-power Bockstein is a correct and
   useful coordinate-free packaging, but mathematically it is the standard
   Tor connecting class for `P subset S`.  No source located applies this
   class to the ordinary divisor-product lattice of a ppav, but it should not
   be advertised as a new cohomology operation.  For `r>1` it is one
   mod-`p^r` connecting class, not yet a tower of successive Bocksteins.

4. The phrase "Lefschetz defect" already denotes a different invariant,
   including in current work on abelian varieties: Verni defines it from the
   codimension of curve classes carried by divisors.  "Integral hard
   Lefschetz defect" is also established terminology.  Prefer **minimal-class
   divisor-product index** or **integral divisor-product defect** for the
   present finite quotient.

5. The sentence asserting an exact "divided-power cofactor span of the local
   Lagrangian matroid" is ahead of the proof.  The certificates compute wedge
   products of the fully lifted integral divisor forms.  They do not prove
   that the answer is determined by the reduced symmetric centralizer, a
   matroid, or an ordinary cofactor span.  In fact the carry term

   ```text
   (D T_tilde - T_tilde D)/p mod p
   ```

   is part of the reduced divisor form and is precisely the sort of secondary
   information the additive centralizer forgets.  Use "lifted exterior-product
   span" until an invariant cofactor theorem is proved.

## 1. Exact theorem audit

### 1.1 Prime support and factorial threshold: GO

Let `P_A` be the subgroup generated by ordinary products of `g-1` integral
divisors, and let

```text
S_A=(P_A tensor Q) intersect Hdg^(2g-2)(A,Z).
```

For a prime-`p` quotient `f:E^g -> A_K`, pullback is a ring isomorphism on
`Z_q` cohomology for every `q != p`.  Because it preserves the rational Hodge
subspace, Lefschetz `(1,1)` identifies the localized Neron--Severi lattices as
well.  Thus it identifies their ordinary divisor-product lattices.

Upstairs,

```text
f^*(Theta_K)=p(d_1+...+d_g),
f^*(gamma_K)=p^(g-1) sum_i product_(j!=i) d_j,
```

so the minimal class is an ordinary divisor product at every prime away from
`p`.  At `p`, `(g-1)! gamma_K=Theta_K^(g-1)` gives exponent at most
`p^v_p((g-1)!)`.  Hence `p>=g` is exactly the safe range.  No polarization
factor or second factorial is missing.

The claim should say that the quotient class in `S_A/P_A` has the stated
support and exponent.  "Prime-to-`p` primitivity" is acceptable shorthand if
that quotient has already been defined.

### 1.2 Composite-degree corollary: GO

Let `f:E^g -> A` have degree `D`, with `End(E)=Z`, and let `(A,Theta)` be
principally polarized.  Write `G` for the integral symmetric matrix of
`f^*Theta` relative to the product polarization.  Then

```text
det(G)=chi(f^*Theta)=D
```

and the curve-coordinate matrix of `f^*gamma_A` is `adj(G)`.  This adjugate
is integral even though `G` need not be unimodular.  The graph elliptics
`C_v` for primitive `v` are complete intersections of `g-1` kernel divisors,
and the matrices `vv^t` span `Sym_g(Z)`.  Therefore `f^*gamma_A` is an
integral signed sum of ordinary divisor products.

At a prime `q` not dividing `D`, pullback identifies the integral cohomology
and Neron--Severi lattices and descends that membership.  At primes dividing
`D`, the factorial identity gives the remaining bound.  Consequently

```text
ord(gamma_A mod P_A)
  divides product_(p|D) p^v_p((g-1)!).
```

In particular, `gcd(D,(g-1)!)=1` implies primitive divisor-product
saturation.  The statement is for the chosen isogeny `f`; different isogenies
may give stronger bounds, which is harmless.

This composite theorem is the clean headline.  The prime-gluing theorem is
its sharp one-prime specialization plus the explicit local calculations.

### 1.3 Tor boundary: correct, but elementary packaging

Put `P=P_A tensor Z_p`, `S=S_A tensor Z_p`, `Q=S/P`, and
`r=v_p((g-1)!)`.  Since `P` and `S` are torsion-free, tensoring

```text
0 -> P -> S -> Q -> 0
```

with `Z/p^r` gives

```text
Q[p^r] -> P/p^r P -> S/p^r S,
```

where the first arrow identifies `Q[p^r]` with the kernel.  If
`q=gamma_A+P`, its image is exactly

```text
p^r gamma_A mod p^r P.
```

It vanishes if and only if `gamma_A` belongs to `P`, because multiplication
by `p^r` is injective on `S`.  At the first wall, `r=1`, this is a genuine
mod-`p` connecting/Tor class.  Calling it an "internal divided-power
Bockstein" is defensible after this definition, but:

- it is not an ambient cohomological Bockstein or Steenrod operation;
- it is not an Arf or Maslov invariant;
- the word "divided-power" describes the element being lifted, not an
  independently constructed divided-power operation on `P`; and
- for `r>1` the class is a taut but useful membership detector, not yet a
  sequence of higher operations.

No exact predecessor was located for applying this Tor boundary to the
ordinary integral divisor-product lattice of a ppav.  Moonen--Polishchuk and
Kahn study genuine divided-power structures in Chow or etale Chow rings,
which is a different construction.

## 2. Explicit `p=3,g=4` normalization

### 2.1 Principal graph basis

For the displayed integral symmetric lift `A`, with row-basis convention,

```text
B = [[I/p,A/p],[0,I]]
```

satisfies

```text
B (pJ) B^t = J.
```

For a source Hodge form with symmetric coefficient `S`, integrality in this
basis forces `S=pT`; the transformed form is

```text
Omega_T = [[(TA-AT)/p,T],[-T,0]].
```

Thus the congruence `TA-AT=0 mod p` and the sign/order of the upper-left
block in the report are correct.  The statement should keep saying that `A`
is an integral symmetric lift of the mod-`p` slope.

Because `End(E)=Z`, the rational Hodge divisor space is exactly the symmetric
coefficient space.  The commutator map has mod-three rank five, while the
printed ten-element lift has determinant `3^5`; this proves that the tested
rank-ten lattice is the full integral Neron--Severi lattice.

### 2.2 Factorial and separating functional

The certificate uses the principal theta form `J`, computes `J^3`, checks
coefficientwise divisibility by `6`, and evaluates the functional on
`J^3/6`.  It does not accidentally evaluate the undivided cube.  By
multilinearity, checking the 220 unordered monomials in a complete
ten-element divisor basis checks every triple product.

The functional proves only nonmembership.  Exact order three then uses both
additional inputs:

```text
6 gamma in P                     (factorial ceiling),
gamma in P tensor Z_2            (prime-support theorem).
```

Together these kill the two-primary part, while the nonzero mod-three
functional leaves exact order three.  The focused SymPy replay reproduced
the frozen output and hashes on 2026-08-11.

## 3. Priority boundary

### 3.1 What is preempted or immediate

**Algebraic minimal class outside the integral divisor subring.**  Debarre's
introduction records Poincare's formula: on a Jacobian, `W_(g-d)` has class
`theta^d/d!`.  For a genus-three Jacobian with `NS=Z theta`, this immediately
gives exact order two modulo ordinary divisor squares.  Beauville--Ritzenthaler
record that over an algebraically closed field every indecomposable ppav
threefold is a Jacobian (the decomposable case is a product of Jacobians).
Thus the bare dimension-three algebraicity-versus-product-defect statement
is not a novelty claim.

The same warning applies to the broad factorial phenomenon in higher genus:
on a Jacobian with Picard rank one, Poincare's formula makes the minimal
class algebraic while the divisor subring supplies only the factorial
multiple.  The new claim cannot be simply "algebraicity does not imply
divisor generation."

**Divided powers and factorial denominators.**  Moonen--Polishchuk construct
canonical divided powers for the Pontryagin product on positive-dimensional
Chow classes and explain the obstruction to transporting them integrally to
the intersection product.  Kahn constructs divided powers in etale Chow
groups, discusses Esnault's nondivisibility example, and contrasts etale and
ordinary Chow.  Beckmann--de Gaay Fortman connect algebraicity of the minimal
class with integral Fourier/IHC properties.  None of these theorems identifies
the primitive class with the subgroup generated by ordinary integral divisor
monomials.

**Matroidal prime obstructions.**  Engel--de Gaay Fortman--Schreieder attach
prime divisibility of algebraic curve multiples to regular-matroid
degenerations.  Their obstruction concerns algebraicity of the minimal class,
not the internal index of the ordinary divisor-product sublattice on a fixed
elliptic-power isogeny member.  It is adjacent priority and makes "matroid"
especially dangerous unless a precise new Lagrangian/delta-matroid is defined.

**Spinor principal minors.**  Van Geemen--Marrani identify the
characteristic-two principal-minor image of the Lagrangian Grassmannian with
a spinor variety.  They do not study integral lifts, Neron--Severi product
lattices, or the Tor boundary.  Their result supports a future spinorial
reformulation only; it does not currently prove one.

### 3.2 Strongest unpreempted candidate

The strongest safe priority formulation after this bounded audit is:

> For ppavs in a fixed non-CM elliptic-power isogeny class, ordinary integral
> divisor-product saturation is not isogeny invariant.  An explicit
> elementary two-gluing in dimension three has minimal-class index two, and
> an explicit elementary three-gluing in dimension four has index three;
> more generally the defect is supported on primes common to the isogeny
> degree and `(g-1)!`, with the stated factorial exponent bound.

No exact predecessor for that package was located.  This is an absence claim
only at the coverage level below.

### 3.3 Terminology collision

Verni's `arXiv:2501.10192v2`, Definition 2.1, defines the **Lefschetz defect**
of a smooth projective variety from the codimension in `N_1(X)_Q` of the
curve classes carried by a prime divisor.  Theorem 1.3 then characterizes this
invariant for abelian varieties in terms of their isogeny factors.  This is
close enough in both vocabulary and subject that "integral Lefschetz defect"
will invite confusion.

Recommended terminology, in order:

1. `minimal-class divisor-product index` for the order of the one class;
2. `integral divisor-product defect group` for `S/P`; or
3. `integral Lefschetz-product index` only if a link to Verni's terminology is
   explicitly disclaimed.

## 7. Promotion recommendation

Mathematical verdict: **GO with minor exposition corrections**.

Priority verdict: **GO only for the refined elliptic-isogeny-locus theorem,
with a bounded `to our knowledge` qualifier**.

Before any manuscript promotion:

1. rename the defect or explicitly distinguish it from Verni's invariant;
2. state the dimension-three classical Jacobian preemption;
3. replace the unproved cofactor/matroid classification sentence by the
   lifted exterior-product formulation;
4. call the Bockstein a Tor boundary and reserve higher-Bockstein language
   for a proved filtration; and
5. keep the `p=3,g=4` claim cohomological unless algebraicity of its primitive
   minimal class is independently established.

The current theorem package is a valuable exact component, but not an Annals
crown by itself.  The high-ceiling successor is a uniform local
classification of `S/P` for all self-dual elliptic-isogeny lattices, with an
intrinsic finite-geometric invariant, realization of every allowable defect,
and a theorem separating algebraicity from ordinary divisor generation.
