# C904: the exceptional-quintic residue ideal

Date: 2026-08-11
Status: exact negative theorem for the last nonscalar Fano-curve escape;
the intrinsic relative Chow-half gate remains open
Scope: quarantined Annals research; no manuscript or Lean edits

## Verdict

The exceptional plane quintic cannot be upgraded to an odd universal cycle
by inserting arbitrary nonscalar curves on the Fano surface and arbitrary
integral endomorphisms of the intermediate Jacobian.

The obstruction is stronger than the earlier even-trace calculation.  Let
$J$ be the generic exotic marked intermediate Jacobian, let

$$
                     R=\operatorname{End}(J),
        \qquad R_2=R\otimes\mathbf F_2,
$$

and, for a divisor/curve $C$ on the Fano surface $F$, let $N_C$ be its
Rosati norm operator on $H_1(J,\mathbf Z)$.  Put

$$
  \mathfrak m_F=R_2\langle N_C:C\in\operatorname{NS}(F)\rangle R_2.
$$

> **Fano-norm residue theorem.**  For the generic exotic gluing,
> 
> $$
> \dim_{\mathbf F_2}R_2=25,
> \qquad
> \dim_{\mathbf F_2}\mathfrak m_F=24,
> \qquad
> R_2/\mathfrak m_F\cong\mathbf F_2.
> $$
> 
> The quotient map
> 
> $$
>                  \epsilon:R_2\longrightarrow\mathbf F_2
> $$
> 
> is a ring character, satisfies $\epsilon(1)=1$, and kills every Fano
> norm $N_C$.

Consequently no finite integral expression

$$
                     \sum_i r_iN_{C_i}s_i,
          \qquad r_i,s_i\in R,
$$

is congruent to the identity modulo two.  This rules out the proposed
adjugate repair even after allowing the complete saturated $NS(F)$ and the
complete integral endomorphism order.

It does **not** prove that the abstract class in
$CH_1(D_{+,K})/2$ is nonzero.  An intrinsic Shen/diagonal cycle which does
not factor through a curve on $F$ lies outside the theorem.

## 1. Why this was the only remaining exceptional-quintic opening

The relative plane-quintic construction gives a family of one-cycles on the
cubic parameterized by $F$ whose normal function is $-5a:F\to J$.  The
earlier scalar audit showed that an $A_5$-equivariant curve $C\subset F$
has even scalar norm.  That did not exclude a nonscalar $N_C$ invertible
modulo two.

If such a norm existed, restriction of the plane-quintic family to $C$,
followed by the Jacobian correspondence of $C$, would give an integral
cycle on $J\times X$ inducing $-5N_C$.  An odd determinant/adjugate would
then combine with the already-relative cycle inducing $2\operatorname{Id}$
to produce the identity.  Allowing pre- and postcomposition by $R$ replaces
one norm by the full two-sided ideal generated by all norms.  Thus the
membership question

$$
                         1\stackrel{?}{\in}\mathfrak m_F
$$

is the exact closure test for every endomorphism-dressed version of this
route.

The theorem answers it negatively.

## 2. Complete curve-norm lattice

The full saturated divisor lattice of $F$ is already known:

$$
 NS(F)=a^*NS(J)+\mathbf Z C_s,
 \qquad a^*\Theta=2C_s.
$$

For a restricted divisor $C=a^*D$, let $A_D$ be the Rosati-symmetric
endomorphism corresponding to $D$.  Polarizing

$$
                    \frac{(\Theta+D)^5}{5!}=\det(I+A_D)
$$

gives the exact norm formula

$$
                       N_C=\operatorname{tr}_{\mathbf C}(A_D)I-A_D.
$$

Here the complex trace is half the trace on the rank-ten integral homology
lattice.  For $D=\Theta$ this gives $N_{a^*\Theta}=4I$, and the missing
incidence generator has

$$
                             N_{C_s}=2I.
$$

It therefore vanishes modulo two.  Hence the fifteen certified integral
divisors on $J$ generate every Fano-curve norm relevant modulo two; no
curve in $Pic^0(F)$ adds a cohomological operator.

## 3. Exact residue calculation

The certificate reconstructs, from the principal exotic homology lattice:

1. the complete rank-25 integral order $R$ by imposing integrality on every
   rational $5\times5$ coefficient endomorphism;
2. the complete rank-15 integral Neron--Severi lattice;
3. the fourteen-dimensional mod-two span of the associated Fano norms; and
4. every product $rN_Cs$ on order bases.

Their span has dimension 24 inside the 25-dimensional order.  Its
annihilator is one-dimensional.  The resulting functional $\epsilon$ sends
the identity to one, kills all norm generators, and satisfies

$$
                  \epsilon(rs)=\epsilon(r)\epsilon(s)
$$

on an integral order basis.  Bilinearity then proves multiplicativity on
all of $R_2$.  Thus the codimension-one span is a proper two-sided maximal
ideal and the quotient is exactly $\mathbf F_2$.

This explains the earlier numerical symptoms.  Even trace was only the
scalar shadow of a genuine residue character of the noncommutative integral
order.

## 4. Consequence for relative universal cycles

Every operator obtained from the exceptional-quintic family by restricting
to Fano curves, passing through their Jacobians, and dressing on either side
by integral endomorphisms lies in $\mathfrak m_F$ modulo two.  The known
relative Fano-incidence cycle induces $2R$ and also vanishes modulo two.
Therefore any integral combination of these two sources still has
$\epsilon=0$, while an identity correspondence has $\epsilon=1$.

This closes all of the following variants simultaneously:

- choose a nonscalar curve on $F$ with odd determinant norm;
- combine several individually singular norms until their sum is invertible;
- pre- or postcompose with any integral exotic endomorphism;
- use adjugates, matrix Bezout, or graph-transpose corrections; and
- combine any such odd-looking operator with the known multiplier two.

The result is an operator-level obstruction.  It neither computes the full
Chow restriction kernel nor proves that a completely intrinsic horizontal
cycle on $D_+$ cannot exist.

## 5. EJ, TT, and alternative attacks

The high-value Tao-style correction was to stop asking whether one norm is
scalar or invertible and instead compute the two-sided ideal it generates in
the full integral order.  The surprise is the canonical one-dimensional
residue quotient: the failed nonscalar constructions are not scattered
accidents but one maximal-ideal phenomenon.

Three other direct attacks were checked against the live dead-path ledger.

1. **Integral Franchetta.**  A relative integral Franchetta theorem for the
   special Fano-surface pencil would turn the existing cohomological parity
   wall into a full negative answer.  Available Franchetta results use
   rational Chow coefficients and do not control this two-primary class, so
   this route supplies no theorem at the required integral level.
2. **Odd subgroup quotients.**  A relative universally-$CH_0$-trivial
   resolution of $X/C_3$ or $X/C_5$ could in principle supply an odd norm
   correspondence.  The norm is nonscalar, and no such relative quotient
   theorem or integral universal cycle is presently established.  This is
   not imported as a result.
3. **The explicit $F+F-F$ carrier.**  The existing complete saturated
   middle-divisor calculation already gives degree ideal $12\mathbf Z$ for
   $F\times D\to J$ with $D\in NS(F\times F)$.  It cannot supply the missing
   odd theta-pair multisection.

The cusp, ambient-support, tautological universal-sheaf, ordered matching,
and elliptic-support routes remain dead for the reasons in the C904 task
card and were not reopened.
