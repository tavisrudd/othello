## 3. Major findings

1. **Priority attribution verified.**  At printed page 3, Introduction, the
   paper says that Faulkner Valiente--Miller Eismeier Theorem 2.9, Corollary 2.10,
   and Proposition 2.14 already imply the Smith factors and the abstract
   `Lambda/2Lambda` saturation quotient.  Those theorem numbers and contents are
   correct.  Applying their compatible split filtration to the transpose
   contraction `i_omega : Lambda^5 -> Lambda^3` gives graded cokernels
   `P^3/(1)=0` and `P^1/(2)`, hence torsion `(Z/2)^10` and nonzero Smith factors
   `1^110,2^10`.  Their result does not select the manuscript's explicit
   divided-power representatives or connect them to the Fano endpoint.
   **Severity:** none.  **Remedy:** none.

2. **Krämer comparison verified.**  Printed page 8, (18), reproduces the three
   skyscraper shifts in Krämer Corollary 6 correctly:
   `Q_0[2]`, `Q_0`, and `Q_0[-2]`.  Krämer works in the complex constructible
   derived category; the same decomposition-theorem and exceptional-fibre
   argument is valid over `Q`.  Calling the resulting derived-category
   decomposition **noncanonical** is necessary and correct.  Its hypercohomology
   gives rational corrections only in degrees 2, 4, and 6 and no odd-degree
   correction.  It does not determine integral mod-two glue.  The spelling
   **Thomas Krämer** in the manuscript and bibliography, and
   the citation `[Kra16, Corollary 6]`, are correct.
   **Severity:** none.  **Remedy:** none.

3. **Surviving priority boundary verified.**  The precise surviving statement is
   printed-page-2 equation (3): under `(b_*,e^*)`, `H^3(M,Z)` is the fibre product
   of `S` and `H^3(X,Z)` cut out by
   `[s]=[theta^[2] wedge y(gamma)]`, where `y` is the integral cylinder
   isomorphism fixed by the Fano correspondence.  Section 4, especially
   Proposition 4.3 and (14), supplies the labelled endpoint lifts.  None of the
   reviewed prior sources states this simultaneous integral placement.  Its
   exact dual is printed pages 7--8: (16) identifies the escape quotient with
   `Hom(ker(b_* : H^3(M,Z) -> S),Z)`; (3) then identifies that kernel with
   `2H^3(X,Z)`, and exceptional Gysin classes become exactly `2E_M`.  This is the
   content that remains after removing priority from the abstract Smith data.
   **Severity:** none.  **Remedy:** none.

4. **No unqualified firstness survives.**  A full-PDF check found no novelty
   formula such as "first", "new", or "to our knowledge" attached to the
   results.  The Introduction instead uses the qualified statements "already
   computed" for Krämer and "already imply" for Faulkner Valiente--Miller
   Eismeier.  Artebani--Kloosterman--Pacini concerns a birational model of
   `Theta/{+-1}`, while Debarre records the classical minimal class of the Fano
   surface; neither contains the simultaneous integral lattice or its dual.
   Thus the appropriate absence statement is only: **the Fano-labelled fibre
   product and its dual were not found in the reviewed sources**.
   **Severity:** none.  **Remedy:** none.

5. **Mechanism and abstract gates verified.**  The proof still proceeds through
   the link/Gysin and Mayer--Vietoris calculation, the complete-graph incidence
   blocks, the Fano endpoint/Pontryagin identity, and integral duality.  The
   revised prose changes credit and exposes the dual short exact sequence; it
   does not replace any of those mechanisms.  The rendered abstract contains
   179 whitespace tokens under a deliberately conservative PDF extraction
   count (the frozen TeX-aware count is 138 prose words), hence is below 250.
   **Severity:** none.  **Remedy:** none.
