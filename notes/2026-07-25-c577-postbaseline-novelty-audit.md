# C577 post-baseline literature and novelty audit

**Lane:** `clebsch`

**Date:** 2026-07-25

**Scope:** Paper II additions not already disposed by the C399, C406, and
C417 audits: the cohomological \(H_3\) rank proof, graded evaluation
algebra, self-associated/Gorenstein specialization, nine-space and
projective-cover bridges, arithmetic gluing, and the general cubic
compression lemmas.

**Verdict:** `NO PRE-EMPTION OF THE PAPER'S SPECIFIC COMPOSITION LOCATED;
GENERAL COHOMOLOGY, PROJECTIVE-COVER, SCHUR-EVALUATION, GALE/GORENSTEIN,
AND CUBIC-ALGEBRA FRAMEWORKS ARE CLASSICAL`

## Coverage summary

This audit consumes the exact source ledgers and read depths of C406, C417,
and C621 rather than repeating their searches. Five load-bearing sources in
that inherited coverage were read at **full text**: Edge, Dye,
Filmus--Lindzey, Bamberg--Klawuhn, and
Rodríguez-Pajares--Ruano--Salizzoni. Six further inherited sources were read
at **partial** depth on the relevant passages:
Eisenbud--Popescu, Elias--Rossi, Broer--Chuai, the Sin Smith-normal-form
survey, Chandler--Sin--Xiang, and Yanez.

This pass added one source at **partial** depth,
López--Soprunov--Villarreal, and consulted the ATLAS of Group
Representations and the GAP Character Table Library at
**abstract/metadata only** depth. It also ran four exact OpenAlex screens
and the corresponding Crossref top-five screens. No forward-citation
closure was used, so the three-service citation-graph rule was not
triggered. Shared-cache verification covered 279 entries with zero hash
problems.

## What was already covered

The C406 priority audit already owns the matching-secant quotient, exact
\(3,6,10\) images, balanced-half recovery, cubic-first orientation,
secant-product tensor syzygies, six profiles, normalized orbit weights, and
the exact profile plane. It classifies the raw one-factorizations and
matching-design language as classical, and the conic-ideal composition as
likely new only within its recorded coverage.

The C417 audit already owns the affine base-choice cocycle framework,
modular averaging obstruction, covariants modulo the conic ideal,
projective-cover/depth setting, and the \([2,8,1]\) versus \([2,9,1]\)
relative-cubic obstruction. It classifies the general cohomology and
integral-to-modular machinery as classical and only the conic-twisted,
secant-decorated realization as likely new within coverage.

The C621 focused audit already owns the general self-association,
arithmetically Gorenstein, Schur-square, Gale-duality, and inverse-system
criteria. It makes no novelty claim for those general implications.

## Coverage gaps and safe wording

MathSciNet, zbMATH Open, and Google Scholar were **not covered**.
Semantic Scholar was not needed for a citation graph and was **not
covered**. The published decomposition-matrix PDF for \(L_2(11)\) was not
read at text depth. No unrestricted priority verdict follows.

Safe paper-level positioning is:

> The individual matching designs, modular-representation framework,
> evaluation-code/Gorenstein criteria, and projective-cover machinery are
> classical. No predecessor was located in the recorded coverage for their
> composition with the conic-ideal secant quotient, especially the explicit
> projective-cover map and the signed cubic inverse system of the
> \(B_3/H_3\) quotient configurations.

The current manuscript makes no explicit novelty claim for the uncovered
additions, so this audit requires no new priority sentence. Its main
editorial consequence is to describe the projective-cover result as a
geometric realization of a standard modular object, not as a new
projective-cover theorem.
