## EJ: structural value extracted

1. **Silver is a rational Jordan theorem.**  Work on the whole generalized
   `zeta_6` eigenspace over `K=Q(zeta_6)` with one nilpotent operation
   operator `N`.  Finite nilpotent `K[N]`-modules are Krull--Schmidt and have
   indecomposables `J_r`.  Endpoint `J_3`, strict blowup biproducts, and
   center `J_3` exclusion imply irrationality of `X x P^2`; no Gamma seed,
   Euler form, or directed flag is consumed by the cancellation.
2. **A center `J_3` costs six formal dimensions.**  One generalized
   cyclotomic eigenspace has dimension `nu_6/2`, so `J_3 => nu_6>=6`.
   Every computed prime-Fano/WCI/cyclic-cover/cubic-pencil class has
   `nu_6<=2` and is conditionally excluded at once.  The screen is stable
   under smooth birational equivalence numerically.
3. **The threshold is sharp.**  A rank-six, `rho=2`-shaped formal model has
   hard Lefschetz, twisted self-duality, full `Phi_6^3` monodromy, and
   `N^2!=0`.  Unit/top degree does not reserve a trivial pair.  No formal rank
   shortcut closes conic or del Pezzo Mori fibres.
4. **Strictness is one extension problem.**  For two blocks the obstruction
   is `[A]` modulo `N_VX-XN_W`, equivalently an `Ext^1_(K[N])` class.  For
   several blocks, split the formal filtration recursively through
   `Ext^1(M_p,F_(p-1))`.  Associated grades erase exactly these classes.
5. **The toric residual theorem is not a Silver pilot.**  Both `P^5` and
   `P^3` have empty primitive-sixth packets; `Bl_(P^3)P^5` projects to zero in
   the minimal category.  The first nonzero pilot is the non-toric cubic
   center `Bl_X P^5`.
6. **Both Mori-fibre branches are coniveau problems.**  A strict
   support-raising `N` has square zero over a curve; over a surface its square
   lands in a point packet.  Clean conic-node excision and zero stationary
   `A_5` projection are the two local tests for this common mechanism.
7. **The internal toric pairing gate is exact but separate.**  Whole-ratio
   fibres concentrate compact cycles at the bounded lifts.  The full
   `P^3` matrix still requires the pushed-down compact/ordinary excision
   zigzag and the full tame `f_Q+ZW` Lefschetz pair, not only four germs.

## TT: tempting inferences rejected

- A formal direct sum `E^3` does not define the endpoint: it supports
  `J_1^3`, `J_2+J_1`, and `J_3` with identical formal QDM and grades.
- Finite exceptional-root monodromy is semisimple in characteristic zero; it
  permutes unframed branches and cannot select a nilpotent chain.  Only a
  transported unipotent base-hyperplane/Serre action remains plausible.
- Pairwise off-diagonal matrices are not simultaneously invariant in a
  multi-block filtration.  The obstruction must be split recursively.
- Iritani's `iota^*` is a comparison-gauge block, not an operation-`N`
  extension until `N` exists and the induced commutator is computed.
- Four local `A_1` groups determine rank, not `4,10,20`; those entries live
  in the regular-fibre/path attachment of the full Lefschetz pair.
- The Clifford `ij` socle is a two-colour branch composite, not the square of
  one radical action.  It threatens Silver only after a strict bridge turns
  it into `N_1N_0`.
- Vanishing stationary `A_5` projection is not strict coniveau without
  clean-root targeting, support-locality, no horizontal escape, and an
  exhaustive target decomposition.

longer a prerequisite for minimal Silver.
