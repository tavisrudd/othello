## The problem

The target is a Hadamard matrix of order `2092 = 4 * 523`. Two routes reach it.

The **bordered** route asks for the supplementary difference set
`4-{522; 260, 261, 261, 261; 520}`: four subsets `X_i` of `Z/522` with

    sum_i X_i X_i^{-1} = 523 + 520 T

in the integral group ring. A hit becomes a Hadamard matrix of order 2092
through a Goethals--Seidel array with a border. This is where nearly all of the
campaign's work sits.

The **plain** route asks for the same object on `Z/523` with no border, whose
hit is a Hadamard matrix of order 2092 outright. It is the standing
alternative, currently not the active arm.

Both are searched as sign vectors, and every level of the campaign is a
statement about the aggregate autocorrelation of those vectors over a quotient
`Z/522 -> Z/m`. Two names recur. A **`q<m>` view** is that aggregate at level
`m`; the campaign's ladder is `q29`, `q58`, `q87`, `q174`. The **carrier** is
the full `Z/522` correlation itself — the rung above `q174`, where a state that
is exact at every level below still has to be repaired.

## Where the campaign stands

### The multiplier-assumed program is closed

A multiplier shard assumes each `X_i` is invariant under the subgroup generated
by a unit of `Z/522`. One uniform necessary test — the exact level identity
`sum_i sum_c B_{i,c}^2 = 1043 + 520 (f - 1)` for the coset counts at level
`d = 522/f`, decided as a subset-sum over projected orbits — empties 148 of the
167 nontrivial units, with 744 recorded exclusions, in twelve seconds of one
core. It reproduces the bespoke `g = 41` and `g = 91` closures, adds `g = 133`
outright, and kills every unit of order 3, 6, 12, 21, 28, 42 and 84, including
120 units the bespoke program had never examined.

What survives the uniform test is the order-14 subgroup generated by 53, its
order-7 subgroup generated by 181, two subgroups of order four and three of
order two. The banked `g = 53` work already exhausted invariance under the full
order-14 group and that shard is exactly empty, so the genuinely open remainder
of this entire program is invariance under the order-7 subgroup and under
groups of order at most four — assumptions weak enough that the search space is
correspondingly large.

Fourteen exact reductions are banked with independent replay, together with the
structural closures of `g = 41` (the `q174` projection energy is at least 2675
on every one of the 1,984,512 sealed `q18` source interfaces against a required
2083), `g = 91` (the impossible Diophantine equation `13 n27 + 15 n29 = 34` at
`q0`) and the `g = 133` and `g = 41` quotient-only prunings, which are
exhausted. The derivations are in the archive; nothing in that program is the
frontier any more.

### The unrestricted bordered route is the live arm

Because every nontrivial multiplier is closed or weak, the search cannot
inherit an orbit structure and runs on unrestricted sign vectors. Its shape is
a full-neighbourhood tabu step with exact incremental per-swap deltas, seeded
on exact order-six `q29` margin shells and lifted up the ladder.

Closed around the frontier:

- The order-six `q29` margin shell is closed as a search problem: the
  full-neighbourhood step banks exact shell states routinely, and the corpus is
  now **39 inequivalent shells**, up from the twelve the campaign ran on.
- The `q174` plateau is settled as search depth, not arithmetic. No congruence
  constrains the deviation at any modulus whose prime factors are below a
  million, the floor stays 32, and the fibre holds about `2^1322` exact
  solutions. The plateau sits roughly 130 bits (±60) into a 400-bit descent.
- Group-scoped search is closed: the exhaustive two-transfer census over all
  twelve then-banked plateau states is empty.
- The carrier `Z/522` rung above an exact `q174` state is scored. It has 173
  free correlation classes, not 174; it carries 99.5% of the deviation a `q174`
  state cannot see; its own full-neighbourhood search plateaus near 14,400; and
  the wider column neighbourhood loses to it. The campaign's route is therefore
  **solve `q174` first, then repair the fibre**.
- The character domain is closed as a source of moves. The whole condition is a
  flat spectrum at `4 * 523`, so moving there cannot change the objective; the
  move set it does offer is exactly the per-block Galois multipliers, 81
  elements above a fixed `q174` state and 1,296 above a fixed `q29` shell. An
  exhaustive census of all of them on every banked plateau state improves
  nothing, and the smallest genuine move costs a factor of seven. The residual
  is isotropic on the rung — all four movable character-order classes carry the
  same energy per character — so a reweighted objective has nothing to grip.
- Corpus width is not a lever: tripling the shell corpus costs 0.6% at equal
  wall clock. Shell *identity* may be one — a single run per shell spreads
  11.7% — but that spread is the size of the run-to-run spread, so it is a lead
  and not a measurement.
- The seventeen low-order characters are solved. They are exactly the exact
  `q18` shell, an exact quadruple is banked, and it lifts against a banked
  exact `q29` shell through the proved Gale--Ryser margin construction on all
  four blocks — giving a concrete carrier state exact at 45 of the 521
  characters at once. The order-two character additionally forces `u_1 = 0 mod
  4` on the special block, which 17 of 24 banked plateau states violate.

### The plain `Z/523` route is the standing alternative

Cyclotomy on `Z/523` is empty; the real target there is the order-three spin
shard, which is heuristically dense with solutions. A random
threshold-accepting walk stalls at carrier 73 and is immune to two orders of
magnitude of extra compute, which is what motivated building the
full-neighbourhood tabu step in the first place. Returning to this shard with
the current instrument remains available and has not been tried since.

## Open moves, in the order they are worth doing

1. **Search inside the margin fibre.** By CRT a block is an 18-by-29 binary
   matrix whose row sums are its `q18` coefficients and whose column sums are
   its `q29` coefficients, so the 2-by-2 alternating swap preserves both and
   holds all 45 low-order characters exact for a whole descent. A banked exact
   `q18` quadruple bridged against a banked exact `q29` shell gives the
   starting point, and a random point of its fibre is statistically
   indistinguishable from an unconstrained random state, so the constraint
   costs nothing at the start. The question to measure is whether a descent
   restricted to that move set reaches the unrestricted plateau — about 30,272
   in the full-carrier units of the q18 report — at equal wall clock. The
   campaign's exact incremental delta algebra applies to the swap unchanged.
2. **Replicate the per-shell sweep on the tail.** Shells 37, 23, 2, 9 and 10
   were the best of the 39 at one fifteen-second run each, with the best
   reaching carrier 13,648 — below anything the rotating corpus produced. Run
   enough rounds per shell to lift the between-shell variance above the
   between-seed variance; if it survives, concentrate the budget on the
   survivors instead of rotating all 39. Nothing cheap screens a shell today:
   its order-six margin count predicts the carrier depth reached from it not at
   all (r = 0.01) and its `q174` depth only weakly (r = 0.32).
3. **Return to the plain `Z/523` spin shard** with the full-neighbourhood step,
   as the standing alternative to the bordered ladder.
4. **The multiplier remainder**, if the unrestricted arm stalls: invariance
   under the order-7 subgroup generated by 181, and under the subgroups of
   order at most four.

## Mystery ledger

Settled items are recorded in the report that settled them and, for the
reduction program, in the archive. What is open:

- **Unrestricted order 2092.** Every multiplier-assumed result is conditional
  on an assumption now known to be false or weak, so the unassumed compressed
  search is the whole problem. Open by construction; owned by the live arm.
- **Affine multiplier classes beyond commuting translations and common unit
  dilations remain uncompiled.** Open since the reduction program; no successor
  is allocated, and the shard-window closure removed most of its value.
- **Why the search compresses the four movable character-order classes by the
  same factor of about twenty-seven and then stops.** The uniformity is
  explained — it is the lattice-step reading squared — but the depth is not. No
  experiment yet distinguishes "the move set runs out" from "the classes are
  jointly obstructed there". The seventeen low-order characters are the
  cheapest probe.
- **Whether shells differ at all.** The best-to-worst spread across the 39
  shells is the same size as the seed-to-seed spread on a fixed corpus, and
  nothing the campaign records about a shell predicts the depth reached from
  it. Gated on the replicated sweep in open move 2.
- **Whether a descent restricted to the margin fibre reaches the unrestricted
  depth.** The fibre holds 45 characters exact and its random point is as good
  as an unconstrained random state, so nothing in the starting distribution
  argues against it; the restriction of the move set is what has to be
  measured. Open, and it is open move 1.
- **How large the exact `q18` solution set is.** A climber plus a radius-four
  repair reaches it in eleven rounds against an exhaustive count of `7.1e74`
  assignments, so the density is high, but nothing measures it. Open, with no
  successor allocated.
