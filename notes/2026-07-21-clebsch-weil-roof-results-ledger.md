- **P1 spine:** theorem or proof mechanism in Paper 1's central arc.
- **P1 closing flagship:** the principal theorem at the end of Paper 1's arc.
- **P1 major support:** a substantial closing theorem that materially strengthens the arc without
  becoming a coequal spine.
- **P1 support:** a compact survival/forgetting row, lemma, or scoped cliffhanger input.
- **P1 boundary:** a proved non-identification or erasure theorem needed to state Paper 1 honestly.
- **P2 flagship candidate:** a manuscript-defining mechanism reserved for the crowns-owned sequel.
- **P2 major support:** a substantial sequel theorem below the flagship tier.
- **P2 support:** a certified input or carrier reserved for the sequel.
- **Unallocated:** certified result retained in the programme but assigned to neither manuscript.

below is the current manuscript priority, not a mathematical claim.
