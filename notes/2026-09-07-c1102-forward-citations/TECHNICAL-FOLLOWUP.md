## Rank formula: a direct characteristic-two predecessor

Kagamihara–Tsuchiya, *Stabilizer Rényi entropy of 3-uniform hypergraph states*,
arXiv:2602.23687v2, **partial**, III.A Theorem 1 and proof, p. 3, gives
m_alpha = 2^(-N) sum_x 2^((1-alpha)r(x)), where r(x) is the rank over F_2
of C(x)+C(x)^T. The proof reduces the derivative to a finite-field quadratic
form: 2^r nonzero Fourier coefficients have magnitude 2^(-r/2).
The companion's odd-prime formula uses the same rank-reduction structure.
Position the latter as an odd-characteristic extension with concrete geometric
rank distributions; no unrestricted first-rank-formula claim is supportable.
Whether that extension was already published remains an open audit question.

Chen–Yan–Zhou, *Magic of quantum hypergraph states*, arXiv:2308.01886v2,
**partial**, Proposition 1 and Theorem 1, Eqs. (13)–(15), relates Pauli moments
to traces of induced phase unitaries for qubit hypergraph states. The following
discussion specializes three-uniform states to quadratic Boolean functions.
This supplies a second direct predecessor for derivative-based moment evaluation.
It should be cited along with the rank reduction, without claiming that this
partial reading excludes every odd-prime extension elsewhere in the paper.

## A different meaning of nonlocal magic

Piotr Sierant, *Exact quantification of nonlocal magic*, arXiv:2608.28563v2,
**partial**, Results Eq. (1), Theorem 1 Eq. (2), and explanatory derivation,
optimizes multiqubit stabilizer fidelity over arbitrary local unitaries on each
side of a bipartition. The resulting formula depends on Schmidt coefficients.
The companion instead excludes global-Clifford equivalence to a product by
an invariant entropy ceiling. These are different optimization problems
(auditor comparison). Prefer the explicit phrase “Clifford-product exclusion”
for the companion's property; do not imply that its entropy test computes the
quantity in this source. This targeted promotion is adjudicated as relevant
terminology/background, not a direct proof of the companion's specific exclusions.

## Code and invariant comparisons

Prakash–Saha, *Low Overhead Qutrit Magic State Distillation*, arXiv:2403.06228v3,
**partial**, section 2 and section 3 opening, explicitly constructs triorthogonal
CSS codes by puncturing, with a product logical qutrit resource, and introduces
a length-9m ternary triorthogonal space in Eqs. (8)–(9). This confirms the
standard puncturing/transversality mechanism in the cited source. Its displayed
characteristic-three setup does not identify the companion's odd-prime geometric
cubics; this is a comparison of the read construction, not a paper-wide negative.

Kalra–Prakash, *Invariant Theory, Magic State Distillation, and Bounds on Classical
Codes*, arXiv:2501.10163v3, **partial**, sections 4.1.1–4.1.2, Theorems 2–3,
uses polynomial invariants to constrain weight enumerators of GF(4) codes.
Theorem 2 describes the invariant ring generated by f=x^2+3y^2 and
g=y^2(x^2-y^2)^2. This is a concrete distinction from an invariant polynomial
serving as a logical gate phase. Do not replace it by a broad assertion that
invariant theory was previously absent from magic-state distillation.
