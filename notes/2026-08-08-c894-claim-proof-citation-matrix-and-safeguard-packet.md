## EJ + TT closeout

**EJ.** The matrix exposes a useful asymmetry: an arithmetic predecessor
would mostly change attribution, whereas a predecessor for restriction
isomorphism changes the paper hierarchy. The human search should therefore
report the two questions separately rather than treating “Paley/Jacobi” as
one novelty verdict.

**TT.** The \(q=11\) completion is best viewed as reconstruction after
forgetting, not as another theorem branch. It belongs after the Clebsch
covering corollary: the geometric proof selects a root, the local tournament
forgets the projective realization, and bordering reconstructs the abstract
conference parent. This placement also makes clear why it has no bearing on
the unresolved all-\(k\) branches.
