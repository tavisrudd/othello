# C201 Gate 2 — split-`Z3` coverage-directed optimization

**Lane:** `relconic`

## Result

The indexed split-`Z3` family now has a deterministic coverage-directed
optimizer.  Every state is a genuine nucleus-plus-four-orbit thirteen-arc:
all six pair conditions and all four indexed ternary conditions are checked
before optimization.  One local step considers every legal replacement of
each of the four orbit labels and takes the replacement maximizing the union
of the 78 secant lines.  A restart ends only at an exhaustive one-orbit local
optimum.

The initial optimization improved the best 100,000-draw sample from 824 to
805.  The finalized replay uses that checked incumbent as one start and 499
seeded random arc starts.  The 500 local optima had ordinary-uncovered
sizes 805--935.  Nine restarts reached 805; the lexicographically least final
record was

```text
labels = (0, 250, 406, 1298)
|U(A)| = 805
```

The optimizer therefore improves the sampled record from 824 to 805, but the
best result still misses the necessary `|U(A)| <= 65` gate by 740 points.
Quadratic evaluation was skipped.

The full local-optimum histogram is emitted by the replay script.  Step counts
were

```text
0:1, 1:145, 2:205, 3:109, 4:36, 5:4.
```

The final witness is independently reconstructed from its orbit labels,
checked directly as a thirteen-arc, checked against the indexed ternary
conditions, and recounted from all 78 secant lines.  A fixed nontrivial
projectivity plus point relabeling preserves both the arc condition and the
uncovered count.

A final cheap strengthening exhausts the complete two-orbit replacement
neighborhood of the best witness.  For each of its six retained orbit pairs,
all 730,380 compatible replacement pairs are rejected or evaluated using the
exact pair and ternary bitsets.  No candidate improves 805.  Thus the witness
is one- and two-orbit locally optimal.  This is still not a global optimum
certificate; a three-orbit neighborhood returns to hundreds of millions of
candidates and was rejected by the bounded gate.

## Scope

This is a bounded deterministic heuristic result, not an exhaustive family
classification.  Exhaustive one-orbit descent proves only local optimality.
The repeated value 805 across nine basins is useful evidence but is not a
proof that 805 is the global minimum.

Accordingly the split-`Z3` family may be closed only after a certified global
coverage upper bound, or C201 itself may be closed more modestly as a failure
of the tested bounded natural-family mechanisms.  Blind enumeration of the
more than 43 billion compatible orbit quadruples remains rejected.
