**Lane:** `crowns`

**Date:** 2026-07-19

**Status:** queued; second-EV famous-problem probe

## Question and gate

Determine whether C374's minimal-support holonomy and triple-marginal moment invariants classify a
nontrivial bounded family of six-party non-GRS `AME(6,q)` states, rather than separating only the
single Clebsch state from GRS presentations.

Begin with primary-source closure on LU invariants for minimal-support AME states, then select one
finite family with at least two certified non-GRS monomial classes.  Compute the invariant only on
that family.  Continue only if it separates LU classes or collapses them by an exact equivalence
map.  Stop if it factors through the ordinary weight enumerator, known orthogonal-array invariants,
or an unbounded continuous search would be required.

Success is a family-level exact classification theorem or a sharp invariant failure.  Do not claim
the quantum MDS conjecture, experimental advantage, or classification of arbitrary AME states.
