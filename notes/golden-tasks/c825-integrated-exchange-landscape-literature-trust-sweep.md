## Objective

Determine whether the strongest single Golden quantum-statistics paper may
jointly headline the continuous real-control optimum, the order-six Hermitian
holonomy/Pareto and single-sector rigidity results, and C818's quantitative
stability theorem.  This is a publication-facing novelty, attribution, and
trust audit; it does not authorize manuscript edits.

## Binding audit rules

Follow `notes/literature-audit-conventions.md` in full.  In particular:

- every individually named source carries the prescribed read-depth field,
  version, access route, and cache key/SHA-256 when cached;
- the opening summary reports the number of full-text reads;
- every screened set records size, provenance, fields screened, and the exact
  discriminator;
- any exhaustive citing-set negative uses pinned seeds and independent
  OpenAlex, Crossref, and Semantic Scholar counts, with errors separated from
  empty results;
- searched-and-empty and inaccessible are kept distinct;
- every fetched paper is added to the shared literature cache;
- all absence and priority language remains no stronger than the recorded
  coverage permits.

## Outcome

The conventions-compliant sweep characterized eleven sources at five full-
text, three partial, and three abstract/metadata-only depths; independently
checked three pinned citation graphs through OpenAlex, Crossref, and Semantic
Scholar; and audited all eight claims separately.  It recommends the integrated
theorem-first package, led by C818's defect-to-real-orbit stability theorem.
Squared-spectrum rigidity survives only with the narrow sign-blind
cross-block/exchange formulation.  No continuous-control or exact Pareto
predecessor was located in the bounded coverage.  The Et-Taoui--Makhlouf and
Cheng--Lv--Sun article bodies remain unreached, forcing conservative wording
but not a sequel split.  No manuscript file was changed.  Full report:
`notes/2026-08-02-c825-integrated-exchange-landscape-literature-trust-sweep.md`.
