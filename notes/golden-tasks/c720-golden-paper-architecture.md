**Lane:** `golden`

**Status:** queued; lane entry task

## Objective

Freeze a standalone mathematics-paper architecture containing the complete
C704--C710 post-700 development around the golden conference operator and
its functorially related shadow sisters.  Give every result a core/body/
appendix placement, with a precise theorem spine, strict noninterference

## Working identity

**Working title:** *The golden conference operator and its shadow sisters*.

**Central mechanism:** one marked operator \(C\), with \(C^2=5I\), is sent
through middle exterior power, golden compression, Majorana commutator,
determinant/Pfaffian, adjugation, and centered squaring.  The resulting

commuting theorem rather than a list of formula coincidences.

   distinguish classical Segre/Igusa and outer-automorphism geometry from

   Before freezing the explanatory hierarchy, run the two bounded
   mechanism tests in

   nor extracts material from Paper III.  Shared background is cited; every
   Golden theorem is paper-owned from its own source reports.
   The plan must account for every C704--C710 result without silently
   exporting any of them to Paper III or an unallocated sibling paper.
   It may separately discuss whether any overlapping exposition should move
   out of Paper III after review, but may not perform or presume that change.
8. After architecture acceptance, create the authoritative manuscript root
   `papers/golden-operator/` and its paper-owned verification surface.
