## Gates

1. **Stability gate.**  Establish an exhaustive normal form or an invariant
   substitute before claiming a global distance bound.  Prefer an exact formula
   or sharp obstruction; stop at a qualified local/ansatz theorem if global
   classification is unavailable.
2. **Novelty gate.**  Audit separately: continuous-control global optimality,
   the exact mixed-plane Pareto segment, single-sector rigidity, the averaged
   defect functional, and the structural parity/Pfaffian proof.  Follow the
   repository literature-audit conventions and keep every absence claim
   qualified across recorded coverage gaps.
3. **Promotion gate.**  No manuscript edit before gates 1--2.  If they pass,
   build the smallest coherent variant: one main continuous-control theorem,
   one complex Pareto/rigidity corollary, and the compressed structural proof.
   Prefer replacing subordinate material over increasing narrative width.
4. **Review gate.**  Run the complete paper checks, visual inspection, and a
   context-free A/B assessment against the current authoritative paper.  Adopt
   nothing without a clear net gain and no trust/literature regression.
