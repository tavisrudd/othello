# C757 — Golden determinantal cubic-wall nodes

**Lane:** `golden`

**Status:** in progress

## Objective

Determine the singular scheme and exact node count of the cubic wall
\(\{Z_T=0\}\subset\mathbf P(A_X)\) supplied by the Golden
\(3\times3\) linear determinantal representation.  Compare the result with
the classical node bound for determinantal cubic threefolds, and strengthen
the manuscript only after exact certification and an independent replay.

## Acceptance gate

- derive the singular equations from the frozen Golden cubic;
- certify every projective singular point over an explicit exact field and
  verify its local quadratic type;
- prove global exhaustiveness, not merely find witnesses;
- record the classical comparison with an exact consulted source;
- commit the report, generator, compact certificate, hashes, and independent
  replay as one reproducible bundle;
- pass the paper-owned checks and warning-free manuscript build.
