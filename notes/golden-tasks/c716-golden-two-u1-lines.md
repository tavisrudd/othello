# C716 — Golden synthesis of two-\(U(1)\) anomaly lines

**Lane:** `golden`

**Status:** queued after C715

## Objective

Lift the golden anomaly transducer from rational points of the Segre cubic
to lines on it, thereby realizing and classifying six-Weyl anomaly data for
two Abelian gauge factors in the C707 operator language.

## Gates

1. For two charge vectors \(q,r\), derive the line-containment criterion
   from \(\sum_i(q_i+tr_i)^3=0\): the individual linear/cubic equations and
   the two mixed anomaly equations
   \(\sum q_i^2r_i=\sum q_ir_i^2=0\).
2. Import only the needed classical Fano-variety result: 15 plane components
   for nonchiral lines and six degree-five del Pezzo components for chiral
   lines.  Fix its exact relation to the C707 marking.
3. Use the C715 inverse to synthesize explicit physical control families
   representing every component type, with rational witnesses and exact
   amplitude verification.
4. Decide whether a line of amplitude vectors comes from a linear,
   fractional-linear, or necessarily nonlinear path-control family.  Prove
   the minimal degree or record the obstruction.
5. Translate the two mixed anomaly equations into determinant or Pfaffian
   identities for two coupled golden controls.
6. Test whether the six chiral components acquire a natural outer-\(S_6\)
   action or operational invariant beyond their known algebraic geometry.

## Acceptance

- One structural theorem relating two-\(U(1)\) anomaly cancellation, Segre
  lines, and golden controls.
- At least one exact nonchiral and one exact chiral synthesized family, with
  independent replay.
- A clear verdict on whether the operator lift adds new mathematics beyond
  a reparametrization of the known Fano surface.

## Boundary

Do not reprove the full Fano classification unless the operator marking
exposes a genuinely shorter proof.  Do not infer dark-sector phenomenology
from charge arithmetic alone.

## Dependencies

C715's inverse and exceptional-set theorem.
