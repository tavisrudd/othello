# C814: Golden quantum-statistics mathematical ceilings

**Lane:** `golden`

**Status:** queued; mathematics research only

## Objective

Determine the strongest remaining mathematical extensions of the Golden
quantum-statistics programme without editing any manuscript.  Produce exact
results, sharp counterexamples, or proved obstruction boundaries for later
independent review and a separate paper-inclusion decision.

This is the single Golden umbrella for the programme.  It must not duplicate
the Clebsch-owned C810 adversarial aligned-certificate-distance task or C812
higher cut-moment separation task.  Import their frozen results when available;
if either is still open, record the dependency and continue with an independent
branch.

## Mandatory pivot protocol

Before every pivot from one research branch or attack to another, run and
record all three passes:

1. `ej`: extract every free or cheap strengthening, implication, or newly
   exposed invariant from the current branch;
2. `tt`: ask what structural classification, natural generality, hidden
   hypothesis, or neighboring theory has been missed;
3. `aa`: compare genuinely different attacks, including at least one route
   with different intermediate objects or evidence shape.

Take any cheap task-owned upgrades exposed by these passes before pivoting.
The task report must identify each pivot and the resulting decision.  A failed
novelty crown triggers the bounded novelty-extraction convention before that
branch closes.

## Research branches

### A. Robust and unlabelled inverse boundary

- Determine whether the unlabelled multiset of balanced exchange spectra, the
  purity histogram, the triple-block union profile, or a precisely stated
  combination determines a conference switching class.
- Begin with classification-backed exact collision searches at the first
  orders having multiple switching classes; stop broadening a statistic at
  the first sharp collision unless an `ej`/`tt`/`aa` pass justifies the next
  invariant.
- Translate C810's minimum-distance and decoding-radius results, when frozen,
  into the strongest exact robust-reconstruction statement supported by the
  evidence.  Do not re-run its search programme independently.

### B. Higher exchange statistics

- Import C812's first exact same-order separation or collision for third and
  higher balanced-cut moments.
- Determine which block-union profiles control the successive moments and
  whether any bounded family of moments is faithful on an audited class.
- Distinguish labelled reconstruction, unlabelled distributions, and local
  optical observables throughout.

### C. Continuous-control exchange frontier

- Replace balanced Boolean cuts by normalized real diagonal controls for the
  Golden six-mode transfer.
- Determine the attainable region, sharp inequalities, and equality cases for
  the intrinsic exchange quantities
  \(h_3(K^\dagger K)\), \(s_{(2,1)}(K^\dagger K)\),
  \(e_3(K^\dagger K)\), and the oriented determinant where defined.
- Test whether balanced Boolean controls are genuine Pareto extremizers or
  merely one rigid stratum.  Use the existing Golden fibre and chamber results
  as inputs, not as conclusions to be restated.

### D. Generalized conference-class rigidity

- Test the cut-independent exchange-spectrum classification for complex
  Hermitian conference matrices and, only if supported, for a sharply defined
  two-eigenvalue Seidel/ETF class.
- Start with the cheapest exact falsifiers and isolate precisely which steps of
  the real signed Ramsey proof survive.  Prefer a sharp obstruction or
  counterexample to a vague extension.
