# C742 — Unmarked Golden reconstruction and global degeneracy complex

**Lane:** `golden`

**Status:** queued after C739 manuscript placement

## Objective

Determine whether Paper IV's normalized observable tensor diagram reconstructs
the Golden source rather than merely propagating from a supplied fully marked
presentation.  In the strongest positive form, prove that every primitive
integral outer-\(S_6\)-equivariant synchronized Pfaffian system with Joubert top
cubic comes from a unique regular conference two-graph, up to the already
identified support-orientation, pole, switching-frame, and
golden-conjugation torsors.

Globalize the result on the correct moduli space.  Test whether one pole-marked
golden cross-block complex has the pair-collision divisor, Jacobian rank-drop
scheme, simultaneous-Pfaffian base scheme, and Segre polar base as successive
intrinsic critical/Fitting strata.  A sharp nonexistence or descent-obstruction
theorem is an acceptable outcome; the task must not force unequal schemes into
one false master discriminant.

## Claim ladder

Resolve these claims separately; failure at one level must not be hidden by a
weaker success at another.

1. **Category:** define the observable input, integral lattice, equivalence
   relation, projective scalars, and all four torsors before stating
   reconstruction.
2. **Generic reconstruction:** decide whether the normalized cubic and
   synchronized skew map recover the unoriented conference two-graph without
   supplying \(C_T\) in advance.
3. **Integral rigidity:** decide whether primitive integrality removes every
   scalar or lattice ambiguity beyond common sign.
4. **Coherent-family recovery:** reconstruct the outer six-family and its
   covariance, not just one abstract order-six conference class.
5. **Pole descent:** construct the golden eigenbundles and cross block on the
   pole-marked cover, then determine the exact obstruction to forgetting the
   pole.
6. **Global degeneracy complex:** produce one bundle map or derived complex
   whose correctly indexed Fitting/critical strata recover the distinct C739

   constraints.
7. **Nilpotent mechanism:** explain the Jacobian scheme's lower-dimensional
   nilpotent defect at the ten (3+3) points by local algebra, deformation
   theory, or a second independent exact implementation.
8. **Return compatibility:** prove whether the canonical \(36\to6\) subgroup
   incidence is induced by the same reconstructed object and makes the
   projective \(6\to10\to36\to6\) cycle commute.

## `tt` and red-team gate

Before accepting a positive theorem, try to break it with each of the
following.

- Remove the six-cell target or full outer action and exhibit the nonstandard
  \(S_5\) Pfaffian presentation as a competing object.
- Do not define the observable six-cell target by inserting the conference
  matrices or their switching frames.  Specify its abstract outer module,
  skew-coordinate lattice, and action without the source to be reconstructed;
  otherwise the theorem is circular.  Compare this genuinely unmarked target
  with C728's source-relative monomial realization.
- Vary common scale, support orientation, switching frames, affine pole, and
  golden embedding independently; prove that the proposed quotient removes
  exactly the claimed ambiguities and no others.
- Test whether two nonisomorphic integral lifts have the same top cubic or
  determinant sextic.
- Distinguish reconstruction of an abstract conference isomorphism type from
  recovery of labelled axes, variables, outer carrier, and coherent family.
- Check stack stabilizers and special collision strata; a generic
  reconstruction theorem may fail precisely at the ten nodes or fifteen
  lines.
- Do not use a finite norm along \(M_{0,7}\to M_{0,6}\): its fibres are
  one-dimensional.  Any pushforward claim must state its derived amplitude,
  coherence, and base-change hypotheses.
- Do not infer a global matrix factorization on \(M_{0,6}\) from descent of
  its determinant cubic.
- Attempt a counterexample in which the same reduced collision support has a
  different nilpotent thickening.
- Keep the order-eight HMSV geometry as a boundary test, not a new exploration
  branch or evidence that a Golden iteration exists there.

The final report must state which attacks failed, which hypotheses they made
necessary, and whether the surviving theorem is generic, stack-theoretic,
integral, or scheme-theoretic.

## `aa` gate

At the first genuine stall, stop pushing the current proof and compare at
least three attacks with the same objective but different input/output shapes:

1. **Representation/classification attack:** reconstruct the conference
   functional from multiplicity-one maps, integral lattices, stabilizers, and
   orbit data.
2. **Moduli/Fitting attack:** construct tautological skew bundles on
   \((\mathbf P^1)^6\), \(M_{0,7}\), or their compactifications and compute
   intrinsic degeneracy ideals.
3. **Local-algebra attack:** derive the ten-point nilpotent structure from
   completed local rings, tangent cones, or an explicit deformation normal
   form.
4. **Groupoid/gerbe attack:** encode pole, sign, switching, and golden
   conjugation as descent data and identify the exact obstruction class.
5. **Certificate-first attack:** compute a bounded exact classification or
   primary decomposition with a compact certificate and an independent
   implementation, then extract the human theorem it suggests.

Record why the selected route dominates and carry one genuinely independent
route far enough to test its central inference.  A negative verdict requires
two failed attacks that encounter the same precisely stated obstruction; one
stalled calculation is not an obstruction theorem.

## Acceptance

One of the following must hold.

### Positive crown

- A reconstruction theorem recovers the conference two-graph and coherent
  outer six-family from an explicitly defined observable tensor diagram whose
  target does not already contain the conference matrices or switching frames.
- The residual torsors are necessary and sufficient, with no suppressed pole
  or determinant-line choice.
- One global pole-marked complex explains the strict degeneracy filtration,
  including the ten-point nilpotent defect.
- The canonical \(36\to6\) return is compatible with the reconstructed object.
- Complete human proofs, attribution, exact evidence, and independent replay
  pass, followed by a manuscript placement verdict.

### Sharp obstruction crown

- An explicit pair of inequivalent sources with the same proposed observable,
  or a precise descent/Fitting obstruction, disproves the strongest theorem.
- The minimal additional datum or weakest viable replacement theorem is
  proved.
- The obstruction survives the required `aa` attacks and red-team examples.
- The paper receives a short boundary theorem rather than speculative global
  language.
