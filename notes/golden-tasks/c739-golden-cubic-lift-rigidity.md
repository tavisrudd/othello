## Research probes

### P1. Invariant-cubic source

Prove the exact uniqueness statement for the relevant signed/outer
(S_6)-module: determine the invariant and covariant spaces in degrees two
through six, identify the Segre cubic and its polar map intrinsically, and
place every currently known cubic realization in that representation-theoretic
space.  Record all twists, orientations, and characteristic restrictions.

### P2. Lift rigidity

Define a natural equivalence relation on coherent order-six conference,
Pfaffian, (3\times3) cross-golden determinant, and synchronized spinor
presentations.  Search for competing lifts before attempting uniqueness.
Classify the lifts under explicit integrality, outer-equivariance, normalization,
and marking hypotheses, or exhibit the smallest counterexample and identify
which hypothesis fails.  The target theorem is a precise version of:

> the coherent Golden family is the unique integral outer-equivariant
> Pfaffian/determinantal lift of the Segre cubic.

### P3. Recovery and minimal information

Turn the known forward and reverse arrows into a reconstruction lattice.
For each shadow, determine whether it recovers the cubic, unordered switching
line/two-graph, support half, orientation, golden embedding, and selected
sister.  Prove lower bounds for every claimed necessary bit and identify any
hidden torsor.  Include the determinant-sextic/dimer equivalence and the sharp
failure of the bare deep-hole conic.

### P4. Master degeneracy scheme

Compare scheme-theoretically, not only set-theoretically, the Segre polar base
scheme, differential rank-loss locus, pair-collision divisor, inverse-map
exceptional locus, simultaneous Majorana zero loci, and vectorlike/two-Abelian
anomaly strata.  Determine which are equal, reduced pullbacks, ramified
pullbacks, or proper refinements of one master discriminant.  A clean negative
dictionary is acceptable where equality fails.

### P5. The (6\to10\to36\to6) cycle

Use the order-ten sign Gram factor, 36 extremal halves, sign-kernel borderings,
Sylvester graph, and outer-involution model to seek a canonical marked quotient
back to the six Golden sisters.  Determine the minimal extra marking if no
unmarked (36\to6) map exists.  Explain the direct iteration stop through the
redundancy change and the weighted identity

\[
K^2=10K+75I,\qquad (K-5I)^2=100I.
\]

### P6. Dimension-six boundary

Formulate the even-order (2m) analogue in which the middle exterior and
commutator Pfaffian both have degree (m).  Test only the smallest informative
neighboring cases needed to decide which mechanisms are generic and which
require (m=3), the outer automorphism of (S_6), or the Segre cubic.  The
deliverable is a theorem/no-go table, not a new census branch.

### P7. Transfer and use tests

State and test a portability criterion: when a code, graph, lattice, or
geometric construction recovers the marked regular two-graph, exactly which
Golden shadows follow functorially?  Extract three bounded demonstrations:

1. a robust recognition protocol using five-cycle certification, optimal
   three-sign sister identification, and two-error syndrome correction;
2. rational one- and two-(U(1)) anomaly synthesis through
   \((\mathbf P^1)^6/\!/\operatorname{PGL}_2\), without promoting anomaly
   arithmetic to a gauge-theory model; and
3. a symbolic/combinatorial switching-class recognizer comparing the sextic
   and dimer presentations.

These are mathematical demonstrations only.  Platform engineering remains
C719, and bosonic complementarity remains C718.

### P8. Arithmetic and categorical diagnostics

and (E_8\to II_{10,10}) repair follow from the lift classification or require
independent data.  Determine whether the six matrix factorizations support a
meaningful (S_6)-equivariant categorical statement.  Stop at a precise
obstruction if a derived/categorical formulation adds no theorem-level content.
