# C735 — Golden manuscript consolidation and proof architecture

**Lane:** `golden`

**Status:** ready; post-C716 consolidation pivot

## Objective

Turn the completed Golden theorem portfolio into an operator-first manuscript
whose exposition is as strong as its mathematics.  Consolidate before opening
another exploratory Golden branch: make the common propagation mechanism
complete in the paper, subordinate provenance and applications to it, and
document every theorem's proof, attribution, and trust boundary.

The frozen C720 charter remains the historical admission decision.  C735
owns a dated post-charter placement delta rather than rewriting that charter.

## Required outputs

1. A dated post-charter architecture report covering C715, C716, and
   C727--C729.  For every result, assign exactly one disposition:
   main theorem, main-text corollary, compressed example, appendix, or sequel.
2. A theorem--proof--trust ledger for every theorem and proposition currently
   in `papers/golden-operator/golden_operator.tex`, recording:
   - exact statement and hypotheses;
   - paper-owned, classical, or transported content;
   - complete human-proof or citation location;
   - computational/formal dependency and replay artifact;
   - remaining proof-writing or citation debt.
3. A revised table of contents and length budget that reflects the manuscript
   actually being written.  Target a severe 40--50-page main argument and a
   55--65-page paper including controlled appendices unless the proof ledger
   demonstrates that a longer version is necessary.
4. A complete operator-first draft through the central mechanism:
   marked covariance; exterior/Joubert/commutator cubics; polar map and
   assembled adjugate; golden compression and determinantal geometry.
5. A synchronized verification ledger and claim-level attribution map for
   every result already present in the manuscript, including C727--C729.

## Structural adjustments

- Put the abstract marked operator and its common proof first.
- Move recovery/provenance after the propagation theorem it supplies.
- Place anomaly synthesis and two-\(U(1)\) lines inside the instruments and
  fermionic consequence section, not beside the core mechanism.
- Split the current omnibus propagation statement when one theorem carries
  distinct conceptual scales.  Keep one headline theorem and name genuine
  consequences separately.
- Keep C716's one-moving-path theorem and C728's synchronized-spinor theorem
  in the main text, compactly.
- Keep C729's conceptual \(6\to10\) reflection mechanism in the main text;
  move Paley orbit/design refinements and large exact tables outward unless
  they prove a headline assertion.
- Make exceptional, Clifford, code, and lattice sections skippable without
  breaking the central proof.

## Gates

1. **Placement gate:** every completed post-charter Golden result has one
   explicit home or sequel verdict; nothing remains assigned only to a report.
2. **Proof gate:** the manuscript contains complete reader-facing proofs of
   the central operator spine, not theorem statements backed only by frozen
   task reports.
3. **Hierarchy gate:** a cold reader can identify the source object, principal
   theorem, three governing mechanisms, and novelty boundary from the first
   five pages.
4. **Trust gate:** every finite claim in the manuscript appears in the
   paper-owned verification ledger with its exact bundle and replay status.
5. **Build gate:** the isolated manuscript build is warning-free, and theorem
   labels, citations, section references, page budget, and organization text
   agree with the revised structure.
