## Goal

Humanize the stronger computer-assisted reverse theorem found by `ej3`: on at
least seven vertices, a two-graph is determined up to complementation by the
four-sets containing zero or four coherent triples.  Deduce that Greaves--Suda's
determinant-(-3) design reconstructs every conference two-graph in its range,
explain the genuine small-order exceptions, and isolate the first higher
balanced-cut moment not forced by the conference 3-design parameters.

## Starting evidence

- Greaves--Suda, arXiv `2402.17528`, Example 2.3, owns the forward
  (3\text{-}(4n+2,4,n-1)) determinant-design construction.
- C788 identifies its balanced induced-block count with exchange purity and
  gives the universal mean and variance.
- An exhaustive normalized enumeration through seven vertices gives the sharp
  general pattern.  At seven vertices, all (2^15=32768) two-graphs have
  (16384) aligned-four-set images and every fibre has size exactly two, the
  complementary pair.  For four, five, and six vertices larger fibres occur
  (maximum sizes 6, 12, and 12 respectively).
- The exact seven-vertex statement already gives the unrestricted
  computer-assisted theorem.  Restrict two candidate two-graphs to every
  seven-subset.  On each they agree or are complementary.  Adjacent
  seven-subsets overlap in six vertices, so inspection of any common triple
  forces the same choice; connectedness of the Johnson graph on seven-subsets
  makes that choice global.  Conference regularity disappears from the reverse
  direction entirely.
- The exact Paley checks at orders 14 and 18 show that the graph on triples
  generated by co-membership in determinant-(-3) blocks has exactly two
  components, the coherent and incoherent triples.  Thus the design recovers
  the two-graph up to complement in those cases.
- At order ten the Steiner parameter (\lambda_3=1) makes the simple
  triple-overlap graph 30 isolated four-triple components, but general
  seven-local faithfulness predicts that the full marked design is still
  faithful.  Assigning a binary coherent/incoherent color to each Steiner
  block and imposing the two-two condition on every nonblock four-set gives an
  exact GF(2) system of rank 28 and nullity two; exactly two admissible
  colorings survive, one complementary pair of weight 15.
- The Paley balanced-cut histograms are (5^{546},7^{1170}) at order 14 and
  (22^{918},23^{3672},24^{2448},25^{6120},26^{7344},27^{2584},29^{1224})
  at order 18.  Their third centered moments are respectively
  (-840/1331) and (9216/17875); unlike mean and variance, this level depends
  on triple-block intersection data not fixed by the 3-design parameters.

The seven-vertex census remains a compact exact falsifier, but C794 has now
