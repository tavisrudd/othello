# C768 — Full-text literature and attribution audit

**Lane:** `golden`

**State:** queued; required before submission wording

## Objective

Close the paper-facing attribution and absence claims under
`notes/literature-audit-conventions.md`.

## Coverage

- determinant/permanent and immanant scattering;
- boson--fermion complementarity, including partial distinguishability;
- conference matrices, real ETFs, and six-line qutrit measurements;
- Segre/Joubert and outer-(S_6) invariant theory;
- one- and two-(U(1)) anomaly parametrization;
- photonic fermion emulation, antisymmetric three-qutrit preparation, transfer
  tomography, and programmable multimode hardware.

## Acceptance

- every named source carries a read-depth marker and access/version record;
- cached full texts carry keys and SHA-256 hashes;
- load-bearing source characterizations are checked against full text;
- any experimental-absence statement records queries, screened sets, and
  coverage gaps;
- MathSciNet, Google Scholar, zbMATH, and citation-graph coverage are stated
  explicitly;
- the manuscript uses no “first” claim and only audit-licensed
  “to our knowledge” language.
