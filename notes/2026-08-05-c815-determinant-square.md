# C815 — the determinant square of the order-six commutator bracket

**Date:** 2026-08-05
**Task:** C815
**Lane:** `clebsch`
**Status:** landed and validated; gap class B row OPER-1's determinant-square clause is closed

## What was proved

New module `lean/RelativeConicArcs/GoldenCommutatorDeterminant.lean`:

- `det_skewOfUpperEntries_eq_pfaffianSix_sq` — for the order-six matrix built
  from fifteen free strictly-upper-triangular entries, the determinant is the
  square of the first-row Pfaffian expansion `pfaffianSix`.
- `eq_skewOfUpperEntries` — every order-six matrix with vanishing diagonal
  whose transpose is its negative is that matrix on its own fifteen upper
  entries. The diagonal is a hypothesis rather than a consequence: skewness
  alone gives `2 A i i = 0`, which is not enough over an arbitrary commutative
  ring.
- `det_eq_pfaffianSix_sq` — the general statement, over any commutative ring,
  with no invertibility or characteristic assumption.
- `det_conferenceBracket_eq_sixteen_triangleCubic_sq` — composing with the
  existing `pfaffianSix_conferenceBracket_eq_four_triangleCubic` gives
  `det[D_x, C] = 16 Z(x)^2` for the fixed order-six integral conference
  matrix, where `Z` is its triangle-holonomy cubic.

Supporting declarations: `bracketMatrix_skew`, `bracketMatrix_diag`,
`conferenceMatrixOver_transpose`.

Mathlib carries no Pfaffian theory and no `det_fin_six`, so the identity is a
polynomial comparison after determinant expansion. The expansion that works is
one `rw [Matrix.det_succ_row_zero]` followed by a `simp` carrying
`Matrix.det_succ_row_zero`, `Fin.sum_univ_succ` and `Fin.succAbove`, then
`ring`. Without `Fin.succAbove` in the simp set the cofactor indices survive as
opaque atoms and `ring` fails on a goal that is not a polynomial identity in
the entries; that failure looks like a false statement and is not one. The
whole module elaborates in about eight seconds with default heartbeat and
recursion limits.
