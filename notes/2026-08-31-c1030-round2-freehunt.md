## SEV2 — Witness-annihilating merge: `None < Some` wipes found witnesses from two engines' reports

**Location**: `/home/tavis/src/othello/ergodis-private/src/g41_joint_quotient_search.rs:936-939` and, line-for-line identical, `/home/tavis/src/othello/ergodis-private/src/g53_sparse_defect.rs:476-479` (both re-verified immediately before this write-up):

```rust
    if right.first_witness_root < left.first_witness_root {
        left.first_witness_root = right.first_witness_root;
        left.first_witness = right.first_witness;
    }
```

The fixed sibling copy, `/home/tavis/src/othello/ergodis-private/src/g41_q29_evolve.rs:1960-1965`:

```rust
    if right.first_q29_root.is_some()
        && (left.first_q29_root.is_none() || right.first_q29_root < left.first_q29_root)
    {
```

**Mechanism**: `first_witness_root` is `Option<u32>`, and Rust's derived `Option` ordering puts `None` below every `Some`. The intended semantics is "keep the witness from the smallest root ordinal". The raw `<` instead makes `None` win every comparison in one direction and lose it in the other — and both directions destroy the witness: with `left = Some(w)`, `right = None`, the condition is true and the witness is overwritten with `None`; with `left = None`, `right = Some(w)`, the condition is false and the incoming witness is discarded. A witness therefore survives a merge only when *both* sides hold one.

That would already lose witnesses in any mixed hit/miss campaign, but the reduction framework makes it unconditional. `reduce_roots` (`/home/tavis/src/othello/papers/complete-repair-ports/ergodis/src/root_execution.rs:45-46`, fold at `:62`) documents "Callers must supply an associative reducer with `identity()` as its identity element" and folds `aggregate = reduce(aggregate, output)` starting from `identity()`. Both call sites seed the identity with `first_witness_root: None` (`g41_joint_quotient_search.rs:1003-1004`; the analogous initializer in `census_g53_sparse_q4_roots`). The very first fold is therefore `merge(None-identity, per-root-output)` — the discarding direction — so the witness of the first hit is dropped on arrival, and every later hit is dropped the same way. The supplied "identity" is not an identity for this reducer; the documented contract is violated, and the violation is total rather than probabilistic.

The per-root reports really do populate the fields: `g53_sparse_defect.rs:434-439` sets `constructive_hits: u32::from(witness.is_some())`, `first_witness_root: witness.map(|_| ordinal.0)`, `first_witness: witness`; `g41_joint_quotient_search.rs:866-867` sets both fields on a hit after replaying the witness through all ten quotient equations (`replay_witness`, `:851`). The counters are merged with plain checked addition and survive; only the witness content is annihilated.

**Concrete trigger**: any invocation of `search_g41_joint_quotient` (bin `g41_joint_quotient_search`) or `census_g53_sparse_q4_roots` (bin `g53_sparse_q4_join`), at any thread count including 1, in which at least one root produces a hit. The emitted JSON then reads `constructive_quotient_hits: N` (respectively `constructive_hits: N`) with `N >= 1` while `first_witness_root` and `first_witness` are `null`. The identity-element fold guarantees the wipe, so no special scheduling or mix of hits and misses is needed; hits themselves are plausible because `census_g41_joint_full_mitm` constructs witnesses for all 768 campaign roots, so exact selections exist in every root's domain.

**Reachability of harm**: the run's numeric claims stay correct — hits were replay-verified at discovery time, before the merge, and the hit counters are summed correctly; in `g41_joint_quotient_search` the same digits also survive independently in `best_residual_witness` when the hit reduced the residual to zero. No committed note or evidence file carries either report's fields (checked: no `constructive_quotient_hits` or engine-`first_witness` occurrence under `notes/` or `ergodis-private/evidence/`), and the sealed `g53_sparse_q4_proof` does not consume the buggy census (it calls `census_g53_q4_fibres`/`verify_g53_sparse_q4_census` in `g53_sparse_q4_oracle.rs`, a different path). Hence SEV2, not SEV1: a silently wrong field (`null` asserting "no witness recorded" against a nonzero hit counter in the same object), reachable on every run, but not currently feeding a paper-facing claim — and self-contradicting enough to be caught by an attentive reader.

**Impact**: the constructive-witness output of two discovery engines is dead — any downstream consumer extracting the replayable witness from the report gets `null` regardless of what the search found, and the reproducibility value of a hit (the exact digits that passed the ten-equation replay) is lost with it. The g53_sparse_defect copy matters more prospectively: its report distinguishes `constructive_hits` from `exhaustive_misses`, so it is built to carry positive authority whose witness is precisely the part being destroyed.

**Described fix (not applied)**: adopt the `g41_q29_evolve.rs:1960-1965` guard in both raw copies (`right.is_some() && (left.is_none() || right < left)`), or compare on `(root.is_none(), root)` to make `None` sort last; add a reducer unit test asserting `merge(identity(), x) == x` for a witness-bearing `x` — the exact contract `root_execution.rs:46` states — which would have caught both copies and will protect the next one.
