# C909 — arbitrary-depth finite-etale graph NS lattice and PD theorem

Date: 2026-08-11

Status: theorem for compatible finite-etale, block-respecting graph data; no manuscript, PDF, mirror, Lean, or commit change

## Verdict

The arbitrary-depth finite-etale application closes positively. After finite unramified splitting, the complete graph Neron--Severi coefficient lattice is a symmetric matrix-of-ideals lattice. For a depth--eigen pair `(a,lambda)`, cross coefficients have exact valuation

\[
 e_{(a,\lambda),(b,\mu)}=
 \begin{cases}
 \max(a,b),&\lambda=\mu,\\
 a+b,&\lambda\ne\mu.
 \end{cases}
\tag{1}
\]

Both values satisfy `2e>=a+b`. The DVR rank-one criterion therefore proves that the full NS lattice is generated by actual rank-one square-zero divisors, including unit blocks and at `p=2`. Consequently the cohomological divided-power envelope of the full NS lattice equals its ordinary product image in every degree.

The result requires compatible finite-etale slope data that lift to one finite-etale `Z_p` algebra and a source polarization decomposition respected by its idempotents. It does not discuss non-etale/nilpotent carries.

## 1. Convention and compatible data

Work over `Z_p`. Let the source coefficient polarization be

\[
 G=P B,\qquad P=\bigoplus_a p^aI_{M_a},\qquad B=\bigperp_aB_a,
\tag{2}
\]

where every `B_a` is unimodular. Assume a block-respecting graph: the slope is self-adjoint in the convention required by this source form, and the compatible finite-etale slope algebras at all truncated levels come from a finite-etale `Z_p` algebra acting on the coefficient module and preserving the depth summands `M_a`.

After a finite unramified extension `O/Z_p`, its self-adjoint primitive idempotents give an exact `B`-orthogonal decomposition

\[
 M_O=\bigperp_{a,\lambda}L_{a,\lambda},\qquad
 P|_{L_{a,\lambda}}=p^a,\qquad
 T|_{L_{a,\lambda}}=\tau_\lambda I.
\tag{3}
\]

Here `T` is an exact integral lift. Since the algebra is monogenic and etale, distinct primitive labels have distinct residues, so

\[
 \tau_\lambda-\tau_\mu\in O^\times\quad(\lambda\ne\mu).
\tag{4}
\]

No diagonalization of any `B_a` is made or needed. The source polarization condition is expressed with `B` (or `B^{-1}` after the dual graph convention); the following divisor calculation uses the symmetric coefficient `A`, so it is unchanged by that bookkeeping choice.

## 2. Exact graph coefficient lattice

Use the graph basis

\[
 C=\begin{pmatrix}P^{-1}&P^{-1}T\\0&I\end{pmatrix}.
\tag{5}
\]

For a symmetric source divisor coefficient `A=A^t`, direct multiplication gives

\[
 C\begin{pmatrix}0&A\\-A&0\end{pmatrix}C^t=
 \begin{pmatrix}
 P^{-1}(AT^t-TA)P^{-1}&P^{-1}A\\
 -AP^{-1}&0
 \end{pmatrix}.
\tag{6}
\]

Thus the complete local graph NS coefficient lattice consists exactly of

\[
 A=A^t,\qquad P^{-1}A\in M_g(O),\qquad
 P^{-1}(AT^t-TA)P^{-1}\in M_g(O).
\tag{7}
\]

The transpose of the middle condition is `AP^{-1}` integral, so the two graph cross-pairings are both present without an additional hypothesis.

For `A_{a\lambda,b\mu}` in the decomposition (3), the middle condition imposes valuation at least `max(a,b)`. The final condition is

\[
 p^{-a-b}(\tau_\mu-\tau_\lambda)A_{a\lambda,b\mu}\in O.
\tag{8}
\]

If `lambda!=mu`, (4) makes the difference a unit, so this imposes valuation `a+b`. If `lambda=mu`, the expression is zero and imposes nothing further. This proves the exact formula (1), including the within-eigenblock cross terms at unequal depths.

Equivalently, the lattice is

\[
 \left(\bigoplus_{a,\lambda}p^a\operatorname{Sym}(L_{a,\lambda}^*)\right)
 \oplus
 \left(\bigoplus_{\substack{(a,\lambda)<(b,\mu)\\\lambda=\mu}}
 p^{\max(a,b)}(L_{a,\lambda}^*\otimes L_{b,\lambda}^*)_{\rm sym}\right)
 \oplus
 \left(\bigoplus_{\lambda\ne\mu}p^{a+b}
 (L_{a,\lambda}^*\otimes L_{b,\mu}^*)_{\rm sym}\right).
\tag{9}
\]

The last display is read blockwise over all depth labels in the final summand. Depth zero gives the unit-block cases automatically.

## 3. Rank-one and PD consequences

For every pair of blocks, (1) gives

\[
 e_{ij}\geq\max(a_i,a_j)\geq\left\lceil(a_i+a_j)/2\right\rceil.
\tag{10}
\]

The exact DVR criterion in `2026-08-11-c909-dvr-symmetric-ideal-rank-one-pd.md` applies. Hence (9) is generated over `O` by its rank-one forms. Explicitly, for every cross generator use the signed identity

\[
 p^e(uv^t+vu^t)=p^e(u+v)(u+v)^t-p^euu^t-p^evv^t.
\tag{11}
\]

All three forms are permitted because `e>=a_i,a_j`; there is no division by two. This proves the claim at `p=2` as well.

Every rank-one coefficient form pulls back to a decomposable alternating two-form on the elliptic power, and therefore squares to zero. Isogeny pullback is injective on torsion-free integral cohomology, so these are square-zero divisor classes on the quotient. Thus every divisor is an integral sum `D=sum R_i` of actual square-zero divisors, and

\[
 D^{[k]}=D^k/k!=\sum_{|I|=k}\prod_{i\in I}R_i
\tag{12}
\]

is an ordinary divisor product. Therefore

\[
 \operatorname{PD}\langle\operatorname{NS}(A)\rangle^k
 =\operatorname{im}\bigl(\operatorname{Sym}^k\operatorname{NS}(A)\to H^{2k}(A,\mathbf Z_p)\bigr)
\tag{13}
\]

in every degree, for the prescribed local graph NS lattice. Product-image formation commutes with the finite free unramified base change in (3), so faithful flatness descends (12). Applying this at every bad prime, and the literal elliptic-power result elsewhere, gives the corresponding global cohomological statement.

## Boundary

1. Compatibility is load-bearing: finite-etale algebras separately specified at truncated levels are insufficient unless they lift to the common finite-etale action used in (3).
2. Block-respecting is load-bearing: `P` must be scalar on the depth--idempotent blocks. Without it, (6) is still correct but does not reduce to the matrix-of-ideals formula (9).
3. This is a cohomological statement about the specified marked graph presentation. It is not a Chow theorem, an assertion about new geometric divisors after base extension, or a theorem for arbitrary ppavs.
4. No nilpotent/non-etale slope calculation is used or implied.
