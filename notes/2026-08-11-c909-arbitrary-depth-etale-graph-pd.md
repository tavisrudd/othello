# C909 — arbitrary-depth finite-etale graph NS lattice and PD theorem

Date: 2026-08-11

Status: theorem for block-respecting graph data finite-etale at every depth; no manuscript, PDF, mirror, Lean, or commit change

## Verdict

The arbitrary-depth finite-etale application closes positively. No common finite-etale algebra across different depths is needed. After finite unramified splitting separately at each depth, the complete graph Neron--Severi coefficient lattice is a symmetric matrix-of-ideals lattice. For two split blocks `i=(a,lambda)` and `j=(b,mu)`, choose scalar lifts `t_i,t_j` and put `delta_ij=v_p(t_j-t_i)`, with `v_p(0)=infinity`. Their cross coefficients have exact valuation

\[
 e_{ij}=\max\{a,b,a+b-\delta_{ij}\}.
\tag{1}
\]

The last term is irrelevant if the scalar difference is zero. If the split blocks are primitive components of one common finite-etale algebra, this specializes to `max(a,b)` for the same primitive label and `a+b` for different labels. In every case `e_ij>=max(a,b)>=ceil((a+b)/2)`. The DVR rank-one criterion therefore proves that the full NS lattice is generated by actual rank-one square-zero divisors, including unit blocks and at `p=2`. Consequently the cohomological divided-power envelope of the full NS lattice equals its ordinary product image in every degree.

The result requires a block-respecting source decomposition and a finite-etale slope algebra at every depth. It does not discuss non-etale/nilpotent carries.

## 1. Convention and compatible data

Work over `Z_p`. Let the source coefficient polarization be

\[
 G=P B,\qquad P=\bigoplus_a p^aI_{M_a},\qquad B=\bigperp_aB_a,
\tag{2}
\]

where every `B_a` is unimodular. Assume the graph slope `T` preserves each depth summand `M_a` and is self-adjoint in the convention imposed by `B` (equivalently by `B^{-1}` in the dual graph convention). For every positive `a`, assume the algebra generated by `T_a mod p^a` is finite etale over `Z/p^a`; no relation between the finite-etale algebras at distinct depths is assumed.

After one finite unramified extension `O/Z_p` splitting all the finitely many depth algebras, the primitive idempotents at depth `a` lift to exact `T`-stable summands. Indeed, the distinct residual roots give pairwise coprime factors of the characteristic polynomial; Hensel factorization and the usual coprime-primary-projector construction lift the corresponding summands. Thus

\[
 M_O=\bigoplus_{a,\lambda}L_{a,\lambda},\qquad
 P|_{L_{a,\lambda}}=p^a,\qquad
 T_i=t_iI+p^aS_i\quad(i=(a,\lambda)),
\tag{3}
\]

Here `S_i` is integral and `t_i` lifts the scalar root on the indicated truncated-depth component. The self-adjoint idempotents make these summands `B`-orthogonal when that assertion is useful, but no diagonalization of any `B_a` is made or needed. Roots belonging to distinct primitive factors at the *same* depth have distinct residues, whereas across different depths their difference need not be a unit.

The source polarization condition is expressed with `B` (or `B^{-1}` after the dual graph convention); the following divisor calculation uses the ordinary symmetric coefficient `A`, so it is unchanged by that bookkeeping choice and remains valid for a nonorthonormal unit block `B_a`.

## 2. Exact graph coefficient lattice

Use the graph basis

\[
 C=\begin{pmatrix}P^{-1}&P^{-1}T\\0&I\end{pmatrix}.
\tag{5}
\]

For a symmetric source divisor coefficient `A=A^t`, direct multiplication gives

\[
 C\begin{pmatrix}0&A\\-A&0\end{pmatrix}C^t=
 \begin{pmatrix}
 P^{-1}(AT^t-TA)P^{-1}&P^{-1}A\\
 -AP^{-1}&0
 \end{pmatrix}.
\tag{6}
\]

Thus the complete local graph NS coefficient lattice consists exactly of

\[
 A=A^t,\qquad P^{-1}A\in M_g(O),\qquad
 P^{-1}(AT^t-TA)P^{-1}\in M_g(O).
\tag{7}
\]

The transpose of the middle condition is `AP^{-1}` integral, so the two graph cross-pairings are both present without an additional hypothesis.

For `i=(a,lambda)`, `j=(b,mu)`, the middle condition imposes

\[
 A_{ij}\in p^{\max(a,b)}M_{r_i\times r_j}(O).
\tag{8}
\]

Writing `T_i=t_iI+p^aS_i` and `T_j=t_jI+p^bS_j`, the `ij` block of the final condition is

\[
 p^{-a-b}\{(t_j-t_i)A_{ij}+p^bA_{ij}S_j^t-p^aS_iA_{ij}\}
 \in M_{r_i\times r_j}(O).
\tag{9}
\]

The two `S` terms are automatically integral under (8): their valuations are at least `max(a,b)-a` and `max(a,b)-b`, respectively. Thus (9) is equivalent to the scalar condition

\[
 p^{-a-b}(t_j-t_i)A_{ij}\in M_{r_i\times r_j}(O).
\tag{12}
\]

Intersecting (8) and (10) gives precisely the ideal `p^e_ij`, with `e_ij` as in (1). If a choice of lifts changes `delta_ij`, it can only do so when `delta_ij>=min(a,b)`; then `a+b-delta_ij<=max(a,b)`, so `e_ij` is independent of that choice and depends only on the truncated-depth data.

Equivalently, the lattice is the exact symmetric matrix-of-ideals lattice

\[
 \bigoplus_i p^{a_i}\operatorname{Sym}(L_i^*)
 \ \oplus\!\!\bigoplus_{i<j}p^{e_{ij}}
 (L_i^*\otimes L_j^*)_{\rm sym}.
\tag{13}
\]

Depth zero gives the unit-block cases automatically. Under the stronger hypothesis of one common finite-etale algebra, (1) reduces to the two-case formula `max(a,b)`/`a+b` from the earlier version of this note.

## 3. Rank-one and PD consequences

For every pair of blocks, (1) gives

\[
 e_{ij}\geq\max(a_i,a_j)\geq\left\lceil(a_i+a_j)/2\right\rceil.
\tag{10}
\]

The exact DVR criterion in `2026-08-11-c909-dvr-symmetric-ideal-rank-one-pd.md` applies. Hence (11) is generated over `O` by its rank-one forms. Explicitly, for every cross generator use the signed identity

\[
 p^e(uv^t+vu^t)=p^e(u+v)(u+v)^t-p^euu^t-p^evv^t.
\tag{11}
\]

All three forms are permitted because `e>=a_i,a_j`; there is no division by two. This proves the claim at `p=2` as well. The rank-one elements here lie in the actual coefficient lattice after the finite free unramified coefficient extension `NS(X)\otimes_{Z_p}O`, not in a putative lattice of new geometric line bundles.

Every rank-one coefficient form pulls back to a decomposable alternating two-form on the elliptic power, and therefore squares to zero. Isogeny pullback is injective on torsion-free integral cohomology, so these are square-zero divisor classes on the quotient. Thus every divisor is an integral sum `D=sum R_i` of actual square-zero divisors, and

\[
 D^{[k]}=D^k/k!=\sum_{|I|=k}\prod_{i\in I}R_i
\tag{14}
\]

is an ordinary divisor product. Therefore

\[
 \operatorname{PD}\langle\operatorname{NS}(A)\rangle^k
 =\operatorname{im}\bigl(\operatorname{Sym}^k\operatorname{NS}(A)\to H^{2k}(A,\mathbf Z_p)\bigr)
\tag{15}
\]

in every degree, for the prescribed local graph NS lattice. Product-image formation commutes with the finite free unramified coefficient base change in (3), so faithful flatness descends (14). Applying this at every bad prime, and the literal elliptic-power result elsewhere, gives the corresponding global cohomological statement.

## Boundary

1. Block-respecting is load-bearing: `P` must be scalar on the depth--slope blocks. Without it, (6) is still correct but does not reduce to the matrix-of-ideals formula (11).
2. Cross-depth compatibility is not needed for rank-one saturation. Its only effect is the scalar valuation `delta_ij` in (1). A common etale algebra forces the familiar unit/zero alternatives, but separately specified finite-etale depth algebras give the same inequality (12).
3. This is a cohomological statement about the specified marked graph presentation. It is not a Chow theorem, an assertion about new geometric divisors after base extension, or a theorem for arbitrary ppavs.
4. No nilpotent/non-etale slope calculation is used or implied.
