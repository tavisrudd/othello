# C904 truncated Witt-ghost exponent audit

Date: 2026-08-11

Status: bounded claim-specific source check; no manuscript or Lean edits

## Executive verdict

**Yes, after fixing the lattice precisely.**  For the additive necklace
coordinates of the big Witt ring over \(\mathbf Z\), truncated at ordinary
indices \(1,\ldots,h\), the ghost map has presentation

\[
  g_m=\sum_{d\mid m} d b_d \qquad (1\leq m\leq h).
  \tag{1}
\]

Integral Möbius inversion transforms (1) into the diagonal presentation
\(m b_m\).  Consequently

\[
 \operatorname{coker}(g_{\leq h})
   \cong \bigoplus_{m=1}^{h}\mathbf Z/m,
 \qquad
 \exp\operatorname{coker}(g_{\leq h})
   =\operatorname{lcm}(1,\ldots,h),
 \tag{2}
\]

and its \(p\)-primary exponent is exactly

\[
 p^{\max_{m\leq h}v_p(m)}=p^{\lfloor\log_p h\rfloor}.
 \tag{3}
\]

One terminology correction matters: \(\operatorname{diag}(1,\ldots,h)\) is
a diagonal presentation obtained by unimodular row operations, but it is not
usually the Smith normal form because its entries do not form a divisibility
chain.  Formulae (2)--(3), rather than that inaccurate SNF label, are the exact
claims.

The same exponent appears on the **indecomposable** symmetric-function
lattice: Newton's identities give

\[
 p_m\equiv (-1)^{m-1}m e_m \pmod{I^2}
 \tag{4}
\]

for the augmentation ideal \(I\).  It is not licensed to call (3) the
cokernel exponent of the full power-sum subring in the full integral
symmetric-function lattice.  Products \(p_\lambda\) carry additional
partition/factorial integral structure; Hazewinkel explicitly notes that the
power-sum monomials are a basis only over \(\mathbf Q\), not over
\(\mathrm{Symm}_{\mathbf Z}\).

For the C904 regular-primary graph problem, (3) is therefore an exact and
very suggestive **model**, not yet an identification.  It explains the
observed height thresholds \(h=p,p^2,\ldots\) and supplies the uncapped factor
\(p^{\lfloor\log_p h\rfloor}\).  The divided-power comparison supplies the
separate factorial ceiling.  The missing theorem is a filtered integral map
from the carry-enhanced graph Neron--Severi product complex to the necklace
ghost complex which detects the distinguished minimal class.

This audit uses one full-text source.  The two original papers located as the
load-bearing historical sources were not obtained at full text in this
bounded pass; their attribution is therefore recorded through the full-text
Hazewinkel survey and publisher/repository metadata, not asserted from unread
contents.

## 1. Exact lattice calculation

Write a truncated integral Euler product as

\[
 f(t)=\prod_{n=1}^{h}(1-t^n)^{-b_n}\pmod {t^{h+1}},
 \qquad b_n\in\mathbf Z.
\]

Then

\[
 t\frac{d}{dt}\log f(t)
   =\sum_{m=1}^{h}\left(\sum_{d\mid m}d b_d\right)t^m
      \pmod {t^{h+1}},
\]

which is (1).  Let \(U\) be the lower-triangular divisor-incidence matrix
whose Möbius entries compute

\[
 (Ug)_m=\sum_{d\mid m}\mu(m/d)g_d.
\]

The diagonal of \(U\) is one, so \(U\in\operatorname{GL}_h(\mathbf Z)\), and

\[
 (Ug)_m=m b_m.
\]

This proves (2).  Taking the largest \(p\)-power among the integers
\(1,\ldots,h\) proves (3).  Equivalently, the \(p\)-typical chain of ordinary
indices \(1,p,\ldots,p^r\), \(p^r\leq h<p^{r+1}\), has successive equations

\[
 g_{p^j}-g_{p^{j-1}}=p^j b_{p^j},
\]

and hence exponent \(p^r\).

This is precisely the arithmetic encoded by the Dold congruences:

\[
 \sum_{d\mid m}\mu(m/d)g_d\equiv0\pmod m.
 \tag{5}
\]

Thus (3) is not a numerical analogy inside Witt theory; it is the exact
cokernel exponent of its truncated additive ghost lattice.

## 2. Which Witt and symmetric-function statement is safe

Three coordinate systems should not be conflated.

1. **Standard Witt coordinates.**  The big-Witt ghost polynomials are
   nonlinear in the standard coordinates.  There is no literal integer
   matrix whose columns are those coordinate variables.
2. **Necklace/Euler-exponent coordinates over \(\mathbf Z\).**  Addition is
   componentwise in the \(b_n\), and the ghost map is the linear matrix (1).
   This is the lattice for which (2)--(3) hold.
3. **Integral symmetric functions.**  The ghost components of the universal
   complete-symmetric-function series are the power sums.  Newton's identity
   gives (4) after passing to indecomposables \(I/I^2\), so the same diagonal
   generator lattice occurs there.  It does not identify the full lattices
   generated by all \(p_\lambda\) and all \(e_\lambda\).

The paper-facing phrase should therefore be either

> the \(p\)-primary exponent of the length-\(h\) necklace-to-ghost cokernel

or

> the \(p\)-primary exponent of the truncated power-sum-to-elementary
> indecomposable lattice.

It should not say simply "the truncated symmetric-function lattice" without
the word "indecomposable."

## 3. Fit to the regular-primary graph carry

The match with the proposed graph formula is structurally good:

- radical height \(h\) and ordinary Witt truncation length \(h\) have the
  same new layers exactly at \(h=p^r\);
- the exact Witt obstruction at that layer is division by \(p^r\), matching
  the observed order-two and order-four stages;
- the current conjectural geometric exponent
  \(p^{\min\{v_p((g-1)!),\lfloor\log_p h\rfloor\}}\) separates naturally
  into the Witt height exponent (3) and the existing factorial ceiling from
  the comparison between ordinary and divided powers.

But the graph Neron--Severi lattice contains the integral carry

\[
 (DT-TD)/p \pmod p,
\]

and its products live in an exterior cohomology algebra.  Neither the Dold
congruences nor the big-Witt formulas see that carry automatically.  The
following are load-bearing before the Witt calculation becomes a proof.

1. Construct a canonical filtered morphism (or associated-graded
   identification) from the carry-enhanced local graph product complex to a
   truncated necklace/ghost complex.
2. Prove that ordinary divisor products land in the ghost image, not merely
   in a lattice with the same index in the first computed examples.
3. Prove that the minimal divided class maps to a coordinate detecting
   \(m=p^r\).  Dold congruences give an upper bound once the map exists; they
   do not give this nonvanishing/lower bound.
4. Show that higher integral lifts of the slope and the self-adjoint bilinear
   type do not alter the comparison, or add those data to the model.
5. Control interactions among several primary blocks.  The one-chain ghost
   lattice models a regular block, not an arbitrary self-dual primary module.

Accordingly, the strongest safe present formulation is:

> The regular-primary exponent conjecture has the exact numerical form of
> the truncated \(p\)-primary big-Witt ghost cokernel, capped by the
> divided-power factorial.  Proving it amounts to identifying the
> carry-enhanced divisor-product obstruction with a self-adjoint/exterior
> analogue of that ghost cokernel.

## 5. Priority and proof recommendation

The exponent (3) is classical arithmetic and must not be claimed as new.  The
unpreempted-looking step is the geometric comparison theorem.  The cleanest
proof target is:

> **Regular-primary ghost comparison.**  The \(p\)-primary saturation defect
> complex of a cyclic self-adjoint graph gluing of radical height \(h\) admits
> a filtered comparison with the length-\(h\) necklace ghost complex; the
> minimal divided class maps to the maximal Dold coordinate, and the only
> additional annihilator is the degree factorial.

If the comparison is an isomorphism on the relevant cyclic summand, formulas
(2)--(5) make the exponent calculation essentially formal.  If it fails, the
failure is likely mathematically useful: it isolates exactly which symplectic
carry or exterior-product datum lies beyond ordinary Witt theory.
