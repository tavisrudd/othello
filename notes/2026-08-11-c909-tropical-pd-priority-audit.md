## Exact comparison

Yu's valuation coordinates `x_ij` satisfy

\[
                       x_{ii}+x_{jj}\le2x_{ij}.
\]

With `x_ii=a_i` and `x_ij=e_ij`, this is exactly C909's midpoint condition.
Her result is over the ordered real Puiseux field and concerns tropicalization,
tropical convex hull, and tropical matrix factorization. It does **not** state:

1. that a prescribed integral lattice
   `A_ii in p^a_i O, A_ij in p^e_ij O` is generated over `O` by the rank-one
   matrices it contains;
2. the exact arithmetic hull
   `e'_ij=max(e_ij,ceil((a_i+a_j)/2))` or its DVR cokernel;
3. the dyadic order-two divided-square obstruction;
4. the graph-basis Neron--Severi valuation formula
   `max(a,b,a+b-v_p(t_j-t_i))`; or
5. faithful-flat descent to ordinary integral divisor-product lattices on an
   abelian variety.

Thus the tropical theorem materially compresses and contextualizes C909, but
does not pre-empt its arithmetic or geometric statements.
