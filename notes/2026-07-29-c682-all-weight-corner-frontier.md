## Eventual peak families

Let \(m_\rho(n)\) be the multiplicity of the binary-icosahedral irreducible
\(\rho\) in \(\operatorname{Sym}^n\).  For \(n\ge60\), the strict local
peaks
\[
 m_\rho(n)\ge2,\qquad
 m_\rho(n)>\max\{m_\rho(n-6),m_\rho(n+6)\}
\]
are periodic with period \(60\).  Their residues are
\[
\boxed{
\begin{aligned}
n\bmod20&\in\{0,12\} &&(\rho=1),\\
n\bmod20&\in\{1,11\} &&(\rho=2),\\
n\bmod20&\in\{2,10\} &&(\rho=3),\\
n\bmod20&=6 &&(\rho=3').
\end{aligned}}
\]
Equivalently, the twenty-one residues modulo \(60\) are
\[
\begin{gathered}
0,1,2,6,10,11,12,\quad
20,21,22,26,30,31,32,\\
40,41,42,46,50,51,52.
\end{gathered}
\]
No \(4,4',5,6,\) or \(2'\) block has an eventual strict peak.

This follows from the classical Kostant numerators over
\(\mathbf Q[F_{12},h_{20}]\):
\[
\begin{array}{c|l}
\rho&\text{free-generator degrees}\\ \hline
1&0,30\\
2&1,11,19,29\\
3&2,10,12,18,20,28\\
3'&6,10,14,16,20,24.
\end{array}
\]
Writing the coefficient function for
\((1-z^{12})^{-1}(1-z^{20})^{-1}\) as the number of solutions to
\(3a+5b=k\), its eventual \(15\)-step recurrence makes each multiplicity
difference \(m_\rho(n)-m_\rho(n\pm6)\) \(60\)-periodic.  Checking one
period after the positivity threshold gives the displayed list.

Thus every infinite strict-peak family is controlled by one of four free
modules of ranks \(2,4,6,6\).

## What remains for an all-weight theorem

Periodicity of the multiplicity profile does not make the return matrices
periodic.  Along a fixed family \(n=n_0+60q\), their entries are
polynomial/rational functions of the semigroup parameter \(q\).  The base
case \(q=0\) cannot prove that the mixing minors remain nonzero for every
\(q\ge0\).

The next proof gate has two parts:

1. construct the finite Weyl matrices for the \(1,2,3,3'\) Kostant modules;
2. exhibit and factor a mixing minor for each of the twenty-one residue
   classes modulo \(60\),
   proving it has no nonnegative integral root—or identify its first root.

The \(3\)-module matrix is already available from the intrinsic-symbol
bundle.  Natural free generators for the remaining modules are:

- \(1\): \(1,t\);
- \(2\): the tautological linear covariant and its first transvectants with
  \(F,h,t\), in degrees \(1,11,19,29\);
- \(3'\): the degree-six transvectant-kernel copy and its transvectants with
  \(F,h,t\), in degrees \(6,10,14,16,20,24\).

This is the finite symbolic problem that now owns the all-weight gate.

There is a second logical boundary: the current search targets strict
multiplicity peaks, the observed location of every short-return deficit.
An all-weight theorem must either prove that off-peak corners are forced to
saturate or audit their corresponding minors too.  No such implication is
assumed here.
