# Trust boundary for the q=13 passant-incidence code

The library defines the binary code whose coordinates are the internal points and whose parity
checks are the passant lines of the standard nonsingular conic over the field with thirteen
elements.  It proves the normalized coordinate geometry, rank-to-dimension transport, the
weight-eight tangent-graph reduction, and the symbolic weight-ten pencil reductions.

The exhaustive rank and minimum-word statements are not in this boundary.  They belong to the
separate q=13 certificate package, whose locally checked finite model is compared with this
geometry by a compatibility bridge.

The declared terminals are kernel proofs and the trust spine permits no user axiom in their
closure.  Classical choice, quotient soundness, and propositional extensionality are the standard
Lean axioms reported by the terminal audit.
