\begin{abstract}
Let \(C\) be a linear \([6,3,4]_q\) MDS code and let
\(\lvert\Psi_C\rangle=q^{-3/2}\sum_{c\in C}\lvert c\rangle\) be its
equal-phase Calderbank--Shor--Steane (CSS) state.  We prove that every product-unitary intertwiner
between two such states is local Clifford, for every prime power \(q\).
The proof shortens the stabilizer to four parties.  Its nonidentity
correlation tensor is diagonal on all \(q^2-1\) local Weyl axes, and the
rank-one contraction locus recovers those axes intrinsically.  We apply
this rigidity to an odd-prime-field non-Reed--Solomon pencil of
six-point arcs.  Projective, monomial-code, local-Clifford, and
local-unitary equivalence are all classified there by one degree-eight
quotient \(z\).  We also determine the split-torus versus
\(\mathrm{SL}_2(q)\) logical-Clifford phase.  The same distinction between
operator and scalar data governs the remaining results: fixed-copy scalar
contractions are generically constant, but exact marginal and four-copy
witnesses still detect special strata.  A four-sheet transport operator
explains one such rank-jump divisor, \((z-2)(9z-4)\).
\end{abstract}
