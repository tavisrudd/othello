\begin{abstract}
Let \(C\) be a linear \([2m,m,m+1]_q\) MDS code and let
\(\lvert\Psi_C\rangle=q^{-m/2}\sum_{c\in C}\lvert c\rangle\) be its
equal-phase Calderbank--Shor--Steane (CSS) state.  For every prime power
\(q\) and \(m\geq2\), we prove that every product-unitary intertwiner
between two such states is local Clifford.  Consequently, the associated
\([[2m-1,1,m]]_q\) quantum MDS code admits no transversal non-Clifford
logical unitary.  The proof shortens both \(C\) and \(C^\perp\) to
\(m+1\) parties.  The nonidentity correlation tensor is then diagonal
on all \(q^2-1\) local Weyl axes, and its rank-one contraction locus
recovers those axes intrinsically.

For \(m=3\), we apply this rigidity to an odd-prime-field
non-Reed--Solomon pencil of six-point arcs.  Projective, monomial-code,
local-Clifford, and local-unitary equivalence are classified by one
degree-eight quotient \(z\).  Viewing any party as the input of a
\([[5,1,3]]_q\) encoder, we also classify the fixed-party logical
Clifford symmetries: the symplectic logical group is
\(\mathrm{SL}_2(q)\) exactly for conic/generalized Reed--Solomon arcs
and is the split torus otherwise.  Fixed-copy scalar contractions are
generically constant, while exact marginal and four-copy witnesses
detect special strata.
\end{abstract}
