\section{Verification and trust boundary}\label{sec:verification}

The principal results are conceptual proofs.  This paper contains no
paper-facing finite census, generated certificate, or numerical experiment.
Its trust boundary has three layers: complete manuscript proofs, selected
kernel-checked algebraic cores, and standard results imported by citation.

The formal source namespace is
\texttt{RelativeConicArcs.AMELU}.  In the checked Lean toolchain
\texttt{v4.32.0-rc1}, the relevant exact cores lie in the support-profile,
tensor-rigidity, holonomy-centralizer, stabilizer-dictionary,
encoder-transversal, and multipartite modules.  Their current aggregate
audit reports only \texttt{propext}, \texttt{Classical.choice}, and
\texttt{Quot.sound} for these declarations.  The aggregate belongs to the
pre-split formal companion: a paper-specific semantic gate and recursive
formal-root contract have not yet been created.  Accordingly, this paper
does not describe the whole theorem package as end-to-end formalized.

The cleaning theorem uses three cited exact inputs: stabilizer cleaning,
localization of nested commutators, and the exact three-cleanable-region
Clifford obstruction.  The manuscript proves the leakage estimate, the
finite Weyl--Fourier concentration step, the stabilizer overlap gap, and
their AME composition.  Equations \((5.3)\)--\((5.11)\) are closed-form
deductions; they do not depend on a computational search or floating-point
evaluation.

The appendices serve as mechanism comparisons.  Their single-marginal and
aggregate thresholds are weaker than the cleaning radius, and none is used
to prove Theorem~\ref{thm:quantitative-rounding}.  The partial-Weyl
recognition criterion is likewise independent of the stabilizer-AME
support squeeze used in the headline theorem.
