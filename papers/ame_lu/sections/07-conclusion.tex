\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

An \((m+1)\)-party marginal of a stabilizer
\(\AME(2m,q)\) state retains the full configuration of local Weyl axes.
Rank-one contractions recover those axes intrinsically, so every
product-unitary intertwiner is Clifford factor by factor.  The
minimum-support atlas is the structural completion of this rigidity
mechanism: it records how the recovered frames fit together and classifies
the residual Clifford equivalence exactly.

The applications expose two consequences of that structure.  For the
equal-phase linear MDS--CSS states over odd prime fields considered here,
diagonal isoduality is exactly the boundary between the full projective
logical \(\F_q^2\rtimes\mathrm{SL}_2(q)\) group and the affine split-torus
subgroup.  On the six-party pencil over odd prime fields, the degree-eight
projective quotient \(z\) is also the complete LU coordinate.  Fixed-copy
scalar contractions, by contrast, discard the recovered axes and are
generically constant on irreducible code families.

Over extension fields, the atlas classification remains exact with local
blocks in \(\mathrm{Sp}_{2e}(\F_p)\), but the two representation-theoretic
bridges from additive Clifford maps to Frobenius sectors and back are not
proved here.  Determining when an extension-field atlas admits a semilinear
canonical form is the remaining exact classification problem.
