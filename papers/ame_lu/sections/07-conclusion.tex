\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

An \((m+1)\)-party marginal of a stabilizer
\(\AME(2m,q)\) state retains the full configuration of local Weyl axes.
That covariant information rigidifies every product-unitary intertwiner,
even though fixed-copy scalar contractions discard the axes and are
generically constant on irreducible code families.  For MDS--CSS states,
the same minimum-support data makes the residual Clifford problem
computable: diagonal isoduality is exactly the boundary between the full
projective logical \(\F_q^2\rtimes\mathrm{SL}_2(q)\) group and the affine
split-torus subgroup.

The six-party pencil shows how this quantum rigidity returns a classical
moduli coordinate.  Over odd prime fields, its degree-eight projective
quotient \(z\) is also the complete LU coordinate.  Over extension fields,
the atlas classification remains exact with local blocks in
\(\mathrm{Sp}_{2e}(\F_p)\), but the two representation-theoretic bridges
from additive Clifford maps to Frobenius sectors and back are not proved
here.  Determining when an extension-field atlas admits a semilinear
canonical form is the remaining exact classification problem.
