\section{Four-party Weyl tensors and local-unitary rigidity}

This section will prove the headline theorem.  MDS shortening on four
parties gives a \(q^2\)-element stabilizer subgroup whose nonidentity
correlation tensor is diagonal on the full local Weyl basis.  The
rank-one contraction axes of a diagonal tensor of order at least three are
intrinsic.  Product-unitary equivalence therefore forces every local
adjoint action to permute Weyl axes and hence to be Clifford.  Two
four-party marginals covering all six parties suffice.
