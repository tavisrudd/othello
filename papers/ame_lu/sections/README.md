# Section map

`main.tex` is the build driver.  The section files follow the mathematical
dependency order:

1. object, question, and adopted theorem package;
2. six-arcs, MDS kernels, CSS states, and AME tensors;
3. full- and partial-Weyl marginals and uniform LU-intertwiner rigidity;
4. minimum-support atlases and encoder consequences;
5. discreteness and quantitative stability;
6. exact MDS--CSS logical images and their scalar lifts;
7. the scalar `z` and complete LU/LC classification of the pencil;
8. the six-party logical-Clifford phase and operational consequences;
9. marginal and copy-contraction local-unitary invariants;
10. proof, computation, literature, and formalization boundaries; and
11. conclusions and the extension-field boundary.

The all-MDS/CSS intertwiner theorem precedes the pencil classification:
the former is characteristic-uniform, while the latter uses the odd-prime
admitted parameter domain.
