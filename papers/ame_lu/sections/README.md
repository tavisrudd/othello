# Section map

`main.tex` is the build driver. The main text follows the reader-facing proof
hierarchy:

1. the operational LU-versus-LC question, exact theorem, quantitative theorem,
   prior-work boundary, and roadmap;
2. additive Weyl and stabilizer-AME support conventions;
3. exact local-unitary rigidity from matched Weyl-basis marginals;
4. classification by minimum-support transition maps and the encoder
   consequence;
5. cleaning-based quantitative rounding, robust transition compatibility,
   and the stabilizer-character obstruction; and
6. conclusions and open quantitative questions.

The appendices contain material that is not needed for a first reading:

- partial-Weyl recognition beyond AME states;
- alternative two- and `k`-uniform stability mechanisms, weaker
  single-marginal and aggregate rounding routes, and the two-unitary-gate
  translation; and
- the verification and formalization boundary.

The exact MDS--CSS group classification and six-point finite-geometry
applications belong to the separate `mds_css_transversal_groups` paper.
