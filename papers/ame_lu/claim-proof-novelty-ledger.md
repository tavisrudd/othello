## Novelty boundary

The paper may say that it proves the all-prime-power, all-`m`,
arbitrary-additive stabilizer-AME intertwiner theorem and the stated
quantitative consequences. It does not say “first.” The exact qualitative
three-cleanable-region Clifford obstruction is prior work; only the
leakage-aware estimate, explicit Weyl--Fourier rounding, AME composition,
robust transition compatibility, and stabilizer-character boundary are presented as
the paper's quantitative contribution.

All MDS--CSS exact-group, diagonal-isoduality, pencil, Clebsch, scalar
separator, transport, and party-extension claims are omitted here and owned by
Paper II.
