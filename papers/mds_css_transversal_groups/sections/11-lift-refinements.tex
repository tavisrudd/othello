\section{Physical realizations and lift refinements}
\label{sec:lift-refinements}

This appendix refines the projective classification of
Section~\ref{sec:exact-logical}.  None of its results is needed to identify
the exact projective logical group.

\subsection{Uniform local frames}

Assume that \(S=\diag(s_1,\ldots,s_{2m})\) satisfies
\(SC=C^\perp\).  Fix an input coordinate \(j\), put \(t_i=s_i/s_j\), and
write
\[
 A=\begin{pmatrix}a&b\\c&d\end{pmatrix}\in\mathrm{SL}_2(q).
\]
The site-dependent realization from
Proposition~\ref{prop:site-dependent-realization} is
\[
 \phi_i(A)=
 \begin{pmatrix}a&t_i^{-1}b\\t_i c&d\end{pmatrix}
 =
 \begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&t_i\end{pmatrix}
 A
 \begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&t_i^{-1}\end{pmatrix}.
\]
Thus every \(\phi_i\) is a homomorphism, \(\phi_j\) is the identity,
and the elementary matrices in \eqref{eq:duality-shears} satisfy all
\(\mathrm{SL}_2(q)\) relations coordinatewise.  The calculation applies at
the extended evaluation point and uses only diagonal equivalence
\(SC=C^\perp\), not the evaluation formula for a GRS code.

The following refinement is not needed for the transversal-group
classification; it answers whether the site-dependent actions can be made
identical by changing the local Clifford frame at each coordinate.

\begin{proposition}[Uniform local frames]
\label{prop:uniform-local-frames}
The realization in Proposition~\ref{prop:site-dependent-realization} can be
made uniform by fixed local Clifford frame changes if and only if every ratio
\(t_i=s_i/s_j\) is a square.  Equivalently, the obstruction is the collection
of relative determinant square classes in
\[
 \mathrm{PGL}_2(q)/\mathrm{PSL}_2(q)
 \cong\F_q^\times/(\F_q^\times)^2.
\]
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Conjugation by \(\diag(1,t_i)\) is inner on \(\mathrm{SL}_2(q)\) exactly
when \(t_i\) is a square.  Indeed, the centralizer of
\(\mathrm{SL}_2(q)\) in \(\mathrm{GL}_2(q)\) is scalar, and a scalar
multiple of this diagonal matrix has determinant one exactly in the square
case.  Since \(t_j=1\), a nonsquare \(t_i\) prevents uniformization by fixed
local Clifford frame changes: the \(i\)-th factor represents the diagonal
outer automorphism.  Equivalently, the obstruction is the relative class of
\(t_i\) in
\[
 \mathrm{PGL}_2(q)/\mathrm{PSL}_2(q)
 \cong\F_q^\times/(\F_q^\times)^2.
\]
Here \(\mathrm{PGL}_2(q)=\mathrm{GL}_2(q)/\F_q^\times\), and
\(\mathrm{PSL}_2(q)\) is the image of \(\mathrm{SL}_2(q)\) in that quotient.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Coherent lifts and the affine obstruction}

The projective classification records the action on Pauli labels.  We next
ask whether unitary representatives can be phased coherently so that they
respect multiplication exactly.

For odd prime \(q\), and more generally for every odd prime power, the finite
Stone--von Neumann representation of the Heisenberg group \(H_q\) extends to
a genuine representation of \(\mathrm{SL}_2(q)\ltimes H_q\)
\cite[Theorem~1]{Gerardin1976}.\footnote{The exceptional uniqueness clause at
\(q=3\) in G\'erardin's theorem does not affect existence.}  Apply its Weil
factor to each \(\phi_i(A)\).  The resulting tensor product is
multiplicative in \(A\).  In the symmetric Weyl convention
\(D_{a,b}=\chi(ab/2)X(a)Z(b)\), a CSS label
\((c,h)\in C\oplus C^\perp\) has tensor lift
\[
 \bigotimes_iD_{c_i,h_i}
 =\chi(c\mathbin{\cdot}h/2)X(c)Z(h)=X(c)Z(h),
\]
because \(c\mathbin{\cdot}h=0\).  Exact Egorov covariance therefore
preserves the CSS stabilizer group with its trivial character, and hence the
state ray, rather than only its projective label space.  Consequently the
one-qudit Clifford scalar extension restricted to the linear
\(\mathrm{SL}_2(q)\) factor splits coherently with the propagated tensor
symmetries.

The affine extension does not split.  Choose \(a,b\in\F_q\) with
\(\chi(ab)\ne1\), and put \(X=X(a)\), \(Z=Z(b)\).  Their projective classes
commute, whereas every choice of unitary representatives satisfies
\[
 ZX=\chi(ab)XZ,\qquad \chi(ab)\ne1 .
\]
Scalar changes of \(X\) and \(Z\) do not change their commutator.  Hence the
translation subgroup \(\F_q^2\) has no homomorphic lift to the one-qudit
unitary Clifford group.  The coherent lift is therefore
\(H_q\rtimes\mathrm{SL}_2(q)\), not a section of
\(\F_q^2\rtimes\mathrm{SL}_2(q)\) through the scalar quotient.  This is the
Heisenberg central extension, not a residual metaplectic or Schur-multiplier
obstruction on \(\mathrm{SL}_2(q)\).

\begin{remark}[Coordinate permutations]
Neither lift question decides what happens when physical coordinates may be
permuted.  That is a separate extension of the fixed-coordinate projective
group by the realized coordinate-permutation group.  Appendix
\ref{sec:party-extensions} states its splitting criterion, proves a uniform
icosahedral family, and points to supplementary finite examples.
\end{remark}
