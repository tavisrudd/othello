\section{Verification and scope}\label{sec:verification}

The all-length multiplier-line theorem and the exact transversal-group
dichotomy are proved in the text.  They use no finite census.  The imported
stabilizer-AME rigidity and phase-correction results are proved in the
companion paper \cite{RuddAMEStabilizerRigidity2026}; they are not counted as
independent results of this paper.

The six-point applications have a separate exact-arithmetic evidence layer.
The paper-local supplement contains the generators, compact JSON
certificates, and a deterministic standard-library verifier for the pencil
quotient, holonomy reconstruction, Clebsch and \(H_3\) marginal calculations,
the four-copy separator at \(q=13\), transport divisor, and twelve
party-extension rows.  The command
\begin{center}
\texttt{python3 supplement/verify.py --replay}
\end{center}
checks the manifest hashes and regenerates every certificate in memory.  The
supplement records each finite domain, termination condition, independent
replay, and negative scope statement.  These computations support the
displayed finite applications; they are not premises of
Theorem~\ref{cor:diagonal-isodual-transversal-group}.

The formal companion is written in Lean~4 with toolchain \texttt{v4.32.0-rc1}
and a pinned Mathlib revision, in the shared source namespace
\texttt{RelativeConicArcs.AMELU}.  It has two semantic roots: an import-only
gate whose transitive closure is this paper's checked surface, and an audit
module that imports only that gate and prints the axiom dependencies of every
declaration cited here.
\begin{center}
gate: \texttt{RelativeConicArcs.Gates.MDSCSSTransversalGeometry}\\
audit: \texttt{RelativeConicArcs.Gates.MDSCSSTransversalGeometryAxioms}
\end{center}
A content-addressed record of that closure, covering its modules, imports,
terminal names, axiom facts, exporter digest, and toolchain identity,
accompanies the Lean source, and the supplement's crosswalk names the
declarations behind each group of statements below.

Checking by kernel reduction, with no appeal to compiled evaluation, covers the
MDS--CSS and stabilizer dictionaries, the diagonal-multiplier line and nullity
test, the pencil quotient and the extension-field scalar algebra, syndrome
geometry, the contraction and transport cycle algebra, and the abstract
consequences of a supplied party-extension complement.  The exact carrier, the
admitted-pencil classification, the marginal and four-copy separators, the
fixed-party logical-phase kernel, and the transport determinant, rank, and
orbit-geometry bridges are stated as interfaces.  Each derives its conclusion
from a structure whose fields assert the geometric propagation,
orbit-recognition, or finite-certificate facts that the text or the supplement
establishes, so importing the gate proves none of those inputs.  Three
six-party graph cardinalities are discharged by exhaustive native evaluation,
which exposes declaration-local implementation axioms of the pinned toolchain;
the rank-four multiplicity identity and the marginal-moment separator are
proved from the star count and inherit its evaluation axiom.  Every other cited
declaration depends on no more than propositional extensionality, choice, and
quotient soundness.  The odd-field Weil lift, the Heisenberg nonsplitting
argument, the complete translation fiber, and the twelve concrete complements
retain the manuscript or certificate boundaries stated above.

The local-unitary classification by \(z\) is restricted to odd prime fields.
Over extension fields, Frobenius is already an additional local Clifford and
the full additive-Clifford orbit problem is not claimed.  The exact group
theorem is fixed-party and transversal: it does not classify party-moving
enlargements outside the twelve certificate-checked rows, non-product physical
implementations, nonstabilizer AME states, or unrelated quantum-code families.
