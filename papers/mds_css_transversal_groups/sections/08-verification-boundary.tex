\section{Verification and scope}\label{sec:verification}

The all-length multiplier-line theorem and the exact transversal-group
dichotomy are proved in the text.  They use no finite census.  The imported
stabilizer-AME rigidity and phase-correction results are proved in the
companion paper \cite{RuddAMEStabilizerRigidity2026}; they are not counted as
independent results of this paper.

The six-point applications have a separate exact-arithmetic evidence layer.
The paper-local supplement contains the generators, compact JSON
certificates, and a deterministic standard-library verifier for the pencil
quotient, holonomy reconstruction, Clebsch and \(H_3\) marginal calculations,
the dimension-thirteen four-copy separator, transport divisor, and twelve
party-extension rows.  The command
\begin{center}
\texttt{python3 supplement/verify.py --replay}
\end{center}
checks the manifest hashes and regenerates every certificate in memory.  The
supplement records each finite domain, termination condition, independent
replay, and negative scope statement.  These computations support the
displayed finite applications; they are not premises of
Theorem~\ref{cor:diagonal-isodual-transversal-group}.

The formal companion is written in Lean~4 with toolchain \texttt{v4.32.0-rc1}
and a pinned Mathlib revision, in the shared source namespace
\texttt{RelativeConicArcs.AMELU}.  This paper's checked surface is exactly the
transitive closure of the import-only gate
\texttt{RelativeConicArcs.Gates.MDSCSSTransversalGeometry}; the companion
module \texttt{RelativeConicArcs.Gates.MDSCSSTransversalGeometryAxioms} imports
only that gate and prints the axiom dependencies of every declaration cited
here.  A content-addressed record of the closure, covering source hashes,
imports, terminal names, axiom facts, and toolchain identity, accompanies the
Lean source; the supplement names the exact declaration behind each statement.

Kernel checking covers the MDS--CSS and stabilizer dictionaries, the
diagonal-multiplier line and nullity test, the pencil quotient and the
extension-field scalar algebra, syndrome geometry, the contraction and
transport algebra, and the abstract consequences of a supplied party-extension
complement.  The exact carrier, the admitted-pencil classification, the
marginal and four-copy separators, and the transport rank and orbit bridges are
stated as interfaces: each derives its conclusion from a structure whose fields
assert the geometric propagation, orbit-recognition, or finite-certificate
facts that the text or the supplement establishes, so importing the gate proves
none of those inputs.  Three six-party graph cardinalities are discharged by
exhaustive native evaluation and expose declaration-local implementation axioms
of the pinned toolchain; the rank-four multiplicity identity and the
marginal-moment separator are proved from the star count and inherit its
evaluation axiom.  Every other cited declaration depends on no more than
propositional extensionality, choice, and quotient soundness.  The odd-field Weil lift, the Heisenberg
nonsplitting argument, the complete translation fiber, and the twelve concrete
complements retain the manuscript or certificate boundaries stated above.

The local-unitary classification by \(z\) is restricted to odd prime fields.
Over extension fields, Frobenius is already an additional local Clifford and
the full additive-Clifford orbit problem is not claimed.  The exact group
theorem is fixed-party and transversal: it does not classify party-moving
enlargements outside the twelve certificate-checked rows, non-product physical
implementations, nonstabilizer AME states, or unrelated quantum-code families.
