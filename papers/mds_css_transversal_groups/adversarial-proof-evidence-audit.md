1. The headline group theorem is not presented as a consequence of the finite
   six-point census. Its all-length proof is conceptual.

   not duplicated theorem ownership.
3. Odd-prime fixed-party scope is visible in the theorem and abstract;
   extension-field full Clifford groups and arbitrary party motion are excluded.
4. Every retained finite claim remains connected to the copied canonical
   evidence bundle and its deterministic replay.

all internal equation renumbering, figure comprehension, and the exact formal
