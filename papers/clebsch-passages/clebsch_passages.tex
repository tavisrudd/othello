\begin{abstract}
We determine the rational square class of Hitchin's degree-two incidence
cover of harmonic cubics.  Its function field is obtained by adjoining
\(\sqrt{5J_0}\).  We define the Clebsch chart over
\(\Q(\sqrt5)\), where \(J_0=16\sigma_3^2\), and prove that the two
configurations over the rational cubic \(xyz\) form the complete reduced
fibre with residue algebra \(\Q(\sqrt5)\).  The projective orthogonal
transformation exchanging them has nonsquare spinor class after reduction
modulo \(11\).  Independently, the degree-six
zonal harmonics on the ten icosahedral face axes contain a
Clebsch four-space as their Petersen eigenspace, and their normalized
spherical cubic restricts exactly as
\[
 \frac1{4\pi}\int_{S^2}F_y^3
 =-\frac{784000}{1247103}\sigma_3(y).
\]
The two realizations use isomorphic Clebsch modules; no rational or integral
identification between their displayed embeddings is asserted.
\end{abstract}

\noindent\textbf{Keywords.} Clebsch configuration; Hitchin cover;
icosahedral invariant; arithmetic double cover; spherical harmonics;
Gaunt invariant.
