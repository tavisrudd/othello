\begin{abstract}
Several finite constructions attached to the Clebsch matching geometry
retain different parts of the same orientation data.  We organize their
survival and loss through carrier torsors and a four-sheet cover with
explicit cycle holonomy.  The resulting comparison links finite
passages with theta, Fourier, quantum, Mathieu, and
characteristic-zero realizations.  The first purpose of this draft is
to determine whether these comparisons form one theorem complex with a
standalone mathematical question.
\end{abstract}

\noindent\textbf{Keywords.} Clebsch configuration; finite cover;
holonomy; torsor; Fourier transform; Mathieu group.
