\begin{abstract}
We determine the rational square class of Hitchin's degree-two incidence
cover of harmonic cubics.  Its function field is obtained by adjoining
\(\sqrt{5J_0}\), and on the Clebsch four-space
\(J_0=16\sigma_3^2\); the nonbranch restriction is therefore the constant
golden torsor \(\Spec\Q(\sqrt5)\).  The two configurations over the cubic
\(xyz\) are conjugate over \(\Q(\sqrt5)\), and their exchanger has
nonsquare spinor class after reduction modulo \(11\).  Independently, the
degree-six zonal harmonics on the ten icosahedral face axes contain the
Clebsch four-space as their Petersen eigenspace, and their normalized
spherical cubic restricts exactly as
\[
 \frac1{4\pi}\int_{S^2}F_y^3
 =-\frac{784000}{1247103}\sigma_3(y).
\]
Both results are expressed by the cubic \(\sigma_3\) on the same
four-space; no integral comparison between the two constructions is
asserted.
\end{abstract}

\noindent\textbf{Keywords.} Clebsch configuration; Hitchin cover;
icosahedral invariant; arithmetic double cover; spherical harmonics;
Gaunt invariant.
