From deep holes, the cubic takes shape, finds its bearings, and \textbf{stands

\end{center}

\begin{abstract}
We determine the rational twist in Hitchin's degree-two incidence cover of
harmonic cubics: its function field is
\(\Q(\mathbf P(H))(\sqrt{5J_0})\).  A complete golden fibre over \([xyz]\)
determines the factor \(5\), while the Clebsch chart satisfies
\(J_0=16\sigma_3^2\).  After fixing the marked chart and compatible labels,
we attach the resulting sign to the order-six conference operator \(C\),
with \(C^2=5I\).

We prove that its oriented cubic has four equivalent descriptions: triangle
holonomy, the diagonal of \(*\!\bigwedge^3C\), a commutator Pfaffian, and a
cross-golden determinant.  Its six outer translates are exactly the signed
Joubert coordinates on the Segre cubic; centered determinants give the
Segre--Igusa polar map, and a coordinate section gives the diagonal Clebsch
cubic surface.  The carrier is structurally exceptional: order six is the
unique nontrivial symmetric conference order with cut-independent balanced
exchange spectrum.  At higher orders the exchange trace remains universal;
the classical aligned \(3\)-design fixes the mean and variance of the second
exchange moment and, when marked, reconstructs the signing up to switching
and global negation.

Finally, degree-six zonal harmonics on the ten icosahedral face axes contain
the Clebsch four-space as their Petersen eigenspace, and their normalized
spherical cubic restricts to
\[
 \frac1{4\pi}\int_{S^2}F_y^3
 =-\frac{784000}{1247103}\sigma_3(y).
\]
All sign comparisons are relative to the stated marked data; no canonical
labeling or identification of the two ambient harmonic representations is
claimed.
\end{abstract}

\noindent\textbf{Keywords.} Clebsch configuration; Hitchin cover;
icosahedral invariant; arithmetic double cover; spherical harmonics;
Gaunt invariant; symmetric conference matrix.
