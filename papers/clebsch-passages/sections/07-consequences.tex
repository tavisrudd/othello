\section{The common cubic line}
\label{sec:consequences}

The two legs of Theorem~\ref{thm:main} meet on
\(\langle\sigma_3\rangle\), but their normalizations do not.  Over
\(\F_{11}\), the signed matching tensor restricts to \(4\sigma_3\).  Over
\(\Q\), the degree-six Gaunt cubic restricts to
\(-784000/1247103\,\sigma_3\), whose denominator is divisible by \(11\).
Thus the cross-characteristic statement is equality of the integral
invariant line, not reduction of a rational scalar.

The same distinction separates the rational cover from its explicit
special fibre.  The equation \(w^2=5J_0\) fixes the generic square class,
and its restriction to the Clebsch chart is the constant golden torsor.
The displayed fibre and exchanger reduce at \(11\), but no global integral
incidence model at that prime is asserted.
