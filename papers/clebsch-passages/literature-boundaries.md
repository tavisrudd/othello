headline literature sweep, the 2026-08-02 aligned-faithfulness follow-up, and
the 2026-08-20 recognition-theorem audit.
It licenses no unqualified claim of priority.  Those bounded searches read the
cited primary sources and screened Crossref, OpenAlex, Semantic Scholar, and

## Promotion rule

A novelty-sensitive sentence may enter the manuscript only if it preserves
the row's prior-art boundary, avoids an unqualified priority claim, and points
to a search record with read depths and access gaps. Exact mathematical
statements do not need novelty adjectives.
