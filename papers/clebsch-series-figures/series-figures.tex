\noindent
This document previews the reusable figures of
\texttt{papers/clebsch-series-figures}.  It is a proof sheet, not a
manuscript: each figure is defined in its own \texttt{\string\input}-able
file and is meant to be placed by a host paper with its own caption.  See
\texttt{README.md} for the input recipe and for the list of edges that
encode unproved claims.

\clearpage
\section*{D1 --- the series map}

The five-paper map, with Paper III on top descending the golden sign into
the central torsor, Papers I and II joined to it by horizontal recognition
passages, and Papers IV and V below.  Two conventions carry meaning.  The
Paper IV edge is \emph{dashed}: no theorem carries its reconstructed plane
to the oriented cubic torsor, so the bridge is an open obligation rather
than an established arrow.  The Paper V node states an \emph{intent}: that
paper is unwritten and the Rosetta theorem is not proved.

\bigskip

\section*{D3 --- the quantifier ladder}

Each bar is a theorem's quantifier range.  Where the theorem's answer is a
single exceptional object, it is marked as a distinguished point at the end
of the general range.  The visual argument is general hypothesis,
exceptional conclusion.

\begin{figure}[H]
\centering
