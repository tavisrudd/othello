\section{Conclusion and open problems}\label{sec:boundary}

Under the hypotheses of Theorem~\ref{thm:main}, the syndrome--Hankel
dictionary, recursive carrier theorem, and simultaneous contraction determine
the two highest coset-weight shells for the stated point-deleted GRS/EGRS
family.  They give exact shell counts and the corresponding MDS/NMDS
one-column extensions and family-aggregate enumerators.  Above the
threshold, the trapped-row-space proposition confines high-weight syndromes
to the persistent rank-two locus in every characteristic except the two
binary cases \(r\in\{6,7\}\), which the fixed-level theorems settle for full
support; the
remaining arbitrary-level question is the arithmetic of the Lucas carriers
below the threshold.
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2em]
\item Redundancy five is complete for every $q\geq7$.
\item Redundancy six is a complete deep-hole classification over every
      prime power \(q\geq7\).  Redundancy seven has a complete deep-hole
      classification over the same range; its radius is six except at
      $q=8$, where its radius is seven and
      exact extraction gives ten deep directions in the diagonal tangent and
      central-nucleus orbits.
\item The exact R8--R10 calculations are not included here and are not used
      in the preceding theorems.
\item At arbitrary redundancy, the reduced carrier and its degree-one modular
      union are exact, and above the threshold only the persistent locus and
      the two binary carriers at \(r\in\{6,7\}\) trap a syndrome.
      Split-squarefree arithmetic on Lucas carriers below the threshold
      remains open outside the evaluated levels and the finite fields recorded
      in the supplement.
\end{enumerate}

In particular, the paper does not settle the general Reed--Solomon
deep-hole conjecture.  It proves the reduced carrier and split-free
containment under the displayed field-size bound, and the separate
coding-theoretic radius input promotes that containment to the exact
high-weight-coset classification in every characteristic, except for
\(r\in\{6,7\}\) in characteristic two, where the full-support case is
classified and the point-deleted arithmetic of the carrier is open.  Whether the
threshold \(Q^*_{r,s}\) is sharp is open: the finite closures at redundancy
eleven over \(\F_{16}\), \(\F_{27}\), \(\F_{32}\), and \(\F_{49}\) lie below
it and are certificate-closed only.

The last sporadic fields at redundancies five, six, and seven are
\(19,13,11\), while the corresponding first prime-power field-order gates
are \(23,29,37\).  These opposite trends ask whether sufficiently high
redundancy has any sporadic fields outside the persistent and modular
loci.
