# Redundancy-nine slice data

All coefficients in this file are in `F_7`.  For a vector
`[c_0,...,c_d]`, write

`[c_0,...,c_d](L) = c_0+c_1 L+...+c_d L^d`.

The data are generated by Certificate R9 and independently checked by its
replay.  They supply the finite polynomial identity used in the printed
component proof; they are not a carrier census.

## Squarefree quartic normal form

Every geometric squarefree binary quartic is projectively equivalent, up to
scale, to

`h_L=[1,0,L,0,1]`,

away from the usual singular values of `L`.  For the six ordered four-root
bases below, let `Delta_i(L)` be the discriminant in the moving root of the
residual branch quartic.

| `i` | Fixed roots | Coefficients of `Delta_i`, low first |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | `[0,1,2,3]` | `[6,0,6,0,3,4,5,5,0,0,2,6,1,3,3,0,0,4,5,2,6,0,0,1,1]` |
| 2 | `[0,1,2,4]` | `[0,0,4,5,1,5,2,3,1,6,1,5,4,6,0,3,0,0,4,1,2,0,6,0,4]` |
| 3 | `[0,1,2,5]` | `[2,6,2,1,1,3,0,1,6,5,0,2,3,4,4,4,5,4,0,4,6,3,1,5,5]` |
| 4 | `[0,1,2,6]` | `[0,0,0,0,0,0,3,0,0,6,5,0,0,1,0,0,2,4,0,0,3,0,0,6,5]` |
| 5 | `[0,1,3,4]` | `[6,1,3,4,6,5,4,6,6,4,0,0,2,6,1,0,1,2,5,4,1,5,4,3,5]` |
| 6 | `[1,2,3,4]` | `[6,5,5,6,0,4,0,5,6,3,6,6,0,3,4,1,4,2,3]` |

The following coefficient vectors define `b_i(L)`:

| `i` | Coefficients of `b_i`, low first |
|---:|---|
| 1 | `[6,4,1,2,3,5,5,1,6,5,0,0,4,5,3,6,2,1,1,4,3,1,2,6,4,2,6,4,6,3,0,5,5,2,6]` |
| 2 | `[1,0,5,1,1,5,4,2,6,5,0,4,6,4,4,4,1,3,0,1,3,3,5,1,6,4,3,6,5,0,2,4,4,5,2]` |
| 3 | `[]` |
| 4 | `[]` |
| 5 | `[]` |
| 6 | `[0,3,5,4,0,1]` |

Direct multiplication in `F_7[L]` gives the Bezout identity

`sum_(i=1)^6 b_i(L) Delta_i(L) = 1`.

Consequently no geometric value of `L` makes all six slices
nonintegral.
In fact `b_3=b_4=b_5=0`, so bases 1, 2, and 6 already prove
exhaustion; the other three slices remain useful independent regression
controls.

## Multiple-root normal forms

The remaining geometric root partitions have the following normal forms and
reduced branch data.  Branch vectors are polynomials in the moving root,
again low first.

| Partition | Quartic vector | Fixed roots | Reduced branch vector | Reduced discriminant |
|---|---|---|---|---:|
| `4` | `[1,0,0,0,0]` | `[1,2,3,4]` | `[2,2,1]` | 3 |
| `3+1` | `[0,1,0,0,0]` | `[0,2,4,6]` | `[4,4,3]` | 3 |
| `2+2` | `[0,0,1,0,0]` | `[0,1,2,3]` | `[5,5,2,1]` | 1 |
| `2+1+1` | `[0,1,6,0,0]` | `[0,1,2,3]` | `[3,5,6,6,6]` | 5 |

Every reduced discriminant is nonzero.  The squarefree and multiple-root
tables therefore exhaust all nonzero geometric binary quartics and prove
that each has a geometrically integral residual slice.

## Rational base-selection polynomial

Fix a carrier quartic `h` over a characteristic-seven field and ordered base
roots `r=(r_1,...,r_4)`.  Put

`R_r(t)=product_i(t-r_i)`, `P_r,x(t)=R_r(t)(t-x)`,

and form `D_h,r(x)`, `N_s,h,r(x)`, `N_u,h,r(x)` from the two printed Hankel
rows.  Set

`K_h,r(x)=N_s,h,r(x)^2-4 N_u,h,r(x) D_h,r(x)`.

Over the rational function field in the `r_i`, remove
`gcd(K_h,r, partial_x K_h,r)` and clear denominators from the discriminant
of the resulting squarefree part.  Its primitive numerator is the
base-selection polynomial `B_h(r)`.  Equivalently, `B_h` is the first
nonzero principal subresultant coefficient that gives the discriminant of
the squarefree part.  The tables above prove geometrically that `B_h` is not
the zero polynomial for every nonzero `h`; projective transport preserves
this nonvanishing.

Each coefficient of `P_r,x` is affine in `x`, has degree at most one in
each `r_i`, and total `r`-degree at most four.  Hence `D,N_s,N_u` have
degree at most two in each `r_i` and total degree at most eight.
The quartic `K` has degree at most four in each `r_i` and total degree at
most sixteen.  The quartic discriminant, or the corresponding lower
principal subresultant on a multiple-root stratum, has degree at most six
in the coefficients.  Therefore

`deg_(r_i) B_h <= 24`, `deg_total B_h <= 96`.

Multiplying by the four-root Vandermonde adds total degree six.  The product
is nonzero and has total degree at most 102.  For `q>102`,
Schwartz--Zippel leaves an `F_q`-rational ordered base with distinct roots
and `B_h(r) != 0`.  The first characteristic-seven field in this range is
`q=343`.
