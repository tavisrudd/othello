## Acceptance rule

Each verdict must be either `green` or a correction list tied to exact
manuscript locations.  Every correction must be closed in a new immutable
candidate, after which both readers confirm the replacement PDF hash.  An
author or an AI system used in producing the manuscript does not count as an
independent reader.
