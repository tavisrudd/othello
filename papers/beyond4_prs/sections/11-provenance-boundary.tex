\section{Scope and open problems}\label{sec:boundary}

The first levels and the contained/transverse fork point to one stable
picture.  The bottom-cover point count is uniform and linear:
Theorem~\ref{thm:uniform-transverse} gives its exact threshold.  The
remaining question is geometric containment.

\begin{conjecture}[Stable-component classification]
\label{conj:stable-polar}
The assertion \(\mathrm{SC}(j)\) holds at every redundancy \(j\geq6\):
the recursively pointed contained bad scheme has no component outside
\(\mathcal P_j\cup\mathcal M_j\).
\end{conjecture}

This is not the conclusion of Theorem~\ref{thm:uniform-transverse}:
the conjecture is a scheme-theoretic component assertion with no
field-size conclusion; the theorem supplies the arithmetic threshold.

\begin{corollary}[Large-characteristic stable list]
\label{cor:large-characteristic-stable}
Assume \(\mathrm{SC}(j)\) for \(6\leq j\leq r\), let
\(\operatorname{char}\F_q>r-1\), and let \(q\geq Q_r\).  Then the
split-free directions are exactly the persistent tangent and
conjugate-secant families.  If the covering radius is \(r-1\), this is
also the projective deep-hole list.  In particular, the conclusion
applies over every prime field with \(q\geq Q_r\).
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
Theorem~\ref{thm:uniform-transverse} leaves
\(\mathcal P_r\cup\mathcal M_r\), and Lucas' theorem makes
\(\mathcal M_r\) empty.  The persistent families are split-free.
\end{proof}

Proposition~\ref{prop:r6-contained} and
Corollary~\ref{cor:r7-contained} prove the first two assertions
unconditionally.  The characteristic hypothesis removes the modular
term, not the large-characteristic R5 cyclic carrier; the theorem
eliminates that carrier on the transverse branch.

The present results establish the first levels of this picture but do
not constitute an arbitrary-redundancy classification.
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2em]
\item Redundancy five is complete for every $q\geq7$.
\item Redundancy six is a complete all-field deep-hole classification.
      Redundancy seven has a complete all-field split-free
      classification; its $q=7,8,9$ covering-radius gate remains
      separate.
\item At higher redundancy the remaining input is
      \(\mathrm{SC}(j)\).  The bottom monodromy, point count,
      collision alternative, and transverse degree budget are uniform.
\end{enumerate}

Thus Theorem~\ref{thm:uniform-transverse} is conditional rather than an
arbitrary-redundancy classification: exclusion of additional
higher-level contained components remains open.  In particular, the
paper does not settle the general Reed--Solomon deep-hole conjecture.

The last sporadic fields at redundancies five, six, and seven are
\(19,13,11\), while the corresponding point-count gates are
\(23,29,37\).  These opposite trends ask whether sufficiently high
redundancy has any sporadic fields outside the persistent and modular
loci.

The structural conclusion is nevertheless stable.  Beyond redundancy
four, deepness is no longer controlled by one invariant or by isolated
bad fibers.  The persistent geometry is a coherent rank-two polar flag;
modular geometry appears through characteristic-specific contraction
kernels; and the remaining arithmetic is the Frobenius action on a
normalized ordered-root cover.  Redundancy five is the first
exceptional-cover case, while redundancies six and seven show how that
cover is propagated or contained.
