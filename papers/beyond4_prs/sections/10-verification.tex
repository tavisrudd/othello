The public labels are defined by
\texttt{supplement/CERTIFICATE-SCHEMA.md}.  The paper-local bundle,
literal replay commands, toolchain locks, hashes, byte counts, and
working directories are recorded in
\texttt{supplement/REPRODUCING.md},
\texttt{supplement/EVIDENCE-MANIFEST.json}, and
\texttt{supplement/RELEASE-MANIFEST.md}.  The single entry point
\texttt{python3 supplement/verify.py} checks the bundle manifest,
classification records and their expected hashes, and the local PDF
and supplement-artifact rows printed in the release manifest; its
\texttt{--replay} option runs the paper-local Python replays.

At redundancy eight, Lean formalizes three projective marker
contractions (including infinity and adjacent swaps), the
geometric-$S_3$ slice interface, deletion and polar budgets $30$ and
$14$, the exact integer-$42$/prime-power-$43$ threshold passage, the
conditional synthesis implication, persistent cardinality, the four
orbit-count pairs, the tangent cocycle, and the bounded
characteristic-seven carrier statement.  It also formalizes the
redundancy-nine residual identities, witness-to-shallow synthesis
implication, and four-marker integer budgets.  It does not formalize
geometric integrality, Hasse--Weil existence after deletion, the
$\PGL_2/\PGammaL_2$ action, identification with PRS syndrome families,
or geometric exhaustion; those remain printed mathematical inputs.
The exact declarations, import gates, source anchors, and axiom-audit
output are listed in
\texttt{supplement/LEAN-STATEMENTS.md}.
