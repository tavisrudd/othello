follow the three rows of Table~\ref{tab:dependency-map}; the intervening
definitions and lemmas discharge the cited carrier, collision, and
point-count steps.  Table~\ref{tab:classification-status} separately
records where a split-free classification needs a covering-radius
input before it becomes a deep-hole classification.
