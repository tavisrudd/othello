We classify deep holes of full-length projective Reed--Solomon codes beyond
redundancy four, beginning with redundancy five, the first previously open
case. At redundancies five and six, the classifications are complete for every
prime power \(q\geq7\). At redundancy seven, we classify all split-free syndrome
directions for \(q\geq7\) and all deep holes for \(q\geq11\); at \(q=8\), exact
distances leave precisely ten deep directions in two orbits. At redundancies
eight, nine, and ten, exact deep-hole classifications hold for
\(q\geq43,53,59\), respectively.

The key mechanism is coherent polar contraction, which propagates
split-squarefree witnesses detected in a two-row Hankel kernel. For every
\(r\geq6\), it unconditionally identifies the reduced recursively contained
locus as the catalecticant rank-two scheme together with one maximal
adjacent-zero Lucas carrier. Under explicit intermediate-stage hypotheses and
\(q\geq6r-15+\lfloor2\sqrt{6r-17}\rfloor\), every split-free syndrome lies in
this union; when \(\operatorname{char}\mathbb F_q>r-1\) and the covering radius
equals \(r-1\), only the tangent and conjugate-secant deep-hole families remain.
At redundancy ten, the degree-nine Lucas carrier is shallow over every
\(\mathbb F_{2^m}\), \(m\geq4\). Public software and replayable certificates record
the trust boundaries.
