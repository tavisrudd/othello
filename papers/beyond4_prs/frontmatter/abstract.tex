We classify deep holes of full-length projective Reed--Solomon codes beyond
redundancy four. At redundancies five and six, the classifications are complete
for every prime power \(q\geq7\). At redundancy seven, we classify all split-free
syndrome directions for \(q\geq7\) and all deep holes for \(q\geq11\); at
\(q=8\), exact distances leave precisely ten deep directions in two orbits. At
redundancies eight, nine, and ten, exact deep-hole classifications hold for
\(q\geq43,53,59\), respectively.

Beyond these fixed levels, we prove unconditionally that, for every redundancy
\(r\geq6\), the reduced recursively contained locus consists exactly of the
catalecticant rank-two scheme and one maximal adjacent-zero Lucas carrier.
Under explicit stagewise hypotheses, this gives an arbitrary-redundancy
classification in the stated field range and leaves only the tangent and
conjugate-secant families in high characteristic wherever the covering-radius
theorem applies. At redundancy ten, we also prove that the degree-nine Lucas
carrier is shallow over every \(\mathbb F_{2^m}\), \(m\geq4\). Public software and
replayable certificates support the classifications while recording their
trust boundaries.
