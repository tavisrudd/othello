# Rank-four subtori in the full type-I3 Cox model

The certificate supports the proposition on rank-four subtori and descent.
It concerns the marked projective Cox representation and the full type-I3
Galois image. It does not depend on the companion paper's quantum argument.

## Inputs and conventions

The two integral character matrices and the Picard root basis are those
already transcribed in `derive_slice_cover.py`. That input file is frozen by
SHA-256 in the reconstruction program and in `rank-four-SHA256SUMS`.
The reconstruction reads only its literal matrices, without executing its
top-level certificate pipeline. Cox classes are the five exceptional
classes, ten line classes, and one conic class in the marked blowup of P2.
Weights use a lift with the final Picard coordinate zero; another lift
changes them by a common translation.

Matrices act on character columns. A rank-four subtorus corresponds to a
saturated rank-one kernel of the restriction map on characters. Each group
generator acts on that kernel by +1 or -1. The four simultaneous sign
systems therefore exhaust all candidates. Their ranks are 5,4,5,5, in the
order (-1,-1), (-1,+1), (+1,-1), (+1,+1). The unique line is generated by
the primitive vector m=(-1,-1,-3,-3,3).

The restriction lattice is Z5/Zm. Since the first coefficient of m is -1,
the last four standard basis classes give an integral quotient basis.
The sixteen restricted weights are distinct. Their affine Galois actions
have a four-element orbit E3,E4,L34,Q and a twelve-element orbit.

## Exhaustion and independent check

All binomial(16,5)=4368 subsets are considered once, in lexicographic order.
For each, subtract its first weight from the other four. The absolute
determinant in the quotient basis is its lattice index when nonzero;
zero denotes affine dependence. Exactly 1992 determinants have absolute
value one. A five-subset descends only if it is stable under both generator
permutations. None is stable. No symmetry reduction, random choice, or
early termination is used.

The independent checker does not import the generator or SymPy. It uses
rational Gaussian elimination for the four sign systems, reconstructs the
Cox weights, and checks determinants by the rank-five Leibniz formula
det[m,w1-w0,w2-w0,w3-w0,w4-w0]. It verifies the full affine actions before
their quotient actions and checks the four rank-three weight spaces of
dimension three. Negative controls reject 2m as a primitive generator,
detect the resulting doubled lattice index, and show that omitting the
affine translations fails to preserve the weight set.

## Replay

From the paper root:

```sh
uv run --with sympy==1.14.0 python3 verification/derive_rank_four.py
uv run python3 verification/check_rank_four.py
sha256sum -c verification/rank-four-SHA256SUMS
```

Both programs are deterministic, require assertions enabled, and leave the
tracked files unchanged. The first also compares the generated TeX values.
To regenerate the JSON and TeX, add `--write` to the first command, then
refresh the checksum manifest after reviewing the changed evidence. The
normal `make check` gate runs both programs and validates the hashes.

The trust boundary is exact program execution and the transcription of the
source matrices, followed by the geometric argument in the manuscript.
There is no Lean formalization of this proposition. Unimodularity alone
does not prove rationality: the descent hypothesis fails in this model.
