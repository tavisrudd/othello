\begin{abstract}
The Gamma integral structure turns the class of a point into a flat covector
which reads ordinary rank.  We ask when its vanishing on a formal-monodromy
packet is a birational invariant, and we reduce the question to one marked
continuation problem at each threshold of a single equivariant
cobordism.  Granting that continuation---a gauged-admissible marked
W{\l}odarczyk completion together with the marked threshold compatibility of
Hypotheses~\ref{hyp:marked-threshold-wall}
and~\ref{hyp:marked-threshold-zero}, at every finite Artin
truncation---\(X\times\mathbf P^m\) is irrational for every smooth complex cubic
threefold \(X\) and every \(m\geq0\).  Only birational maps with these
endpoints are ever assumed.

The endpoint contrast is unconditional, and the transport mechanism is proved
under the gauged-admissibility conditions above.  We
reconstruct the primitive-sixth block of Cai's cubic connection, derive the
central charge of the point class from the small \(I\)-function, and read its
coefficient on both primitive-sixth lines from a Barnes expansion, whereas
projective space has no such packet.  In one equivariant cobordism, the orbit
cylinder of a common-open point supplies a class restricting to both endpoint
point classes, rotation localization kills every intermediate fixed stratum,
and a rank-one derived clutching theorem identifies consecutive degrees along
each remaining tail, together with their obstruction theories and virtual
classes, making every tail holonomic and tempered.

What is not proved is the comparison across thresholds: at each one, an
isomorphism of the two adjacent cyclic Rees \(z\)-modules which intertwines
formal monodromy and carries the marked row, taken on reduced nearby cycles at
a zero mode.  Given those maps the transport is linear algebra.  A marked
Gamma/window continuation conjecture, new to this paper, would imply them and
is the route by which we expect them to be removed.  Countermodels show that
formal monodromy, pairing, integrality, tailwise holonomicity, and localized
Fourier support do not replace them.
\end{abstract}

\noindent\textbf{Keywords.} Gamma class; quantum cohomology; birational wall
