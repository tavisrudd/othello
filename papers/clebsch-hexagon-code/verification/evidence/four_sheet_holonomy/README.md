# Four-sheet cover-holonomy evidence

This bundle supplies the exact finite evidence for the paper's four-sheet
linear-sheaf theorem.

- `cover_holonomy.py` constructs the reduced \(9\)-by-\(9\) transport operator,
  expands its determinant both by a Leibniz cycle ledger and by
  fraction-free elimination, classifies the relative octahedral-frame cells,
  and compares all 720 section and transport matrices in the displayed
  finite fields.
- `contraction_rank_drop.py` independently constructs the original
  \(24\)-by-\(21\) quotient matrix, proves the two-component rank-drop
  divisor by exact polynomial elimination and maximal-minor witnesses, and
  replays its finite-field specializations.
- The adjacent JSON files are canonical certificates regenerated by the two
  checkers.

Run `python3 verify.py`.  It first checks every tracked file against
`manifest.sha256`, then regenerates and compares both certificates.  The
bundle proves the displayed cycle identities, divisor, multiplicities,
relative-frame groups, and finite section-to-transport bridge.  Its trusted
boundary is exact integer and rational polynomial arithmetic, deterministic
finite-field Gaussian elimination, and exact permutation-group closure.
The manuscript separately proves the elementary sheaf reduction and
cycle-to-holonomy interpretation.

The calculation is confined to the admitted odd-characteristic non-GRS
pencil and the fixed four-sheet contraction.  It does not recover the whole
pencil, prove four-copy minimality, classify arbitrary local unitaries, or
identify the full octahedral group with the automorphism group of the bare
bipartite multigraph.
