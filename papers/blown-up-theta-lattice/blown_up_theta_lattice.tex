\begin{abstract}
Let \(\Theta\) be the theta divisor of the intermediate Jacobian of a
smooth complex cubic threefold \(X\), and let
\(M=\operatorname{Bl}_0\Theta\).  We compute the integral middle lattice of
\(M\), equivalently \(IH^3(\Theta,\Z)\).  It is free of rank \(130\), and
its simultaneous restriction to \(X\) and Gysin image in \(J\) has
nontrivial mod-two glue.  If \(L(\alpha)=\theta\wedge\alpha\), then
\[
 b_*H^3(M,\Z)=
 \Sat\bigl(L\textstyle\bigwedge^3H^1(J,\Z)\bigr)
 =L\textstyle\bigwedge^3H^1(J,\Z)
  +\theta^{[2]}\wedge H^1(J,\Z),
\]
and the saturation quotient is \((\Z/2)^{10}\).  The Fano difference map
identifies the geometric glue and its dual escape lattice.  We also
determine the integral direct image of the resolution.  Two outer point
objects split over \(\Z\), while the central perverse factor has
costalk-to-stalk map \(\Z\xrightarrow{3}\Z\).  Thus the rational central
point summand does not split integrally, and in characteristic three it is
replaced by an indecomposable nonsemisimple perverse sheaf.  This gives one
resolution with distinct global mod-two and local mod-three defects.
\end{abstract}

\noindent\textbf{Keywords.} Cubic threefold; intermediate Jacobian; theta
divisor; intersection cohomology; symplectic lattice; Fano surface.
