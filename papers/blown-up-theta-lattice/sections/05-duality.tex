\section{The dual escape lattice}\label{sec:duality}

We prove Theorem~\ref{thm:main-escape}.  Poincare duality identifies
\(H^5(M,\Z)/\operatorname{tors}\) unimodularly with
\(\operatorname{Hom}(H^3(M,\Z),\Z)\).  Under this identification,
\(b_*:H^5(M,\Z)/\operatorname{tors}\to H^7(J,\Z)\) is restriction of
functionals along \(b^*:\bigwedge^3\Lambda\to H^3(M,\Z)\), followed by
the wedge duality \((\bigwedge^3\Lambda)^\vee\cong\bigwedge^7\Lambda\).
Since \(b^*\bigwedge^3\Lambda\) is a direct summand,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:degree-five-push}
 b_*:H^5(M,\Z)/\operatorname{tors}\longrightarrow\bigwedge^7\Lambda
\end{equation}
is surjective with primitive rank-ten kernel.

The exceptional Gysin map corresponds to
\[
 \beta\longmapsto\left(u\longmapsto
 \langle\beta,e^*u\rangle_X\right).
\]
Surjectivity of \(e^*\) and unimodularity of the middle pairing on \(X\)
identify its image with the kernel of \eqref{eq:degree-five-push}.

It remains to quotient by pullbacks from \(J\).  For
\(w\in H^5(J,\Z)=\bigwedge^5\Lambda\), the functional defined by \(b^*w\)
is
\[
 u\longmapsto\langle w,b_*u\rangle_J.
\]
The lattice \(S=b_*H^3(M,\Z)\) is saturated in
\(\bigwedge^5\Lambda\), so unimodularity of the middle wedge pairing shows
that \(\bigwedge^5\Lambda\to S^\vee\) is surjective.  Therefore
\begin{equation}\label{eq:escape-dual}
 E_M\cong\operatorname{Hom}(\ker(b_*:H^3(M,\Z)\to S),\Z).
\end{equation}

In the fibre-product coordinates \eqref{eq:main-fibre-product},
\[
 \ker b_*=\{(0,\gamma):y(\gamma)\equiv0\pmod2\}.
\]
Since \(y\) is unimodular, restriction identifies this kernel with
\(2H^3(X,\Z)\).  Thus \eqref{eq:escape-dual} is free of rank ten.  An
exceptional Gysin functional pairs against \(2H^3(X,\Z)\), so its image in
\eqref{eq:escape-dual} is exactly twice the unimodular dual lattice.  This
is \(2E_M\), proving Theorem~\ref{thm:main-escape}.

\begin{remark}\label{rem:two-torsion-location}
The group \((\Z/2)^{10}\) occurs twice for dual reasons.  In degree three
it is the saturation quotient of the image in \(J\).  In degree five it is
the quotient of a free rank-ten lattice by the exceptional sublattice.
The escape lattice itself is not torsion.
\end{remark}
