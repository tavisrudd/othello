\begin{abstract}
We characterize the exceptional non-GRS $[6,3,4]_{11}$ MDS code by the
geometry of its maximum-distance syndromes.  Among six-arcs of
$\PG(2,11)$, the projective deep-hole syndrome locus lies on a conic
exactly for the Clebsch class.  The syndrome conic gives an exact
decoder, recovers the automorphism group, and determines an invariant
bipartition of the ambiguous leader supports.  We also give the sharp
nearest-conic gap and classify the full-conic extension-locus examples
for arcs of size $4$ through $7$.
\end{abstract}

\noindent\textbf{Keywords.} Clebsch hexagon; MDS code; deep hole; finite
projective plane; arc; conic; decoding.
