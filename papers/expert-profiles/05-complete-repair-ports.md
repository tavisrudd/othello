## Paper spine and venue fit

The paper has six coupled components:

1. the complete bounded repair port and its support, coefficient, and probability layers;
2. exact weighted-functional transfer and its pointed confinement boundary;
3. prescribed positive-density realization in asymptotically good fixed-alphabet families;
4. reliability and radius-truncated EXIT;
5. the full-port pointed-Tutte/perspective structure; and
6. cubic versus quartic-nucleus/harmonic flagships.

The natural first audience is **Designs, Codes and Cryptography** or **Advances in Mathematics of
Communications**. **Finite Fields and Their Applications** becomes plausible if the finite-field
geometry and trace-duality story is foregrounded. **IEEE Transactions on Information Theory** is a
reach unless the operational consequence of complete-port equality is made more prominent.

The editorial risk is audience bifurcation: a storage-code referee may accept the repair invariant
but want a sharper coding consequence, while a finite geometer may admire the exact seed but regard
the concatenation theorem as elementary. The paper must make the *conjunction* unavoidable.

## Named candidates

### 1. Frédérique Oggier

- **Current position / fit:** the University of Birmingham's
  [current research portal](https://research.birmingham.ac.uk/en/persons/frederique-oggier/)
  lists her as Honorary Associate Professor. An older
  [NTU page](https://www3.ntu.edu.sg/home/Frederique/index.html) remains online, so verify primary
  affiliation immediately before any formal suggestion. She co-introduced the exact minimum-
  hitting-set notion used here as local repair tolerance and works on coding for distributed
  storage.
- **Role:** **Priority-sensitive reader / expert commentator**; plausible specialist referee, but
  an editor should not use her as the only novelty referee: her paper is the closest definitional
  predecessor.
- **Questions she is likely to press (inference):** Is `tau` genuinely different from local repair
  tolerance, or only renamed? Why is the *complete* repair family operationally necessary? Does the
  transfer theorem preserve repair bandwidth, access, or coefficient data in addition to supports?
- **What would excite her (inference):** a decoder/storage interpretation where `tau-nu` measures a
  demonstrable robustness gain; an exact theorem showing that the entire failure-tolerance profile,
  not just one number, survives concatenation.

### 2. Eitan Yaakobi

- **Current position / fit:** Professor, Technion Faculty of Computer Science; the
  [faculty directory](https://cs.technion.ac.il/people/faculty/index.php?index=names) lists information
  and coding theory for storage and memories, and his [ISM laboratory](https://ism.cswp.cs.technion.ac.il/)
  focuses on storage systems. His work with Kurz is close to the dual-distance/recovery-set boundary.
- **Role:** **Likely referee fit** for the coding claim and operational meaning; less ideal as the
  sole referee for the twisted-cubic geometry.
- **Questions:** Are the two transfer gates merely sufficient, or necessary in a useful class? What
  does complete-port equality buy beyond exact locality and availability? Can the result be stated
  as a compositional theorem for repair schemes rather than one construction?
- **Excitement:** a sharp and reusable support-level concatenation theorem with nondegenerate boundary
  examples; a concrete storage failure model where the mixed coordinate types outperform an
  availability-only design.

### 3. Alberto Ravagnani

- **Current position / fit:** Associate Professor in Mathematical Communication Theory, Eindhoven
  University of Technology; [TU/e profile](https://research.tue.nl/en/persons/alberto-ravagnani/).
  His work centers on algebraic/combinatorial coding invariants, and his recent joint work on refined
  support-indexed weight distributions is the closest located adjacent refined-support treatment.
- **Role:** **Priority-sensitive reader / likely referee fit** for the support-invariant and duality claims. Close-prior-art
  caution: the editor should disclose that the manuscript distinguishes itself from his recent work,
  and should seek a second independent novelty opinion.
- **Questions:** Is the repair hypergraph recoverable from an established refined weight enumerator?
  Is literal support equality the right categorical invariant? Which theorem fails if multiplicities
  of dual words are retained?
- **Excitement:** a clean transform or duality connecting complete repair ports with refined weight
  distributions; a MacWilliams-type or matroid-port formulation making the transfer theorem visibly
  reusable.

## Questions this community leaves for the paper spine

- Classify the minimum blocking sets and maximum disjoint repair families, not only `tau` and `nu`.
- Determine whether complete bounded repair hypergraphs admit a MacWilliams/refined-enumerator
  transform.
- Transfer coefficient-bearing repair equations, bandwidth, or access cost—not just supports.
- Replace the single twisted-cubic--axis seed by a geometric criterion producing many exact ports.
- Optimize the outer AG family under the functional-dual constraint and plot the resulting achievable
  region against established LRC bounds.
