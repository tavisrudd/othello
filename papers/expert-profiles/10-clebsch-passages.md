## Proof room

The paper connects the rational square class of Hitchin's incidence cover and its golden fibre to
an exact degree-six icosahedral Gaunt/Steinhardt cubic. The best lead is **Nigel Hitchin**. The
compact team is:

- **Hitchin + Igor Dolgachev / Laurent Manivel** — incidence cover, Clebsch and icosahedral
  invariant geometry;
- **Brian Conrad or Asher Auel** — descent, square classes, specialization, integral models;
- **Paul Steinhardt / David Nelson** — degree-six bond-order harmonics and physical normalization.

## Hard-proof routing

- Incidence-cover square class and golden fibre: Hitchin plus Conrad/Auel.
- Clebsch/icosahedral invariant identification: Dolgachev–Manivel.
- Mod-\(11\) specialization: Conrad/Auel; never promote fibre specialization to good reduction of
  the full comparison.
- Gaunt/Steinhardt tensor and normalization: Steinhardt/Nelson plus Manivel.

reader. Hitchin and other source owners are priority-sensitive, so pair them with an independent
