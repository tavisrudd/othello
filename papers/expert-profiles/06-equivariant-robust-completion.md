## Paper spine and venue fit

The paper counts fresh quadratic-Frobenius conjugate pairs extending an invariant arc. Its spine is
the exact empty-Baer-line carrier count plus the visibility/collision correction, culminating in a
uniform two-pair theorem for invariant eight-arcs in `PG(2,25)`, all-order extension for base order
`s >= 5`, and robust deletion-and-repair bounds. The square-root scale is explicitly classical; the
candidate contribution is the orbit-valued exact assembly and its robust-exchange consequence.

Best fit: **Finite Fields and Their Applications**, **Designs, Codes and Cryptography**, or
**Journal of Geometry**. A referee will judge it primarily as finite geometry, with the formal
verification strengthening confidence but not significance.

## Named candidates

### 1. Simeon Ball

- **Current position / fit:** Universitat Politècnica de Catalunya, Department of Mathematics;
  [current page](https://web.mat.upc.edu/simeon.michael.ball/) (updated December 2025). His stated
  interests include finite geometry, codes, arcs, and linear-algebraic methods; he coauthored the
  survey used for the classical arc-completion boundary.
- **Role:** top-tier **likely referee fit** for significance and proof architecture.
- **Questions he is likely to press (inference):** Can the pair-extension bound be proved by a
  polynomial/Segre-tangent method? Is the `s=5` certificate concealing a conceptual theorem? Is the
  `sqrt(2q)` scale genuinely informative under the weaker saturation hypothesis?
- **What would excite him (inference):** a certificate-free proof at `s=5`; a sharp all-`k,s`
  phase diagram or an extremal family meeting the orbit-saturation bound.

### 2. Michel Lavrauw

- **Current position / fit:** Professor, University of Primorska;
  [home page](https://mlavrauw.github.io/) and [research profile](https://osebje.famnit.upr.si/~michel.lavrauw/research.html).
  His interests include field reduction, Galois geometry, arcs, MDS codes, and finite-field methods.
- **Role:** excellent **likely referee fit**, especially for the Baer/Galois-geometric formulation and
  FFTA/DCC venue fit.
- **Questions:** Is the fixed-subplane/carrier construction best expressed by field reduction or
  linear sets? What survives for higher-degree Galois orbits? Does the collision correction have a
  geometric classification in terms of linear sets or secant pencils?
- **Excitement:** a degree-`d` orbit-extension theorem with a clean field-reduction interpretation;
  an inverse theorem classifying equality or small excess geometrically.

### 3. Leo Storme

- **Current position / fit:** tenured member of the Ghent University incidence-geometry group;
  [group page](https://geometry.ugent.be/) and [UGent research profile](https://biblio.ugent.be/person/801000632072).
  His indexed topics include arcs, Baer subplanes, blocking sets, finite geometry, and linear codes.
- **Role:** strong **likely referee fit** for arc-completion context and historical priority.
- **Questions:** How does pair-saturation compare quantitatively with ordinary completeness? Are the
  `PG(2,25)` invariant eight-arcs already classifiable by known arc methods? Can line-counting or
  blocking-set bounds remove the exceptional computation?
- **Excitement:** a structural classification of the extremal/near-extremal profiles and a theorem
  that feeds back into ordinary complete-arc bounds.

## Conflict and close-prior-art cautions

- Baker--Wantz, Dye, and Blokhuis--Seress--Wilbrink are intellectually central predecessors, but
  current professional availability was not verified for this slate. Treat their papers as
  citation/priority anchors; verify any living author's present status before considering a
  commentator or referee role.
- Ball and Lavrauw have coauthored directly relevant finite-geometry work. Do not automatically use
  both as independent readers; screen recent coauthorship and ordinary institutional ties for every
  proposed pair. This document makes no assertion about a journal-defined formal conflict.
- A computational-arc specialist should not be the only significance referee, and a broad finite
  geometer should not be the only certificate referee.

## Questions this community leaves for the paper spine

- Remove the `PG(2,25)` exceptional certificate by a conceptual proof.
- Classify equality and low-excess charge profiles as geometric configurations, not just arithmetic
  identities.
- **Follow-on:** identify the exchange graph on invariant arcs under delete-one-orbit/add-another-
  orbit moves; this belongs to the alternate-repair extension, not the focused submission spine.
- Extend quadratic conjugate pairs to higher-degree Galois orbits while controlling internal
  dependence of the candidate orbit.
- Find sharp examples for the parameterized repair bound and orbit-saturation inequality.
