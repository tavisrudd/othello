# Exchange landscapes, orientation, and rigidity in the Golden six-mode conference interferometer

[![DOI](https://img.shields.io/badge/DOI-10.5281%2Fzenodo.21766747-blue.svg)](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21766747)

[Read the paper (PDF).](golden_quantum_statistics.pdf)

The paper studies the six-mode interferometer defined by a real symmetric
conference matrix \(C\) with \(C^2=5I\).  A diagonal control couples its two
three-dimensional golden eigenspaces.  Singular values classify the unframed
transfer; oriented ports add the determinant sign as the unique minimal-degree
relative-orientation invariant, while calibrated frames expose
matrix-dependent amplitudes such as the permanent.

Order six is the unique nontrivial symmetric conference order with
cut-independent balanced exchange spectrum.  Its balanced Boolean controls
jointly maximize every degree-three Schur sector.  Hermitian conference phase
moves those sectors along an exact Pareto segment, and an averaged holonomy
defect quantitatively controls distance to the real switching class.  The
paper closes with the calibrated determinant-sign readout and the precise
resource boundary between ordinary-photon tests and direct three-fermion
emulation.

This is a theory and design-limit analysis, not a report of a built device or
a fifth numbered Clebsch-series paper.
