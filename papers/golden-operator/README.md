# The golden conference operator and its shadow sisters

This directory is the authoritative manuscript root for the Golden paper.

The paper begins with an abstract fully marked golden conference presentation
and proves forward operator propagation.  Clebsch Papers I and III are
independent provenance sources, not manuscript inputs to edit or reorganize.

Current state:

- the title, abstract, principal theorem, and proof roadmap are in
  golden_operator.tex;
- the frozen architecture and complete placement map are in
  notes/2026-07-31-c720-golden-paper-charter.md;
- the marked theorem imported by C727 is in
  notes/2026-07-31-c720-c727-operator-interface.md;
- verification policy and source-bundle imports are under verification/.

Build from this directory with:

    make

The manuscript must not claim that the finite model constructs a gauge theory,
interacting fermionic phase, topological qubit, or positive dimer partition
function.
