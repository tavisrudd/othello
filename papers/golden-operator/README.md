# The golden conference operator and its shadow sisters

This directory contains the manuscript *The golden conference operator and
its shadow sisters*.

## Headline

Let (C) be a symmetric zero-diagonal sign matrix of order six with
(C^2=5I), together with a coherent outer (S_6)-marking. The paper proves
that the natural cubic constructions from this operator factor through one
five-dimensional invariant quotient of six marked points on (mathbf P^1),
and that the normalized marked lift is unique.

The six cubic coordinates have equivalent descriptions as middle-exterior
coefficients, commutator Pfaffians, and determinants between the two golden
eigenspaces. Their adjugates recover the classical polar map from the Segre
cubic to the Igusa quartic. Balanced sign vectors give a universal
maximum-determinant characterization of (C^2=5I), while the same data produce
an order-ten conference shadow. The final applications to fermionic amplitudes
and Abelian anomaly equations are consequences, not premises, of the operator
theorem.

The proof uses an abstract marked conference presentation. The five-paper
Clebsch portfolio provides independent provenance and motivation for the marked
construction; this paper does not reorganize or require any of those
manuscripts.
