\section{Exact recovery optimization}
\label{sec:exact-recovery-optimization}

Theorem~\ref{prop:prescribed-coset-composition} is already an algorithm: its
min--sum states are the intermediate functionals that scalar summaries
discard.  ERGO-comp (Exact Recovery and Generalized-weight Optimization
Compiler) evaluates that recursion.  The numerical tables store the exact
fibrewise minima; predecessor labels and minimizing lifts, stored separately,
reconstruct a coefficient-level witness for every reported optimum.

\input{figures/ergo-comp-pipeline}

We first state the algorithm in flattened $\F_q$-linear coordinates.  Let
$T$ have dimension $t$, let the outer message space have dimension $m$, and
let the inner label space have dimension $s$.  For the $i$th outer coordinate,
write
\[
 A_i:\operatorname{Hom}_{\F_q}(T,\F_q^s)
       \longrightarrow \operatorname{Hom}_{\F_q}(T,\F_q^m)
\]
for its contribution to the outer restriction map, and let
$\lambda_i(b)$ be the prescribed-coset support cost of the inner label $b$.
Different inner blocks may have different cost functions.

\begin{proposition}[Exact hierarchical recovery optimizer]
\label{prop:exact-hierarchical-optimizer}
\coverage{absent}%
\uses{prop:prescribed-coset-composition}%
For $c\in\operatorname{Hom}_{\F_q}(T,\F_q^m)$, define
\begin{equation}
 D_0(c)=\begin{cases}0,&c=0,\\+\infty,&c\ne0,\end{cases}
 \qquad
 D_i(c)=\min_b\bigl(D_{i-1}(c-A_i b)+\lambda_i(b)\bigr).
 \label{eq:hierarchical-recovery-dp}
\end{equation}
Then $D_N(c)$ is the exact prescribed-coset support cost of the concatenated
code at label $c$.  Storing a minimizing label $b$ and predecessor state at
each finite entry returns an exact block-labelled recovery witness, not only
its scalar cost.  The same recursion applies to the target-normalized formula
after adjoining its target-image labels to the state.

If every label is enumerated, put $Q=q^{mt}$ and $S=q^{st}$.  The direct
table algorithm uses $O(NQS)$ min--sum transitions, $O(Q)$ working cost
memory, and $O(NQ)$ predecessor entries when all witnesses are retained.
Unreachable labels, quotient coordinates, projectively equivalent columns,
and repeated block types may be compiled out without changing the optimum or
the replayed witness.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
After $i$ blocks, $D_i(c)$ is the least sum of inner support costs among
labels $(b_1,\ldots,b_i)$ satisfying
$\sum_{j\leq i}A_jb_j=c$.  This invariant is true at $i=0$.  Conditioning on
the last label $b_i$ gives exactly~\eqref{eq:hierarchical-recovery-dp}, so
induction proves the claim.  The leaf supports lie in disjoint blocks, hence
their costs add.  Backtracking the stored minimizers reconstructs the labels;
the inner prescribed-coset witnesses then reconstruct the coefficient-level
recovery system.  There are $Q$ states and $S$ candidate labels per block,
which gives the stated bounds.  The target-normalized case is the same
invariant applied to the paired labels in~\eqref{eq:target-cost-composition}.
\uses{prop:prescribed-coset-composition}%
\proves{prop:exact-hierarchical-optimizer}%
\end{proof}

The resulting table supplies all exact helper costs needed by the finite
confinement formula, including its zero and nonzero outer-functional sectors.
It can therefore emit a replayable recovery witness when a threshold fails,
or a complete exhausted-state certificate when the requested label is
unreachable.  Iterating the compiler through a tower preserves the
intermediate labels required at the next level; collapsing each table to one
minimum would destroy precisely this compositional information.

The same support functions also lead to an exact capacity-aware repair
scheduler.  Suppose demand $i$ has at most $A$ admissible recovery choices.
Choice $a$ consumes a nonnegative integer load vector
$\ell_{i,a}\in\mathbb Z_{\geq0}^H$ on $H$ helpers, and capacities are
$\boldsymbol c=(c_1,\ldots,c_H)$.  A demand may be skipped.  This model
distinguishes support occupancy from coefficient-weighted download and permits
different capacities at different helpers.

\begin{proposition}[Exact capacitated repair scheduling]
\label{prop:capacitated-repair-scheduling}
\coverage{absent}%
Let $\mathcal S=\prod_{h=1}^H\{0,\ldots,c_h\}$.  A dynamic program indexed by
the current load $u\in\mathcal S$, with transitions
$u\mapsto u+\ell_{i,a}$ whenever the result is within capacity and with a
skip transition, returns the maximum number of simultaneously served demands
and an exact assignment witness.  It uses
\begin{equation}
 O\!\left(DAH\prod_{h=1}^H(c_h+1)\right)
 \quad\text{time and}\quad
 O\!\left(\prod_{h=1}^H(c_h+1)\right)
 \quad\text{working memory},
 \label{eq:capacitated-repair-bound}
\end{equation}
apart from stored predecessors.

If there are positive integer weights $w_h$ and a common $M>0$ such that
$w\mathbin\cdot\ell_{i,a}=M$ for every admissible choice, a state serving
exactly $r$ demands lies on the graded shell
\[
 \mathcal S_r=\{u\in\mathcal S:w\mathbin\cdot u=rM\}.
\]
Thus the full box may be replaced by the union of its reachable shells.  Two
distinct states on one shell are incomparable in the componentwise order, so
ordinary Pareto-dominance pruning cannot remove either one.  The grading is
therefore both an exact compression and a certificate of the limit of that
common pruning rule.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
After processing the first $i$ demands, associate to every reachable load the
largest served count and a predecessor realizing it.  The skip and assignment
transitions enumerate exactly the feasible decisions for demand $i+1$;
induction gives optimality and witness recovery.  Scanning at most $A$
choices across $H$ coordinates for every demand and box state gives
\eqref{eq:capacitated-repair-bound}.  Under the grading hypothesis, additivity
places every $r$-repair load on $\mathcal S_r$.  If $u\leq v$ componentwise
and both lie on the same shell, positivity of $w$ forces $u=v$, proving the
antichain assertion.
\proves{prop:capacitated-repair-scheduling}%
\end{proof}

\subsection{Measured solver boundary}

The specialized dynamic programs are most useful when compilation exposes a
small algebraic state space.  They are not replacements for a general
constraint solver when the residual model contains unrelated side
constraints.  The fair deployment boundary is therefore:
\[
 \text{algebraic compilation}
 \longrightarrow
 \begin{cases}
   \text{specialized exact DP},&\text{when the compiled state is sufficient},\\
   \text{CP-SAT},&\text{for the remaining general constraints}.
 \end{cases}
\]
Direct CP-SAT is an appropriate end-to-end baseline.  A second,
\emph{structured CP-SAT} baseline receives the same symmetry reduction and
algebraic preprocessing as ERGO-comp, isolating the value of the specialized
state engine from the value of compilation itself.

The largest gains occur on graded shells and other low-dimensional reachable
state sets hidden inside a large capacity box.  The companion therefore solves
the instance directly when the compiled state is sufficient, and otherwise
supplies an exact preprocessing front end for a residual CP-SAT model while
retaining recovery witnesses and confinement thresholds.
