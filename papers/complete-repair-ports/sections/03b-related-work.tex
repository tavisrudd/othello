\paragraph{Support and recovery.}
Relative generalized Hamming weights and relative dimension/length profiles
already describe support thresholds for nested codes
\cite{LuoEtAl2005,KuriharaUyematsuMatsumoto2012,GeilEtAl2014,SanJose2025,Geil2026}.
Recovery-set formulations retain the choices needed for availability and
allocation \cite{GopalanEtAl2012,PamiesEtAl2013,RawatMazumdarVishwanath2015,
AbdelGhaffarWeber2017,GruicaEtAl2026}.  Coefficients are also established:
M\'arquez-Corbella, Mart\'inez-Moro, and Munuera characterize recovery by dual
words and minimal recovery by minimal dual words, and give executable recovery
methods \cite[Proposition~1 and Corollary~2]{MarquezCorbellaEtAl2019}.
Galindo et al.\ transport a component recovery set and its coefficients to a
matrix-product code \cite[Proposition~8]{GalindoEtAl2023v1}; concatenation
also yields LRC constructions \cite{LiuEtAl2022,JinFu2026}.
Our reverse-control question asks when every bounded global equation, or every
bounded minimal support, must come from the target block.

Service-rate regions allocate requests under helper capacities
\cite{AktasEtAl2021,AlfaranoEtAl2024}; inclusion-minimal supports suffice
\cite[Proposition~3.3]{AlfaranoRavagnaniSoljanin2022}.
Our operational transfer preserves that bounded family.
Disjoint simplex repair \cite{CheeEtAl2024}, online batch recovery
\cite{DuzgunEtAl2026}, and download/subpacketization models
\cite{ShahEtAl2010,YeBarg2016,GuruswamiWootters2016,MatthewsEtAl2026}
pose distinct workload or resource constraints.

\paragraph{Composition and state.}
Zhang et al.\ define minimum union support for a prescribed tuple of cosets
\cite{ZhangEtAl2019}; maximizing over tuples gives generalized covering
quantities \cite{ElimelechFirerSchwartz2021}.
Functional-dual decomposition and min--sum elimination occur in concatenated
decoding \cite{Forney1966,ChenLingXing2005,GuruswamiSudan2002,BlomqvistEtAl2020}.
The generalized distributive law supplies the general message-passing principle
\cite[Theorem~3.1]{AjiMcEliece2000}.  The recovery formula specifies its fibres,
normalization, and coefficient lifts; it does not introduce min--sum elimination.
Labelled soft information and presentation-dependent enumerators serve related
decoding and enumeration tasks \cite{LoskotBeaulieu2006,Nogin2005}.

Kashyap's linear cut-state quotient provides the coding-theoretic setting for
boundary compression \cite[Section~2.2 and Theorem~3.4]{Kashyap2007}.
Contextual equivalence is likewise established in weighted automata and tree
series \cite[Section~6.4, Theorem~8]{Mohri2009}, \cite{Maletti2008}.
Funk, Mayhew, and Newman define indistinguishability by exterior extensions and
prove its union congruence
\cite[Definition~2.2 and Proposition~2.3]{FunkMayhewNewman2019}.
Appendix~\ref{sec:contextual-refinement} fixes the original leaf confinement
boundary and derives concrete recovery probes and a radius-controlled
separator bound.  Its numerical observations do not include arbitrary
reweightings or escape from newly formed macroblocks.
