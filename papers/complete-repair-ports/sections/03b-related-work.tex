\subsection{Relation to adjacent formulations}
\label{sec:related-work}

\paragraph{Local recovery and workloads.}
Relative generalized Hamming weights and relative dimension/length profiles
describe support and access thresholds for nested codes
\cite{LuoEtAl2005,KuriharaUyematsuMatsumoto2012,GeilEtAl2014,SanJose2025,
Geil2026}; our identity $\mu_t(P)=M_t(D_P,K_P)$ specializes this theory to the
canonical target/helper pair.  Recovery-set formulations retain the helper choices needed for
availability and allocation \cite{GopalanEtAl2012,PamiesEtAl2013,RawatMazumdarVishwanath2015,
AbdelGhaffarWeber2017,GruicaEtAl2026}.  Coefficient witnesses are also established:
M\'arquez-Corbella, Mart\'inez-Moro, and Munuera
\cite[Proposition~1 and Corollary~2]{MarquezCorbellaEtAl2019}
characterize recovery by dual words and minimal recovery by minimal dual
words, and compute sharp recovery structures with executable recovery
methods.  Here those coefficients additionally determine the functional
labels that couple blocks.  Disjoint
simplex recovery optimizes the simultaneous use of such support families \cite{CheeEtAl2024}.  The physical-resource model also matters: regenerating codes optimize download
and subpacketization \cite{ShahEtAl2010,YeBarg2016,GuruswamiWootters2016,
MatthewsEtAl2026}.

Service-rate regions allocate requests among recovery sets under capacities
\cite{AktasEtAl2021,AlfaranoEtAl2024}.  Restricting to inclusion-minimal sets
already preserves the region
\cite[Proposition~3.3]{AlfaranoRavagnaniSoljanin2022}.
Theorem~\ref{thm:minimal-support-confinement} supplies the transfer step:
target-touching outer dual distance preserves the entire bounded minimal
family even when individual equations can leave the target block.
Online and asynchronous batch recovery impose further constraints on
successive choices \cite{DuzgunEtAl2026}; our allocation consequence concerns
the stated simultaneous capacity problem.

\paragraph{What passes through concatenation.}
Zhang et al.\ define the minimum union support for a prescribed tuple of
cosets and then its maximum over tuples
\cite{ZhangEtAl2019}.
Generalized covering radii develop the latter invariant
\cite{ElimelechFirerSchwartz2021}.
Our ordinary local cost is the prescribed-tuple quantity; the transfer
formula also retains target normalization, outer functional compatibility,
and the separate cost of a nonzero inner-kernel equation.

Concatenation and matrix-product constructions produce LRCs with prescribed
parameters \cite{LiuEtAl2022,JinFu2026}; Jin and Fu's Construction~3.2 fixes
a binary $[3,2,2]$ inner code and an $\F_4$ outer code.
Galindo et al.\ transport a component recovery set and its coefficients
to a matrix-product code \cite[Proposition~8]{GalindoEtAl2023v1}.
Our confinement question asks for the reverse control as well: at what
radius must every global equation, or every minimal recovery support, come
from the target block?  The labelled formula answers the equation question
for the represented inner code and target split; the target-touching
distance criterion answers the minimal-support question.

The functional-dual decomposition is classical, and min--sum elimination also
appears in concatenated decoding \cite{Forney1966,ChenLingXing2005,
GuruswamiSudan2002,BlomqvistEtAl2020}.  The generalized distributive law
\cite[Theorem~3.1]{AjiMcEliece2000} provides the general message-passing
principle; our composition theorem specifies the recovery fibres,
normalization, and coefficient lifts to which it applies.  Symbol-indexed soft
information, presentation-dependent input--output enumerators, and message
weights retain labels for decoding or enumeration
\cite{LoskotBeaulieu2006,Nogin2005}; our target-normalized costs instead
identify the first nonconfined support.

\paragraph{How much interface information is necessary.}
Contextual equivalence and congruence are established ideas in automata and
finite-interface decomposition.  For deterministic weighted automata,
future weighted behaviour, after weight normalization, gives state
minimization under the applicable semiring hypotheses
\cite[Section~6.4, Theorem~8]{Mohri2009}; analogous
Myhill--Nerode statements exist for recognizable tree series
\cite{Maletti2008}.  In coding and represented-matroid settings, minimal tree
realizations and finite equivalence across separations likewise compress
interface information.  Kashyap's cut-state quotient
\cite[Section~2.2 and Theorem~3.4]{Kashyap2007} supplies the classical
linear-code setting for the boundary dimension used in
Proposition~\ref{prop:boundary-width-compilation}.
Funk, Mayhew, and Newman define indistinguishability by all exterior
extensions and prove its union congruence
\cite[Definition~2.2 and Proposition~2.3]{FunkMayhewNewman2019}.
In the recovery formulation, the contexts are outer linear codes,
the response is the exact target-normalized recovery-support cost, and
Theorem~\ref{prop:rank-bounded-outer-tests} proves a radius-controlled
separator bound and bounded witness cover.  The witness-propagating min--sum
composition is Theorem~\ref{prop:prescribed-coset-composition}.
