\node[box] (recovery) {normalized inner recovery equations\\$\cP_x^{(\le r)}(I)$};
\node[box, right=of recovery] (cost) {block-functional costs\\$\mu_x,\ \lambda,\ z_x$\\fixed inner encoder};

\draw[arrow] (support.south) -- ++(0,-6mm) -|

\end{tikzpicture}
\caption{The transfer spine.  In the fixed-inner, $L$-linear concatenation
regime, weighted block-functional costs give an exact confinement gate.
When $d(O_N^\perp)\to\infty$ and $r+1<z_x(I)$, exact helper supports and
normalized equations are copied into every block.  Reliability is derived from
the copied support clutter; the diagram makes no necessity claim for arbitrary
asymptotically good families.}
