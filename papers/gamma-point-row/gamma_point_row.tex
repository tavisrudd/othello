\begin{abstract}
The Gamma integral structure turns the class of a point into the flat Euler
covector which reads ordinary rank.  We prove exact transport identities for
this distinguished row across a simple VGIT wall at extremal specialization
and across an ordinary flop on a fixed continuation domain.  We then give
analytic and algebraic countermodels showing that formal monodromy, pairing,
integrality, and localized Fourier support do not suffice to compose such
local comparisons.

For a global birational comparison, a common-open orbit cylinder in a
W{\l}odarczyk cobordism gives one endpoint point row, and rotation
localization kills every intermediate fixed stratum.  We reduce each
remaining neutral fixed-graph coefficient to a balanced Gamma-ratio kernel.
The further assertion that the complete nonlinear graph sum has one
Mellin--Barnes continuation is isolated as an explicit hypothesis; it is not
claimed to follow from the available linear GLSM contour theorem.  Under
this hypothesis, Rees homogenization and a finite common quotient make
nonvanishing of the point row on a formal-monodromy primary packet
birationally invariant.

For a smooth cubic threefold \(X\), we isolate the only cubic-specific input.
Cai's small quantum-connection matrices give a rank-two block with
primitive-sixth formal monodromy.  We reconstruct its indicial polynomial
directly, and an elementary \({}_2F_3\) Barnes calculation shows that the
Gamma point row is nonzero on both primitive-sixth lines.  Quantum K\"unneth
then makes the packet nonzero on \(X\times\mathbf P^m\), whereas
\(\mathbf P^{m+3}\) has no primitive-sixth packet.  Conditional birational
invariance therefore yields irrationality of \(X\times\mathbf P^m\) for every
\(m\geq0\).  The unconditional endpoint calculation and the exact local
transport theorems remain independent of the global continuation
hypothesis.
\end{abstract}

\noindent\textbf{Keywords.} Gamma class; quantum cohomology; birational wall
