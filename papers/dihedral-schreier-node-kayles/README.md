# Node Kayles on Conic Schreier Graphs: Dihedral and Polyhedral Templates

## Headline

Let (Csubsetoperatorname{PG}(2,q)) be a nonsingular conic over an odd
finite field. An off-conic point induces an involution of
(Ccongmathbf P^1(q)). In the associated capacity-two avoidance game,
selected off-conic points forbid the conic points on their joining lines; the
legal continuations form a Node-Kayles position on a fixed-point-deleted
Schreier graph.

The paper proves the general involution-generated reduction and applies it to
three tame families. Two selected points realize every dihedral group
(D_{2m}), with cycle and path templates and Dawson's-chess nimbers. Three
selected points force the central involution and the even-rotation families,
whose templates include (K_4), Möbius ladders, prisms, and ordinary ladders.
The remaining tame cases are the polyhedral groups (S_4) and (A_5). The
resulting Grundy classification is an explicit congruence function of (q)
within each torus family and has a converse realization theorem.
