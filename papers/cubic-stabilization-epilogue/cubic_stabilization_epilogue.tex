\title{Irrationality after One Stabilization of Universally
  \(CH_0\)-Trivial Cubic Threefolds}
\author{Tavis Rudd}
\date{August 2026}

\begin{document}
\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
Does universal \(CH_0\)-triviality force rationality after stabilization?
We construct a non-isotrivial one-parameter family of smooth cubic
threefolds \(X_b\) for which \(X_b\) is universally \(CH_0\)-trivial but
\(X_b\times\PP^1\) is irrational.  More generally, we prove that
\(X\times\PP^1\) is irrational for every smooth cubic threefold \(X\).

The cycle argument proves a local cofactor-saturation theorem for principally
polarized elliptic-power quotients; semisimple graph slopes are a sufficient
mechanism.  Six elliptic quotients of the
intermediate Jacobians in the nonstandard \(A_5\)-invariant cubic pencil
satisfy its hypotheses.  Voisin's theorem then gives universal
\(CH_0\)-triviality fibre by fibre.

For irrationality, we count primitive-sixth eigenvalues of framed formal
monodromy on the numerical small even quantum connection.  Iritani's blowup
formula transports the count through weak factorization; a divisor-tagging
argument handles its possibly noninjective center specialization.  The count
is nonzero for a cubic and zero for every point, curve, and surface.
Thus the cycle-theoretic and quantum detectors diverge on the same smooth
fourfolds.
\end{abstract}

\noindent\textbf{Keywords.} cubic threefold; universal \(CH_0\)-triviality;
irrationality; intermediate Jacobian; quantum connection; weak factorization.
