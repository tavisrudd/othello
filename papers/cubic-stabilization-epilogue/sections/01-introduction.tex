\section{Introduction}

A smooth projective variety is universally \(CH_0\)-trivial if the degree map
\[
  \deg:\CH_0(Y_L)\longrightarrow\Z
\]
is an isomorphism for every field extension \(L/\C\).  This condition is a
central consequence of stable rationality, and its failure is a standard
stable-rationality obstruction.  Its validity, however, need not provide a
rational parametrization.  We ask how far that gap can persist after one
explicit stabilization.

Our examples are fourfolds of the form \(Y=X\times\PP^1\), where \(X\) is a
smooth cubic threefold in the nonstandard \(A_5\)-invariant pencil.  Two
independent mechanisms act on the same \(Y\).  The intermediate Jacobian of
\(X\) has an algebraic primitive minimal class; this gives universal
\(CH_0\)-triviality.  The small even quantum connection of every smooth cubic
has primitive-sixth framed formal monodromy; Iritani's quantum blowup formula
shows that its multiplicity survives one stabilization.

Here ``nonstandard'' has a precise representation-theoretic meaning.  Let
\(W_5\) be the irreducible five-dimensional complex representation of
\(A_5\), with character \((5,1,-1,0,0)\) on the classes
\((1,2A,3A,5A,5B)\).  The symmetric-cube character formula gives
\[
 \dim \bigl(\operatorname{Sym}^3W_5^*\bigr)^{A_5}=2.
\]
Thus \(\PP((\operatorname{Sym}^3W_5^*)^{A_5})\cong\PP^1\) is a pencil of cubic
hypersurfaces in \(\PP(W_5)\).  We denote its smooth locus by \(B^\circ\).
It is the complement of the discriminant in the pencil and is connected.
This is Hartlieb's irreducible-character component \(M_{H_1}\), as opposed
to the component coming from the reducible permutation representation
\(\mathbf1\oplus W_4\) \cite[Lemma~5.5]{Hartlieb}.

\begin{theorem}[Separation after one stabilization]
\label{thm:separation-family}
Let \(\mathcal X\to B^\circ\) be the smooth family in the nonstandard
\(A_5\)-invariant pencil of cubic threefolds.  For every
 \(b\in B^\circ(\C)\),
\[
 \mathcal X_b\text{ is universally }CH_0\text{-trivial},
 \qquad
 \mathcal X_b\times\PP^1\text{ is universally }CH_0\text{-trivial and irrational}.
\]
The family is non-isotrivial in coarse moduli.
\end{theorem}

The irrationality statement does not depend on the symmetry of the pencil.

\begin{theorem}[One-step irrationality]
\label{thm:every-cubic}
For every smooth complex cubic threefold \(X\), the fourfold
\(X\times\PP^1\) is irrational.
\end{theorem}

We do not claim that \(X\) is stably irrational.  From the second
stabilization onward, weak-factorization centers can themselves have cubic
dimension, and the low-dimensional exclusion used here no longer applies.

\subsection*{How the two proofs meet}

The cycle-theoretic half starts from six elliptic quotients of the Fano
surface.  Their Rosati Gram matrix is \(6I-J\), so any five axes give an
isogeny \(E^5\to J(X)\) of polarization type \((1,6,6,6,6)\).  The
two-primary principal kernels form \(\PP^1(\F_4)\).  Three retain the full
simplex symmetry; the other two are exchanged by Frobenius and have
stabilizer \(A_5\).  The cycle argument needs only this unordered exotic
pair.  At two its graph slope has
minimal polynomial \(t^2+t+1\); at three every \(A_5\)-stable graph is
scalar.  Both local forms admit primitive mixed-adjugate formulas, which produce
\(\Theta^4/4!\) as an integral product of divisor classes.

The quantum half uses a different invariant.  We count primitive sixth-root
eigenvalues of framed formal monodromy on the numerical small even quantum
connection.  Cai's cubic calculation gives a nonzero count, and the
projective-bundle formula doubles it on \(X\times\PP^1\).  Iritani's blowup
comparison is additive for this count.  Its center Novikov map can be
noninjective; formal divisor tags restore injectivity and the divisor equation
returns to the original specialization.  Surface classification then shows
that every center of dimension at most two contributes zero.

Local cofactor saturation forces the primitive minimal class into the integral
divisor-product lattice; semisimple graph slopes give a sufficient mechanism.
The second argument is a direct weak-factorization calculation with the
framed quantum invariant.  The special \(A_5\)-geometry enters only in the
first argument; the second applies to every smooth cubic threefold.

\subsection*{Relation with earlier rationality results}

Clemens and Griffiths proved that every smooth complex cubic threefold is
irrational by means of its intermediate Jacobian \cite{CG}.  That obstruction
does not by itself control a product with projective space, and stable
rationality of smooth cubic threefolds remains open.  Cai recently recovered
irrationality from the primitive-sixth-root formal monodromy of the cubic
quantum connection \cite{Cai}.  Iritani and Iritani--Koto prove the quantum
\(D\)-module decompositions for blowups and projective bundles
\cite{IritaniBlowup,IritaniKoto}; Katzarkov--Kontsevich--Pantev--Yu identify
the nef-canonical normal form used for low-dimensional centers
\cite{KKPYY}.  Theorem~\ref{thm:every-cubic} combines these inputs at the next
dimension.

On the cycle side, Voisin reduces universal \(CH_0\)-triviality of a cubic
threefold to algebraicity of the primitive minimal class of its intermediate
Jacobian \cite{Voisin}.  Beckmann--de Gaay Fortman place such minimal classes
in a general integral-Fourier framework \cite{BdGF}.  For the very general
cubic threefold the minimal class is not algebraic: every curve class on the
intermediate Jacobian is an even multiple of it, so a very general cubic
threefold admits no decomposition of the diagonal
\cite[Theorem~1.3 and Corollary~1.4]{EdGFS}.  What Voisin's criterion leaves is
the countable union of moduli subvarieties on which the class is algebraic;
Theorem~\ref{thm:exotic-minimal-class} places an explicit exceptional family
inside that union.  Here the six elliptic
quotients supplied by Roulleau and the special period locus isolated by
Hartlieb make the class explicit: the new point is its membership in the
ordinary integral divisor-product lattice for every smooth member of the
nonstandard \(A_5\)-pencil.
