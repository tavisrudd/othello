\section{Introduction}

A smooth projective variety is universally \(CH_0\)-trivial if the degree map
\[
  \deg:\CH_0(Y_L)\longrightarrow\Z
\]
is an isomorphism for every field extension \(L/\C\).  This condition is a
central consequence of stable rationality, and its failure is a standard
stable-rationality obstruction.  Its validity, however, need not provide a
rational parametrization.  We prove that even after one explicit
stabilization it does not predict rationality for smooth cubic threefolds.

\begin{theorem}[One-step irrationality]
\label{thm:every-cubic}
For every smooth complex cubic threefold \(X\), the fourfold
\(X\times\PP^1\) is irrational.
\end{theorem}

The proof uses ordinary Hodge atoms in the sense of
Katzarkov--Kontsevich--Pantev--Yu \cite{KKPYY}.  Their blowup and
projective-bundle formulas package weak factorization into a free abelian
ledger on atoms, and their ordinary non-rationality criterion says that a
smooth \(d\)-fold containing an atom not realizable in dimension at most
\(d-2\) is irrational.  We construct such an atom for a cubic threefold.
At the small hyperplane point, the two-dimensional even zero packet of the
Euler multiplication is a single Jordan block.  A regular-
\(F\)-manifold discriminant argument shows that the corresponding branch is
indeed a connected component of the unramified spectral cover, rather than
an accidental coalescence at one point.  On its even rank-two atomic
\(F\)-bundle, a canonical elementary modification turns the nilpotent
leading block into a logarithmic one.  The Poincar\'e pairing forces this
modification to be regular and flatness forces its residue to move by
conjugation.  The resulting residue discriminant \(\delta^\sharp\) is an
ordinary atom invariant.  For the cubic atom
\[
  \delta^\sharp=\frac49,
\]
whereas the only possible curve representative, a genus-five curve, has
\(\delta^\sharp=0\).  Surface representatives are excluded by parity rank
and the classification of minimal surfaces.  Since
\(\CF(X\times\PP^1)=2\CF(X)\), the cubic atom occurs on the stabilized
fourfold and the ordinary atom criterion proves Theorem~\ref{thm:every-cubic}.

The theorem is unconditional relative to the numbered ordinary Hodge-atom
theorems imported from \cite{KKPYY}, in the usual sense that a proof is
relative to its cited external results.  At the time of writing that source
is an unrefereed preprint, and its projective-bundle theorem ultimately
uses the Iritani--Koto decomposition theorem \cite{IritaniKoto}.  No enhanced
atom, Euler pairing, Serre automorphism, or non-archimedean
Riemann--Hilbert statement is used in our proof.

The conclusion propagates immediately through stabilized birational
correspondences.  In particular, if \(Y\times\PP^1\) is birational to
\(X\times\PP^1\) for a smooth cubic threefold \(X\), then
\(Y\times\PP^1\) is irrational.  Kuznetsov's construction for a smooth
prime Fano threefold \(V\) of genus eight gives rank-two projective bundles
\(\PP_V(U)\) and \(\PP_X(E^*)\) related by a flop
\cite[Theorems~2.17--2.18]{KuznetsovV14}; hence
\(V\times\PP^1\) is birational to \(X\times\PP^1\) and is also
irrational.  The finer equality \(\nu_6(V)=2\) below remains conditional on
the reconstruction-tail and divisor-tagging hypotheses of
Section~\ref{sec:framed-monodromy}.

\subsection*{Unconditional separation results}

Each separation statement rests on two independent inputs.  Irrationality
comes from the cubic Hodge atom.  Universal \(CH_0\)-triviality is proved
for the same cubic by a classical or cycle-theoretic theorem, and then
passes to the product by the projective-bundle formula for Chow groups.
Thus the vanishing of the usual decomposition-of-the-diagonal obstruction
and the atom obstruction read genuinely different structures on the same
fourfold.

Universally \(CH_0\)-trivial cubic threefolds are known in abundance.
Voisin proves that the ten-dimensional moduli space of smooth cubic
threefolds contains a nonempty countable union of subvarieties of
codimension at most three parametrizing threefolds with universally trivial
\(CH_0\) \cite[Theorem~4.5]{Voisin}.

\begin{corollary}[Separation in codimension three]
\label{cor:voisin-separation}
There is a nonempty countable union of subvarieties of codimension at most
three in the moduli space of smooth cubic threefolds such that, for every
member \(X\), both \(X\) and \(X\times\PP^1\) are universally
\(CH_0\)-trivial, while \(X\times\PP^1\) is irrational.
\end{corollary}

Independently, Colliot-Th\'el\`ene proves that a smooth cubic hypersurface
whose equation is a sum of forms in separated groups of at most three
variables is universally \(CH_0\)-trivial \cite{CT}, which applies to the
Fermat equation directly.

\begin{corollary}[Fermat]
\label{cor:fermat-separation}
The fourfold
\[
  \{x_1^3+x_2^3+x_3^3+x_4^3+x_5^3=0\}\times\PP^1
\]
is universally \(CH_0\)-trivial and irrational.
\end{corollary}

Yang--Yu--Zhu construct a two-dimensional family of smooth cubic
threefolds admitting unirational parametrizations of degrees two and three
\cite[Theorems~1.1 and 3.3]{YYZ}.  Parametrizations of coprime degrees give
a decomposition of the diagonal, so every member of that family is
universally \(CH_0\)-trivial; the same degrees persist on
\(X\times\PP^m\) for every \(m\) \cite[Corollary~3.5]{YYZ}.

\begin{corollary}[Coprime-degree cubics]
\label{cor:coprime-separation}
Let \(X\) be one of the cubic threefolds of \cite[Theorem~3.3]{YYZ}.  Then
\(X\times\PP^1\) admits unirational parametrizations of coprime degrees, is
universally \(CH_0\)-trivial, and is irrational.
\end{corollary}

Yang--Yu--Zhu ask whether a variety with unirational parametrizations of
coprime degrees can fail to be stably rational \cite[Question~1.4]{YYZ}.
Corollary~\ref{cor:coprime-separation} settles the first nontrivial
stabilization for their examples.  It does not settle stable irrationality:
our atom obstruction ceases to exclude rationality from the second
stabilization onward because a four- or five-dimensional stabilized variety
may be allowed atoms represented in dimension three, and the cubic atom
itself already has such a representative.

For our second separation result we give a different uniform proof of
universal \(CH_0\)-triviality along the nonstandard \(A_5\)-invariant
pencil.  There the primitive minimal class of the intermediate Jacobian is
algebraic by an integral saturation theorem for the six-axis lattice, for
every member at once.  This construction also reaches beyond the
separated-variable criterion of Colliot-Th\'el\`ene: all but finitely many
moduli points represented by the pencil are not projectively equivalent to
a cubic of separated-variable type.

\begin{theorem}[Separation on the \(A_5\)-pencil]
\label{thm:separation-family}
Let \(W_5\) be the five-dimensional irreducible \(A_5\)-module, and let
\(B^\circ\) be the smooth locus inside
\[
 \PP\bigl((\operatorname{Sym}^3W_5^*)^{A_5}\bigr).
\]
For the resulting family \(\mathcal X\to B^\circ\) and every
\(b\in B^\circ(\C)\),
\[
 \mathcal X_b\text{ and }\mathcal X_b\times\PP^1
 \text{ are universally }CH_0\text{-trivial},
\]
and \(\mathcal X_b\times\PP^1\) is irrational.  The family is
non-isotrivial in coarse moduli.  All but finitely many points of its
coarse-moduli image are not represented by cubics whose defining form is a
sum of cubic forms in disjoint groups of at most three variables.
\end{theorem}

The invariant cubic space in Theorem~\ref{thm:separation-family} is
two-dimensional.  Indeed, the character of \(W_5\) is
\((5,1,-1,0,0)\) on the classes \(1,2A,3A,5A,5B\), and the symmetric-cube
character formula gives
\[
 \dim \bigl(\operatorname{Sym}^3W_5^*\bigr)^{A_5}=2.
\]
Thus its projectivization is a pencil with connected smooth locus
\(B^\circ\).  Its coarse-moduli image is Hartlieb's \(M_{H_1}\); this is
not the component associated with \(\mathbf1\oplus W_4\)
\cite[Lemma~5.5]{Hartlieb}.  Since the family is non-isotrivial, its image
in coarse moduli is a curve, one dimension below the locus of \cite{YYZ}.

\begin{remark}
\label{rem:fermat-in-pencil}
The pencil contains the Fermat cubic threefold; it is one of the two members
that also admit the permutation action of \(A_5\)
\cite[Lemma~5.5]{Hartlieb}.  The mechanism is that \(W_5\) is induced from
a nontrivial cubic character \(\chi\) of a point stabilizer
\(A_4\subset A_5\): Frobenius reciprocity gives
\[
 \bigl\langle\operatorname{Ind}_{A_4}^{A_5}\chi,\;W_5\bigr\rangle
 =\bigl\langle\chi,\;\operatorname{Res}_{A_4}W_5\bigr\rangle=1,
\]
and both sides have dimension five, so
\(\operatorname{Ind}_{A_4}^{A_5}\chi\cong W_5\).  Induction from a
character realizes \(W_5\) by monomial matrices whose nonzero entries are
cube roots of unity, and such a matrix fixes
\(x_1^3+\cdots+x_5^3\).  Corollary~\ref{cor:fermat-separation} is therefore
the member of Theorem~\ref{thm:separation-family} at which universal
\(CH_0\)-triviality is also classical, and Yang--Yu--Zhu give that member
an explicit degree-three unirational parametrization
\cite[Remark~3.6]{YYZ}.
\end{remark}

\subsection*{A finer conditional framed invariant}

The ordinary atom proof above is deliberately minimal: it follows one
rank-two cubic atom and uses only the finite residue discriminant needed to
exclude low-dimensional representatives.  We also study a finer invariant
of the entire numerical small even quantum connection.  Definition
\ref{def:framed-sixth-multiplicity} counts the primitive-sixth eigenvalues
of framed formal monodromy on the original loop disc and gives an invariant
\(\nu_6\).  The cubic packet calculation is unconditional and yields
\(\nu_6(X)=2\).  More generally, the product formula for Gromov--Witten
theory gives \(\nu_6(T\times\PP^m)=(m+1)\nu_6(T)\), so in particular
\(\nu_6(X\times\PP^1)=4\) unconditionally.

The actual Iritani and Iritani--Koto comparison isomorphisms preserve the
original-\(z\)-disc framing after coefficient extension.  The string and
divisor equations exactly remove the scalar degree-zero and fixed
degree-two pieces of their reconstruction coordinates.  We therefore split
the remaining uncertainty into two statements in
Section~\ref{sec:framed-monodromy}: Hypothesis~5.7R concerns only the
residual reconstruction tail, while Hypothesis~5.7T is
the separate specialization step in the divisor-tagging argument.  The latter
is already unnecessary for nef-canonical targets, for \(\PP^1\) and
\(\PP^2\), and---assuming 5.7R---for ruled surfaces over positive-genus
curves; in the present low-dimensional proof it remains only for rational
ruled and nonminimal surfaces.  A rank-two formal-germ theorem proves the
local mechanism needed for the cubic packet, but we do not identify the
reconstruction value with a point of that formal germ.

Conditional on Hypothesis~5.7R, \(\nu_6\) satisfies the displayed
projective-bundle and blowup formulas.  Conditional on both
Hypotheses~5.7R and~5.7T, it is birationally invariant through dimension
four.  These stronger results are not needed for
Theorem~\ref{thm:every-cubic}; they supply instead a finer numerical package
and, for genus eight, the conditional identity \(\nu_6(V)=2\).

\subsection*{Relation with earlier rationality results}

Clemens and Griffiths proved that every smooth complex cubic threefold is
irrational by means of its intermediate Jacobian \cite{CG}.  That
obstruction does not by itself control a product with projective space.  The
very general cubic threefold is now known to be stably irrational
\cite[Corollary~1.4]{EdGFS}, but stable rationality remains open for an
arbitrary smooth cubic threefold.  Cai recently recovered irrationality
from the primitive-sixth-root formal monodromy of the cubic quantum
connection \cite{Cai}.  Iritani and Iritani--Koto prove the quantum
\(D\)-module decompositions for blowups and projective bundles
\cite{IritaniBlowup,IritaniKoto}; the ordinary Hodge-atom package of
Katzarkov--Kontsevich--Pantev--Yu \cite{KKPYY} packages these decompositions
into birational atomic data.  Theorem~\ref{thm:every-cubic} uses that
ordinary package together with the new rank-two residue discriminant,
without the enhanced Serre-automorphism machinery of \cite{KKPYY} and
without transporting framed monodromy through reconstruction displacements.

On the cycle side, Voisin reduces universal \(CH_0\)-triviality of a cubic
threefold to algebraicity of the primitive minimal class of its intermediate
Jacobian \cite{Voisin}.  Beckmann--de Gaay Fortman place such minimal
classes in a general integral-Fourier framework \cite{BdGF}.  For the very
general cubic threefold the minimal class is not algebraic: every curve
class on the intermediate Jacobian is an even multiple of it, so a very
general cubic threefold admits no decomposition of the diagonal
\cite[Theorem~1.3 and Corollary~1.4]{EdGFS}.  Known pieces of the algebraic
locus include Voisin's components of codimension at most three, the
almost-diagonal cubics of \cite{CT}, and the coprime-degree family of
\cite{YYZ}.  Theorem~\ref{thm:six-axis-divided-powers} places the
nonstandard \(A_5\)-curve in the same algebraic-minimal-class locus by a
uniform integral argument, and
Proposition~\ref{prop:A5-nonseparated} shows that all but finitely many
moduli points on that pencil lie outside the separated-variable locus
covered by \cite{CT}.  Thus for a general member the six-axis construction
accounts for universal \(CH_0\)-triviality in a case not covered by that
criterion.  We do not claim that the \(A_5\)-curve is generically disjoint
from the coprime-degree locus of \cite{YYZ} or from every other previously
known part of Voisin's algebraic-minimal-class locus.

\subsection*{Proof map}

Section~\ref{sec:envelope} computes the six-axis polarization and identifies
the exotic cubic gluing.  Section~\ref{sec:minimal-class} proves the local
divisor-product theorem, applies it to the primitive minimal class, and
shows that the resulting \(A_5\)-curve is generically outside the
separated-variable locus.
Section~\ref{sec:atomic-one-step} proves Theorem~\ref{thm:every-cubic}
unconditionally from ordinary Hodge atoms and a rank-two residue
discriminant, with the small cubic block reduction isolated for later reuse.
Section~\ref{sec:framed-monodromy} defines the finer invariant \(\nu_6\),
proves exact low-degree shift and projective-space product formulas, records
the formal-germ rank-two rigidity theorem and the two residual hypotheses,
and computes \(\nu_6(X)=2\) and \(\nu_6(X\times\PP^1)=4\) from the same
cubic packet.  The final section combines the
unconditional irrationality theorem with the cycle-theoretic results and
records the exact scope of the conditional framed refinement.
