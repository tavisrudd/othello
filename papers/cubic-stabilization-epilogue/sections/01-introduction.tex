\section{Introduction}

A smooth projective variety is universally \(CH_0\)-trivial if the degree map
\[
  \deg:\CH_0(Y_L)\longrightarrow\Z
\]
is an isomorphism for every field extension \(L/\C\).  This condition is a
central consequence of a decomposition of the diagonal and obstructs stable
rationality.  Its vanishing, however, need not provide a rational
parametrization.  We ask how far the failure can persist after one explicit
stabilization.

Our examples are fourfolds of the form \(Y=X\times\PP^1\), where \(X\) is a
smooth cubic threefold in the nonstandard \(A_5\)-invariant pencil.  Two
independent mechanisms act on the same \(Y\).  The intermediate Jacobian of
\(X\) has an algebraic primitive minimal class; this gives universal
\(CH_0\)-triviality.  The big quantum connection of every smooth cubic
contains a formal-monodromy atom with primitive sixth roots; weak
factorization shows that this atom survives the product with \(\PP^1\).

\begin{theorem}[Separation after one stabilization]
\label{thm:separation-family}
Let \(X\to B^\circ\) be the smooth locus of the nonstandard
\(A_5\)-invariant pencil of cubic threefolds.  For every
\(b\in B^\circ(\C)\),
\[
 X_b\text{ is universally }CH_0\text{-trivial},
 \qquad
 X_b\times\PP^1\text{ is universally }CH_0\text{-trivial and irrational}.
\]
The family is non-isotrivial.
\end{theorem}

The irrationality statement does not depend on the symmetry of the pencil.

\begin{theorem}[One-step irrationality]
\label{thm:every-cubic}
For every smooth complex cubic threefold \(X\), the fourfold
\(X\times\PP^1\) is irrational.
\end{theorem}

We do not claim that \(X\) is stably irrational.  From the second
stabilization onward, weak-factorization centers can themselves have cubic
dimension, and the low-dimensional exclusion used here no longer applies.

\subsection*{How the two proofs meet}

The cycle-theoretic half starts from six elliptic quotients of the Fano
surface.  Their Rosati Gram matrix is \(6I-J\), so any five axes give an
isogeny \(E^5\to J(X)\) of polarization type \((1,6,6,6,6)\).  The
two-primary principal kernels form \(\PP^1(\F_4)\).  Three retain the full
simplex symmetry; the other two are exchanged by Frobenius and have
stabilizer \(A_5\).  The golden orientation reconstructed by the companion
cubic selects one member of this exotic pair.  At two its graph slope has
minimal polynomial \(t^2+t+1\); at three the gluing is scalar.  Both local
forms admit primitive mixed-adjugate formulas, which produce
\(\Theta^4/4!\) as an integral product of divisor classes.

The quantum half uses a different invariant.  We count primitive sixth-root
eigenvalues of formal monodromy on the atoms of the maximal big-quantum
\(F\)-bundle.  Cai's cubic calculation gives a nonzero count.  The
projective-bundle formula doubles it on \(X\times\PP^1\).  If this fourfold
were rational, weak factorization would express that count using centers of
dimension at most two.  Surface classification and the low-dimensional atom
formula show that every such center has count zero.

The finite six-axis carrier first arose as the common output of distinct

\subsection*{Relation with earlier rationality results}

Clemens and Griffiths proved that every smooth complex cubic threefold is
irrational by means of its intermediate Jacobian \cite{CG}.  That obstruction
does not by itself control a product with projective space, and stable
rationality of smooth cubic threefolds remains open.  Cai recently recovered
irrationality from the primitive-sixth-root formal monodromy of the cubic
quantum connection \cite{Cai}.  Katzarkov--Kontsevich--Pantev--Yu supply the
atom formalism, blowup and projective-bundle formulas, and the resulting
nonrationality criterion \cite{KKPYY}.  Theorem~\ref{thm:every-cubic} combines
these inputs at the next dimension: after one projective stabilization, the
cubic atom still cannot be contributed by any weak-factorization center.

On the cycle side, Voisin reduces universal \(CH_0\)-triviality of a cubic
threefold to algebraicity of the primitive minimal class of its intermediate
Jacobian \cite{Voisin}.  Beckmann--de Gaay Fortman place such minimal classes
in a general integral-Fourier framework \cite{BdGF}.  Here the six elliptic
quotients supplied by Roulleau and the special period locus isolated by
Hartlieb make the class explicit: the new point is its membership in the
ordinary integral divisor-product lattice for every smooth member of the
nonstandard \(A_5\)-pencil.
