\section{The separation theorem}
\label{sec:synthesis}

\begin{proof}[Proof of Theorem~\ref{thm:separation-family}]
Corollary~\ref{cor:universal-ch0} gives universal \(CH_0\)-triviality of
\(X_b\).  The projective bundle formula for Chow groups gives the same
property for \(X_b\times\PP^1\).  Theorem~\ref{thm:every-cubic} proves that
this fourfold is irrational.  Non-isotriviality follows from the nonconstant
intermediate-Jacobian period map on the \(A_5\)-component.
\end{proof}

The theorem separates two tests on one variety.  The algebraic class
\(\Theta^4/4!\) removes the decomposition-of-diagonal obstruction.  The
primitive-sixth-root atom survives one stabilization and obstructs a rational
factorization.  The first mechanism is special to the exotic \(A_5\)-family;
the second holds for every cubic threefold.

The result stops after one stabilization.  For
\(X\times\PP^m\) with \(m\ge2\), weak-factorization centers may have dimension
at least three and can carry cubic-type atoms.  The low-dimensional exclusion
in Proposition~\ref{prop:atom-values} no longer proves noncancellation.
Likewise, the fibrewise minimal-class argument gives no relative universal
cycle over the pencil.  These are separate problems and are not implicit in
the theorem above.

The reconstruction series ends with a finite marked carrier.  Its maximal
golden normalization supplies the \(\F_4\)-sheet used here, and the six
elliptic axes realize the corresponding integral symplectic envelope.  The
geometric consequence is therefore more than a second occurrence of the
same \(A_5\)-set: the recovered orientation selects the principal gluing on
which the primitive minimal class becomes integral.  On the resulting
fourfolds, universal \(CH_0\)-triviality and rationality part company.
