% Formal annotations.
%
% Each theorem-like environment in this manuscript carries a machine-readable
% record of its relationship to the Lean companion in lean/.  Two macros supply
% it, both typographically empty:
%
%   \coverage{...}  the strength at which the statement is formalized, one of
%                   absent, fragment, conditional_deduction, complete;
%   \lean{...}      the reviewer terminals carrying it, named without their
%                   common namespace and separated by commas.
%
% \lean follows the convention of the Lean blueprint system of P. Massot, whose
% print-mode macros are likewise empty; \coverage is local to this manuscript
% and replaces that system's two-valued formalization flag, because a statement
% here may also be represented by a strictly weaker fragment or by a conditional
% deduction with its external premises exposed.  The annotations are read by
% lean/verification/check_formal_artifact.py, which requires them to agree
% exactly with the per-claim record in lean/verification/claims.json.
\newcommand{\lean}[1]{\ignorespaces}
\newcommand{\coverage}[1]{\ignorespaces}
