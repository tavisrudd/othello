# Frobenius-equivariant pair extension and robust repair of eight-arcs

**Title:** *Frobenius-equivariant pair extension and robust repair of
eight-arcs.*

## Headline

The paper studies arcs invariant under a quadratic Frobenius involution. It
proves an exact orbit-valued criterion for adjoining conjugate pairs while
preserving the arc condition, identifies the collision correction that makes
the count exact, and turns the count into a robust orbit-replacement theorem.

For invariant eight-arcs in (operatorname{PG}(2,25)), every arc has at least
four distinct legal conjugate pairs. In the two-fixed-point profile the exact
minimum is (32), with equality represented by five residual-group orbits of
sizes (200,400,400,200,400). More generally, the certified profile envelope
gives at least (318) alternate repairs after a selected orbit is deleted, and
the parameterized ((k+2)	o k) theorem gives the corresponding general
puncture-and-re-extend bound.

The paper also records the Clebsch specialization: after scalar extension to
(operatorname{GF}(121)), every (operatorname{GF}(11))-rational six-arc has
(4180) legal paired extensions and (4179) alternate repairs after one is
selected. This is a corollary of the general carrier count, not a separate
Clebsch theorem.

## Evidence and status

The quadratic criterion, semantic global count, collision inverse, robust
exchange theorem, and (PG(2,25)) classification have kernel-checked Lean
support. Exact finite profiles also have reproducible certificate evidence;
the classical projective-geometry inputs and bounded priority boundary are
stated explicitly in the manuscript.

The source contains the focused LaTeX manuscript, bibliography, PDF, and
verification surface. It is a staged paper release; publication identifiers,
final export choices, and submission remain author decisions.
