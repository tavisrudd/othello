# A referee's route through the proof

The primary manuscript is *Irrationality of Cubic Threefolds after One
Stabilization*. The two papers under `companions/` and the separate sharpness
paper are not inputs to its numerical or Hodge proofs.

## Three reading routes

1. **Cubic irrationality.** Read `thm:every-cubic` in the introduction,
   the rank-two construction in `sections/02-qdm-marker.tex`, the blowup and
   surface proofs in `sections/03-blowup-centers.tex`, and the cubic calculation
   and final contradiction in `sections/04-cubic-proof.tex`. This is a complete
   proof of the unconditional headline; the independent split-basis appendix
   is an alternative check of the cubic calculation.
2. **All seventeen Fano families.** Continue to
   `sections/05-fano-classification.tex`. The rank-three odd-dimension selector
   complements the rank-two residue. The nine matrices and every-member
   geometric provenance needed to audit the endpoints are in
   `sections/10-fano-data.tex`. The eight positive rationality results enter
   separately. No Hodge group is needed for this theorem.
3. **Rational Hodge conservation.** Part II keeps the entire cohomology fiber
   while restricting the parameter base. `sections/06-hodge-conservation.tex`
   contains the fixed-base injection proof, selection and surface vanishing,
   endpoint recovery, cancellation in the representation group, and rational
   descent. `sections/07-torelli.tex` applies a separate very-general Torelli
   theorem. The arithmetic appendix has separate isogeny and polarization
   inputs and counts geometric classes only.

## The critical interfaces

- **Whole primary factors.** `prop:generic-spectral-connection-splitting`
  separates connections, not just leading matrices. Ranks are full even
  ranks. Individual Jordan blocks are never independently selected.
- **Canonical lattice.** `lem:A0preserve` makes the rank-two elementary
  modification regular singular. `prop:rank2-rigidity` transports its residue
  by conjugacy. `I_exp` requires different exponent classes modulo integers;
  `I_lat` also detects resonant blocks, and therefore needs the original
  lattice and its regular inverse preserved.
- **Actual center coefficients.** `lem:faithful-center-base-change` uses the
  reduced source generated by combined divisor/Novikov monomials and
  independent target divisor characters. These recover curve-class data
  that the raw Novikov map can lose. `lem:hodge-fixed-comparison` repeats
  the injection proof on the fixed base, rather than restricting an
  injection through a quotient without proof.
- **Surface vanishing before fourfold invariance.** The ruled-product
  potential includes the full even bulk and the elliptic case. Birational
  reduction of ruled surfaces uses point centers only. The rank-three
  selector additionally needs the odd-vanishing argument when a nef
  minimal surface has second Betti number one.
- **Product continuation.** The small GW product formula identifies the
  actual tensor connection and original lattice. Cyclic persistence in
  every even bulk direction, including mixed ones, supplies the endpoint
  values. No big mixed-bulk tensor formula is asserted.
- **Hodge recovery.** One rational reductive group handles the factorization.
  Tate suspensions are forgotten. The selected odd sum is unlabelled;
  individual eigenvalue-labelled factors need not descend to the rationals.
  Cancellation and pure weight three recover a rational Hodge isomorphism,
  with no integral or polarization claim.

## Inputs, computation and formal coverage

`verification/imported-sources.json` records source statements and convention
matches. The cubic calculation is written out in the paper. For the extended
Fano table, `verification/fano-matrices/README.md` records exact inputs,
replay commands, source normalizations and the independent arithmetic check.
The finite programs establish matrix identities, not geometric comparison,
classification or deformation theorems.

The Lean companion is a partial formal artifact. The exact inventory and
limitations are in `lean/verification/claims.json`. Existing algebraic
fragments and deductions retain their boundaries; the twelve additional
geometric statements in the two-part integration are registered as absent.
The entry point is `lean/TavisRuddFiniteGeom/Papers/CubicStabilizationM1/PaperInterface/Main.lean`.
`lean/verification/expected_axioms.txt` is the expected axiom list, not a
record of a fresh kernel run.

From the paper root, `make check` validates source correspondence, replays the
finite matrix checks, builds the PDF and rejects TeX warnings. It neither
builds Lean nor checks a captured axiom transcript. See `lean/README.md` and
`verification/README.md` for those separate semantics. The optional
`nix run .#blueprint-web` rendering is a reading aid, not a Lean replay.

## Optional generality

The general bundle and additive appendices retain Iritani–Koto's theorem.
They include the arbitrary-threefold criterion, the additive discriminant
spectrum on `K0(Var_C)/(L-1)`, equal-Hodge-diamond examples, and higher
stabilization factorization constraints. Their stronger scope is not supplied
by the specialized P1 lemma. The rank-three lattice limitation there concerns
a higher-rank logarithmic modification, not the odd-dimension selector used
in the Fano classification.
