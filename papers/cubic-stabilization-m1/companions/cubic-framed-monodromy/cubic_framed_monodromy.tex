\title{Framed Formal Monodromy of Cubic Threefolds}
\author{Tavis Rudd}
\date{August 2026}

\begin{document}

\begin{abstract}
We define a framed formal-monodromy count \(\nu_6\) for the numerical small
even quantum connection and compute
\(\nu_6(X)=2\) and \(\nu_6(X\times\PP^1)=4\) for every smooth cubic
threefold.  The calculation and product formula are unconditional.  Under an
explicit reconstruction-tail hypothesis, the count satisfies blowup and
projective-bundle formulas; under a separate residual divisor-tagging
hypothesis for the remaining surface centers, it is birationally invariant
through dimension four.  These hypotheses give a conditional second proof
of one-step irrationality and the conditional identity \(\nu_6(V)=2\) for
prime Fano threefolds of genus eight.  We isolate the exact comparison
statements still needed to make the refinement unconditional.
\end{abstract}

\noindent\textbf{Keywords.} cubic threefold; quantum \(D\)-modules; formal
monodromy; primitive sixth roots; blowups; projective bundles.
