\section{Introduction}

Let \(W_5\) be the five-dimensional irreducible representation of \(A_5\).
The invariant space
\[
 \bigl(\operatorname{Sym}^3W_5^*\bigr)^{A_5}
\]
has dimension two, so its projectivization is a pencil of cubic threefolds.
We prove universal \(CH_0\)-triviality uniformly over its smooth locus by
constructing the primitive minimal class of the intermediate Jacobian.

\begin{theorem}[The nonstandard \(A_5\)-pencil]
\label{thm:separation-family}
\coverage{conditional_deduction}%
\lean{separationFamily_of_sixAxis_residue_and_period_inputs}%
Let \(B^\circ\) be the smooth locus in
\(\PP((\operatorname{Sym}^3W_5^*)^{A_5})\), and let
\(\mathcal X\to B^\circ\) be the resulting family.  For every
\(b\in B^\circ(\C)\),
\[
 \mathcal X_b\quad\text{and}\quad\mathcal X_b\times\PP^1
\]
are universally \(CH_0\)-trivial, while
\(\mathcal X_b\times\PP^1\) is irrational.  The family is non-isotrivial in
coarse moduli.  Exactly one point of its coarse-moduli image is represented
by a cubic whose defining form is a sum of cubic forms in pairwise disjoint
nonempty groups of at most three variables: the Fermat point.
\end{theorem}

The geometric input is a six-axis configuration on the Fano surface of lines.
Its polarization matrix has a unit direction and a four-dimensional
depth-one part.  The principal gluing kernel is selected by a quadratic
two-primary orientation, and its integral graph coordinates satisfy a
rank-one generation theorem in every degree.  Voisin's criterion then turns
the resulting algebraic primitive minimal class into universal
\(CH_0\)-triviality.  Irrationality after one stabilization is imported from
\cite{RuddM1}; it is logically independent of the cycle construction here.

The character of \(W_5\) is \((5,1,-1,0,0)\) on the classes
\(1,2A,3A,5A,5B\).  The symmetric-cube character formula gives
\[
 \dim\bigl(\operatorname{Sym}^3W_5^*\bigr)^{A_5}=2.
\]
Hartlieb identifies the resulting coarse-moduli image with his
\(M_{H_1}\), a one-dimensional special subvariety
\cite[Lemma~5.5 and Proposition~5.7]{Hartlieb}.

\begin{remark}
\label{rem:fermat-in-pencil}
The pencil contains the Fermat cubic.  The representation \(W_5\) is induced
from a nontrivial cubic character of a point stabilizer \(A_4\subset A_5\);
in a monomial realization, every nonzero matrix entry is a cube root of
unity, so \(x_1^3+\cdots+x_5^3\) is invariant.  This is the unique
separated-variable point in the coarse-moduli image.
\end{remark}

Section~\ref{sec:minimal-class} proves the all-degree integral saturation
theorem and its universal-\(CH_0\) consequence.  The final section proves
Theorem~\ref{thm:separation-family} and records the relation with the
