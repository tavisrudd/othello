\section{Introduction}

Let \(X\subset\PP^4\) be a smooth complex cubic threefold.  Clemens and
Griffiths proved that \(X\) is irrational \cite{CG}, but their
intermediate-Jacobian obstruction does not directly survive on
\(X\times\PP^1\): in a factorization of fourfolds, a surface center can
contribute precisely the weight-three Hodge structure carried by \(X\).  We
prove that the stabilized fourfold is nevertheless irrational.

\begin{theorem}[One-step irrationality]
\label{thm:every-cubic}
\coverage{conditional_deduction}%
\lean{rankTwoResidueMarker_eq_of_birational,%
  cubicThreefold_oneProjectiveLine_conclusion_of_residueMarker}%
For every smooth complex cubic threefold \(X\), the fourfold
\(X\times\PP^1\) is irrational.
\end{theorem}

The proof uses the generic even quantum \(D\)-module (QDM).  Over an algebraic
closure of its generic coefficient field, Euler multiplication splits the
formal connection into blocks.  We place those blocks in an effective
commutative ledger.  Iritani's blowup theorem and Iritani--Koto's
projective-bundle theorem give additive operation formulas, with each copy of
a blowup center retained as a separate occurrence.  Weak factorization then
makes every additive block marker that vanishes on the possible centers a
birational invariant.

The marker used here detects a rank-two block whose centered leading Euler
operator is nonzero square-zero and whose two formal exponent classes are
distinct modulo the integers.  Pairing horizontality makes the required
elementary modification regular; flatness moves its residue by conjugation.
For a cubic threefold, Beauville's quantum products give exponent
representatives \(-1/6\) and \(-5/6\), with difference \(2/3\).  The marker
vanishes on points, curves, and surfaces, doubles on product with
\(\PP^1\), and vanishes on projective space.  This proves the theorem.

The comparison inputs are Iritani's blowup decomposition and
Iritani--Koto's projective-bundle decomposition
\cite{IritaniBlowup,IritaniKoto}.  What is new here is their use as an
occurrence-indexed additive ledger through weak factorization, together with
the formal-exponent fold and its low-dimensional nullity.  Cai computes the
cubic quantum differential equation in a compatible normalization
\cite{Cai}; that calculation neither constructs this birational marker nor
proves the one-stabilization conclusion.

The same construction gives a threefold criterion without stabilization.

\begin{corollary}[Threefold marker criterion]
\label{cor:threefold-marker}
\coverage{fragment}%
\lean{threefold_not_rational_of_occurrenceIndexedMarker}%
Let \(Y\) be a smooth projective complex threefold.  If its generic even QDM
contains a block marked by \eqref{eq:atomic-fold}, then \(Y\) is irrational.
\end{corollary}

Indeed, the only centers in a weak factorization of threefolds have dimension
at most one, where the marker vanishes, while the generic quantum product of
\(\PP^3\) is semisimple.  This is a QDM-side irrationality criterion.  It is
not identified here with semiorthogonal components or with a derived
categorical rationality criterion.

The result also propagates through stabilized birational correspondences.
Kuznetsov's construction applies to an arbitrary smooth prime Fano threefold
\(V\) of genus eight: it associates a smooth Pfaffian cubic threefold \(X\)
and rank-two projective bundles over \(V\) and \(X\) related by a flop
\cite[Theorems~2.17--2.18]{KuznetsovV14}.
This gives the following consequence.

\begin{corollary}[One stabilization of \(V_{14}\)]
\label{cor:v14-one-step}
\coverage{conditional_deduction}%
\lean{genusEight_oneProjectiveLine_not_rational_of_residueContext}%
For every smooth prime Fano threefold \(V\) of genus eight,
\(V\times\PP^1\) is irrational.
\end{corollary}

The theorem also separates irrationality from universal \(CH_0\)-triviality
on several known loci of cubic threefolds.  Universal \(CH_0\)-triviality
passes to a projective bundle by the projective-bundle formula for Chow
groups.

\begin{corollary}[Separation in codimension three]
\label{cor:voisin-separation}
\coverage{fragment}%
\lean{primitiveMinimalClassFamily_universalCH0_and_irrational_stabilization,%
 exists_universalCH0_cubic_with_irrational_stabilization}%
\uses{thm:every-cubic}%
\imports{Voisin:minimal-class-locus}%
There is a nonempty countable union of subvarieties of codimension at most
three in the moduli space of smooth cubic threefolds such that, for every
member \(X\), both \(X\) and \(X\times\PP^1\) are universally
\(CH_0\)-trivial, while \(X\times\PP^1\) is irrational.
\end{corollary}

\begin{corollary}[Fermat]
\label{cor:fermat-separation}
\coverage{conditional_deduction}%
\lean{fermatCubic_universalCH0_and_irrational_stabilization}%
\uses{thm:every-cubic}%
\imports{CT:separated-variable-criterion}%
The fourfold
\[
  \{x_1^3+x_2^3+x_3^3+x_4^3+x_5^3=0\}\times\PP^1
\]
is universally \(CH_0\)-trivial and irrational.
\end{corollary}

\begin{corollary}[Coprime-degree cubics]
\label{cor:coprime-separation}
\coverage{conditional_deduction}%
\lean{coprimeUnirationalDegrees_universalCH0_and_irrational_stabilization}%
\uses{thm:every-cubic}%
\imports{YYZ:coprime-degree-family, YYZ:coprime-degrees-persist}%
Let \(X\) be one of the cubic threefolds of
\cite[Theorem~3.3]{YYZ}.  Then \(X\times\PP^1\) admits unirational
parametrizations of coprime degrees, is universally \(CH_0\)-trivial, and is
irrational.
\end{corollary}

Voisin proves the codimension-three statement
\cite[Theorem~4.5]{Voisin}.  The Fermat case follows from
Colliot-Th\'el\`ene's separated-variable criterion \cite{CT}.  Yang--Yu--Zhu construct
the coprime-degree family \cite[Theorems~1.1 and 3.3]{YYZ}.  The last
corollary settles their question at the first nontrivial stabilization, not
at every stabilization.

Section~\ref{sec:atomic-one-step} constructs the block ledger, proves its QDM
operation formulas, defines the formal-exponent marker, computes the cubic
block, and proves center nullity.  Section~\ref{sec:applications} gives the
threefold, genus-eight, and universal-\(CH_0\) consequences.
