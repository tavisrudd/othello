\title{Irrationality of Cubic Threefolds after One Stabilization}
\author{Tavis Rudd}
\date{August 2026}

\begin{abstract}
We prove that \(X\times\PP^1\) is irrational for every smooth complex cubic
threefold \(X\).  Our invariant counts certain rank-two blocks of the generic
even quantum \(D\)-module.  A counted block has centered leading term
\(N\ne0\) with \(N^2=0\), and after a canonical elementary modification its
two residue eigenvalues are distinct modulo \(\Z\).  Iritani's blowup
decomposition and weak factorization make this count birationally invariant
because every point, curve, and surface center contributes zero to the blowup
formula.
Iritani--Koto's projective-bundle decomposition computes its value after
stabilization; the resulting count distinguishes \(X\times\PP^1\) from
\(\PP^4\).  The same count gives an irrationality criterion for smooth
projective threefolds, the one-stabilization theorem for prime Fano threefolds
of genus eight, and irrational universally \(CH_0\)-trivial fourfolds.
\end{abstract}

\noindent\textbf{Keywords.} cubic threefold; universal \(CH_0\)-triviality;
irrationality; quantum \(D\)-modules; formal exponents; birational invariants;
weak factorization; Fano threefolds.
